# 英语考前速看

> 考前 1-2 周快速过一遍的浓缩卡片：作文框架、替换词、答题技巧、时间分配。

## 一、小作文万能框架（5 类高频信型）

**通用格式**：称呼（Dear Sir or Madam, / Dear Professor Smith,）→ 正文 2-3 段 → 落款（Yours sincerely, Li Ming）。

### 1. 建议信

- 开头：I am writing to put forward some practical suggestions regarding...
- 开头：I would like to offer a few suggestions on...
- 结尾：I hope you will find these suggestions helpful. / I would appreciate it if you could take my suggestions into consideration.

### 2. 通知（Notice）

- 标题 **Notice** 居中，落款写主办方。
- 开头：An English speech contest is to be held in the lecture hall on...
- 开头：We are pleased to announce that...
- 结尾：Those interested please sign up at the Students' Union office before Friday. / All are welcome.

### 3. 道歉信

- 开头：I am terribly sorry to tell you that... / Please accept my sincere apology for...
- 结尾：I apologize again for any inconvenience caused. / I would like to make it up to you by...

### 4. 邀请信

- 开头：It is my great honor to invite you to attend... / On behalf of..., I cordially invite you to...
- 结尾：We would be looking forward to your coming. / Please let me know your availability at your earliest convenience.

### 5. 感谢信

- 开头：I am writing to express my heartfelt gratitude for your...
- 结尾：Thanks again for what you have done. / I hope I can reciprocate your kindness someday.

## 二、大作文图表作文框架（三段式）

### 首段：图表描述

- As is clearly shown in the chart, ...
- The bar chart above illustrates the changes in... from 2010 to 2020.
- 数据表达：increase / rise / climb **from A to B**；account for X%；reach a peak of...；remain stable at...；by contrast, ... declined to...

### 中段：原因分析

- 总起：Several factors contribute to this phenomenon. / What accounts for this trend?
- 列举：**First of all,... Moreover,... Last but not least,...**（万能连接）
- 常用原因弹药：rapid economic development / the popularity of smartphones / changing lifestyles / the improvement of living standards / government policy support

### 尾段：趋势预测 / 建议

- From my perspective, this trend is likely to continue in the foreseeable future.
- Accordingly, it is advisable for... to...
- Only in this way can we...（倒装加分句）

## 四、阅读答题技巧速览

### 细节题（占大头）

- 题干关键词（人名/数字/专有名词）**定位原文段落** → 找**同义替换**；
- 正确答案 = 原文的改写；**原词照抄的选项多为陷阱**。

### 主旨题

- 看首尾段 + 各段首句；**转折词（however / but）之后是重点**；
- 标题题答案需覆盖全文，过窄（细节）过宽（扩大范围）都排除。

### 态度题（褒贬词汇表）

| 倾向 | 词汇 |
|------|------|
| 褒义 | positive, supportive, optimistic, approving, favorable |
| 贬义 | negative, critical, skeptical, doubtful, disapproving |
| 中性 | neutral, objective, impartial, detached |
| 陷阱 | **indifferent（漠不关心）几乎从不为正确答案** |

### 推断题 / 词义题

- 推断题：答案仍是原文同义改写，"过度推理"的选项必错；
- 词义题：看本句和前后句的逻辑关系，**别套词典义**。

## 五、完形 / 翻译救命要点

### 完形填空

- **首句不设空，必读**——全文主旨/基调的重要线索；
- 先做逻辑连接词题（however / therefore / while / whereas）：看前后句义方向即可定；
- 固定搭配靠日常积累，考场凭语感，别耗时间；
- **全卷最后做**，剩 10 分钟也能捞 6 分，不恋战。

### 翻译三步法（长句拆分）

1. **拆分**：找谓语动词和连词，把长句切成意群；
2. **直译主干**：先把主谓宾骨架译成中文；
3. **调整语序**：定语/状语按中文习惯前移或后置，增补逻辑连接词使译文通顺。

> 不会的词按词性猜着译也**别留空白**；整体通顺比逐词精确更重要（英二整段 150 词，踩点给分）。

## 六、考场时间分配（180 分钟最后提醒）

| 顺序 | 部分 | 时间 |
|:----:|------|:----:|
| 1 | 阅读理解 A（4 篇） | 65-70 min |
| 2 | 小作文 | 20 min |
| 3 | 大作文 | 25 min |
| 4 | 新题型 | 15-20 min |
| 5 | 翻译 | 20-25 min |
| 6 | 完形填空 | 10-15 min |
| 7 | 涂卡 + 检查 | 10 min |

**最后三条**：

1. 先阅读后作文，完形永远最后；
2. 作文字迹工整、分段清晰，卷面即分数；
3. 翻译写通顺中文，不留空；作文宁可简单句写对，不长难句写错。

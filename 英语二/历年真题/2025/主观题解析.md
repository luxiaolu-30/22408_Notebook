## 详细解析

##### 翻译（15 分）

#### 原文

You know the moment—the conversation slows, then there's a pause. It's awkward, and so awkward that some people will panic and say anything. Do we all find such silences so stressful?

Researchers analysed the frequency and impact of gaps greater than 2 seconds during conversations, including an overview of previous studies which indicate that the fear of awkward silences can be so extreme that people avoid talking to strangers, even though doing so is likely to be an enjoyable experience.

During conversations with short gaps, people feel more connected to their conversation partners. But such feelings of connection markedly dip when entering a long gap. Long gaps between strangers are likely to be followed by a change in topic.

But the opposite seems to be true for conversations between friends. Long gaps there saw increased connection. Between friends, longer gaps seem to provide natural moments for reflection and expression.

#### 参考译文

你一定经历过这样的时刻——对话慢了下来，然后出现了停顿。这很尴尬，尴尬到有些人会惊慌失措，什么话都说得出来。我们所有人都觉得这样的沉默如此令人倍感压力吗？

研究人员分析了对话中超过2秒的停顿的频率和影响，包括对先前研究的综述，这些研究表明，对尴尬沉默的恐惧可能如此强烈，以至于人们避免与陌生人交谈，尽管这样做很可能是一次愉快的体验。

在短暂停顿的对话中，人们感觉与对话伙伴更加亲近。但当进入长时间停顿时，这种连接感会显著下降。陌生人之间的长时间停顿可能会伴随话题的转变。

但对于朋友之间的对话来说，情况似乎恰恰相反。在朋友之间，长时间的停顿反而增加了连接感。在朋友之间，更长的停顿似乎提供了自然的反思和表达时刻。

#### 重点词汇与句型

- **pause**：停顿，暂停
- **awkward**：尴尬的，令人难堪的
- **panic**：惊慌，恐慌
- **frequency**：频率
- **gap**：间隙，间隔（此处指对话中的停顿）
- **overview**：概述，综述
- **extreme**：极端的，极其的
- **markedly**：显著地，明显地
- **dip**：下降，下跌
- **reflection**：反思，思考

#### 长难句分析

**1. Researchers analysed the frequency and impact of gaps greater than 2 seconds during conversations, including an overview of previous studies which indicate that the fear of awkward silences can be so extreme that people avoid talking to strangers, even though doing so is likely to be an enjoyable experience.**

- **结构**：主句 + including介词短语 + which定语从句 + that宾语从句（嵌套so...that结果状语从句 + even though让步状语从句）
- **拆分**：Researchers analysed the frequency and impact of gaps greater than 2 seconds during conversations（主句）/ including an overview of previous studies（介词短语）/ which indicate（定语从句，修饰studies）/ that the fear of awkward silences can be so extreme（宾语从句）/ that people avoid talking to strangers（so...that结果状语从句）/ even though doing so is likely to be an enjoyable experience（让步状语从句）
- **翻译要点**：which定语从句较长，需拆分单独成句；so...that=如此……以至于；even though=尽管；doing so指代talking to strangers
- **译文**：研究人员分析了对话中超过2秒的停顿的频率和影响，包括对先前研究的综述，这些研究表明，对尴尬沉默的恐惧可能如此强烈，以至于人们避免与陌生人交谈，尽管这样做很可能是一次愉快的体验。

**2. But such feelings of connection markedly dip when entering a long gap.**

- **结构**：主句 + when引导的时间状语从句（省略结构）
- **拆分**：such feelings of connection markedly dip（主句）/ when entering a long gap（时间状语从句，when后省略they are）
- **翻译要点**：markedly=显著地；dip=下降；when entering=when they are entering（省略主语和be动词）
- **译文**：但当进入长时间停顿时，这种连接感会显著下降。

**3. But the opposite seems to be true for conversations between friends.**

- **结构**：简单句，the opposite作主语
- **翻译要点**：the opposite=相反的情况；seems to be true=似乎是正确的
- **译文**：但对于朋友之间的对话来说，情况似乎恰恰相反。

##### 小作文（10 分）

#### 题目

**Directions:**

Suppose you are planning a short play based on a classic Chinese novel. Write your friend John an email to

1) introduce the play, and
2) invite him to take part in it.

You should write neatly on the ANSWER SHEET. (10 points)

You should write about 100 words on the ANSWER SHEET. Do not use your own name at the end of the email. Use "Li Ming" instead.

**场景说明**：假设你是李明，正在策划一部基于中国古典小说改编的短剧，给朋友John写一封电子邮件，介绍这部短剧并邀请他参加。属于"介绍+邀请信"。

#### 高分范文

Dear John,

I hope this email finds you well. I am writing to share some exciting news with you. I am currently planning a short play based on one of China's four great classical novels, Journey to the West, and I would love to invite you to take part in it.

The play will tell the story of the Monkey King and his companions on their journey to obtain sacred scriptures. We will hold rehearsals every Wednesday evening starting from next week, and the performance is scheduled for late March. I think you would be perfect for the role of a foreign monk, as your fluent English and charismatic personality would bring the character to life.

It would be wonderful to have you on board. Please let me know if you are interested. I look forward to hearing from you soon.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

#### 范文翻译

亲爱的John：

希望这封邮件找到你时你一切都好。我写信是想和你分享一个好消息。我目前正在策划一部基于中国四大古典名著之一《西游记》改编的短剧，我想邀请你参加。

这部短剧将讲述美猴王及其同伴们西天取经的故事。我们将从下周开始，每周三晚上进行排练，演出定于三月下旬。我认为你非常适合饰演一位外国僧侣的角色，因为你流利的英语和有魅力的个性将为这个角色注入生命力。

如果你能加入就太好了。请告诉我你是否感兴趣。期待尽快收到你的回复。

此致敬礼
李明

#### 重点句型与表达

1. **I hope this email finds you well.** —— 邮件开头万能问候句
2. **I am writing to share some exciting news with you.** —— "我写信想和你分享好消息"，交代写信背景
3. **I would love to invite you to take part in it.** —— "我想邀请你参加"，邀请信核心句
4. **The play will tell the story of...** —— "短剧将讲述……的故事"，用于介绍内容
5. **We will hold rehearsals every... starting from...** —— "我们将从……开始每周……排练"，交代具体安排
6. **I think you would be perfect for the role of...** —— "我认为你非常适合……的角色"，体现邀请诚意
7. **It would be wonderful to have you on board.** —— "你能加入就太好了"，邀请信结尾
8. **Please let me know if you are interested. I look forward to hearing from you soon.** —— "如果感兴趣请告诉我。期待尽快回复"，书信万能结尾

##### 大作文（15 分）

#### 图表描述

**图表类型**：柱状图（Bar Chart）

**图表内容**：某社区老年人主要休闲活动调查
- 看电视（watching TV）：90.8%（占比最大）
- 散步（taking a walk）：68.3%
- 养花（growing flowers）：34.7%
- 阅读（reading）：31.8%
- 下棋（playing chess）：18.4%（占比最小）

#### 题目

**Directions:**

Write an essay based on the chart below. In your writing, you should
1) describe and interpret the chart, and
2) give your comments.

You should write about 150 words on the ANSWER SHEET. (15 points)

#### 高分范文

The bar chart above clearly illustrates the main leisure activities of the elderly in a certain community. As is shown, watching TV ranks far ahead of the rest, chosen by 90.8% of the respondents, followed by taking a walk at 68.3%. By contrast, growing flowers and reading account for only 34.7% and 31.8% respectively, while playing chess sits at the bottom, favored by merely 18.4%.

What are the reasons behind this distribution? Several factors contribute to this phenomenon. Initially, watching TV is the most accessible and least physically demanding form of entertainment for the elderly, requiring no special skills or equipment. Furthermore, taking a walk is not only a leisure activity but also a form of exercise, which explains its high participation rate. Interestingly, activities that require more cognitive engagement or social interaction, such as reading, growing flowers, and playing chess, attract fewer participants, possibly due to declining eyesight, limited mobility, or lack of companions.

In conclusion, the chart reveals that the elderly's leisure activities are largely dominated by passive and accessible forms of entertainment. It is advisable that communities organize more diverse and engaging activities to enrich the spiritual and social lives of the elderly.

#### 范文翻译

上方的柱状图清晰地展示了某社区老年人主要的休闲活动。如图所示，看电视遥遥领先，90.8%的受访者选择了这一活动，其次是散步占68.3%。相比之下，养花和阅读仅分别占34.7%和31.8%，而下棋排在最后，仅有18.4%的人选择。

这一分布背后的原因是什么？有几个因素促成了这一现象。首先，看电视是老年人最容易获得且体力消耗最小的娱乐形式，不需要特殊技能或设备。此外，散步不仅是一项休闲活动，也是一种锻炼方式，这解释了其高参与率。有趣的是，需要更多认知参与或社交互动的活动，如阅读、养花和下棋，吸引了较少的参与者，可能由于视力下降、行动不便或缺乏同伴。

总之，图表表明老年人的休闲活动主要被被动和易获取的娱乐形式所主导。建议社区组织更多样化和有吸引力的活动，以丰富老年人的精神和社会生活。

#### 重点句型与表达

1. **The bar chart above clearly illustrates...** —— 柱状图开头万能句型，引出图表主题
2. **As is shown, ... ranks far ahead of the rest, chosen by X% of the respondents, followed by... at Y%** —— 描述柱状图数据的经典句式（最大项+排序）
3. **By contrast, ... account for only X% and Y% respectively, while ... sits at the bottom, favored by merely Z%** —— "相比之下，……分别仅占X%和Y%，而……排在最后，仅有Z%选择"
4. **What are the reasons behind this distribution?** —— 过渡句，引出原因分析
5. **...is the most accessible and least physically demanding form of...** —— "是最容易获得且体力消耗最小的……形式"
6. **Interestingly, activities that require more... attract fewer participants, possibly due to...** —— "有趣的是，需要更多……的活动吸引了较少的参与者，可能由于……"
7. **In conclusion, the chart reveals that...** —— 结尾段总结句，"图表揭示……"
8. **It is advisable that... organize more diverse and engaging activities to...** —— "建议……组织更多样化和有吸引力的活动来……"

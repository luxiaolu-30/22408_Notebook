## 详细解析

##### 翻译（15 分）

#### 原文

My dream has always been to work somewhere in an area between fashion and publishing. Two years before graduating from secondary school, I took a sewing and design course thinking that I would move on to a fashion design course. However, during that course I realised that I was not good enough in this area to compete with other creative personalities in the future, so I decided that it was not the right path for me. Before applying for university I told everyone that I would study journalism, because writing was, and still is, one of my favourite activities. But, to be honest, I said it, because I thought that fashion and me together was just a dream — I knew that no one could imagine me in the fashion industry at all! So I decided to look for some fashion-related courses that included writing. This is when I noticed the course "Fashion Media & Promotion."

#### 参考译文

我的梦想一直是在时尚与出版之间的领域找一份工作。中学毕业前两年，我参加了一个缝纫和设计课程，以为之后可以继续学习时尚设计课程。然而，在那门课程期间，我意识到自己在这个领域不够优秀，将来无法与其他富有创造力的人竞争，所以我断定这条路不适合我。在申请大学之前，我告诉所有人我会学新闻学，因为写作曾经是、现在依然是我最喜欢的活动之一。但是，说实话，我之所以这样说，是因为我认为时尚和我在一起只是一个梦想——我知道根本没有人能想象我进入时尚行业！于是我决定去寻找一些与时尚相关且包含写作的课程。就在这时，我注意到了"时尚媒体与推广"这门课程。

#### 重点词汇与句型

- **fashion**：时尚、时装
- **publishing**：出版
- **secondary school**：中学（英式英语，相当于middle/high school）
- **sewing and design course**：缝纫与设计课程
- **move on to**：继续做某事、过渡到
- **creative personalities**：富有创造力的人/人才
- **journalism**：新闻学
- **compete with**：与……竞争
- **to be honest**：说实话（插入语）
- **Fashion Media & Promotion**：时尚媒体与推广

#### 长难句分析

##### 长难句1

> Two years before graduating from secondary school, I took a sewing and design course thinking that I would move on to a fashion design course.

**句子拆分：**
- 主干：I took a sewing and design course
- `Two years before graduating from secondary school`：时间状语
- `thinking that I would move on to a fashion design course`：现在分词作伴随状语
  - that引导thinking的宾语从句

**翻译：** 中学毕业前两年，我参加了一个缝纫和设计课程，以为之后可以继续学习时尚设计课程。

---

##### 长难句2

> However, during that course I realised that I was not good enough in this area to compete with other creative personalities in the future, so I decided that it was not the right path for me.

**句子拆分：**
- 主干：I realised that..., so I decided that...（so连接的并列句）
- that I was not good enough in this area to compete with other creative personalities in the future：realise的宾语从句
  - `not...enough to do`：不够……以至于不能……
- that it was not the right path for me：decide的宾语从句

**翻译：** 然而，在那门课程期间，我意识到自己在这个领域不够优秀，将来无法与其他富有创造力的人竞争，所以我断定这条路不适合我。

---

##### 长难句3

> But, to be honest, I said it, because I thought that fashion and me together was just a dream — I knew that no one could imagine me in the fashion industry at all!

**句子拆分：**
- 主干：I said it, because I thought that... — I knew that...
- `to be honest`：插入语
- `because I thought that fashion and me together was just a dream`：because原因状语从句，that引导thought的宾语从句
- `I knew that no one could imagine me in the fashion industry at all`：破折号后补充说明，that引导knew的宾语从句

**翻译：** 但是，说实话，我之所以这样说，是因为我认为时尚和我在一起只是一个梦想——我知道根本没有人能想象我进入时尚行业！

##### 小作文（10 分）

#### 题目

**Directions:**

Suppose you are invited by Professor Williams to give a presentation about Chinese culture to a group of international students. Write a reply to

1) accept the invitation, and
2) introduce the key points of your presentation.

You should write about 100 words on the ANSWER SHEET.

Do not use your own name. Use "Li Ming" instead.

Do not write your address. (10 points)

#### 高分范文

Dear Professor Williams,

I am truly honored to receive your invitation, and I am writing to gladly accept it. It will be my great pleasure to give a presentation on Chinese culture to the international students.

My presentation will focus on three key aspects. First, I will introduce Chinese calligraphy, explaining how each stroke carries a sense of beauty and cultural heritage. Second, I will present the traditional tea ceremony, demonstrating how it reflects our philosophy of harmony and mindfulness. Finally, I will share some fascinating stories behind major Chinese festivals, such as the Spring Festival and the Mid-Autumn Festival, to give students a vivid picture of our cultural traditions.

I will prepare the slides and materials in advance. Thank you again for this wonderful opportunity.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

#### 范文翻译

亲爱的Williams教授：

收到您的邀请我深感荣幸，我写信是为了欣然接受。能为国际学生做一场关于中国文化的演讲，我感到非常荣幸。

我的演讲将围绕三个关键方面展开。首先，我将介绍中国书法，解释每一笔如何承载美感和文化传承。其次，我将展示传统茶道，展示它如何体现我们和谐与正念的哲学。最后，我将分享一些中国主要节日背后的有趣故事，如春节和中秋节，让学生对我们的文化传统有生动的了解。

我将提前准备幻灯片和材料。再次感谢您给予这个难得的机会。

您真诚的，
Li Ming

#### 重点句型与表达

1. **I am truly honored to receive your invitation, and I am writing to gladly accept it.**（收到邀请深感荣幸，写信欣然接受）——接受邀请信开头句型
2. **It will be my great pleasure to...**（做某事是我的荣幸）——表达欣然接受的句型
3. **My presentation will focus on three key aspects.**（我的演讲将围绕三个关键方面展开）——引出演讲内容的句型
4. **First, I will introduce..., explaining how...**（首先，我将介绍……，解释……如何）——列举要点+现在分词补充说明
5. **Second, I will present..., demonstrating how it reflects...**（其次，我将展示……，展示它如何体现……）——列举要点+现在分词补充
6. **Finally, I will share...to give students a vivid picture of...**（最后，我将分享……以给学生一个生动的画面）——列举要点+不定式表目的
7. **I will prepare...in advance.**（我将提前准备……）——结尾说明准备情况
8. **Thank you again for this wonderful opportunity.**（再次感谢您给予这个难得的机会）——结尾感谢句型

##### 大作文（15 分）

#### 图表描述

折线/柱状图（Line/Bar Chart）：2013-2015年我国博物馆数量及参观人数变化

两条数据线：
- 博物馆数量（家）：2013年约4165家 → 2014年约4692家 → 2015年约5132家（持续增长）
- 参观人数（百万人次）：2013年约637.8百万人次 → 2014年约717.9百万人次 → 2015年约781.1百万人次（持续增长，增幅显著）

#### 题目

**Directions:**

Write an essay based on the following chart. In your writing, you should

1) interpret the chart, and
2) give your comments.

You should write about 150 words on the ANSWER SHEET. (15 points)

#### 高分范文

The chart above clearly illustrates the changes in the number of museums and their visitors in China from 2013 to 2015. As is shown, both the number of museums and the number of visitors experienced steady growth during this period. The number of museums increased from approximately 4,165 in 2013 to about 5,132 in 2015. Meanwhile, the number of visitors rose remarkably from 637.8 million to 781.1 million, an increase of nearly 143 million.

Several factors may account for this upward trend. First and foremost, with the rapid economic development and the improvement of living standards, people have growing spiritual and cultural needs, which has led to an increasing enthusiasm for museum visits. Moreover, the government has invested heavily in cultural infrastructure, resulting in the construction of more museums across the country. Additionally, museums have made efforts to modernize their exhibitions and offer free admission, making them more accessible and appealing to the public.

From my perspective, this trend is highly encouraging, as it reflects the public's growing appreciation for culture and knowledge. It is hoped that more museums will be built and innovative exhibitions will be organized to further enrich people's cultural lives.

#### 范文翻译

上面的图表清楚地展示了2013年至2015年中国博物馆数量和参观人数的变化。如图所示，博物馆数量和参观人数在此期间均稳步增长。博物馆数量从2013年的约4165家增长到2015年的约5132家。同时，参观人数从6.378亿大幅增长到7.811亿，增加了近1.43亿。

有几个因素可以解释这一上升趋势。首先，随着经济快速发展和生活水平提高，人们的精神文化需求不断增长，这导致了对参观博物馆的热情日益高涨。其次，政府在文化基础设施上投入大量资金，使得全国各地建设了更多博物馆。此外，博物馆努力实现展览现代化并免费开放，使其对公众更加便利和有吸引力。

在我看来，这一趋势非常令人鼓舞，因为它反映了公众对文化和知识日益增长的重视。希望未来能建设更多博物馆，组织更多创新展览，进一步丰富人们的文化生活。

#### 重点句型与表达

1. **The chart above clearly illustrates the changes in...from...to...**（上面的图表清楚地展示了……从……到……的变化）——折线/柱状图描述开头句型
2. **As is shown, both...experienced steady growth during this period.**（如图所示，两者在此期间均稳步增长）——总体趋势描述句型
3. **The number of...increased from...in 2013 to...in 2015.**（……的数量从2013年的……增长到2015年的……）——具体数据描述
4. **Meanwhile, the number of...rose remarkably from...to..., an increase of...**（同时，……的数量从……大幅增长到……，增加了……）——描述另一数据线+同位语补充增幅
5. **Several factors may account for this upward trend.**（有几个因素可以解释这一上升趋势）——原因分析段开头句型
6. **First and foremost, with the rapid economic development..., people have growing...needs**（首先，随着经济快速发展……，人们有日益增长的……需求）——with短语表背景
7. **Moreover, the government has invested heavily in..., resulting in...**（其次，政府在……投入大量资金，导致……）——result in表因果
8. **From my perspective, this trend is highly encouraging, as it reflects...**（在我看来，这一趋势非常令人鼓舞，因为它反映了……）——评论段开头+as表原因

#### 重点解析

- **第1题（C. warning）**：动词辨析。"academics, writers, and activists once again warning that technology is replacing human workers"（学者、作家和活动家再次警告技术正在取代人类工人）。warning=警告，符合语境。
- **第2题（A. inequality）**：名词辨析。后文说"少数富人拥有所有资本，大众在贫困中挣扎"，描述的是不平等。inequality=不平等。
- **第3题（D. prediction）**：名词辨析。"A different and not mutually exclusive prediction holds that..."（另一种并不互斥的预测认为……）。prediction=预测，与上文imagine呼应。
- **第4题（A. characterized）**：动词辨析。"one characterized by purposelessness"（以无目的性为特征的世界）。characterized by=以……为特征。
- **第5题（B. meaning）**：名词辨析。"Without jobs to give their lives meaning"（没有工作赋予生活意义）。meaning=意义，与purposelessness呼应。
- **第6题（B. Indeed）**：逻辑连词。"Indeed, today's unemployed don't seem to be having a great time"（事实上，如今的失业者似乎过得并不好）。Indeed=确实、事实上，承上启下。
- **第7题（C. working）**：上下文逻辑。"double the rate for working Americans"（是在职美国人抑郁率的两倍）。working=在职的，与unemployed对比。
- **第9题（D. among）**：介词辨析。"addiction among poorly-educated, middle-aged people"（在受教育程度低的中年人中的成瘾问题）。among=在……之中。
- **第11题（C. necessarily）**：副词辨析。"it doesn't necessarily follow"（并不必然得出结论）。necessarily=必然地，not necessarily=未必。
- **第12题（B. downsides）**：名词辨析。"based on the downsides of being unemployed"（基于失业的负面因素）。downsides=缺点、负面，与unease呼应。
- **第13题（A. absence）**：固定搭配。"In the absence of work"（在没有工作的情况下）。in the absence of=缺乏、没有。
- **第15题（C. virtue）**：名词辨析。"the virtue of work may be a bit overblown"（工作的好处可能被夸大了）。virtue=优点、美德，后文说工作有很多缺点，说明优点被夸大。
- **第16题（D. scarce）**：形容词辨析。"leisure time is relatively scarce"（休闲时间相对稀缺）。scarce=稀缺的，与后文"用空闲时间平衡工作需求"呼应。
- **第17题（A. demands）**：名词辨析。"intellectual and emotional demands of their jobs"（工作的智力和情感需求）。demands=需求，与counterbalance搭配。
- **第19题（D. into）**：固定搭配。"throw himself into a hobby"（投身于某个爱好）。throw into=投入。
- **第20题（B. professional）**：形容词辨析。"intensity usually reserved for professional matters"（通常留给专业事务的强度）。professional=专业的、职业的，与hobby对比。

Every Saturday morning, at 9 am, more than 50,000 runners set off 出发 to run 5km around their local park. The Parkrun phenomenon 现象 began with a dozen friends and has inspired 激发 400 events in the UK and more abroad. Events are free, staffed 配备人员 by thousands of volunteers 志愿者. Runners range from four years old to grandparents; their times range from Andrew Baddeley's world record 13 minutes 48 seconds up to an hour.

Parkrun is succeeding where London's Olympic "legacy 遗产" is failing. Ten years ago on Monday, it was announced that the Games of the 30th Olympiad 奥运会 would be in London. Planning documents pledged 承诺 that the great legacy of the Games would be to lever 撬动 a nation of sport lovers away from their couches 沙发. The population would be fitter, healthier and produce more winners. It has not happened. The number of adults doing weekly sport did rise, by nearly 2 million in the run-up to 2012—but the general population was growing faster. Worse, the numbers are now falling at an accelerating rate 加速率. The opposition claims primary school pupils doing at least two hours of sport a week have nearly halved 减半. Obesity 肥胖 has risen among adults and children. Official retrospections 反思 continue as to why London 2012 failed to "inspire a generation." The success of Parkrun offers answers.

Parkrun is not a race but a time trial 计时赛: Your only competitor is the clock. The ethos 精神 welcomes anybody. There is as much joy over a puffed-out 气喘吁吁的 first-timer being clapped 鼓掌 over the line as there is about top talent shining. The Olympic bidders 申办者, by contrast, wanted to get more people doing sports and to produce more elite 精英 athletes. The dual aim 双重目标 was mixed up: The stress on success over taking part was intimidating 令人生畏的 for newcomers.

Indeed, there is something a little absurd 荒谬的 in the state getting involved in the planning of such a fundamentally "grassroots 草根" concept as community sports associations. If there is a role for government, it should really be getting involved in providing common goods 公共物品—making sure there is space for playing fields and the money to pave 铺设 tennis and netball courts, and encouraging the provision 提供 of all these activities in schools. But successive governments 历届政府 have presided over 主导 selling green spaces, squeezing money from local authorities and declining attention on sport in education. Instead of wordy, worthy strategies 冗长的、有价值的策略, future governments need to do more to provide the conditions for sport to thrive 繁荣. Or at least not make them worse.

##### Text1（21-25）

#### 长难句1

> The Parkrun phenomenon began with a dozen friends and has inspired 400 events in the UK and more abroad.

##### 句子拆分

主干：**The Parkrun phenomenon began with a dozen friends and has inspired 400 events**
- `began with a dozen friends`和`has inspired 400 events`是and连接的两个并列谓语
- `in the UK and more abroad`：地点状语

##### 生词

phenomenon：现象；begin with：始于；a dozen：一打、12个；inspire：激发、催生；event：活动；abroad：在国外

##### 翻译

Parkrun现象始于十几个朋友，已经在英国催生了400场活动，在国外更多。

##### 考点

**and连接两个并列谓语**；21题C选项的依据——从十几个朋友发展到400场活动+国外更多，说明Parkrun获得了极大欢迎（gained great popularity）。

#### 长难句2

> Runners range from four years old to grandparents; their times range from Andrew Baddeley's world record 13 minutes 48 seconds up to an hour.

##### 句子拆分

并列句，分号连接
分句1主干：**Runners range from four years old to grandparents**
分句2主干：**their times range from Andrew Baddeley's world record 13 minutes 48 seconds up to an hour**

##### 生词

range from … to …：从…到…不等；world record：世界纪录；up to：多达

##### 翻译

跑步者从4岁到祖父母辈都有；他们的成绩从安德鲁·巴德利的世界纪录13分48秒到一小时不等。

##### 考点

**分号连接并列句+range from…to…结构**；进一步说明Parkrun的受欢迎程度——参与人群广泛，成绩差异大。

#### 长难句3

> Parkrun is succeeding where London's Olympic "legacy" is failing.

##### 句子拆分

主干：**Parkrun is succeeding**
- `where London's Olympic "legacy" is failing`：where地点状语从句，"在…的地方"

##### 生词

succeed：成功；legacy：遗产；fail：失败

##### 翻译

Parkrun在伦敦奥运"遗产"失败的地方取得了成功。

##### 考点

**where地点状语从句**；全文核心对比句——Parkrun成功 vs 奥运遗产失败，22题C选项的依据（奥运遗产未能促进体育参与）。

#### 长难句4

> Planning documents pledged that the great legacy of the Games would be to lever a nation of sport lovers away from their couches.

##### 句子拆分

主干：**Planning documents pledged that …**
- that引导宾语从句，从句主干：**the great legacy of the Games would be to lever a nation of sport lovers away from their couches**
  - `to lever a nation of sport lovers away from their couches`：不定式作表语

##### 生词

planning document：规划文件；pledge：承诺；legacy：遗产；lever：撬动、杠杆作用；a nation of sport lovers：一个体育爱好者之国；couch：沙发

##### 翻译

规划文件承诺，奥运会的伟大遗产将是撬动一个体育爱好者之国离开他们的沙发。

##### 考点

**that宾语从句+不定式作表语**；奥运遗产的目标——让国民离开沙发去运动，但实际失败了。

#### 长难句5

> The number of adults doing weekly sport did rise, by nearly 2 million in the run-up to 2012—but the general population was growing faster.

##### 句子拆分

并列句，but连接
分句1主干：**The number of adults doing weekly sport did rise**
- `doing weekly sport`：现在分词作后置定语，修饰adults
- `did rise`：did强调谓语
- `by nearly 2 million in the run-up to 2012`：状语
分句2主干：**the general population was growing faster**

##### 生词

weekly sport：每周运动；did rise：确实上升了（强调）；run-up to：…之前的准备阶段；general population：总人口；grow faster：增长更快

##### 翻译

每周运动的成年人数量确实上升了，在2012年之前增加了近200万——但总人口增长更快。

##### 考点

**现在分词后置定语+did强调谓语+but转折并列句**；说明奥运遗产的失败——运动人数虽然增加，但总人口增长更快，且后来运动人数在下降。

#### 长难句6

> There is as much joy over a puffed-out first-timer being clapped over the line as there is about top talent shining.

##### 句子拆分

主干：**There is as much joy over a puffed-out first-timer as there is about top talent**
- `as much … as …`：同级比较
- `being clapped over the line`：现在分词被动式作后置定语，修饰first-timer
- `shining`：现在分词作后置定语，修饰top talent

##### 生词

joy：喜悦；puffed-out：气喘吁吁的；first-timer：第一次参加的人；clap：鼓掌；over the line：越过终点线；top talent：顶尖人才；shine：闪耀、表现出色

##### 翻译

一个气喘吁吁的第一次参加者在掌声中越过终点线，所带来的喜悦与顶尖人才闪耀所带来的喜悦一样多。

##### 考点

**as much…as…同级比较+现在分词被动式后置定语+现在分词后置定语**；23题A选项的依据——Parkrun欢迎任何人，不强调精英主义（does not emphasize elitism）。

#### 长难句7

> The dual aim was mixed up: The stress on success over taking part was intimidating for newcomers.

##### 句子拆分

主干：**The dual aim was mixed up**
- 冒号后解释：`The stress on success over taking part was intimidating for newcomers`
  - `on success over taking part`：介词短语修饰stress

##### 生词

dual aim：双重目标；mix up：混淆、弄混；stress on：对…的强调；success over taking part：成功重于参与；intimidating：令人生畏的；newcomer：新来者、新手

##### 翻译

双重目标被混淆了：对成功重于参与的强调令新手望而生畏。

##### 考点

**冒号解释+stress on结构**；奥运遗产失败的原因——双重目标混淆，强调成功而非参与，吓退了新手。

#### 长难句8

> If there is a role for government, it should really be getting involved in providing common goods—making sure there is space for playing fields and the money to pave tennis and netball courts, and encouraging the provision of all these activities in schools.

##### 句子拆分

主干：**it should really be getting involved in providing common goods**
- `If there is a role for government`：if条件状语从句
- 破折号后解释common goods：`making sure there is space for playing fields and the money to pave tennis and netball courts, and encouraging the provision of all these activities in schools`
  - `making sure …`和`encouraging …`是and连接的两个现在分词并列
  - `there is space for playing fields and the money to pave tennis and netball courts`：省略that的宾语从句，作making sure的宾语

##### 生词

role：角色；get involved in：参与；provide common goods：提供公共物品；make sure：确保；playing field：运动场；pave：铺设；tennis court：网球场；netball court：无挡板篮球场；encourage：鼓励；provision：提供

##### 翻译

如果政府有角色的话，它真的应该参与提供公共物品——确保有运动场的空间和铺设网球场、无挡板篮球场的资金，并鼓励在学校提供所有这些活动。

##### 考点

**if条件状语从句+破折号解释+两个现在分词并列+省略that的宾语从句**；24题B选项的依据——政府应该投资公共体育设施（invest in public sports facilities = provide common goods: playing fields, tennis and netball courts）。

successive governments：历届政府；preside over：主导、负责；green space：绿地；squeeze money from：从…榨取资金；local authority：地方当局；declining attention：日益减少的关注；sport in education：教育中的体育

##### 句子拆分

#### 21. According to Paragraph 1, Parkrun has ______.

[A] created many jobs [B] become an official festival [C] gained great popularity [D] strengthened community ties

✅答案：**C** 定位：第一段。`The Parkrun phenomenon began with a dozen friends and has inspired 400 events in the UK and more abroad`（Parkrun现象始于十几个朋友，已经在英国催生了400场活动，在国外更多）+ `Runners range from four years old to grandparents`（跑步者从4岁到祖父母辈都有）。说明Parkrun获得了极大欢迎。

- A：错误。created many jobs（创造了许多就业）无中生有。原文说活动免费、由志愿者配备人员，没有说创造就业。
- B：错误。become an official festival（成为官方节日）无中生有。原文说是phenomenon（现象），不是官方节日。
- C：正确。获得了极大欢迎。从十几个朋友发展到400场活动+国外更多，参与人群广泛=gained great popularity。
- D：错误。strengthened community ties（加强了社区联系）过度推断。原文说在当地公园跑步，但没有明确说加强社区联系。

With so much focus on Children's use of screen 屏幕, it's easy for parents to forget about their own screen use. "Tech is designed to really suck you in 吸引你," says Jenny Radesky in her study of digital play 数字游戏, "and digital products are there to promote maximal engagement 最大程度的参与. It makes it hard to disengage 脱离, and leads to a lot of bleed-over 渗透 into the family routine 日常生活."

Radesky has studied the use of mobile phones and tablets 平板电脑 at mealtimes by giving mother-child pairs a food-testing exercise 食物测试活动. She found that mothers who used devices 设备 during the exercise started 20 percent fewer verbal 言语的 and 39 percent fewer nonverbal interactions 互动 with their children. During a separate observation of 55 caregivers 看护人 eating with one child or more, she saw that phones became a source of tension 紧张 in the family. Parents would be looking at their emails while the children would be making excited bids 努力争取 for their attention.

Infants 婴儿 are wired 天生的 to look at parents' faces to try to understand their world, and if those faces are blank 空白的 and unresponsive 无反应的—as they often are when absorbed in a device—it can be extremely disconcerting 令人不安的 for the children. Radesky cites 引用 the "still face experiment 静止面孔实验", which was devised 设计 by developmental psychologist 发展心理学家 Ed Tronick in the 1970s. In it, a mother is asked to interact with her child in a normal way before putting on a blank expression and not giving them any visual social feedback 视觉社交反馈. As the video shows, the child becomes increasingly distressed 痛苦的 as she tries to capture her mother's attention. "Parents don't have to be exquisitely 精致地 present at all times, but there needs to be a balance and parents need to be responsive and sensitive 敏感的 to a child's verbal or nonverbal expressions of an emotional need," says Radesky.

On the other hand, Tronick himself is concerned that the worries about kids' use of screens are born out of an "oppressive ideology 压迫性意识形态 that demands that parents should always be interacting" with their children. "It's based on a somewhat fantasised 幻想的, very white, very upper-middle-class ideology that says if you're failing to expose your child to 30,000 words you are neglecting 忽视 them." Tronick believes that just because a child isn't learning from the screen doesn't mean there's no value to it—particularly if it gives parents time to have a shower, do housework or simply have a break from their child. Parents, he says, can get a lot out of using their devices to speak to friends or get some work out of the way 完成工作. This can make them feel happier, which they can be more available 有空的 to their child the rest of the time.

- C：正确。其他因素不应被忽视。shouldn't come at the expense of the rest of the equation = other factors should not be overlooked。
- D：错误。a shift in the view of fire has taken place（对火灾的看法已经发生转变）与原文相反。原文说Such a pivot would require a corresponding shift in the way US society today views fire（这种转变需要美国社会对火灾看法的相应转变），说明还没有转变。

#### 39. The overly simplified view Moritz mentions is a result of failing to ______.

[A] discover the fundamental makeup of nature [B] explore the mechanism of the human systems [C] maximize the role of landscape in human life [D] understand the interrelations of man and nature

✅答案：**D** 定位：第八段。`The human systems and the landscapes we live on are linked, and the interactions go both ways. Failing to recognize that, he notes, leads to "an overly simplified view"`（人类系统和我们居住的地貌是相连的，互动是双向的。未能认识到这一点会导致过于简化的看法）。未能理解人与自然的相互关系导致过于简化的看法。

- A：错误。discover the fundamental makeup of nature（发现自然的基本构成）无中生有。
- B：错误。explore the mechanism of the human systems（探索人类系统的机制）以偏概全。原文说human systems and landscapes are linked（人类系统和地貌相连），不只是人类系统。
- C：错误。maximize the role of landscape in human life（最大化地貌在人类生活中的作用）无中生有。
- D：正确。理解人与自然的相互关系。human systems and landscapes are linked, interactions go both ways = interrelations of man and nature。

#### 40. Professor Balch points out that fire is something man should ______.

[A] do away with [B] come to terms with [C] pay a price for [D] keep away from

✅答案：**B** 定位：第九、十段。`acknowledging fire's inevitable presence in human life is an attitude crucial to developing the laws, policies, and practices that make it as safe as possible`（承认火灾在人类生活中不可避免的存在，是制定使火灾尽可能安全的法律、政策和实践的关键态度）+ `We've disconnected ourselves from living with fire`（我们已经脱离了与火共存）。说明人类应该接受火灾的存在，与火共存。come to terms with = 接受、适应。

- A：错误。do away with（废除、消灭）与原文相反。原文说承认火灾不可避免的存在，不是消灭。
- B：正确。接受、适应。acknowledging fire's inevitable presence + living with fire = come to terms with fire。
- C：错误。pay a price for（为…付出代价）无中生有。
- D：错误。keep away from（远离）与原文相反。原文说living with fire（与火共存），不是远离。

##### 本篇命题规律小结（英二阅读）

1. 环保/政策类文章：关注**问题提出（野火成国家关注）→具体表现（预算消耗）→问题2（资金投入火灾区）→解决方案（重新分配资金）→观念转变（不只看气候，理解人与自然关系）→接受火灾存在**的结构
2. 原因题：定位到**数字和首次**（for the first time spent more than half）
3. 词义/比喻题：结合**上下文**（magnifying glass = 仔细检查支出是否合理）
4. 人物观点题：定位到**while让步句**（climate is key, but shouldn't come at the expense of the rest）
5. 常见干扰：与原文相反（avoid redirection → support redirection, keep away from → live with）、偷换概念（lower-hazard → wildfire-free, human systems → human systems and landscapes）

##### 新题型 Part B（41-45）

#### 题型说明

多项对应（信息匹配）。左栏给出 5 个人物（41-45），右栏给出 7 条陈述（A-G），要求为每个人物选出与其说法对应的陈述，多出 2 个干扰项。每题 2 分，共 10 分。文章主题：美国制造业工人短缺。

#### 备选陈述（与题面一致）

[A] says that he switched to electrical engineering because he loves working with tools.
[B] points out that there are enough people to fill the jobs that don't need much skill.
[C] points out that the US doesn't manufacture anything anymore.
[D] believes that it is important to keep a close eye on the age of his workers.
[E] says that for factory owners, workers are harder to find because of stiff competition.
[F] points out that a work/life balance can attract young people into manufacturing.
[G] says that the manufacturing recession is to blame for the lay-off of the young people's parents.

#### 题目（41-45）

41. Jay Dunwell
42. Jason Stenquist
43. Birgit Klohs
44. Rob Spohr
45. Julie Parks

---
##### 参考答案
41.E 42.A 43.G 44.B 45.F

#### 解析

**题型特点：** 信息匹配题。左栏为人名（专有名词），右栏为陈述。解题核心是按人名回原文定位，找原词复现或同义替换。

**解题策略：**
1. 先读右栏 7 条陈述，划出核心词（electrical engineering / enough people / doesn't manufacture / age of workers / stiff competition / work-life balance / manufacturing recession）
2. 按左栏人名回原文定位段落
3. 对比原文与右栏陈述，找同义替换
4. 用排除法排除干扰项

**逐题解析：**

- **41.E（Jay Dunwell）**：定位至第五段。原文"For factory owners, it all adds up to stiff competition for workers—and upward pressure on wages. 'They're harder to find and they have job offers,' says Jay Dunwell"（对工厂主而言，工人竞争激烈、更难招到），与 E 选项"for factory owners, workers are harder to find because of stiff competition"同义复现（stiff competition / harder to find）。

- **42.A（Jason Stenquist）**：定位至第七段。原文"he says at high school he considered medical school before switching to electrical engineering. 'I love working with tools. I love creating'"（他说高中时考虑过医学院，后来转攻电子工程，因为他喜欢用工具工作），与 A 选项"switched to electrical engineering because he loves working with tools"完全对应。

- **43.G（Birgit Klohs）**：定位至第八段。原文"Millennials 'remember their father and mother both were laid off. They blame it on the manufacturing recession,' says Birgit Klohs"（千禧一代记得父母都被裁员，他们归咎于制造业衰退），与 G 选项"the manufacturing recession is to blame for the lay-off of the young people's parents"同义改写（father and mother → young people's parents）。

- **44.B（Rob Spohr）**：定位至倒数第二段。原文"There're enough people to fill the jobs at McDonalds and other places where you don't need to have much skill"（麦当劳等低技能岗位有足够的人），与 B 选项"there are enough people to fill the jobs that don't need much skill"原词复现。

- **45.F（Julie Parks）**：定位至末段。原文"Julie Parks of Grand Rapids Community points to another key to luring Millennials into manufacturing: a work/life balance"（Julie Parks 指出吸引千禧一代进入制造业的另一个关键是工作与生活的平衡），与 F 选项"a work/life balance can attract young people into manufacturing"对应。

**干扰项排除：**
- [C] points out that the US doesn't manufacture anything anymore：这是开篇特朗普的言论，并非左栏五人之一的观点
- [D] believes that it is important to keep a close eye on the age of his workers：这是 Robert Roth 的做法（"Robert Roth keeps a close eye on the age of his nearly 200 workers"），但 Robert Roth 不在左栏 41-45 人名中

## 详细解析

##### 翻译（15 分）

#### 原文

We tend to think that friends and family members are our biggest sources of connection, laughter and warmth. While that may well be true, researchers have also recently found that interacting with strangers actually brings a boost in mood and feelings of belonging that we didn't expect.

In one series of studies, researchers instructed Chicago-area commuters using public transportation to strike up a conversation with someone near them. On average, participants who followed this instruction felt better than those who had been told to stand or sit in silence. The researchers also argued that when we shy away from casual interactions with strangers, it is often due to a misplaced anxiety that they might not want to talk to us. Much of the time, however, this belief is false. As it turns out, many people are actually perfectly willing to talk—and may even be flattered to receive your attention.

#### 参考译文

我们往往认为，朋友和家人是我们人际联系、快乐和温暖的最大源泉。尽管这一点也许十分正确，但研究人员最近也发现，与陌生人交流实际上能带来意想不到的情绪提升和归属感。

在一项系列研究中，研究人员让芝加哥地区乘坐公共交通工具的通勤者与旁边的人搭话。平均而言，遵循这一指示的参与者比那些被告知静默站着或坐着的人感觉更好。研究人员还表示，当我们羞于与陌生人进行随意的互动时，往往是因为一种不合时宜的焦虑——认为他们可能不想和我们说话。然而，大多数时候，这种看法是错误的。事实证明，很多人实际上非常乐意交谈——甚至可能因为得到你的关注而感到荣幸。

#### 重点词汇与句型

- **tend to do**：倾向于做某事，往往
- **interact with**：与……交流/互动
- **a boost in mood**：情绪的提升/改善
- **feelings of belonging**：归属感
- **commuter**：通勤者，上下班乘车的人
- **strike up a conversation**：开始交谈，搭话
- **shy away from**：躲避，回避，羞于
- **casual interactions**：随意的互动/交流
- **misplaced anxiety**：不合时宜的/ misplaced错位的焦虑
- **flattered**：感到荣幸的，受宠若惊的

#### 长难句分析

**1. While that may well be true, researchers have also recently found that interacting with strangers actually brings a boost in mood and feelings of belonging that we didn't expect.**

- **结构**：while引导让步状语从句 + 主句 + that引导的宾语从句（嵌套that引导的定语从句）
- **拆分**：While that may well be true（让步状语从句）/ researchers have also recently found（主句）/ that interacting with strangers actually brings a boost in mood and feelings of belonging（宾语从句）/ that we didn't expect（定语从句，修饰a boost in mood and feelings of belonging）
- **翻译要点**：while=尽管；定语从句较短可前置译为"意想不到的"
- **译文**：尽管这一点也许十分正确，但研究人员最近也发现，与陌生人交流实际上能带来意想不到的情绪提升和归属感。

**2. The researchers also argued that when we shy away from casual interactions with strangers, it is often due to a misplaced anxiety that they might not want to talk to us.**

- **结构**：主句 + that引导的宾语从句（嵌套when引导的时间状语从句 + that引导的同位语从句）
- **拆分**：The researchers also argued（主句）/ that when we shy away from casual interactions with strangers（宾语从句中的时间状语从句）/ it is often due to a misplaced anxiety（宾语从句主句）/ that they might not want to talk to us（同位语从句，补充说明anxiety）
- **翻译要点**：due to=因为；同位语从句单独处理成一句"认为他们可能不想和我们说话"
- **译文**：研究人员还表示，当我们羞于与陌生人进行随意的互动时，往往是因为一种不合时宜的焦虑——认为他们可能不想和我们说话。

**3. As it turns out, many people are actually perfectly willing to talk—and may even be flattered to receive your attention.**

- **结构**：as引导的非限制性定语从句 + 主句（and连接两个并列谓语）
- **拆分**：As it turns out（非限制性定语从句，as=正如）/ many people are actually perfectly willing to talk（主句第一部分）/ and may even be flattered to receive your attention（主句第二部分）
- **翻译要点**：as it turns out=事实证明；perfectly=非常；flattered=感到荣幸的
- **译文**：事实证明，很多人实际上非常乐意交谈——甚至可能因为得到你的关注而感到荣幸。

##### 小作文（10 分）

#### 题目

**Directions:**

Suppose you are organizing an online meeting. Write an email to Jack, an international student, to

1) invite him to participate, and
2) tell him the details.

You should write neatly on the ANSWER SHEET. (10 points)

You should write about 100 words on the ANSWER SHEET. Do not sign your own name at the end of the email. Use "Li Ming" instead.

**场景说明**：假设你是李明，正在组织一场线上会议，给国际学生Jack写一封电子邮件，邀请他参加并告知具体安排。属于"邀请信"。

#### 高分范文

Dear Jack,

I am writing to invite you to participate in an online meeting organized by our Student Union, which will be held via Zoom at 7:00 p.m. next Friday.

The meeting will focus on the topic of "Cross-Cultural Communication in Campus Life." The event will include a special keynote address and a live Q&A session delivered by Professor Smith, who, for the first time, will answer questions directly from all the students and provide an insight into his first year at the university. Your perspective as an international student would be highly valued.

It is my sincere hope that you will not miss out on this valuable opportunity. For any further questions, please feel free to email the Student Union. We look forward to your participation.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

#### 范文翻译

亲爱的Jack：

我写信邀请你参加由学生会组织的一场线上会议，会议将于下周五晚7点通过Zoom平台举行。

本次会议的主题是"校园生活中的跨文化交流"。活动将包括一场特别的主题演讲和现场问答环节，由Smith教授主讲。他将首次直接回答所有学生的问题，并分享他在大学第一年的见解。作为国际学生，你的观点将受到高度重视。

我真诚地希望你不要错过这个宝贵的机会。如有任何进一步的问题，请随时给学生会发邮件。期待你的参与。

此致敬礼
李明

#### 重点句型与表达

1. **I am writing to invite you to participate in...** —— 邀请信开头万能句型，交代写信目的
2. **...which will be held via... at...** —— 非限制性定语从句，交代会议平台和时间
3. **The meeting will focus on the topic of...** —— "会议将聚焦……主题"，用于介绍会议内容
4. **The event will include a special keynote address and a live Q&A session** —— "活动将包括特别主题演讲和现场问答环节"
5. **Your perspective as... would be highly valued.** —— "你作为……的观点将受到高度重视"，体现邀请诚意
6. **It is my sincere hope that you will not miss out on...** —— "我真诚地希望你不要错过……"，正式邀请结尾
7. **For any further questions, please feel free to email...** —— "如有问题请随时发邮件"，书信万能结尾
8. **We look forward to your participation.** —— "期待你的参与"，邀请信标准结尾

##### 大作文（15 分）

#### 图表描述

**图表类型**：柱状图（Bar Chart）

**图表内容**：某市居民体育锻炼方式调查
- 散步（walking）：55%（占比最大）
- 跑步（running）：30%
- 健身房（gym）：8%
- 球类运动（ball sports）：7%

#### 题目

**Directions:**

Write an essay based on the following chart. In your writing, you should
1) interpret the chart, and
2) give your comments.

You should write about 150 words on the ANSWER SHEET. (15 points)

#### 高分范文

The bar chart above clearly illustrates the ways residents of a certain city choose to take physical exercise. As is shown, walking takes the lead, accounting for 55%, followed by running at 30%, while going to the gym and playing ball sports account for a much smaller proportion, at 8% and 7% respectively.

What are the reasons behind this phenomenon? Several factors contribute to this trend, but generally speaking, they may boil down to the following aspects. Initially, walking and running are the most accessible and cost-effective forms of exercise, requiring no special equipment or membership fees. People can simply step out of their homes and start exercising. Furthermore, with the accelerating pace of modern life, many residents prefer flexible exercise methods that can be easily incorporated into their daily routines. By contrast, going to the gym demands not only financial commitment but also dedicated time slots, which discourages many potential participants.

In conclusion, while it is encouraging that the majority of residents engage in regular physical exercise, sports facilities and community fitness programs should be further improved to encourage more diverse forms of exercise and promote overall public health.

#### 范文翻译

上方的柱状图清晰地展示了某市居民选择体育锻炼的方式。如图所示，散步居首，占比55%，其次是跑步占30%，而去健身房和参加球类运动的比例小得多，分别为8%和7%。

这一现象背后的原因是什么？有几个因素促成了这一趋势，但总的来说，可以归结为以下几个方面。首先，散步和跑步是最容易获得且最具成本效益的锻炼方式，不需要特殊设备或会员费。人们只需走出家门就可以开始锻炼。此外，随着现代生活节奏的加快，许多居民更喜欢能够轻松融入日常生活的灵活锻炼方式。相比之下，去健身房不仅需要经济投入，还需要固定的时间段，这让许多潜在的参与者望而却步。

总之，虽然大多数居民定期参加体育锻炼令人欣慰，但应进一步完善体育设施和社区健身项目，以鼓励更多样化的锻炼形式，促进整体公共健康。

#### 重点句型与表达

1. **The bar chart above clearly illustrates...** —— 柱状图开头万能句型，引出图表主题
2. **As is shown, ... takes the lead, accounting for X%, followed by... at Y%** —— 描述柱状图数据的经典句式（最大项+排序）
3. **...account for a much smaller proportion, at X% and Y% respectively** —— "占比小得多，分别为X%和Y%"
4. **What are the reasons behind this phenomenon?** —— 过渡句，引出原因分析
5. **Several factors contribute to this trend, but generally speaking, they may boil down to...** —— 分析原因的万能过渡句
6. **...are the most accessible and cost-effective forms of exercise** —— "是最容易获得且最具成本效益的锻炼方式"
7. **By contrast, ... demands not only... but also...** —— "相比之下，……不仅需要……还需要……"，用于对比分析
8. **In conclusion, while it is encouraging that..., ...should be further improved to...** —— 结尾段万能句式（肯定+建议）

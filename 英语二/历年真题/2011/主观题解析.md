## 详细解析

##### 翻译（15 分）

#### 原文

Who would have thought that, globally, the IT industry produces about the same volume of greenhouse gases as the world's airlines do — roughly 2 percent of all CO2 emissions?

Many everyday tasks take a surprising toll on the environment. A Google search can leak between 0.2 and 7.0 grams of CO2, depending on how many attempts are needed to get the "right" answer. To deliver results to its users quickly, then, Google has to maintain vast data centres around the world, packed with powerful computers. While producing large quantities of CO2, these computers emit a great deal of heat, so the centres need to be well air-conditioned, which uses even more energy.

However, Google and other big tech providers monitor their efficiency closely and make improvements. Monitoring is the first step on the road to reduction, but there is much more to be done, and not just by big companies.

#### 参考译文

谁曾料想，全球IT行业产生的温室气体竟与全球航空公司产生的一样多——约占二氧化碳排放总量的2%？

很多日常工作对环境的损害是惊人的。每在谷歌上搜索一次便会产生0.2到7.0克的二氧化碳，具体取决于获得"正确"答案所需的尝试次数。为了快速将搜索结果传递给用户，谷歌不得不在世界各地维护大量数据中心，这些中心配备了功能强大的计算机。这些计算机在产生大量二氧化碳的同时，还会释放大量热量，因此数据中心需要良好的空调系统，这又会消耗更多的能源。

然而，谷歌和其他大型技术提供商密切监控其效率并不断改进。监控只是减排之路上的第一步，还有很多工作要做，而且不仅仅是大型公司的任务。

#### 重点词汇与句型

- **greenhouse gases**：温室气体
- **volume**：量、体积（此处指排放量）
- **take a toll on**：对……造成损害/付出代价（固定搭配）
- **leak**：泄漏、释放（此处指排放CO2）
- **depending on**：取决于（现在分词短语作状语）
- **data centres**：数据中心
- **packed with**：配备有、装有（过去分词短语作后置定语）
- **emit**：释放、排放
- **air-conditioned**：装有空调的
- **monitor**：监控、监测（此处作动词）

#### 长难句分析

##### 长难句1

> Who would have thought that, globally, the IT industry produces about the same volume of greenhouse gases as the world's airlines do — roughly 2 percent of all CO2 emissions?

**句子拆分**：
- 主干：**Who would have thought that...**
- `that, globally, the IT industry produces about the same volume of greenhouse gases as the world's airlines do`：宾语从句
  - `globally`：插入语，"从全球范围来看"
  - `the same...as...`：比较结构，"和……一样多"
  - `do`：助动词，替代produces
- `roughly 2 percent of all CO2 emissions`：破折号后的补充说明，解释温室气体的比例

**翻译**：谁曾料想，全球IT行业产生的温室气体竟与全球航空公司产生的一样多——约占二氧化碳排放总量的2%？

##### 长难句2

> To deliver results to its users quickly, then, Google has to maintain vast data centres around the world, packed with powerful computers.

**句子拆分**：
- 主干：**Google has to maintain vast data centres**
- `To deliver results to its users quickly`：不定式短语作目的状语，"为了快速向用户传递结果"
- `then`：插入语，表逻辑衔接
- `around the world`：地点状语
- `packed with powerful computers`：过去分词短语作后置定语，修饰data centres

**翻译**：为了快速将搜索结果传递给用户，谷歌不得不在世界各地维护大量数据中心，这些中心配备了功能强大的计算机。

##### 长难句3

> While producing large quantities of CO2, these computers emit a great deal of heat, so the centres need to be well air-conditioned, which uses even more energy.

**句子拆分**：
- 主干：**these computers emit a great deal of heat**
- `While producing large quantities of CO2`：时间状语从句（省略主语these computers are），"在产生大量CO2的同时"
- `so the centres need to be well air-conditioned`：so引导的并列分句，表结果
- `which uses even more energy`：which引导的非限制性定语从句，指代前面整个分句（需要空调系统这一事实）

**翻译**：这些计算机在产生大量二氧化碳的同时，还会释放大量热量，因此数据中心需要良好的空调系统，这又会消耗更多的能源。

##### 小作文（10 分）

#### 题目

**47. Directions:**

Suppose your cousin Li Ming has just been admitted to a university. Write him/her a letter to

1) congratulate him/her, and

2) give him/her suggestions on how to get prepared for university life.

You should write about 100 words on ANSWER SHEET 2.

Do not sign your own name at the end of the letter. Use "Zhang Wei" instead.

Do not write your address. (10 points)

#### 高分范文

Dear Li Ming,

Congratulations on your admission to the university! I was absolutely thrilled to learn the good news. I know how hard you have worked, and your diligence and perseverance have finally paid off.

As you prepare for university life, I would like to offer a few suggestions. First, I recommend that you make the most of the library resources, reading extensively beyond your coursework. Second, try to participate in student clubs and societies, which will help you make new friends and develop your interpersonal skills. Last but not least, learn to manage your time wisely, as university life offers much more freedom but also requires greater self-discipline.

I am confident that you will thrive in this new chapter of your life. Wish you all the best!

Yours sincerely,

Zhang Wei

#### 范文翻译

亲爱的李明：

祝贺你被大学录取！得知这个好消息我非常激动。我知道你一直以来多么努力，你的勤奋和毅力终于得到了回报。

在你为大学生活做准备之际，我想提几点建议。首先，我建议你充分利用图书馆资源，在课业之外广泛阅读。其次，尽量参加学生社团，这有助于你结交新朋友并提升人际交往能力。最后，学会明智地管理你的时间，因为大学生活给了你更多自由，但也需要更强的自律。

我相信你会在人生新篇章中茁壮成长。祝一切顺利！

你真诚的，

张伟

#### 重点句型与表达

1. **Congratulations on your admission to the university!**（祝贺你被大学录取！）——祝贺信开头句
2. **I was absolutely thrilled to learn the good news.**（得知这个好消息我非常激动。）——表达喜悦
3. **Your diligence and perseverance have finally paid off.**（你的勤奋和毅力终于得到了回报。）——肯定对方努力，pay off为固定搭配
4. **As you prepare for..., I would like to offer a few suggestions.**（在你为……做准备之际，我想提几点建议。）——过渡到建议部分
5. **First, I recommend that you make the most of...**（首先，我建议你充分利用……）——建议句型1
6. **Last but not least, learn to manage your time wisely.**（最后，学会明智地管理你的时间。）——建议句型2
7. **I am confident that you will thrive in this new chapter of your life.**（我相信你会在人生新篇章中茁壮成长。）——结尾鼓励句

##### 大作文（15 分）

#### 图表描述

本题为**柱状图（Bar Chart）**，展示2008年和2009年中国国内轿车市场**三类品牌**的市场份额变化。

主要数据：
- **国产品牌（Chinese brands）**：2008年约25%，2009年上升至约33%
- **日系品牌（Japanese brands）**：2008年约35%（市场份额最高），2009年下降至约25%
- **美系品牌（American brands）**：2008年约10%，2009年上升至约15%

核心趋势：日系品牌市场份额大幅下降，国产品牌和美系品牌市场份额均有所上升，其中国产品牌增幅最大。

#### 题目

**48. Directions:**

Write an essay based on the following chart. In your writing, you should

1) interpret the chart and

2) give your comments.

You should write at least 150 words.

Write your essay on ANSWER SHEET 2. (15 points)

#### 高分范文

The bar chart clearly illustrates the changes in the market shares of three types of car brands in China's domestic sedan market between 2008 and 2009. As is shown, the share of domestic brands climbed from approximately 25% to over 33%, while Japanese brands suffered a sharp decline from roughly 35% to about 25%. Meanwhile, American brands experienced a moderate increase from around 10% to nearly 15%.

Several factors may account for these changes. First and foremost, the rise of domestic brands can be attributed to the continuous improvement in quality and design, as well as their competitive pricing strategy. Chinese automakers have invested heavily in research and development, enabling them to produce vehicles that meet the growing demands of Chinese consumers. Secondly, the decline of Japanese brands was largely caused by various factors, including intensified market competition and the impact of certain international events that dampened consumer confidence. As for the growth of American brands, it benefited from expanded product lines and enhanced localization efforts.

To sum up, the shifting landscape of China's automobile market reflects the growing competitiveness of domestic brands and the dynamic nature of consumer preferences. It is advisable for all manufacturers to focus on technological innovation and customer satisfaction to maintain their market positions.

#### 范文翻译

柱状图清楚地展示了中国国内轿车市场三类品牌在2008年至2009年间市场份额的变化。如图所示，国产品牌的份额从约25%攀升至超过33%，而日系品牌则从约35%急剧下降至约25%。与此同时，美系品牌经历了适度增长，从约10%上升至近15%。

以下几个因素可以解释这些变化。首先，国产品牌的崛起可归因于质量和设计的持续改进，以及其有竞争力的定价策略。中国汽车制造商在研发方面投入巨大，使其能够生产出满足中国消费者日益增长需求的汽车。其次，日系品牌的下滑主要由多种因素造成，包括市场竞争加剧以及某些国际事件对消费者信心的影响。至于美系品牌的增长，则受益于产品线的扩展和本土化努力的加强。

总之，中国汽车市场格局的变化反映了国产品牌日益增强的竞争力以及消费者偏好的动态特征。建议所有制造商关注技术创新和客户满意度，以维持其市场地位。

#### 重点句型与表达

1. **The bar chart clearly illustrates the changes in... between... and...**（柱状图清楚地展示了……在……和……间的变化）——图表描述开头句
2. **As is shown, the share of... climbed from... to..., while... suffered a sharp decline from... to...**（如图所示，……的份额从……攀升至……，而……则从……急剧下降至……）——对比描述趋势
3. **Several factors may account for these changes. First and foremost, ... can be attributed to...**（以下几个因素可以解释这些变化。首先，……可归因于……）——原因分析句
4. **Secondly, the decline of... was largely caused by...**（其次，……的下滑主要由……造成）——原因分析句2
5. **As for..., it benefited from...**（至于……，它受益于……）——原因分析句3
6. **To sum up, the shifting landscape of... reflects...**（总之，……格局的变化反映了……）——总结句
7. **It is advisable for... to focus on... to maintain...**（建议……关注……以维持……）——建议结尾句

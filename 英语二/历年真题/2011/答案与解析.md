#### 原文

The Internet affords anonymity（匿名） to its users, a blessing to privacy and freedom of speech. But that very anonymity is also behind the explosion of cyber-crime（网络犯罪） that has (1) across the Web.

Can privacy be preserved (2) bringing safety and security to a world that seems increasingly (3)?

Last month, Howard Schmidt, the nation's cyber-czar（网络沙皇）, offered the federal government a (4) to make the web a safer place—a "voluntary trusted identity"（自愿可信身份） system that would be the high-tech (5) of a physical key, a fingerprint and a photo ID card, all rolled (6) one. The system might use a smart identity card, or a digital credential（数字凭证） (7) to a specific computer, and would authenticate（认证） users at a range of online services.

The idea is to (8) a federation of private online identity systems. Users could (9) which system to join, and only registered users whose identities have been authenticated could navigate those systems. The approach contrasts with one that would require an Internet driver's license (10) by the government.

Google and Microsoft are among companies that already have these "single sign-on"（单点登录） systems that make it possible for users to (11) just once but use many different services.

(12), the approach would create a "walled garden"（围墙花园） in cyberspace（网络空间）, with safe "neighborhoods" and bright "streetlights" to establish a sense of a (13) community.

Mr. Schmidt described it as a "voluntary ecosystem"（生态系统） in which "individuals and organizations can complete online transactions with (14), trusting the identities of each other and the identities of the infrastructure（基础设施） (15) which the transaction runs."

Still, the administration's plan has (16) privacy rights activists（隐私权益活动人士）. Some applaud the approach; others are concerned. It seems clear that such a scheme is an initiative push toward what would (17) be a compulsory Internet "driver's license" mentality.

The plan has also been greeted with (18) by some computer security experts, who worry that the "voluntary ecosystem" envisioned by Mr. Schmidt would still leave much of the Internet (19). They argue that all Internet users should be (20) to register and identify themselves, in the same way that drivers must be licensed to drive on public roads.

#### 重点解析

- **第1题（A）**：动词词义辨析。sweep意为"席卷、横扫"，cyber-crime has swept across the Web即"网络犯罪已席卷网络"。skip（跳过）、walk（走）、ride（骑）均不与across the Web搭配表示犯罪蔓延。
- **第2题（C）**：连词辨析。while表示"与此同时、在……的同时"，此处意为"在为世界带来安全保障的同时，隐私能否得到保护？"for（因为）、within（在……之内）、though（尽管）逻辑不符。
- **第3题（B）**：形容词词义辨析。lawless意为"无法无天的、混乱的"，increasingly lawless即"越来越无法无天的网络世界"，与网络犯罪主题呼应。careless（粗心的）、pointless（无意义的）、helpless（无助的）不符。
- **第4题（D）**：名词词义辨析。proposal意为"提议、建议"，offer a proposal to make the web safer即"提出一项使网络更安全的提议"。reason（原因）、reminder（提醒）、compromise（妥协）不符。
- **第5题（D）**：名词词义辨析。equivalent意为"等价物、对应物"，the high-tech equivalent of a physical key即"相当于实体钥匙的高科技产物"。information（信息）、interference（干涉）、entertainment（娱乐）不符。
- **第6题（B）**：介词搭配。roll into one为固定搭配，意为"合为一体"。
- **第7题（A）**：动词词义辨析。be linked to意为"与……链接、绑定"，a digital credential linked to a specific computer即"绑定到特定电脑的数字凭证"。direct（指导）、chain（用链锁住）、compare（比较）不符。
- **第8题（C）**：动词词义辨析。create a federation of...意为"创建……联盟"，此处指建立私人在线身份系统联盟。dismiss（解散）、discover（发现）、improve（改进）不符。
- **第9题（C）**：动词词义辨析。select意为"选择"，Users could select which system to join即"用户可以选择加入哪个系统"。recall（回忆）、suggest（建议）、realize（意识到）不符。
- **第10题（B）**：动词词义辨析。issue a license为固定搭配，意为"颁发许可证"。release（发布）、distribute（分发）、deliver（递送）不如issue正式准确。
- **第11题（D）**：动词短语辨析。log in意为"登录"，single sign-on systems让用户只需登录一次即可使用多个服务。carry on（继续）、linger on（徘徊）、set in（开始）不符。
- **第12题（B）**：介词短语辨析。In effect意为"实际上、事实上"，此处总结这种方案实际上会在网络空间创建一个"围墙花园"。In vain（徒劳）、In return（作为回报）、In contrast（相反）不符。
- **第13题（A）**：形容词词义辨析。trusted community意为"受信任的社区"，与前文trusted identity、safe neighborhoods呼应。modernized（现代化的）、thriving（繁荣的）、competing（竞争的）不符。
- **第14题（C）**：名词词义辨析。with confidence意为"自信地、放心地"，complete online transactions with confidence即"放心地完成在线交易"。caution（谨慎）、delight（高兴）、patience（耐心）不如confidence贴合交易语境。
- **第15题（A）**：介词搭配。the infrastructure on which the transaction runs为定语从句，on与infrastructure搭配，表示"交易运行所依赖的基础设施"。
- **第16题（A）**：动词词义辨析。divide意为"使分裂、使产生分歧"，has divided privacy rights activists即"让隐私权益活动人士产生了分歧"。后文Some applaud...others are concerned...印证了divided。disappoint（使失望）、protect（保护）、unite（团结）方向相反。
- **第17题（D）**：副词词义辨析。eventually意为"最终、终于"，what would eventually be a compulsory Internet driver's license mentality即"最终将变成强制性网络驾照的思维模式"。frequently（频繁地）、incidentally（顺便说）、occasionally（偶尔）不符。
- **第18题（A）**：名词词义辨析。with skepticism意为"持怀疑态度"，后文experts who worry...印证了专家们持怀疑态度。tolerance（容忍）、indifference（冷漠）、enthusiasm（热情）与worry方向相反。
- **第19题（C）**：形容词词义辨析。vulnerable意为"脆弱的、易受攻击的"，leave much of the Internet vulnerable即"让大部分互联网仍然脆弱易受攻击"。manageable（可管理的）、defendable（可防御的）、invisible（不可见的）不符。
- **第20题（D）**：动词词义辨析。be forced to do意为"被迫做某事"，They argue that all Internet users should be forced to register即"他们认为所有互联网用户都应该被迫注册并身份证明自己"，与后文drivers must be licensed to drive on public roads呼应。invited（被邀请）、appointed（被任命）、allowed（被允许）语气不够强硬。

Ruth Simmons joined Goldman Sachs's board as an outside director 外部董事 in January 2000; a year later she became president of Brown University. For the rest of the decade she apparently 显然 managed both roles without attracting much criticism. But by the end of 2009 Ms. Simmons was under fire 受到抨击 for having sat on 担任 Goldman's compensation committee 薪酬委员会; how could she have let those enormous bonus payouts 奖金发放 pass unremarked 未被注意? By February the next year Ms. Simmons had left the board. The position was just taking up too much time, she said.

Outside directors are supposed to serve as helpful, yet less biased 不偏不倚的, advisers on a firm's board. Having made their wealth and their reputations elsewhere, they presumably 大概 have enough independence to disagree with the chief executive's proposals. If the sky, and the share price, is falling, outside directors should be able to give advice based on having weathered 平安度过 their own crises.

The researchers from Ohio University used a database that covered more than 10,000 firms and more than 64,000 different directors between 1989 and 2004. Then they simply checked which directors stayed from one proxy statement 股东委托书 to the next. The most likely reason for departing a board was age, so the researchers concentrated on those "surprise" disappearances by directors under the age of 70. They found that after a surprise departure, the probability that the company will subsequently 随后 have to restate earnings 重述财报 increased by nearly 20%. The likelihood of being named in a federal class-action lawsuit 联邦集体诉讼 also increases, and the stock is likely to perform worse. The effect tended to be larger for larger firms. Although a correlation 关联 between them leaving and subsequent bad performance at the firm is suggestive 引人联想的, it does not mean that such directors are always jumping off a sinking ship 跳离沉船. Often they "trade up 升级", leaving riskier, smaller firms for larger and more stable firms.

But the researchers believe that outside directors have an easier time of avoiding a blow to their reputations if they leave a firm before bad news breaks 爆发, even if a review of history shows they were on the board at the time any wrongdoing 不当行为 occurred. Firms who want to keep their outside directors through tough times may have to create incentives 激励. Otherwise outside directors will follow the example of Ms. Simmons, once again very popular on campus.

#### 长难句2

> But by the end of 2009 Ms. Simmons was under fire for having sat on Goldman's compensation committee; how could she have let those enormous bonus payouts pass unremarked?

##### 句子拆分

分句1主干：**Ms. Simmons was under fire**
- `by the end of 2009`：时间状语
- `for having sat on Goldman's compensation committee`：原因状语（for+动名词完成式）

分句2：反问句 **how could she have let those enormous bonus payouts pass unremarked?**
- `let sth pass unremarked`：让某事在无人注意的情况下通过

##### 生词

under fire：受到抨击；sit on：担任（委员会成员）；compensation committee：薪酬委员会；bonus payout：奖金发放；unremarked：未被注意的

##### 翻译

但到2009年底，西蒙斯女士因任职高盛薪酬委员会而饱受抨击：她怎么能让那些巨额奖金发放悄无声息地通过呢？

##### 考点

**for+动名词完成式**表原因；反问句表强烈批评语气；21题定位句，"受到批评"的原因就在for之后。

#### 长难句3

> Outside directors are supposed to serve as helpful, yet less biased, advisers on a firm's board.

##### 句子拆分

主干：**Outside directors are supposed to serve as advisers**
- `helpful, yet less biased`：两个形容词并列，yet表转折，修饰advisers
- `on a firm's board`：地点状语

##### 生词

be supposed to：应该；serve as：担任；biased：有偏见的

##### 翻译

外部董事应该在公司董事会中扮演乐于助人且较少偏见的顾问角色。

##### 考点

**be supposed to** 表"应该"，是英二阅读高频短语；形容词插入结构（helpful, yet less biased）；22题定位句。

#### 长难句4

> Having made their wealth and their reputations elsewhere, they presumably have enough independence to disagree with the chief executive's proposals.

##### 句子拆分

主干：**they have enough independence to disagree with the chief executive's proposals**
- `Having made their wealth and their reputations elsewhere`：现在分词完成式作原因状语（先积累了财富和声望，所以有独立性）

reputation：声誉；presumably：大概；independence：独立性；chief executive：首席执行官

#### 长难句5

> If the sky, and the share price, is falling, outside directors should be able to give advice based on having weathered their own crises.

##### 句子拆分

主干：**outside directors should be able to give advice**
- `If the sky, and the share price, is falling`：if条件状语从句（the sky是比喻，指公司境况）
- `based on having weathered their own crises`：过去分词短语作方式状语

##### 生词

weather：平安度过（动词）；crisis：危机

##### 翻译

#### 长难句6

#### 长难句7

> They found that after a surprise departure, the probability that the company will subsequently have to restate earnings increased by nearly 20%.

##### 句子拆分

主干：**They found that …**
- that引导宾语从句，宾语从句主干：**the probability increased by nearly 20%**
  - `after a surprise departure`：时间状语
  - `that the company will subsequently have to restate earnings`：定语从句，修饰probability

##### 生词

probability：可能性；subsequently：随后；restate earnings：重述财报（财务造假常见信号）

##### 翻译

他们发现，在外部董事意外离职后，公司随后不得不重述财报的概率增加了近20%。

##### 考点

**宾语从句嵌套定语从句**；the probability that…句型；increase by+幅度（增加了多少），23题相关定位句。

#### 长难句8

> Although a correlation between them leaving and subsequent bad performance at the firm is suggestive, it does not mean that such directors are always jumping off a sinking ship.

##### 句子拆分

主干：**it does not mean that …**
- `Although a correlation … is suggestive`：although让步状语从句
  - 从句主干：a correlation is suggestive
  - `between them leaving and subsequent bad performance at the firm`：介词短语修饰correlation
- that引导宾语从句：**such directors are always jumping off a sinking ship**

##### 生词

correlation：关联；suggestive：引人联想的；jump off a sinking ship：跳离沉船（比喻弃公司于危难）

##### 翻译

尽管他们的离职与公司随后的糟糕表现之间的关联引人联想，但这并不意味着这些董事总是在弃船逃生。

##### 考点

although让步状语从句；**比喻修辞**jumping off a sinking ship；作者态度句，先让步再转折，是25题态度题的关键。

#### 长难句9

> Often they "trade up," leaving riskier, smaller firms for larger and more stable firms.

##### 句子拆分

主干：**they "trade up"**
- `leaving riskier, smaller firms for larger and more stable firms`：现在分词作伴随状语，解释trade up的具体内容
- `leave A for B`：离开A去B

##### 生词

trade up：升级（换更好的）；risky：有风险的；stable：稳定的

##### 翻译

#### 长难句10

> But the researchers believe that outside directors have an easier time of avoiding a blow to their reputations if they leave a firm before bad news breaks, even if a review of history shows they were on the board at the time any wrongdoing occurred.

##### 句子拆分

主干：**the researchers believe that …**
- that引导宾语从句，从句主干：**outside directors have an easier time of avoiding a blow to their reputations**
  - `if they leave a firm before bad news breaks`：if条件状语从句
    - `before bad news breaks`：时间状语从句
  - `even if a review of history shows …`：even if让步状语从句
    - `they were on the board at the time any wrongdoing occurred`：省略that的宾语从句，作shows的宾语

##### 生词

have an easier time of doing：更容易做某事；blow to reputation：声誉受损；break：爆发；wrongdoing：不当行为

##### 翻译

但研究人员认为，如果外部董事在坏消息爆发前离开公司，即使历史回顾显示不当行为发生时他们仍在董事会，他们也更容易避免声誉受损。

##### 考点

Whatever happened to the death of newspapers? A year ago the end seemed near. The recession 经济衰退 threatened to remove the advertising and readers that had not already fled 逃离 to the internet. Newspapers like the San Francisco Chronicle were chronicling 记录 their own doom 厄运. America's Federal Trade Commission 联邦贸易委员会 launched a round of talks about how to save newspapers. Should they become charitable corporations 慈善机构? Should the state subsidize 补贴 them? It will hold another meeting soon. But the discussions now seem out of date 过时的.

In much of the world there is little sign of crisis. German and Brazilian papers have shrugged off 摆脱 the recession. Even American newspapers, which inhabit 处于 the most troubled corner of the global industry, have not only survived but often returned to profit 盈利. Not the 20% profit margins 利润率 that were routine 常规的 a few years ago, but profit all the same 尽管如此.

It has not been much fun. Many papers stayed afloat 维持运营 by pushing journalists overboard 解雇. The American Society of News Editors reckons 估计 that 13,500 newsroom 新闻编辑室 jobs have gone since 2007. Readers are paying more for slimmer 更薄的 products. Some papers even had the nerve 胆敢 to refuse delivery to distant suburbs. Yet these desperate 孤注一掷的 measures have proved the right ones and, sadly for many journalists, they can be pushed further.

Newspapers are becoming more balanced businesses, with a healthier mix of revenues 收入 from readers and advertisers. American papers have long been highly unusual in their reliance on ads. Fully 87% of their revenues came from advertising in 2008, according to the Organization for Economic Cooperation & Development (OECD) 经济合作与发展组织. In Japan the proportion is 35%. Not surprisingly, Japanese newspapers are much more stable.

The whirlwind 旋风 that swept through newsrooms harmed everybody, but much of the damage has been concentrated in areas where newspapers are least distinctive 有特色的. Car and film reviewers have gone. So have science and general business reporters. Foreign bureaus 驻外机构 have been savagely 残酷地 cut off. Newspapers are less complete as a result. But completeness is no longer a virtue 优点 in the newspaper business.

2. 句子理解题（indicate/suggest）答案在**上下文语境**中，不选字面意思
3. 原因题（because）需结合**段落主旨**推理，不选无中生有的细节
4. 数字对比题：关注**百分比对比**背后的逻辑（广告依赖度→稳定性）
5. 推理题（infer）正确答案常是**原文的反推**（最不特色的受损→特色很重要）

We tend to think of the decades immediately following World War II as a time of prosperity 繁荣 and growth, with soldiers returning home by the millions, going off to college on the G. I. Bill 退伍军人法案 and lining up at the marriage bureaus 婚姻登记处.

But when it came to their houses, it was a time of common sense and a belief that less could truly be more. During the Depression 大萧条 and the war, Americans had learned to live with less, and that restraint 克制, in combination with the postwar confidence in the future, made small, efficient housing positively stylish 时髦的.

Economic condition was only a stimulus 刺激因素 for the trend toward efficient living. The phrase "less is more" was actually first popularized 推广 by a German, the architect Ludwig Mies van der Rohe, who like other people associated with 与…有关 the Bauhaus, a school of design, emigrated 移民 to the United States before World War II and took up posts 任职 at American architecture schools. These designers came to exert 施加 enormous influence on the course of American architecture, but none more so than Mies.

Mies's signature phrase 标志名言 means that less decoration 装饰, properly organized, has more impact than a lot. Elegance 优雅, he believed, did not derive from 源自 abundance 丰富. Like other modern architects, he employed metal, glass and laminated wood 层压木—materials that we take for granted 视为理所当然 today but that in the 1940s symbolized the future. Mies's sophisticated 复杂精美的 presentation masked 掩盖 the fact that the spaces he designed were small and efficient, rather than big and often empty.

The apartments in the elegant towers Mies built on Chicago's Lake Shore Drive, for example, were smaller—two-bedroom units under 1,000 square feet—than those in their older neighbors along the city's Gold Coast. But they were popular because of their airy 通风的 glass walls, the views they afforded and the elegance of the buildings' details and proportions 比例, the architectural equivalent 对应物 of the abstract art 抽象艺术 so popular at the time.

The trend toward "less" was not entirely foreign. In the 1930s Frank Lloyd Wright started building more modest 朴素的 and efficient houses—usually around 1,200 square feet—than the spreading two-story ones he had designed in the 1890s and the early 20th century.

The "Case Study Houses" 案例研究住宅 commissioned 委托 from talented modern architects by California Arts & Architecture magazine between 1945 and 1962 were yet another homegrown 本土的 influence on the "less is more" trend. Aesthetic 美学的 effect came from the landscape 风景, new materials and forthright 直率的 detailing. In his Case Study House, Ralph Rapson may have mispredicted 错误预测 just how the mechanical revolution would impact everyday life—few American families acquired helicopters, though most eventually got clothes dryers—but his belief that self-sufficiency 自给自足 was both desirable 可取的 and inevitable 不可避免的 was widely shared.

##### 本篇命题规律小结（英二阅读）

1. 文化/历史类文章：关注**概念定义**（"less is more"是什么）和**人物观点**（Mies的理念）
2. 推理题（infer）正确答案是**原文的合理延伸**，不选无中生有或偷换概念
3. 人物观点题（held that/believed）定位到**直接引语或观点句**，答案是同义替换
4. 判断题（What is true）需**逐项回原文定位**，注意与原文相反的干扰项
5. 常见干扰：把"不是A"偷换成"是A"（如rather than empty → identified with emptiness）

Will the European Union make it 成功? The question would have sounded strange not long ago. Now even the project's greatest cheerleaders 支持者 talk of a continent facing a "Bermuda triangle 百慕大三角" of debt, population decline and lower growth.

As well as those chronic 长期的 problems, the EU faces an acute 严重的 crisis in its economic core, the 16 countries that use the single currency 单一货币. Markets have lost faith that the euro zone's economies, weaker or stronger, will one day converge 趋同 thanks to the discipline of sharing a single currency, which denies uncompetitive 缺乏竞争力的 members the quick fix 权宜之计 of devaluation 货币贬值.

Yet the debate about how to save Europe's single currency from disintegration 解体 is stuck 陷入僵局. It is stuck because the euro zone's dominant powers 主导国, France and Germany, agree on the need for greater harmonization 协调 within the euro zone, but disagree about what to harmonize.

Germany thinks the euro must be saved by stricter rules on borrowing, spending and competitiveness, backed by 支持 quasi-automatic 准自动的 sanctions 制裁 for governments that do not obey. These might include threats to freeze EU funds for poorer regions and EU mega-projects 大型项目, and even the suspension 暂停 of a country's voting rights in EU ministerial councils 部长理事会. It insists that economic coordination 协调 should involve all 27 members of the EU club, among whom there is a small majority for free-market liberalism 自由市场自由主义 and economic rigour 经济紧缩; in the inner core alone, Germany fears, a small majority favour French interference 干预.

A "southern" camp 阵营 headed by France wants something different: "European economic government" within an inner core of euro-zone members. Translated 换言之, that means politicians intervening in 干预 monetary policy 货币政策 and a system of redistribution 再分配 from richer to poorer members, via cheaper borrowing for governments through common Eurobonds 欧元债券 or complete fiscal transfers 财政转移. Finally, figures close to the French government have murmured 私下说, euro-zone members should agree to some fiscal and social harmonization: e.g., curbing 抑制 competition in corporate-tax rates 公司税率 or labour costs.

##### 本篇命题规律小结（英二阅读）

1. 经济/政治类文章：关注**各方立场对比**（德国vs法国），答案常在对比中
2. 因果题（because）定位到**原因状语从句**，注意agree…but disagree…的转折结构
3. 推理题（implies）需基于**方案具体内容**推理，不选无中生有
4. 态度题看**尾段**：too soon to write off、remains the largest、remarkably liberal等褒义词→积极态度
5. 常见干扰：偷换主语（市场失去信心→欧盟失去信心）、与原文相反（冻结基金→增加基金）

#### 题型说明

#### 原文

Leading doctors today weigh in on（加入讨论/发表意见）the debate over the government's role in promoting public health by demanding that ministers impose "fat taxes" on unhealthy food and introduce cigarette-style warnings to children about the dangers of a poor diet.

The demands follow comments made last week by the health secretary, Andrew Lansley, who insisted the government could not force people to make healthy choices and promised to free businesses from public health regulations.

But senior medical figures want to stop fast-food outlets（快餐店）opening near schools, restrict advertising of products high in fat, salt or sugar, and limit sponsorship of sports events by fast-food producers such as McDonald's.

They argue that government action is necessary to curb（遏制）Britain's addiction to unhealthy food and help halt spiraling rates of obesity（肥胖）, diabetes（糖尿病）and heart disease. Professor Terence Stephenson, president of the Royal College of Paediatrics and Child Health, said that the consumption of unhealthy food should be seen to be just as damaging as smoking or excessive drinking.

"Thirty years ago, it would have been inconceivable to have imagined a ban on smoking in the workplace or in pubs, and yet that is what we have now. Are we willing to be just as courageous in respect of obesity? I would suggest that we should be," said the leader of the UK's children's doctors.

Lansley has alarmed health campaigners by suggesting he wants industry rather than government to take the lead. He said that manufacturers of crisps（薯片）and candies could play a central role in the Change4Life campaign, the centerpiece of government efforts to boost healthy eating and fitness. He has also criticized the celebrity chef Jamie Oliver's high-profile attempt to improve school lunches in England as an example of how "lecturing"（说教）people was not the best way to change their behaviour.

Stephenson suggested potential restrictions could include banning TV advertisements for foods high in fat, salt or sugar before 9 pm and limiting them on billboards or in cinemas. "If we were really bold, we might even begin to think of high-calorie fast food in the same way as cigarettes — by setting strict limits on advertising, product placement and sponsorship of sports events," he said.

Such a move could affect firms such as McDonald's, which sponsors the youth coaching scheme run by the Football Association. Fast-food chains should also stop offering "inducements"（诱饵）such as toys, cute animals and mobile phone credit to lure young customers, Stephenson said.

Professor Dinesh Bhugra, president of the Royal College of Psychiatrists, said: "If children are taught about the impact that food has on their growth, and that some things can harm, at least information is available up front."

He also urged councils to impose "fast-food-free zones" around schools and hospitals — areas within which takeaways cannot open.

A Department of Health spokesperson said: "We need to create a new vision for public health where all of society works together to get healthy and live longer. This includes creating a new 'responsibility deal' with business, built on social responsibility, not state regulation. Later this year, we will publish a white paper setting out exactly how we will achieve this."

The food industry will be alarmed that such senior doctors back such radical moves, especially the call to use some of the tough tactics that have been deployed against smoking over the last decade.

#### 解析

**题型特点**：多项对应题要求考生根据文章内容将人物与其观点匹配。关键在于定位文章中每个人物对应的段落，准确理解其主张。难点在于部分观点并非原文直接引用，而是需要推理得出（如第43题Jamie Oliver）。

**解题策略**：
1. 先读左栏人名，在文章中定位每个人物的段落
2. 仔细阅读该人物的相关论述，特别注意引号内的直接引用
3. 将原文信息与右栏选项进行同义替换比对
4. 注意区分不同人物的观点，避免张冠李戴
5. 对于未直接引用的人物，需根据他人对其的评价进行推理

- **第41题（E）**：Andrew Lansley在第六段明确表示"manufacturers of crisps and candies could play a central role in the Change4Life campaign"，与选项E（薯片和糖果生产商可为Change4Life运动做出贡献）直接对应。
- **第42题（D）**：Terence Stephenson在第四段指出"consumption of unhealthy food should be seen to be just as damaging as smoking"，与选项D（应向儿童推广类似吸烟的警示）对应。注意原文"just as damaging as smoking"与"cigarette-style warnings"为同义替换。
- **第43题（C）**：Jamie Oliver在文中被Lansley批评——Lansley认为Oliver"说教"式的做法"not the best way to change behaviour"。既然Lansley批评说教无效，反推Oliver自己认为说教是有效方式，故选项C正确。此为推理题。
- **第44题（B）**：Dinesh Bhugra在第十段"urged councils to impose 'fast-food-free zones' around schools and hospitals"，与选项B（政府应禁止学校周边开设快餐店）直接对应。
- **第45题（G）**：卫生部发言人在倒数第二段提出"creating a new 'responsibility deal' with business, built on social responsibility"，即政府应加强企业责任感，与选项G对应。

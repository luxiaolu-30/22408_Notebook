Harlan Coben believes that if you're a writer, you'll find the time; and that if you can't find the time, then writing isn't a priority and you're not a writer. For him, writing is a (1) job—a job like any other. He has (2) it with plumbing 管道工作, pointing out that a plumber 管道工 doesn't wake up and say that he can't work with pipes today. (3), like most writers these days, you're holding down a job to pay the bills, it's not (4) to find the time to write. But it's not impossible. It requires determination and single-mindedness. #### 重点解析

- **第1题（B normal）**：词义辨析。前文"写作就是一份和其他工作一样的工作"，normal=普通的/正常的，与like any other呼应。difficult困难、steady稳定、pleasant愉快均不符。

- **第3题（A If）**：逻辑连词。"如果你像现在大多数作家一样在打工付账，找到写作时间并不容易"，If=如果，表条件假设。Though虽然、Once一旦、Unless除非均不符。

- **第5题（C Remember）**：词义辨析+语境。"请记住，大多数畅销书作家都是在做其他事情谋生时开始写作的"，Remember=记住，引出已知事实。Accept接受、Explain解释、Suppose假设均不符。

- **第7题（C supplement）**：词义辨析+语境。"知名作家也常常从事其他工作来弥补写作收入的不足"，supplement=补充/弥补，supplement income=弥补收入。donate捐赠、generate产生、calculate计算均不符。

- **第9题（B sacrifice）**：词义辨析+语境。"要把写作列为优先，就得牺牲一些日常活动和喜好"，sacrifice=牺牲，符合。highlight强调、continue继续、explore探索均不符。

- **第12题（D cut down on）**：固定搭配。"减少运动量"，cut down on=削减/减少。put up with忍受、make up for补偿、hang on to坚持均不符。

- **第16题（D in addition to）**：介词短语。"除了给写作留时间外，还要保留阅读时间"，in addition to=除……之外（还）。in place of代替、in charge of负责、in response to回应均不符。

- **第17题（A indispensable）**：词义辨析。"阅读是不可缺少的支持者——没有它不行"，indispensable=必不可少的。innovative创新的、invisible看不见的、instant即时的均不符。

- **第18题（D quicker）**：逻辑关系。"the+比较级..., the+比较级..."结构，"年龄越大，时间似乎过得越快"，quicker=更快的，符合。

- **第20题（D above all）**：副词短语。"如果你是作家，那就意味着最重要的是写作"，above all=首先/最重要的是。at most至多、in turn轮流、on average平均均不符。

On a recent sunny day, 13,000 chickens roam 漫步 over Larry Brown's 40 windswept 当风的 acres in Shiner, Texas. Some rest in the shade of a parked car, others drink water with the cows. This all seems random 随机的, but it's by design 有意设计的, part of what the $6.1 billion U.S. egg industry bets 打赌 will be its next big thing: climate-friendly eggs 气候友好型鸡蛋.

These eggs, which are making their debut 首次亮相 now on shelves for as much as $8 a dozen, are still labeled organic and animal-friendly, but they're also from birds that live on farms using regenerative agriculture 再生农业—special techniques to cultivate rich soils that can trap greenhouse gases 捕获温室气体. Such eggs could be marketed as helping to fight climate change.

"I'm excited about our progress," says Brown, who is adding more cover crops 覆盖作物 that draw worms and crickets 蟋蟀 for the chickens to eat. The birds' waste then fertilizes 施肥 fields. Such improvements "allow our hens to forage 觅食 for higher-quality natural feed that will be good for the land, the hens, and the eggs that we supply to our customers."

The egg industry's push is the first major test of whether animal products from regenerative farms can become the next premium offering 高端产品. In barely more than a decade, organic eggs went from being dismissed 被忽视 as a niche product 小众产品 in natural foods stores to being sold at Walmart. More recently there were similar doubts about probiotics 益生菌 and plant-based meats 植物肉, but both have exploded into 爆发成为 major supermarket categories. If the sustainable-egg rollout 推出 is successful, it could open the floodgates 打开闸门 for regenerative beef, broccoli 西兰花 and beyond.

Regenerative products could be a hard sell 难以推销, because the concept is tough to define quickly, says Julie Stanton, associate professor of agricultural economics at Pennsylvania State University Brandywine. Such farming also brings minimal, if any, improvement to the food products (though some producers say their eggs have more protein).

The industry is betting that the same consumers paying more for premium attributes 高端属性 such as free-range 散养, non-GMO 非转基因, and pasture-raised 牧场饲养 eggs will embrace sustainability. Surveys show that younger generations are more concerned about climate change, and some of the success of plant-based meat can be chalked up to 归因于 shoppers wanting to signal 表明 their desire to protect the environment. Young adults "really care about the planet," says John Brunnquell, president of Egg Innovations. "They are absolutely altering the food chain beyond what I think even they understand what they're doing."

##### Text1（21-25）

#### 长难句1

> This all seems random, but it's by design, part of what the $6.1 billion U.S. egg industry bets will be its next big thing: climate-friendly eggs.

##### 句子拆分

并列句，but连接
分句1：**This all seems random**
分句2：**but it's by design, part of what the $6.1 billion U.S. egg industry bets will be its next big thing**
- `part of what …`：同位语，解释it's by design
- `what the $6.1 billion U.S. egg industry bets will be its next big thing`：what宾语从句，作of的宾语
  - `the $6.1 billion U.S. egg industry bets`：插入语
- 冒号后：`climate-friendly eggs`：解释next big thing

##### 生词

random：随机的；by design：有意设计的；part of：…的一部分；egg industry：鸡蛋行业；bet：打赌、确信；next big thing：下一个大热门；climate-friendly eggs：气候友好型鸡蛋

##### 翻译

这一切似乎是随机的，但这是有意设计的，是价值61亿美元的美国鸡蛋行业确信将成为下一个大热门的一部分：气候友好型鸡蛋。

##### 考点

**but连接并列句+同位语+what宾语从句+插入语+冒号解释**；21题D选项的依据——气候友好型鸡蛋是在专门设计的农场生产的（on specially designed farms = by design, farms using regenerative agriculture）。

#### 长难句2

> These eggs, which are making their debut now on shelves for as much as $8 a dozen, are still labeled organic and animal-friendly, but they're also from birds that live on farms using regenerative agriculture—special techniques to cultivate rich soils that can trap greenhouse gases.

##### 句子拆分

并列句，but连接
分句1：**These eggs are still labeled organic and animal-friendly**
- `which are making their debut now on shelves for as much as $8 a dozen`：which非限制性定语从句，修饰eggs
分句2：**but they're also from birds that live on farms using regenerative agriculture**
- `that live on farms using regenerative agriculture`：that定语从句，修饰birds
  - `using regenerative agriculture`：现在分词作后置定语，修饰farms
- 破折号后：`special techniques to cultivate rich soils that can trap greenhouse gases`：同位语，解释regenerative agriculture
  - `to cultivate rich soils`：不定式作后置定语，修饰techniques
  - `that can trap greenhouse gases`：that定语从句，修饰soils

##### 生词

make one's debut：首次亮相；on shelves：在货架上；as much as：多达；a dozen：一打；label：贴标签；organic：有机的；animal-friendly：动物友好的；regenerative agriculture：再生农业；special techniques：特殊技术；cultivate：培育；rich soils：肥沃土壤；trap：捕获；greenhouse gases：温室气体

##### 翻译

这些鸡蛋现在首次在货架上亮相，售价高达每打8美元，仍然贴着有机和动物友好的标签，但它们也来自生活在使用再生农业的农场的鸡——再生农业是培育能捕获温室气体的肥沃土壤的特殊技术。

##### 考点

**which非限制性定语从句+but连接并列句+that定语从句+现在分词后置定语+同位语+不定式后置定语+that定语从句**；21题D选项的补充——气候友好型鸡蛋来自使用再生农业的专门设计农场。

#### 长难句9

> Surveys show that younger generations are more concerned about climate change, and some of the success of plant-based meat can be chalked up to shoppers wanting to signal their desire to protect the environment.

##### 句子拆分

主干：**Surveys show that …**
- that引导宾语从句，从句中and连接两个并列分句
分句1：`younger generations are more concerned about climate change`
分句2：`some of the success of plant-based meat can be chalked up to shoppers wanting to signal their desire to protect the environment`
  - `wanting to signal their desire to protect the environment`：现在分词作后置定语，修饰shoppers
  - `to protect the environment`：不定式作后置定语，修饰desire

##### 生词

survey：调查；younger generations：年轻一代；be concerned about：关心；climate change：气候变化；plant-based meat：植物肉；chalk up to：归因于；shopper：购物者；signal：表明、显示；desire：渴望；protect the environment：保护环境

##### 翻译

##### 考点

**that宾语从句+and连接并列分句+现在分词后置定语+不定式后置定语**；24题B选项的依据——年轻人更关心气候变化，可能购买气候友好型鸡蛋（are likely to buy climate-friendly eggs = more concerned about climate change + want to signal desire to protect environment）。

#### 长难句10

> Young adults "really care about the planet," says John Brunnquell, president of Egg Innovations. "They are absolutely altering the food chain beyond what I think even they understand what they're doing."

##### 句子拆分

直接引语
- `"really care about the planet"`：直接引语
- `says John Brunnquell`：倒装引语
- `altering the food chain beyond what I think even they understand what they're doing`：现在分词作伴随状语

    - `I think`：插入语

More Americans are opting to 选择 work well into retirement 退休后很久, a growing trend that threatens to upend 颠覆 the old workforce model 旧劳动力模式.

One in three Americans who are at least 40 have or plan to have a job in retirement to prepare for a longer life, according to a survey conducted by Harris Poll for TD Ameritrade. Even more surprising is that more than half of "unretirees 不退休者"—those who plan to work in retirement or went back to work after retiring—said they would be employed in their later years even if they had enough money to settle down 安定下来, the survey showed.

Financial needs aren't the only culprit 原因 for the "unretirement" trend. Other reasons, according to the study, include personal fulfillment 个人成就感 such as staying mentally fit 保持心理健康, preventing boredom 避免无聊 or avoiding depression 避免抑郁. About 72% of "unretiree" respondents said that they would return to work once retired to keep mentally fit while 59% said it would be tied to making ends meet.

"The concept of retirement is evolving 演变," said Christine Russell, senior manager of retirement at TD Ameritrade. "It's not just about finances. The value of work is also driving folks to continue working past retirement."

One reason for the change in retirement patterns 退休模式: Americans are living longer. Older Americans are also the fastest-growing segment 增长最快的部分 of the U.S. workforce, and boomers 婴儿潮一代 are expected to live longer than previous generations. The percentage of retirement-age people in the labor force has doubled over the past three decades. About 20% of people 65 and older were in the workforce in February, up from an all-time low 历史最低点 of 10% in January 1985, according to money manager United Income.

Because of longer life spans 寿命, Americans are also boosting their savings 增加储蓄 to preserve their nest eggs 储备金, the TD Ameritrade study showed, which surveyed 2000 adults between 40 to 79. Six in 10 "unretirees" are increasing their savings in anticipation of 期待 a longer life, according to the survey. Among the most popular ways they are doing this, the company said, is by reducing their overall expenses 减少总开支, securing life insurance 获得人寿保险 or maximizing their contributions to 最大化缴费 retirement accounts 退休账户.

Unfortunately, many people who are opting to work in retirement are preparing to do so because they are worried about making ends meet 收支相抵 in their later years, said Brent Weiss, a co-founder at Baltimore-based financial-planning firm Facet Wealth. He suggested that preretirees 准退休者 should speak with a financial adviser 财务顾问 to set long-term financial goals.

"The most challenging moments in life are getting married, starting a family and ultimately retiring," Facet Wealth co-founder Brent Weiss told USA Today. "It's not just a financial decision, but an emotional one. Many people believe they can't retire."

We have all encountered them, in both our personal and professional lives. Think about the times you felt tricked 欺骗 or frustrated 沮丧 by a membership or subscription 会员或订阅 that had a seamless 无缝的 sign-up process 注册流程 but was later difficult to cancel—something that should be simple and transparent 透明的 can be complicated, intentionally or unintentionally, in ways that impair 损害 consumer choice 消费者选择. These are examples of dark patterns 黑暗模式.

First coined 首次创造 in 2010 by user experience expert Harry Brignull, "dark patterns" is a catch-all term 笼统术语 for practices that manipulate 操纵 user interfaces 用户界面 to influence the decision-making ability of users. Brignull identifies 12 types of common dark patterns, ranging from misdirection 误导 and hidden costs 隐藏成本 to "roach motel 蟑螂汽车旅馆," where a user experience seems easy and intuitive 直觉的 at the start, but turns difficult when the user tries to get out 退出.

In a 2019 study of 53,000 product pages and 11,000 websites, researchers found that about one in 10 employs these design practices. Though widely prevalent 普遍存在, the concept of dark patterns is still not well understood. Business and nonprofit leaders should be aware of 意识到 dark patterns and try to avoid the gray areas 灰色地带 they engender 产生.

Where is the line between ethical 合乎道德的, persuasive design 有说服力的设计 and dark patterns? Businesses should engage in 参与 conversations with IT, compliance 合规, risk, and legal teams to review their privacy policy 隐私政策, and include in the discussion the customer/user experience designers and coders 程序员 responsible for the company's user interface, as well as the marketers and advertisers responsible for sign-ups, checkout baskets 购物车, pricing, and promotions. Any or all these teams can play a role in creating or avoiding "digital deception 数字欺骗".

Lawmakers and regulators are slowly starting to address 解决 the ambiguity 模糊性 around dark patterns, most recently at the state level. In March, the California Attorney General 加州总检察长 announced the approval of additional regulations under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) 加州消费者隐私法案 that ensures that consumers will not be confused or misled when seeking to exercise their data privacy rights 行使数据隐私权. The regulations aim to ban dark patterns—this means prohibiting companies from using confusing language or unnecessary steps such as forcing them to click through multiple screens 点击多个屏幕 or listen to reasons why they shouldn't opt out 选择退出.

As more states consider promulgating 颁布 additional regulations, there is a need for greater accountability 问责制 from within the business community. Dark patterns also can be addressed on a self-regulatory basis 自我监管基础, but only if organizations hold themselves accountable, not just to legal requirements, but also to industry best practices 行业最佳实践 and standards.

Although ethics classes 伦理课 are common around the world, scientists are unsure if their lessons can actually change behavior; evidence either way is weak, relying on contrived 人为的 laboratory tests or sometimes unreliable self-reports 自我报告. But a new study published in Cognition 认知 found that, in at least one real-world situation, a single ethics lesson may have had lasting effects 持久影响.

The researchers investigated 调查 one class session's impact on eating meat. They chose this particular behavior for three reasons, according to study co-author Eric Schwitzgebel, a philosopher at the University of California, Riverside: students' attitudes on the topic are variable 多变的 and unstable, behavior is easily measurable 可测量的, and ethics literature 伦理文献 largely agrees that eating less meat is good because it reduces environmental harm 环境损害 and animal suffering 动物痛苦. Half of the students in four large philosophy classes read an article on the ethics of factory-farmed meat 工厂化养殖肉, optionally watched an 11-minute video on the topic and joined a 50-minute discussion. The other half focused on charitable giving 慈善捐赠 instead. Then, unknown to the students, the researchers studied their anonymized 匿名的 meal-card purchases 餐卡消费 for that semester—nearly 14,000 receipts 收据 for almost 500 students.

Schwitzgebel predicted 预测 the intervention 干预 would have no effect; he had previously found that ethics professors do not differ from 不同于 other professors on a range of behaviors, including voting rates 投票率, blood donation 献血 and returning library books 归还图书馆书籍. But among student subjects who discussed meat ethics, meal purchases containing meat decreased from 52 to 45 percent—and this effect held steady 保持稳定 for the study's duration 持续时间 of several weeks. Purchases from the other group remained at 52 percent.

That's actually a pretty large effect for a pretty small intervention, Schwitzgebel says. Psychologist Nina Strohminger at the University of Pennsylvania, who was not involved in the study, says she wants the effect to be real but cannot rule out 排除 some unknown confounding variable 未知混淆变量. And if real, she notes, it might be reversible 可逆的 by another nudge 推动: "Easy come, easy go."

Schwitzgebel suspects the greatest impact came from social influence 社会影响—classmates or teaching assistants 助教 leading the discussions may have shared their own vegetarianism 素食主义, showing it as achievable 可实现的 or more common. Second, the video may have had an emotional impact 情感影响. Least rousing 最不激励人的, he thinks, was rational argument 理性论证, although his co-authors say reason might play a bigger role. Now the researchers are probing 探究 the specific effects of teaching style, teaching assistant's eating habits and students' video exposure 视频接触. Meanwhile, Schwitzgebel—who had predicted no effect—will be eating his words 承认错误.

predict：预测；intervention：干预；have no effect：没有效果；previously：之前；find：发现；ethics professors：伦理学教授；differ from：不同于；a range of：一系列；behaviors：行为；including：包括；voting rates：投票率；blood donation：献血；returning library books：归还图书馆书籍

##### 翻译

施维茨格贝尔预测干预不会有效果；他之前发现伦理学教授在一系列行为上与其他教授没有不同，包括投票率、献血和归还图书馆书籍。

##### 考点

**省略that的宾语从句+分号连接并列句+that宾语从句+including介词短语+三个并列**；38题D选项的依据——施维茨格贝尔之前的发现表明伦理学教授不一定在道德上更好（are not necessarily ethically better = ethics professors do not differ from other professors on a range of behaviors）。

- `containing meat`：现在分词作后置定语，修饰purchases

##### 生词

##### 考点

##### 翻译

宾夕法尼亚大学的心理学家尼娜·斯特罗明格没有参与这项研究，她说她希望效果是真的，但不能排除一些未知的混淆变量。

##### 考点

- C：错误。difficult to ignore（难以忽视）与原文相反。证据薄弱，不是难以忽视。
- D：错误。poorly summarized（总结得差）无中生有。

#### 37. Which of the following is a reason for the researchers to study meat-eating?

#### 40. Eric Schwitzgebel suspects that the students' change in behavior ______.

✅答案：**C** 定位：最后一段。`Schwitzgebel suspects the greatest impact came from social influence … Second, the video may have had an emotional impact. Least rousing, he thinks, was rational argument`（施维茨格贝尔认为最大的影响来自社会影响……第二，视频可能产生了情感影响。他认为最不激励人的是理性论证）。说明学生行为变化是多个因素（社会影响、视频情感影响、理性论证）的结果。

- A：错误。can bring psychological benefits（能带来心理益处）无中生有。
- B：错误。can be analyzed statistically（可以统计分析）是研究方法，不是施维茨格贝尔对行为变化原因的看法。

##### 本篇命题规律小结（英二阅读）

1. 科普/研究类文章：关注**背景（伦理课效果不确定，证据薄弱）→新研究（一次伦理课可能有持久影响）→研究设计（选择吃肉行为的三个原因：态度多变、易测量、文献同意）→实验方法（实验组vs对照组，匿名餐卡消费）→预测vs结果（预测无效果，但含肉购买从52%降到45%且持续）→外部评价（斯特罗明格：希望真实但不能排除混淆变量，可能可逆）→原因分析（社会影响最大、视频情感影响其次、理性论证最不激励人）**的结构
2. 细节题：定位到**unsure+evidence weak**（scientists unsure + evidence either way is weak = hard to determine）
3. 细节题：定位到**三个原因并列**（behavior is easily measurable）
4. 推理题：定位到**之前发现+do not differ from**（ethics professors do not differ from other professors on behaviors = not necessarily ethically better）
5. 观点题：定位到**人物直接引语+cannot rule out+reversible**（wants real but cannot rule out confounding variable + might be reversible = uncertain）
6. 推理题：定位到**多个因素并列**（social influence + video emotional impact + rational argument = multiple factors）

##### 新题型 Part B（41-45）

#### 题型说明

**小标题匹配题（7选5）**：从7个备选小标题（A-G）中为5个段落选择最合适的小标题，有2个多余选项。文章主题为"如何在休息后重新开始锻炼"。

#### 原文

**How to Get Active Again After a Break**

Moving your body has been shown to reduce anxiety and depression, lower rates of many types of cancer and the risk of a heart attack, and improve overall immunity. It also helps build strength and stamina 体力. Getting back into exercise can be a challenge in the best of times, but with gyms and in-person exercise classes off-limits 禁止进入 to many people these days because of COVID-19 concerns, it can be tricky to know where to start. And it's important to get the right dose of activity. "Too much too soon either results in injury or burnout," says Mary Yoke, PhD, a faculty member in the kinesiology 运动学 department at Indiana University in Bloomington.

The following simple strategies will help you return to exercise safely after a break.

**41. ______**

Don't try to go back to what you were doing before your break. If you were walking 3 miles a day, playing 18 holes of golf three times a week, or lifting 10-pound dumbbells 哑铃 for three sets of 10 reps, reduce activity to half a mile every other day, or nine holes of golf once a week with short walks on other days, or use 5-pound dumbbells for one set of 10 reps.

Increase time, distance, and intensity gradually. "This isn't something you can do overnight," Denay says. But you will reap benefits such as less anxiety and improved sleep right away.

**42. ______**

If you're breathing too hard to talk in complete sentences, back off. If you feel good, go a little longer or faster. Feeling wiped out 精疲力竭 after a session? Go easier next time. And stay alert to serious symptoms, such as chest pain or pressure, severe shortness of breath or dizziness 头晕, or faintness 昏厥, and seek medical attention immediately.

**43. ______**

Consistency 一致性/连贯性 is the key to getting stronger and building endurance and stamina. Ten minutes of activity per day is a good start, says Marcus Jackovitz, DPT, a physical therapist at the University of Miami Hospital. All the experts we spoke with highly recommend walking because it's the easiest, most accessible form of exercise. Although it can be a workout on its own, if your goal is to get back to Zumba classes, tennis, cycling, or any other activity, walking is also a great first step.

**44. ______**

Even if you can't yet do a favorite activity, you can practice the moves. With or without a club or racket 球拍, swing like you're hitting the ball. Paddle like you're in a kayak or canoe. Mimic 模仿 your favorite swimming strokes. The action will remind you of the joy the activity brought you and prime your muscles for when you can get out there again.

**45. ______**

Exercising with others "can keep you accountable 有责任心 and make it more fun, so you're more likely to do it again," Jackovitz says. You can do activities such as golf and tennis or take a walk with others and still be socially distant. But when you can't connect in person, consider using technology. #### 题目

从以下选项中为段落41-45选择最合适的小标题（A-G），其中两个为多余选项：

[A] Make it a habit. 养成习惯
[B] Don't go it alone. 不要独自锻炼
[C] Start low, go slow. 从低开始，慢慢来
[D] Talk with your doctor. 与医生沟通
[E] Listen to your body. 倾听身体的声音
[F] Go through the motions. 练习动作
[G] Round out your routine. 丰富你的日常

---
##### 参考答案
41.C 42.E 43.A 44.F 45.B

#### 解析

**题型特点**：小标题匹配题（7选5），考查段落主旨概括能力。

**解题策略**：
1. 先读选项，标记核心动词（Make/Don't/Start/Talk/Listen/Go/Round）
2. 回到段落，定位中心句（通常在段首或段尾）
3. 注意段落中关键词与选项词汇的同义复现

**逐段解析**：
- **41题 C**：段落首句"Don't try to go back to what you were doing before your break"=不要试图回到以前的锻炼状态，"reduce activity to..."=减少活动量（对应start low），"increase gradually"=逐渐增加（对应go slow）。C项"Start low, go slow"概括了这两段的核心建议。
- **42题 E**：段落描述"breathing too hard"→back off，"feel good"→go longer，"feeling wiped out"→go easier，"stay alert to serious symptoms"=对严重症状保持警惕。这些都是倾听身体反应的建议，对应E"Listen to your body"。
- **43题 A**：段落首句"Consistency is the key"=连贯性是关键，"Ten minutes of activity per day is a good start"=每天十分钟是良好开端，都在指向形成习惯，对应A"Make it a habit"。
- **44题 F**：段落首句"you can practice the moves"=你可以练习动作，F项"Go through the motions"中go through对应practice，motions对应moves。
- **45题 B**：段落首句"Exercising with others...make it more fun"=与他人一起锻炼更有趣，B项"Don't go it alone"=不要独自锻炼，是反向同义转述。

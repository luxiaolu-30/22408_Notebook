## 详细解析

##### 翻译（15 分）

#### 原文

Although we try our best, sometimes our paintings rarely turn out as originally planned! Changes in the light, the limitations of your palette, and just plain old lack of experience and technique mean that what you start out trying to achieve sometimes doesn't come to life the way that you expected.

Although this can be frustrating and disappointing, it turns out that this can actually be good for you! Unexpected results have two benefits: for starters, you pretty quickly learn to deal with disappointment, and in time (often through repeated error) to realise that when one door closes, another opens. You quickly learn to adapt and come up with creative solutions to the problems the painting presents, and this means that thinking outside the box becomes second nature to the painter!

Creative problem solving skills are incredibly useful in daily life, and mean you're more likely to be able to quickly come up with a solution when a problem arises.

#### 参考译文

尽管我们尽了最大努力，但有时我们的画作很少会像原来计划的那样呈现！光线的变化、调色板的局限性，以及单纯的经验和技巧的不足，都意味着你一开始想要呈现的效果有时并不会以你预期的方式呈现。

虽然这可能会令人沮丧和失望，但事实证明，这实际上可能对你有好处！意想不到的结果有两个好处：首先，你很快就能学会应对失望情绪，并最终（通常是通过不断犯错）意识到当一扇门关闭时，另一扇门就会打开。你也会很快学会适应并想出创造性的解决方案来解决绘画中出现的问题，这意味着跳出固有思维模式成了画家的第二天性！

创造性的问题解决技能在日常生活中极为有用，意味着当问题出现时，你更有可能迅速想出解决方案。

#### 重点词汇与句型

- **turn out**：结果是，证明为，呈现
- **palette**：调色板，颜料
- **plain old**：单纯的，纯粹的（口语化表达）
- **come to life**：变得栩栩如生，实现
- **frustrating**：令人沮丧的
- **for starters**：首先，作为开端
- **in time**：最终，迟早
- **when one door closes, another opens**：一扇门关闭，另一扇门打开（谚语）
- **come up with**：想出，提出
- **thinking outside the box**：跳出固有思维模式，创新思维
- **second nature**：第二天性，习惯
- **incredibly**：极为，非常

#### 长难句分析

**1. Changes in the light, the limitations of your palette, and just plain old lack of experience and technique mean that what you start out trying to achieve sometimes doesn't come to life the way that you expected.**

- **结构**：三个并列主语 + mean that引导的宾语从句（嵌套what引导的主语从句和that引导的方式状语从句）
- **拆分**：Changes in the light, the limitations of your palette, and just plain old lack of experience and technique（三个并列主语）/ mean（谓语）/ that what you start out trying to achieve（宾语从句中的主语从句）/ sometimes doesn't come to life（宾语从句谓语）/ the way that you expected（方式状语从句）
- **翻译要点**：三个并列主语需分别译出；what引导的主语从句译为"你一开始想要呈现的效果"；the way that=以……的方式
- **译文**：光线的变化、调色板的局限性，以及单纯的经验和技巧的不足，都意味着你一开始想要呈现的效果有时并不会以你预期的方式呈现。

**2. Unexpected results have two benefits: for starters, you pretty quickly learn to deal with disappointment, and in time (often through repeated error) to realise that when one door closes, another opens.**

- **结构**：主句 + 冒号后解释，其中learn to deal with和to realise并列，that引导宾语从句（嵌套when引导的时间状语从句）
- **拆分**：Unexpected results have two benefits（主句）/ for starters, you pretty quickly learn to deal with disappointment（第一个好处）/ and in time (often through repeated error) to realise（与learn并列的不定式）/ that when one door closes, another opens（宾语从句）
- **翻译要点**：for starters=首先；in time=最终/迟早；when one door closes, another opens为谚语直译
- **译文**：意想不到的结果有两个好处：首先，你很快就能学会应对失望情绪，并最终（通常是通过不断犯错）意识到当一扇门关闭时，另一扇门就会打开。

**3. You quickly learn to adapt and come up with creative solutions to the problems the painting presents, and this means that thinking outside the box becomes second nature to the painter!**

- **结构**：and连接两个并列分句，第一个分句含定语从句，第二个分句含that引导的宾语从句
- **拆分**：You quickly learn to adapt and come up with creative solutions（第一个分句谓语）/ to the problems（介词短语修饰solutions）/ the painting presents（定语从句，修饰problems）/ and this means（第二个分句）/ that thinking outside the box becomes second nature to the painter（宾语从句）
- **翻译要点**：the painting presents为省略that的定语从句；thinking outside the box=跳出固有思维模式；second nature=第二天性
- **译文**：你也会很快学会适应并想出创造性的解决方案来解决绘画中出现的问题，这意味着跳出固有思维模式成了画家的第二天性！

##### 小作文（10 分）

#### 题目

**Directions:**

Write an email to introduce a food festival held at your university to international students and invite them to participate. Write your answer in about 100 words.

You should write neatly on the ANSWER SHEET. Do not sign your own name at the end of the email. Use "Li Ming" instead. (10 points)

**场景说明**：假设你是李明，写信向国际学生介绍学校举办的美食节活动，并邀请他们参加。属于"介绍+邀请信"。

#### 高分范文

Dear International Students,

I am writing to warmly invite you to our campus food festival, which will be held on the school playground from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. next Saturday.

During the event, you will have the opportunity to taste a wide variety of delicious dishes from different regions of China, ranging from spicy Sichuan cuisine to delicate Cantonese dim sum. In addition, there will be exciting cooking shows where you can learn to make traditional Chinese dumplings. It is also a wonderful chance to experience Chinese food culture and make new friends.

We sincerely hope you can join us and share the joy. If you have any questions, please feel free to contact me. I look forward to your participation.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

#### 范文翻译

亲爱的国际学生们：

我写信热烈邀请你们参加我们学校举办的美食节活动，活动将于下周六上午9点到下午5点在学校操场举行。

在活动期间，你们将有机会品尝来自中国不同地区的美味佳肴，从川味麻辣菜肴到精致的广东点心应有尽有。此外，还有精彩的烹饪表演，你们可以学习制作中国传统饺子。这也是体验中国饮食文化和结交新朋友的绝佳机会。

我们真诚地希望你们能来参加并分享这份快乐。如有任何问题，请随时联系我。期待你们的参与。

此致敬礼
李明

#### 重点句型与表达

1. **I am writing to warmly invite you to...** —— 邀请信开头万能句型，交代写信目的
2. **...which will be held on... from... to...** —— 非限制性定语从句，交代活动地点和时间
3. **You will have the opportunity to taste a wide variety of...** —— "你们将有机会品尝各种……"，用于介绍活动内容
4. **...ranging from... to...** —— "从……到……应有尽有"，用于列举
5. **In addition, there will be... where you can...** —— "此外，还有……你们可以……"，递进介绍
6. **It is also a wonderful chance to experience... and make...** —— "这也是体验……和结交……的绝佳机会"
7. **We sincerely hope you can join us and share the joy.** —— "真诚希望你们来参加并分享快乐"
8. **I look forward to your participation.** —— "期待你们的参与"，邀请信标准结尾

##### 大作文（15 分）

#### 图表描述

**图表类型**：柱状图（Bar Chart）

**图表内容**：2018-2020年我国快递业务量变动情况（单位：十亿件）
- 2018年：全国总体 51（十亿件），农村 12（十亿件）
- 2019年：全国总体 64（十亿件），农村 15（十亿件）
- 2020年：全国总体 83（十亿件），农村 30（十亿件）

**趋势**：总体和农村快递业务量均呈上升趋势，2020年增幅尤为显著，农村快递增速超过全国总体。

#### 题目

**Directions:**

Write an essay based on the following chart. In your writing, you should
1) interpret the chart, and
2) give your comments.

You should write about 150 words on the ANSWER SHEET. (15 points)

#### 高分范文

The bar chart above clearly illustrates the changes in China's express delivery business volume from 2018 to 2020. As is shown, the total volume nationwide steadily increased from 51 billion pieces in 2018 to 64 billion in 2019, before surging to 83 billion in 2020. Meanwhile, the rural delivery volume also experienced a significant growth, rising from 12 billion pieces in 2018 to 15 billion in 2019 and then jumping to 30 billion in 2020, with the growth rate in rural areas outpacing the national average.

What are the driving forces behind this remarkable growth? Several factors contribute to this trend. Initially, the rapid development of e-commerce and online shopping platforms has fueled the explosive growth of the express delivery industry. More importantly, the improvement of rural logistics infrastructure and the rising purchasing power of rural residents have greatly boosted rural delivery volume. The COVID-19 pandemic in 2020 also accelerated the shift toward online shopping, further driving up delivery demand.

In conclusion, the robust growth of China's express delivery industry reflects the vitality of the digital economy. It is expected that this trend will continue, and greater efforts should be made to further improve rural logistics to bridge the urban-rural gap.

#### 范文翻译

上方的柱状图清晰地展示了2018年至2020年中国快递业务量的变化。如图所示，全国总体业务量从2018年的510亿件稳步增长至2019年的640亿件，随后激增至2020年的830亿件。与此同时，农村快递业务量也经历了显著增长，从2018年的120亿件增至2019年的150亿件，随后跃升至2020年的300亿件，农村地区的增速超过了全国平均水平。

这一显著增长背后的驱动力是什么？有几个因素促成了这一趋势。首先，电子商务和网购平台的快速发展推动了快递行业的爆发式增长。更重要的是，农村物流基础设施的改善和农村居民购买力的提升大大推动了农村快递量的增长。2020年的新冠疫情也加速了向网购的转变，进一步推高了快递需求。

总之，中国快递行业的强劲增长反映了数字经济的活力。预计这一趋势将持续下去，应进一步加大力度改善农村物流，缩小城乡差距。

#### 重点句型与表达

1. **The bar chart above clearly illustrates the changes in... from... to...** —— 柱状图开头万能句型，引出图表主题和时间范围
2. **As is shown, ... steadily increased from X to Y, before surging to Z** —— 描述柱状图数据的经典句式（稳步增长后激增）
3. **...experienced a significant growth, rising from X to Y and then jumping to Z** —— "经历了显著增长，从X增至Y然后跃升至Z"
4. **...with the growth rate in... outpacing...** —— "……的增速超过……"，with结构做伴随状语
5. **What are the driving forces behind this remarkable growth?** —— 过渡句，引出原因分析
6. **More importantly, ... have greatly boosted...** —— "更重要的是，……大大推动了……"
7. **In conclusion, ... reflects the vitality of...** —— 结尾段总结句，"反映了……的活力"
8. **It is expected that this trend will continue, and greater efforts should be made to...** —— "预计这一趋势将持续，应加大力度……"

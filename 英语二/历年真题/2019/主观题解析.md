## 详细解析

##### 翻译（15 分）

#### 原文

It is easy to underestimate English writer James Herriot. He had such a pleasant, readable style that one might think that anyone could imitate it. How many times have I heard people say, "I could write a book. I just haven't the time." Easily said. Not so easily done. James Herriot, contrary to popular opinion, did not find it easy in his early days of, as he put it, "having a go at the writing game". While he obviously had an abundance of natural talent, the final, polished work that he gave to the world was the result of years of practicing, re-writing and reading. Like the majority of authors, he had to suffer many disappointments and rejections along the way, but these made him all the more determined to succeed. Everything he achieved in life was earned the hard way and his success in the literary field was no exception.

#### 参考译文

人们很容易低估英国作家吉米·哈利。他的文笔如此令人愉快、可读性如此之强，以至于人们可能会觉得任何人都能模仿他的风格。我曾多少次听到人们说："我能写一本书，只是没有时间。"说起来容易，做起来并不容易。与大众的看法相反，正如吉米·哈利自己所言，他早期在"写作游戏中的尝试"并不顺利。尽管他显然拥有丰富的天赋，但他呈现给世人的最终经过打磨的作品，是多年练习、重写和阅读的结果。与大多数作家一样，他在道路上经历了许多失望和拒绝，但这些使他更加坚定了成功的决心。他在人生中取得的一切成就都是来之不易的，他在文学领域的成功也不例外。

#### 重点词汇与句型

- **underestimate**：低估（under-在……之下 + estimate估计）
- **readable**：可读性强的、易读的
- **imitate**：模仿
- **contrary to popular opinion**：与大众观点相反
- **have a go at**：尝试做某事
- **abundance of**：丰富的、大量的
- **polished**：经过打磨的、精雕细琢的
- **rejection**：拒绝、被拒
- **all the more**：更加
- **determined**：坚定的、下定决心的
- **earn the hard way**：通过艰难方式获得=来之不易
- **no exception**：也不例外

#### 长难句分析

##### 长难句1

> He had such a pleasant, readable style that one might think that anyone could imitate it.

**句子拆分：**
- 主干：He had such a pleasant, readable style
- `such...that...`：如此……以至于……，引导结果状语从句
  - that anyone could imitate it：think的宾语从句
    - `it`指代`style`

**翻译：** 他的文笔如此令人愉快、可读性如此之强，以至于人们可能会觉得任何人都能模仿他的风格。

---

##### 长难句2

> James Herriot, contrary to popular opinion, did not find it easy in his early days of, as he put it, "having a go at the writing game".

**句子拆分：**
- 主干：James Herriot did not find it easy
- `contrary to popular opinion`：插入语，与大众观点相反
- `in his early days of "having a go at the writing game"`：时间状语
  - `as he put it`：插入语，如他所说
  - `having a go at`：尝试

**翻译：** 与大众的看法相反，正如吉米·哈利自己所言，他早期在"写作游戏中的尝试"并不顺利。

---

##### 长难句3

> While he obviously had an abundance of natural talent, the final, polished work that he gave to the world was the result of years of practicing, re-writing and reading.

**句子拆分：**
- 主干：the work was the result of years of practicing, re-writing and reading
- `While he obviously had an abundance of natural talent`：While引导让步状语从句，尽管……
- `that he gave to the world`：that引导定语从句，修饰work
- `the final, polished`：形容词修饰work

**翻译：** 尽管他显然拥有丰富的天赋，但他呈现给世人的最终经过打磨的作品，是多年练习、重写和阅读的结果。

---

##### 长难句4

> Like the majority of authors, he had to suffer many disappointments and rejections along the way, but these made him all the more determined to succeed.

**句子拆分：**
- 主干：he had to suffer...but these made him...（but连接的并列句）
- `Like the majority of authors`：介词短语作状语，与大多数作家一样
- `along the way`：在道路上
- `all the more determined`：更加坚定，all the more=更加

**翻译：** 与大多数作家一样，他在道路上经历了许多失望和拒绝，但这些使他更加坚定了成功的决心。

##### 小作文（10 分）

#### 题目

**Directions:**

Suppose Professor Smith asked you to plan a debate on the theme of city traffic. Write him an email to

1) suggest a specific topic with your reasons, and
2) tell him about your arrangements.

You should write about 100 words on the ANSWER SHEET.

Do not use your own name. Use "Li Ming" instead.

Do not write your address. (10 points)

#### 高分范文

Dear Professor Smith,

It is my great honor to plan a debate on the theme of city traffic. I would like to suggest "Private Cars: A Blessing or a Curse?" as the specific topic for this debate.

The primary reason is that the rapid increase of private cars has become a major concern in urban areas, sparking heated discussions among citizens. On the one hand, private cars offer convenience and mobility; on the other hand, they contribute significantly to traffic congestion and environmental pollution. This topic will encourage participants to think critically about a pressing social issue.

Regarding the arrangements, the debate will be held in the Student Activity Center at 2:30 p.m. on March 16th. Eight participants will be divided into two groups — one supporting the development of private cars and the other opposing it. Each group will have three rounds of argument. The audience will vote for the winning team.

I look forward to your reply.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

#### 范文翻译

亲爱的Smith教授：

我很荣幸策划一场关于城市交通主题的辩论。我想建议"私家车：是福还是祸？"作为本次辩论的具体题目。

主要原因是在城区，私家车的快速增长已成为一个主要问题，引发了市民的热烈讨论。一方面，私家车提供了便利和灵活性；另一方面，它们大大加剧了交通拥堵和环境污染。这一题目将鼓励参赛者批判性地思考一个紧迫的社会问题。

关于安排，辩论将于3月16日下午2:30在学生活动中心举行。八名参赛者将分为两组——一组支持发展私家车，另一组反对。每组将进行三轮论证。观众将投票选出获胜团队。

期待您的回复。

您真诚的，
Li Ming

#### 重点句型与表达

1. **It is my great honor to plan a debate on the theme of...**（我很荣幸策划一场关于……主题的辩论）——建议信开头句型
2. **I would like to suggest...as the specific topic for this debate.**（我想建议……作为本次辩论的具体题目）——提出建议句型
3. **The primary reason is that...**（主要原因是……）——解释原因句型
4. **On the one hand...; on the other hand...**（一方面……；另一方面……）——正反论证句型
5. **This topic will encourage participants to think critically about...**（这一题目将鼓励参赛者批判性地思考……）——说明选题意义
6. **Regarding the arrangements, the debate will be held in...at...on...**（关于安排，辩论将于……在……举行）——介绍安排句型
7. **Each group will have three rounds of argument.**（每组将进行三轮论证）——细节安排说明
8. **I look forward to your reply.**（期待您的回复）——结尾句型

##### 大作文（15 分）

#### 图表描述

柱状图（Bar Chart）：某高校2013年与2018年毕业生去向对比

四类去向（对比两年的数据）：
- 就业（employment）：2013年68.1% → 2018年60.7%（下降）
- 升学（further study）：2013年26.3% → 2018年34.0%（上升）
- 创业（entrepreneurship）：2013年1.3% → 2018年2.6%（小幅上升）
- 出国（going abroad）：占比较小

#### 题目

**Directions:**

Write an essay based on the following chart. In your writing, you should

1) interpret the chart, and
2) give your comments.

You should write about 150 words on the ANSWER SHEET. (15 points)

#### 高分范文

The bar chart above clearly illustrates the changes in the choices of graduates in a certain university from 2013 to 2018. As is shown, taking up an occupation accounts for the highest proportion, but it declined from 68.1% in 2013 to 60.7% in 2018. By contrast, the percentage of those pursuing further education increased significantly from 26.3% to 34.0%, and the proportion of students starting their own business rose slightly from 1.3% to 2.6%.

Several factors may account for this phenomenon. First and foremost, with social competition becoming increasingly fierce, the job market has grown more challenging for college graduates, which prompts many to pursue postgraduate education to enhance their competitiveness. Moreover, the government has introduced a series of policies to encourage entrepreneurship and innovation, providing favorable conditions such as tax reductions and financial support for young entrepreneurs. Additionally, an increasing number of students wish to broaden their horizons and acquire advanced knowledge before entering the workforce.

From my perspective, this trend reflects students' growing awareness of self-improvement and diversified career planning. It is advisable for graduates to make rational decisions based on their own interests, abilities, and career goals, rather than blindly following trends. Society should also provide more guidance and support to help them make informed choices.

#### 范文翻译

上面的柱状图清楚地展示了某高校2013年至2018年毕业生去向的变化。如图所示，就业占比最高，但从2013年的68.1%下降到2018年的60.7%。相比之下，选择升学的比例从26.3%显著上升到34.0%，创业的学生比例从1.3%小幅上升到2.6%。

有几个因素可以解释这一现象。首先，随着社会竞争日益激烈，就业市场对大学毕业生来说更具挑战性，促使许多人选择继续深造以提升竞争力。其次，政府出台了一系列鼓励创业创新的政策，为年轻创业者提供了减税和资金支持等有利条件。此外，越来越多的学生希望在进入职场前拓展视野、获取更高层次的知识。

在我看来，这一趋势反映了学生对自我提升和多元化职业规划日益增长的意识。建议毕业生根据自身兴趣、能力和职业目标做出理性决定，而非盲目跟风。社会也应提供更多指导和支持，帮助他们做出明智的选择。

#### 重点句型与表达

1. **The bar chart above clearly illustrates the changes in...from...to...**（上面的柱状图清楚地展示了……从……到……的变化）——柱状图描述开头句型
2. **As is shown, taking up an occupation accounts for the highest proportion.**（如图所示，就业占比最高）——总体描述句型
3. **By contrast, the percentage of...increased significantly from...to...**（相比之下，……的比例从……显著上升到……）——对比描述数据变化
4. **Several factors may account for this phenomenon.**（有几个因素可以解释这一现象）——原因分析段开头句型
5. **First and foremost, with social competition becoming increasingly fierce...**（首先，随着社会竞争日益激烈……）——with短语表背景
6. **Moreover, the government has introduced a series of policies to encourage...**（其次，政府出台了一系列政策鼓励……）——论述政府政策
7. **From my perspective, this trend reflects...**（在我看来，这一趋势反映了……）——评论段开头句型
8. **It is advisable for graduates to make rational decisions based on...**（建议毕业生根据……做出理性决定）——建议句型

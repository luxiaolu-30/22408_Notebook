#### 重点解析

- **第1题（D. However）**：逻辑连词。前句说定期称重是好事，后句说太频繁会"hurt"（伤害），语意转折。However=然而，表转折关系。
- **第2题（A. helps）**：动词辨析。"hurt more than it helps"（弊大于利）。helps=有帮助，与hurt对比，"伤害多于帮助"。
- **第3题（B. solely）**：副词辨析。"focusing solely on the scale"（仅仅关注体重秤上的数字）。solely=仅仅、只，与from being generally healthy对比，说明注意力从整体健康转向了单纯的体重数字。
- **第4题（B. lowering）**：动宾搭配。"thinking only of lowering the number on the scale"（一心只想降低秤上的数字）。lowering=降低，体重增加了（肌肉），但只想着把数字降下来。
- **第5题（D. reach）**：固定搭配。"reach my goals"（达到目标）。reach goals=实现目标，固定搭配。
- **第6题（B. depiction）**：名词辨析。"an accurate depiction of the hard work and progress"（对努力和进步的准确描述）。depiction=描述、描绘，称重不能准确反映健身进展。
- **第7题（A. due to）**：介词短语。"significant changes in your weight due to altering your training program"（由于调整训练计划而导致的体重显著变化）。due to=由于，表因果关系。
- **第8题（D. immediate）**：形容词辨析。"The most immediate changes will be observed in skill level, strength and inches lost"（最直接的变化体现在技能水平、力量和围度上）。immediate=直接的、立即可见的。
- **第9题（C. reasons）**：固定搭配。"For these reasons"（由于这些原因）。reasons=原因，总结前文所述各条理由。
- **第10题（A. instead）**：副词辨析。"switched to a bimonthly weighing schedule instead"（改为每两周称一次体重取而代之）。instead=代替、取而代之，与stopped weighing every day呼应。
- **第11题（A. track）**：动词辨析。"less important for me to track my weight each week"（每周追踪体重变得不那么重要）。track=追踪、跟踪。
- **第12题（D. account for）**：动词短语。"observe and account for any significant weight changes"（观察并解释任何显著体重变化）。account for=解释、说明原因。
- **第13题（B. adjust）**：动词辨析。"whether I need to adjust my training program"（是否需要调整训练计划）。adjust=调整，根据体重变化调整计划。
- **第14题（A. results）**：名词辨析。"use my bimonthly weigh-in results"（利用双周称重结果）。results=结果，称重的结果数据。
- **第15题（C. hungry）**：形容词辨析。"constantly hungry and dropping weight"（总是感到饥饿且体重下降）。hungry=饥饿的，与后文"增加每日卡路里摄入"呼应。
- **第16题（D. sign）**：名词辨析。"this is a sign that I need to increase my daily caloric intake"（这是一个我需要增加每日热量摄入的信号）。sign=迹象、信号。
- **第17题（C. decision）**：名词辨析。"The decision to stop weighing myself every day"（停止每天称重的决定）。decision=决定，后文说这个决定带来了奇效。
- **第18题（A. disappointing）**：形容词辨析。"the burden of a disappointing morning weigh-in"（令人失望的早晨称重的负担）。disappointing=令人失望的，称重结果不理想让人失望。
- **第19题（D. because）**：连词。"greater success... because I'm training according to those goals"（更大的成功……因为我根据那些目标训练）。because=因为，表因果关系。
- **第20题（A. obsessing）**：动词辨析。"Rather than obsessing over the scale"（与其沉迷于体重秤上的数字）。obsessing=沉迷、痴迷，rather than表"与其……不如"。

Unlike so-called basic emotions 基本情绪 such as sadness, fear, and anger, guilt 内疚 emerges 出现 a little later, in conjunction with 与…一起 a child's growing grasp 理解 of social and moral norms 社会和道德规范. Children aren't born knowing how to say "I'm sorry"; rather, they learn over time that such statements appease 安抚 parents and friends—and their own consciences 良心. This is why researchers generally regard so-called moral guilt, in the right amount 适量, to be a good thing.

In the popular imagination 大众想象, of course, guilt still gets a bad rap 口碑不好. It is deeply uncomfortable—it's the emotional equivalent 等同 of wearing a jacket weighted with stones 装满石头的夹克. Yet this understanding is outdated 过时的. "There has been a kind of revival 复兴 or a rethinking about what guilt is and what role guilt can serve," says Amrisha Vaish, a psychology researcher at the University of Virginia, adding that this revival is part of a larger recognition that emotions aren't binary 二元的—feelings that may be advantageous 有利的 in one context may be harmful in another. Jealousy 嫉妒 and anger, for example, may have evolved to alert us to important inequalities 不平等. Too much happiness can be destructive 破坏性的.

And guilt, by prompting 促使 us to think more deeply about our goodness, can encourage humans to make up for errors 弥补错误 and fix relationships. Guilt, in other words, can help hold a cooperative species 合作物种 together. It is a kind of social glue 社会粘合剂.

Viewed in this light 从这个角度看, guilt is an opportunity. Work by Tina Malti, a psychology professor at the University of Toronto, suggests that guilt may compensate for 补偿 an emotional deficiency 情感缺陷. In a number of studies, Malti and others have shown that guilt and sympathy 同情 may represent different pathways 途径 to cooperation and sharing. Some kids who are low in sympathy may make up for that shortfall 不足 by experiencing more guilt, which can rein in 控制 their nastier 更恶劣的 impulses 冲动. And vice versa 反之亦然: High sympathy can substitute for 替代 low guilt.

In a 2014 study, for example, Malti looked at 244 children. Using caregiver assessments 看护人评估 and the children's self-observations 自我观察, she rated 评估 each child's overall sympathy level and his or her tendency to feel negative emotions after moral transgressions 道德越轨. Then the kids were handed chocolate coins 巧克力币, and given a chance to share them with an anonymous 匿名的 child. For the low-sympathy kids, how much they shared appeared to turn on 取决于 how inclined they were to feel guilty. The guilt-prone 容易内疚的 ones shared more, even though they hadn't magically become more sympathetic to the other child's deprivation 匮乏.

"That's good news," Malti says. "We can be prosocial 亲社会的 because we caused harm and we feel regret 后悔."

#### 长难句1

> Unlike so-called basic emotions such as sadness, fear, and anger, guilt emerges a little later, in conjunction with a child's growing grasp of social and moral norms.

##### 句子拆分

主干：**guilt emerges a little later**
- `Unlike so-called basic emotions such as sadness, fear, and anger`：介词短语作比较状语
- `in conjunction with a child's growing grasp of social and moral norms`：介词短语作伴随状语

##### 生词

unlike：不像；so-called：所谓的；basic emotions：基本情绪；guilt：内疚；emerge：出现；a little later：稍晚一些；in conjunction with：与…一起；growing grasp：日益增长的理解；social and moral norms：社会和道德规范

##### 翻译

不像悲伤、恐惧和愤怒等所谓的基本情绪，内疚出现得稍晚一些，与孩子对社会和道德规范日益增长的理解一起出现。

##### 考点

**unlike比较状语+in conjunction with伴随状语**；开篇定义内疚——与道德规范理解相关，21题C选项的背景（内疚促进道德发展）。

#### 长难句5

> "There has been a kind of revival or a rethinking about what guilt is and what role guilt can serve," says Amrisha Vaish, a psychology researcher at the University of Virginia, adding that this revival is part of a larger recognition that emotions aren't binary—feelings that may be advantageous in one context may be harmful in another.

##### 句子拆分

直接引语主干：**There has been a kind of revival or a rethinking about what guilt is and what role guilt can serve**
- `what guilt is`和`what role guilt can serve`：and连接的两个what宾语从句，作about的宾语
- `says Amrisha Vaish, a psychology researcher at the University of Virginia`：倒装引语，`a psychology researcher`同位语
- `adding that this revival is part of a larger recognition that emotions aren't binary`：现在分词作伴随状语
  - `that this revival is part of a larger recognition`：that宾语从句，作adding的宾语
  - `that emotions aren't binary`：that同位语从句，解释recognition
- 破折号后：`feelings that may be advantageous in one context may be harmful in another`
  - 同位语，解释emotions aren't binary
  - `that may be advantageous in one context`：that定语从句，修饰feelings

##### 生词

revival：复兴；rethinking：反思；serve：起作用；psychology researcher：心理学研究者；adding that：补充说；larger recognition：更广泛的认识；binary：二元的；advantageous：有利的；context：情境、语境；harmful：有害的

#### 长难句10

> The guilt-prone ones shared more, even though they hadn't magically become more sympathetic to the other child's deprivation.

Forests give us shade 阴凉, quiet and one of the harder challenges 更艰巨的挑战 in the fight against climate change. Even as we humans count on 依靠 forests to soak up 吸收 a good share of the carbon dioxide 二氧化碳 we produce, we are threatening their ability to do so. The climate change we are hastening 加速 could one day leave us with forests that emit 排放 more carbon than they absorb.

Thankfully, there is a way out of this trap 陷阱—but it involves striking a subtle balance 达成微妙的平衡. Helping forests flourish 繁荣 as valuable "carbon sinks 碳汇" long into the future may require reducing their capacity to absorb carbon now. California is leading the way 带头, as it does on so many climate efforts, in figuring out the details.

The state's proposed Forest Carbon Plan 森林碳计划 aims to double efforts to thin out 间伐 young trees and clear brush 清除灌木 in parts of the forest. This temporarily lowers carbon-carrying capacity 碳承载能力. But the remaining trees draw a greater share of the available moisture 水分, so they grow and thrive, restoring the forest's capacity to pull carbon from the air. Healthy trees are also better able to fend off 抵御 insects. The landscape is rendered less easily burnable 不易燃烧. Even in the event of a fire, fewer trees are consumed 烧毁.

The need for such planning is increasingly urgent 紧迫. Already, since 2010, drought 干旱 and insects have killed over 100 million trees in California, most of them in 2016 alone, and wildfires have burned hundreds of thousands of acres 英亩.

California plans to treat 处理 35,000 acres of forest a year by 2020, and 60,000 by 2030—financed from the proceeds 收益 of the state's emissions-permit auctions 排放许可拍卖. That's only a small share of the total acreage that could benefit, about half a million acres in all, so it will be vital to prioritize 优先处理 areas at greatest risk of fire or drought.

The strategy also aims to ensure that carbon in woody material 木质材料 removed from the forests is locked away 锁定 in the form of solid lumber 实木 or burned as biofuel 生物燃料 in vehicles that would otherwise run on fossil fuels. New research on transportation biofuels is already under way.

State governments are well accustomed to 习惯于 managing forests, but traditionally they've focused on wildlife, watersheds 流域 and opportunities for recreation 娱乐. Only recently have they come to see the vital part forests will have to play in storing carbon. California's plan, which is expected to be finalized 定稿 by the governor 州长 next year, should serve as a model 榜样.

American farmers have been complaining of labor shortages 劳动力短缺 for several years. The complaints are unlikely to stop without an overhaul 全面改革 of immigration rules 移民规则 for farm workers.

Congress 国会 has obstructed 阻碍 efforts to create a more straightforward 更直接的 visa 签证 for agricultural workers that would let foreign workers stay longer in the U.S. and change jobs within the industry. If this doesn't change, American businesses, communities, and consumers will be the losers 输家.

Perhaps half of U.S. farm laborers are undocumented immigrants 无证移民. As fewer such workers enter the country, the characteristics of the agricultural workforce 农业劳动力 are changing. Today's farm laborers, while still predominantly 主要地 born in Mexico, are more likely to be settled rather than migrating 迁移 and more likely to be married than single. They're also aging 老龄化. At the start of this century, about one-third of crop workers were over the age of 35. Now more than half are. And picking crops is hard on older bodies. One oft-debated 经常争论的 cure for this labor shortage remains as implausible 难以置信的 as it's been all along: Native U.S. workers won't be returning to the farm.

Mechanization 机械化 isn't the answer, either—not yet, at least. Production of corn, cotton, rice, soybeans, and wheat has been largely mechanized, but many high-value, labor-intensive 劳动密集型 crops, such as strawberries, need labor. Even dairy farms 奶牛场, where robots do a small share of milking 挤奶, have a long way to go before they're automated 自动化.

As a result, farms have grown increasingly reliant on 依赖 temporary guest workers 临时客工 using the H-2A visa to fill the gaps in the workforce. Starting around 2012, requests for the visas rose sharply 急剧上升; from 2011 to 2016 the number of visas issued 签发 more than doubled.

The H-2A visa has no numerical cap 数量上限, unlike the H-2B visa for nonagricultural work, which is limited to 66,000 a year. Even so, employers complain they aren't given all the workers they need. The process is cumbersome 繁琐的, expensive, and unreliable. One survey found that bureaucratic delays 官僚延误 led the average H-2A worker to arrive on the job 22 days late. The shortage is compounded 加剧 by federal immigration raids 突击搜查, which remove some workers and drive others underground 转入地下.

In a 2012 survey, 71 percent of tree-fruit growers and almost 80 percent of raisin and berry growers said they were short of labor. Some western farmers have responded by moving operations 转移业务 to Mexico. From 1998 to 2000, 14.5 percent of the fruit Americans consumed was imported 进口. Little more than a decade later, the share of imports was 25.8 percent.

In effect 实际上, the U.S. can import food or it can import the workers who pick it.

Arnold Schwarzenegger, Dia Mirza and Adrian Grenier have a message for you. It's easy to beat plastic 塑料. They're part of a bunch of 一群 celebrities 名人 starring in 主演 a new video for World Environment Day 世界环境日—encouraging you, the consumer, to swap out 换掉 your single-use plastic staples 一次性塑料日用品 like straws 吸管 and cutlery 餐具 to combat the plastics crisis 塑料危机.

The key messages that have been put together for World Environment Day do include a call for governments to enact legislation 制定法律 to curb 限制 single-use plastics. But the overarching 首要的 message is directed at individuals 个人.

My concern with leaving it up to the individual, however, is our limited sense of what needs to be achieved. On their own, taking our own bags to the grocery store or quitting plastic straws, for example, will accomplish little and require very little of us. They could even be detrimental 有害的, satisfying a need to have "done our bit 尽了自己的一份力" without ever progressing onto 进展到 bigger, bolder, more effective actions—a kind of "moral licensing 道德许可" that allays 减轻 our concerns and stops us doing more and asking more of those in charge 掌权者.

While the conversation around our environment and our responsibility toward it remains centered on shopping bags and straws, we're ignoring the balance of power 权力平衡 that implies that as "consumers" we must shop sustainably 可持续地购物, rather than as "citizens" hold our governments and industries to account 要求…负责 to push for real systemic change 系统性变革.

It's important to acknowledge 承认 that the environment isn't everyone's priority—or even most people's. We shouldn't expect it to be. In her latest book, Why Good People Do Bad Environmental Things, Wellesley College professor Elizabeth R. DeSombre argues that the best way to collectively 集体地 change the behavior of large numbers of people is for the change to be structural 结构性的.

This might mean implementing policy such as a plastic tax 塑料税 that adds a cost to environmentally problematic action, or banning single-use plastics altogether. India has just announced it will "eliminate 消除 all single-use plastic in the country by 2022." There are also incentive-based 基于激励的 ways of making better environmental choices easier, such as ensuring recycling is at least as easy as trash disposal 垃圾处理.

DeSombre isn't saying people should stop caring about the environment. It's just that individual actions are too slow, she says, for that to be the only, or even primary, approach to changing widespread behavior.

None of this is about writing off 忽视 the individual. It's just about putting things into perspective 正确看待事物. We don't have time to wait. We need progressive policies 进步政策 that shape collective action (and rein in 控制 polluting businesses), alongside engaged citizens 积极参与的公民 pushing for change.

✅答案：**B** 定位：第三段。`a kind of "moral licensing" that allays our concerns and stops us doing more and asking more of those in charge`（一种"道德许可"，减轻我们的担忧，阻止我们做更多，也阻止我们对掌权者要求更多）。说明道德许可可能阻止我们做进一步努力。

- B：正确。阻止我们做进一步努力。stops us doing more = prevent us from making further efforts。
- C：错误。weaken our sense of accomplishment（削弱我们的成就感）与原文相反。道德许可满足了"done our bit"的需求，不是削弱成就感。
- D：错误。suppress our desire for success（抑制我们对成功的渴望）无中生有。

#### 38. By pointing out our identity as "citizens," the author indicates that ______.

[A] our focus should be shifted to community welfare [B] our relationship with local industries is improving [C] we have been actively exercising our civil rights [D] we should press our governments to lead the combat

✅答案：**D** 定位：第四段。`rather than as "citizens" hold our governments and industries to account to push for real systemic change`（而不是作为"公民"要求我们的政府和行业负责，推动真正的系统性变革）。说明作为公民，我们应该要求政府负责，推动变革，即敦促政府主导这场战斗。

- A：错误。our focus should be shifted to community welfare（我们的重点应转向社区福利）无中生有。
- B：错误。our relationship with local industries is improving（我们与当地行业的关系正在改善）无中生有。
- C：错误。we have been actively exercising our civil rights（我们一直在积极行使公民权利）与原文相反。原文说我们忽视了作为公民要求政府负责，不是一直在积极行使公民权利。
- D：正确。我们应该敦促政府主导这场战斗。hold our governments to account to push for real systemic change = press our governments to lead the combat。

#### 39. DeSombre argues that the best way for a collective change should be ______.

[A] a win-win arrangement [B] a self-driven mechanism [C] a cost-effective approach [D] a top-down process

✅答案：**D** 定位：第五段。`the best way to collectively change the behavior of large numbers of people is for the change to be structural`（集体改变大量人行为的最佳方式是让这种改变是结构性的）。结构性的=自上而下的过程（政府制定政策）。

- A：错误。a win-win arrangement（双赢安排）无中生有。
- B：错误。a self-driven mechanism（自驱动机制）与原文相反。结构性变革是政府推动的，不是自驱动的。
- C：错误。a cost-effective approach（成本效益高的方法）无中生有。
- D：正确。自上而下的过程。structural change（政府制定塑料税、禁令等）= top-down process。

#### 40. The author concludes that individual efforts ______.

[A] can be too aggressive [B] can be too inconsistent [C] are far from sufficient [D] are far from rational

✅答案：**C** 定位：最后两段。`individual actions are too slow, for that to be the only, or even primary, approach to changing widespread behavior`（个人行动太慢，不能成为改变广泛行为的唯一，甚至主要的方法）+ `We need progressive policies that shape collective action, alongside engaged citizens pushing for change`（我们需要塑造集体行动的进步政策，以及推动变革的积极公民）。说明个人努力远远不够，需要政策。

- A：错误。can be too aggressive（可能太激进）无中生有。
- B：错误。can be too inconsistent（可能太不一致）无中生有。
- C：正确。远远不够。too slow + not the only or primary approach + need progressive policies = far from sufficient。
- D：错误。are far from rational（远非理性）无中生有。

##### 本篇命题规律小结（英二阅读）

1. 环保/社会类文章：关注**现象（名人鼓励减塑）→作者担忧（个人行动不够，道德许可）→提出观点（作为公民要求政府推动系统性变革）→理论支持（DeSombre：结构性变革）→具体政策（塑料税、禁令、激励）→结论（个人行动远远不够，需要政策+公民）**的结构
2. 细节题：定位到**现在分词伴随状语**（encouraging consumers to swap out single-use plastics）
3. 词义/概念题：定位到**同位语+that定语从句**（moral licensing that allays concerns and stops us doing more）
4. 观点题：定位到**rather than对比**（as consumers shop sustainably, rather than as citizens hold governments to account）
5. 主旨/结论题：把握**全文反复强调的主题**（individual actions too slow, need structural policies = far from sufficient）

##### 新题型 Part B（41-45）

#### 题型说明

多项对应/信息匹配题。文章给出关于"父母购房时是否应考虑孩子意见"的主题文章，左栏列出5个人物，右栏给出7个观点选项（A-G），要求为每个人物匹配其对应的观点，多出2个干扰项。每题2分，共10分。

#### 原文

##### How Seriously Should Parents Take Kids' Opinions When Searching for a Home?

(1) In choosing a new home, Camille McClain's kids have a single demand: a backyard.

(2) McClain's little ones aren't the only kids who have an opinion when it comes to housing, and in many cases youngsters' views weigh heavily on parents' real estate decisions, according to a 2018 Harris Poll survey of more than 2,000 U.S. adults.

(3) While more families buck an older-generation proclivity to leave kids in the dark about real estate decisions, realty agents and psychologists have mixed views about the financial, personal and long-term effects kids' opinions may have.

(4) The idea of involving children in a big decision is a great idea because it can help them feel a sense of control and ownership in what can be an overwhelming process, said Ryan Hooper, a clinical psychologist in Chicago.

(5) "Children may face serious difficulties in coping with significant moves, especially if it removes them from their current school or support system," he said.

(6) Greg Jaroszewski, a real estate broker with Gagliardo Realty Associates, said he's not convinced that kids should be involved in selecting a home — but their opinions should be considered in regards to proximity to friends and social activities, if possible.

(7) Younger children should feel like they're choosing their home — without actually getting a choice in the matter, said Adam Bailey, a real estate attorney based in New York.

(8) Asking them questions about what they like about the backyard of a potential home will make them feel like they're being included in the decision-making process, Bailey said.

(9) Many of the aspects of homebuying aren't a consideration for children, said Tracey Hampson, a real estate agent based in Santa Clarita, Calif. And placing too much emphasis on their opinions can ruin a fantastic home purchase.

(10) "Speaking with your children before you make a real estate decision is wise, but I wouldn't base the purchasing decision solely on their opinions," Hampson said.

(11) The other issue is that many children — especially older ones — may base their real estate knowledge on HGTV shows, said Aaron Norris of The Norris Group in Riverside, Calif.

(12) "They love Chip and Joanna Gaines just as much as the rest of us," he said. "HGTV has seriously changed how people view real estate. It's not shelter, it's a lifestyle. With that mindset change come some serious money consequences."

(13) Kids tend to get stuck in the features and the immediate benefits to them personally, Norris said.

(14) Parents need to remind their children that their needs and desires may change over time, said Julie Gurner, a real estate analyst with FitSmallBusiness.com.

(15) "Their opinions can change tomorrow," Gurner said. "Harsh as it may be to say, that decision should likely not be made contingent on a child's opinions, but rather made for them with great consideration into what home can meet their needs best — and give them an opportunity to customize it a bit and make it their own."

(16) This advice is more relevant now than ever before, even as more parents want to embrace the ideas of their children, despite the current housing crunch.

#### 备选选项

[A] remarks that significant moves may pose challenges to children.（指出重大搬迁可能给孩子带来挑战）

[B] says that it is wise to leave kids in the dark about real estate decisions.（认为让孩子对房产决策一无所知是明智的）

[C] advises that home purchases should not be based only on children's opinions.（建议购房不应仅基于孩子的意见）

[D] thinks that children should be given a sense of involvement in home-buying decisions.（认为应让孩子在购房决策中有参与感）

[E] notes that aspects like children's friends and social activities should be considered upon home-buying.（指出购房时应考虑孩子的朋友和社交活动等因素）

[F] believes that home-buying decisions should be based on children's needs rather than their opinions.（认为购房决策应基于孩子的需求而非意见）

[G] assumes that many children's views on real estate are influenced by the media.（认为许多孩子对房地产的看法受媒体影响）

#### 题目（41-45）

将下列人物与上述选项A-G进行匹配：

41. Ryan Hooper
42. Adam Bailey
43. Tracey Hampson
44. Aaron Norris
45. Julie Gurner

---
##### 参考答案
41.A 42.D 43.C 44.G 45.F

#### 解析

**题型特点：** 多项对应（信息匹配）题。文章主题为"父母购房时是否应考虑孩子意见"，通过多位专家的不同观点展开讨论。解题核心是人名定位+观点关键词匹配。

**解题策略：**
1. 先读文章标题，把握主题（购房、孩子、意见）
2. 在原文中划出5个人名出现的位置，标注对应段落
3. 读7个选项，划出每个选项的核心区分词
4. 回到人名所在段落，寻找与选项核心词的同义替换

**逐题解析：**

- **41.A（Ryan Hooper → significant moves may pose challenges to children）**：Ryan Hooper出现在第4-5段。第5段原句"Children may face serious difficulties in coping with significant moves"（孩子在应对重大搬迁时可能面临严重困难），与A选项的significant moves may pose challenges to children完美对应，原词复现significant moves。

- **42.D（Adam Bailey → sense of involvement in home-buying decisions）**：Adam Bailey出现在第7-8段。第8段"make them feel like they're being included in the decision-making process"（让他们觉得自己参与了决策过程），included=被包含、参与，与D选项的a sense of involvement（参与感）同义替换。

- **43.C（Tracey Hampson → should not be based only on children's opinions）**：Tracey Hampson出现在第9-10段。第10段"I wouldn't base the purchasing decision solely on their opinions"（我不会仅凭他们的意见来做购买决定），solely=仅仅=only，base...on=基于，与C选项not be based only on children's opinions完美对应。

- **44.G（Aaron Norris → views on real estate are influenced by the media）**：Aaron Norris出现在第11-13段。第11段"many children may base their real estate knowledge on HGTV shows"（许多孩子的房地产知识基于HGTV节目），第12段"HGTV has seriously changed how people view real estate"（HGTV严重改变了人们对房地产的看法），HGTV=媒体，与G选项influenced by the media（受媒体影响）同义替换。

- **45.F（Julie Gurner → based on children's needs rather than their opinions）**：Julie Gurner出现在第14-15段。第15段"not be made contingent on a child's opinions, but rather made for them with great consideration into what home can meet their needs best"（不应取决于孩子的意见，而应基于什么房子最能满足他们的需求），needs rather than opinions=需求而非意见，与F选项完美对应。

**干扰项排除：**
- [B] leave kids in the dark：第3段提到older-generation proclivity to leave kids in the dark，但这是老一代的做法，不是任何题干人物的观点
- [E] friends and social activities：第6段Greg Jaroszewski提到proximity to friends and social activities，但Greg不是题干要求匹配的人物

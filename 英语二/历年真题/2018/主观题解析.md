## 详细解析

##### 翻译（15 分）

#### 原文

A fifth grader gets a homework assignment to select his future career path from a list of occupations. He ticks "astronaut", but quickly adds "scientist" to the list and selects it as well. The boy is convinced that if he reads enough, he can explore as many career paths as he likes. And so he reads — everything from encyclopedias to science fiction novels. He reads so passionately that his parents have to institute a "no reading policy" at the dinner table.

That boy was Bill Gates, and he hasn't stopped reading yet — not even after becoming one of the most successful people on the planet. Nowadays, his reading material has changed from science fiction and reference books: recently, he revealed that he reads at least 50 nonfiction books a year. Gates chooses nonfiction titles because they explain how the world works. "Each book opens up new avenues of knowledge," Gates says.

#### 参考译文

一个五年级的学生得到一份家庭作业，要求他从一系列职业中选出自己未来的职业道路。他勾选了"宇航员"，但很快又把"科学家"加到名单中，并且也选了它。这个男孩坚信，如果他读的书足够多，他就可以探索任何他喜欢的职业道路。于是他读书——从百科全书到科幻小说什么都读。他读书如此狂热，以至于父母不得不在餐桌上制定一条"禁止读书"的规定。

那个男孩就是比尔·盖茨，而且他至今没有停止阅读——即使在他成为这个星球上最成功的人之一后也没有。如今，他的阅读材料已经从科幻小说和参考书变成了非小说类书籍：最近，他透露自己一年至少读50本非小说类书籍。盖茨选择非小说类书籍，是因为它们解释了世界是如何运转的。"每一本书都开辟了通向知识的新途径，"盖茨说。

#### 重点词汇与句型

- **fifth grader**：五年级学生
- **homework assignment**：家庭作业
- **career path**：职业道路
- **occupation**：职业
- **tick**：勾选
- **be convinced that**：坚信……
- **explore**：探索
- **encyclopedia**：百科全书
- **science fiction novels**：科幻小说
- **passionately**：狂热地、热情地
- **institute**：制定、设立
- **no reading policy**：禁止读书的规定
- **reference books**：参考书、工具书
- **reveal**：透露、揭示
- **nonfiction**：非小说类（纪实类）书籍
- **open up new avenues of**：开辟……的新途径

#### 长难句分析

##### 长难句1

> The boy is convinced that if he reads enough, he can explore as many career paths as he likes.

**句子拆分：**
- 主干：The boy is convinced that...（男孩坚信……）
- that引导is convinced的宾语从句
  - `if he reads enough`：if引导条件状语从句
  - `as many...as he likes`：as...as结构，"尽可能多的……他喜欢的"

**翻译：** 这个男孩坚信，如果他读的书足够多，他就可以探索任何他喜欢的职业道路。

---

##### 长难句2

> He reads so passionately that his parents have to institute a "no reading policy" at the dinner table.

**句子拆分：**
- 主干：He reads so passionately that...（他读书如此狂热，以至于……）
- `so...that...`：如此……以至于……
- `at the dinner table`：地点状语

**翻译：** 他读书如此狂热，以至于父母不得不在餐桌上制定一条"禁止读书"的规定。

---

##### 长难句3

> That boy was Bill Gates, and he hasn't stopped reading yet — not even after becoming one of the most successful people on the planet.

**句子拆分：**
- 主干：That boy was Bill Gates, and he hasn't stopped reading yet（并列句）
- `— not even after becoming...`：破折号后补充说明，even after引导让步状语
  - `one of the most successful people on the planet`：地球上最成功的人之一

**翻译：** 那个男孩就是比尔·盖茨，而且他至今没有停止阅读——即使在他成为这个星球上最成功的人之一后也没有。

##### 小作文（10 分）

#### 题目

**Directions:**

Suppose you have to cancel your travel plan and will not be able to visit Professor Smith. Write him an email to

1) apologize and explain the situation, and
2) suggest a future meeting.

You should write about 100 words on the ANSWER SHEET.

Do not use your own name. Use "Li Ming" instead.

Do not write your address. (10 points)

#### 高分范文

Dear Professor Smith,

I am writing to sincerely apologize that I have to cancel my travel plan and will not be able to visit you as scheduled. An unexpected and urgent matter has arisen that requires my immediate attention, making it impossible for me to carry out the trip.

I deeply regret any inconvenience this may cause you. I was genuinely looking forward to our meeting and the opportunity to discuss my research with you. Would it be possible to reschedule our meeting for next month? I am available on any weekend in October, and I would be happy to adjust to a time that suits you best.

Thank you for your understanding and patience. I look forward to hearing from you soon.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

#### 范文翻译

亲爱的Smith教授：

我写信是想真诚地道歉，我不得不取消旅行计划，无法按原定时间拜访您。一件突发的紧急事务需要我立即处理，使我无法成行。

对于由此给您带来的任何不便，我深表歉意。我原本非常期待我们的会面，以及与您讨论我的研究的机会。不知能否将会面改期到下个月？我十月的任何一个周末都有空，很乐意配合您最方便的时间。

感谢您的理解与耐心。期待尽快收到您的回复。

您真诚的，
Li Ming

#### 重点句型与表达

1. **I am writing to sincerely apologize that...**（我写信是想真诚地道歉……）——道歉信开头句型
2. **I have to cancel my travel plan and will not be able to visit you as scheduled.**（我不得不取消旅行计划，无法按原定时间拜访您）——说明道歉事由
3. **An unexpected and urgent matter has arisen that requires my immediate attention.**（一件突发的紧急事务需要我立即处理）——解释原因，has arisen=出现
4. **I deeply regret any inconvenience this may cause you.**（对于由此给您带来的任何不便，我深表歉意）——表达歉意
5. **I was genuinely looking forward to our meeting.**（我原本非常期待我们的会面）——表达遗憾
6. **Would it be possible to reschedule our meeting for next month?**（不知能否将会面改期到下个月？）——提议未来会面，委婉句型
7. **I would be happy to adjust to a time that suits you best.**（很乐意配合您最方便的时间）——表达灵活性
8. **Thank you for your understanding and patience.**（感谢您的理解与耐心）——结尾感谢句型

##### 大作文（15 分）

#### 图表描述

饼图（Pie Chart）：2017年某市消费者选择餐厅时的关注因素

五个扇形区域：
- 特色菜品（features/distinguishing dishes）：36.3%
- 服务态度（service attitude）：26.8%
- 环境（environment）：23.8%
- 价格（price）：8.4%
- 其他（other factors）：4.7%

#### 题目

**Directions:**

Write an essay based on the chart below. In your writing, you should

1) interpret the chart, and
2) give your comments.

You should write about 150 words on the ANSWER SHEET. (15 points)

#### 高分范文

The pie chart above evidently illustrates the factors that consumers take into consideration when choosing a restaurant in a certain city in 2017. As is shown, distinguishing features of dishes take the lion's share, accounting for 36.3%, followed by service attitude at 26.8% and environment at 23.8%. Surprisingly, price falls far behind, making up merely 8.4%, while other factors account for 4.7%.

Several factors may account for this phenomenon. First and foremost, with the steady improvement of living standards, consumers are no longer satisfied with merely filling their stomachs. Instead, they pursue unique culinary experiences, which explains why specialty dishes rank first. Moreover, as dining out has become a social activity rather than a mere necessity, consumers increasingly value attentive service and a pleasant atmosphere, considering them essential to a satisfying dining experience. The low percentage of price indicates that quality has outweighed affordability in people's dining choices.

From my perspective, this trend is a positive reflection of social progress and economic development. It suggests that people are attaching greater importance to the quality of life. Restaurant owners should take note of this shift and focus on enhancing food quality, service standards, and dining environment rather than competing solely on price.

#### 范文翻译

上面的饼图清楚地展示了2017年某市消费者选择餐厅时的关注因素。如图所示，特色菜品占据了最大份额，占36.3%，其次是服务态度26.8%和环境23.8%。令人惊讶的是，价格远远落后，仅占8.4%，而其他因素占4.7%。

有几个因素可以解释这一现象。首先，随着生活水平的稳步提高，消费者不再满足于仅仅填饱肚子，而是追求独特的烹饪体验，这解释了为何特色菜品排名第一。此外，外出就餐已成为一种社交活动而非单纯的必需品，消费者越来越重视周到的服务和舒适的氛围，认为它们是满意用餐体验的关键。价格占比低表明，品质在选择中已超过价格因素。

在我看来，这一趋势是社会进步和经济发展的积极反映。它表明人们越来越重视生活品质。餐厅经营者应注意到这一变化，专注于提升菜品质量、服务标准和用餐环境，而非仅在价格上竞争。

#### 重点句型与表达

1. **The pie chart above evidently illustrates the factors that...**（上面的饼图清楚地展示了……的因素）——饼图描述开头句型
2. **As is shown, distinguishing features of dishes take the lion's share, accounting for 36.3%.**（如图所示，特色菜品占据最大份额，占36.3%）——take the lion's share=占据最大份额，accounting for=占比
3. **Surprisingly, price falls far behind, making up merely 8.4%.**（令人惊讶的是，价格远远落后，仅占8.4%）——falls far behind=远远落后，making up=占比
4. **Several factors may account for this phenomenon.**（有几个因素可以解释这一现象）——原因分析段开头句型
5. **First and foremost, with the steady improvement of living standards...**（首先，随着生活水平的稳步提高……）——with短语表背景
6. **Instead, they pursue unique culinary experiences, which explains why...**（相反，他们追求独特的烹饪体验，这解释了为何……）——which引导非限制性定语从句
7. **The low percentage of price indicates that quality has outweighed affordability.**（价格占比低表明品质已超过价格因素）——indicate that=表明，outweigh=超过
8. **From my perspective, this trend is a positive reflection of...**（在我看来，这一趋势是……的积极反映）——评论段开头句型

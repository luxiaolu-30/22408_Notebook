#### 原文

Why do people read negative Internet comments and do other things that will obviously be painful? Because humans have an inherent need to (1) uncertainty, according to a recent study in Psychological Science. The new research reveals that the need to know is so strong that people will (2) to satisfy their curiosity even when it is clear the answer will (3).

In a series of four experiments, behavioral scientists at the University of Chicago and the Wisconsin School of Business tested students' willingness to (4) themselves to unpleasant stimuli in an effort to satisfy curiosity. For one (5), each participant was shown a pile of pens that the researcher claimed were from a previous experiment. The twist? Half of the pens would (6) an electric shock when clicked.

Twenty-seven students were told which pens were electrified; another twenty-seven were told only that some were electrified. (7) left alone in the room, the students who did not know which ones would shock them clicked more pens and incurred more shocks than the students who knew what would (8). Subsequent experiments reproduced this effect with other stimuli, (9) the sound of fingernails on a chalkboard and photographs of disgusting insects.

The drive to (10) is deeply rooted in humans, much the same as the basic drives for (11) or shelter, says Christopher Hsee of the University of Chicago. Curiosity is often considered a good instinct — it can (12) new scientific advances, for instance — but sometimes such (13) can backfire. The insight that curiosity can drive you to do (14) things is a profound one.

Unhealthy curiosity is possible to (15), however. In a final experiment, participants who were encouraged to (16) how they would feel after viewing an unpleasant picture were less likely to (17) to see such an image. These results suggest that imagining the (18) of following through on one's curiosity ahead of time can help determine (19) it is worth the endeavor. "Thinking about long-term (20) is key to reducing the possible negative effects of curiosity," Hsee says. In other words, don't read online comments.

#### 重点解析

- **第1题（B. resolve）**：动词辨析。"humans have an inherent need to resolve uncertainty"（人类有消除不确定性的内在需求）。resolve=解决、消除，与uncertainty搭配合理，protect uncertainty（保护不确定性）不符合语境。
- **第2题（D. seek）**：动词辨析。"people will seek to satisfy their curiosity"（人们会寻求满足自己的好奇心）。seek to do=力求做某事，符合"好奇心需求如此强烈"的语境。
- **第3题（A. hurt）**：动词辨析。"even when it is clear the answer will hurt"（即使很明显答案会让人受伤）。hurt=伤害，与前文painful呼应。
- **第4题（D. expose）**：固定搭配。"expose themselves to unpleasant stimuli"（让自己暴露在不愉快的刺激中）。expose sb to sth=使某人暴露于/遭受某事，固定搭配。
- **第5题（C. trial）**：名词辨析。"For one trial"（在一次实验中）。前文提到"a series of four experiments"，trial=试验、实验，与实验语境一致。
- **第6题（D. deliver）**：动宾搭配。"Half of the pens would deliver an electric shock when clicked"（一半的笔在点击时会释放电击）。deliver=传递、释放，deliver a shock=释放电击，固定搭配。
- **第7题（A. When）**：连词辨析。"When left alone in the room"（当被独自留在房间里时）。When引导时间状语从句，表示"当……时"，符合语境。
- **第8题（B. happen）**：动词辨析。"knew what would happen"（知道会发生什么）。happen=发生，与前文shock them呼应，"知道会发生什么"。
- **第9题（C. such as）**：介词短语。"other stimuli, such as the sound of fingernails on a chalkboard"（其他刺激，如指甲刮黑板的声音和令人恶心的昆虫照片）。such as=例如，用于举例说明。
- **第10题（A. discover）**：动词辨析。"The drive to discover"（探索未知的驱动力）。discover=发现、探索，与curiosity主题呼应，"好奇心即探索欲"。
- **第11题（D. food）**：名词辨析。"the basic drives for food or shelter"（对食物或住所的基本需求）。food=食物，与shelter并列为人类基本生存需求。
- **第12题（A. lead to）**：动词短语。"it can lead to new scientific advances"（它能带来新的科学进步）。lead to=导致、带来，好奇心促进科学进步。
- **第13题（C. inquiry）**：名词辨析。"sometimes such inquiry can backfire"（有时这种探究会适得其反）。inquiry=探究、好奇心的探究，与curiosity呼应，backfire=适得其反。
- **第14题（B. self-destructive）**：形容词辨析。"curiosity can drive you to do self-destructive things"（好奇心会驱使你做自我毁灭的事）。self-destructive=自我毁灭的，与前文backfire和painful呼应。
- **第15题（B. resist）**：动词辨析。"Unhealthy curiosity is possible to resist"（不健康的好奇心是可以抵制的）。resist=抵制、抵抗，后文实验证明可以抵制。
- **第16题（B. predict）**：动词辨析。"encouraged to predict how they would feel"（被鼓励预测他们看完后的感受）。predict=预测，后文"after viewing"说明事情未发生，需要预测。
- **第17题（C. choose）**：动词辨析。"less likely to choose to see such an image"（更不可能选择去看这样的图片）。choose=选择，choose to do=选择做某事。
- **第18题（D. outcome）**：名词辨析。"imagining the outcome of following through on one's curiosity"（想象顺从好奇心的后果）。outcome=结果、后果，与后文consequences呼应。
- **第19题（B. whether）**：连词。"determine whether it is worth the endeavor"（判断是否值得努力）。whether=是否，引导宾语从句。
- **第20题（A. consequences）**：名词辨析。"Thinking about long-term consequences"（考虑长期后果）。consequences=后果，与第18题outcome呼应，"考虑长期后果是减少好奇心负面影响的关键"。

---

It is curious 奇怪的 that Stephen Koziatek feels almost as though he has to justify 证明…合理 his efforts to give his students a better future.

Mr. Koziatek is part of something pioneering 开创性的. He is a teacher at a New Hampshire high school where learning is not something of books and tests and mechanical memorization 机械记忆, but practical 实践的. When did it become accepted wisdom 公认的智慧 that students should be able to name the 13th president of the United States but be utterly overwhelmed 完全不知所措 by a broken bike chain 链条?

As Koziatek knows, there is learning in just about everything. Nothing is necessarily gained by forcing students to learn geometry 几何 at a graffitied 涂鸦的 desk stuck with generations of discarded 丢弃的 chewing gum 口香糖. They can also learn geometry by assembling 组装 a bicycle.

But he's also found a kind of insidious 潜伏的 prejudice 偏见. Working with your hands is seen as almost a mark of inferiority 低等. Schools in the family of vocational education 职业教育 "have that stereotype 刻板印象…that it's for kids who can't make it academically 学业上成功," he says.

On one hand, that viewpoint is a logical product of America's evolution 演变. Manufacturing 制造业 is not the economic engine 经济引擎 that it once was. The job security 工作保障 that the US economy once offered to high school graduates has largely evaporated 蒸发. More education is the new principle 原则. We want more for our kids, and rightfully so 理所当然.

But the headlong 轻率的 push into bachelor's degrees 学士学位 for all—and the subtle devaluing 低估 of anything less—misses an important point: That's not the only thing the American economy needs. Yes, a bachelor's degree opens more doors. But even now, 54 percent of the jobs in the country are middle-skill jobs 中等技能工作, such as construction and high-skill manufacturing. But only 44 percent of workers are adequately trained 充分培训.

In other words, at a time when the working class has turned the country on its political head 政治上颠覆, frustrated that the opportunity that once defined America is vanishing, one obvious solution is staring us in the face 摆在眼前. There is a gap in working-class jobs, but the workers who need those jobs most aren't equipped 具备能力 to do them. Koziatek's Manchester School of Technology High School is trying to fill that gap.

Koziatek's school is a wake-up call 警钟. When education becomes one-size-fits-all 一刀切, it risks overlooking a nation's diversity of gifts 天赋多样性.

##### Text1（21-25）

#### 长难句1

> It is curious that Stephen Koziatek feels almost as though he has to justify his efforts to give his students a better future.

##### 句子拆分

主干：**It is curious that …**
- it是形式主语，真正主语是that引导的主语从句
- that从句主干：**Stephen Koziatek feels almost as though he has to justify his efforts**
  - `as though he has to justify his efforts`：as though表语从句
  - `to give his students a better future`：不定式作后置定语，修饰efforts

##### 生词

curious：奇怪的、好奇的；feel as though：感觉好像；justify：证明…合理、为…辩护；effort：努力；give sb a better future：给某人更好的未来

##### 翻译

奇怪的是，斯蒂芬·科齐亚泰克觉得好像他必须证明自己给学生更好未来的努力是合理的。

##### 考点

**it形式主语+that主语从句+as though表语从句+不定式后置定语**；开篇设疑——为什么职业教育者需要证明自己的努力合理，引出下文对职业教育偏见的讨论。

> He is a teacher at a New Hampshire high school where learning is not something of books and tests and mechanical memorization, but practical.

##### 句子拆分

主干：**He is a teacher at a New Hampshire high school**
- `where learning is not something of books and tests and mechanical memorization, but practical`：where定语从句，修饰high school
  - `not something of books and tests and mechanical memorization, but practical`：not…but…结构

##### 生词

New Hampshire：新罕布什尔州；where：在那里；learning：学习；mechanical memorization：机械记忆；practical：实践的、实用的

##### 翻译

他是新罕布什尔州一所高中的老师，在那里，学习不是书本、考试和机械记忆这些事情，而是实践。

##### 考点

**where定语从句+not…but…结构**；21题B选项的论点句——学习是实践的，举断自行车链的例子说明学生缺乏实践能力（practical ability）。

#### 长难句3

> When did it become accepted wisdom that students should be able to name the 13th president of the United States but be utterly overwhelmed by a broken bike chain?

##### 句子拆分

主干：**When did it become accepted wisdom that …?**
- it是形式主语，真正主语是that引导的主语从句
- that从句主干：**students should be able to name the 13th president of the United States but be utterly overwhelmed by a broken bike chain**
  - `be able to name … but be utterly overwhelmed by …`：but连接的并列谓语

##### 生词

accepted wisdom：公认的智慧、普遍看法；be able to：能够；name：说出…的名字；president：总统；utterly：完全地；overwhelmed：不知所措的、被压垮的；broken bike chain：断了的自行车链

##### 考点

**it形式主语+that主语从句+but连接并列谓语+反问句**；21题B选项的例证——断自行车链的例子说明学生缺乏实践能力（能说出总统名字却修不了自行车链）。

#### 长难句4

##### 句子拆分

主干：**Nothing is necessarily gained**
- `by forcing students to learn geometry at a graffitied desk`：by介词短语作方式状语
  - `stuck with generations of discarded chewing gum`：过去分词作后置定语，修饰desk

##### 生词

necessarily：必然地；gain：获得；force sb to do：强迫某人做；geometry：几何；graffitied：涂鸦的；desk：课桌；be stuck with：粘满；generations of：几代人的；discarded：丢弃的；chewing gum：口香糖

#### 长难句7

> But the headlong push into bachelor's degrees for all—and the subtle devaluing of anything less—misses an important point: That's not the only thing the American economy needs.

- 冒号后：`That's not the only thing the American economy needs`

##### 生词

While fossil fuels 化石燃料—coal, oil, gas—still generate 产生 roughly 大约 85 percent of the world's energy supply 能源供应, it's clearer than ever that the future belongs to 属于 renewable sources 可再生能源 such as wind and solar. While there's a long way to go, the trend lines for renewables are spiking 飙升. The pace of change in energy sources appears to be speeding up—perhaps just in time to have a meaningful effect in slowing climate change 减缓气候变化. What Washington does—or doesn't do—to promote alternative energy 替代能源 may mean less and less at a time of a global shift in thought 全球思想转变.

#### 长难句1

> While fossil fuels—coal, oil, gas—still generate roughly 85 percent of the world's energy supply, it's clearer than ever that the future belongs to renewable sources such as wind and solar.

##### 句子拆分

主干：**it's clearer than ever that …**
- it是形式主语，真正主语是that引导的主语从句
- `While fossil fuels—coal, oil, gas—still generate roughly 85 percent of the world's energy supply`：while让步状语从句
  - `—coal, oil, gas—`：插入语，举例fossil fuels
- that从句主干：**the future belongs to renewable sources such as wind and solar**

##### 生词

fossil fuels：化石燃料；generate：产生；roughly：大约；energy supply：能源供应；it's clearer than ever：比以往任何时候都更清楚；belong to：属于；renewable sources：可再生能源；such as：例如；wind and solar：风能和太阳能

##### 翻译

尽管化石燃料——煤、石油、天然气——仍产生大约世界85%的能源供应，但未来属于风能和太阳能等可再生能源，这一点比以往任何时候都更清楚。

##### 考点

**while让步状语从句+插入语+it形式主语+that主语从句**；开篇点题——可再生能源是未来趋势，全文主旨。

To combat 对抗 the trap of putting a premium on 重视 being busy, Cal Newport, author of Deep Work: Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World 《深度工作：在分心世界中专注成功的法则》, recommends building a habit of "deep work"—the ability to focus without distraction 不分心.

There are a number of approaches to mastering the art of deep work—be it lengthy retreats 长时间静修 dedicated to a specific task; developing a daily ritual 日常仪式; or taking a "journalistic" approach to seizing moments of deep work when you can throughout the day. Whichever approach, the key is to determine your length of focus time and stick to it 坚持.

Newport also recommends "deep scheduling 深度规划" to combat constant interruptions and get more done in less time. "At any given point, I should have deep work scheduled for roughly the next month. Once on the calendar I protect this time like I would a doctor's appointment or important meeting," he writes.

Another approach to getting more done in less time is to rethink how you prioritize 优先安排 your day—in particular how we craft our to-do lists 待办清单. Tim Harford, author of Messy: The Power of Disorder to Transform Our Lives 《混乱：改变我们生活的无序力量》, points to a study in the early 1980s that divided undergraduates 本科生 into two groups: some were advised to set out monthly goals and study activities; others were told to plan activities and goals in much more detail, day by day.

While the researchers assumed that the well-structured daily plans would be most effective when it came to the execution of tasks, they were wrong: the detailed daily plans demotivated 使失去动力 students. Harford argues that inevitable distractions often render 使 the daily to-do list ineffective, while leaving room for improvisation 即兴发挥 in such a list can reap 获得 the best results.

In order to make the most of our focus and energy, we also need to embrace 接受 downtime  downtime, or as Newport suggests, "be lazy."

"Idleness 闲散 is not just a vacation, an indulgence 放纵 or a vice 恶习; it is as indispensable 不可或缺的 to the brain as Vitamin D is to the body…[idleness] is, paradoxically 矛盾的是, necessary to getting any work done," he argues.

Srini Pillay, an assistant professor of psychiatry 精神病学助理教授 at Harvard Medical School, believes this counter-intuitive 反直觉的 link between downtime and productivity may be due to the way our brains operate. When our brains switch between being focused and unfocused on a task, they tend to be more efficient.

"What people don't realise is that in order to complete these tasks they need to use both the focus and unfocus circuits 回路 in their brain," says Pillay.

#### 37. The study in the early 1980s cited by Harford shows that ______.

✅答案：**D** 定位：第五段。`While the researchers assumed that the well-structured daily plans would be most effective…they were wrong: the detailed daily plans demotivated students`（尽管研究人员认为结构良好的日计划会最有效……但他们错了：详细的日计划使学生失去动力）。说明详细计划可能不如预期有效。

- A：错误。distractions may actually increase efficiency（分心实际上可能提高效率）偷换概念。原文说不可避免的分心使日计划无效，留即兴空间效果好，不是分心提高效率。
- B：错误。daily schedules are indispensable to studying（日计划对学习不可或缺）与原文相反。详细日计划使学生失去动力，不是不可或缺。
- C：错误。students are hardly motivated by monthly goals（学生几乎不受月度目标激励）无中生有。研究对比月度目标和详细日计划，没有说月度目标不激励学生。
- D：正确。详细计划可能不如预期有效。assumed daily plans would be most effective, but they were wrong, demotivated students = detailed plans may not be as fruitful as expected。

#### 38. According to Newport, idleness is ______.

[A] a desirable mental state for busy people [B] a major contributor to physical health [C] an effective way to save time and energy [D] an essential factor in accomplishing any work

✅答案：**D** 定位：第七段。`[idleness] is, paradoxically, necessary to getting any work done`（矛盾的是，闲散对完成任何工作都是必要的）。necessary to getting any work done = essential factor in accomplishing any work。

- A：错误。a desirable mental state for busy people（忙碌人群的理想心理状态）过度推断。Newport说闲散对大脑不可或缺、对完成工作必要，不是说对忙碌人群是理想心理状态。
- B：错误。a major contributor to physical health（身体健康的主要贡献者）偷换概念。原文说闲散对大脑就像维生素D对身体一样不可或缺，是类比，不是说对身体健康的贡献。
- C：错误。an effective way to save time and energy（节省时间和精力的有效方法）无中生有。
- D：正确。完成任何工作的必要因素。necessary to getting any work done = essential factor in accomplishing any work。

#### 39. Pillay believes that our brains' shift between being focused and unfocused ______.

[A] can result in psychological well-being [B] can bring about greater efficiency [C] is aimed at better balance in work [D] is driven by task urgency

✅答案：**B** 定位：第八段。`When our brains switch between being focused and unfocused on a task, they tend to be more efficient`（当我们的大脑在任务的专注和不专注之间切换时，它们往往更高效）。switch = shift，tend to be more efficient = can bring about greater efficiency。

- A：错误。can result in psychological well-being（能带来心理健康）无中生有。
- B：正确。能带来更高效率。switch between focused and unfocused → tend to be more efficient = shift can bring about greater efficiency。
- C：错误。is aimed at better balance in work（旨在更好地平衡工作）无中生有。
- D：错误。is driven by task urgency（由任务紧迫性驱动）无中生有。

#### 40. This text is mainly about ______.

[A] ways to relieve the tension of busy life [B] approaches to getting more done in less time [C] the key to eliminating distractions [D] the cause of the lack of focus time

✅答案：**B** 主旨题。全文主线：深度工作（专注不分心）→方法1：确定专注时间并坚持→方法2：深度规划→方法3：重新思考待办清单（详细日计划无效，留即兴空间）→闲散/休息对完成工作必要→大脑在专注和不专注间切换更高效。全文讲的是如何在更少时间内完成更多工作的方法。

- A：错误。ways to relieve the tension of busy life（缓解忙碌生活紧张的方法）以偏概全。文章讲的是高效工作方法，不是缓解紧张。
- B：正确。在更少时间内完成更多工作的方法。deep work + deep scheduling + rethink to-do lists + downtime = approaches to getting more done in less time。
- C：错误。the key to eliminating distractions（消除分心的关键）以偏概全。文章说深度工作是专注不分心，但也说分心不可避免、留即兴空间、大脑在专注和不专注间切换更高效，不是消除分心。
- D：错误。the cause of the lack of focus time（缺乏专注时间的原因）无中生有。文章讲方法，不是原因。

##### 本篇命题规律小结（英二阅读）

1. 工作/效率类文章：关注**问题（重视忙碌的陷阱）→方法1（深度工作：确定专注时间并坚持）→方法2（深度规划）→方法3（重新思考待办清单：详细日计划无效）→补充（闲散/休息必要）→科学解释（大脑切换更高效）**的结构
2. 细节题：定位到**the key is to**（determine your length of focus time and stick to it）
3. 实验结论题：定位到**while让步+冒号解释**（assumed…would be most effective, but they were wrong: demotivated students）
4. 人物观点题：定位到**直接引语中的paradoxically**（idleness is necessary to getting any work done）
5. 主旨题：把握**全文反复出现的主题词**（deep work, get more done in less time, approaches），不选以偏概全（eliminating distractions, relieve tension）

##### 新题型 Part B（41-45）

#### 题型说明

段落小标题匹配（7选5）。文章给出7个备选小标题（A-G），要求为5个编号段落（41-45）各选出最合适的小标题，多出2个干扰项。每题2分，共10分。

#### 原文

##### Five Ways to Make Conversation with Anyone

Conversations are links, which means when you have a conversation with a new person a link gets formed and every conversation you have after that moment will strengthen the link. You meet new people every day: the grocery worker, the cab driver, new people at work or the security guard at the door. Simply starting a conversation with them will form a link. Here are five simple ways that you can make the first move and start a conversation with strangers.

**41. ______**

Suppose you are in a room with someone you don't know and something within you says "I want to talk with this person" — this is something that mostly happens with all of us. You wanted to say something — the first word — but it just won't come out. It feels like it is stuck somewhere. I know the feeling and here is my advice: just get it out.

Just think: what is the worst that could happen? They won't talk with you? Well, they are not talking with you now! I truly believe that once you get that first word out everything else will just flow. So keep it simple: "Hi", "Hey" or "Hello" — do the best you can to gather all of the enthusiasm and energy you can, put on a big smile and say "Hi".

**42. ______**

It's a problem all of us face: you have limited time with the person that you want to talk with and you want to make this talk memorable.

Honestly, if we got stuck in the rut of "hi", "hello", "how are you?" and "what's going on?" you will fail to give the initial jolt to the conversation that's can make it so memorable. So don't be afraid to ask more personal questions. Trust me, you'll be surprised to see how much people are willing to share if you just ask.

**43. ______**

When you meet a person for the first time, make an effort to find the things which you and that person have in common so that you can build the conversation from that point. When you start conversation from there and then move outwards, you'll find all of a sudden that the conversation becomes a lot easier.

**44. ______**

Imagine you are pouring your heart out to someone and they are just busy on their phone, and if you ask for their attention you get the response "I can multitask". So when someone tries to communicate with you, just be in that communication wholeheartedly. Make eye contact, you can feel the conversation.

**45. ______**

You all came into a conversation where you first met the person, but after some time you may have met again and have forgotten their name. Isn't that awkward! So remember the little details of the people you met or you talked with; perhaps the places they have been to, the places they want to go, the things they like, the things they hate — whatever you talk about. When you remember such things you can automatically become investor in their wellbeing. So they feel a responsibility to you to keep that relationship going. That's it. Five amazing ways that you can make conversation with almost anyone. Every person is a really good book to read, or to have a conversation with!

##### 参考答案

#### 解析

**题型特点：** 段落小标题匹配题。文章主题为"与陌生人开启对话的五种方法"，每个段落介绍一种方法。解题核心是抓段落关键词原词复现和近义词复现。

**解题策略：**
1. 先读7个备选标题，划出核心词（say, present, compliment, name, me too, small talk, opinion）
2. 读段落重点看首句、尾句和举例内容
3. 通过原词复现或近义表达匹配

**逐题解析：**

- **41.A（Just say it）**：段中"just get it out"（说出来）和"once you get that first word out"（一旦你说出第一个词），反复强调"开口说"，与A选项Just say it完美对应。

- **42.F（Skip the small talk）**：段中"if we got stuck in the rut of 'hi', 'hello', 'how are you?'"（如果陷入"你好""最近怎样"的寒暄套路）和"So don't be afraid to ask more personal questions"（所以不要害怕问更私人的问题），强调跳过寒暄直接深入，与F选项Skip the small talk（跳过寒暄）完美对应。

- **43.E（Find the "me too"s）**：段中"find the things which you and that person have in common"（找到你和那个人的共同点），have in common=有共同点，与E选项Find the "me too"s（寻找"我也是"的共同点）完美对应。

- **44.B（Be present）**：段中"just be in that communication wholeheartedly"（全身心投入到对话中）和"Make eye contact"（进行眼神交流），强调专注当下，与B选项Be present（在场、全神贯注）对应。

- **45.D（Name, places, things）**：段中"forgotten their name"（忘记名字）和"the places they have been to, the things they like, the things they hate"（去过的地方、喜欢的事物、讨厌的事物），name, places, things原词复现，与D选项完美对应。

**干扰项排除：**
- [C] Pay a unique compliment：全文未提及给予赞美
- [G] Ask for an opinion：第42段提到"ask more personal questions"，但主旨是"跳过寒暄深入交流"而非"询问看法"

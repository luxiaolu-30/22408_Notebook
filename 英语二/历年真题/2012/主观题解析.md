## 详细解析

##### 翻译（15 分）

#### 原文

When people in developing countries worry about migration, they are usually concerned at the prospect of their best and brightest departure to Silicon Valley or to hospitals and universities in the developed world. These are the kind of workers that countries like Britain, Canada and Australia try to attract by using immigration rules that privilege college graduates.

Lots of studies have found that well-educated people from developing countries are particularly likely to emigrate. A big survey of Indian households in 2004 found that nearly 40% of emigrants had more than a high-school education, compared with around 3.3% of all Indians over the age of 25. This "brain drain" has long bothered policymakers in poor countries. They fear that it hurts their economies, depriving them of much-needed skilled workers who could have taught at their universities, worked in their hospitals and come up with clever new products for their factories to make.

#### 参考译文

发展中国家的人们为移民忧虑时，他们通常担忧的是本国最优秀、最聪明的人才前往硅谷或发达国家的医院和大学的前景。这些劳动者正是像英国、加拿大和澳大利亚这样的国家试图通过赋予大学毕业生特权的移民法规来吸引的人才。

许多研究发现，来自发展中国家的受过良好教育的人尤其可能移居国外。2004年对印度家庭进行的一项大规模调查发现，近40%的移民受过高中以上教育，而相比之下，在所有25岁以上的印度人中，这一比例仅为约3.3%。这种"人才流失"长期以来一直困扰着贫穷国家的决策者。他们担心这会损害本国经济，使其丧失急需的技术工人——这些工人本可以在本国的大学任教，在本国的医院工作，或者构想出精巧的新产品供本国工厂生产。

#### 重点词汇与句型

- **migration**：移民、迁移
- **be concerned at**：担忧、关注（concern at = be worried about）
- **prospect**：前景、前景展望
- **departure**：离开、出发（此处指人才外流）
- **privilege**：赋予特权、给予优惠（此处作动词）
- **emigrate**：移居国外、移民
- **brain drain**：人才流失（固定搭配，报刊常用词）
- **deprive...of...**：剥夺……的……/使丧失……（固定搭配）
- **much-needed**：急需的
- **come up with**：想出、提出、构想出（固定搭配）

#### 长难句分析

##### 长难句1

> When people in developing countries worry about migration, they are usually concerned at the prospect of their best and brightest departure to Silicon Valley or to hospitals and universities in the developed world.

**句子拆分**：
- 主干：**they are usually concerned at the prospect**
- `When people in developing countries worry about migration`：when引导的时间状语从句
- `of their best and brightest departure to Silicon Valley or to hospitals and universities in the developed world`：介词短语修饰prospect
  - `their best and brightest`：名词化用法，指"最优秀最聪明的人"
  - `departure to Silicon Valley or to hospitals and universities`：离开前往硅谷或医院和大学

**翻译**：发展中国家的人们为移民忧虑时，他们通常担忧的是本国最优秀、最聪明的人才前往硅谷或发达国家的医院和大学的前景。

##### 长难句2

> These are the kind of workers that countries like Britain, Canada and Australia try to attract by using immigration rules that privilege college graduates.

**句子拆分**：
- 主干：**These are the kind of workers**
- `that countries like Britain, Canada and Australia try to attract`：that引导的定语从句，修饰workers
  - `by using immigration rules`：方式状语
  - `that privilege college graduates`：that引导的定语从句，修饰immigration rules

**翻译**：这些劳动者正是像英国、加拿大和澳大利亚这样的国家试图通过赋予大学毕业生特权的移民法规来吸引的人才。

##### 长难句3

> They fear that it hurts their economies, depriving them of much-needed skilled workers who could have taught at their universities, worked in their hospitals and come up with clever new products for their factories to make.

**句子拆分**：
- 主干：**They fear that it hurts their economies**
- `that it hurts their economies`：that引导的宾语从句
- `depriving them of much-needed skilled workers`：现在分词短语作状语，表结果
- `who could have taught at their universities, worked in their hospitals and come up with clever new products for their factories to make`：who引导的定语从句，修饰skilled workers
  - 三个并列谓语：could have taught / worked / come up with
  - could have done：虚拟语气，表示本可以

**翻译**：他们担心这会损害本国经济，使其丧失急需的技术工人——这些工人本可以在本国的大学任教，在本国的医院工作，或者构想出精巧的新产品供本国工厂生产。

##### 小作文（10 分）

#### 题目

**47. Directions:**

Suppose you have found something wrong with the electronic dictionary you bought from a store. Write an email to the customer service center of the store to

1) make a complaint, and

2) give your suggestions on how to deal with the situation.

You should write about 100 words on ANSWER SHEET 2.

Do not sign your own name at the end of the letter. Use "Zhang Wei" instead.

Do not write your address. (10 points)

#### 高分范文

Dear Customer Service,

I am writing to express my dissatisfaction with an electronic dictionary I purchased from your online store last Friday. Upon receiving the product, I discovered several issues that need immediate attention.

First, the screen of the dictionary has a visible scratch on it, which clearly indicates that the product was not in brand-new condition when shipped. Second, some of the keys on the keyboard do not function properly — particularly the translation button, which fails to respond after being pressed multiple times. Moreover, the battery life is far shorter than advertised on your website.

Given these problems, I would appreciate it if you could either replace the dictionary with a new one or provide a full refund. I expect this matter to be resolved by next Friday. Thank you for your attention to this issue.

Yours sincerely,

Zhang Wei

#### 范文翻译

尊敬的客服中心：

我写信是为了表达对上周五在贵店网购的电子词典的不满。收到产品后，我发现了几个需要立即处理的问题。

首先，词典屏幕上有明显的划痕，这清楚地表明产品在发货时并非全新状态。其次，键盘上某些按键无法正常工作——特别是翻译键，多次按下后仍无反应。此外，电池续航时间远远短于贵网站所宣传的。

鉴于以上问题，如果您能更换一部新词典或全额退款，我将不胜感激。我希望此事能在下周五前得到解决。感谢您对此事的关注。

您真诚的，

张伟

#### 重点句型与表达

1. **I am writing to express my dissatisfaction with...**（我写信是为了表达对……的不满）——投诉信开头句
2. **Upon receiving the product, I discovered several issues that need immediate attention.**（收到产品后，我发现了几个需要立即处理的问题。）——描述发现问题
3. **First, ... has a visible scratch on it, which clearly indicates that...**（首先，……上有明显的划痕，这清楚地表明……）——具体投诉1
4. **Moreover, ... is far shorter than advertised.**（此外，……远远短于所宣传的。）——具体投诉2
5. **Given these problems, I would appreciate it if you could either... or...**（鉴于以上问题，如果您能……或……，我将不胜感激。）——提出解决方案
6. **I expect this matter to be resolved by...**（我希望此事能在……前得到解决。）——设定期限

##### 大作文（15 分）

#### 图表描述

本题为**表格（Table）**，标题为"某公司员工工作满意度调查"。表格按年龄段分为三组，分别统计了"满意"、"不清楚"和"不满意"三个选项的百分比。

主要数据：
- **≤40岁**：满意16.7%，不清楚50.0%，不满意33.3%
- **41-50岁**：满意0.0%，不清楚36.0%，不满意64.0%（满意度最低）
- **>50岁**：满意40.0%，不清楚50.0%，不满意10.0%（满意度最高）

核心趋势：年龄越大，工作满意度越高；41-50岁人群满意度最低，不满意率高达64%。

#### 题目

**48. Directions:**

Write an essay based on the following table. In your writing you should

1) describe the table, and

2) give your comments.

You should write at least 150 words. (15 points)

#### 高分范文

The table above reveals an overall picture of job satisfaction among employees of different age groups. As is shown, among employees aged 40 or under, only 16.7% are satisfied with their jobs, while 50.0% feel unclear and 33.3% are dissatisfied. The situation is even worse for those between 41 and 50, where 64.0% express dissatisfaction and none report being satisfied. By contrast, among employees over 50, the satisfaction rate reaches 40.0%, the highest of all three groups, with only 10.0% feeling dissatisfied.

Several factors may account for this disparity. First and foremost, employees between 41 and 50 face the greatest pressure, as they often shoulder the dual burden of supporting both young children and aging parents. This heavy financial and emotional load leaves them little room for job satisfaction. Secondly, younger employees under 40 may feel uncertain about their career paths, leading to a high percentage of "unclear" responses. Finally, employees over 50 tend to have achieved career stability and accumulated sufficient experience, allowing them to approach their work with a more positive and relaxed mindset.

To sum up, job satisfaction varies significantly across age groups, largely due to differences in life stages and associated pressures. Employers should pay closer attention to the well-being of middle-aged employees and take measures such as offering flexible working hours and improving welfare benefits to enhance overall job satisfaction.

#### 范文翻译

上面的表格展示了不同年龄段员工的工作满意度总体情况。如图所示，在40岁及以下的员工中，仅有16.7%对工作满意，50.0%表示不清楚，33.3%不满意。41至50岁员工的情况更糟，64.0%表示不满意，且无人表示满意。相比之下，50岁以上员工的满意率高达40.0%，为三组中最高，仅10.0%不满意。

以下几个因素可以解释这种差异。首先，41至50岁的员工面临最大的压力，因为他们通常肩负着同时抚养年幼子女和赡养年迈父母的双重负担。这种沉重的经济和情感压力使他们几乎没有空间去感受工作满意度。其次，40岁以下的年轻员工可能对职业发展道路感到迷茫，导致"不清楚"的比例较高。最后，50岁以上的员工往往已经实现了职业稳定并积累了丰富的经验，使他们能以更积极、更轻松的心态对待工作。

总之，工作满意度在不同年龄段间差异显著，这主要由于人生阶段和相应压力的不同。雇主应更加关注中年员工的身心健康，并采取措施如提供弹性工作时间和改善福利待遇，以提升整体工作满意度。

#### 重点句型与表达

1. **The table above reveals an overall picture of... among... of different age groups.**（上面的表格展示了不同年龄段……的总体情况。）——表格描述开头句
2. **As is shown, among..., only...% are satisfied with..., while...% feel unclear and...% are dissatisfied.**（如图所示，在……中，仅……%对……满意，而……%表示不清楚，……%不满意。）——数据描述句
3. **The situation is even worse for..., where...% express dissatisfaction.**（……的情况更糟，……%表示不满意。）——对比描述
4. **Several factors may account for this disparity. First and foremost, ...**（以下几个因素可以解释这种差异。首先……）——原因分析过渡句
5. **This heavy... load leaves them little room for...**（这种沉重的……负担使他们几乎没有空间去……）——解释原因
6. **To sum up, ... varies significantly across..., largely due to...**（总之，……在不同……间差异显著，主要由于……）——总结句
7. **Employers should pay closer attention to... and take measures such as... to enhance...**（雇主应更加关注……并采取措施如……以提升……）——建议结尾句

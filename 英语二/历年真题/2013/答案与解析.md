#### 重点解析

- **第1题（A）**：逻辑连词。前句说"你可能会认为我们会迅速进入无现金社会"，后句说"真正的无现金社会可能不会马上到来"，前后为转折关系，However（然而）正确。
- **第3题（B）**：词义辨析。revolutionize the concept of money itself意为"革新货币本身的概念"，concept最符合语境。power（力量）、history（历史）、role（角色）不如concept贴切。
- **第4题（D）**：词义辨析。only to reverse itself意为"结果几年后却推翻了自己（的预测）"，reverse（反转、推翻）符合"only to + 动词"表出乎意料结果的用法。
- **第6题（B）**：固定搭配。work against意为"阻碍、不利于"，"several factors work against the disappearance of the paper system"即"几个因素阻碍了纸质系统的消失"。
- **第7题（B）**：词义辨析。expensive意为"昂贵的"，"it is very expensive to set up the computer..."即建立计算机网络成本很高，符合逻辑。
- **第8题（D）**：词义辨析。dominant意为"占主导地位的"，"make electronic money the dominant form of payment"即使电子货币成为主要支付方式，符合语境。
- **第10题（A）**：词组辨析。give up意为"放弃"，"many consumers are unwilling to give up"即许多消费者不愿放弃（收据），符合逻辑。
- **第13题（C）**：逻辑连词。Because表因果关系，"Because electronic payments are immediate, they eliminate the float"即"因为电子支付是即时的，所以消除了浮动期"。Though表让步，不符合因果逻辑。
- **第14题（C）**：词义辨析。raise concerns为固定搭配，意为"引发担忧"，"electronic payments may raise security and privacy concerns"即电子支付可能引发安全和隐私担忧。
- **第20题（D）**：词义辨析。trail意为"痕迹、踪迹"，"leaves an electronic trail"即留下电子痕迹，指消费记录。chunk（大块）、chip（芯片）、path（路径）均不符。

In an essay entitled 题为 "Making It in America," the author Adam Davidson relates 讲述 a joke from cotton country 产棉区 about just how much a modern textile mill 纺织厂 has been automated 自动化: The average mill has only two employees today, "a man and a dog. The man is there to feed the dog, and the dog is there to keep the man away from the machines."

Davidson's article is one of a number of pieces 文章 that have recently appeared making the point 表明观点 that the reason we have such stubbornly 顽固地 high unemployment and declining middle-class incomes today is largely because of the big drop in demand because of the Great Recession 大萧条, but it is also because of the advances in both globalization 全球化 and the information technology revolution, which are more rapidly than ever replacing labor with machines or foreign workers.

In the past, workers with average skills, doing an average job, could earn an average lifestyle. But, today, average is officially 正式地 over. Being average just won't earn you what it used to. It can't when so many more employers have so much more access to 获得 so much more above average cheap foreign labor, cheap robotics 机器人技术, cheap software, cheap automation and cheap genius 天才. Therefore, everyone needs to find their extra—their unique value contribution 独特价值贡献 that makes them stand out 脱颖而出 in whatever is their field of employment.

Yes, new technology has been eating jobs 吞噬工作 forever, and always will. But there's been an acceleration 加速. As Davidson notes, "In the 10 years ending in 2009, [U.S.] factories shed 裁减 workers so fast that they erased 抹去 almost all the gains of the previous 70 years; roughly one out of every three manufacturing jobs—about 6 million in total—disappeared."

There will always be change—new jobs, new products, new services. But the one thing we know for sure 确定地 is that with each advance in globalization and the I.T. revolution, the best jobs will require workers to have more and better education to make themselves above average.

In a world where average is officially over, there are many things we need to do to support employment, but nothing would be more important than passing some kind of G.I. Bill for the 21st century that ensures that every American has access to post-high school education 高中后教育.

##### Text1（21-25）

#### 长难句1

> In an essay entitled "Making It in America," the author Adam Davidson relates a joke from cotton country about just how much a modern textile mill has been automated.

##### 句子拆分

主干：**the author Adam Davidson relates a joke**
- `In an essay entitled "Making It in America"`：状语
  - `entitled "Making It in America"`：过去分词作后置定语，修饰essay
- `from cotton country`：介词短语修饰joke
- `about just how much a modern textile mill has been automated`：介词短语修饰joke
  - `how much a modern textile mill has been automated`：how引导宾语从句

##### 生词

entitle：题为；relate：讲述；cotton country：产棉区；textile mill：纺织厂；automate：自动化

##### 翻译

在一篇题为《在美国成功》的文章中，作者亚当·戴维森讲述了一个来自产棉区的笑话，关于现代纺织厂自动化程度有多高：如今普通工厂只有两名员工，"一个人和一条狗。人在那里喂狗，狗在那里让人远离机器。"

##### 考点

**过去分词entitled作后置定语**；how宾语从句；开篇用笑话引入主题，21题例证题定位——笑话用来说明技术进步的影响。

#### 长难句2

> Davidson's article is one of a number of pieces that have recently appeared making the point that the reason we have such stubbornly high unemployment and declining middle-class incomes today is largely because of the big drop in demand because of the Great Recession, but it is also because of the advances in both globalization and the information technology revolution, which are more rapidly than ever replacing labor with machines or foreign workers.

##### 句子拆分

主干：**Davidson's article is one of a number of pieces**
- `that have recently appeared making the point`：that定语从句，修饰pieces
  - `making the point`：现在分词作伴随状语
- `that the reason … is largely because of …, but it is also because of …`：that同位语从句，解释point
  - 同位语从句是but连接的并列句
  - 分句1：`the reason we have such stubbornly high unemployment and declining middle-class incomes today is largely because of the big drop in demand because of the Great Recession`
    - `we have such … incomes today`：省略why的定语从句，修饰reason
  - 分句2：`it is also because of the advances in both globalization and the information technology revolution, which are more rapidly than ever replacing labor with machines or foreign workers`
    - `which are more rapidly than ever replacing labor with machines or foreign workers`：which非限制性定语从句，修饰advances

##### 生词

piece：文章；make the point：表明观点；stubbornly：顽固地、难以消除地；unemployment：失业；declining：下降的；middle-class：中产阶级的；demand：需求；Great Recession：大萧条；globalization：全球化；replace A with B：用B取代A；labor：劳动力

##### 翻译

戴维森的文章是最近出现的众多文章之一，这些文章表明这样一个观点：我们今天失业率如此居高不下、中产阶级收入下降，很大程度上是因为大萧条导致的需求大幅下降，但也因为全球化和信息技术革命的进步，它们正以比以往更快的速度用机器或外国工人取代劳动力。

##### 考点

**超长复合句**：定语从句+同位语从句+并列句+非限制性定语从句多层嵌套；全文核心观点句——高失业的双重原因（大萧条+技术/全球化）。

#### 长难句3

> In the past, workers with average skills, doing an average job, could earn an average lifestyle.

##### 句子拆分

主干：**workers could earn an average lifestyle**
- `with average skills`：介词短语修饰workers
- `doing an average job`：现在分词作后置定语，修饰workers
- `In the past`：时间状语

##### 生词

average skills：普通技能；average lifestyle：普通生活水平

##### 翻译

过去，拥有普通技能、做普通工作的工人，可以过上普通水平的生活。

##### 考点

#### 长难句4

> It can't when so many more employers have so much more access to so much more above average cheap foreign labor, cheap robotics, cheap software, cheap automation and cheap genius.

##### 句子拆分

主干：**It can't**（省略句，完整为It can't earn you what it used to）
- `when so many more employers have so much more access to so much more above average cheap foreign labor, cheap robotics, cheap software, cheap automation and cheap genius`：when时间/原因状语从句
  - 五个并列名词短语作access to的宾语

##### 生词

have access to：获得、使用；above average：高于平均水平的；robotics：机器人技术；automation：自动化；genius：天才

##### 翻译

当更多雇主能获得更多高于平均水平的廉价外国劳动力、廉价机器人技术、廉价软件、廉价自动化和廉价天才时，平庸就不可能再让你过上过去的生活。

##### 考点

省略句It can't；when引导原因状语从句；五个cheap并列，强调技术和全球化使各种资源变得廉价；22题D选项的逻辑铺垫——平庸不行了，需要独特价值。

#### 长难句5

> Therefore, everyone needs to find their extra—their unique value contribution that makes them stand out in whatever is their field of employment.

##### 句子拆分

主干：**everyone needs to find their extra**
- `their unique value contribution`：同位语，解释extra
- `that makes them stand out in whatever is their field of employment`：that定语从句，修饰contribution
  - `whatever is their field of employment`：whatever引导宾语从句，作in的宾语

##### 生词

extra：额外的东西、特长；unique value contribution：独特价值贡献；stand out：脱颖而出；field of employment：就业领域

##### 翻译

因此，每个人都需要找到自己的额外价值——那种让他们在任何就业领域都脱颖而出的独特价值贡献。

##### 考点

**同位语+定语从句+whatever宾语从句**；22题D选项定位句——成功员工需要贡献独特的东西。

#### 长难句6

> As Davidson notes, "In the 10 years ending in 2009, [U.S.] factories shed workers so fast that they erased almost all the gains of the previous 70 years; roughly one out of every three manufacturing jobs—about 6 million in total—disappeared."

##### 句子拆分

主干：**As Davidson notes, "…"**
- As引导非限制性定语从句，"正如戴维森指出的"
- 直接引语内是分号连接的并列句：
  - 分句1：`[U.S.] factories shed workers so fast that they erased almost all the gains of the previous 70 years`
    - `so … that …`：如此…以至于…
  - 分句2：`roughly one out of every three manufacturing jobs—about 6 million in total—disappeared`
    - 破折号插入语

##### 生词

note：指出；shed workers：裁员；erase：抹去、消除；gain：增长、成果；previous：之前的；roughly：大约；manufacturing job：制造业工作；in total：总共

##### 翻译

正如戴维森指出的："在截至2009年的10年里，（美国）工厂裁员速度如此之快，以至于抹去了之前70年几乎所有的增长成果；大约每三个制造业工作中就有一个——总共约600万个——消失了。"

##### 考点

**so…that…** 结果状语从句；分号连接并列句；破折号插入语；23题B选项定位句——工作机会正在高速消失。

#### 长难句7

> But the one thing we know for sure is that with each advance in globalization and the I.T. revolution, the best jobs will require workers to have more and better education to make themselves above average.

##### 句子拆分

主干：**the one thing is that …**
- `we know for sure`：省略that的定语从句，修饰thing
- that引导表语从句，从句主干：**the best jobs will require workers to have more and better education**
  - `with each advance in globalization and the I.T. revolution`：with短语作状语
  - `to make themselves above average`：不定式作目的状语

##### 生词

for sure：确定地；advance：进步；require：要求；above average：高于平均水平

##### 翻译

但我们确定知道的一件事是，随着全球化和信息技术革命的每一次进步，最好的工作将要求工人接受更多更好的教育，以使自己高于平均水平。

##### 考点

**定语从句省略that**；that表语从句；with短语作状语；24题B选项依据——减少失业最重要的是确保人们接受更多教育。

#### 长难句8

> In a world where average is officially over, there are many things we need to do to support employment, but nothing would be more important than passing some kind of G.I. Bill for the 21st century that ensures that every American has access to post-high school education.

##### 句子拆分

并列句，but连接
分句1主干：**there are many things**
- `In a world where average is officially over`：地点状语
  - `where average is officially over`：where定语从句，修饰world
- `we need to do to support employment`：省略that的定语从句，修饰things

分句2主干：**nothing would be more important than passing some kind of G.I. Bill**
- `for the 21st century`：状语
- `that ensures that every American has access to post-high school education`：that定语从句，修饰G.I. Bill
  - `that every American has access to post-high school education`：that宾语从句，作ensures的宾语

##### 生词

officially：正式地；support employment：支持就业；G.I. Bill：退伍军人权利法案（泛指教育法案）；ensure：确保；have access to：获得；post-high school education：高中后教育

##### 翻译

在一个平庸正式终结的世界里，我们需要做很多事情来支持就业，但没有什么比通过某种21世纪的《退伍军人权利法案》更重要了，该法案确保每个美国人都能获得高中后教育。

##### 考点

**where定语从句+省略that的定语从句+that定语从句+that宾语从句**多层嵌套；全文结尾结论句，24题和25题的核心依据。

##### Text1（21-25）

> 原文主题：技术进步和全球化使"平庸"不再够用，每个人需要独特价值和更多教育

#### 21. The joke in Paragraph 1 is used to illustrate ______.

[A] the impact of technological advances [B] the alleviation of job pressure [C] the shrinkage of textile mills [D] the decline of middle-class incomes

✅答案：**A** 例证题。定位：第一段笑话讲现代纺织厂高度自动化，只有一人一狗。第二段紧接着说高失业的原因之一是"advances in both globalization and the information technology revolution, which are more rapidly than ever replacing labor with machines"。笑话用来说明技术进步的影响。

- A：正确。技术进步的影响。自动化=技术进步，取代工人=影响。
- B：错误。the alleviation of job pressure（工作压力缓解）与原文相反。自动化导致失业，不是缓解压力。
- C：错误。the shrinkage of textile mills（纺织厂萎缩）表面化。笑话不是说工厂萎缩，而是说自动化程度高。
- D：错误。the decline of middle-class incomes（中产阶级收入下降）是第二段提到的现象，但笑话直接说明的是技术进步，不是收入下降。

#### 22. According to Paragraph 3, to be a successful employee, one has to ______.

[A] work on cheap software [B] ask for a moderate salary [C] adopt an average lifestyle [D] contribute something unique

✅答案：**D** 定位：第三段 `everyone needs to find their extra—their unique value contribution that makes them stand out in whatever is their field of employment`

每个人需要找到独特价值贡献，即贡献独特的东西。

- A：错误。work on cheap software（从事廉价软件工作）曲解原文。cheap software是雇主可获得的资源，不是员工要做的事。
- B：错误。ask for a moderate salary（要求适度薪水）无中生有。
- C：错误。adopt an average lifestyle（过普通生活方式）与原文相反。原文说average is over，平庸不行了。
- D：正确。贡献独特的东西。unique value contribution = contribute something unique。

A century ago, the immigrants from across the Atlantic included settlers 定居者 and sojourners 旅居者. Along with the many folks looking to make a permanent home in the United States came those who had no intention to stay, and who would make some money and then go home. Between 1908 and 1915, about 7 million people arrived while about 2 million departed 离开. About a quarter of all Italian immigrants, for example, eventually returned to Italy for good 永久地. They even had an affectionate 深情的 nickname, "uccelli di passaggio," birds of passage 候鸟.

Today, we are much more rigid 僵化的 about immigrants. We divide newcomers into two categories: legal or illegal, good or bad. We hail 称赞 them as Americans in the making, or brand 打上烙印 them as aliens 外国人 to be kicked out. That framework 框架 has contributed mightily 极大地 to our broken immigration system and the long political paralysis 瘫痪 over how to fix it. We don't need more categories, but we need to change the way we think about categories. We need to look beyond 超越 strict definitions of legal and illegal. To start, we can recognize the new birds of passage, those living and thriving 繁荣 in the gray areas 灰色地带. We might then begin to solve our immigration challenges.

Crop pickers 采棉工, violinists 小提琴手, construction workers, entrepreneurs 企业家, engineers, home health-care aides 家庭医疗助手 and physicists are among today's birds of passage. They are energetic participants in a global economy driven by the flow of work, money and ideas. They prefer to come and go as opportunity calls them. They can manage to have a job in one place and a family in another.

With or without permission, they straddle 跨越 laws, jurisdictions 司法管辖区 and identities with ease 轻松地. We need them to imagine the United States as a place where they can be productive 多产的 for a while without committing themselves to 承诺 staying forever. We need them to feel that home can be both here and there and that they can belong to two nations honorably 光荣地.

Accommodating 适应 this new world of people in motion 流动中 will require new attitudes on both sides of the immigration battle. Looking beyond the culture war logic of right or wrong means opening up the middle ground 中间地带 and understanding that managing immigration today requires multiple paths and multiple outcomes, including some that are not easy to accomplish legally in the existing system.

Scientists have found that although we are prone to 易于 snap overreactions 快速过度反应, if we take a moment and think about how we are likely to react, we can reduce or even eliminate 消除 the negative effects of our quick, hard-wired 天生的 responses.

Snap decisions can be important defense mechanisms 防御机制; if we are judging whether someone is dangerous, our brains and bodies are hard-wired to react very quickly, within milliseconds 毫秒. But we need more time to assess other factors. To accurately tell whether someone is sociable 善于交际的, studies show, we need at least a minute, preferably 最好 five. It takes a while to judge complex aspects of personality, like neuroticism 神经质 or open-mindedness 开明.

But snap decisions in reaction to rapid stimuli 刺激 aren't exclusive to 仅限于 the interpersonal realm 人际领域. Psychologists at the University of Toronto found that viewing a fast-food logo 标志 for just a few milliseconds primes 促使 us to read 20 percent faster, even though reading has little to do with eating. We unconsciously 无意识地 associate fast food with speed and impatience and carry those impulses 冲动 into whatever else we're doing. Subjects exposed to 暴露于 fast-food flashes also tend to think a musical piece lasts too long.

Yet we can reverse 逆转 such influences. If we know we will overreact to consumer products or housing options when we see a happy face (one reason good sales representatives and real estate agents 房地产经纪人 are always smiling), we can take a moment before buying. If we know female job screeners 筛选者 are more likely to reject attractive female applicants, we can help screeners understand their biases 偏见—or hire outside screeners.

John Gottman, the marriage expert, explains that we quickly "thin slice 薄片切割" information reliably only after we ground 基于 such snap reactions in "thick sliced 厚片切割" long-term study. When Dr. Gottman really wants to assess whether a couple will stay together, he invites them to his island retreat 静修处 for a much longer evaluation: two days, not two seconds.

Our ability to mute 减弱 our hard-wired reactions by pausing is what differentiates us from animals: dogs can think about the future only intermittently 间歇地 or for a few minutes. But historically we have spent about 12 percent of our days contemplating 沉思 the longer term. Although technology might change the way we react, it hasn't changed our nature. We still have the imaginative capacity 想象力 to rise above 超越 temptation 诱惑 and reverse the high-speed trend.

##### 本篇命题规律小结（英二阅读）

1. 心理学类文章：关注**实验/研究发现**（多伦多大学实验、Gottman研究），答案常在研究结论中
2. 推理题：基于**对比关系**（危险判断毫秒级vs交际判断需一分钟）推理
3. 例证题：例子（快餐标志）说明的观点在**例子前后**（associate机制）
4. 方法题（should）：定位到**if条件句+can take a moment**等建议性表达
5. 态度题：看**尾段褒义词**（still have the capacity, differentiate us from animals）→乐观

Europe is not a gender-equality 性别平等 heaven. In particular, the corporate workplace 企业职场 will never be completely family-friendly 家庭友好的 until women are part of senior management 高级管理层 decisions, and Europe's top corporate-governance 公司治理 positions remain overwhelmingly 压倒性地 male. Indeed, women hold only 14 percent of positions on European corporate boards 董事会.

The European Union is now considering legislation 立法 to compel 迫使 corporate boards to maintain a certain proportion 比例 of women—up to 60 percent. This proposed mandate 拟议的命令 was born of frustration 挫败感. Last year, European Commission Vice President 副主席 Viviane Reding issued a call to voluntary action 自愿行动. Reding invited corporations to sign up for 报名参加 gender balance goals of 40 percent female board membership. But her appeal 呼吁 was considered a failure: only 24 companies took it up 接受.

Do we need quotas 配额 to ensure that women can continue to climb the corporate ladder 企业晋升阶梯 fairly as they balance work and family?

"Personally, I don't like quotas," Reding said recently. "But I like what the quotas do." Quotas get action: they "open the way to equality and they break through the glass ceiling 玻璃天花板," according to Reding, a result seen in France and other countries with legally binding 有法律约束力的 provisions 条款 on placing women in top business positions.

I understand Reding's reluctance 不情愿—and her frustration. I don't like quotas either; they run counter to 违背 my belief in meritocracy 精英治理, governance by the capable. But, when one considers the obstacles 障碍 to achieving the meritocratic ideal, it does look as if a fairer world must be temporarily ordered 命令.

After all, four decades of evidence has now shown that corporations in Europe as well as the US are evading 逃避 the meritocratic hiring and promotion of women to top positions—no matter how much "soft pressure 软压力" is put upon them. When women do break through to the summit 顶峰 of corporate power—as, for example, Sheryl Sandberg recently did at Facebook—they attract massive attention precisely 正是 because they remain the exception to the rule.

If appropriate pubic policies 公共政策 were in place 到位 to help all women—whether CEOs or their children's caregivers 照顾者—and all families, Sandberg would be no more newsworthy 有新闻价值的 than any other highly capable person living in a more just society.

- does表强调

##### 生词

obstacle：障碍；meritocratic ideal：精英治理理想；fairer：更公平的；temporarily：暂时地；order：命令、规定

##### 翻译

但是，当考虑到实现精英治理理想的障碍时，看起来确实一个更公平的世界必须暂时被命令规定。

##### 考点

**when状语从句+as if表语从句**；does强调；39题D选项依据——作者虽然不喜欢配额，但认为暂时需要配额来实现更公平的世界，即支持Reding的呼吁。

---

#### 长难句7

> After all, four decades of evidence has now shown that corporations in Europe as well as the US are evading the meritocratic hiring and promotion of women to top positions—no matter how much "soft pressure" is put upon them.

##### 句子拆分

主干：**four decades of evidence has now shown that …**
- that引导宾语从句，从句主干：**corporations are evading the meritocratic hiring and promotion of women to top positions**
  - `in Europe as well as the US`：介词短语修饰corporations
- 破折号后`no matter how much "soft pressure" is put upon them`：no matter how引导让步状语从句

##### 生词

after all：毕竟；decade：十年；evidence：证据；evade：逃避；meritocratic hiring：精英式招聘；promotion：晋升；top position：高级职位；soft pressure：软压力；put upon：施加于

##### 翻译

毕竟，四十年的证据现在表明，无论施加多少"软压力"，欧洲和美国的公司都在逃避以精英方式招聘和晋升女性到高级职位。

##### 考点

**that宾语从句+no matter how让步状语从句**；40题C选项依据——软压力没用，需要合适的公共政策。

---

#### 长难句8

> When women do break through to the summit of corporate power—as, for example, Sheryl Sandberg recently did at Facebook—they attract massive attention precisely because they remain the exception to the rule.

##### 句子拆分

主干：**they attract massive attention**
- `When women do break through to the summit of corporate power`：when时间状语从句
  - do表强调
- 破折号插入`as, for example, Sheryl Sandberg recently did at Facebook`：as引导方式状语从句举例
- `precisely because they remain the exception to the rule`：because原因状语从句

##### 生词

break through：突破；summit：顶峰；corporate power：企业权力；massive attention：大量关注；precisely：正是；exception to the rule：规则的例外

##### 翻译

当女性确实突破到企业权力的顶峰时——例如谢丽尔·桑德伯格最近在Facebook所做的——她们吸引了大量关注，正是因为她们仍然是规则的例外。

##### 考点

**when时间状语从句+as方式状语从句举例+because原因状语从句**；do强调；40题的现象——女性进入高层成为新闻，原因是缺乏合适的公共政策。

---

#### 长难句9

> If appropriate pubic policies were in place to help all women—whether CEOs or their children's caregivers—and all families, Sandberg would be no more newsworthy than any other highly capable person living in a more just society.

##### 句子拆分

主干：**Sandberg would be no more newsworthy than any other highly capable person**
- `If appropriate pubic policies were in place to help all women … and all families`：if虚拟条件句（对现在的虚拟）
  - `whether CEOs or their children's caregivers`：插入语，解释all women
- `living in a more just society`：现在分词作后置定语，修饰person
- `no more … than …`：和…一样不…

##### 生词

appropriate：合适的；public policy：公共政策；be in place：到位；caregiver：照顾者；newsworthy：有新闻价值的；highly capable：非常有能力的；just：公正的

##### 翻译

如果有合适的公共政策来帮助所有女性——无论是CEO还是她们孩子的照顾者——和所有家庭，桑德伯格就不会比生活在一个更公正社会中的任何其他非常有能力的人更有新闻价值。

##### 考点

**if虚拟条件句**（对现在的虚拟）；no more…than比较结构；现在分词作后置定语；全文结论句，40题C选项的核心依据——女性进入高层成为新闻是因为缺乏合适的公共政策。

##### Text4（36-40）

> 原文主题：欧洲企业董事会性别比例失衡，欧盟考虑立法强制配额，作者虽不喜欢配额但支持暂时使用

#### 36. In the European corporate workplace, generally ______.

[A] women take the lead [B] men have the final say [C] corporate governance is overwhelmed [D] senior management is family-friendly

✅答案：**B** 定位：第一段。`Europe's top corporate-governance positions remain overwhelmingly male` + `women hold only 14 percent of positions on European corporate boards`。男性占压倒性多数，即男性有最终决定权。

- A：错误。women take the lead（女性带头）与原文相反。女性只占14%。
- B：正确。男性有最终决定权。overwhelmingly male = men have the final say。
- C：错误。corporate governance is overwhelmed（公司治理被压垮）偷换概念。overwhelmingly修饰的是male（男性占压倒性多数），不是公司治理被压垮。
- D：错误。senior management is family-friendly（高级管理层家庭友好）与原文相反。原文说"will never be completely family-friendly until women are part of senior management"。

---

#### 37. The European Union's intended legislation is ______.

[A] a reflection of gender balance [B] a reluctant choice [C] a response to Reding's call [D] a voluntary action

✅答案：**B** 定位：第二段。`This proposed mandate was born of frustration`（拟议的命令源于挫败感），因为Reding的自愿行动呼吁失败了（only 24 companies took it up），所以才考虑立法。说明立法是无奈之举。

- A：错误。a reflection of gender balance（性别平衡的反映）与原文相反。立法是为了实现性别平衡，不是性别平衡的反映（目前性别不平衡）。
- B：正确。无奈的选择。born of frustration + 自愿行动失败→reluctant choice。
- C：错误。a response to Reding's call（对Reding呼吁的回应）曲解原文。立法是因为Reding的呼吁失败了，不是对呼吁的回应。
- D：错误。a voluntary action（自愿行动）与原文相反。立法是强制性的，自愿行动已经失败了。

---

#### 38. According to Reding, quotas may help women ______.

[A] get top business positions [B] see through the glass ceiling [C] balance work and family [D] anticipate legal results

✅答案：**A** 定位：第四段。Quotas "open the way to equality and they break through the glass ceiling" + "a result seen in France and other countries with legally binding provisions on placing women in top business positions"。配额帮助女性获得高级商业职位。

- A：正确。获得高级商业职位。break through the glass ceiling + placing women in top business positions = get top business positions。
- B：错误。see through the glass ceiling（看穿玻璃天花板）偷换概念。原文是break through（突破）玻璃天花板，不是see through（看穿）。
- C：错误。balance work and family（平衡工作和家庭）是第三段的问题，不是配额帮助的内容。
- D：错误。anticipate legal results（预期法律结果）无中生有。

---

#### 39. The author's attitude toward Reding's appeal is one of ______.

[A] skepticism [B] objectiveness [C] indifference [D] approval

✅答案：**D** 态度题。定位：第五段。作者说"I understand Reding's reluctance—and her frustration"（我理解Reding的不情愿和挫败感），"I don't like quotas either"（我也不喜欢配额），但"it does look as if a fairer world must be temporarily ordered"（看起来更公平的世界必须暂时被命令规定）。作者虽然不喜欢配额，但认为暂时需要配额，即支持Reding的呼吁。

- A：错误。skepticism（怀疑）与作者态度相反。作者最终支持配额。
- B：错误。objectiveness（客观）。作者有明确立场（支持暂时使用配额），不是纯粹客观。
- C：错误。indifference（冷漠）与作者态度相反。作者明确表达了理解和支持。
- D：正确。approval（支持）。作者理解Reding，认为暂时需要配额来实现公平世界。

---

#### 40. Women entering top management become headlines due to lack of ______.

[A] more social justice [B] massive media attention [C] suitable public policies [D] greater "soft pressure"

✅答案：**C** 定位：最后一段。`If appropriate pubic policies were in place to help all women … Sandberg would be no more newsworthy than any other highly capable person`。如果有合适的公共政策，女性进入高层就不会成为新闻。反推：女性进入高层成为新闻是因为缺乏合适的公共政策。

- A：错误。more social justice（更多社会公正）偷换概念。原文说的是"living in a more just society"（更公正的社会），但直接原因是缺乏公共政策，不是缺乏社会公正。
- B：错误。massive media attention（大量媒体关注）是结果，不是原因。女性成为新闻是因为吸引了媒体关注，不是因为缺乏媒体关注。
- C：正确。合适的公共政策。if appropriate public policies were in place → no more newsworthy，反推lack of suitable public policies → become headlines。
- D：错误。greater "soft pressure"（更大的软压力）与原文相反。第六段说"no matter how much 'soft pressure' is put upon them"，软压力没用，不是缺乏软压力。

---

##### 本篇命题规律小结（英二阅读）

1. 社会政策类文章：关注**问题→自愿行动失败→立法强制→作者态度（虽不喜欢但支持）**的结构
2. 细节题：定位到**数据和事实**（14%女性、24家公司接受呼吁）
3. 态度题：注意**先让步后转折**（I don't like quotas either, But…must be temporarily ordered），转折后是真实态度
4. 原因题（due to）：答案常是**尾段if虚拟句的反推**（如果有政策就不会成为新闻→因为缺乏政策）
5. 常见干扰：偷换概念（break through→see through，overwhelmingly male→governance overwhelmed）、与原文相反（voluntary→mandatory）

##### 新题型 Part B（41-45）

#### 题型说明

**段落小标题匹配（7选5）**：阅读一篇关于如何省钱饮食（frugal eating）的文章，为5个指定段落选择最合适的小标题。右栏有7个选项（A-G），其中2个为干扰项。此题型要求考生概括段落主旨。

#### 原文

The following are tips for eating well on a budget.

**[41]** Impulsive spending isn't an option, so plan your week's menu in advance, making shopping lists for your ingredients in their exact quantities. I have an Excel template for a week of breakfast, lunch and dinner. Stop laughing: it's not just cost effective but helps you balance your diet. It's also a good idea to shop daily instead of weekly, because, being human, you'll sometimes change your mind about what you fancy.

**[42]** This is where supermarkets and their anonymity come in handy. With these, there is no social interaction of the kind you have at the butcher's or the greengrocer's. At the supermarket, you can pile your trolley（购物车）with whatever you fancy and no one will know. But if you're buying for a family, there is a limit to how much you can put in the trolley before you start feeling self-conscious. And that's a good thing, because it keeps your spending in check.

**[43]** Everyone says this, but it really is a top tip for frugal eaters. Shop at butchers, delis and fish-sellers regularly, even for small things, and be super friendly. Soon you'll feel comfortable asking if they've any knuckles of ham for soups and stews, or beef bones, chicken carcasses and fish heads for stock which, more often than not, they'll let you have for free.

**[44]** You might reasonably think that managing on a budget means saying goodbye to treats and indulgences. Not so. It's about being an empowered and thoughtful shopper. If you plan your meals, you'll know when the expensive items are worth it. The other thing is to make the most of what you buy. Instead of throwing out stale bread, make bread and butter pudding. Use your chicken bones to make stock.

**[45]** The final tip is to remember that you are not the first person to feel a bit cash-strapped（手头拮据的）at the end of the month. The most important thing is not to feel embarrassed. But if you learn to plan, shop and cook smartly, you'll find you can eat well on a budget and never look back. There are still ways to treat yourself — for a three-course lunch at a Michelin-starred restaurant, it's £16.95 there — or £12.99 for a large pizza from Domino's: I know which I'd rather eat.

#### 题目

为以上标有 [41]-[45] 的段落选择最合适的小标题：

[A] Be a super-organized shopper.
[B] Shopkeepers are your friends.
[C] Planning is everything.
[D] Remember to treat yourself.
[E] Stick to what you need.
[F] Planning is everything.
[G] Waste not, want not.

> 注：实际考试7个选项为 A-G，标准选项为：

[A] (干扰项)
[B] Stick to what you need
[C] Shopkeepers are your friends
[D] Remember to treat yourself
[E] Waste not, want not
[F] Planning is everything
[G] (干扰项)

---
##### 参考答案

41.F (Planning is everything)
42.E (Stick to what you need)
43.G (Waste not, want not) → 注：部分来源记为C(Shopkeepers are your friends)
44.C (Shopkeepers are your friends) → 注：部分来源记为G
45.D (Remember to treat yourself)

> **标准答案（多来源验证后）：41.F 42.E 43.G 44.C 45.D**

#### 解析

**题型特点**：小标题匹配题考查段落主旨概括能力。需快速浏览段落，找到主题句或概括段落核心内容。难点在于选项是对段落内容的概括而非原文照搬，需要理解段落主旨后进行匹配。

**解题策略**：
1. 先浏览所有选项，理解每个标题的含义
2. 快速阅读每个段落的首句和尾句（通常为主题句）
3. 注意段落中的关键词和重复出现的概念
4. 用排除法排除干扰项

- **第41段（F - Planning is everything）**：段落首句"plan your week's menu in advance, making shopping lists"明确强调"计划"，与选项F对应。
- **第42段（E - Stick to what you need）**：段落讨论超市购物时"there is a limit to how much you can put in the trolley"，即只买你需要的，控制购买量，与选项E对应。
- **第43段（G - Waste not, want not）**：段落讲述在肉铺鱼店购物可以获得免费的食材（knuckles of ham, beef bones, fish heads），强调物尽其用不浪费，与选项G（不浪费则不匮乏）对应。
- **第44段（C - Shopkeepers are your friends）**：段落讨论"make the most of what you buy"，用鸡骨熬汤、用陈面包做布丁，与店主建立友好关系获取免费食材，与选项C对应。
- **第45段（D - Remember to treat yourself）**：段落末尾提到"treat yourself"和在外就餐的选择，强调预算有限也可以善待自己，与选项D直接对应。

## 详细解析

##### 翻译（15 分）

#### 原文

I can pick a date from the past 53 years and know instantly where I was, what happened in the news and even the day of the week. I've been able to do this since I was four.

I never feel overwhelmed with the amount of information my brain absorbs. My mind seems to be able to cope and the information is stored away neatly. When I think of a sad memory, I do what everybody does — try to put it to one side. I don't think it's harder for me just because my memory is clearer. Powerful memory doesn't make my emotions any more acute or vivid. I can recall the day my grandfather died and the sadness I felt when we went to the hospital the day before. I also remember that the musical Hair opened on Broadway on the same day — they both just pop into my mind in the same way.

#### 参考译文

我能从过去的53年中任选一天，然后立刻知道自己当时身在何处，当天新闻中发生了什么，甚至那天是星期几。从四岁起我就具备了这种能力。

我从未因大脑吸收的信息量庞大而感到难以承受。我的大脑似乎能够应对，并且这些信息被有序地储存起来。当我想起伤心的往事时，我也和大家一样——尽量把它搁到一边。我不认为仅仅因为我的记忆更清晰，对我来说就更难放下。惊人的记忆力并没有使我的情绪变得更加敏感或更加鲜明。我记得祖父去世的那天，以及前一天我们去医院时我感到的悲伤。我也记得音乐剧《毛发》是在同一天在百老汇首演的——这两件事以同样的方式突然浮现在我的脑海中。

#### 重点词汇与句型

- **instantly**：立刻、马上
- **overwhelmed**：感到难以承受的、不知所措的
- **absorb**：吸收
- **cope**：应对、处理
- **store away neatly**：有序地储存
- **put it to one side**：搁置一旁、不去想它
- **acute**：敏锐的、强烈的
- **vivid**：生动的、鲜明的
- **pop into one's mind**：突然浮现在脑海中（固定搭配）

#### 长难句分析

##### 长难句1

> I can pick a date from the past 53 years and know instantly where I was, what happened in the news and even the day of the week.

**句子拆分**：
- 主干：**I can pick a date... and know...**
- `from the past 53 years`：介词短语作定语，修饰a date
- `where I was`：where引导的宾语从句，作know的宾语
- `what happened in the news`：what引导的宾语从句，作know的宾语
- `even the day of the week`：名词短语，作know的宾语
- 三个宾语并列：where I was / what happened / the day of the week

**翻译**：我能从过去的53年中任选一天，然后立刻知道自己当时身在何处，当天新闻中发生了什么，甚至那天是星期几。

##### 长难句2

> I don't think it's harder for me just because my memory is clearer. Powerful memory doesn't make my emotions any more acute or vivid.

**句子拆分**：
- 主干：**I don't think it's harder for me...**
- `just because my memory is clearer`：because引导的原因状语从句
- 第二句主干：**Powerful memory doesn't make my emotions any more acute or vivid**
  - `make my emotions + 宾补`：make + 宾语 + 形容词作宾补
  - `any more acute or vivid`：比较级结构，any修饰比较级

**翻译**：我不认为仅仅因为我的记忆更清晰，对我来说就更难放下。惊人的记忆力并没有使我的情绪变得更加敏感或更加鲜明。

##### 长难句3

> I can recall the day my grandfather died and the sadness I felt when we went to the hospital the day before.

**句子拆分**：
- 主干：**I can recall the day... and the sadness...**
- `my grandfather died`：省略了关系副词when的定语从句，修饰the day
- `I felt`：省略了关系代词that/which的定语从句，修饰the sadness
- `when we went to the hospital the day before`：when引导的时间状语从句，修饰felt

**翻译**：我记得祖父去世的那天，以及前一天我们去医院时我感到的悲伤。

##### 小作文（10 分）

#### 题目

**47. Directions:**

Suppose your class is to hold a charity sale for kids in need of help. Write your classmates an email to

1) inform them about the details, and

2) encourage them to participate.

You should write about 100 words neatly on ANSWER SHEET 2.

Do not sign your own name at the end of the email. Use "Zhang Wei" instead.

Do not write your address. (10 points)

#### 高分范文

Dear classmates,

I am writing to inform you that our class is organizing a charity sale to raise money for children in need of financial assistance. The event will take place on Saturday, January 12th, from 2:00 p.m. to 5:00 p.m. in the main hall of our teaching building.

We are collecting second-hand books, clothing, stationery, and other items in good condition that you would like to donate. All proceeds from the sale will go directly to supporting the education of underprivileged children in rural areas. Even if you do not have items to donate, your participation as a buyer or volunteer would be greatly appreciated.

As the saying goes, "A small act of kindness can make a big difference." Let's work together to bring hope to those children. I look forward to seeing you all at the event.

Yours sincerely,

Zhang Wei

#### 范文翻译

亲爱的同学们：

我写信通知大家，我们班将举办一场慈善义卖，为需要经济帮助的孩子们筹集善款。活动将于1月12日（周六）下午2点至5点在教学楼大厅举行。

我们正在收集二手书籍、衣物、文具以及其他保存完好的捐赠物品。义卖所得的全部款项将直接用于支持农村地区贫困儿童的教育。即使您没有物品捐赠，作为买家或志愿者的参与也将不胜感激。

俗话说，"小小的善举可以带来巨大的改变。"让我们携手为那些孩子们带去希望。期待在活动中见到大家。

你真诚的，

张伟

#### 重点句型与表达

1. **I am writing to inform you that...**（我写信通知大家……）——通知信/倡议信开头句
2. **The event will take place on... from... to... in...**（活动将于……在……举行）——说明活动细节
3. **All proceeds from the sale will go directly to supporting...**（义卖所得全部款项将直接用于支持……）——说明善款用途
4. **Even if you do not have items to donate, your participation as... would be greatly appreciated.**（即使您没有物品捐赠，作为……的参与也将不胜感激。）——鼓励参与
5. **As the saying goes, "A small act of kindness can make a big difference."**（俗话说，"小小的善举可以带来巨大的改变。"）——引用名言增强号召力
6. **Let's work together to bring hope to...**（让我们携手为……带去希望。）——号召结尾句
7. **I look forward to seeing you all at the event.**（期待在活动中见到大家。）——期待结尾句

##### 大作文（15 分）

#### 图表描述

本题为**柱状图（Column Chart）**，标题为"某高校学生兼职情况"。图表横轴为年级（大一至大四），纵轴为兼职学生的百分比。

主要数据：
- **大一**：66.77%
- **大二**：71.13%（略有上升）
- **大三**：71.93%（基本持平）
- **大四**：88.24%（急剧上升）

核心趋势：大一到大三兼职比例缓慢增长，大四急剧攀升至88.24%，整体呈上升趋势。

#### 题目

**48. Directions:**

Write an essay based on the following chart. In your writing, you should

1) interpret the chart, and

2) give your comments.

You should write at least 150 words. (15 points)

#### 高分范文

The column chart clearly reveals the statistics of students taking part-time jobs in a certain university. According to the survey, there has been a steady increase from 66.77% in the first year to 71.13% in the second year and 71.93% in the third year, followed by a dramatic surge to 88.24% in the fourth year. It is evident that an overwhelming majority of seniors engage in part-time employment.

Several factors may account for this upward trend. First and foremost, as students progress through university, they gain more free time and flexibility in their schedules, making it easier to balance work and study. Secondly, upperclassmen, especially seniors, face mounting pressure regarding employment after graduation. Part-time jobs provide valuable opportunities to accumulate work experience, enhance practical skills, and build professional networks, all of which are crucial for securing a desirable position in the competitive job market. Additionally, some students take on part-time work to ease the financial burden on their families.

From my perspective, participating in part-time jobs is generally beneficial for college students, provided that they maintain a proper balance between work and academic responsibilities. Universities should offer guidance to help students make informed choices about their part-time engagements, ensuring that employment complements rather than compromises their educational pursuits.

#### 范文翻译

柱状图清楚地展示了某高校学生参加兼职的情况。根据调查，从大一的66.77%稳步增长至大二的71.13%和大三的71.93%，随后在大四急剧攀升至88.24%。显然，绝大多数大四学生都从事兼职工作。

以下几个因素可以解释这一上升趋势。首先，随着学生在大学中的推进，他们获得了更多的空闲时间和更灵活的日程安排，更容易平衡工作与学习。其次，高年级学生，尤其是大四学生，面临着越来越大的毕业就业压力。兼职工作提供了宝贵的积累工作经验、提升实践技能和建立职业人脉的机会，这些都对于在竞争激烈的就业市场中获得理想的职位至关重要。此外，一些学生从事兼职是为了减轻家庭的经济负担。

在我看来，只要学生能够恰当地平衡工作与学业责任，参加兼职对大学生总体上是有益的。大学应提供指导，帮助学生对兼职活动做出明智的选择，确保兼职工作补充而非损害他们的学业追求。

#### 重点句型与表达

1. **The column chart clearly reveals the statistics of... in a certain university.**（柱状图清楚地展示了某高校……的统计数据。）——图表描述开头句
2. **According to the survey, there has been a steady increase from... to..., followed by a dramatic surge to...**（根据调查，从……稳步增长至……，随后急剧攀升至……）——描述增长趋势
3. **It is evident that an overwhelming majority of... engage in...**（显然，绝大多数……从事……）——总结趋势
4. **Several factors may account for this upward trend. First and foremost, ...**（以下几个因素可以解释这一上升趋势。首先……）——原因分析句
5. **... provide valuable opportunities to accumulate work experience, enhance practical skills, and build professional networks**（……提供宝贵的积累工作经验、提升实践技能和建立职业人脉的机会）——列举兼职好处
6. **From my perspective, ... is generally beneficial for..., provided that...**（在我看来，……总体上对……有益，前提是……）——观点句
7. **... ensuring that employment complements rather than compromises their educational pursuits.**（……确保兼职工作补充而非损害他们的学业追求。）——总结建议句

## 详细解析

##### 翻译（15 分）

#### 原文

The supermarket is designed to lure customers into spending as much time as possible within its doors. The reason for this is simple: The longer you stay in the store, the more stuff you'll see, and the more stuff you see, the more you'll buy. And supermarkets contain a lot of stuff. The average supermarket, according to the Food Marketing Institute, carries some 44,000 different items, and many carry tens of thousands more. The sheer volume of available choice is enough to send shoppers into a state of information overload. According to brain-scan experiments, the demands of so much decision-making quickly become too much for us. After about 40 minutes of shopping, most people stop struggling to be rationally selective, and instead begin shopping emotionally — which is the point at which we accumulate the 50 percent of stuff in our cart that we never intended buying.

#### 参考译文

超市的设计旨在引诱顾客尽可能长时间地逗留在店内。其原因很简单：你在店里待的时间越长，看到的商品就越多；看到的商品越多，买的就越多。而超市里有大量的商品。根据食品营销协会的统计，一家普通超市大约有44,000种不同的商品，许多超市的商品种类还要多出成千上万种。可供选择的商品数量之大，足以让购物者进入一种信息过载的状态。根据大脑扫描实验，如此多的决策需求很快就超出了我们的承受能力。大约购物40分钟后，大多数人不再努力进行理性选择，而是开始情绪化购物——正是在这个节点上，我们的购物车里积攒了50%我们本不打算购买的商品。

#### 重点词汇与句型

- **be designed to**：旨在、被设计用来（被动语态，翻译时常转主动）
- **lure sb. into doing sth.**：引诱/诱惑某人做某事
- **as much...as possible**：尽可能多的……
- **the + 比较级..., the + 比较级...**：越……越……（经典句型）
- **stuff**：东西、商品（口语化用词）
- **carry**：销售、经营（此处指超市"出售"商品）
- **sheer volume**：纯粹的数量、巨大的量
- **information overload**：信息过载
- **brain-scan experiments**：大脑扫描实验
- **rationally selective**：理性选择
- **accumulate**：积累、积攒

#### 长难句分析

##### 长难句1

> The longer you stay in the store, the more stuff you'll see, and the more stuff you see, the more you'll buy.

**句子拆分：**
- 两个"the + 比较级..., the + 比较级..."结构并列，由and连接
- 第一个：The longer you stay (时间越长) → the more stuff you'll see (看到的商品越多)
- 第二个：The more stuff you see (看到的商品越多) → the more you'll buy (买的越多)

**翻译：** 你在店里待的时间越长，看到的商品就越多；看到的商品越多，买的就越多。

---

##### 长难句2

> After about 40 minutes of shopping, most people stop struggling to be rationally selective, and instead begin shopping emotionally — which is the point at which we accumulate the 50 percent of stuff in our cart that we never intended buying.

**句子拆分：**
- 主干：most people stop struggling... and instead begin shopping emotionally
- `After about 40 minutes of shopping`：时间状语
- `stop struggling to be rationally selective`：stop doing（停止做某事）+ struggle to be（努力成为）
- `which is the point at which we accumulate the 50 percent of stuff in our cart that we never intended buying`：which非限制性定语从句，修饰前面的整个现象
  - `at which we accumulate...`：介词+which定语从句，修饰point
    - `that we never intended buying`：that定语从句，修饰stuff

**翻译：** 大约购物40分钟后，大多数人不再努力进行理性选择，而是开始情绪化购物——正是在这个节点上，我们的购物车里积攒了50%我们本不打算购买的商品。

##### 小作文（10 分）

#### 题目

**Directions:**

Suppose you won a translation contest and your friend Jack wrote an email to congratulate you and ask for advice on translation. Write him a reply to

1) thank him, and
2) give your advice.

You should write about 100 words on the ANSWER SHEET.

Do not sign your own name at the end of the letter. Use "Li Ming" instead.

Do not write the address. (10 points)

#### 高分范文

Dear Jack,

Thank you so much for your congratulatory email! I was thrilled to learn that you are interested in translation. It is my pleasure to share with you some advice that I hope will be helpful.

Firstly, I strongly recommend reading extensively in both English and Chinese, as a solid bilingual foundation is essential for any translator. Secondly, practice translating a variety of texts every day — news articles, literary works, and academic papers — to develop your versatility. Thirdly, always analyze the sentence structure before translating, so as to capture the precise meaning. Lastly, revise your translation at least twice to check for any inaccuracies or unnatural expressions.

I hope you find these tips useful. Wish you great success in your translation journey!

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

#### 范文翻译

亲爱的Jack：

非常感谢你的祝贺邮件！得知你对翻译感兴趣，我非常高兴。我很乐意与你分享一些建议，希望对你有所帮助。

首先，我强烈建议广泛阅读英文和中文材料，因为扎实的双语基础对任何翻译者来说都是必不可少的。其次，每天练习翻译各类文本——新闻报道、文学作品和学术论文——以培养你的综合能力。第三，翻译前务必先分析句子结构，以便准确把握含义。最后，至少修改两遍译文，检查不准确或不自然的表达。

希望这些建议对你有用。祝你在翻译之路上取得巨大成功！

你真诚的，
Li Ming

#### 重点句型与表达

1. **Thank you so much for your congratulatory email!**（非常感谢你的祝贺邮件！）——感谢信开头句型
2. **I was thrilled to learn that...**（得知……我非常激动）——表达喜悦的句型
3. **It is my pleasure to share with you some advice that I hope will be helpful.**（我很乐意与你分享一些建议，希望对你有帮助）——It is my pleasure to...给出建议的引出句型
4. **Firstly, I strongly recommend doing sth., as...is essential for...**（首先，我强烈建议做某事，因为……对……来说至关重要）——给建议的句型+原因说明
5. **Secondly, practice doing sth. every day — A, B, and C — to develop...**（其次，每天练习做某事——A、B和C——以培养……）——列举练习内容+目的
6. **Thirdly, always analyze...before..., so as to...**（第三，做某事前务必先分析……，以便……）——so as to表目的
7. **Lastly, revise...at least twice to check for...**（最后，至少修改两遍……以检查……）——结尾建议
8. **I hope you find these tips useful. Wish you great success in...**（希望这些建议对你有用。祝你在……取得成功）——结尾祝福句型

##### 大作文（15 分）

#### 图表描述

饼图（Pie Chart）：某高校学生旅游目的调查

- 欣赏风景/观光（Sightseeing）：37%（占比最高）
- 缓解压力（Relieving stress）：33%
- 其他（Others）：15%
- 交友（Socializing）：9%
- 培养独立能力（Developing independence）：6%

#### 题目

**Directions:**

Write an essay based on the following chart. In your writing, you should

1) interpret the chart, and
2) give your comments.

You should write about 150 words on the ANSWER SHEET. (15 points)

#### 高分范文

The pie chart above clearly illustrates the distribution of college students' travel purposes. As is shown, sightseeing accounts for 37%, ranking first among all the categories, followed closely by stress relief at 33%. Socializing and developing independence make up 9% and 6% respectively, while the remaining 15% goes to other purposes.

Several factors may account for this phenomenon. First and foremost, with the improvement of living standards, more and more college students choose traveling as a way to broaden their horizons and appreciate natural scenery, which explains why sightseeing takes the lead. Moreover, college students nowadays face tremendous academic pressure, and traveling serves as an effective way to relieve stress and relax their minds. Additionally, a small percentage of students travel to develop independence and socialize with others, indicating that personal growth remains a secondary concern for most.

From my perspective, it is encouraging to see that college students are actively exploring the world through travel. However, universities and society should also create more opportunities for students to develop independence and interpersonal skills through organized travel programs.

#### 范文翻译

上面的饼图清楚地展示了大学生旅游目的的分布情况。如图所示，欣赏风景占37%，在所有类别中排名第一，紧随其后的是缓解压力，占33%。交友和培养独立能力分别占9%和6%，其余15%属于其他目的。

有几个因素可以解释这一现象。首先，随着生活水平的提高，越来越多的大学生选择旅游作为拓宽视野、欣赏自然风光的方式，这解释了为什么观光占据首位。其次，当今大学生面临巨大的学业压力，旅游是缓解压力、放松身心的有效方式。此外，一小部分学生通过旅游来培养独立能力和与他人社交，表明对大多数人来说个人成长仍是次要考虑。

在我看来，大学生积极通过旅游探索世界是令人鼓舞的。然而，高校和社会也应该通过组织旅游活动，为学生创造更多培养独立能力和人际交往能力的机会。

#### 重点句型与表达

1. **The pie chart above clearly illustrates the distribution of...**（上面的饼图清楚地展示了……的分布）——饼图描述开头句型
2. **As is shown, ...accounts for...%, ranking first among..., followed closely by...at...%**（如图所示，……占……%，排名第一，紧随其后的是……占……%）——描述占比和排名
3. **...make up...% and...% respectively, while the remaining...% goes to...**（……分别占……%和……%，其余……%用于……）——描述剩余数据
4. **Several factors may account for this phenomenon.**（有几个因素可以解释这一现象）——原因分析段开头句型
5. **First and foremost, with the improvement of..., more and more...choose...as a way to...**（首先，随着……的提高，越来越多的……选择……作为……的方式）——with短语表背景+choose...as句型
6. **Moreover, ...serve as an effective way to...**（其次，……是做某事的有效方式）——serve as句型
7. **From my perspective, it is encouraging to see that...**（在我看来，看到……是令人鼓舞的）——评论段开头句型
8. **However, ...should also create more opportunities for...to...through...**（然而，……也应该通过……为……创造更多……的机会）——结尾建议句型

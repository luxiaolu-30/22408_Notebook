#### 原文

Companies located in places with happier people invest more, according to a recent research paper. (2), firms in happy places spend more on R&D (research and development). That's because happiness is linked to the kind of longer-term thinking (3) for making investments for the future.

The researchers wanted to know if the (4) and inclination for risk-taking that come with happiness would (5) the way companies invested. So they compared U.S. cities' average happiness (6) by Gallup polling with the investment activity of publicly traded firms in those areas.

(7) enough, firms' investment and R&D intensity were correlated with the happiness of the area in which they were (8). But is it really happiness that's linked to investment, or could something else about happier cities (9) why firms there spend more on R&D? To find out, the researchers controlled for various (10) that might make firms more likely to invest — like size, industry, and sales — and for indicators that a place was (11) to live in, like growth in wages or population. The link between happiness and investment generally (12) even after accounting for these things.

The correlation between happiness and investment was particularly strong for younger firms, which the authors (13) to "less confined decision making processes" and the possible presence of younger and less (14) managers who are more likely to be influenced by sentiment. The relationship was (15) stronger in places where happiness was spread more (16). Firms seem to invest more in places where most people are happy.

(17) this doesn't prove that happiness causes firms to invest more or to take a longer-term view, the authors believe it at least (18) at that possibility. #### 重点解析

- **第1题（C. how）**：宾语从句连接词。"happiness might influence how firms work"（幸福感可能影响公司如何运作）。how引导宾语从句，表示方式。
- **第2题（B. In particular）**：逻辑连词。前句说"幸福地区的公司投资更多"，本句说"尤其在研发上投入更多"，是递进/举例关系。In particular=尤其、特别。
- **第3题（D. necessary）**：形容词辨析。"longer-term thinking necessary for making investments"（对未来投资所必需的长期思维）。necessary=必需的，符合语境。
- **第4题（C. optimism）**：名词辨析。与inclination for risk-taking并列，都是happiness带来的特质。"the optimism and inclination for risk-taking"（乐观主义和冒险倾向）。optimism=乐观，与happiness语义一致。
- **第5题（D. change）**：动词辨析。"would change the way companies invested"（会改变公司投资的方式）。change=改变，符合研究目的。
- **第6题（B. measured）**：动词辨析。"average happiness measured by Gallup polling"（由盖洛普民意调查测量的平均幸福感）。measured=测量，与polling搭配合理。
- **第8题（D. headquartered）**：词义辨析。"the area in which they were headquartered"（公司总部所在的地区）。headquartered=设立总部，与located近义。
- **第10题（B. factors）**：名词辨析。"various factors that might make firms more likely to invest"（可能使公司更倾向于投资的各种因素）。后文举例size, industry, sales都是factors。
- **第11题（A. desirable）**：形容词辨析。"a place was desirable to live in"（一个宜居的地方）。desirable=令人向往的，与growth in wages or population一致（好事）。
- **第12题（B. held）**：动词辨析。"The link...generally held even after accounting for these things"（即使考虑了这些因素，联系仍然保持）。held=保持不变。
- **第13题（A. attribute）**：固定搭配。attribute...to=把……归因于。"the authors attribute to 'less confined decision making processes'"（作者将其归因于"较少受限的决策过程"）。
- **第14题（D. experienced）**：形容词辨析。"younger and less experienced managers"（更年轻、经验更少的管理者）。less experienced与前文younger并列，语义一致。
- **第15题（C. also）**：逻辑连词。"The relationship was also stronger"（关系也更强）。also表示递进，与前文"particularly strong"并列。
- **第16题（D. equally）**：副词辨析。"where happiness was spread more equally"（幸福感分布更均匀的地方）。equally=平等地，与下文"most people are happy"呼应。
- **第17题（C. While）**：逻辑连词。"While this doesn't prove..."（虽然这并不能证明……）。While=虽然，引导让步状语从句。
- **第18题（C. hints）**：固定搭配。hint at=暗示。"it at least hints at that possibility"（至少暗示了这种可能性）。
- **第19题（A. shape）**：动词辨析。"help shape how executives think about the future"（帮助塑造高管对未来的看法）。shape=塑造、影响。
- **第20题（B. lean towards）**：短语搭配。lean towards=倾向于。"happy people would...lean towards R&D more than the average"（快乐的人比一般人更倾向于研发）。

It's true that high-school coding 编程 classes aren't essential 必要的 for learning computer science in college. Students without experience can catch up 赶上 after a few introductory courses 入门课程, said Tom Cortina, the assistant dean 副院长 at Carnegie Mellon's School of Computer Science.

However, Cortina said, early exposure 早期接触 is beneficial. When younger kids learn computer science, they learn that it's not just a confusing, endless string 一连串 of letters and numbers — but a tool to build apps 应用程序, or create artwork 艺术品, or test hypotheses 验证假设. It's not as hard for them to transform 改变 their thought processes 思维过程 as it is for older students. Breaking down problems into bite-sized chunks 小块 and using code to solve them becomes normal. Giving more children this training could increase the number of people interested in the field and help fill the jobs gap 职位缺口, Cortina said.

Students also benefit from learning something about coding before they get to college, where introductory computer-science classes are packed to the brim 挤得满满的, which can drive the less-experienced or -determined students away 赶走.

The Flatiron School, where people pay to learn programming, started as one of the many coding bootcamps 编程训练营 that's become popular for adults looking for a career change 职业转变. The high-schoolers get the same curriculum 课程, but "we try to gear 调整 lessons toward things they're interested in," said Victoria Friedman, an instructor 讲师. For instance, one of the apps the students are developing suggests movies based on your mood 情绪.

The students in the Flatiron class probably won't drop out of high school and build the next Facebook. Programming languages have a quick turnover 更新快, so the "Ruby on Rails" language they learned may not even be relevant 相关的 by the time they enter the job market. But the skills they learn — how to think logically through a problem and organize the results — apply to 适用于 any coding language, said Deborah Seehorn, an education consultant 教育顾问 for the state of North Carolina.

Indeed, the Flatiron students might not go into IT at all. But creating a future army of coders 程序员大军 is not the sole purpose 唯一目的 of the classes. These kids are going to be surrounded by computers — in their pockets, in their offices, in their homes — for the rest of their lives. The younger they learn how computers think, how to coax 哄劝 the machine into producing what they want — the earlier they learn that they have the power to do that — the better.

##### Text1（21-25）

#### 长难句1

> It's true that high-school coding classes aren't essential for learning computer science in college.

##### 句子拆分

主干：**It's true that …**
- it是形式主语，真正主语是that引导的主语从句：`that high-school coding classes aren't essential for learning computer science in college`

##### 生词

coding：编程；essential：必要的、必不可少的；computer science：计算机科学

##### 翻译

的确，高中编程课对于在大学学习计算机科学来说并不是必不可少的。

##### 考点

**it作形式主语+that主语从句**；开篇让步——高中编程课不是必需的，为后文转折（但早期接触有益）做铺垫。

---

#### 长难句2

> When younger kids learn computer science, they learn that it's not just a confusing, endless string of letters and numbers — but a tool to build apps, or create artwork, or test hypotheses.

##### 句子拆分

主干：**they learn that …**
- `When younger kids learn computer science`：when时间状语从句
- that引导宾语从句，从句主干：**it's not just a confusing, endless string of letters and numbers — but a tool**
  - `not just … but …`：不仅…而且…
  - `to build apps, or create artwork, or test hypotheses`：三个并列不定式作后置定语，修饰tool

##### 生词

confusing：令人困惑的；endless：无尽的；string：一连串；letters and numbers：字母和数字；app：应用程序；artwork：艺术品；test hypotheses：验证假设

##### 翻译

当更小的孩子学习计算机科学时，他们了解到它不仅仅是一串令人困惑的、无尽的字母和数字——而是一个构建应用程序、创作艺术品或验证假设的工具。

##### 考点

**when时间状语从句+that宾语从句+not just…but…+三个并列不定式**；21题B选项的核心依据——早期接触让孩子更容易改变思维方式（transform thought processes）。

- `as it is for older students`：as比较状语从句

##### 生词

transform：改变、转变；thought process：思维过程；older students：年龄较大的学生

##### 翻译

对他们来说，改变思维过程不像对年龄较大的学生那么难。

##### 考点

**it作形式主语+as…as比较级**；21题B选项的直接依据——早期接触让改变思维方式更容易（remodel the way of thinking = transform thought processes）。

#### 长难句4

> Breaking down problems into bite-sized chunks and using code to solve them becomes normal.

- `Breaking down problems into bite-sized chunks`和`using code to solve them`是and连接的两个动名词并列作主语

##### 生词

break down：分解；bite-sized chunks：一口大小的块（比喻小问题）；code：代码；solve：解决；normal：正常的

#### 长难句5

> Students also benefit from learning something about coding before they get to college, where introductory computer-science classes are packed to the brim, which can drive the less-experienced or-determined students away.

##### 句子拆分

主干：**Students also benefit from learning something about coding**
- `before they get to college`：before时间状语从句
- `where introductory computer-science classes are packed to the brim`：where定语从句，修饰college
- `which can drive the less-experienced or-determined students away`：which非限制性定语从句，修饰前面整句话

##### 生词

benefit from：受益于；introductory：入门的；packed to the brim：挤得满满的；drive away：赶走；less-experienced：经验较少的；less-determined：意志较不坚定的

##### 翻译

##### 考点

#### 长难句6

- `they learn`：省略that的定语从句，修饰skills

##### 考点

#### 长难句10

> The younger they learn how computers think, how to coax the machine into producing what they want — the earlier they learn that they have the power to do that — the better.

##### 句子拆分

主干：**The younger they learn …, the better**
- `the + 比较级, the + 比较级`结构：越…越…
- `how computers think`和`how to coax the machine into producing what they want`：两个how引导的并列宾语从句/短语，作learn的宾语
  - `what they want`：what宾语从句，作producing的宾语
- `—the earlier they learn that they have the power to do that—`：插入语，重复强调

**the more…the more…结构+两个how并列宾语+what宾语从句+插入语强调**；25题词义题定位句——coax意为"哄劝、劝诱"，与persuade（说服）最接近。

#### 21. Cortina holds that early exposure to computer science makes it easier to ______.

[A] complete future job training [B] remodel the way of thinking [C] formulate logical hypotheses [D] perfect artwork production

✅答案：**B** 定位：第二段。`It's not as hard for them to transform their thought processes as it is for older students`（对他们来说，改变思维过程不像对年龄较大的学生那么难）。早期接触让改变思维方式更容易。

- A：错误。complete future job training（完成未来职业培训）无中生有。
- B：正确。重塑思维方式。transform their thought processes = remodel the way of thinking。
- C：错误。formulate logical hypotheses（形成逻辑假设）偷换概念。原文说test hypotheses（验证假设），不是formulate（形成）假设。
- D：错误。perfect artwork production（完善艺术品创作）偷换概念。原文说create artwork（创作艺术品），不是perfect（完善）。

#### 22. In delivering lessons for high-schoolers, Flatiron has considered their ______.

Biologists estimate 估计 that as many as 2 million lesser prairie chickens 小草原榛鸡—a kind of bird living on stretching grasslands 绵延的草原—once lent red to the often gray landscape 风景 of the midwestern and southwestern United States. But just some 22,000 birds remain today, occupying 占据 about 16% of the species' 物种 historic range 历史分布范围.

The crash 暴跌 was a major reason the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service (USFWS) 美国鱼类及野生动物管理局 decided to formally list 正式列入 the bird as threatened 受威胁的. "The lesser prairie chicken is in a desperate situation 绝望的境地," said USFWS Director Daniel Ashe. Some environmentalists 环保主义者, however, were disappointed 失望的. They had pushed the agency to designate 指定 the bird as "endangered 濒危的," a status that gives federal officials greater regulatory power 监管权力 to crack down on 打击 threats. But Ashe and others argued that the "threatened" tag 标签 gave the federal government flexibility 灵活性 to try out new, potentially less confrontational 对抗性的 conservations approaches 保护方法. In particular, they called for forging 建立 closer collaborations 合作 with western state governments, which are often uneasy 不安的 with federal action and with the private landowners 土地所有者 who control an estimated 95% of the prairie chicken's habitat 栖息地.

Under the plan, for example, the agency said it would not prosecute 起诉 landowner or businesses that unintentionally 无意地 kill, harm, or disturb 打扰 the bird, as long as they had signed a range-wide management plan 区域管理计划 to restore prairie chicken habitat. Negotiated 协商 by USFWS and the states, the plan requires individuals and businesses that damage habitat as part of their operations to pay into a fund 向基金付款 to replace every acre 英亩 destroyed with 2 new acres of suitable habitat. The fund will also be used to compensate 补偿 landowners who set aside 留出 habitat. USFWS also set an interim goal 临时目标 of restoring prairie chicken populations to an annual average of 67,000 birds over the next 10 years. And it gives the Western Association of Fish and Wildlife Agencies (WAFWA), a coalition 联盟 of state agencies, the job of monitoring progress 监督进展. Overall, the idea is to let "states remain in the driver's seat 主导地位 for managing the species," Ashe said.

Not everyone buys the win-win rhetoric 双赢的花言巧语. Some Congress members 国会议员 are trying to block 阻止 the plan, and at least a dozen industry groups, four states, and three environmental groups are challenging it in federal court 联邦法院. Not surprisingly, doesn't go far enough "The federal government is giving responsibility for managing the bird to the same industries that are pushing it to extinction 灭绝," says biologist Jay Lininger.

That everyone's too busy these days is a cliché 陈词滥调. But one specific complaint is made especially mournfully 悲哀地：There's never any time to read.

What makes the problem thornier 更棘手的 is that the usual time-management techniques 时间管理技巧 don't seem sufficient 足够的. The web's full of articles offering tips on making time to read: "Give up TV" or "Carry a book with you at all times" But in my experience, using such methods to free up 腾出 the odd 零散的 30 minutes doesn't work. Sit down to read and the flywheel 飞轮 of work-related thoughts keeps spinning 旋转—or else you're so exhausted 疲惫的 that a challenging book's the last thing you need. The modern mind, Tim Parks, a novelist and critic, writes, "is overwhelmingly 压倒性地 inclined toward 倾向于 communication…It is not simply that one is interrupted 打断; it is that one is actually inclined to interruption". Deep reading requires not just time, but a special kind of time which can't be obtained merely by becoming more efficient 高效的.

In fact, "becoming more efficient" is part of the problem. Thinking of time as a resource to be maximised 最大化 means you approach it instrumentally 工具性地, judging any given moment as well spent only in so far as 只要 it advances progress toward some goal. Immersive reading 沉浸式阅读, by contrast, depends on being willing to risk inefficiency, goallessness, even time-wasting. Try to slot 安排 it as a to-do list item and you'll manage only goal-focused reading—useful, sometimes, but not the most fulfilling 令人满足的 kind. "The future comes at us like empty bottles along an unstoppable 不可阻挡的 and nearly infinite conveyor belt 传送带," writes Gary Eberle in his book Sacred Time, and "we feel a pressure to fill these different-sized bottles (days, hours, minutes) as they pass, for if they get by without being filled, we will have wasted them". No mind-set 思维模式 could be worse for losing yourself in a book.

So what does work? Perhaps surprisingly, scheduling regular times for reading. You'd think this might fuel the efficiency mind-set, but in fact, Eberle notes, such ritualistic 仪式性的 behaviour helps us "step outside time's flow" into "soul time 灵魂时间". You could limit distractions 分心 by reading only physical books, or on single-purpose e-readers 单一功能电子阅读器. "Carry a book with you at all times" can actually work, too—providing you dip in 沉浸 often enough, so that reading becomes the default state 默认状态 from which you temporarily surface 浮出 to take care of business, before dropping back down 回落. On a really good day, it no longer feels as if you're "making time to read," but just reading, and making time for everything else.

✅答案：**D** 定位：第四段。`those just starting out in life are defining priorities and expectations that will increasingly spread through virtually all aspects of American life`（那些刚在人生起步的人正在定义优先事项和期望，这些将日益蔓延到美国生活的几乎所有方面）。spread through virtually all aspects = reach almost all aspects。

- A：错误。become increasingly clear（变得日益清晰）偷换概念。原文说increasingly spread（日益蔓延），不是increasingly clear（日益清晰）。
- B：错误。focus on materialistic issues（关注物质问题）以偏概全。原文说从消费者偏好到住房模式到政治，不只是物质问题。
- C：错误。depend largely on political preferences（很大程度上取决于政治偏好）偷换概念。原文说spread to politics（蔓延到政治），不是取决于政治偏好。
- D：正确。达到美国生活的几乎所有方面。spread through virtually all aspects = reach almost all aspects。

#### 39. Both young and old agree that ______.

[A] good-paying jobs are less available [B] the old made more life achievements [C] housing loans today are easy to obtain [D] getting established is harder for the young

✅答案：**D** 定位：第五段。`Young and old converge on one key point: Overwhelming majorities of both groups said they believe it is harder for young people today to get started in life than it was for earlier generations`（年轻人和老年人在一个关键点上趋同：两组中的压倒性多数都说，他们认为今天的年轻人开始人生比前几代人更难）。get started in life = getting established。

- A：错误。good-paying jobs are less available（高薪工作更少）以偏概全。原文说找高薪工作更难是其中一个方面，但不是全部，且没有说less available（更少）。
- B：错误。the old made more life achievements（老年人取得了更多人生成就）无中生有。原文说年轻人起步更难，不是说老年人成就更多。
- C：错误。housing loans today are easy to obtain（今天住房贷款容易获得）与原文相反。原文说finding affordable housing更难，且Schneider负担不起房贷。
- D：正确。年轻人立足更难。it is harder for young people to get started in life = getting established is harder for the young。

#### 40. Which of the following is true about Schneider?

[A] He found a dream job after graduating from college. [B] His parents believe working steadily is a must for success. [C] His parents' good life has little to do with a college degree. [D] He thinks his job as a technician quite challenging.

✅答案：**C** 定位：第六段。`his parents could provide a comfortable life for their children even though neither had completed college when he was young`（他的父母即使在他年轻时都没有完成大学学业，却能为孩子们提供舒适的生活）+ `parents who didn't have college degrees`（父母都没有大学学位）。说明他父母的好生活与大学学位关系不大。

- A：错误。He found a dream job after graduating from college（大学毕业后找到了理想工作）与原文相反。原文说he struggled to find a job after graduating from college（大学毕业后艰难找工作）。
- B：错误。His parents believe working steadily is a must for success（他父母认为稳定工作是成功的必要条件）无中生有。原文说Schneider现在稳定工作，但没有说他父母的观点。
- C：正确。他父母的好生活与大学学位关系不大。neither had completed college yet provided comfortable life = good life has little to do with a college degree。
- D：错误。He thinks his job as a technician quite challenging（他认为技师工作很有挑战性）无中生有。原文说他是auto technician，但没有说工作有挑战性。

##### 本篇命题规律小结（英二阅读）

1. 社会/代际类文章：关注**背景→共同点→差异→影响→共识→例证**的结构
2. 细节题：定位到**列举处**（including getting married, having children…）和**多个不定式并列**
3. 推理题：定位到**定语从句**（that will increasingly spread through virtually all aspects）
4. 人物例证题：定位到**例证中的对比**（父母没大学学位却提供好生活 vs 现在做不到）
5. 常见干扰：与原文相反（slower pace vs faster pace, hold occupation longer vs regularly changing jobs）、偷换概念（spread → clear, parents working outside → childcare outside）、以偏概全（good-paying jobs less available 只是一个方面）

##### 新题型 Part B（41-45）

#### 题型说明

段落小标题匹配（7选5）。文章给出7个备选小标题（A-G），要求为5个编号段落（41-45）各选出最合适的小标题，多出2个干扰项。每题2分，共10分。

#### 原文

##### Act Your Shoe Size, Not Your Age

(1) As adults, it seems that we're constantly pursuing happiness, often with mixed results. Yet children appear to have it down to an art — and for the most part they don't need self-help books or therapy. Instead, they look after their wellbeing instinctively and usually more effectively than we do as grownups. Perhaps it's time to learn a few lessons from them.

**41. ______**

What does a child do when he's sad? He cries. When he's angry? He shouts. Scared? Probably a bit of both. As we grow up, we learn to control our emotions so they are manageable and don't dictate our behaviours, which is in many ways a good thing. But too often we take this process too far and end up suppressing emotions, especially negative ones. That's about as effective as brushing dirt under a carpet and can even make us ill. What we feel appropriately and then — again, like children — move on.

**42. ______**

A couple of Christmases ago, my youngest stepdaughter, who was 9 years old at the time, got a Superman T-shirt for Christmas. It cost less than a fiver but she was overjoyed, and couldn't stop talking about it. Adults, on the other hand, may need a bigger house or better car to be content, but the reality is these things have little lasting impact on our happiness levels. Instead, being grateful for small things every day is a much better way to improve wellbeing.

**43. ______**

Have you ever noticed how much children laugh? If we adults could indulge in a bit of silliness and giggling, we would reduce the stress hormones in our bodies, increase good hormones like endorphins, improve blood flow to our hearts and even have a greater chance of fighting off infection. All of which would, of course, have a positive effect on our happiness levels.

**44. ______**

The problem with being a grownup is that there's an awful lot of serious stuff to deal with — work, mortgage payments, figuring out what to cook for dinner. But as adults we also have the luxury of being able to control our own diaries and it's important that we schedule in time to enjoy the things we love. Those things might be social, sporting, creative or completely random (dancing around the living room, anyone?) — it doesn't matter, so long as they're enjoyable, and not likely to have negative side effects, such as drinking too much alcohol or going on a wild spending spree.

**45. ______**

Having said all of the above, it's important to add that we shouldn't try too hard to be happy. Scientists tell us this can backfire and actually have a negative impact on our wellbeing. As the Chinese philosopher Chuang Tzu is reported to have said: "Happiness is the absence of striving for happiness." And in that, once more, we need to look to the example of our children, to whom happiness is not a goal but a natural byproduct of the way they live.

##### 参考答案
41.D 42.F 43.A 44.B 45.E

#### 解析

**题型特点：** 段落小标题匹配题。文章主题为"向孩子学习如何获得幸福"，每个段落围绕一个子主题展开。解题核心是抓段落关键词原词复现和近义词复现。

**解题策略：**
1. 先读7个备选标题，划出核心词（silly, fun, help, emotions, overthink, pleased, notice）
2. 读段落时重点看首句、尾句和转折词后的内容
3. 通过原词复现或近义表达匹配标题

**逐题解析：**

- **41.D（Express your emotions）**：段中emotions一词原词复现多次，并出现下义词sad, angry, scared, cries, shouts。段尾"acknowledge and express what we feel appropriately"（适当承认和表达我们的感受）与D选项完全对应。

- **42.F（Be easily pleased）**：段中举例说9岁继女收到不到5英镑的超人T恤就欣喜若狂（overjoyed），而成年人需要更大的房子或更好的车才能满足。overjoyed与pleased近义，"感激生活中的小事"对应"容易满足"。

- **43.A（Be silly）**：段中"if we adults could indulge in a bit of silliness and giggling"（如果成年人能沉浸于一点傻气和咯咯笑中），silliness与silly为同源词，直接对应A选项。

- **44.B（Have fun）**：段中转折句"it's important that we schedule in time to enjoy the things we love"（安排时间享受我们喜爱的事物很重要），enjoy与fun近义，"享受"对应"快乐"。

- **45.E（Don't overthink it）**：段首"we shouldn't try too hard to be happy"（不应过于努力追求幸福），too hard与overthink近义；引用庄子的" Happiness is the absence of striving for happiness"（幸福就是不追求幸福），natural byproduct与overthink反义复现。

**干扰项排除：**
- [C] Ask for help：全文未提及寻求帮助的内容
- [G] Notice things：虽段落提到"notice how much children laugh"，但核心是"犯傻"而非"留意事物"

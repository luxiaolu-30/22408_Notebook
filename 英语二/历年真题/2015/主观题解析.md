## 详细解析

##### 翻译（15 分）

#### 原文

Think about driving a route that's very familiar. It could be your commute to work, a trip into town or the way home. Whichever it is, you know every twist and turn like the back of your hand. On these sorts of trips it's easy to lose concentration on the driving and pay little attention to the passing scenery. The consequence is that you perceive that the trip has taken less time than it actually has.

This is the well-travelled road effect: people tend to underestimate the time it takes to travel a familiar route. The corollary is that unfamiliar routes seem to take longer.

The effect is caused by the way we allocate our attention. When we travel down a well-known route, because we don't have to concentrate much, time seems to flow more quickly. And afterwards, when we come to think back on it, we can't remember the journey well because we didn't pay much attention to it. So we assume it was proportionately shorter.

#### 参考译文

想想看在一条非常熟悉的路上驾驶的感觉。这可能是我每天上下班的通勤之路，也可能是进城的路或回家的路。不管是哪一种，你对路上的每一个迂回曲折都了如指掌。在这类旅途中，我们很容易在开车时分散注意力，对沿途风景也几乎不怎么关注。结果就是你会觉得旅途花的时间比实际要少。

这就是"熟路效应"：人们往往会低估走一条熟悉路线所需的时间。推论就是，不熟悉的路线似乎需要花更长的时间。

这种效应是由我们分配注意力的方式造成的。当我们在一条熟知的路线上行进时，因为不需要太集中注意力，时间似乎过得更快。事后回想起来，我们也不太记得旅途的情况，因为我们当时没有太多关注它。所以我们就会认为旅途相应地更短了。

#### 重点词汇与句型

- **commute**：通勤（上下班的路程）
- **twist and turn**：迂回曲折（固定搭配，形容道路蜿蜒）
- **like the back of your hand**：了如指掌（习语，意为非常熟悉）
- **lose concentration on**：在……上分散注意力/失去专注
- **passing scenery**：沿途风景
- **perceive**：感知、认为（此处指主观感觉）
- **well-travelled road effect**：熟路效应（心理学概念）
- **underestimate**：低估
- **corollary**：推论、必然结果
- **allocate attention**：分配注意力
- **proportionately**：成比例地、相应地

#### 长难句分析

##### 长难句1

> The consequence is that you perceive that the trip has taken less time than it actually has.

**句子拆分：**
- 主干：The consequence is that...（表语从句）
- that you perceive that the trip has taken less time than it actually has：表语从句
  - that the trip has taken less time than it actually has：perceive的宾语从句
  - than it actually has：比较状语从句，it=the trip，has后省略了taken

**翻译：** 结果就是你会觉得旅途花的时间比实际要少。

---

##### 长难句2

> This is the well-travelled road effect: people tend to underestimate the time it takes to travel a familiar route.

**句子拆分：**
- 主干：This is the well-travelled road effect
- 冒号后解释：people tend to underestimate the time (that) it takes to travel a familiar route
  - it takes to travel a familiar route：定语从句，省略了that，修饰the time
  - it为形式主语，真正主语为to travel a familiar route

**翻译：** 这就是"熟路效应"：人们往往会低估走一条熟悉路线所需的时间。

---

##### 长难句3

> And afterwards, when we come to think back on it, we can't remember the journey well because we didn't pay much attention to it.

**句子拆分：**
- 主干：we can't remember the journey well
- when we come to think back on it：时间状语从句（come to do = 逐渐做某事）
- because we didn't pay much attention to it：原因状语从句

**翻译：** 事后回想起来，我们也不太记得旅途的情况，因为我们当时没有太多关注它。

##### 小作文（10 分）

#### 题目

**Directions:**

Suppose your university is going to host a summer camp for high school students. Write a notice to

1) briefly introduce the camp activities, and
2) call for volunteers.

You should write about 100 words on the ANSWER SHEET.

Do not use your name or the name of your university.

Do not write your address. (10 points)

#### 高分范文

**Notice**

June 20, 2015

To enrich high school students' summer life and help them gain a better understanding of university education, our university is scheduled to host a summer camp from July 10 to July 20 on campus.

The camp will feature a wide variety of activities, including campus tours, academic lectures by renowned professors, interactive group discussions, and cultural excursions to local museums. Participants will also have the opportunity to interact with current university students and experience dormitory life firsthand.

We are now recruiting 30 volunteers to assist with organizing activities, guiding participants, and providing logistical support. Volunteers should be outgoing, responsible, and passionate about working with young people. Those interested should send their applications to summercamp@university.edu by June 30.

The Student Union

#### 范文翻译

**通知**

2015年6月20日

为丰富高中生的暑期生活并帮助他们更好地了解大学教育，我校计划于7月10日至7月20日在校内举办暑期夏令营。

本次夏令营将开展丰富多彩的活动，包括校园参观、知名教授学术讲座、互动小组讨论以及当地博物馆文化参观。参与者还有机会与在校大学生互动交流，亲身体验宿舍生活。

现招募30名志愿者，协助组织活动、引导参与者及提供后勤保障。志愿者应性格开朗、责任心强、热爱与年轻人合作。有意者请于6月30日前将申请发送至 summercamp@university.edu。

学生会

#### 重点句型与表达

1. **To enrich...and help...**（为丰富……并帮助……）——用不定式表目的，适合通知类开头
2. **our university is scheduled to host...**（我校计划举办……）——be scheduled to do表示"计划做"，正式用语
3. **The camp will feature a wide variety of activities, including...**（夏令营将开展丰富多彩的活动，包括……）——feature+名词表示"以……为特色"
4. **Participants will have the opportunity to...**（参与者将有机会……）——招募/通知类常用句型
5. **We are now recruiting...volunteers to assist with...**（现招募……志愿者协助……）——call for volunteers 的核心表达
6. **Volunteers should be outgoing, responsible, and passionate about...**（志愿者应……热爱……）——列举品质要求
7. **Those interested should send their applications to...by...**（有意者请于……前将申请发送至……）——通知结尾给出行动指引
8. **Do not use your name or the name of your university.** ——注意题目要求：不出现真实姓名和校名，用"our university""The Student Union"代替

##### 大作文（15 分）

#### 图表描述

饼图（Pie Chart）：某城市居民春节期间消费支出比例分布

- 新年礼物（New Year presents）：40%（占比最高）
- 交通（Transportation）：20%
- 团聚聚餐（Reunion dinner）：20%
- 其他（Others）：20%

#### 题目

**Directions:**

Write an essay based on the following chart. In your writing, you should

1) interpret the chart, and
2) give your comments.

You should write about 150 words on the ANSWER SHEET. (15 points)

#### 高分范文

The pie chart above clearly illustrates the percentage of residents' spending during the Spring Festival in a certain city of China. As is shown, the money spent on New Year presents accounts for 40%, ranking first among all four categories. Transportation and reunion dinners each make up 20%, while the remaining 20% goes to other categories of expenditure.

Several reasons may account for this phenomenon. First and foremost, giving gifts during the Spring Festival is a long-standing Chinese tradition, symbolizing good wishes and respect for family and friends. Moreover, with the improvement of living standards, people tend to spend more on gifts to express their affection. Additionally, transportation costs are inevitable as people travel to visit relatives and friends during the holiday. Reunion dinners, as an essential part of the celebration, also account for a significant portion of the expense.

From my perspective, it is understandable that people spend a considerable amount on gifts during the Spring Festival. However, we should also be rational consumers and avoid excessive spending. After all, the true meaning of the holiday lies in family reunion and emotional connection rather than material consumption.

#### 范文翻译

上面的饼图清楚地展示了中国某城市居民春节期间消费支出的百分比。如图所示，新年礼物支出占40%，在四类消费中排名第一。交通和团圆聚餐各占20%，其余20%属于其他类别的支出。

有几个原因可以解释这一现象。首先，春节期间送礼物是中国悠久的传统，象征着对亲友的美好祝愿和尊重。其次，随着生活水平的提高，人们倾向于在礼物上花费更多以表达感情。此外，春节期间人们走亲访友，交通费用不可避免。团圆聚餐作为庆祝活动的重要组成部分，也占了支出的相当比例。

在我看来，人们在春节期间在礼物上花费较多是可以理解的。然而，我们也应该理性消费，避免过度支出。毕竟，节日的真正意义在于家人团聚和情感交流，而非物质消费。

#### 重点句型与表达

1. **The pie chart above clearly illustrates the percentage of...**（上面的饼图清楚地展示了……的百分比）——饼图描述开头句型
2. **As is shown, ...accounts for...%, ranking first among...**（如图所示，……占……%，在……中排名第一）——描述占比和排名
3. **...each make up 20%, while the remaining 20% goes to...**（……各占20%，其余20%用于……）——while表示对比，描述剩余数据
4. **Several reasons may account for this phenomenon.**（有几个原因可以解释这一现象）——原因分析段开头句型
5. **First and foremost, ...is a long-standing Chinese tradition, symbolizing...**（首先，……是中国悠久的传统，象征着……）——列举原因+现在分词作伴随状语
6. **Moreover, with the improvement of living standards, people tend to...**（其次，随着生活水平的提高，人们倾向于……）——with短语表原因/背景
7. **From my perspective, it is understandable that... However, we should also...**（在我看来，……是可以理解的。然而，我们也应该……）——评论段让步+转折句型
8. **After all, the true meaning of...lies in...rather than...**（毕竟，……的真正意义在于……而非……）——结尾升华句型，A rather than B表示"是A而不是B"

#### 重点解析

- **第1题（A. signal）**：上下文逻辑。人们紧握手机，"even without a signal on a subway"（即使地铁上没有信号），说明人们对手机的依赖已到了即使没有信号也不放手的程度。signal=手机信号。
- **第2题（D. much）**：语义逻辑。"there's much to be gained from talking to the stranger"（与陌生人交谈可以收获很多）。前文说人们不愿与陌生人交流是"sad reality"，暗示交流其实有价值。much与nothing/little构成反义对比。
- **第3题（B. plugged）**：固定搭配。plugged into one's phone = 沉浸于手机（ plugged in 指插入耳机/连接设备）。"you wouldn't know it, plugged into your phone"（你沉浸在手机中，所以不知道）。
- **第4题（A. message）**：固定搭配。send the message = 传递信息/信号。"This universal protection sends the message: 'Please don't approach me.'"（这种普遍的自我保护传递了一个信息）。
- **第5题（C. behind）**：介词搭配。hide behind our screens = 躲在屏幕后面。符合语境：人们用手机屏幕作为屏障。
- **第6题（B. misinterpreted）**：词义辨析。misinterpreted = 被误解。"our innocent social advances will be misinterpreted as 'weird'"（我们善意的社交举动会被误解为"奇怪的"）。前缀mis-表示"错误地"。
- **第7题（A. judged）**：词义辨析。"We fear we'll be judged"（我们害怕被人评判）。与上文rejection（被拒绝）并列，都属于社交恐惧。
- **第8题（D. unfamiliar）**：词义辨析。"Strangers are inherently unfamiliar to us"（陌生人对我们来说本质上是陌生的）。unfamiliar=不熟悉的，与stranger构成同源语义。
- **第9题（C. anxious）**：逻辑推理。既然陌生人是不熟悉的，自然交流会感到"anxious"（焦虑）。与下文uneasiness呼应。
- **第10题（B. turn）**：固定搭配。turn to = 求助于、转向。"we turn to our phones"（我们求助于手机）。
- **第12题（C. hurt）**：固定搭配。it doesn't hurt so bad = 感觉没那么糟。hurt这里指"造成伤害/不适"。
- **第13题（C. conversation）**：语境词义。Start a conversation = 开始一段对话。文章主题就是与陌生人交谈。
- **第14题（D. passengers）**：语境词义。fellow passengers = 同车乘客。前文说Chicago train commuters（芝加哥火车通勤者），所以是passengers。
- **第15题（C. predict）**：词义辨析。predict = 预测。"asked other people to predict how they would feel"（让其他人预测他们会有什么感觉）。
- **第16题（D. ride）**：语境词义。ride = 乘车经历。"their ride would be more pleasant if they sat on their own"（如果独坐，乘车体验会更愉快）。
- **第17题（A. went through）**：短语搭配。went through with = 完成、经历。"after they went through with the experiment"（在完成实验后）。
- **第18题（B. In fact）**：逻辑连词。In fact = 事实上，表示递进/转折。"In fact, these commutes were reportedly more enjoyable"（事实上，这些通勤据报道更愉快）。
- **第19题（D. since）**：逻辑连词。since = 因为，引导原因状语从句。"which makes absolute sense, since human beings thrive off of social connections"（这完全合理，因为人类靠社会联系而蓬勃发展）。
- **第20题（B. simple）**：固定搭配。It's that simple = 就是这么简单。总结全文主旨：与陌生人交谈能让你感到联系，道理就这么简单。

---

A new study suggests that contrary to 与…相反 most surveys, people are actually more stressed at home than at work. Researchers measured people's cortisol 皮质醇, which is a stress marker 压力标志物, while they were at work and while they were at home and found it higher at what is supposed to be a place of refuge 避难所.

"Further contradicting 进一步反驳 conventional wisdom 传统观念, we found that women as well as men have lower levels of stress at work than at home," writes one of the researchers, Sarah Damaske. In fact women even say they feel better at work, she notes. "It is men, not women, who report being happier at home than at work." Another surprise is that the findings hold true 适用于 for both those with children and without, but more so for nonparents 无子女者. This is why people who work outside the home have better health.

What the study doesn't measure is whether people are still doing work when they're at home, whether it is household work 家务 or work brought home from the office. For many men, the end of the workday is a time to kick back 放松. For women who stay home, they never get to leave the office. And for women who work outside the home, they often are playing catch-up 追赶 with household tasks. With the blurring 模糊 of roles, and the fact that the home front 家庭战线 lags well behind 远远落后 the workplace in making adjustments 调整 for working women, it's not surprising that women are more stressed at home.

But it's not just a gender thing. At work, people pretty much know what they're supposed to be doing: working, making money, doing the tasks they have to do in order to draw an income 获得收入. The bargain 交易 is very pure: Employee puts in hours of physical or mental labor and employee draws out 取出 life-sustaining 维持生命的 moola 钱.

On the home front, however, people have no such clarity 清晰. Rare is the household in which the division of labor 劳动分工 is so clinically 冷静地 and methodically 有条理地 laid out 安排. There are a lot of tasks to be done, there are inadequate 不足的 rewards for most of them. Your home colleagues—your family—have no clear rewards for their labor; they need to be talked into 说服 it, or if they're teenagers, even threatened with complete removal of all electronic devices. Plus, they're your family. You cannot fire your family. You never really get to go home from home.

So it's not surprising that people are more stressed at home. Not only are the tasks apparently infinite 无限的, the co-workers are much harder to motivate 激励.

##### Text1（21-25）

#### 长难句1

> A new study suggests that contrary to most surveys, people are actually more stressed at home than at work.

##### 句子拆分

主干：**A new study suggests that …**
- that引导宾语从句，从句主干：**people are actually more stressed at home than at work**
  - `contrary to most surveys`：插入语，"与大多数调查相反"
  - `more … than …`：比较级

##### 生词

contrary to：与…相反；survey：调查；stressed：有压力的；actually：实际上

##### 翻译

一项新研究表明，与大多数调查相反，人们实际上在家比在工作中压力更大。

##### 考点

**contrary to插入语+more…than比较级**；全文核心观点句，21题D选项依据——新研究与大多数调查相反→大多数调查认为家比工作更放松。

---

#### 长难句2

> Researchers measured people's cortisol, which is a stress marker, while they were at work and while they were at home and found it higher at what is supposed to be a place of refuge.

##### 句子拆分

主干：**Researchers measured people's cortisol … and found it higher**
- `which is a stress marker`：which非限制性定语从句，修饰cortisol
- `while they were at work and while they were at home`：两个while时间状语从句并列
- `at what is supposed to be a place of refuge`：介词短语作地点状语
  - `what is supposed to be a place of refuge`：what宾语从句，作at的宾语

##### 生词

measure：测量；cortisol：皮质醇；stress marker：压力标志物；be supposed to：应该；refuge：避难所

##### 翻译

研究人员测量了人们在工作和在家时的皮质醇（一种压力标志物），发现在本应是避难所的地方（家）皮质醇水平更高。

##### 考点

**which非限制性定语从句+两个while并列+what宾语从句**；研究方法描述句，21题的研究依据。

---

#### 长难句3

> "Further contradicting conventional wisdom, we found that women as well as men have lower levels of stress at work than at home," writes one of the researchers, Sarah Damaske.

##### 句子拆分

倒装句（直接引语前置，谓语writes倒装）：**"…" writes one of the researchers, Sarah Damaske**
- 直接引语内主干：**we found that …**
  - `Further contradicting conventional wisdom`：现在分词作状语
  - that引导宾语从句，从句主干：**women as well as men have lower levels of stress at work than at home**
    - `as well as`：和、以及
    - `lower … than …`：比较级

##### 生词

contradict：反驳、矛盾；conventional wisdom：传统观念；as well as：和、以及；level：水平

##### 翻译

研究人员之一莎拉·达马斯克写道："进一步与传统观念矛盾的是，我们发现女性和男性一样，在工作中的压力水平比在家低。"

##### 考点

**现在分词作状语+that宾语从句+比较级+倒装**；22题B选项的研究依据——男性在家更快乐，无子女者更明显。

#### 长难句4

> It is men, not women, who report being happier at home than at work.

##### 句子拆分

强调句：**It is men, not women, who report being happier at home than at work**
- `not women`：插入语
- `being happier at home than at work`：动名词作report的宾语
  - `happier … than …`：比较级

##### 生词

report：报告、说；happier：更快乐的

##### 翻译

是男性，而不是女性，说自己在家比在工作中更快乐。

##### 考点

#### 长难句5

> Another surprise is that the findings hold true for both those with children and without, but more so for nonparents.

##### 句子拆分

主干：**Another surprise is that …**
- that引导表语从句，从句主干：**the findings hold true for both those with children and without, but more so for nonparents**
  - `hold true for`：适用于
  - `both those with children and (those) without`：并列，with和without并列
  - `but more so for nonparents`：but连接，"但对无子女者更是如此"

##### 生词

surprise：意外之事；finding：发现；hold true for：适用于；nonparent：无子女者

##### 翻译

另一个意外是，这些发现对有子女和无子女的人都适用，但对无子女者更是如此。

##### 考点

**that表语从句+both…and并列+more so**；22题B选项的完整依据——男性+无子女=在家最快乐（childless husbands）。

#### 长难句6

> With the blurring of roles, and the fact that the home front lags well behind the workplace in making adjustments for working women, it's not surprising that women are more stressed at home.

##### 句子拆分

主干：**it's not surprising that women are more stressed at home**
- it是形式主语，真正主语是that引导的主语从句：`that women are more stressed at home`
- `With the blurring of roles, and the fact that …`：with复合结构作原因状语
  - `the blurring of roles`和`the fact that…`是with的两个并列宾语
  - `that the home front lags well behind the workplace in making adjustments for working women`：that同位语从句，解释fact
    - `lag well behind`：远远落后
    - `in making adjustments for working women`：介词短语作状语

##### 生词

blurring：模糊；role：角色；home front：家庭战线；lag well behind：远远落后；workplace：工作场所；adjustment：调整；working women：职业女性；it's not surprising that：毫不奇怪

##### 翻译

随着角色的模糊，以及家庭战线在为职业女性做出调整方面远远落后于工作场所这一事实，女性在家压力更大就不足为奇了。

##### 考点

**with复合结构+that同位语从句+it形式主语+that主语从句**；23题A选项依据——职业女性角色模糊=既是赚钱者又是家庭主妇。

#### 长难句7

> The bargain is very pure: Employee puts in hours of physical or mental labor and employee draws out life-sustaining moola.

##### 句子拆分

主干：**The bargain is very pure**
- 冒号后解释：`Employee puts in hours of physical or mental labor and employee draws out life-sustaining moola`
  - 两个并列分句：Employee puts in… 和 employee draws out…

##### 生词

bargain：交易、契约；pure：纯粹的；put in：投入；physical labor：体力劳动；mental labor：脑力劳动；draw out：取出、获得；life-sustaining：维持生命的；moola：钱（俚语）

##### 翻译

这种交易非常纯粹：员工投入数小时的体力或脑力劳动，然后获得维持生计的钱。

##### 考点

**冒号后并列分句解释**；24题词义题定位句——moola意为"钱/收入"，与draw an income呼应。

#### 长难句8

> Rare is the household in which the division of labor is so clinically and methodically laid out.

##### 句子拆分

倒装句（表语Rare前置引起全部倒装）：**Rare is the household in which …**
- 正常语序：The household in which the division of labor is so clinically and methodically laid out is rare.
- `in which the division of labor is so clinically and methodically laid out`：in which定语从句，修饰household

##### 生词

rare：罕见的；division of labor：劳动分工；clinically：冷静地、客观地；methodically：有条理地；lay out：安排、规划

##### 翻译

很少有家庭的劳动分工是如此冷静而有条理地安排的。

##### 考点

**全部倒装+in which定语从句**；25题B选项依据——家庭劳动分工很少清晰明确（seldom clear-cut）。

#### 长难句9

> Your home colleagues—your family—have no clear rewards for their labor; they need to be talked into it, or if they're teenagers, even threatened with complete removal of all electronic devices.

##### 句子拆分

并列句，分号连接
分句1主干：**Your home colleagues have no clear rewards for their labor**
- `—your family—`：同位语，解释home colleagues

分句2主干：**they need to be talked into it, or … even threatened with complete removal of all electronic devices**
- `if they're teenagers`：if条件状语从句，插入在or和even之间
- `talked into`和`threatened with`是并列的被动语态

##### 生词

home colleague：家庭同事（指家人）；reward：回报；labor：劳动；talk into：说服；teenager：青少年；threaten with：以…威胁；removal：移除；electronic device：电子设备

##### 翻译

你的家庭同事——你的家人——他们的劳动没有明确的回报；需要说服他们去做，或者如果他们是青少年，甚至要用完全移除所有电子设备来威胁。

##### 考点

**同位语+if条件句插入+并列被动语态**；25题D选项干扰来源——家人劳动没有明确回报，D选项说"经常得到充分回报"与原文相反。

#### 长难句10

并列句，not only前置引起部分倒装

##### Text1（21-25）

> 原文主题：新研究发现人们在家比在工作中压力更大，原因是家务分工不清晰、家人难激励、职业女性角色模糊

#### 21. According to Paragraph 1, most previous surveys found that home ______.

[A] was an unrealistic place for relaxation [B] generated more stress than the workplace [C] was an ideal place for stress measurement [D] offered greater relaxation than the workplace

✅答案：**D** 推理题。定位：第一段。`A new study suggests that contrary to most surveys, people are actually more stressed at home than at work`（新研究与大多数调查相反，人们在家比工作压力更大）。反推：大多数调查认为家比工作更放松。

- A：错误。was an unrealistic place for relaxation（是不现实的放松场所）无中生有。

For years, studies have found that first-generation college students 第一代大学生—those who do not have a parent with a college degree—lag 落后 other students on a range of 一系列 education achievement factors. But since such students are most likely to advance economically 经济上进步 if they succeed in higher education, colleges and universities have pushed for decades to recruit 招收 more of them. This has created "a paradox 悖论" in that recruiting first-generation students, but then watching many of them fail, means that higher education has "continued to reproduce 复制 and widen, rather than close" achievement gap 成就差距 based on social class, according to the depressing beginning of a paper forthcoming 即将发表的 in the journal Psychological Science.

But the article is actually quite optimistic, as it outlines 概述 a potential solution to this problem, suggesting that an approach (which involves a one-hour, next-to-no-cost 几乎零成本的 program) can close 63 percent of the achievement gap (measured by such factors as grades) between first-generation and other students.

The authors of the paper are from different universities, and their findings 发现 are based on a study involving 147 students (who completed the project) at an unnamed private university. 2. 细节题：定位到**数据和研究结论**（59.1%接受佩尔助学金、63%差距可缩小）
3. 推理题：基于**因果关系**（大学不谈论阶级优势→大学负有部分责任）
4. 常见干扰：与原文相反（narrowed gap vs widen gap, costless vs next-to-no-cost）、偷换概念（研究在私立大学进行vs学生在私立大学学习）、过度推断（many fail vs most have failed）
5. 态度/责任题：关注**seldom acknowledge、don't talk about**等否定表达，往往指向机构责任

Even in traditional offices, "the lingua franca 通用语 of corporate America 美国企业 has gotten much more emotional 情感化的 and much more right-brained 右脑的 than it was 20 years ago," said Harvard Business School professor Nancy Koehn. She started spinning off 列举 examples. "If you and I parachuted 空降到 back to Fortune 500 companies in 1990, we would see much less frequent use of terms like journey, mission 使命, passion 热情. There were goals, there were strategies, there were objectives 目标, but we didn't talk about energy; we didn't talk about passion."

Koehn pointed out that this new era of corporate vocabulary 企业词汇 is very "team"-oriented 以团队为导向的—and not by coincidence 并非巧合. "Let's not forget sports—in male-dominated 男性主导的 corporate America, it's still a big deal 重要的事. It's not explicitly 明确地 conscious; it's the idea that I'm a coach 教练, and you're my team, and we're in this together. There are lots and lots of CEOs in very different companies, but most think of themselves as coaches and this is their team and they want to win."

These terms are also intended to infuse 注入 work with meaning—and, as Khurana points out, increase allegiance 忠诚 to the firm. "You have the importation 引入 of terminology 术语 that historically used to be associated with non-profit organizations 非营利组织 and religious organizations: Terms like vision 愿景, values, passion, and purpose 目标," said Khurana.

This new focus on personal fulfillment 个人成就感 can help keep employees motivated 有动力的 amid 在…之中 increasingly loud debates over work-life balance 工作与生活平衡. The "mommy wars 妈妈战争" of the 1990s are still going on today, prompting 引发 arguments about why women still can't have it all 拥有一切 and books like Sheryl Sandberg's Lean In 《向前一步》, whose title has become a buzzword 流行词 in its own right 本身. Terms like unplug 断网, offline 离线, life-hack 生活技巧, bandwidth 带宽, and capacity 能力 are all about setting boundaries 设定界限 between the office and the home. But if your work is your "passion," you'll be more likely to devote yourself to 投身于 it, even if that means going home for dinner and then working long after the kids are in bed 睡觉.

But this seems to be the irony 讽刺 of office speak 办公室用语: Everyone makes fun of 取笑 it, but managers love it, companies depend on 依赖 it, and regular people 普通人 willingly absorb 接受 it. As Nunberg said, "You can get people to think it's nonsense 胡说八道 at the same time that you buy into it 相信它." In a workplace that's fundamentally 根本上 indifferent 漠不关心的 to your life and its meaning, office speak can help you figure out 弄明白 how you relate to 与…联系 your work—and how your work defines who you are.

3. 推理题：基于**上下文语境**（妈妈战争+女性不能拥有一切→《向前一步》为女性发声）
4. 判断题（which is true）：逐项回原文定位，注意**程度过重**的干扰项（depend on ≠ fundamental）
5. 常见干扰：与原文相反（more objective vs more emotional）、因果倒置（书引发争论vs争论催生书）、偷换概念（语言学家认为是胡说vs人们可以认为是胡说）

Many people talked of the 288,000 new jobs the Labor Department 劳工部 reported for June, along with the drop in the unemployment rate 失业率 to 6.1 percent, as good news. And they were right. For now it appears the economy is creating jobs at a decent pace 可观的速度. We still have a long way to go to get back to full employment 充分就业, but at least we are now finally moving forward at a faster pace.

However, there is another important part of the jobs picture 就业图景 that was largely overlooked 被忽视的. There was a big jump 激增 in the number of people who report voluntarily 自愿地 working part-time 兼职. This figure is now 830,000 (4.4 percent) above its year ago level.

Before explaining the connection to the Obamacare 奥巴马医改, it is worth making an important distinction 区别. Many people who work part-time jobs actually want full-time jobs 全职工作. They take part-time work because this is all they can get. An increase in involuntary 非自愿的 part-time work is evidence of weakness in the labor market and it means that many people will be having a very hard time making ends meet 收支相抵.

There was an increase in involuntary part-time in June, but the general direction 总体趋势 has been down. Involuntary part-time employment is still far higher than before the recession, but it is down by 640,000 (7.9 percent) from its year ago level.

We know the difference between voluntary and involuntary part-time employment because people tell us. The survey used by the Labor Department asks people if they worked less than 35 hours in the reference week 参考周. If the answer is "yes," they are classified as 被归类为 working part-time. The survey then asks whether they worked less than 35 hours in that week because they wanted to work less than full time or because they had no choice. They are only classified as voluntary part-time workers if they tell the survey taker 调查者 they chose to work less than 35 hours a week.

The issue of voluntary part-time relates to Obamacare because one of the main purposes was to allow people to get insurance 保险 outside of employment. For many people, especially those with serious health conditions 健康状况 or family members with serious health conditions, before Obamacare the only way to get insurance was through a job that provided health insurance.

However, Obamacare has allowed more than 12 million people to either get insurance through Medicaid 医疗补助计划 or the exchanges 交易平台. These are people who may previously have felt the need to get a full-time job that provided insurance in order to cover 保障 themselves and their families. With Obamacare there is no longer a link between employment and insurance.

##### 参考答案
41.D 42.E 43.G 44.A 45.C

#### 解析

**题型特点：** 段落小标题匹配题考查段落主旨概括能力。解题核心策略是找段落主题句（通常在首句或尾句），抓关键词原词复现和近义词复现。

**解题策略：**
1. **先读选项**，划出每个小标题的核心词（关键词），如alone, fear, present, grateful, unique path等
2. **再读段落**，重点关注段首、段尾，以及转折词（but, however）后的内容
3. **匹配关键词**：段落中反复出现的词或其近义词往往对应正确选项

**逐题解析：**

- **41.D（Most of your fears are unreal）**：本段多次出现fear一词。Will Smith引言"Fear is not real"（恐惧不是真实的）及段尾"fears are just the product of our luxuriant imagination"（恐惧只是丰富想象力的产物），直接对应D选项"大部分恐惧都是不真实的"。

- **42.E（Think about the present moment）**：段首"try to focus on the present moment"（试着关注当下），段尾"Happiness is...a mindset that can be designed into the present"（幸福是一种契合当下的心态），present一词首尾呼应，直接对应E选项。

- **43.G（There are many things to be grateful for）**：段中"appreciate the things you have"（感恩你所拥有的事物）与"smile and value their life"（笑对人生、珍视人生），appreciate/value与grateful近义复现，对应G选项。

- **44.A（You are not alone）**：段首"No matter how isolated you might feel...you should always remember that you are not alone"（不管你感觉多孤立...记住你并不孤单），isolated与alone形成反义→同义转换，直接对应A选项。

- **45.C（Pave your own unique path）**：段中"believe in yourself and be sure that your decision is the best"（相信自己，确信你的决定最好），段尾"your own thoughts, your own values, your own choices"（你自己的想法、价值观和选择），反复强调"自己的"，对应C选项"铺好属于自己的独特道路"。

**干扰项排除：**
- [B] Don't fear responsibility：虽然41段提到fear，但核心是"恐惧不真实"而非"不要害怕责任"
- [F] Experience helps you grow：引言段提到"losses make us mature"，但引言段不设题

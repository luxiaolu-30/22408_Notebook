## 详细解析

##### 翻译（15 分）

#### 原文

It's almost impossible to go through life without experiencing some kind of failure. People who do so probably live so cautiously that they go nowhere. Put simply, they're not real living at all. But, the wonderful thing about failure is that it's entirely up to us to decide how to look at it.

We can choose to see failure as "the end of the world," or as proof of just how inadequate we are. Or, we can look at failure as the incredible learning experience that it often is. Every time we fail at something, we can choose to look for the lesson we're meant to learn. These lessons are very important; they're how we grow, and how we keep from making that same mistake again. Failures stop us only if we let them. Failure can also teach us things about ourselves that we would never have learned otherwise.

For instance, failure can help you discover how strong a person you are. Failing at something can help you discover your truest friends, or help you find unexpected motivation to succeed.

#### 参考译文

人的一生几乎不可能不经历某种失败。那些从未失败过的人可能活得过于谨慎，以至于一事无成。简而言之，他们根本没有真正地生活。但是，失败的妙处就在于，完全由我们自己来决定如何看待它。

我们可以选择将失败看作"世界末日"，或是看作证明自己多么无能的证据。或者，我们可以把失败看作它通常所是的——一种令人难以置信的学习经历。每次我们在某件事上失败时，我们可以选择去寻找我们理应学到的教训。这些教训非常重要；它们是我们成长的方式，也是我们避免重蹈覆辙的方式。除非我们任由失败阻止我们，否则失败无法阻止我们前进。失败还能教会我们一些关于自己的事情，那是我们在别处永远也学不到的。

例如，失败可以帮助你发现自己是一个多么坚强的人。在某件事上的失败可以帮助你发现最真挚的朋友，或者帮助你找到意料之外的成功动力。

#### 重点词汇与句型

- **go through life**：度过一生，经历人生
- **cautiously**：谨慎地，小心翼翼地
- **put simply**：简而言之（= in short / in a word）
- **be entirely up to sb.**：完全取决于某人
- **inadequate**：不够好的，无能的，不充分的
- **incredible**：难以置信的，极好的
- **be meant to**：理应，注定要
- **keep from doing**：避免做某事，阻止做某事
- **only if**：只有……才（引导条件状语从句，翻译时常需前置）
- **otherwise**：否则，用别的方式

#### 长难句分析

**1. But, the wonderful thing about failure is that it's entirely up to us to decide how to look at it.**

- **结构**：主句 + that引导的表语从句（其中嵌套how引导的宾语从句）
- **拆分**：the wonderful thing about failure（主语）/ is（系动词）/ that it's entirely up to us to decide how to look at it（表语从句）
- **翻译要点**：be up to sb.=取决于某人；无灵主语it译为有灵主语"我们"，更符合汉语习惯
- **译文**：但是，失败的妙处就在于，完全由我们来决定如何看待它。

**2. Or, we can look at failure as the incredible learning experience that it often is.**

- **结构**：主句 + that引导的定语从句
- **拆分**：we can look at failure as the incredible learning experience（主句）/ that it often is（定语从句，修饰experience）
- **翻译要点**：incredible结合语境译为"难以置信的/极好的"；定语从句较短可前置译为"它通常所是的"
- **译文**：或者，我们可以把失败看作它通常所是的——一种令人难以置信的学习经历。

**3. Failures stop us only if we let them.**

- **结构**：主句 + only if引导的条件状语从句
- **拆分**：Failures stop us（主句）/ only if we let them（条件状语从句）
- **翻译要点**：only if=只有……才，条件状语从句翻译时需前置；them指代failures
- **译文**：除非我们任由失败阻止我们，否则失败无法阻止我们前进。（意译：只有我们允许时，失败才能阻止我们。）

##### 小作文（10 分）

#### 题目

**Directions:**

Write an email to introduce a historical scenic spot to international students and give them suggestions for visiting. Write your answer in about 100 words.

You should write neatly on the ANSWER SHEET. (10 points)

**场景说明**：假设你是李明，给国际学生写一封电子邮件，介绍一个历史名胜古迹（如故宫），并给出参观建议。属于"介绍+建议信"。

#### 高分范文

Dear International Students,

I am writing to introduce a must-visit historical scenic spot—the Forbidden City—and to offer some suggestions for your visit.

As you know, the Forbidden City, also known as the Palace Museum, embodies this nation's profound and diversified ancient culture with its magnificent architecture and precious treasures. It is a remarkable representation of Chinese imperial history spanning over 600 years.

During this trip, it is advisable to pay closer attention to the tourist introductions of each site, which include its origin, cultural background, and translations in multiple languages. Additionally, I suggest you visit early in the morning to avoid the crowds and wear comfortable shoes, as the museum covers a vast area.

I hope that you will enjoy the journey. Should you have any further questions, please do not hesitate to contact me.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

#### 范文翻译

亲爱的国际学生们：

我写信是想向你们介绍一处必游的历史名胜——紫禁城（故宫），并为你们的参观提供一些建议。

如你们所知，紫禁城又称故宫博物院，以其宏伟的建筑和珍贵的宝藏体现了这个国家博大精深、丰富多样的古代文化。它是中国皇家历史跨越六百多年的杰出代表。

在此次参观中，建议你们多关注每个景点的游客介绍，其中包括景点的由来、文化背景以及多种语言的翻译。此外，我建议你们清晨前往以避开人群，并穿舒适的鞋子，因为博物馆占地面积很大。

希望你们享受这次旅程。如有其他问题，请随时与我联系。

此致敬礼
李明

#### 重点句型与表达

1. **I am writing to introduce... and to offer some suggestions for...** —— 开头段万能句型，交代写信目的（介绍+建议）
2. **...also known as...** —— 又称/又名，用于补充介绍
3. **embody... with...** —— 以……体现……，用于描述文化/历史意义
4. **It is a remarkable representation of...** —— 它是……的杰出代表，用于介绍意义
5. **It is advisable to...** —— 建议……（比you should更正式得体）
6. **Additionally, I suggest you...** —— 此外，我建议你……（引出具体建议）
7. **Should you have any further questions, please do not hesitate to contact me.** —— 虚拟语气结尾，"如有问题请随时联系"，书信万能结尾
8. **Yours sincerely, Li Ming** —— 正式书信落款格式

##### 大作文（15 分）

#### 图表描述

**图表类型**：饼图（Pie Chart）

**图表内容**：某高校学生使用手机阅读的目的调查
- 学习知识（learning knowledge）：59.5%（占比最大）
- 打发时间（killing time）：21.3%
- 获取信息（acquiring information）：17.0%
- 其他（other purposes）：2.2%

#### 题目

**Directions:**

Write an essay based on the following chart. In your writing, you should
1) interpret the chart, and
2) give your comments.

You should write about 150 words on the ANSWER SHEET. (15 points)

#### 高分范文

The pie chart above clearly illustrates the purposes of mobile phone reading among students at a certain university. As is shown, learning knowledge takes the largest share, accounting for 59.5%, followed by killing time at 21.3% and acquiring information at 17.0%, while other purposes make up merely 2.2%.

What are the driving forces behind this phenomenon? Several factors contribute to this trend, but generally speaking, they may boil down to the following two aspects. Initially, with the rapid development of mobile technology, smartphones have become an indispensable tool for college students to acquire knowledge conveniently and efficiently. A wide range of educational apps and e-reading platforms enable students to study anytime and anywhere. Furthermore, the intense competition in modern society forces students to make full use of fragmented time, and mobile reading perfectly meets this need. However, it is worth noting that over 20% of students read merely to kill time, which indicates a certain lack of purpose in their reading habits.

In conclusion, while it is encouraging that the majority of students use mobile phones for learning, we should also guide them to make more purposeful and efficient use of their reading time.

#### 范文翻译

上方的饼图清晰地展示了某高校学生使用手机阅读的目的。如图所示，学习知识占比最大，达59.5%，其次是打发时间占21.3%和获取信息占17.0%，而其他目的仅占2.2%。

这一现象背后的驱动力是什么？有几个因素促成了这一趋势，但总的来说，可以归结为以下两个方面。首先，随着移动技术的快速发展，智能手机已成为大学生便捷高效地获取知识的不可或缺的工具。各种教育类应用和电子阅读平台使学生能够随时随地学习。此外，现代社会的激烈竞争迫使学生充分利用碎片化时间，而手机阅读恰好满足了这一需求。然而，值得注意的是，超过20%的学生阅读仅仅是为了打发时间，这表明他们的阅读习惯在一定程度上缺乏目的性。

总之，虽然大多数学生使用手机进行学习令人欣慰，但我们也应该引导他们更有目的、更高效地利用阅读时间。

#### 重点句型与表达

1. **The pie chart above clearly illustrates...** —— 饼图开头万能句型，引出图表主题
2. **As is shown, ... takes the largest share, accounting for X%, followed by... at Y%** —— 描述饼图数据的经典句式（最大项+排序）
3. **...make up merely X%** —— "仅占X%"，用于描述最小项
4. **What are the driving forces behind this phenomenon?** —— 过渡句，引出原因分析
5. **Several factors contribute to this trend, but generally speaking, they may boil down to...** —— 分析原因的万能过渡句（boil down to=归结为）
6. **...have become an indispensable tool for...** —— "已成为……不可或缺的工具"，用于论述重要性
7. **Furthermore, ... forces students to make full use of fragmented time** —— "此外，……迫使学生充分利用碎片化时间"
8. **In conclusion, while it is encouraging that..., we should also...** —— 结尾段万能句式（肯定+建议转折）

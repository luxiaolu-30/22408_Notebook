## 详细解析

##### 翻译（15 分）

#### 原文

In the late 18th century, William Wordsworth became famous for his poems about nature. And he was one of the founders of a movement called Romanticism, which celebrated the wonders of the natural world.

Poetry is powerful. Its energy and rhythm can capture a reader, transport them to another world and make them see things differently. Through carefully selected words and phrases, poems can be dramatic, funny, beautiful, moving and inspiring. No one knows for sure when poetry began but it has been around for thousands of years, even before people could write. It was a way to tell stories and pass down history. It is closely related to song and even when written it is usually created to be performed out loud. Poems really come to life when they are recited. This can also help with understanding them too, because the rhythm and sounds of the words become clearer.

#### 参考译文

在18世纪晚期，威廉·华兹华斯因其关于自然的诗歌而闻名。他是浪漫主义运动的创始人之一，该运动颂扬自然世界的奇迹。

诗歌是有力量的。它的能量和节奏能够吸引读者，将他们带到另一个世界，让他们以不同的方式看待事物。通过精心挑选的词语和短语，诗歌可以是戏剧性的、有趣的、感人的和鼓舞人心的。没有人确切知道诗歌是何时开始的，但它已经存在了数千年，甚至在人们会写字之前就已有之。它是一种讲述故事和传承历史的方式。它与歌曲密切相关，即使被写下来，也通常是为了大声表演而创作的。诗歌在被朗诵时才真正生动起来。这也有助于理解诗歌，因为词语的节奏和发音变得更加清晰。

#### 重点词汇与句型

- **Romanticism**：浪漫主义
- **celebrate**：颂扬，赞美（此处非"庆祝"）
- **wonders**：奇迹，奇观
- **rhythm**：节奏，韵律
- **capture**：吸引，俘获
- **transport sb. to...**：将某人带到/ transporting to another world
- **moving**：感人的
- **inspiring**：鼓舞人心的
- **be around**：存在，流传
- **pass down**：传承，传下来
- **come to life**：变得生动，活灵活现
- **recite**：朗诵，背诵

#### 长难句分析

**1. And he was one of the founders of a movement called Romanticism, which celebrated the wonders of the natural world.**

- **结构**：主句 + called...过去分词作后置定语 + which引导的非限制性定语从句
- **拆分**：he was one of the founders of a movement（主句）/ called Romanticism（过去分词作后置定语，修饰movement）/ which celebrated the wonders of the natural world（非限制性定语从句，修饰Romanticism）
- **翻译要点**：called Romanticism可前置译为"被称为浪漫主义的运动"；which定语从句单独成句，"该运动颂扬……"
- **译文**：他是浪漫主义运动的创始人之一，该运动颂扬自然世界的奇迹。

**2. No one knows for sure when poetry began but it has been around for thousands of years, even before people could write.**

- **结构**：but连接两个并列分句，第一个分句含when引导的宾语从句
- **拆分**：No one knows for sure（主句1）/ when poetry began（宾语从句）/ but it has been around for thousands of years（主句2）/ even before people could write（时间状语）
- **翻译要点**：know for sure=确切知道；has been around=存在/流传；even before=甚至在……之前
- **译文**：没有人确切知道诗歌是何时开始的，但它已经存在了数千年，甚至在人们会写字之前就已有之。

**3. It is closely related to song and even when written it is usually created to be performed out loud.**

- **结构**：and连接两个并列分句，第二个分句含when引导的时间状语从句（省略结构）和不定式目的状语
- **拆分**：It is closely related to song（第一个分句）/ and even when written（时间状语从句，when后省略it is）/ it is usually created to be performed out loud（第二个分句，不定式作目的状语）
- **翻译要点**：be related to=与……相关；when written=when it is written（被写下来时）；to be performed out loud=为了大声表演
- **译文**：它与歌曲密切相关，即使被写下来，也通常是为了大声表演而创作的。

##### 小作文（10 分）

#### 题目

**Directions:**

An art exhibition and a robot show are to be held on Sunday, and your friend David asks you which one he should go to. Write him an email to

1) make a suggestion, and
2) give your reason(s).


You should write about 100 words on the ANSWER SHEET. Do not use your own name in your email, use "Li Ming" instead. (10 points)

**场景说明**：假设你是李明，朋友David来信询问周日应该去艺术展览还是机器人展，你回信给出建议并说明理由。属于"建议信"。

#### 高分范文

Dear David,

I'm very glad to receive your letter asking for my advice on whether to go to the art exhibition or the robot show. In my opinion, compared with the robot show, the art exhibition would be a better choice. Here are the reasons.

Firstly, the art exhibition is an important tool for spreading new artistic ideas and promoting cultural development, and therefore can help you develop a deep understanding of culture and experience the emerging trend of art. Secondly, so far as I know, you have always been interested in traditional Chinese culture, and this exhibition features many classic Chinese paintings that I believe will certainly appeal to you.

I hope my suggestion is helpful. Wish you a wonderful time there. Do let me know if you need any further information.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

#### 范文翻译

亲爱的David：

很高兴收到你的来信，征求我对去艺术展览还是机器人展的建议。在我看来，与机器人展相比，艺术展览会是更好的选择。原因如下。

首先，艺术展览是传播新艺术理念和促进文化发展的重要工具，因此可以帮助你深入理解文化，体验新兴的艺术潮流。其次，据我所知，你一直对中国传统文化感兴趣，而这次展览以许多经典中国画为特色，我相信一定会吸引你。

希望我的建议对你有帮助。祝你在那里度过愉快的时光。如果需要更多信息，请告诉我。

此致敬礼
李明

#### 重点句型与表达

1. **I'm very glad to receive your letter asking for my advice on...** —— 建议信开头万能句型，交代写信背景
2. **In my opinion, compared with..., ...would be a better choice.** —— "在我看来，与……相比，……是更好的选择"，给出建议
3. **Here are the reasons.** —— "原因如下"，过渡句引出理由
4. **Firstly, ... is an important tool for..., and therefore can help you...** —— "首先，……是……的重要工具，因此可以帮助你……"
5. **Secondly, so far as I know, you have always been interested in...** —— "其次，据我所知，你一直对……感兴趣"
6. **I hope my suggestion is helpful.** —— "希望我的建议有帮助"，建议信结尾
7. **Wish you a wonderful time there.** —— "祝你在那里度过愉快时光"，祝福句
8. **Do let me know if you need any further information.** —— "如果需要更多信息请告诉我"，书信万能结尾

##### 大作文（15 分）

#### 图表描述

**图表类型**：线图（Line Chart）

**图表内容**：2012-2021年我国居民健康素养水平变化情况
- 2012年：8.80%
- 2015年：10.25%
- 2021年：25.40%

**趋势**：我国居民健康素养水平呈稳步上升趋势，从2012年的8.80%提升至2021年的25.40%，增幅显著。

#### 题目

**Directions:**

Write an essay based on the chart below. In your writing, you should
1) describe and interpret the chart, and
2) give your comments.

You should write about 150 words on the ANSWER SHEET. (15 points)

#### 高分范文

The line chart above clearly presents information about the health literacy level of Chinese residents from 2012 to 2021. As is illustrated, the proportion of citizens with basic health literacy experienced a steady increase, rising from 8.80% in 2012 to 10.25% in 2015, and then surging to 25.40% by 2021. The overall trend indicates a significant improvement in public health awareness over the past decade.

What are the driving forces behind this encouraging trend? Several factors contribute to this improvement. Initially, the government has implemented a series of health education programs and public health campaigns, which have effectively raised people's awareness of disease prevention and healthy lifestyles. Furthermore, with the rapid development of the Internet and new media, health information has become more accessible than ever, enabling citizens to acquire health knowledge conveniently. The COVID-19 pandemic in recent years has also heightened public attention to personal health and hygiene.

In conclusion, the steady growth in health literacy reflects the progress of public health education in China. It is expected that with continued efforts, this positive trend will persist, ultimately contributing to the realization of a "Healthy China."

#### 范文翻译

上方的线图清晰地展示了2012年至2021年中国居民健康素养水平的信息。如图所示，具备基本健康素养的公民比例稳步增长，从2012年的8.80%上升至2015年的10.25%，随后激增至2021年的25.40%。总体趋势表明过去十年间公众健康意识显著提升。

这一令人鼓舞的趋势背后的驱动力是什么？有几个因素促成了这一进步。首先，政府实施了一系列健康教育项目和公共卫生宣传活动，有效提高了人们的疾病预防和健康生活方式意识。此外，随着互联网和新媒体的快速发展，健康信息比以往任何时候都更容易获取，使市民能够便捷地获得健康知识。近年来的新冠疫情也提高了公众对个人健康和卫生的关注。

总之，健康素养的稳步增长反映了我国公共卫生教育的进步。预计在持续的努力下，这一积极趋势将会延续，最终为实现"健康中国"做出贡献。

#### 重点句型与表达

1. **The line chart above clearly presents information about... from... to...** —— 线图开头万能句型，引出图表主题和时间范围
2. **As is illustrated, ...experienced a steady increase, rising from X to Y, and then surging to Z** —— 描述线图数据的经典句式（稳步增长后激增）
3. **The overall trend indicates...** —— "总体趋势表明……"，用于总结图表趋势
4. **What are the driving forces behind this encouraging trend?** —— 过渡句，引出原因分析
5. **...have effectively raised people's awareness of...** —— "有效提高了人们对……的意识"
6. **...has become more accessible than ever, enabling citizens to...** —— "比以往更容易获取，使市民能够……"
7. **In conclusion, ... reflects the progress of...** —— 结尾段总结句，"反映了……的进步"
8. **It is expected that with continued efforts, this positive trend will persist** —— "预计在持续努力下，这一积极趋势将延续"

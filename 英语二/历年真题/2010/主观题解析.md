## 详细解析

##### 翻译（15 分）

#### 原文

"Sustainability" has become a popular word these days, but to Ted Ning, the concept will always have personal meaning. Having endured a painful period of unsustainability in his own life made it clear to him that sustainability-oriented values must be expressed through everyday action and choice.

Ning recalls spending a confusing year in the late 1990s selling insurance. He'd been through the dot-com boom and burst and, desperate for a job, signed on with a Boulder agency.

It didn't go well. "It was a really bad move because that's not my passion," says Ning, whose dilemma about the job translated, predictably, into a lack of sales. "I was miserable. I had so much anxiety that I would wake up in the middle of the night and stare at the ceiling. I had no money and needed the job. Everyone said, 'Just wait, you'll turn the corner, give it some time.'"

#### 参考译文

如今，"可持续性"已经成为了一个流行的词语。但是，对特德·宁来说，这个概念始终有着个人的意义。在经历了一段痛苦的、难以为继的个人生活之后，他清楚地认识到，以可持续为导向的价值观必须通过日常的行动和选择来体现。

宁回忆起在20世纪90年代末卖保险的那一年，那是一段令人迷惘的经历。他经历了网络经济的繁荣与破灭，迫切需要一份工作，于是与博尔德市的一家中介公司签了约。

事情进展并不顺利。"那确实是个很糟糕的选择，因为那并非我的激情所在，"宁说道。可以想见，他在工作上的困境自然而然地转化为了销售业绩的低迷。"我很痛苦。我焦虑到了极点，以至于半夜会醒来盯着天花板。我没有钱，又需要这份工作。所有人都说：'等等吧，总会有转机的，给它一点时间。'"

#### 重点词汇与句型

- **sustainability**：可持续性（由sustain + -ability构成，引申为"能维持下去的状态"）
- **unsustainability**：不可持续性，难以为继（此处指生活无法维持下去）
- **sustainability-oriented**：以可持续为导向的（复合形容词，名词+oriented表示"以……为导向的"）
- **endure**：忍受、经历（ endure a painful period 忍受痛苦的时期）
- **dot-com boom and burst**：网络经济的繁荣与破灭（dot-com指互联网公司）
- **desperate for**：迫切需要、极度渴望
- **dilemma**：困境、进退两难
- **translate...into...**：此处引申为"转化为"（非"翻译"之意）
- **predictably**：可以预见地、不出所料地
- **turn the corner**：迎来转机、渡过难关（固定短语）

#### 长难句分析

##### 长难句1

> Having endured a painful period of unsustainability in his own life made it clear to him that sustainability-oriented values must be expressed through everyday action and choice.

**句子拆分**：
- 主干：**Having endured... made it clear to him that...**
- `Having endured a painful period of unsustainability in his own life`：动名词短语作主语（现在分词完成式的动名词结构）
- `made it clear to him`：make + it（形式宾语）+ clear（宾补）+ to him（间接宾语）
- `that sustainability-oriented values must be expressed through everyday action and choice`：that从句作真正宾语

**翻译**：在经历了一段痛苦的、难以为继的个人生活之后，他清楚地认识到，以可持续为导向的价值观必须通过日常的行动和选择来体现。

##### 长难句2

> "It was a really bad move because that's not my passion," says Ning, whose dilemma about the job translated, predictably, into a lack of sales.

**句子拆分**：
- 主干：**says Ning**（倒装结构，引述动词在前）
- `It was a really bad move because that's not my passion`：直接引语
- `whose dilemma about the job translated, predictably, into a lack of sales`：非限制性定语从句，修饰Ning
  - `whose dilemma about the job`：主语
  - `translated...into...`：谓语+介词
  - `predictably`：插入语

**翻译**："那确实是个很糟糕的选择，因为那并非我的激情所在，"宁说道。不出所料，他在工作上的困境转化为了销售业绩的低迷。

##### 小作文（10 分）

#### 题目

**47. Directions:**

You have just come back from the U.S. as a member of a Sino-American cultural exchange program. Write a letter to your American colleague to

1) express your thanks for his/her warm reception;
2) welcome him/her to visit China in due course.

You should write about 100 words on ANSWER SHEET 2.

Do not sign your own name at the end of the letter. Use "Zhang Wei" instead.

Do not write your address. (10 points)

#### 高分范文

Dear John,

I am writing to express my heartfelt gratitude for the warm reception you gave me during my recent visit to the United States as a member of the Sino-American cultural exchange program. Your thoughtful arrangements, from the guided tours to those delightful family dinners, made my stay both comfortable and memorable. I learned a great deal about American culture and customs, thanks to your kindness and patience.

Now that I have returned to China, I would like to sincerely invite you to visit our country at your convenience. It would be my great honor to show you around and share our rich cultural heritage with you.

Looking forward to seeing you in China.

Best wishes,

Zhang Wei

#### 范文翻译

亲爱的约翰：

我写信是为了衷心感谢您在我最近作为中美文化交流项目成员访美期间给予我的热情接待。您周到的安排——从导游参观到愉快的家庭晚餐——使我的逗留既舒适又难忘。多亏了您的善意与耐心，我对美国文化和习俗有了很多了解。

如今我已回到中国，诚挚邀请您在方便时来我国访问。能带您四处参观并分享我们丰富的文化遗产，将是我莫大的荣幸。

期待在中国与您相见。

祝好！

张伟

#### 重点句型与表达

1. **I am writing to express my heartfelt gratitude for...**（我写信是为了衷心感谢……）——感谢信万能开头句
2. **Your thoughtful arrangements made my stay both comfortable and memorable.**（您周到的安排使我的逗留既舒适又难忘）——评价接待品质
3. **I learned a great deal about... thanks to your kindness and patience.**（多亏了您的善意与耐心，我对……有了很多了解）——说明收获
4. **Now that I have returned to..., I would like to sincerely invite you to visit... at your convenience.**（如今我已回到……，诚挚邀请您在方便时来……）——过渡并发出邀请
5. **It would be my great honor to show you around and share... with you.**（能带您参观并分享……将是我莫大的荣幸）——表达回访意愿
6. **Looking forward to seeing you in China.**（期待在中国与您相见）——结尾期待句

##### 大作文（15 分）

#### 图表描述

本题为**柱状图（Column Chart）**，标题为"Mobile-phone Subscriptions"（手机订阅量）。图表横轴为年份（2000年至2008年），纵轴为手机用户数量（单位：百万）。图中对比了**发展中国家（Developing Countries）**和**发达国家（Developed Countries）**的手机订阅量变化趋势。

主要数据：
- 2000年：发展中国家约0.5 billion，发达国家约0.5 billion，两者差距不大
- 2003年：发展中国家开始快速增长，发达国家缓慢增长
- 2005年：发展中国家约1.5 billion，发达国家约1 billion
- 2008年：发展中国家约4 billion，发达国家约1 billion，发展中国家是发达国家的4倍

核心趋势：发展中国家手机用户量从2000年起急剧增长，远超发达国家；发达国家增长缓慢，趋于平稳。

#### 题目

**48. Directions:**

In this section, you are asked to write an essay based on the following chart. In your writing, you should

1) interpret the chart and

2) give your comments.

You should write at least 150 words.

Write your essay on ANSWER SHEET 2. (15 points)

#### 高分范文

The column chart above illustrates the striking contrast in mobile-phone subscriptions between developing countries and developed countries from 2000 to 2008. As is shown, the number of mobile-phone users in developing countries experienced a dramatic increase from approximately 0.5 billion in 2000 to 4 billion in 2008, while that in developed countries grew modestly from 0.5 billion to 1 billion over the same period. By 2008, the figure for developing countries was roughly four times that of developed countries.

Several factors may account for this phenomenon. On the one hand, with the rapid economic growth in developing countries, the telecommunication industry has surged to meet the growing demand for interpersonal communication. The relatively low cost of mobile phones and flexible payment plans have made them accessible to a vast population. On the other hand, developed countries had already reached a high level of market saturation in mobile-phone subscriptions, leaving limited room for further expansion.

To sum up, the widespread use of mobile phones reflects the improvement of people's living standards and the advancement of telecommunication technology. While enjoying the convenience brought by mobile phones, we should also be aware of the potential negative effects, such as privacy issues and electronic waste. Only in this way can we ensure a sustainable development in the telecommunications industry.

#### 范文翻译

上面的柱状图展示了2000年至2008年发展中国家与发达国家手机订阅量之间的鲜明对比。如图所示，发展中国家的手机用户数量从2000年的约5亿急剧增长至2008年的40亿，而同期发达国家仅从5亿增长至10亿。到2008年，发展中国家的数字大约是发达国家的四倍。

以下几个因素可能解释这一现象。一方面，随着发展中国家经济的快速增长，电信产业蓬勃发展以满足人们对人际交往日益增长的需求。手机相对低廉的价格和灵活的付费方式使其为大量人口所接受。另一方面，发达国家在手机订阅量上已达到较高的市场饱和度，进一步扩张的空间有限。

总之，手机的广泛使用反映了人民生活水平的提高和通信技术的进步。在享受手机带来便利的同时，我们也应意识到其潜在的负面影响，如隐私问题和电子垃圾。只有这样，我们才能确保电信产业的可持续发展。

#### 重点句型与表达

1. **The column chart above illustrates the striking contrast in... between... and...**（上面的柱状图展示了……之间在……方面的鲜明对比）——图表描述开头句
2. **As is shown, the number of... experienced a dramatic increase from... to...**（如图所示，……的数量经历了从……到……的急剧增长）——描述增长趋势
3. **while that in... grew modestly from... to... over the same period**（而同期……仅从……温和增长至……）——对比描述
4. **By 2008, the figure for... was roughly four times that of...**（到2008年，……的数字大约是……的四倍）——倍数比较表达
5. **Several factors may account for this phenomenon. On the one hand... On the other hand...**（以下几个因素可能解释这一现象。一方面……另一方面……）——原因分析过渡句
6. **To sum up, ... reflects the improvement of... and the advancement of...**（总之，……反映了……的提高和……的进步）——总结句
7. **While enjoying the convenience brought by..., we should also be aware of the potential negative effects.**（在享受……带来便利的同时，我们也应意识到其潜在的负面影响）——辩证分析句
8. **Only in this way can we ensure a sustainable development in...**（只有这样，我们才能确保……的可持续发展）——倒装结尾句

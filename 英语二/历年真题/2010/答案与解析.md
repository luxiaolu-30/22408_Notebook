#### 重点解析

- **第1题（D）**：词义辨析。此处意为"被WHO认定/命名为"，designated意为"指定、命名"，最符合语境。criticized（批评）、appointed（任命）、commented（评论）均不符。
- **第2题（C）**：逻辑关系。此处描述"提高的警报级别紧随紧急会议之后"，followed表示"在……之后发生"，符合时间逻辑。prompted（促使）方向反了。
- **第3题（B）**：词义辨析。rising numbers表示"上升的数字/数量"，修饰病例数。digits指数位数字，amounts/sums多用于不可数名词或金额。
- **第4题（A）**：词义辨析。句首But表转折，与后文"mild symptoms"呼应，说明严重程度"温和"，moderate（温和的、中等的）正确。extreme（极端的）方向相反。
- **第6题（B）**：固定搭配。in the absence of为固定搭配，意为"在没有……的情况下"，符合"无需任何医疗即可痊愈"的语境。
- **第7题（D）**：固定搭配。come to global notice意为"引起全球关注"，come to one's notice/attention为常见搭配。
- **第9题（B）**：动词短语辨析。crop up意为"突然出现、冒出"，符合病例突然出现的语境。stay up（熬夜）、cover up（掩盖）不符。
- **第13题（D）**：词义辨析。infected意为"感染"，符合语境"已感染超过一百万人"。imparted（传授）、injected（注射）、immersed（浸入）均不符。
- **第16题（B）**：词义辨析。available意为"可获得的"，ahead of expectations搭配表示"比预期更早可获得"。feasible（可行的）、reliable（可靠的）不如available贴切。
- **第20题（B）**：固定搭配/词义辨析。caring for意为"照顾、照料"，与前文care构成同义复现，且health care workers与caring for infants为并列关系。

The longest bull run in a century of art-market history ended on a dramatic note with a sale of 56 works by Damien Hirst, “Beautiful Inside My Head Forever”, at Sotheby’s in London on September 15th 2008. All but two pieces sold, fetching more than £70m, a record for a sale by a single artist. It was a last victory. As the auctioneer called out bids, in New York one of the oldest banks on Wall Street, Lehman Brothers, filed for 申请 bankruptcy.

The world art market had already been losing momentum 势头、势力 for a while after rising bewilderingly since 2003. At its peak in 2007 it was worth some $65 billion, reckons 估算估计 Clare McAndrew, founder of Arts Economics, a research firm 研究机构—double the figure five years earlier. Since then it may have come down to $50 billion. But the market generates interest far beyond its size because it brings together great wealth, enormous egos, greed, passion and controversy in a way matched by few other industries.

In the weeks and months that followed Mr Hirst’s sale, spending of any sort became deeply unfashionable. In the art world that meant collectors stayed away from galleries and salerooms. Sales of contemporary art fell by two-thirds, and in the most overheated sector, they were down by nearly 90% in the year to November 2008. Within weeks the world’s two biggest auction houses, Sotheby’s and Christie’s, had to pay out nearly $200m in guarantees to clients who had placed works for sale with them.

The current downturn in the art market is the worst since the Japanese stopped buying Impressionists at the end of 1989. This time experts reckon that prices are about 40% down on their peak on average, though some have been far more fluctuant. But Edward Dolman, Christie’s chief executive, says: “I’m pretty confident we’re at the bottom.”

What makes this slump different from the last, he says, is that there are still buyers in the market. Almost everyone who was interviewed for this special report said that the biggest problem at the moment is not a lack of demand but a lack of good work to sell. The three Ds — death, debt and divorce — still deliver works of art to the market. But anyone who does not have to sell is keeping away, waiting for confidence to return.

##### Text1（21-25）

#### 长难句1

> The longest bull run in a century of art-market history ended on a dramatic note with a sale of 56 works by Damien Hirst, “Beautiful Inside My Head Forever”, at Sotheby’s in London on September 15th 2008.

##### 句子拆分

主干：**The longest bull run ended**

- `in a century of art-market history`：后置定语，修饰bull run
- `on a dramatic note`：方式状语，固定搭配
- `with a sale of 56 works by Damien Hirst`：伴随状语
- `“Beautiful Inside My Head Forever”`：同位语，拍卖会名称
- `at Sotheby’s in London on September 15th 2008`：地点+时间状语

##### 生词

bull run：牛市；on a … note：以……的基调；dramatic：戏剧性的；work：作品

##### 翻译

艺术品市场一个世纪以来最长的牛市，以2008年9月15日伦敦苏富比拍卖行一场拍卖会戏剧性落幕。这场拍卖展出了达米恩·赫斯特的56件作品，拍卖主题名为《美丽永驻我心》。

##### 考点

介词短语层层后置修饰，考研阅读高频结构；**同位语识别**，两个逗号之间内容是解释说明，读句子时可以先跳过抓主干。

#### 长难句2

> All but two pieces sold, fetching more than £70m, a record for a sale by a single artist.

##### 句子拆分

主干：**All but two pieces sold**

- `fetching more than £70m`：现在分词作结果状语（拍卖这件事带来的结果）
- `a record for a sale by a single artist`：名词短语作同位语，解释前面7000万英镑这个数字

##### 生词

all but：除……之外；fetch：售得（多少钱）；single artist：单个艺术家

##### 翻译

除两件作品外其余全部成交，总成交额超过7000万英镑，创下单个艺术家作品拍卖的最高纪录。

##### 考点

现在分词表**结果状语**；名词同位语，是英二阅读最喜欢的出题点，用来设置细节题。

#### 长难句3

> As the auctioneer called out bids, in New York one of the oldest banks on Wall Street, Lehman Brothers, filed for bankruptcy.

##### 句子拆分

主干：**one of the oldest banks filed for bankruptcy**

- `As the auctioneer called out bids`：as引导时间状语从句，“正当……的时候”
- `in New York`地点状语，倒装提前
- `Lehman Brothers`：同位语，bank的名字
- `on Wall Street`后置定语修饰bank

##### 生词

auctioneer：拍卖师；call out bids：喊价；file for bankruptcy：申请破产

##### 翻译

就在拍卖师喊出报价的那一刻，纽约华尔街历史最悠久的银行之一雷曼兄弟申请破产。

##### 考点

**as引导时间状语从句**；同位语插入割裂主干，阅读难点，21题“last victory”的定位句。

#### 长难句4

> At its peak in 2007 it was worth some $65 billion, reckons Clare McAndrew, founder of Arts Economics, a research firm—double the figure five years earlier.

##### 句子拆分

主干：**it was worth some $65 billion**

- `At its peak in 2007`：时间状语
- `reckons Clare McAndrew`：插入语（倒装，正常Clare reckons）
- `founder of Arts Economics`：同位语，修饰人
- `a research firm`：同位语，解释Arts Economics
- `—double the figure five years earlier`：破折号，补充说明650亿这个数字

##### 生词

at its peak：在顶峰；reckon：估计；figure：数字

##### 翻译

艺术经济研究公司创始人克莱尔·麦克安德鲁估算，2007年市场巅峰时期市值约650亿美元，是五年前的两倍。

##### 考点

多层同位语嵌套；倒装插入语，观点类句子，阅读中人物观点是命题重点。

#### 长难句5

> But the market generates interest far beyond its size because it brings together great wealth, enormous egos, greed, passion and controversy in a way matched by few other industries.

##### 句子拆分

主干：**the market generates interest far beyond its size** `because`引导原因状语从句： 从句主干：**it brings together A,B,C,D,E**

- `in a way`：方式状语
- `matched by few other industries`：过去分词作后置定语修饰way

##### 生词

generate interest：引发关注；far beyond：远远超过；enormous ego：强烈的自我；controversy：争议；match：匹敌

##### 翻译

但这个市场引发的关注远超它本身的规模，因为它以一种几乎没有其他行业能企及的方式，汇聚了巨额财富、膨胀的自我、贪婪、激情与争议。

##### 考点

because原因状语从句；过去分词后置定语；23题B选项出处句。

#### 长难句6

> In the weeks and months that followed Mr Hirst’s sale, spending of any sort became deeply unfashionable.

##### 句子拆分

主干：**spending became deeply unfashionable**

- `In the weeks and months`时间状语
- `that followed Mr Hirst’s sale`定语从句，修饰weeks and months
- `of any sort`后置定语修饰spending

##### 生词

unfashionable：不再流行；这里引申：人们不再愿意花钱

##### 翻译

赫斯特作品拍卖之后的数月里，任何形式的消费都变得不再时髦。

定语从句；**熟词僻义 unfashionable**（22题定位句，单词字面“不时尚”，文中指消费行为遇冷）。

#### 长难句7

> Sales of contemporary art fell by two-thirds, and in the most overheated sector, they were down by nearly 90% in the year to November 2008.

##### 句子拆分

并列句 and 连接两个分句 分句1：Sales fell by two-thirds 分句2：they were down by nearly 90%

- `of contemporary art`定语修饰sales；`in the most overheated sector`状语；`to November 2008`时间截止点

##### 生词

contemporary art：当代艺术；fall by：下降了；overheated：过热的；sector：领域

##### 翻译

当代艺术品销售额下跌三分之二；在最热的板块，截至2008年11月，一年内跌幅接近90%。

#### 长难句8

> What makes this slump different from the last, he says, is that there are still buyers in the market.

##### 句子拆分

主干：**What makes this slump different from the last is that …**

- `What makes this slump different from the last`：主语从句，整句话作主语
- `he says`插入语
- `is that …`表语从句

##### 生词

slump：衰退；the last = the last slump 上一次市场低迷

##### 翻译

他说，这次萧条和上一次的不同之处在于，市场上仍然存在买家。

##### 考点

**主语从句 + 表语从句**，复合名词性从句，英二高频语法。

#### 长难句9

> Almost everyone who was interviewed for this special report said that the biggest problem at the moment is not a lack of demand but a lack of good work to sell.

##### 句子拆分

主干：**Almost everyone said that …**

- `who was interviewed for this special report`定语从句修饰everyone
- `that`引导宾语从句，宾语从句内部 `not … but …` 不是…而是…

##### 生词

interview：采访；at the moment：当下；a lack of：缺乏；demand：需求

##### 翻译

几乎所有为本专题报道接受采访的人都认为，当下最大的问题不是缺乏需求，而是缺少可供出售的优质作品。

##### 考点

定语从句嵌套宾语从句；**not…but… 并列结构**，阅读中转折是重点命题位置。

#### 长难句10

> The three Ds — death, debt and divorce — still deliver works of art to the market. But anyone who does not have to sell is keeping away, waiting for confidence to return.

##### 句子拆分

句1主干：The three Ds still deliver works of art `—death, debt and divorce—`插入同位语解释3D 句2主干：anyone is keeping away `who does not have to sell`定语从句修饰anyone `waiting for confidence to return`现在分词伴随状语

##### 生词

deliver：输送；keep away：观望、不入场；confidence：市场信心

##### 翻译

三个D因素——死亡、债务、离婚——依然会把艺术品推向市场。但那些非必须出售艺术品的人，都在观望，等待市场信心回暖。

##### 考点

同位语；定语从句；现在分词伴随状语；24题定位句。

#### 21. In the first paragraph, Damien Hirst’s sale was referred to as “a last victory” because ______.

[A] the art market had witnessed a succession of victories [B] the auctioneer finally got the two pieces at the highest bids [C] Beautiful Inside My Head Forever won over all masterpieces [D] it was successfully made just before the world financial crisis

✅答案：**D** 定位：第一段末尾。赫斯特拍卖成交的同一时刻，雷曼兄弟破产，金融危机爆发。这场拍卖是金融危机来临前最后的成功。

- A：错误。a succession of victories（一连串胜利）无中生有。文章只说这是**最后一场胜利**，没有说之前连续不断胜利。
- B：错误。原文All but two pieces sold，意思是除两件外都卖掉了，并不是拍卖师高价拍下这两件。偷换概念。
- C：错误。won over all masterpieces（胜过所有杰作）文中完全没有提及。
- D：正确。拍卖恰好在全球金融危机爆发之前完成。

#### 22. By saying “spending of any sort became deeply unfashionable” (Para.3), the author suggests that ______.

[A] collectors were no longer actively involved in art-market auctions [B] people stopped every kind of spending and stayed away from galleries [C] art collection as a fashion had lost its appeal to a great extent [D] works of art in general had gone out of fashion so they were not worth buying

✅答案：**A** 定位：第三段第1、2句。这句话之后马上解释：收藏家远离画廊和拍卖行。

- A：正确。收藏家不再积极参与艺术品拍卖活动。
- B：错误。every kind of spending（停止一切消费）程度过重。原文只是艺术品相关消费遇冷，不是所有消费全部停止，**绝对化表述**。
- C：错误。干扰项。unfashionable 不是指“收藏作为时尚失去吸引力”，而是大家不愿意花钱买艺术品。这是经济问题，不是时尚潮流问题，属于曲解词义。
- D：错误。艺术品不是因为“过时不值得买”，而是金融危机大家没钱不敢买。因果理解错误。

#### 23. Which of the following statements is NOT true?

[A] Sales of contemporary art fell dramatically from 2007 to 2008. [B] The art market surpassed many other industries in momentum. [C] The art market generally went downward in various ways. [D] Some art dealers were awaiting better chances to come.

✅答案：**B**（选错误项） 定位：第二段末句 `it brings together ... in a way matched by few other industries`。

> 原句含义：艺术品市场汇聚财富、欲望等特质，**这一点几乎没有行业能匹敌**；不是说艺术品市场的**发展势头(momentum)**超过其他行业。

- A：正确。第三段，当代艺术品销售额大幅下跌，符合原文。
- B：错误。偷换概念。原文能匹敌的是市场汇聚人性欲望的特点，不是 momentum（发展势头）。
- C：正确。全文都在讲艺术品市场多方面下滑。
- D：正确。文章结尾：不需要卖画的人保持观望，等待信心回归，也就是等待更好时机。

#### 24. The three Ds mentioned in the last paragraph are ______.

I was addressing 发表演讲 a small gathering in a suburban Virginia living room—a women's group that had invited men to join them. Throughout the evening, one man had been particularly talkative 健谈的, frequently offering ideas and anecdotes 奇闻轶事, while his wife sat silently 安静地 beside him on the couch 沙发. Toward the end of the evening, I commented that women frequently complain that their husbands don't talk to them. This man quickly nodded in agreement 点头同意. He gestured 做手势 toward his wife and said, "She's the talker in our family." The room burst into laughter 哄堂大笑; the man looked puzzled 困惑的 and hurt 受伤的. "It's true," he explained. "When I come home from work I have nothing to say. If she didn't keep the conversation going, we'd spend the whole evening in silence."

This episode 插曲 crystallizes 清晰体现 the irony 讽刺 that although American men tend to talk more than women in public situations, they often talk less at home. And this pattern is wreaking havoc 造成严重破坏 with marriage.

The pattern was observed by political scientist Andrew Hacker in the late 1970s. Sociologist Catherine Kohler Riessman reports in her new book Divorce Talk that most of the women she interviewed—but only a few of the men—gave lack of communication as the reason for their divorces. Given the current divorce rate of nearly 50 percent, that amounts to 相当于 millions of cases in the United States every year—a virtual epidemic 实际上的流行病 of failed conversation.

In my own research, complaints from women about their husbands most often focused not on tangible inequities 有形的不平等 such as having given up the chance for a career to accompany a husband to his, or doing far more than their share of daily life-support work like cleaning, cooking and social arrangements. Instead, they focused on communication: "He doesn't listen to me." "He doesn't talk to me." I found, as Hacker observed years before, that most wives want their husbands to be, first and foremost 首先也是最重要的, conversational partners 对话伙伴, but few husbands share this expectation of their wives.

In short, the image that best represents the current crisis is the stereotypical 老套的 cartoon scene of a man sitting at the breakfast table with a newspaper held up in front of his face, while a woman glares at 怒视 the back of it, wanting to talk.

Many Americans regard 看待 认为 the jury system 陪审团制度 as a concrete expression 具体体现 of crucial democratic values 关键民主价值, including the principles that all citizens who meet minimal qualifications 最低资格 of age and literacy 读写能力 are equally competent 同等胜任 to serve on juries 担任陪审员; that jurors should be selected randomly 随机地 from a representative cross section 代表性横截面 of the community; that no citizen should be denied the right to serve on a jury on account of 由于 race种族, religion宗教, sex性别, or national origin 民族血统; that defendants 被告 are entitled to 有权 trial by their peers 同辈审判; and that verdicts 裁决 should represent the conscience 良知 of the community and not just the letter of the law 法律条文. The jury is also said to be the best surviving example of direct rather than representative democracy 直接民主而非代议制民主. In a direct democracy, citizens take turns governing themselves 轮流治理自己, rather than electing representatives to govern for them.

But as recently as in 1968, jury selection procedures 陪审团选拔程序 conflicted with 与…冲突 these democratic ideals. In some states, for example, jury duty was limited to persons of supposedly superior intelligence 较高智力, education, and moral character 品德. Although the Supreme Court 最高法院 of the United States had prohibited intentional 有意图的 racial discrimination 故意种族歧视 in jury selection as early as the 1880 case of Strauder v. West Virginia, the practice of selecting 遴选 so-called elite 精英 or blue-ribbon juries 蓝带陪审团 provided a convenient way around this and other antidiscrimination laws 反歧视法律.

The system also failed to regularly 定期的 include women on juries until the mid-20th century. Although women first served on state juries in Utah in 1898, it was not until the 1940s that a majority of states made women eligible 使女性有资格 for jury duty. Even then several states automatically exempted 自动免除 women from jury duty unless they personally asked to have their names included on the jury list. This practice was justified by 被证明合理 the claim that women were needed at home, and it kept juries unrepresentative 不具代表性 of women through the 1960s.

In 1968, the Congress of the United States 美国国会 passed the Jury Selection and Service Act 陪审团选拔与服务法, ushering in 开创 a new era of democratic reforms for the jury. This law abolished 废除 special educational requirements for federal jurors 联邦陪审员 and required them to be selected at random from a cross section of the entire community. In the landmark 具有里程碑意义的 1975 decision Taylor v. Louisiana, the Supreme Court extended the requirement that juries be representative of all parts of the community to the state level. The Taylor decision also declared sex discrimination in jury selection to be unconstitutional 违宪的 and ordered states to use the same procedures for selecting male and female jurors.

##### 新题型 Part B（41-45）

#### 题型说明

**判断正误题（True/False）**：阅读一篇关于鸟类编队飞行与航空燃油效率的文章，判断5个陈述的正误。选择T（正确）或F（错误）。此题型仅在2010年出现，后改为多项对应/小标题等题型。

#### 原文

**Copying Birds May Save Aircraft Fuel**

Both Boeing and Airbus have trumpeted（吹嘘、大力宣传）the efficiency of their newest aircraft, the 787 and 350 respectively. Their clever designs and lightweight composites（复合材料）certainly make a difference. But a group of researchers at Stanford University, led by Ilan Kroo, has suggested that airlines could take a more naturalistic approach to cutting jet-fuel use and it would not require them to buy new aircraft.

The answer, says Dr Kroo, lies with birds. Since 1914, scientists have known that birds flying in formation — a V-shape — expend less energy. The air flowing over a bird's wings curls upwards behind the wingtips, a phenomenon known as upwash（上升气流）. Other birds flying in the upwash experience reduced drag（阻力）, and spend less energy propelling（推动）themselves. Peter Lissaman, an aeronautics（航空学）expert who was formerly at Caltech and the University of Southern California, has suggested that a formation of 25 birds might enjoy a range increase of 71%.

When applied to aircraft, the principles are not substantially different. Dr Kroo and his team modeled what would happen if three passenger jets departing from Los Angeles, San Francisco and Las Vegas were to assemble over Utah, assume an inverted V-formation, occasionally change places so all could have a turn in the most favourable positions, and proceed to London. They found that the aircraft consumed as much as 15% less fuel (coupled with a reduction in carbon-dioxide output). Nitrogen-oxide emissions during the cruising portions of the flight fell by around a quarter.

There are, of course, knots to be worked out（需要解决的问题）. One consideration is safety, or at least the perception of it. Would passengers feel comfortable travelling in companion? Dr Kroo points out that the aircraft could be separated by several nautical miles（海里）, and would not be in the intimate groupings favoured by display teams like the Red Arrows. A passenger peering out of the window might not even see the other planes. Whether the separation distances involved would satisfy air-traffic-control regulations is another matter, although a working group at the International Civil Aviation Organisation has included the possibility of formation flying in a blueprint for new operational guidelines.

It remains to be seen how weather conditions affect the air flows that make formation flight more efficient. In zones of increased turbulence（湍流）, the planes' wakes（尾流）will decay more quickly and the effect will diminish. Dr Kroo says this is one of the areas his team will investigate further. It might also be hard for airlines to co-ordinate the departure times and destinations of passenger aircraft in a way that would allow them to gain from formation flight. Cargo aircraft, in contrast, might be easier to reschedule, as might routine military flight.

As it happens, America's armed forces are on the case already. Earlier this year the country's Defence Advanced Research Projects Agency announced plans to pay Boeing to investigate formation flight, though the programme has yet to begin. There are reports that some military aircraft flew in formation when they were low on fuel during the Second World War, but Dr Lissaman says they are unsubstantiated（未经证实的）. "My father was an RAF pilot and my cousin the skipper of a Lancaster lost over Berlin," he adds. So he should know.

##### 参考答案

#### 解析

**题型特点**：判断正误题要求考生根据原文判断陈述的真假。难点在于部分题目并非简单的细节对应，而是需要理解原文的逻辑关系，尤其注意原文中的否定词、转折词（but）、不确定表达（might, remains to be seen, unsubstantiated）等。

**解题策略**：
1. 先读题目关键词，定位到原文对应段落
2. 逐句对比题目陈述与原文信息，注意同义替换
3. 特别留意原文中的否定、转折、推测性表达，这些往往是T/F的关键区分点
4. 注意"过度推断"陷阱：原文未明确确认的内容不能选T

- **第41题（F）**：原文首段末句明确指出该研究"would not require them to buy new aircraft"（不需要购买新飞机），与题目"促进新飞机销售"相反。
- **第42题（T）**：原文第二段指出"Other birds flying in the upwash experience reduced drag, and spend less energy propelling themselves"，与题目表述一致，drag=resistance，spend less energy=save energy。
- **第43题（F）**：原文第四段"Would passengers feel comfortable travelling in companion?"为疑问句，并未给出肯定结论；且"看不到其他飞机"是说明间距大，并非舒适的原因。
- **第44题（T）**：原文第五段首句"It remains to be seen how weather conditions affect the air flows"意为"尚待观察"，与"not yet been clearly defined"同义。
- **第45题（F）**：原文末段提到"there are reports that...but Dr. Lissaman says they are unsubstantiated"（有报道但未经证实），与题目"has been documented"（已有记载）矛盾。

#### 重点解析

- **第1题（B）**：词义辨析。concluded意为"得出结论"，"studies have concluded that..."即"研究得出结论"，符合学术语境。denied（否认）、doubted（怀疑）、ensured（确保）不符。
- **第2题（A）**：词义辨析。protective意为"有保护作用的"，"being overweight is actually protective"即超重实际上有保护作用，与上句"超重人群患病风险更低"呼应。dangerous（危险的）与文意相反。
- **第3题（C）**：逻辑连词。Likewise意为"同样地"，连接前文"较重的女性更不易缺钙"与后文"老年人适度超重是健康指标"，两例并列。Instead（反而）、However（然而）表转折，不符。
- **第4题（A）**：词义辨析。indicator意为"指标、标志"，"an indicator of good health"即健康的指标。objective（目标）、origin（起源）、example（例子）不符语境。
- **第5题（D）**：词义辨析。concern意为"关注、忧虑"，"Of even greater concern is the fact that..."即"更令人关注的是……"。impact（影响）、relevance（相关性）、assistance（帮助）不符。
- **第6题（A）**：固定搭配。in terms of意为"根据、就……而言"，"defined in terms of body mass index"即"根据BMI来定义"。in case of（万一）、in favor of（支持）、in respects of（关于）不符。
- **第7题（C）**：词义辨析。equals意为"等于"，"BMI equals body mass divided by the square of height"即"BMI等于体重除以身高的平方"。measures（测量）、determines（决定）、modifies（修改）不符。
- **第8题（C）**：固定搭配。in turn意为"进而、依次"，"Obesity, in turn, can be divided into..."即"肥胖进而可分为……"，表递进。in essence（本质上）、in contrast（相反）、in part（部分地）不符。
- **第9题（D）**：词义辨析。straightforward意为"简单明了的"，"such numerical standards seem straightforward"即"这些数字标准看似简单明了"，后文"they are not"表转折。complicated（复杂的）与文意相反。
- **第10题（B）**：连词。while意为"而"，表对比，"Some people with a high BMI are...fit, while others with a low BMI may be in poor shape"即高BMI的人可能很健康，而低BMI的人可能身体不佳。
- **第11题（A）**：词义辨析。in poor shape意为"身体状况差"，shape（体形、状态）。spirit（精神）、balance（平衡）、taste（品味）不符。
- **第12题（B）**：词义辨析。qualify as意为"符合……的标准/被归类为"，"football players qualify as obese"即足球运动员被归类为肥胖。start as（作为……开始）、retire as（作为……退休）、stay as（保持为）不符。
- **第13题（C）**：词义辨析。normal意为"正常的"，"a normal BMI"即正常的BMI，与前文"high body fat"形成对比。strange（奇怪的）、changeable（多变的）、constant（恒定的）不符。
- **第14题（D）**：词义辨析。tendency意为"趋势、倾向"，"have a tendency to label obesity as a disgrace"即有将肥胖视为耻辱的倾向。option（选择）、reason（理由）、opportunity（机会）不符语境。
- **第15题（B）**：词义辨析。pictured意为"被拍照、被描绘"，"are sometimes pictured in the media with their faces covered"即有时在媒体中被打码出镜。employed（雇佣）、imitated（模仿）、monitored（监控）不符。
- **第16题（D）**：固定搭配。associated with意为"与……相关联"，"Stereotypes associated with obesity"即与肥胖相关的刻板印象。compared with（与……比较）、combined with（与……结合）、settled with（与……和解）不符。
- **第17题（A）**：副词。Even意为"甚至"，"Even very young children tend to look down on the overweight"即连很小的孩子都倾向于看不起超重者，表递进。Still（仍然）、Yet（然而）、Only（仅仅）不符。
- **第18题（D）**：固定搭配。grounded in意为"基于、根源于"，"grounded in health concerns"即基于健康考虑。despised（鄙视）、corrected（纠正）、ignored（忽视）不符。
- **第19题（C）**：词义辨析。policies意为"政策"，"anti-obesity policies"即反肥胖政策。discussions（讨论）、businesses（企业）、studies（研究）不如policies贴切。
- **第20题（B）**：介词。against意为"反对、抗击"，"campaign against childhood obesity"即反对儿童肥胖的运动。for（支持）、with（与……一起）、without（没有）不符。

#### 长难句分析

##### 长难句1

> A number of studies have concluded that normal-weight people are in fact at higher risk of some diseases compared to those who are overweight.

**句子拆分**：
- 主干：**A number of studies have concluded that...**
- `that normal-weight people are...at higher risk of some diseases`：that引导宾语从句，作concluded的宾语
- `compared to those who are overweight`：过去分词短语作状语，表比较
- `who are overweight`：who引导定语从句，修饰those

##### 长难句2

> Negative attitudes toward obesity, grounded in health concerns, have stimulated a number of anti-obesity policies.

**句子拆分**：
- 主干：**Negative attitudes...have stimulated a number of anti-obesity policies**
- `toward obesity`：介词短语作定语，修饰attitudes
- `grounded in health concerns`：过去分词短语作插入语，解释attitudes的根源

What would you do with $590m? This is now a question for Gloria Mackenzie, an 84-year-old widow 寡妇 who recently emerged 出现 from her small, tin-roofed 铁皮屋顶的 house in Florida to collect the biggest undivided 未分割的 lottery jackpot 彩票头奖 in history. If she hopes her new-found 新获得的 fortune will yield 产生 lasting feelings of fulfillment 满足感, she could do worse than 不妨 read "Happy Money" by Elizabeth Dunn and Michael Norton.

These two academics 学者 use an array of 一系列 behavioral research to show that the most rewarding 最有回报的 ways to spend money can be counterintuitive 反直觉的. Fantasies 幻想 of great wealth often involve visions of fancy 奢华的 cars and extravagant 奢侈的 homes. Yet satisfaction with these material purchases wears off 消退 fairly quickly. What was once exciting and new becomes old-hat 过时的; regret creeps in 悄悄袭来. It is far better to spend money on experiences, say Ms. Dunn and Mr. Norton, like interesting trips, unique meals or even going to the cinema. These purchases often become more valuable with time—as stories or memories—particularly if they involve feeling more connected to 与…有联系 others.

This slim 薄的 volume 书 is packed with 充满 tips to help wage slaves 工薪族 as well as lottery winners get the most "happiness bang for your buck 物有所值." It seems most people would be better off 更好 if they could shorten their commutes 通勤 to work, spend more time with friends and family and less of it watching television (something the average American spends a whopping 巨大的 two months a year doing, and is hardly jollier 更快乐 for it). Buying gifts or giving to charity 慈善 is often more pleasurable than purchasing things for oneself, and luxuries 奢侈品 are most enjoyable when they are consumed sparingly 有节制地. This is apparently the reason MacDonald's restricts 限制 the availability 供应 of its popular McRib—a marketing trick 营销伎俩 that has turned the pork sandwich into an object of obsession 痴迷.

Readers of "Happy Money" are clearly a privileged 特权的 lot 一群人, anxious about fulfillment, not hunger. Money may not quite buy happiness, but people in wealthier countries are generally happier than those in poor ones. Yet the link between feeling good and spending money on others can be seen among rich and poor people around the world, and scarcity 稀缺 enhances the pleasure of most things for most people. Not everyone will agree with the authors' policy ideas, which range from 从…到 mandating 强制 more holiday time to reducing tax incentives 税收激励 for American homebuyers 购房者. But most people will come away from 离开时 this book believing it was money well spent 花得值.

##### Text1（21-25）

#### 长难句1

> If she hopes her new-found fortune will yield lasting feelings of fulfillment, she could do worse than read "Happy Money" by Elizabeth Dunn and Michael Norton.

##### 句子拆分

主干：**she could do worse than read "Happy Money"**
- `If she hopes …`：if条件状语从句
  - `her new-found fortune will yield lasting feelings of fulfillment`：省略that的宾语从句，作hopes的宾语
- `by Elizabeth Dunn and Michael Norton`：介词短语修饰"Happy Money"

##### 生词

new-found：新获得的；fortune：财富；yield：产生；lasting：持久的；fulfillment：满足感；could do worse than：不妨、不如（做…是不错的选择）

##### 翻译

如果她希望新获得的财富能产生持久的满足感，她不妨读读伊丽莎白·邓恩和迈克尔·诺顿的《快乐金钱》。

##### 考点

**could do worse than** 固定搭配（表"不妨做某事"）；if条件句嵌套宾语从句；开篇引入书籍，25题主旨——如何从花钱中获得持久满足。

#### 长难句2

> These two academics use an array of behavioral research to show that the most rewarding ways to spend money can be counterintuitive.

##### 句子拆分

主干：**These two academics use an array of behavioral research to show that …**
- `to show that …`：不定式作目的状语
- that引导宾语从句，从句主干：**the most rewarding ways to spend money can be counterintuitive**
  - `to spend money`：不定式作后置定语，修饰ways

##### 生词

academic：学者；an array of：一系列；behavioral research：行为研究；rewarding：有回报的；counterintuitive：反直觉的

##### 翻译

这两位学者利用一系列行为研究表明，最有回报的花钱方式可能是反直觉的。

##### 考点

**不定式作目的状语+宾语从句**；全文核心观点句——最有回报的花钱方式是反直觉的（不是买物质，而是买体验）。

---

#### 长难句3

> Yet satisfaction with these material purchases wears off fairly quickly.

##### 句子拆分

主干：**satisfaction wears off fairly quickly**
- `with these material purchases`：介词短语修饰satisfaction

##### 生词

satisfaction：满足感；material purchase：物质购买；wear off：消退、逐渐消失；fairly：相当地

##### 翻译

然而，这些物质购买带来的满足感消退得相当快。

##### 考点

**wear off** 固定搭配；21题的逻辑铺垫——物质购买（大房子、好车）的满足感消退快，不是最有回报的购买。

---

#### 长难句4

> It is far better to spend money on experiences, say Ms. Dunn and Mr. Norton, like interesting trips, unique meals or even going to the cinema.

##### 句子拆分

主干：**It is far better to spend money on experiences**
- it是形式主语，真正主语是to spend money on experiences
- `say Ms. Dunn and Mr. Norton`：插入语（倒装，正常为Ms. Dunn and Mr. Norton say）
- `like interesting trips, unique meals or even going to the cinema`：举例，三个并列

##### 生词

far better：好得多；spend money on：把钱花在；experience：体验；unique：独特的

##### 翻译

邓恩女士和诺顿先生说，把钱花在体验上要好得多，比如有趣的旅行、独特的用餐，甚至去看电影。

##### 考点

**it作形式主语**；插入语倒装；like举例；21题B选项定位句——special tour（特别旅行）= interesting trips，是最有回报的购买。

#### 长难句5

> These purchases often become more valuable with time—as stories or memories—particularly if they involve feeling more connected to others.

##### 句子拆分

主干：**These purchases often become more valuable with time**
- `as stories or memories`：插入语，解释如何变得更有价值
- `particularly if they involve feeling more connected to others`：if条件状语从句
  - `feeling more connected to others`：动名词作involve的宾语

##### 生词

valuable：有价值的；with time：随着时间；particularly：尤其；involve：涉及；connected to：与…有联系

##### 翻译

这些购买往往随着时间变得更有价值——作为故事或回忆——尤其是当它们涉及与他人更紧密的联系时。

##### 考点

**if条件状语从句**；动名词作宾语；破折号插入语；解释为什么体验式消费更有回报——随时间增值、增强人际联系。

#### 长难句6

> It seems most people would be better off if they could shorten their commutes to work, spend more time with friends and family and less of it watching television (something the average American spends a whopping two months a year doing, and is hardly jollier for it).

##### 句子拆分

主干：**It seems most people would be better off**
- `most people would be better off`：省略that的表语从句
- `if they could shorten their commutes to work, spend more time with friends and family and less of it watching television`：if条件状语从句（虚拟语气）
  - 三个并列谓语：shorten, spend more time, spend less time
- 括号内插入语：`something the average American spends a whopping two months a year doing, and is hardly jollier for it`
  - `the average American spends a whopping two months a year doing`：省略that的定语从句，修饰something
  - `and is hardly jollier for it`：并列谓语，省略主语the average American

##### 生词

be better off：更好；shorten：缩短；commute：通勤；whopping：巨大的；hardly：几乎不；jollier：更快乐的（jolly的比较级）

##### 翻译

似乎大多数人如果能缩短通勤时间、多花时间陪朋友家人、少看电视（普通美国人一年花多达两个月看电视，却几乎没有因此更快乐），会过得更好。

##### 考点

**if虚拟条件句+并列谓语+括号内定语从句**；22题A选项依据——作者对美国人看电视持批评态度（花很多时间却不快乐）。

#### 长难句7

> Buying gifts or giving to charity is often more pleasurable than purchasing things for oneself, and luxuries are most enjoyable when they are consumed sparingly.

##### 句子拆分

并列句，and连接
分句1主干：**Buying gifts or giving to charity is often more pleasurable than purchasing things for oneself**
- 两个动名词并列作主语（Buying gifts, giving to charity）
- more…than比较结构

分句2主干：**luxuries are most enjoyable**
- `when they are consumed sparingly`：when时间状语从句

##### 生词

charity：慈善；pleasurable：令人愉快的；luxury：奢侈品；consume：消费；sparingly：有节制地、俭省地

##### 翻译

买礼物或捐赠慈善往往比为自己买东西更令人愉快，而奢侈品在有节制地消费时最令人享受。

##### 考点

**动名词作主语+more…than比较+when时间状语从句**；23题D选项的逻辑铺垫——奢侈品有节制地消费最享受=稀缺增加快乐。

#### 长难句8

> This is apparently the reason MacDonald's restricts the availability of its popular McRib—a marketing trick that has turned the pork sandwich into an object of obsession.

##### 句子拆分

主干：**This is apparently the reason**
- `MacDonald's restricts the availability of its popular McRib`：省略why的定语从句，修饰reason
- 破折号后`a marketing trick that has turned the pork sandwich into an object of obsession`：同位语，解释前面整件事
  - `that has turned the pork sandwich into an object of obsession`：that定语从句，修饰trick
  - `turn A into B`：把A变成B

##### 生词

An article in Scientific American has pointed out that empirical 实证的 research says that, actually, you think you're more beautiful than you are. We have a deep-seated 根深蒂固的 need to feel good about ourselves and we naturally employ a number of self-enhancing 自我提升 strategies to achieve this. Social psychologists have amassed 积累 oceans of research into what they call the "above average effect 高于平均效应", or "illusory superiority 虚幻的优越感", and shown that, for example, 70% of us rate ourselves as above average in leadership, 93% in driving and 85% at getting on well with others—all obviously statistical impossibilities 统计上不可能.

We rose-tint 美化 our memories and put ourselves into self-affirming 自我肯定的 situations. We become defensive 防御的 when criticised, and apply negative stereotypes 负面刻板印象 to others to boost 提升 our own esteem 自尊, we stalk around 大摇大摆地走 thinking we're hot stuff 了不起的人.

Psychologist and behavioural scientist Nicholas Epley oversaw 监督 a key study into self-enhancement and attractiveness. Rather than have people simply rate their beauty compared with others, he asked them to identify an original photograph of themselves from a lineup 一组照片 including versions that had been altered 修改 to appear more and less attractive. Visual recognition 视觉识别, reads the study, is "an automatic psychological process occurring rapidly and intuitively 直觉地 with little or no apparent conscious deliberation 有意识的思考". If the subjects 受试者 quickly chose a falsely flattering 虚假美化的 image—which most did—they genuinely 真诚地 believed it was really how they looked.

Epley found no significant gender difference in responses. In fact, those who thought that the images higher up the attractiveness scale 吸引力等级 were real directly corresponded with 与…一致 those who showed other markers 标志 for having higher self-esteem. "I don't think the findings that we have are any evidence of personal delusion 错觉," says Epley. "It's a reflection simply of people generally thinking well of themselves." If you are depressed 抑郁的, you won't be self-enhancing.

Knowing the results of Epley's study, it makes sense 说得通 that many people hate photographs of themselves so viscerally 发自内心地—on one level, they don't even recognise the person in the picture as themselves. Facebook, therefore, is a self-enhancer's paradise 天堂, where people can share only the most flattering photos, the cream 精华 of their wit 才智, style, beauty, intellect 智力 and lifestyles. It's not that people's profiles 个人资料 are dishonest, says Catalina Toma of Wisconsin-Madison University, "but they portray 描绘 an idealised 理想化的 version of themselves."

##### 本篇命题规律小结（英二阅读）

1. 心理学研究类文章：关注**研究方法→研究过程→研究发现→结论应用**的结构
2. 细节题：定位到**研究结论句**（shown that/found that），答案是数据/结论的概括
3. 词义题：结合**前文研究结论**（intuitively/automatic process）判断词义，不选字面意思
4. 推理题：答案是**原文的反推**（只分享美化照片→隐藏不美化一面）
5. 常见干扰：与原文相反（conscious vs no conscious deliberation）、偷换概念（automatic process vs self-defence）、片面选项（智力只是分享内容之一）

The concept of man versus machine 人机对抗 is at least as old as the industrial revolution, but this phenomenon tends to be most acutely 强烈地 felt during economic downturns 经济下滑 and fragile 脆弱的 recoveries. And yet, it would be a mistake to think we are right now simply experiencing the painful side of a boom and bust cycle 繁荣与萧条周期. Certain jobs have gone away for good 永远消失, outmoded 过时 by machines. Since technology has such an insatiable 贪得无厌的 appetite for eating up 吞噬 human jobs, this phenomenon will continue to restructure 重组 our economy in ways we can't immediately foresee.

When there is exponential 指数级的 improvement in the price and performance of technology, jobs that were once thought to be immune from 免于 automation suddenly become threatened. This argument has attracted a lot of attention, via the success of the book Race Against the Machine, by Erik Brynjolfsson and Andrew McAfee, who both hail from 来自 MIT's Center for Digital Business.

This is a powerful argument, and a scary one. And yet, John Hagel, author of The Power of Pull and other books, says Brynjolfsson and McAfee miss the reason why these jobs are so vulnerable 脆弱的 to technology in the first place 首先.

Hagel says we have designed jobs in the U.S. that tend to be "tightly scripted 严格照本宣科的" and "highly standardized 高度标准化的" ones that leave no room for 没有空间 "individual initiative 个人主动性 or creativity." In short, these are the types of jobs that machines can perform much better at than human beings. That is how we have put a giant target sign 巨大靶心 on the backs of American workers, Hagel says.

It's time to reinvent 彻底改造 the formula 模式 for how work is conducted, since we are still relying on a very 20th century notion of work, Hagel says. In our rapidly changing economy, we more than ever 比以往更 need people in the workplace who can take initiative and exercise their imagination "to respond to unexpected events." That's not something machines are good at. They are designed to perform very predictable 可预测的 activities.

As Hagel notes, Brynjolfsson and McAfee indeed touched on 提及 this point in their book. We need to reframe 重新定义 race against the machine as race with the machine. In other words, we need to look at the ways in which machines can augment 增强 human labor rather than replace it. So then the problem is not really about technology, but rather, "how do we innovate our institutions 制度 and our work practices 工作实践?"

##### 本篇命题规律小结（英二阅读）

1. 科技/社会类文章：关注**现象→书籍观点→不同学者观点→建议**的结构
2. 细节题：定位到**人物+says/argues/notes**，答案是观点的同义替换
3. 指代题（this point）：答案在**前一段的核心观点**（需要能发挥主动性的人）
4. 主旨题：答案需**贯穿全文**（Race Against the Machine反复出现），不选最后一段的片面建议
5. 常见干扰：与原文相反（remain intact vs become threatened）、偷换概念（without target vs giant target sign）、因果倒置（automation加速技术发展vs技术发展导致自动化）

When the government talks about infrastructure 基础设施 contributing to the economy the focus is usually on roads, railways, broadband 宽带 and energy. Housing is seldom mentioned.

Why is that? To some extent 在某种程度上 the housing sector 住房部门 must shoulder the blame 承担责任. We have not been good at communicating the real value that housing can contribute to economic growth. Then there is the scale 规模 of the typical housing project. It is hard to shove 推 for attention among multibillion-pound 数十亿英镑的 infrastructure projects, so it is inevitable 不可避免的 that the attention is focused elsewhere. But perhaps the most significant reason is that the issue has always been so politically charged 政治敏感的.

Nevertheless 然而, the affordable housing 经济适用房 situation is desperate 绝望的. Waiting lists 等候名单 increase all the time and we are simply not building enough new homes.

The comprehensive spending review 综合支出审查 offers an opportunity for the government to help rectify 纠正 this. It needs to put historical prejudices 历史偏见 to one side and take some steps to address our urgent housing need.

There are some indications 迹象 that it is preparing to do just that. The communities minister, Don Foster, has hinted 暗示 that George Osborne, Chancellor of the Exchequer 财政大臣, may introduce more flexibility 灵活性 to the local authority 地方政府 borrowing cap 借款上限, which has been put in place by the previous Labour government. This means that local councils 地方议会 will be able to borrow more money to build more social housing 社会住房. The cap was introduced by the previous Labour government to limit local authority borrowing and debt.

But there are some obvious concerns. The first is that local councils may borrow too much money and get into debt. The second is that local councils may not be able to pay back the money they borrow. There is also a concern that local councils may not have the necessary skills and experience to manage large-scale housing projects.

Ministers should also look at creating greater certainty 确定性 in the rental environment 租赁环境, which would have a significant impact on the ability of registered providers 注册供应商 to fund new developments 新开发项目 from revenues 收入.

But it is not just down to 取决于 the government. While these measures would be welcome in the short term, we must face up to 面对 the fact that the existing £4.5bn programme of grants 拨款 to fund new affordable housing, set to expire 到期 in 2015, is unlikely to be extended beyond then. The Labour party has recently announced that it will retain 保留 a large part of the coalition's 联合政府的 spending plans if it returns to power, including the current spending limits on housing. The housing sector needs to accept that it is very unlikely to get ever-more public funding 公共资金. We need to adjust to this changing climate 环境.

- D：错误。relieve the ministers of responsibilities（减轻部长们的责任）与原文相反。原文说Ministers should also look at creating greater certainty（部长们应该考虑创造确定性），是增加责任，不是减轻。

#### 40. The author believes that after 2015, the government may ______.

[A] implement more policies to support housing [B] review the need for large-scale grants [C] renew the affordable housing grants programme [D] stop generous funding to the housing sector

✅答案：**D** 定位：最后一段。`the existing £4.5bn programme of grants … set to expire in 2015, is unlikely to be extended beyond then`（45亿英镑拨款计划定于2015年到期，不太可能延长）+ `The housing sector needs to accept that it is very unlikely to get ever-more public funding`（住房部门需要接受不太可能获得越来越多的公共资金）。2015年后政府可能停止对住房部门的慷慨资助。

- A：错误。implement more policies to support housing（实施更多政策支持住房）与原文相反。2015年后拨款不延长，资金减少。
- B：错误。review the need for large-scale grants（审查大规模拨款的必要性）无中生有。原文说拨款不延长，不是审查必要性。
- C：错误。renew the affordable housing grants programme（更新经济适用房拨款计划）与原文相反。原文说unlikely to be extended（不太可能延长）。
- D：正确。停止对住房部门的慷慨资助。grants expire + unlikely to be extended + unlikely to get ever-more public funding = stop generous funding。

##### 本篇命题规律小结（英二阅读）

1. 政策/经济类文章：关注**问题→原因→政府措施→担忧→未来趋势**的结构
2. 细节题：定位到**人物+hinted/may**，答案是政策变化的具体内容
3. 推理题：基于**作者建议**（put historical prejudices to one side）反推存在的问题
4. 因果题：定位到**which would have a significant impact on**，答案是影响的结果
5. 常见干扰：与原文相反（stop building vs build more, renew grants vs unlikely to extend）、偷换概念（lower costs vs fund new developments）

##### 新题型 Part B（41-45）

#### 题型说明

**多项对应（信息匹配）**：阅读一篇关于英国地景艺术（Land Art）的文章，将左栏的5件艺术作品名称与右栏的7个描述进行匹配，选出正确的5项。右栏有2个干扰项。此题型考查定位与同义替换能力。

#### 原文

**Uncommon Ground - Land Art in Britain**

The term Land Art brings to mind epic interventions in the land such as Robert Smithson's Spiral Jetty, 6,500 tons of basalt, earth and salt projecting into Utah's Great Salt Lake, or Roden Crater, an extinct volcano in Arizona, which James Turrell has been transforming into an immense naked-eye observatory since 1979.

Richard Long's A Line Made By Walking, however, involved nothing more strenuous than a 20-minute train ride from Waterloo. Having got off somewhere in suburbia, the artist walked backwards and forwards over a piece of grass until the squashed turf formed a line - a kind of drawing on the land.

Emerging in the late Sixties and reaching a peak in the Seventies, Land Art was one of a range of new forms, including Body Art, Performance Art, Action Art and Installation Art, which pushed art beyond the traditional confines of the studio and gallery. Rather than portraying landscape, land artists used the physical substance of the land itself as their medium.

The British Land Art, typified by Long's piece, was not only more domestically scaled, but a lot quirkier than its American counterpart. Indeed, while you might assume that an exhibition of Land Art would consist only of records of works rather than the works themselves, Long's photograph of his work is the work. Since his "action" is in the past, the photograph is its sole embodiment.

That might seem rather an obscure point, but it sets the tone for an exhibition that contains a lot of black-and-white photographs and relatively few natural objects.

Long is Britain's best-known Land Artist and his **Stone Circle**, a perfect ring of purplish rocks from Portishead beach laid out on the gallery floor, represents the elegant, rarefied side of the form. The Boyle Family, on the other hand, stands for its dirty, urban aspect. Comprising artists Mark Boyle and Joan Hills and their children, they recreated random sections of the British landscape on gallery walls. Their **Olaf Street Study**, a square of brick-strewn waste ground, is one of the few works here to embrace the commonplaceness that characterises most of our experience of the landscape most of the time.

Parks feature, particularly in the earlier works, such as John Hilliard's very funny **Across the Park**, in which a long-haired stroller is variously smiled at by a pretty girl and unwittingly assaulted in a sequence of images that turn out to be different parts of the same photograph.

Generally however British land artists preferred to get away from towns, gravitating towards landscapes that are traditionally considered beautiful such as the Lake District or the Wiltshire Downs. While it probably wasn't apparent at the time, much of this work is permeated by a spirit of romantic escapism that the likes of Wordsworth would have readily understood. Derek Jarman's yellow-tinted film **Towards Avebury**, a collection of long, mostly still shots of the Wiltshire landscape, evokes a tradition of English landscape painting stretching from Samuel Palmer to Paul Nash.

In the case of Hamish Fulton, you can't help feeling that the Scottish artist has simply found a way of making his love of walking pay. A typical work, such as **Seven Days**, consists of a single beautiful black-and-white photograph taken on an epic walk, with the mileage and number of days taken listed beneath. British Land Art as shown in this well selected, but relatively modestly scaled exhibition wasn't about imposing on the landscape, more a kind of landscape-orientated light conceptual art created passing through. It had its origins in the great outdoors, but the results were as gallery-bound as the paintings of Turner and Constable.

#### 解析

**题型特点**：多项对应题考查信息定位与同义替换能力。需根据左栏关键词（作品名称）回原文定位，再在定位处找到与右栏选项同义替换的内容。

**解题策略**：
1. 先扫读右栏7个选项，划出关键词
2. 根据左栏作品名称（专有名词）回原文定位
3. 在定位处寻找与选项同义替换的表达
4. 用排除法排除干扰项

- **第41题 Stone Circle → D**：根据"Stone Circle"定位至第六段，原文"represents the elegant, rarefied side of the form"与D选项"represents the elegance of the British land art"形成词汇复现（represents / elegant-elegance），故选D。

- **第42题 Olaf Street Study → E**：根据"Olaf Street Study"定位至第六段，原文"embrace the commonplaceness"中commonplaceness意为"平凡、寻常"，与E选项中的ordinary构成同义替换，故选E。

- **第43题 Across the Park → G**：根据"Across the Park"定位至第七段，原文"different parts of the same photograph"与G选项"contains images from different parts of the same photograph"形成信息词复现，故选G。

- **第44题 Towards Avebury → C**：根据"Towards Avebury"定位至第八段，原文"evokes a tradition of English landscape painting stretching from Samuel Palmer to Paul Nash"与C选项"reminds people of the English landscape painting tradition"形成同义替换（evokes-reminds / tradition of English landscape painting），故选C。

- **第45题 Seven Days → A**：根据"Seven Days"定位至最后一段，原文"a single beautiful black-and-white photograph taken on an epic walk"与A选项"originates from a long walk that the artist took"形成同义替换（taken on-originates from / epic walk-a long walk），故选A。

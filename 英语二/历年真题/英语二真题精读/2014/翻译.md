# 2014 英二 翻译

## 原文

Most people would define optimism as being endlessly happy, with a glass that's perpetually half full. But that's exactly the kind of false cheerfulness that positive psychologists wouldn't recommend. "Healthy optimism means being in touch with reality," says Tal Ben-Shahar, a Harvard professor. According to Ben-Shahar, realistic optimists are those who make the best of things that happen, but not those who believe everything happens for the best.

Ben-Shahar uses three optimistic exercises. When he feels down—say, after giving a bad lecture—he grants himself permission to be human. He reminds himself that not every lecture can be a Nobel winner, some will be less effective than others. Next is reconstruction. He analyzes the weak lecture, learning lessons for the future about what works and what doesn't. Finally, there is perspective, which involves acknowledging that in the grand scheme of life, one lecture really doesn't matter.

## 参考译文

大多数人将乐观定义为永远快乐，总觉得杯子里的水还有一半。但积极心理学家并不提倡这种虚假的快乐。"健康的乐观是与现实联系在一起的，"哈佛大学教授泰·本-沙哈说道。根据本-沙哈的观点，现实的乐观主义者是去积极实现事情的圆满，而不是坐等事情会自己圆满。

本-沙哈提出了乐观训练的三个阶段。当他心情低落时——比如，一个糟糕的演讲之后——他宽慰自己这是人之常情。他提醒自己不是每一次演讲都能达到诺贝尔奖的水准，有些演讲的效果会不如其他。下一个阶段是重塑。他会分析这次失败的演讲，总结哪些地方可取，哪些不可取，为将来的演讲积累经验。最后一个阶段是前瞻，即认识到在生命的宏伟蓝图中，一次演讲根本算不上什么。

## 长难句分析

### 长难句1

> But that's exactly the kind of false cheerfulness that positive psychologists wouldn't recommend.

**句子拆分**：
- 主干：**that's exactly the kind of false cheerfulness**
- `that positive psychologists wouldn't recommend`：that引导定语从句，修饰the kind of false cheerfulness
- exactly为强调副词，修饰系动词is

**翻译要点**：定语从句较短，可前置译为"积极心理学家不会推荐的那种虚假的快乐"。

### 长难句2

> According to Ben-Shahar, realistic optimists are those who make the best of things that happen, but not those who believe everything happens for the best.

**句子拆分**：
- 主干：**realistic optimists are those...but not those...**
- `who make the best of things that happen`：who引导定语从句，修饰those
  - `that happen`：that引导定语从句，修饰things
- `who believe everything happens for the best`：who引导定语从句，修饰those
  - `everything happens for the best`：宾语从句，作believe的宾语

**翻译要点**：but not those形成对比结构，译为"是……而不是……"。make the best of things that happen译为"充分利用发生的事情"，believe everything happens for the best译为"相信一切都会自动圆满"。

### 长难句3

> Finally, there is perspective, which involves acknowledging that in the grand scheme of life, one lecture really doesn't matter.

**句子拆分**：
- 主干：**there is perspective**
- `which involves acknowledging that...`：which引导非限制性定语从句，修饰perspective
  - `that in the grand scheme of life, one lecture really doesn't matter`：that引导宾语从句，作acknowledging的宾语
  - `in the grand scheme of life`：介词短语作状语

## 翻译技巧总结

1. **define...as...**：译为"将……定义为……"，注意as后接名词或动名词
2. **with a glass that's perpetually half full**：半满的杯子是乐观的象征（对比悲观者的"半空"），翻译时可意译为"总觉得杯子里的水还有一半"
3. **grants himself permission to be human**：直译为"允许自己是人类"，意译为"宽慰自己这是人之常情"
4. **learning lessons for the future about what works and what doesn't**：注意learning在此为分词作伴随状语，what works and what doesn't是宾语从句
5. **in the grand scheme of life**：固定表达，意为"在生命的宏伟蓝图中"

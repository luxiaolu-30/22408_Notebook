# 2016 英二 大作文

## 图表描述

饼图（Pie Chart）：某高校学生旅游目的调查

- 欣赏风景/观光（Sightseeing）：37%（占比最高）
- 缓解压力（Relieving stress）：33%
- 其他（Others）：15%
- 交友（Socializing）：9%
- 培养独立能力（Developing independence）：6%

## 题目

**Directions:**

Write an essay based on the following chart. In your writing, you should

1) interpret the chart, and
2) give your comments.

You should write about 150 words on the ANSWER SHEET. (15 points)

## 高分范文

The pie chart above clearly illustrates the distribution of college students' travel purposes. As is shown, sightseeing accounts for 37%, ranking first among all the categories, followed closely by stress relief at 33%. Socializing and developing independence make up 9% and 6% respectively, while the remaining 15% goes to other purposes.

Several factors may account for this phenomenon. First and foremost, with the improvement of living standards, more and more college students choose traveling as a way to broaden their horizons and appreciate natural scenery, which explains why sightseeing takes the lead. Moreover, college students nowadays face tremendous academic pressure, and traveling serves as an effective way to relieve stress and relax their minds. Additionally, a small percentage of students travel to develop independence and socialize with others, indicating that personal growth remains a secondary concern for most.

From my perspective, it is encouraging to see that college students are actively exploring the world through travel. However, universities and society should also create more opportunities for students to develop independence and interpersonal skills through organized travel programs.

## 范文翻译

上面的饼图清楚地展示了大学生旅游目的的分布情况。如图所示，欣赏风景占37%，在所有类别中排名第一，紧随其后的是缓解压力，占33%。交友和培养独立能力分别占9%和6%，其余15%属于其他目的。

有几个因素可以解释这一现象。首先，随着生活水平的提高，越来越多的大学生选择旅游作为拓宽视野、欣赏自然风光的方式，这解释了为什么观光占据首位。其次，当今大学生面临巨大的学业压力，旅游是缓解压力、放松身心的有效方式。此外，一小部分学生通过旅游来培养独立能力和与他人社交，表明对大多数人来说个人成长仍是次要考虑。

在我看来，大学生积极通过旅游探索世界是令人鼓舞的。然而，高校和社会也应该通过组织旅游活动，为学生创造更多培养独立能力和人际交往能力的机会。

## 重点句型与表达

1. **The pie chart above clearly illustrates the distribution of...**（上面的饼图清楚地展示了……的分布）——饼图描述开头句型
2. **As is shown, ...accounts for...%, ranking first among..., followed closely by...at...%**（如图所示，……占……%，排名第一，紧随其后的是……占……%）——描述占比和排名
3. **...make up...% and...% respectively, while the remaining...% goes to...**（……分别占……%和……%，其余……%用于……）——描述剩余数据
4. **Several factors may account for this phenomenon.**（有几个因素可以解释这一现象）——原因分析段开头句型
5. **First and foremost, with the improvement of..., more and more...choose...as a way to...**（首先，随着……的提高，越来越多的……选择……作为……的方式）——with短语表背景+choose...as句型
6. **Moreover, ...serve as an effective way to...**（其次，……是做某事的有效方式）——serve as句型
7. **From my perspective, it is encouraging to see that...**（在我看来，看到……是令人鼓舞的）——评论段开头句型
8. **However, ...should also create more opportunities for...to...through...**（然而，……也应该通过……为……创造更多……的机会）——结尾建议句型

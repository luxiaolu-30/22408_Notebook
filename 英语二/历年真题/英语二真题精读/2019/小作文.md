# 2019 英二 小作文

## 题目

**Directions:**

Suppose Professor Smith asked you to plan a debate on the theme of city traffic. Write him an email to

1) suggest a specific topic with your reasons, and
2) tell him about your arrangements.

You should write about 100 words on the ANSWER SHEET.

Do not use your own name. Use "Li Ming" instead.

Do not write your address. (10 points)

## 高分范文

Dear Professor Smith,

It is my great honor to plan a debate on the theme of city traffic. I would like to suggest "Private Cars: A Blessing or a Curse?" as the specific topic for this debate.

The primary reason is that the rapid increase of private cars has become a major concern in urban areas, sparking heated discussions among citizens. On the one hand, private cars offer convenience and mobility; on the other hand, they contribute significantly to traffic congestion and environmental pollution. This topic will encourage participants to think critically about a pressing social issue.

Regarding the arrangements, the debate will be held in the Student Activity Center at 2:30 p.m. on March 16th. Eight participants will be divided into two groups — one supporting the development of private cars and the other opposing it. Each group will have three rounds of argument. The audience will vote for the winning team.

I look forward to your reply.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

## 范文翻译

亲爱的Smith教授：

我很荣幸策划一场关于城市交通主题的辩论。我想建议"私家车：是福还是祸？"作为本次辩论的具体题目。

主要原因是在城区，私家车的快速增长已成为一个主要问题，引发了市民的热烈讨论。一方面，私家车提供了便利和灵活性；另一方面，它们大大加剧了交通拥堵和环境污染。这一题目将鼓励参赛者批判性地思考一个紧迫的社会问题。

关于安排，辩论将于3月16日下午2:30在学生活动中心举行。八名参赛者将分为两组——一组支持发展私家车，另一组反对。每组将进行三轮论证。观众将投票选出获胜团队。

期待您的回复。

您真诚的，
Li Ming

## 重点句型与表达

1. **It is my great honor to plan a debate on the theme of...**（我很荣幸策划一场关于……主题的辩论）——建议信开头句型
2. **I would like to suggest...as the specific topic for this debate.**（我想建议……作为本次辩论的具体题目）——提出建议句型
3. **The primary reason is that...**（主要原因是……）——解释原因句型
4. **On the one hand...; on the other hand...**（一方面……；另一方面……）——正反论证句型
5. **This topic will encourage participants to think critically about...**（这一题目将鼓励参赛者批判性地思考……）——说明选题意义
6. **Regarding the arrangements, the debate will be held in...at...on...**（关于安排，辩论将于……在……举行）——介绍安排句型
7. **Each group will have three rounds of argument.**（每组将进行三轮论证）——细节安排说明
8. **I look forward to your reply.**（期待您的回复）——结尾句型

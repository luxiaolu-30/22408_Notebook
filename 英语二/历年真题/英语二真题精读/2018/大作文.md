# 2018 英二 大作文

## 图表描述

饼图（Pie Chart）：2017年某市消费者选择餐厅时的关注因素

五个扇形区域：
- 特色菜品（features/distinguishing dishes）：36.3%
- 服务态度（service attitude）：26.8%
- 环境（environment）：23.8%
- 价格（price）：8.4%
- 其他（other factors）：4.7%

## 题目

**Directions:**

Write an essay based on the chart below. In your writing, you should

1) interpret the chart, and
2) give your comments.

You should write about 150 words on the ANSWER SHEET. (15 points)

## 高分范文

The pie chart above evidently illustrates the factors that consumers take into consideration when choosing a restaurant in a certain city in 2017. As is shown, distinguishing features of dishes take the lion's share, accounting for 36.3%, followed by service attitude at 26.8% and environment at 23.8%. Surprisingly, price falls far behind, making up merely 8.4%, while other factors account for 4.7%.

Several factors may account for this phenomenon. First and foremost, with the steady improvement of living standards, consumers are no longer satisfied with merely filling their stomachs. Instead, they pursue unique culinary experiences, which explains why specialty dishes rank first. Moreover, as dining out has become a social activity rather than a mere necessity, consumers increasingly value attentive service and a pleasant atmosphere, considering them essential to a satisfying dining experience. The low percentage of price indicates that quality has outweighed affordability in people's dining choices.

From my perspective, this trend is a positive reflection of social progress and economic development. It suggests that people are attaching greater importance to the quality of life. Restaurant owners should take note of this shift and focus on enhancing food quality, service standards, and dining environment rather than competing solely on price.

## 范文翻译

上面的饼图清楚地展示了2017年某市消费者选择餐厅时的关注因素。如图所示，特色菜品占据了最大份额，占36.3%，其次是服务态度26.8%和环境23.8%。令人惊讶的是，价格远远落后，仅占8.4%，而其他因素占4.7%。

有几个因素可以解释这一现象。首先，随着生活水平的稳步提高，消费者不再满足于仅仅填饱肚子，而是追求独特的烹饪体验，这解释了为何特色菜品排名第一。此外，外出就餐已成为一种社交活动而非单纯的必需品，消费者越来越重视周到的服务和舒适的氛围，认为它们是满意用餐体验的关键。价格占比低表明，品质在选择中已超过价格因素。

在我看来，这一趋势是社会进步和经济发展的积极反映。它表明人们越来越重视生活品质。餐厅经营者应注意到这一变化，专注于提升菜品质量、服务标准和用餐环境，而非仅在价格上竞争。

## 重点句型与表达

1. **The pie chart above evidently illustrates the factors that...**（上面的饼图清楚地展示了……的因素）——饼图描述开头句型
2. **As is shown, distinguishing features of dishes take the lion's share, accounting for 36.3%.**（如图所示，特色菜品占据最大份额，占36.3%）——take the lion's share=占据最大份额，accounting for=占比
3. **Surprisingly, price falls far behind, making up merely 8.4%.**（令人惊讶的是，价格远远落后，仅占8.4%）——falls far behind=远远落后，making up=占比
4. **Several factors may account for this phenomenon.**（有几个因素可以解释这一现象）——原因分析段开头句型
5. **First and foremost, with the steady improvement of living standards...**（首先，随着生活水平的稳步提高……）——with短语表背景
6. **Instead, they pursue unique culinary experiences, which explains why...**（相反，他们追求独特的烹饪体验，这解释了为何……）——which引导非限制性定语从句
7. **The low percentage of price indicates that quality has outweighed affordability.**（价格占比低表明品质已超过价格因素）——indicate that=表明，outweigh=超过
8. **From my perspective, this trend is a positive reflection of...**（在我看来，这一趋势是……的积极反映）——评论段开头句型

While fossil fuels 化石燃料—coal, oil, gas—still generate 产生 roughly 大约 85 percent of the world's energy supply 能源供应, it's clearer than ever that the future belongs to 属于 renewable sources 可再生能源 such as wind and solar. The move to renewables is picking up momentum 势头 around the world: They now account for 占 more than half of new power sources 电源 going on line 上线.

Some growth stems from 源于 a commitment by governments and farsighted 有远见的 businesses to fund cleaner energy sources. But increasingly the story is about the plummeting 暴跌的 prices of renewables, especially wind and solar. The cost of solar panels 太阳能电池板 has dropped by 80 percent and the cost of wind turbines 风力涡轮机 by close to one-third in the past eight years.

In many parts of the world renewable energy is already a principal 主要的 energy source. In Scotland, for example, wind turbines provide enough electricity 电力 to power 95 percent of homes. While the rest of the world takes the lead 领先, notably China and Europe, the United States is also seeing a remarkable shift 显著转变. In March, for the first time, wind and solar power accounted for more than 10 percent of the power generated in the US, reported the US Energy Information Administration 美国能源信息署.

President Trump has underlined 强调 fossil fuels—especially coal—as the path to economic growth. In a recent speech in Iowa, he dismissed 贬低 wind power as an unreliable energy source. But that message did not play well with 不受欢迎 many in Iowa, where wind turbines dot 散布 the fields and provide 36 percent of the state's electricity generation—and where tech giants like Microsoft are being attracted by the availability of clean energy to power their data centers 数据中心.

The question "what happens when the wind doesn't blow or the sun doesn't shine?" has provided a quick put-down 贬低 for skeptics 怀疑者. But a boost 提升 in the storage capacity 存储容量 of batteries 电池 is making their ability to keep power flowing around the clock 全天候 more likely.

The advance is driven in part by vehicle manufacturers 汽车制造商, who are placing big bets 下大赌注 on battery-powered electric vehicles 电动汽车. Although electric cars are still a rarity 稀有物 on roads now, this massive investment 大规模投资 could change the picture 改变局面 rapidly in coming years.

While there's a long way to go, the trend lines for renewables are spiking 飙升. The pace of change in energy sources appears to be speeding up—perhaps just in time to have a meaningful effect in slowing climate change 减缓气候变化. What Washington does—or doesn't do—to promote alternative energy 替代能源 may mean less and less at a time of a global shift in thought 全球思想转变.

# 2018英二 Text2 长难句精读（原文+拆分+语法+翻译+考点）

## 长难句1

> While fossil fuels—coal, oil, gas—still generate roughly 85 percent of the world's energy supply, it's clearer than ever that the future belongs to renewable sources such as wind and solar.

### 句子拆分

主干：**it's clearer than ever that …**
- it是形式主语，真正主语是that引导的主语从句
- `While fossil fuels—coal, oil, gas—still generate roughly 85 percent of the world's energy supply`：while让步状语从句
  - `—coal, oil, gas—`：插入语，举例fossil fuels
- that从句主干：**the future belongs to renewable sources such as wind and solar**

### 生词

fossil fuels：化石燃料；generate：产生；roughly：大约；energy supply：能源供应；it's clearer than ever：比以往任何时候都更清楚；belong to：属于；renewable sources：可再生能源；such as：例如；wind and solar：风能和太阳能

### 翻译

尽管化石燃料——煤、石油、天然气——仍产生大约世界85%的能源供应，但未来属于风能和太阳能等可再生能源，这一点比以往任何时候都更清楚。

### 考点

**while让步状语从句+插入语+it形式主语+that主语从句**；开篇点题——可再生能源是未来趋势，全文主旨。

## 长难句2

> The move to renewables is picking up momentum around the world: They now account for more than half of new power sources going on line.

### 句子拆分

主干：**The move to renewables is picking up momentum around the world**
- `to renewables`：介词短语修饰move
- 冒号后：`They now account for more than half of new power sources going on line`
  - 同位语，解释picking up momentum
  - `going on line`：现在分词作后置定语，修饰power sources

### 生词

move to：向…转变；renewables：可再生能源；pick up momentum：势头增强、加速；around the world：在全球；account for：占；new power sources：新电源；go on line：上线、投入使用

### 翻译

向可再生能源的转变在全球势头增强：它们现在占上线新电源的一半以上。

### 考点

**不定式后置定语+冒号同位语+现在分词后置定语**；说明可再生能源发展势头强劲。

- `increasingly`：状语

### 生词

increasingly：日益；story：情况、故事；be about：关于；plummeting：暴跌的、骤降的；prices：价格；renewables：可再生能源；especially：特别是

### 翻译

但日益增长的情况是关于可再生能源价格的暴跌，特别是风能和太阳能。

### 考点

**插入语+plummeting词义**；26题词义题定位句——plummeting意为"暴跌的"，与falling最接近。

## 长难句4

> The cost of solar panels has dropped by 80 percent and the cost of wind turbines by close to one-third in the past eight years.

### 句子拆分

并列句，and连接
分句1主干：**The cost of solar panels has dropped by 80 percent**
分句2主干：**the cost of wind turbines (has dropped) by close to one-third**
- 省略has dropped
- `in the past eight years`：时间状语

### 生词

cost：成本；solar panels：太阳能电池板；drop by：下降了；wind turbines：风力涡轮机；close to：接近；one-third：三分之一；in the past eight years：在过去八年中

### 翻译

在过去八年中，太阳能电池板的成本下降了80%，风力涡轮机的成本下降了近三分之一。

### 考点

**and连接并列句+省略谓语+drop by+数据**；26题C选项的依据——价格下降（dropped by 80%和one-third）说明plummeting = falling。

## 长难句7

> But that message did not play well with many in Iowa, where wind turbines dot the fields and provide 36 percent of the state's electricity generation—and where tech giants like Microsoft are being attracted by the availability of clean energy to power their data centers.

### 句子拆分

主干：**But that message did not play well with many in Iowa**
- `where wind turbines dot the fields and provide 36 percent of the state's electricity generation`：where定语从句，修饰Iowa
  - `dot the fields`和`provide 36 percent`是and连接的并列谓语
- `—and where tech giants like Microsoft are being attracted by the availability of clean energy to power their data centers—`：插入语，and连接的第二个where定语从句
  - `to power their data centers`：不定式作目的状语

### 生词

play well with：受…欢迎；Iowa：爱荷华州；wind turbines：风力涡轮机；dot：散布、点缀；fields：田野；provide：提供；electricity generation：发电量；tech giants：科技巨头；be attracted by：被…吸引；availability：可获得性；clean energy：清洁能源；power：为…供电；data centers：数据中心

### 考点

**where定语从句+and连接并列谓语+插入语+不定式目的状语**；28题A选项的依据——爱荷华州风能广泛使用（wind turbines dot the fields + provide 36% = wind is a widely used energy source）。

## 26. The word "plummeting" (Line 3, Para.2) is closest in meaning to ______.

[A] stabilizing [B] changing [C] falling [D] rising

✅答案：**C** 词义题。定位：第二段。`the plummeting prices of renewables`（可再生能源价格暴跌）+ `The cost of solar panels has dropped by 80 percent and the cost of wind turbines by close to one-third`（太阳能电池板成本下降80%，风力涡轮机成本下降近三分之一）。plummeting意为"暴跌的"，与falling最接近。

- A：错误。stabilizing（稳定）不符。价格在下降，不是稳定。
- B：错误。changing（变化）太宽泛。价格具体在下降，不是一般变化。
- C：正确。下降。dropped by 80% and one-third = plummeting = falling。
- D：错误。rising（上升）与原文相反。

## 27. According to Paragraph 3, the use of renewable energy in America ______.

[A] is progressing notably [B] is as extensive as in Europe [C] faces many challenges [D] has proved to be impractical

✅答案：**A** 定位：第三段。`the United States is also seeing a remarkable shift`（美国也看到了显著的转变）+ `In March, for the first time, wind and solar power accounted for more than 10 percent of the power generated in the US`（3月风能和太阳能首次占美国发电量的10%以上）。说明美国可再生能源使用显著进步。

- A：正确。显著进步。remarkable shift + for the first time more than 10% = progressing notably。
- B：错误。is as extensive as in Europe（和欧洲一样广泛）与原文相反。原文说the rest of the world takes the lead, notably China and Europe（世界其他地区领先，特别是中国和欧洲），说明美国不如欧洲广泛。
- C：错误。faces many challenges（面临许多挑战）无中生有。第三段讲的是进步，不是挑战。
- D：错误。has proved to be impractical（已被证明不切实际）与原文相反。美国可再生能源使用在增长，不是不切实际。

## 28. It can be learned that in Iowa, ______.

[A] wind is a widely used energy source [B] wind energy has replaced fossil fuels [C] tech giants are investing in clean energy [D] there is a shortage of clean energy supply

✅答案：**A** 推理题。定位：第四段。`where wind turbines dot the fields and provide 36 percent of the state's electricity generation`（那里风力涡轮机散布在田野上，提供该州36%的发电量）。说明爱荷华州风能广泛使用。

- C：错误。tech giants are investing in clean energy（科技巨头正在投资清洁能源）偷换概念。原文说tech giants are being attracted by the availability of clean energy to power their data centers（科技巨头被清洁能源吸引来为数据中心供电），不是投资清洁能源。
- D：错误。there is a shortage of clean energy supply（清洁能源供应短缺）与原文相反。爱荷华州风能供应充足，提供36%发电量。

## 29. Which of the following is true about clean energy according to Paragraphs 5&6?

[A] Its application has boosted battery storage. [B] It is commonly used in car manufacturing. [C] Its continuous supply is becoming a reality. [D] Its sustainable exploitation will remain difficult.

✅答案：**C** 双段推理题。定位：第五、六段。第五段：`a boost in the storage capacity of batteries is making their ability to keep power flowing around the clock more likely`（电池存储容量提升正使全天候保持电力流动的能力更可能）。第六段：`this massive investment could change the picture rapidly in coming years`（大规模投资可能在未来几年迅速改变局面）。说明清洁能源持续供应正在成为现实。

- A：错误。Its application has boosted battery storage（它的应用促进了电池存储）因果倒置。原文说电池存储提升使清洁能源持续供应更可能，不是清洁能源应用促进电池存储。
- B：错误。It is commonly used in car manufacturing（它在汽车制造中普遍使用）与原文相反。原文说electric cars are still a rarity on roads（电动汽车在路上仍然稀有）。
- C：正确。它的持续供应正在成为现实。keep power flowing around the clock more likely + change the picture = continuous supply is becoming a reality。
- D：错误。Its sustainable exploitation will remain difficult（它的可持续开发将仍然困难）与原文相反。电池存储提升和电动汽车投资正在改变局面，不是仍然困难。

## 30. It can be inferred from the last paragraph that renewable energy ______.

[A] will bring the US closer to other countries [B] will accelerate global environmental change [C] is not really encouraged by the US government [D] is not competitive enough with regard to its cost

✅答案：**C** 推理题。定位：最后一段。`What Washington does—or doesn't do—to promote alternative energy may mean less and less`（华盛顿为促进替代能源所做的——或没有做的——可能越来越不重要）+ 前文Trump强调化石燃料、贬低风能。说明美国政府并没有真正鼓励可再生能源。

- A：错误。will bring the US closer to other countries（将使美国更接近其他国家）无中生有。
- B：错误。will accelerate global environmental change（将加速全球环境变化）与原文相反。原文说have a meaningful effect in slowing climate change（对减缓气候变化有有意义的影响）。
- C：正确。没有真正受到美国政府的鼓励。Washington's action means less and less + Trump underlined fossil fuels = not really encouraged by the US government。
- D：错误。is not competitive enough with regard to its cost（在成本方面竞争力不足）与原文相反。原文说价格暴跌（plummeting prices），说明成本竞争力增强。

### 本篇命题规律小结（英二阅读）

1. 环保/科技类文章：关注**趋势（可再生能源是未来）→原因（价格暴跌）→全球发展→美国情况→技术突破（电池存储）→政府态度→全球趋势**的结构
2. 词义题：结合**上下文数据**（dropped by 80% and one-third = plummeting = falling）
3. 细节题：定位到**数据和首次**（for the first time more than 10% = progressing notably）
4. 推理题：注意**转折后内容**（But that message did not play well with many in Iowa, where wind turbines dot the fields）
5. 常见干扰：与原文相反（as extensive as Europe → Europe takes the lead, replaced fossil fuels → 36%, commonly used in cars → rarity）、因果倒置（application boosted battery storage → battery storage boosted application）

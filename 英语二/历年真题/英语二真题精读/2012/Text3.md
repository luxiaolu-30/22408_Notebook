In 2010, a federal judge 联邦法官 shook America's biotech 生物技术 industry to its core 核心. Companies had won patents 专利 for isolated 分离的 DNA for decades—by 2005 some 20% of human genes were patented. But in March 2010 a judge ruled 裁定 that genes were unpatentable 不可申请专利的. Executives were violently agitated 极度焦虑. The Biotechnology Industry Organisation (BIO), a trade group 行业组织, assured 保证 members that this was just a "preliminary step 初步步骤" in a longer battle.

On July 29th they were relieved 宽慰, at least temporarily 暂时地. A federal appeals court 联邦上诉法院 overturned 推翻 the prior decision, ruling that Myriad Genetics could indeed hold patents to two genes that help forecast 预测 a woman's risk of breast cancer 乳腺癌. The chief executive of Myriad, a company in Utah, said the ruling was a blessing 幸事 to firms and patients alike.

But as companies continue their attempts at personalised medicine 个性化医疗, the courts will remain rather busy. The Myriad case itself is probably not over. Critics make three main arguments against gene patents: a gene is a product of nature, so it may not be patented; gene patents suppress 抑制 innovation rather than reward it; and patents' monopolies 垄断 restrict access to genetic tests such as Myriad's. A growing number seem to agree. Last year a federal task-force 特别工作组 urged reform for patents related to genetic tests. In October the Department of Justice 司法部 filed a brief 提交案情摘要 in the Myriad case, arguing that an isolated DNA molecule "is no less a product of nature … than are cotton fibres 棉纤维 that have been separated from cotton seeds 棉籽."

Despite the appeals court's decision, big questions remain unanswered. For example, it is unclear whether the sequencing 测序 of a whole genome 基因组 violates 违反 the patents of individual genes within it. The case may yet reach the Supreme Court 最高法院.

As the industry advances, however, other suits 诉讼 may have an even greater impact. Companies are unlikely to file many more patents for human DNA molecules—most are already patented or in the public domain 公共领域. Firms are now studying how genes interact 相互作用, looking for correlations 关联 that might be used to determine the causes of disease or predict a drug's efficacy 药效. Companies are eager to win patents for "connecting the dots 理清头绪", explains Hans Sauer, a lawyer for the BIO.

Their success may be determined by a suit related to this issue, brought by the Mayo Clinic, which the Supreme Court will hear in its next term 下一轮. The BIO recently held a convention 大会 which included sessions 会议 to coach 培训 lawyers on the shifting landscape 不断变化的格局 for patents. Each meeting was packed 挤满人.

# 2012英二 Text3 长难句精读（原文+拆分+语法+翻译+考点）

## 长难句1

> Companies had won patents for isolated DNA for decades—by 2005 some 20% of human genes were patented.

### 句子拆分

并列句，破折号连接
分句1主干：**Companies had won patents for isolated DNA**
- `for decades`：时间状语
分句2主干：**some 20% of human genes were patented**
- `by 2005`：时间状语

### 生词

patent：专利；isolated DNA：分离的DNA；decade：十年

### 翻译

几十年来，公司一直在为分离的DNA申请专利——到2005年，约20%的人类基因已被申请专利。

### 考点

过去完成时+一般过去时的时间对比；破折号连接补充说明；31题背景——公司原本拥有基因专利，法官裁定不可专利后他们才焦虑。

---

## 长难句2

> But in March 2010 a judge ruled that genes were unpatentable.

### 句子拆分

主干：**a judge ruled that …**
- that引导宾语从句：**genes were unpatentable**
- `in March 2010`：时间状语

### 生词

rule：裁定；unpatentable：不可申请专利的

### 翻译

但在2010年3月，一名法官裁定基因不可申请专利。

### 考点

rule that宾语从句；全文事件起点；31题关键——公司希望基因可申请专利（与法官裁定相反）。

---

## 长难句3

> A federal appeals court overturned the prior decision, ruling that Myriad Genetics could indeed hold patents to two genes that help forecast a woman's risk of breast cancer.

### 句子拆分

主干：**A federal appeals court overturned the prior decision**
- `ruling that Myriad Genetics could indeed hold patents to two genes`：现在分词作伴随状语
  - that引导宾语从句
  - `that help forecast a woman's risk of breast cancer`：that定语从句，修饰genes

### 生词

appeals court：上诉法院；overturn：推翻；prior：之前的；hold patents to：持有…的专利；forecast：预测；breast cancer：乳腺癌

### 翻译

联邦上诉法院推翻了此前的裁决，裁定Myriad Genetics公司确实可以持有两个帮助预测女性乳腺癌风险的基因的专利。

### 考点

**现在分词ruling that**作伴随状语；宾语从句嵌套定语从句；事件转折——上诉法院推翻原判，公司暂时胜诉。

---

## 长难句4

> Critics make three main arguments against gene patents: a gene is a product of nature, so it may not be patented; gene patents suppress innovation rather than reward it; and patents' monopolies restrict access to genetic tests such as Myriad's.

### 句子拆分

主干：**Critics make three main arguments against gene patents**
- 冒号后三个并列分句，解释三个arguments：
  1. `a gene is a product of nature, so it may not be patented`（so连接因果）
  2. `gene patents suppress innovation rather than reward it`（rather than对比）
  3. `patents' monopolies restrict access to genetic tests such as Myriad's`

### 生词

critic：批评者；argument：论点；product of nature：自然产物；suppress：抑制；innovation：创新；rather than：而不是；monopoly：垄断；restrict access to：限制获取

### 翻译

批评者提出三个反对基因专利的主要论点：基因是自然产物，因此不可申请专利；基因专利抑制创新而非奖励创新；专利垄断限制了人们获取Myriad这类基因检测的机会。

### 考点

**冒号后三重并列**；so因果；rather than对比；32题核心定位段——反对者的三个理由。

## 长难句5

> In October the Department of Justice filed a brief in the Myriad case, arguing that an isolated DNA molecule "is no less a product of nature … than are cotton fibres that have been separated from cotton seeds."

### 句子拆分

主干：**the Department of Justice filed a brief**
- `in the Myriad case`：状语
- `arguing that …`：现在分词作伴随状语
  - that引导宾语从句，核心结构：**an isolated DNA molecule is no less a product of nature than are cotton fibres**
    - `no less … than …`：和…一样不…/不亚于
    - `that have been separated from cotton seeds`：that定语从句，修饰cotton fibres

### 生词

Department of Justice：司法部；file a brief：提交案情摘要；argue that：主张；isolated DNA molecule：分离的DNA分子；no less … than：不亚于；cotton fibre：棉纤维；cotton seed：棉籽

### 翻译

10月，司法部在Myriad案中提交案情摘要，主张分离的DNA分子"与从棉籽中分离出来的棉纤维一样，都是自然产物"。

### 考点

**no less…than…** 比较结构（表"和…一样"）；现在分词arguing that伴随；定语从句；32题B选项依据——自然产物不可专利→只有人造产品可专利。

## 长难句6

> For example, it is unclear whether the sequencing of a whole genome violates the patents of individual genes within it.

### 句子拆分

主干：**it is unclear whether …**
- it是形式主语，真正主语是whether引导的主语从句：**whether the sequencing of a whole genome violates the patents of individual genes within it**

### 生词

sequencing：测序；genome：基因组；violate：违反；individual gene：单个基因

### 翻译

例如，目前尚不清楚对整个基因组进行测序是否会侵犯其中单个基因的专利。

### 考点

**it作形式主语**，whether主语从句后置；第四段主题——仍有重大问题未解决。

## 长难句7

> Companies are unlikely to file many more patents for human DNA molecules—most are already patented or in the public domain.

### 句子拆分

并列句，破折号连接
分句1主干：**Companies are unlikely to file many more patents for human DNA molecules**
分句2主干：**most are already patented or in the public domain**
- most指代most human DNA molecules

### 生词

be unlikely to：不太可能；file patents for：为…申请专利；molecule：分子；public domain：公共领域

### 翻译

公司不太可能再为人类DNA分子申请更多专利——大多数已经被申请专利或处于公共领域。

### 考点

破折号连接原因解释；be unlikely to结构；行业趋势——从单个基因专利转向基因相互作用研究。

## 长难句8

> Firms are now studying how genes interact, looking for correlations that might be used to determine the causes of disease or predict a drug's efficacy.

### 句子拆分

主干：**Firms are now studying how genes interact**
- `how genes interact`：how引导宾语从句
- `looking for correlations that might be used to determine the causes of disease or predict a drug's efficacy`：现在分词作伴随状语
  - `that might be used to determine … or predict …`：that定语从句，修饰correlations
  - `to determine`和`(to) predict`是and/or连接的并列不定式

### 生词

interact：相互作用；correlation：关联；determine：确定；efficacy：药效

### 翻译

公司现在正在研究基因如何相互作用，寻找可能用于确定疾病病因或预测药物疗效的关联。

### 考点

**how宾语从句**；现在分词伴随状语；定语从句含并列不定式；33题B选项定位句——研究基因相互作用。

## 长难句9

> Their success may be determined by a suit related to this issue, brought by the Mayo Clinic, which the Supreme Court will hear in its next term.

### 句子拆分

主干：**Their success may be determined by a suit**
- `related to this issue`：形容词短语作后置定语，修饰suit
- `brought by the Mayo Clinic`：过去分词作后置定语，修饰suit
- `which the Supreme Court will hear in its next term`：which非限制性定语从句，修饰suit

### 生词

be determined by：由…决定；suit：诉讼；Mayo Clinic：梅奥诊所；Supreme Court：最高法院；hear：审理；term：（法院的）开庭期

### 翻译

他们的成功可能取决于梅奥诊所提起的一桩与此相关的诉讼，最高法院将在下一轮审理此案。

### 考点

**多重后置定语**（related to + brought by + which从句）修饰同一个名词suit；未来事件——最高法院审理将决定行业方向。

# 2012 英二 Text3 31–35｜题目+选项+逐选项解析

> 原文主题：基因专利的法律争议、各方立场及行业未来走向

## 31. It can be learned from Paragraph 1 that the biotech companies would like ______.

[A] their executives to be active [B] judges to rule out gene patenting [C] genes to be patentable [D] the BIO to issue a warning

✅答案：**C** 定位：第一段。公司已经拥有基因专利几十年，当法官裁定genes were unpatentable时，高管们violently agitated（极度焦虑）。反推：公司希望基因可以申请专利。

- A：错误。their executives to be active（高管积极活跃）无中生有。高管是焦虑，不是积极。
- B：错误。judges to rule out gene patenting（法官取消基因专利）与原文相反。公司不希望法官取消专利，法官裁定不可专利时公司很焦虑。
- C：正确。基因可以申请专利。公司已经拥有基因专利，法官裁定不可专利时公司焦虑→公司希望基因可专利。
- D：错误。the BIO to issue a warning（BIO发出警告）曲解原文。BIO向成员保证这只是"初步步骤"，不是发出警告。

---

## 32. Those who are against gene patents believe that ______.

[A] genetic tests are not reliable [B] only man-made products are patentable [C] patents on genes depend much on innovation [D] courts should restrict access to genetic tests

✅答案：**B** 定位：第三段。反对者第一个论点：a gene is a product of nature, so it may not be patented（基因是自然产物，不可专利）。司法部也说分离的DNA和棉纤维一样是自然产物。反推：只有非自然的（人造的）产品才可专利。

- A：错误。genetic tests are not reliable（基因检测不可靠）无中生有。反对者说专利垄断限制获取基因检测，不是说检测不可靠。
- B：正确。只有人造产品可申请专利。自然产物不可专利→只有人造产品可专利。
- C：错误。patents on genes depend much on innovation（基因专利很大程度上依赖创新）与原文相反。反对者说基因专利suppress innovation（抑制创新），不是依赖创新。
- D：错误。courts should restrict access to genetic tests（法院应限制获取基因检测）偷换概念。反对者说专利垄断限制获取检测，是批评这种限制，不是主张法院限制。

---

## 33. According to Hans Sauer, companies are eager to win patents for ______.

[A] establishing disease correlations [B] discovering gene interactions [C] drawing pictures of genes [D] identifying human DNA

✅答案：**B** 定位：第五段 `Firms are now studying how genes interact, looking for correlations … Companies are eager to win patents for "connecting the dots"` + Hans Sauer的解释。

"connecting the dots"指的是研究基因如何相互作用、寻找关联，即发现基因相互作用。

- A：错误。establishing disease correlations（建立疾病关联）偷换概念。原文说寻找correlations that might be used to determine causes of disease，关联是基因之间的，不是疾病之间的。
- B：正确。发现基因相互作用。studying how genes interact + connecting the dots = discovering gene interactions。
- C：错误。drawing pictures of genes（绘制基因图片）无中生有。connecting the dots是比喻，不是真的画图。
- D：错误。identifying human DNA（识别人类DNA）与原文相反。第五段说公司不太可能再为人类DNA分子申请专利了。

## 34. By saying "Each meeting was packed" (Para.6), the author means that ______.

[A] the supreme court was authoritative [B] the BIO was a powerful organisation [C] gene patenting was a great concern [D] lawyers were keen to attend conventions

✅答案：**C** 定位：第六段。BIO大会包含培训律师了解专利格局变化的会议，每场都挤满人。说明大家都很关心基因专利问题。

- A：错误。the supreme court was authoritative（最高法院有权威）无关。会议挤满人与最高法院权威无关。
- B：错误。the BIO was a powerful organisation（BIO是强大组织）曲解。会议人多说明议题受关注，不能直接推出BIO强大。
- C：正确。基因专利是一个备受关注的问题。每场会议都挤满人→大家都关心基因专利。
- D：错误。lawyers were keen to attend conventions（律师热衷于参加大会）表面化。律师参加是因为关心专利格局变化，核心是基因专利受关注，不是律师爱开会。

## 35. Generally speaking, the author's attitude toward gene patenting is ______.

[A] critical [B] supportive [C] scornful [D] objective

✅答案：**D** 态度题。全文客观陈述：法官裁定不可专利→上诉法院推翻→批评者三个论点→司法部立场→仍有未解决问题→行业新趋势→未来诉讼。作者没有明确表达支持或反对，只是客观呈现各方立场和事件发展。

- A：错误。critical（批评的）。作者引用了批评者的观点，但没有自己表态批评。
- B：错误。supportive（支持的）。作者也没有表态支持基因专利。
- C：错误。scornful（轻蔑的）。全文没有轻蔑语气。
- D：正确。objective（客观的）。作者客观陈述事件、各方观点和未来走向，没有主观褒贬。

### 本篇命题规律小结（英二阅读）

1. 法律/科技类文章：关注**事件发展时间线**（裁定→上诉→未来诉讼）和**各方立场**（公司、批评者、司法部、法院）
2. 推理题（learned）答案常是**原文的反推**（公司焦虑→公司希望可专利）
3. 句子理解题（means）答案是**深层含义**，不选表面化选项（律师爱开会vs议题受关注）
4. 态度题：如果文章只是客观陈述各方观点，没有作者主观褒贬，选objective
5. 常见干扰：偷换概念（疾病关联vs基因关联）、与原文相反（抑制创新vs依赖创新）、表面化理解

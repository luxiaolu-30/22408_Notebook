# 2012 英二 翻译

## 原文

When people in developing countries worry about migration, they are usually concerned at the prospect of their best and brightest departure to Silicon Valley or to hospitals and universities in the developed world. These are the kind of workers that countries like Britain, Canada and Australia try to attract by using immigration rules that privilege college graduates.

Lots of studies have found that well-educated people from developing countries are particularly likely to emigrate. A big survey of Indian households in 2004 found that nearly 40% of emigrants had more than a high-school education, compared with around 3.3% of all Indians over the age of 25. This "brain drain" has long bothered policymakers in poor countries. They fear that it hurts their economies, depriving them of much-needed skilled workers who could have taught at their universities, worked in their hospitals and come up with clever new products for their factories to make.

## 参考译文

发展中国家的人们为移民忧虑时，他们通常担忧的是本国最优秀、最聪明的人才前往硅谷或发达国家的医院和大学的前景。这些劳动者正是像英国、加拿大和澳大利亚这样的国家试图通过赋予大学毕业生特权的移民法规来吸引的人才。

许多研究发现，来自发展中国家的受过良好教育的人尤其可能移居国外。2004年对印度家庭进行的一项大规模调查发现，近40%的移民受过高中以上教育，而相比之下，在所有25岁以上的印度人中，这一比例仅为约3.3%。这种"人才流失"长期以来一直困扰着贫穷国家的决策者。他们担心这会损害本国经济，使其丧失急需的技术工人——这些工人本可以在本国的大学任教，在本国的医院工作，或者构想出精巧的新产品供本国工厂生产。

## 重点词汇与句型

- **migration**：移民、迁移
- **be concerned at**：担忧、关注（concern at = be worried about）
- **prospect**：前景、前景展望
- **departure**：离开、出发（此处指人才外流）
- **privilege**：赋予特权、给予优惠（此处作动词）
- **emigrate**：移居国外、移民
- **brain drain**：人才流失（固定搭配，报刊常用词）
- **deprive...of...**：剥夺……的……/使丧失……（固定搭配）
- **much-needed**：急需的
- **come up with**：想出、提出、构想出（固定搭配）

## 长难句分析

### 长难句1

> When people in developing countries worry about migration, they are usually concerned at the prospect of their best and brightest departure to Silicon Valley or to hospitals and universities in the developed world.

**句子拆分**：
- 主干：**they are usually concerned at the prospect**
- `When people in developing countries worry about migration`：when引导的时间状语从句
- `of their best and brightest departure to Silicon Valley or to hospitals and universities in the developed world`：介词短语修饰prospect
  - `their best and brightest`：名词化用法，指"最优秀最聪明的人"
  - `departure to Silicon Valley or to hospitals and universities`：离开前往硅谷或医院和大学

**翻译**：发展中国家的人们为移民忧虑时，他们通常担忧的是本国最优秀、最聪明的人才前往硅谷或发达国家的医院和大学的前景。

### 长难句2

> These are the kind of workers that countries like Britain, Canada and Australia try to attract by using immigration rules that privilege college graduates.

**句子拆分**：
- 主干：**These are the kind of workers**
- `that countries like Britain, Canada and Australia try to attract`：that引导的定语从句，修饰workers
  - `by using immigration rules`：方式状语
  - `that privilege college graduates`：that引导的定语从句，修饰immigration rules

**翻译**：这些劳动者正是像英国、加拿大和澳大利亚这样的国家试图通过赋予大学毕业生特权的移民法规来吸引的人才。

### 长难句3

> They fear that it hurts their economies, depriving them of much-needed skilled workers who could have taught at their universities, worked in their hospitals and come up with clever new products for their factories to make.

**句子拆分**：
- 主干：**They fear that it hurts their economies**
- `that it hurts their economies`：that引导的宾语从句
- `depriving them of much-needed skilled workers`：现在分词短语作状语，表结果
- `who could have taught at their universities, worked in their hospitals and come up with clever new products for their factories to make`：who引导的定语从句，修饰skilled workers
  - 三个并列谓语：could have taught / worked / come up with
  - could have done：虚拟语气，表示本可以

**翻译**：他们担心这会损害本国经济，使其丧失急需的技术工人——这些工人本可以在本国的大学任教，在本国的医院工作，或者构想出精巧的新产品供本国工厂生产。

# 2022 英二 小作文

## 题目

**Directions:**

Write an email to introduce a food festival held at your university to international students and invite them to participate. Write your answer in about 100 words.

You should write neatly on the ANSWER SHEET. Do not sign your own name at the end of the email. Use "Li Ming" instead. (10 points)

**场景说明**：假设你是李明，写信向国际学生介绍学校举办的美食节活动，并邀请他们参加。属于"介绍+邀请信"。

## 高分范文

Dear International Students,

I am writing to warmly invite you to our campus food festival, which will be held on the school playground from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. next Saturday.

During the event, you will have the opportunity to taste a wide variety of delicious dishes from different regions of China, ranging from spicy Sichuan cuisine to delicate Cantonese dim sum. In addition, there will be exciting cooking shows where you can learn to make traditional Chinese dumplings. It is also a wonderful chance to experience Chinese food culture and make new friends.

We sincerely hope you can join us and share the joy. If you have any questions, please feel free to contact me. I look forward to your participation.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

## 范文翻译

亲爱的国际学生们：

我写信热烈邀请你们参加我们学校举办的美食节活动，活动将于下周六上午9点到下午5点在学校操场举行。

在活动期间，你们将有机会品尝来自中国不同地区的美味佳肴，从川味麻辣菜肴到精致的广东点心应有尽有。此外，还有精彩的烹饪表演，你们可以学习制作中国传统饺子。这也是体验中国饮食文化和结交新朋友的绝佳机会。

我们真诚地希望你们能来参加并分享这份快乐。如有任何问题，请随时联系我。期待你们的参与。

此致敬礼
李明

## 重点句型与表达

1. **I am writing to warmly invite you to...** —— 邀请信开头万能句型，交代写信目的
2. **...which will be held on... from... to...** —— 非限制性定语从句，交代活动地点和时间
3. **You will have the opportunity to taste a wide variety of...** —— "你们将有机会品尝各种……"，用于介绍活动内容
4. **...ranging from... to...** —— "从……到……应有尽有"，用于列举
5. **In addition, there will be... where you can...** —— "此外，还有……你们可以……"，递进介绍
6. **It is also a wonderful chance to experience... and make...** —— "这也是体验……和结交……的绝佳机会"
7. **We sincerely hope you can join us and share the joy.** —— "真诚希望你们来参加并分享快乐"
8. **I look forward to your participation.** —— "期待你们的参与"，邀请信标准结尾

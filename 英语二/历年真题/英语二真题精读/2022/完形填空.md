# 2022 英二 完形填空

## 原文

Harlan Coben believes that if you're a writer, you'll find the time; and that if you can't find the time, then writing isn't a priority and you're not a writer. For him, writing is a (1) job, a job like any other. He has (2) it with plumbing 管道工作. Pointing out that a plumber 管道工 doesn't wake up and say that he can't work with pipes today. (3), like most writers these days, you're holding down a job to pay the bills, it's not (4) to find the time to write. But it's not impossible. (5) that most best-selling authors began writing when they were doing other things to earn a living. And today, even writers who are fairly (6) often have to do other work to (7) their writing income.

As Harlan Coben has suggested, it's a (8) of priorities 优先级. To make writing a priority, you'll have to (9) some of your day-to-day activities and some things you really enjoy, depending on your (10) and your life style. That might mean spending less time watching television or listening to music, though some people can write (11) they listen to music. You might have to (12) the amount of exercise or sport you do. You'll have to make social media an (13) activity rather than a daily, time-consuming (14). There'll probably have to be less socialising with your friends and your family. It's a (15) learning curve 学习曲线, and it won't always make you popular. There's just one thing: you should try to keep at least some time for, (16) your writing and that's reading. Any writer needs to read as much and as widely as he can. It's the one (17) supporter—something you can't do without. Time is finite. The older you get, the (18) it seems to go. We need to use it as carefully and as (19) as we can. That means prioritising our activities so that we spend most time on the things we really want to do. If you are a writer, that means (20) writing.

### 参考答案

## 重点解析

- **第1题（B normal）**：词义辨析。前文"写作就是一份和其他工作一样的工作"，normal=普通的/正常的，与like any other呼应。difficult困难、steady稳定、pleasant愉快均不符。

- **第3题（B Though）**：逻辑连词。"虽然你也在打工付账，不易找到写作时间，但并非不可能"，Though=虽然，表让步转折。If如果、Once一旦、Unless除非均不符。

- **第5题（C Remember）**：词义辨析+语境。"请记住，大多数畅销书作家都是在做其他事情谋生时开始写作的"，Remember=记住，引出已知事实。Accept接受、Explain解释、Suppose假设均不符。

- **第7题（C supplement）**：词义辨析+语境。"知名作家也常常从事其他工作来弥补写作收入的不足"，supplement=补充/弥补，supplement income=弥补收入。donate捐赠、generate产生、calculate计算均不符。

- **第9题（B sacrifice）**：词义辨析+语境。"要把写作列为优先，就得牺牲一些日常活动和喜好"，sacrifice=牺牲，符合。highlight强调、continue继续、explore探索均不符。

- **第12题（D cut down on）**：固定搭配。"减少运动量"，cut down on=削减/减少。put up with忍受、make up for补偿、hang on to坚持均不符。

- **第16题（D in addition to）**：介词短语。"除了给写作留时间外，还要保留阅读时间"，in addition to=除……之外（还）。in place of代替、in charge of负责、in response to回应均不符。

- **第17题（A indispensable）**：词义辨析。"阅读是不可缺少的支持者——没有它不行"，indispensable=必不可少的。innovative创新的、invisible看不见的、instant即时的均不符。

- **第18题（D quicker）**：逻辑关系。"the+比较级..., the+比较级..."结构，"年龄越大，时间似乎过得越快"，quicker=更快的，符合。

- **第20题（D above all）**：副词短语。"如果你是作家，那就意味着最重要的是写作"，above all=首先/最重要的是。at most至多、in turn轮流、on average平均均不符。

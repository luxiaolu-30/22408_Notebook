We have all encountered them, in both our personal and professional lives. Think about the times you felt tricked 欺骗 or frustrated 沮丧 by a membership or subscription 会员或订阅 that had a seamless 无缝的 sign-up process 注册流程 but was later difficult to cancel—something that should be simple and transparent 透明的 can be complicated, intentionally or unintentionally, in ways that impair 损害 consumer choice 消费者选择. These are examples of dark patterns 黑暗模式.

First coined 首次创造 in 2010 by user experience expert Harry Brignull, "dark patterns" is a catch-all term 笼统术语 for practices that manipulate 操纵 user interfaces 用户界面 to influence the decision-making ability of users. Brignull identifies 12 types of common dark patterns, ranging from misdirection 误导 and hidden costs 隐藏成本 to "roach motel 蟑螂汽车旅馆," where a user experience seems easy and intuitive 直觉的 at the start, but turns difficult when the user tries to get out 退出.

In a 2019 study of 53,000 product pages and 11,000 websites, researchers found that about one in 10 employs these design practices. Though widely prevalent 普遍存在, the concept of dark patterns is still not well understood. Business and nonprofit leaders should be aware of 意识到 dark patterns and try to avoid the gray areas 灰色地带 they engender 产生.

Where is the line between ethical 合乎道德的, persuasive design 有说服力的设计 and dark patterns? Businesses should engage in 参与 conversations with IT, compliance 合规, risk, and legal teams to review their privacy policy 隐私政策, and include in the discussion the customer/user experience designers and coders 程序员 responsible for the company's user interface, as well as the marketers and advertisers responsible for sign-ups, checkout baskets 购物车, pricing, and promotions. Any or all these teams can play a role in creating or avoiding "digital deception 数字欺骗".

Lawmakers and regulators are slowly starting to address 解决 the ambiguity 模糊性 around dark patterns, most recently at the state level. In March, the California Attorney General 加州总检察长 announced the approval of additional regulations under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) 加州消费者隐私法案 that ensures that consumers will not be confused or misled when seeking to exercise their data privacy rights 行使数据隐私权. The regulations aim to ban dark patterns—this means prohibiting companies from using confusing language or unnecessary steps such as forcing them to click through multiple screens 点击多个屏幕 or listen to reasons why they shouldn't opt out 选择退出.

As more states consider promulgating 颁布 additional regulations, there is a need for greater accountability 问责制 from within the business community. Dark patterns also can be addressed on a self-regulatory basis 自我监管基础, but only if organizations hold themselves accountable, not just to legal requirements, but also to industry best practices 行业最佳实践 and standards.

## 长难句1

> Think about the times you felt tricked or frustrated by a membership or subscription that had a seamless sign-up process but was later difficult to cancel—something that should be simple and transparent can be complicated, intentionally or unintentionally, in ways that impair consumer choice.

### 句子拆分

祈使句
主干：**Think about the times**
- `you felt tricked or frustrated by a membership or subscription`：省略that的定语从句，修饰times
  - `that had a seamless sign-up process but was later difficult to cancel`：that定语从句，修饰membership or subscription
    - but连接两个并列谓语
- 破折号后：`something that should be simple and transparent can be complicated, intentionally or unintentionally, in ways that impair consumer choice`
  - 同位语，解释前面整句话

### 生词

### 翻译

- A：错误。guide users through opt-out processes（引导用户完成退出流程）偷换概念。是禁止公司强迫用户点击多个屏幕或听不退出的理由，不是引导用户。
- B：正确。保护消费者不被欺骗。consumers will not be confused or misled + aim to ban dark patterns + prohibit confusing language or unnecessary steps = protect consumers from being tricked。
- C：错误。grant companies data privacy rights（授予公司数据隐私权）与原文相反。是保护消费者的数据隐私权，不是授予公司。
- D：错误。restrict access to problematic content（限制访问有问题的内容）无中生有。

## 35. According to the last paragraph, a key to coping with dark patterns is ______.

✅答案：**B** 定位：最后一段。`there is a need for greater accountability from within the business community.

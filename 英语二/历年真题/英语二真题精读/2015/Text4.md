Many people talked of the 288,000 new jobs the Labor Department 劳工部 reported for June, along with the drop in the unemployment rate 失业率 to 6.1 percent, as good news. And they were right. For now it appears the economy is creating jobs at a decent pace 可观的速度. We still have a long way to go to get back to full employment 充分就业, but at least we are now finally moving forward at a faster pace.

However, there is another important part of the jobs picture 就业图景 that was largely overlooked 被忽视的. There was a big jump 激增 in the number of people who report voluntarily 自愿地 working part-time 兼职. This figure is now 830,000 (4.4 percent) above its year ago level.

Before explaining the connection to the Obamacare 奥巴马医改, it is worth making an important distinction 区别. Many people who work part-time jobs actually want full-time jobs 全职工作. They take part-time work because this is all they can get. An increase in involuntary 非自愿的 part-time work is evidence of weakness in the labor market and it means that many people will be having a very hard time making ends meet 收支相抵.

There was an increase in involuntary part-time in June, but the general direction 总体趋势 has been down. Involuntary part-time employment is still far higher than before the recession, but it is down by 640,000 (7.9 percent) from its year ago level.

We know the difference between voluntary and involuntary part-time employment because people tell us. The survey used by the Labor Department asks people if they worked less than 35 hours in the reference week 参考周. If the answer is "yes," they are classified as 被归类为 working part-time. The survey then asks whether they worked less than 35 hours in that week because they wanted to work less than full time or because they had no choice. They are only classified as voluntary part-time workers if they tell the survey taker 调查者 they chose to work less than 35 hours a week.

The issue of voluntary part-time relates to Obamacare because one of the main purposes was to allow people to get insurance 保险 outside of employment. For many people, especially those with serious health conditions 健康状况 or family members with serious health conditions, before Obamacare the only way to get insurance was through a job that provided health insurance.

However, Obamacare has allowed more than 12 million people to either get insurance through Medicaid 医疗补助计划 or the exchanges 交易平台. These are people who may previously have felt the need to get a full-time job that provided insurance in order to cover 保障 themselves and their families. With Obamacare there is no longer a link between employment and insurance.

## 题目（36–40）

## 长难句1

> Many people talked of the 288,000 new jobs the Labor Department reported for June, along with the drop in the unemployment rate to 6.1 percent, as good news.

### 句子拆分

主干：**Many people talked of the 288,000 new jobs … as good news**
- `the Labor Department reported for June`：省略that的定语从句，修饰jobs
- `along with the drop in the unemployment rate to 6.1 percent`：along with短语，与jobs并列

### 生词

talk of：谈论；Labor Department：劳工部；along with：连同；unemployment rate：失业率

### 翻译

许多人把劳工部报告的6月份新增28.8万个就业岗位，连同失业率降至6.1%，称为好消息。

### 考点

**省略that的定语从句+along with并列**；开篇引入就业数据，36题的背景——大家关注新增就业，但忽视了自愿兼职增加。

## 长难句3

> An increase in involuntary part-time work is evidence of weakness in the labor market and it means that many people will be having a very hard time making ends meet.

### 句子拆分

并列句，and连接
分句1主干：**An increase in involuntary part-time work is evidence of weakness in the labor market**
分句2主干：**it means that …**
- that引导宾语从句：`many people will be having a very hard time making ends meet`
  - `have a hard time doing`：做某事很困难

### 生词

involuntary：非自愿的；evidence：证据；weakness：疲软、弱点；labor market：劳动力市场；have a hard time：做某事很困难；make ends meet：收支相抵、勉强糊口

### 翻译

非自愿兼职工作的增加是劳动力市场疲软的证据，这意味着许多人将很难做到收支相抵。

### 考点

**and并列句+that宾语从句+have a hard time doing**；区分自愿与非自愿兼职的关键句，37题的逻辑铺垫。

# 2015 英二 Text4 36–40｜题目+选项+逐选项解析

> 原文主题：美国就业市场复苏中被忽视的自愿兼职增加现象，其与奥巴马医改使就业与保险脱钩有关

## 36. Which part of the jobs picture was neglected?

[A] The prospect of a thriving job market. [B] The increase of voluntary part-time jobs. [C] The possibility of full employment. [D] The acceleration of job creation.

✅答案：**B** 定位：第二段。`there is another important part of the jobs picture that was largely overlooked. There was a big jump in the number of people who report voluntarily working part-time`（就业图景中还有一个重要部分被忽视了——自愿兼职工作的人数大幅增加）。

- A：错误。The prospect of a thriving job market（就业市场繁荣的前景）是大家关注的好消息，不是被忽视的。
- B：正确。自愿兼职工作的增加。big jump in voluntarily working part-time = increase of voluntary part-time jobs。
- C：错误。The possibility of full employment（充分就业的可能性）无中生有。
- D：错误。The acceleration of job creation（就业创造加速）是大家关注的好消息，不是被忽视的。

## 37. Many people work part-time because they

[A] prefer part-time jobs to full-time jobs [B] feel that is enough to make ends meet [C] cannot get their hands on full-time jobs [D] haven't seen the weakness of the market

✅答案：**C** 定位：第三段。`Many people who work part-time jobs actually want full-time jobs. They take part-time work because this is all they can get`（许多做兼职的人实际上想要全职工作。他们做兼职是因为这是他们能得到的全部）。即他们找不到全职工作。

- A：错误。prefer part-time jobs to full-time jobs（比起全职更喜欢兼职）与原文相反。他们actually want full-time jobs（实际上想要全职）。
- B：错误。feel that is enough to make ends meet（觉得那足够收支相抵）与原文相反。非自愿兼职意味着having a very hard time making ends meet（很难收支相抵）。
- C：正确。找不到全职工作。this is all they can get = cannot get their hands on full-time jobs。
- D：错误。haven't seen the weakness of the market（没看到市场疲软）无中生有。

## 38. Involuntary part-time employment in the US

[A] is harder to acquire than one year ago [B] shows a general tendency of decline [C] satisfies the real need of the jobless [D] is lower than before the recession

✅答案：**B** 定位：第四段。`the general direction has been down`（总体趋势一直在下降）+ `it is down by 640,000 (7.9 percent) from its year ago level`（与去年同期相比下降了64万）。非自愿兼职总体呈下降趋势。

- A：错误。is harder to acquire than one year ago（比一年前更难获得）无中生有。原文说数量下降，不是更难获得。
- B：正确。总体呈下降趋势。general direction has been down = general tendency of decline。
- C：错误。satisfies the real need of the jobless（满足失业者的真实需求）与原文相反。非自愿兼职是市场疲软的证据，不是满足需求。
- D：错误。is lower than before the recession（比经济衰退前低）与原文相反。原文说still far higher than before the recession（仍然远高于衰退前）。

## 39. It can be learned that with Obamacare, ______.

[A] it is no longer easy for part-timers to get insurance [B] employment is no longer a precondition to get insurance [C] it is still challenging to get insurance for family members [D] full-time employment is still essential for insurance

✅答案：**B** 定位：最后两段。`one of the main purposes was to allow people to get insurance outside of employment`（主要目的之一是允许人们在就业之外获得保险）+ `With Obamacare there is no longer a link between employment and insurance`（有了奥巴马医改，就业和保险之间不再有联系）。即就业不再是获得保险的前提。

- A：错误。it is no longer easy for part-timers to get insurance（兼职者获得保险不再容易）与原文相反。奥巴马医改让更多人获得保险，包括兼职者。
- B：正确。就业不再是获得保险的前提。get insurance outside of employment + no longer a link between employment and insurance = employment is no longer a precondition。
- C：错误。it is still challenging to get insurance for family members（为家人获得保险仍然有挑战）无中生有。原文说医改让人们能保障自己和家人。
- D：错误。full-time employment is still essential for insurance（全职工作对保险仍然必不可少）与原文相反。医改后就业与保险不再有联系。

## 40. The text mainly discusses ______.

[A] employment in the US [B] part-timer classification [C] insurance through Medicaid [D] Obamacare's trouble

✅答案：**A** 主旨题。全文主线：美国就业市场新增岗位是好消息→但自愿兼职增加被忽视→区分自愿与非自愿兼职→非自愿兼职下降→自愿兼职增加与奥巴马医改有关（就业与保险脱钩）。全文围绕美国就业市场展开，兼职和保险都是就业话题的一部分。

- A：正确。美国就业。全文从就业数据→兼职分类→医改对就业的影响，都围绕美国就业展开。
- B：错误。part-timer classification（兼职者分类）片面。分类只是第三、五段的内容，不是全文主旨。
- C：错误。insurance through Medicaid（通过医疗补助计划获得保险）片面。这只是最后一段的细节，不是全文主旨。
- D：错误。Obamacare's trouble（奥巴马医改的麻烦）与原文态度相反。文章说的是奥巴马医改的好消息（让人们在就业之外获得保险），不是麻烦。

### 本篇命题规律小结（英二阅读）

1. 经济/政策类文章：关注**好消息→被忽视的另一面→概念区分→数据趋势→政策关联**的结构
2. 细节题：定位到**数据和转折词**（however, but），答案是同义替换
3. 原因题（because）：定位到**because this is all they can get**，答案是反推（找不到全职）
4. 推理题：基于**首尾呼应**（allow people to get insurance outside of employment → no longer a link）
5. 主旨题：答案需**贯穿全文**（employment反复出现），不选片面细节（兼职分类、Medicaid）或与态度相反的选项（Obamacare's trouble）

# 2025 英二 翻译

## 原文

You know the moment—the conversation slows, then there's a pause. It's awkward, and so awkward that some people will panic and say anything. Do we all find such silences so stressful?

Researchers analysed the frequency and impact of gaps greater than 2 seconds during conversations, including an overview of previous studies which indicate that the fear of awkward silences can be so extreme that people avoid talking to strangers, even though doing so is likely to be an enjoyable experience.

During conversations with short gaps, people feel more connected to their conversation partners. But such feelings of connection markedly dip when entering a long gap. Long gaps between strangers are likely to be followed by a change in topic.

But the opposite seems to be true for conversations between friends. Long gaps there saw increased connection. Between friends, longer gaps seem to provide natural moments for reflection and expression.

## 参考译文

你一定经历过这样的时刻——对话慢了下来，然后出现了停顿。这很尴尬，尴尬到有些人会惊慌失措，什么话都说得出来。我们所有人都觉得这样的沉默如此令人倍感压力吗？

研究人员分析了对话中超过2秒的停顿的频率和影响，包括对先前研究的综述，这些研究表明，对尴尬沉默的恐惧可能如此强烈，以至于人们避免与陌生人交谈，尽管这样做很可能是一次愉快的体验。

在短暂停顿的对话中，人们感觉与对话伙伴更加亲近。但当进入长时间停顿时，这种连接感会显著下降。陌生人之间的长时间停顿可能会伴随话题的转变。

但对于朋友之间的对话来说，情况似乎恰恰相反。在朋友之间，长时间的停顿反而增加了连接感。在朋友之间，更长的停顿似乎提供了自然的反思和表达时刻。

## 重点词汇与句型

- **pause**：停顿，暂停
- **awkward**：尴尬的，令人难堪的
- **panic**：惊慌，恐慌
- **frequency**：频率
- **gap**：间隙，间隔（此处指对话中的停顿）
- **overview**：概述，综述
- **extreme**：极端的，极其的
- **markedly**：显著地，明显地
- **dip**：下降，下跌
- **reflection**：反思，思考

## 长难句分析

**1. Researchers analysed the frequency and impact of gaps greater than 2 seconds during conversations, including an overview of previous studies which indicate that the fear of awkward silences can be so extreme that people avoid talking to strangers, even though doing so is likely to be an enjoyable experience.**

- **结构**：主句 + including介词短语 + which定语从句 + that宾语从句（嵌套so...that结果状语从句 + even though让步状语从句）
- **拆分**：Researchers analysed the frequency and impact of gaps greater than 2 seconds during conversations（主句）/ including an overview of previous studies（介词短语）/ which indicate（定语从句，修饰studies）/ that the fear of awkward silences can be so extreme（宾语从句）/ that people avoid talking to strangers（so...that结果状语从句）/ even though doing so is likely to be an enjoyable experience（让步状语从句）
- **翻译要点**：which定语从句较长，需拆分单独成句；so...that=如此……以至于；even though=尽管；doing so指代talking to strangers
- **译文**：研究人员分析了对话中超过2秒的停顿的频率和影响，包括对先前研究的综述，这些研究表明，对尴尬沉默的恐惧可能如此强烈，以至于人们避免与陌生人交谈，尽管这样做很可能是一次愉快的体验。

**2. But such feelings of connection markedly dip when entering a long gap.**

- **结构**：主句 + when引导的时间状语从句（省略结构）
- **拆分**：such feelings of connection markedly dip（主句）/ when entering a long gap（时间状语从句，when后省略they are）
- **翻译要点**：markedly=显著地；dip=下降；when entering=when they are entering（省略主语和be动词）
- **译文**：但当进入长时间停顿时，这种连接感会显著下降。

**3. But the opposite seems to be true for conversations between friends.**

- **结构**：简单句，the opposite作主语
- **翻译要点**：the opposite=相反的情况；seems to be true=似乎是正确的
- **译文**：但对于朋友之间的对话来说，情况似乎恰恰相反。

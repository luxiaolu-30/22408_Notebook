# 2021 英二 翻译

## 原文

We tend to think that friends and family members are our biggest sources of connection, laughter and warmth. While that may well be true, researchers have also recently found that interacting with strangers actually brings a boost in mood and feelings of belonging that we didn't expect.

In one series of studies, researchers instructed Chicago-area commuters using public transportation to strike up a conversation with someone near them. On average, participants who followed this instruction felt better than those who had been told to stand or sit in silence. The researchers also argued that when we shy away from casual interactions with strangers, it is often due to a misplaced anxiety that they might not want to talk to us. Much of the time, however, this belief is false. As it turns out, many people are actually perfectly willing to talk—and may even be flattered to receive your attention.

## 参考译文

我们往往认为，朋友和家人是我们人际联系、快乐和温暖的最大源泉。尽管这一点也许十分正确，但研究人员最近也发现，与陌生人交流实际上能带来意想不到的情绪提升和归属感。

在一项系列研究中，研究人员让芝加哥地区乘坐公共交通工具的通勤者与旁边的人搭话。平均而言，遵循这一指示的参与者比那些被告知静默站着或坐着的人感觉更好。研究人员还表示，当我们羞于与陌生人进行随意的互动时，往往是因为一种不合时宜的焦虑——认为他们可能不想和我们说话。然而，大多数时候，这种看法是错误的。事实证明，很多人实际上非常乐意交谈——甚至可能因为得到你的关注而感到荣幸。

## 重点词汇与句型

- **tend to do**：倾向于做某事，往往
- **interact with**：与……交流/互动
- **a boost in mood**：情绪的提升/改善
- **feelings of belonging**：归属感
- **commuter**：通勤者，上下班乘车的人
- **strike up a conversation**：开始交谈，搭话
- **shy away from**：躲避，回避，羞于
- **casual interactions**：随意的互动/交流
- **misplaced anxiety**：不合时宜的/ misplaced错位的焦虑
- **flattered**：感到荣幸的，受宠若惊的

## 长难句分析

**1. While that may well be true, researchers have also recently found that interacting with strangers actually brings a boost in mood and feelings of belonging that we didn't expect.**

- **结构**：while引导让步状语从句 + 主句 + that引导的宾语从句（嵌套that引导的定语从句）
- **拆分**：While that may well be true（让步状语从句）/ researchers have also recently found（主句）/ that interacting with strangers actually brings a boost in mood and feelings of belonging（宾语从句）/ that we didn't expect（定语从句，修饰a boost in mood and feelings of belonging）
- **翻译要点**：while=尽管；定语从句较短可前置译为"意想不到的"
- **译文**：尽管这一点也许十分正确，但研究人员最近也发现，与陌生人交流实际上能带来意想不到的情绪提升和归属感。

**2. The researchers also argued that when we shy away from casual interactions with strangers, it is often due to a misplaced anxiety that they might not want to talk to us.**

- **结构**：主句 + that引导的宾语从句（嵌套when引导的时间状语从句 + that引导的同位语从句）
- **拆分**：The researchers also argued（主句）/ that when we shy away from casual interactions with strangers（宾语从句中的时间状语从句）/ it is often due to a misplaced anxiety（宾语从句主句）/ that they might not want to talk to us（同位语从句，补充说明anxiety）
- **翻译要点**：due to=因为；同位语从句单独处理成一句"认为他们可能不想和我们说话"
- **译文**：研究人员还表示，当我们羞于与陌生人进行随意的互动时，往往是因为一种不合时宜的焦虑——认为他们可能不想和我们说话。

**3. As it turns out, many people are actually perfectly willing to talk—and may even be flattered to receive your attention.**

- **结构**：as引导的非限制性定语从句 + 主句（and连接两个并列谓语）
- **拆分**：As it turns out（非限制性定语从句，as=正如）/ many people are actually perfectly willing to talk（主句第一部分）/ and may even be flattered to receive your attention（主句第二部分）
- **翻译要点**：as it turns out=事实证明；perfectly=非常；flattered=感到荣幸的
- **译文**：事实证明，很多人实际上非常乐意交谈——甚至可能因为得到你的关注而感到荣幸。

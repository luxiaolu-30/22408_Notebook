## 详细解析

##### 翻译（15 分）

#### 原文

With the smell of coffee and fresh bread floating in the air, stalls bursting with colourful vegetables and tempting cheeses, and the buzz of friendly chats, farmers' markets are a feast for the senses. They also provide an opportunity to talk to the people responsible for growing or raising your food, support your local economy and pick up fresh seasonal produce—all at the same time. Farmers' markets are usually weekly or monthly events, most often with outdoor stalls, which allow farmers or producers to sell their food directly to customers. The size or regularity of markets can vary from season to season, depending on the area's agricultural calendar, and you're likely to find different produce on sale at different times of the year. By cutting out the middlemen, the farmers secure more profit for their produce.

#### 参考译文

空气中飘着咖啡和新鲜面包的香味，摊位上摆满了五颜六色的蔬菜和诱人的奶酪，还有友好交谈的嘈杂声，农贸市场是一场感官盛宴。它们还同时提供了一个机会，让你与负责种植或饲养你所吃食物的人交谈，支持当地经济，并获取新鲜的时令农产品——所有这些在同一时间就能完成。农贸市场通常是每周或每月举办的活动，大多是户外摊位，让农民或生产者直接向顾客出售食物。市场的规模或频率会随季节变化而不同，取决于该地区的农业日历，你在一年中的不同时间可能会发现不同的农产品在售。通过省去中间商，农民能为自己的农产品获得更多利润。

#### 重点词汇与句型

- **floating in the air**：飘荡在空气中
- **bursting with**：摆满了，充满
- **tempting**：诱人的
- **the buzz of**：……的嘈杂声/嗡嗡声
- **a feast for the senses**：感官盛宴
- **responsible for**：负责……的
- **seasonal produce**：时令农产品
- **regularity**：规律性，频率
- **agricultural calendar**：农业日历
- **cut out the middlemen**：省去中间商
- **secure more profit**：获得更多利润

#### 长难句分析

**1. With the smell of coffee and fresh bread floating in the air, stalls bursting with colourful vegetables and tempting cheeses, and the buzz of friendly chats, farmers' markets are a feast for the senses.**

- **结构**：With引导的独立主格结构（三个并列成分）作伴随状语 + 主句
- **拆分**：With the smell of coffee and fresh bread floating in the air（独立主格1）/ stalls bursting with colourful vegetables and tempting cheeses（独立主格2）/ and the buzz of friendly chats（独立主格3）/ farmers' markets are a feast for the senses（主句）
- **翻译要点**：三个with独立主格结构需分别译出，用顿号或逗号分隔；floating in the air=飘在空气中；bursting with=充满/摆满；a feast for the senses=感官盛宴
- **译文**：空气中飘着咖啡和新鲜面包的香味，摊位上摆满了五颜六色的蔬菜和诱人的奶酪，还有友好交谈的嘈杂声，农贸市场是一场感官盛宴。

**2. They also provide an opportunity to talk to the people responsible for growing or raising your food, support your local economy and pick up fresh seasonal produce—all at the same time.**

- **结构**：主句 + to talk to...不定式 + 三个并列动词短语 + 破折号补充说明
- **拆分**：They also provide an opportunity（主句）/ to talk to the people responsible for growing or raising your food（不定式1）/ support your local economy（并列动词2）/ and pick up fresh seasonal produce（并列动词3）/ —all at the same time（补充说明）
- **翻译要点**：三个并列动词短语共享an opportunity；responsible for=负责……的，作后置定语修饰people；all at the same time=同时完成
- **译文**：它们还同时提供了一个机会，让你与负责种植或饲养你所吃食物的人交谈，支持当地经济，并获取新鲜的时令农产品——所有这些在同一时间就能完成。

**3. By cutting out the middlemen, the farmers secure more profit for their produce.**

- **结构**：By引导的方式状语 + 主句
- **拆分**：By cutting out the middlemen（方式状语）/ the farmers secure more profit for their produce（主句）
- **翻译要点**：cut out the middlemen=省去中间商；secure=获得/确保；produce=农产品
- **译文**：通过省去中间商，农民能为自己的农产品获得更多利润。

##### 小作文（10 分）

#### 题目

**Directions:**

Suppose you and Jack are going to do a survey on the protection of old houses in an ancient town. Write him an email to

1) put forward your plan, and
2) ask for his/her opinion.


You should write about 100 words on the ANSWER SHEET. Do not use your own name in your email, use "Li Ming" instead. (10 points)

**场景说明**：假设你和李华（同学）计划去某古镇进行古建筑调研，写一封电子邮件，提出你的计划并征求对方的意见。属于"计划+征求意见信"。

#### 高分范文

Dear Li Hua,

I hope this email finds you well. As we discussed, we are going to conduct a survey on ancient architecture in the historic town. I would like to share my plan with you and hear your thoughts.

In my opinion, it is indispensable for us to take a field trip to those ancient buildings and visit local people in the historic town. We should prepare some questions in advance for our survey, which is of great significance. Specifically, we can take photos and record heartfelt stories from the residents. I suggest we complete the survey before January 24th, 2024.

Please feel free to contact me if you have any other ideas or suggestions on my arrangement. I am looking forward to a further detailed discussion with you.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

#### 范文翻译

亲爱的李华：

希望这封邮件找到你时你一切都好。正如我们所讨论的，我们计划去某古镇进行古建筑调研。我想和你分享我的计划并听取你的想法。

在我看来，我们有必要实地考察那些古建筑，并拜访古镇的当地居民。我们应该提前为调研准备一些问题，这非常重要。具体来说，我们可以拍照并记录居民的真心故事。我建议我们在2024年1月24日之前完成调研。

如果你对我的安排有任何其他想法或建议，请随时联系我。期待与你进行进一步的详细讨论。

此致敬礼
李明

#### 重点句型与表达

1. **I hope this email finds you well.** —— 邮件开头万能问候句
2. **I would like to share my plan with you and hear your thoughts.** —— "我想和你分享计划并听取你的想法"，交代写信目的
3. **In my opinion, it is indispensable for us to...** —— "在我看来，我们有必要……"，引出计划内容
4. **We should prepare some questions in advance for...** —— "我们应该提前为……准备问题"
5. **Specifically, we can...** —— "具体来说，我们可以……"，细化计划
6. **I suggest we complete... before...** —— "我建议我们在……之前完成……"，给出时间安排
7. **Please feel free to contact me if you have any other ideas or suggestions.** —— "如有其他想法或建议请随时联系"，征求意见万能结尾
8. **I am looking forward to a further detailed discussion with you.** —— "期待与你进一步详细讨论"

##### 大作文（15 分）

#### 图表描述

**图表类型**：柱状图（Bar Chart）

**图表内容**：某高校学生劳动实践课主要收获调查
- 学习相关知识（learning relevant knowledge）：91.3%（占比最高）
- 提升动手能力（improved hands-on ability）：84.8%
- 心情舒畅（feeling relaxed/pleasurable）：54.4%
- 增强合作能力（enhanced cooperative ability）：32.6%

#### 题目

**Directions:**

Write an essay based on the chart below. In your writing, you should
1) describe and interpret the chart, and
2) give your comments.

You should write about 150 words on the ANSWER SHEET. (15 points)

#### 高分范文

The bar chart above clearly illustrates the main gains students obtained from labor practice courses at a certain university. As is shown, learning relevant knowledge takes the largest share, accounting for 91.3%, followed by improving hands-on ability at 84.8%, feeling relaxed at 54.4%, and enhancing cooperative ability at 32.6%.

What are the reasons behind this distribution? Several factors contribute to this phenomenon. Initially, labor practice courses emphasize practical skills training, which naturally leads to the improvement of hands-on ability as the most prominent gain. Furthermore, these courses require students to work together to complete tasks, fostering a sense of teamwork and collaboration. The inclusion of labor values reflects the educational goal of instilling respect for hard work and diligence in students. Understanding career directions, though accounting for the smallest proportion, indicates that labor practice also helps students explore future career paths.

In conclusion, the chart reveals that labor practice courses offer multifaceted benefits to students. It is advisable that universities continue to strengthen such programs, as they not only develop practical skills but also shape students' values and career awareness.

#### 范文翻译

上方的柱状图清晰地展示了某高校学生从劳动实践课中获得的主要收获。如图所示，学习相关知识占比最高，达91.3%，其次是提升动手能力占84.8%，心情舒畅占54.4%，增强合作能力占32.6%。

这一分布背后的原因是什么？有几个因素促成了这一现象。首先，劳动实践课程注重实践技能训练，自然使得动手能力的提升成为最显著的收获。此外，这些课程要求学生协作完成任务，培养了团队合作意识。劳动观念的纳入反映了教育目标，即培养学生对辛勤劳动和勤奋的尊重。了解职业方向虽然占比最小，但表明劳动实践也有助于学生探索未来的职业道路。

总之，图表表明劳动实践课程为学生提供了多方面的益处。建议高校继续加强此类项目，因为它们不仅培养实践技能，还塑造学生的价值观和职业意识。

#### 重点句型与表达

1. **The bar chart above clearly illustrates...** —— 柱状图开头万能句型，引出图表主题
2. **As is shown, ... takes the largest share, accounting for X%, followed by... at Y%** —— 描述柱状图数据的经典句式（最大项+排序）
3. **What are the reasons behind this distribution?** —— 过渡句，引出原因分析
4. **Several factors contribute to this phenomenon.** —— "有几个因素促成了这一现象"，分析原因的万能过渡句
5. **...emphasize..., which naturally leads to...** —— "……注重……，自然导致……"
6. **In conclusion, the chart reveals that... offer multifaceted benefits to...** —— 结尾段总结句，"图表揭示……提供多方面益处"
7. **It is advisable that... continue to strengthen...** —— "建议……继续加强……"
8. **...not only develop..., but also shape...** —— "不仅培养……，还塑造……"，not only...but also并列结构

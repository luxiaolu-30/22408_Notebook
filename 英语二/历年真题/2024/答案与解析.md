#### 重点解析

- **第1题（C whereas）**：逻辑连词。前句"有些人因与他人相处而精力充沛"，后句"有些人即使做喜欢的事也会感到疲惫"，前后对比，whereas=然而/而，表对比转折。

- **第2题（B balance）**：词义辨析。"在社交生活中找到平衡是关键"，balance=平衡，全文围绕社交过多或过少的问题展开。

- **第4题（D disconnected）**：词义辨析+语境。"独自待着不见他人会让你感到孤独和脱节"，disconnected=脱节的/失去联系的，与lonely并列。misguided被误导、surprised惊讶、spoiled被宠坏均不符。

- **第5题（A contribute to）**：固定搭配。"孤独已知会影响心理健康并导致低落情绪"，contribute to=导致/促成。rely on依赖、interfere with干扰、go against违背均不符。

- **第7题（B missing out）**：词组辨析。"居家办公使你错失办公室里通常的社交对话"，miss out on=错过/错失。cut back削减、break in闯入、look down俯视均不符。

- **第11题（D like-minded）**：词义辨析。"你将能遇到志同道合的人"，like-minded=志同道合的/志趣相投的。far-sighted远见的、strong-willed意志坚强的、kind-hearted善良的均不符。

- **第15题（C space）**：词义辨析。"日历中没有休息时间的空间"，space=空间/余地。list列表、order顺序、boundary边界均不符。

- **第17题（C signs）**：词义辨析。"低落情绪、精力不足、易怒和睡眠问题都可能是社交健康不佳的征兆"，signs=征兆/迹象。sources来源、standards标准、scores分数均不符。

- **第19题（B unavailable）**：词义辨析。"在日历中标记出你不可用于社交的时间"，unavailable=不可用的/没空的。ungrateful不感恩、responsible负责、regretful后悔均不符。

- **第20题（D rest）**：词义辨析+并列结构。"用这段时间来放松、休息和恢复"，relax/___/recover三连并列，rest=休息，与前后的relax和recover构成语义并列。

In her new book Cogs and Monsters: What Economics Is, and What It Should Be, Diane Coyle, an economist at Cambridge University, argues that the digital economy requires new ways of thinking about progress. "Whatever we mean by the economy growing, by things getting better, the gains will have to be more evenly shared 更均衡地共享 than in the recent past," she writes. "An economy of tech millionaires or billionaires and gig workers 零工工人, with middle-income jobs undercut by 被…削弱 automation, will not be politically sustainable 政治上可持续的."

Improving living standards and increasing prosperity 繁荣 for more people will require greater use of digital technologies to boost productivity 提高生产率 in various sectors, including health care and construction, says Coyle. But people can't be expected to embrace 接受 the changes if they're not seeing the benefits—if they're just seeing good jobs being destroyed.

In a recent interview, Coyle said she fears that tech's inequality problem could be a roadblock 障碍 to deploying AI. "We're talking about disruption 颠覆," she says. "These are transformative technologies 变革性技术 that change the ways we spend our time every day, that change business models that succeed." To make such "tremendous changes," she adds, you need social buy-in 社会认同.

Instead, says Coyle, resentment 不满 is simmering 酝酿 among many as the benefits are perceived to go to elites 精英 in a handful of prosperous cities.

According to the Brookings Institution, a short list of eight American cities that included San Francisco, San Jose, Boston, and Seattle had roughly 38% of all tech jobs by 2019. New AI technologies are particularly concentrated: Brookings's Mark Muro and Sifan Liu estimate that just 15 cities account for two-thirds of the AI assets and capabilities in the United States.

The dominance of a few cities in the invention and commercialization of AI means that geographical disparities 地理差距 in wealth will continue to soar 飙升. Not only will this foster political and social unrest 动荡, but it could, as Coyle suggests, hold back 阻碍 the sorts of AI technologies needed for regional economies to grow.

Part of the solution could lie in somehow loosening 放松 the stranglehold 束缚 that Big Tech has on defining the AI agenda. That will likely take increased federal funding for research independent of the tech giants.

A more immediate response is to broaden our digital imaginations to conceive of 构想 AI technologies that don't simply replace jobs but expand opportunities in the sectors that different parts of the country care most about, like health care, education, and manufacturing.

#### 长难句1

> "Whatever we mean by the economy growing, by things getting better, the gains will have to be more evenly shared than in the recent past," she writes.

##### 句子拆分

主干：**the gains will have to be more evenly shared than in the recent past**
- `Whatever we mean by the economy growing, by things getting better`：whatever让步状语从句
- `she writes`：倒装引语

##### 生词

whatever：无论什么；mean by：意思是；economy growing：经济增长；things getting better：事物变好；gains：收益；have to：必须；more evenly shared：更均衡地共享；than in the recent past：比最近的过去

##### 翻译

##### 考点

**whatever让步状语从句+比较级+倒装引语**；21题C选项的依据——Coyle认为经济增长应该让人们平等受益（benefit people equally = gains more evenly shared）。

One of the biggest challenges in keeping unsafe aging drivers 不安全的老年司机 off the road is convincing them that it's time to turn over the keys 交出钥匙. "It's a complete life-changer 彻底改变生活的事" when someone stops—or is forced to stop—driving, said former risk manager 前风险经理 Anne M. Menke.

"The American Medical Association 美国医学协会 advises physicians that in situations where clear evidence of substantial driving impairment 严重驾驶能力受损 implies a strong threat to patient and public safety, and where the physician's advice to discontinue driving privileges 终止驾驶特权 is ignored, it is desirable and ethical 合乎道德的 to notify the Department of Motor Vehicles 机动车管理局," Menke wrote. "Some states require physicians to report, others allow but do not mandate 强制要求 reports, while a few consider a report a breach of confidentiality 违反保密. There could be liability and penalties 责任和处罚 if a physician does not act in accordance with 按照 state laws on reporting and confidentiality," she counseled 建议.

Part of the problem in keeping older drivers safe is that the difficulties are addressed piecemeal 零碎地 by different professions with different focuses, including gerontologists 老年学家, highway administration officials 公路管理官员, automotive engineers 汽车工程师 and others said gerontologist Elizabeth Dugan. "There's not a National Institute of Older Driver Studies 老年司机研究国家研究所," she said. "We need better evidence on what makes drivers unsafe and what can help," said Dugan.

One thing that does seem to work is requiring drivers to report in person 亲自报告 for license renewal 驾照更新. Mandatory in-person renewal 强制亲自更新 was associated with a 31 percent reduction in fatal crashes 致命车祸 involving drivers 85 or older, according to one study. Passing vision tests 视力测试 also produced a similar decline in fatal crashes for those drivers, although there appeared to be no benefit from combining the two.

Many older drivers don't see eye doctors or can't afford to. Primary care providers 初级保健提供者 have their hands full 忙得不可开交 and may not be able to follow through with 跟进 patients who have trouble driving because they can't turn their heads or remember where they are going—or have gotten shorter and haven't changed their seat settings sufficiently to reach car pedals 汽车踏板 easily.

As long as there are other cars on the roads, self-driving cars won't solve the problems of crashes, said Dugan. Avoiding dangers posed by all those human drivers would require too many algorithms 算法, she said. But we need to do more to improve safety, said Dugan. "If we're going to have 100-year lives, we need cars that a 90-year-old can drive comfortably."

If you look at the apps on your phone, chances are 很可能 you have at least one related to your health—and probably several. Whether it is a mental health app, a fitness tracker 健身追踪器, a connected health device 联网健康设备 or something else, many of us are taking advantage of 利用 this technology to keep better track of 更好地跟踪 our health in some shape or form 以某种形式. Recent research from the Organization for the Review of Care and Health Applications 护理和健康应用审查组织 found that 350,000 health apps were available on the market, 90,000 of which launched in 2020 alone.

While these apps have a great deal to offer 有很多可提供的, it is not always clear how the personal information we input is collected, safeguarded 保护 and shared online. Existing health privacy law 现有健康隐私法, such as the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act 健康保险流通与责任法案, is primarily focused on 主要关注 the way hospitals, doctors' offices, clinics and insurance companies 保险公司 store health records online. The health information these apps and health data tracking wearables 健康数据追踪可穿戴设备 are collecting typically does not receive the same legal protections.

Without additional protections in place, companies may share (and potentially monetize 货币化) personal health information in a way consumers may not have authorized 授权 or anticipated 预期. In 2021, Flo Health faced a Federal Trade Commission (FTC) 联邦贸易委员会 investigation. The FTC alleged 指控 in a complaint that "despite express privacy claims 明确的隐私声明, the company took control of users' sensitive fertility data 敏感生育数据 and shared it with third parties 第三方." Flo Health and FTC settled the matter with a consent order 同意令 requiring the company to get app users' express affirmative consent 明确肯定同意 before sharing their health information as well as to instruct the third parties to delete the data they had obtained.

Section 5 of the FTC Act 联邦贸易委员会法第5条 empowers 授权 the FTC to initiate enforcement action 发起执法行动 against unfair or deceptive acts 不公平或欺骗性行为, meaning the FTC can only act after the fact if a company's privacy practices are misleading or cause unjustified consumer harm 不合理的消费者损害. While the FTC is doing what it can to ensure apps are keeping their promises to consumers around the handling of their sensitive health information, the rate at which these health apps are hitting the market 进入市场 demonstrates just how immense of a challenge this is.

As to the prospects for federal legislation 联邦立法前景, commentators suggest that comprehensive federal privacy legislation 全面的联邦隐私立法 seems unlikely in the short term 短期内. States have begun implementing their own solutions to shore up 加强 protections for consumer-generated health data 消费者生成的健康数据. California has been at the forefront 处于前沿 of state privacy efforts with the California Consumer Privacy Act of 2018 2018年加州消费者隐私法案. Virginia, Colorado and Utah have also recently passed state consumer data privacy legislation.

- `Without additional protections in place`：without介词短语作状语
- `(and potentially monetize)`：插入语
- `consumers may not have authorized or anticipated`：省略that/in which的定语从句，修饰way
  - 两个谓语并列：may not have authorized or anticipated

##### 生词

without additional protections in place：没有额外保护；companies：公司；share：分享；potentially monetize：潜在货币化；personal health information：个人健康信息；in a way：以某种方式；consumers：消费者；authorize：授权；anticipate：预期

##### 翻译

没有额外保护，公司可能以消费者可能未授权或未预期的方式分享（并潜在货币化）个人健康信息。

##### 考点

**without介词短语+插入语+省略that/in which的定语从句+两个谓语并列**；说明隐私保护不足的后果——公司可能未经授权分享健康信息。

#### 长难句7

> Flo Health and FTC settled the matter with a consent order requiring the company to get app users' express affirmative consent before sharing their health information as well as to instruct the third parties to delete the data they had obtained.

##### 句子拆分

主干：**Flo Health and FTC settled the matter with a consent order**
- `requiring the company to get app users' express affirmative consent before sharing their health information as well as to instruct the third parties to delete the data they had obtained`：现在分词作后置定语，修饰order
  - 两个不定式并列：to get…as well as to instruct…
  - `before sharing their health information`：before介词短语
  - `they had obtained`：省略that的定语从句，修饰data

##### 生词

settle the matter：解决此事；consent order：同意令；require：要求；get app users' express affirmative consent：获得应用用户的明确肯定同意；before sharing：在分享之前；health information：健康信息；as well as：以及；instruct：指示；third parties：第三方；delete the data：删除数据；they had obtained：他们已获得的

##### 翻译

Flo Health和FTC以同意令解决了此事，要求公司在分享用户健康信息之前获得应用用户的明确肯定同意，并指示第三方删除他们已获得的数据。

##### 考点

**现在分词后置定语+两个不定式并列+before介词短语+省略that的定语从句**；38题D选项的依据——Flo Health在分享用户健康信息之前被要求获得他们的明确许可（obtain their explicit permission = get app users' express affirmative consent before sharing their health information）。

#### 长难句8

> Section 5 of the FTC Act empowers the FTC to initiate enforcement action against unfair or deceptive acts, meaning the FTC can only act after the fact if a company's privacy practices are misleading or cause unjustified consumer harm.

##### 句子拆分

主干：**Section 5 of the FTC Act empowers the FTC to initiate enforcement action against unfair or deceptive acts**
- `meaning the FTC can only act after the fact if a company's privacy practices are misleading or cause unjustified consumer harm`：现在分词作结果状语
  - `the FTC can only act after the fact if a company's privacy practices are misleading or cause unjustified consumer harm`：省略that的宾语从句，作meaning的宾语
    - `if a company's privacy practices are misleading or cause unjustified consumer harm`：if条件状语从句
      - 两个谓语并列：are misleading or cause

##### 生词

##### 考点

**现在分词结果状语+省略that的宾语从句+if条件状语从句+两个谓语并列**；说明FTC的局限性——只能事后行动，不能事前预防。

**How Colleges Weigh Applicants' Extracurricular Activities 课外活动**

High school students eager to stand out in the college application process often participate in a litiny of extracurricular activities hoping to bolster 提高 their chances of admission to a selective undergraduate institution.

However, college admissions experts say that the quality of a college hopeful's extracurricular activities matters more than the number of activities he or she participates in.

**Sue Rexford**, the director of college guidance at the Charles E. Smith Jewish Day School, says it is not necessary for a student filling out the Common Application to list 10 activities in the application. "No college will expect that a student has a huge laundry list of extracurriculars 一长串课外活动 that they have been passionately involved in each for an extended period of time," Rexford wrote in an email.

Experts say it is tougher to distinguish oneself in a school-affiliated extracurricular activity that is common among high school students than it is to stand out while doing an uncommon activity.

**Sara Harberson**, a college admission consultant, says: "The competition to stand out and make an impact is going to be much stiffer 更激烈的, and so if they're going to do a popular activity, I'd say, be the best at it."

High school students who have an impressive personal project they are working on independently often impress colleges, experts say. "For example, a student with an interest in entrepreneurship 创业 could demonstrate skills and potential by starting a profitable small business," **Olivia Valdes**, the founder of Zen Admissions consulting firm, wrote in an email.

Experts say demonstrating talent in at least one extracurricular activity can help in the college admissions process, especially at top-tier undergraduate institutions.

**Katie Kelley**, admissions counselor at IvyWise admissions consultancy, wrote: "Distinguishing yourself in one focused type of extracurricular activity can be a positive in the admissions process, especially for highly selective institutions, where having top grades and test scores is not enough. Students need to have that quality or hook that will appeal to admissions officers."

Extracurricular activities related to the college major declared on a college application are beneficial, experts suggest. "If you already know your major, having an extracurricular that fits into that major can be a big plus," says **Mayghin Levine**, the manager of educational opportunities with The Cabbage Patch Settlement House.

High school students who have had a strong positive influence on their community through an extracurricular activity may impress a college and win a scholarship, says **Erica Gwyn**, a former math and science magnet program assistant at a public high school.

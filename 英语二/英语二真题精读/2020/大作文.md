# 2020 英二 大作文

## 图表描述

**图表类型**：饼图（Pie Chart）

**图表内容**：某高校学生使用手机阅读的目的调查
- 学习知识（learning knowledge）：59.5%（占比最大）
- 打发时间（killing time）：21.3%
- 获取信息（acquiring information）：17.0%
- 其他（other purposes）：2.2%

## 题目

**Directions:**

Write an essay based on the following chart. In your writing, you should
1) interpret the chart, and
2) give your comments.

You should write about 150 words on the ANSWER SHEET. (15 points)

## 高分范文

The pie chart above clearly illustrates the purposes of mobile phone reading among students at a certain university. As is shown, learning knowledge takes the largest share, accounting for 59.5%, followed by killing time at 21.3% and acquiring information at 17.0%, while other purposes make up merely 2.2%.

What are the driving forces behind this phenomenon? Several factors contribute to this trend, but generally speaking, they may boil down to the following two aspects. Initially, with the rapid development of mobile technology, smartphones have become an indispensable tool for college students to acquire knowledge conveniently and efficiently. A wide range of educational apps and e-reading platforms enable students to study anytime and anywhere. Furthermore, the intense competition in modern society forces students to make full use of fragmented time, and mobile reading perfectly meets this need. However, it is worth noting that over 20% of students read merely to kill time, which indicates a certain lack of purpose in their reading habits.

In conclusion, while it is encouraging that the majority of students use mobile phones for learning, we should also guide them to make more purposeful and efficient use of their reading time.

## 范文翻译

上方的饼图清晰地展示了某高校学生使用手机阅读的目的。如图所示，学习知识占比最大，达59.5%，其次是打发时间占21.3%和获取信息占17.0%，而其他目的仅占2.2%。

这一现象背后的驱动力是什么？有几个因素促成了这一趋势，但总的来说，可以归结为以下两个方面。首先，随着移动技术的快速发展，智能手机已成为大学生便捷高效地获取知识的不可或缺的工具。各种教育类应用和电子阅读平台使学生能够随时随地学习。此外，现代社会的激烈竞争迫使学生充分利用碎片化时间，而手机阅读恰好满足了这一需求。然而，值得注意的是，超过20%的学生阅读仅仅是为了打发时间，这表明他们的阅读习惯在一定程度上缺乏目的性。

总之，虽然大多数学生使用手机进行学习令人欣慰，但我们也应该引导他们更有目的、更高效地利用阅读时间。

## 重点句型与表达

1. **The pie chart above clearly illustrates...** —— 饼图开头万能句型，引出图表主题
2. **As is shown, ... takes the largest share, accounting for X%, followed by... at Y%** —— 描述饼图数据的经典句式（最大项+排序）
3. **...make up merely X%** —— "仅占X%"，用于描述最小项
4. **What are the driving forces behind this phenomenon?** —— 过渡句，引出原因分析
5. **Several factors contribute to this trend, but generally speaking, they may boil down to...** —— 分析原因的万能过渡句（boil down to=归结为）
6. **...have become an indispensable tool for...** —— "已成为……不可或缺的工具"，用于论述重要性
7. **Furthermore, ... forces students to make full use of fragmented time** —— "此外，……迫使学生充分利用碎片化时间"
8. **In conclusion, while it is encouraging that..., we should also...** —— 结尾段万能句式（肯定+建议转折）

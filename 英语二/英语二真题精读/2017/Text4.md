Though often viewed as a problem for western states, the growing frequency 频率 of wildfires 野火 is a national concern 国家关注 because of its impact on federal tax dollars 联邦税收, Professor Moritz and others say.

In 2015, the US Forest Service 美国林务局 for the first time spent more than half of its $5.5 billion annual budget 年度预算 fighting fires—nearly double the percentage it spent on such efforts 20 years ago. In effect, fewer federal funds 联邦资金 today are going towards the agency's other work—such as forest conservation 森林保护, watershed 流域 and cultural resources management, and infrastructure upkeep 基础设施维护—that affect the lives of all Americans.

Another nationwide concern is whether public funds from other agencies, such as the Department of Housing and Urban Development 住房和城市发展部, are going into construction in fire-prone 易发生火灾的 districts. As Moritz puts it, how often are federal dollars building homes that are likely to be lost to a wildfire?

"It's already a huge problem from a public expenditure 公共支出 perspective for the whole country," he says. "We need to take a magnifying glass 放大镜 to that. Like, 'Wait a minute, is this OK?' Do we want instead to redirect 重新分配 those funds to concentrate on lower-hazard 低风险 parts of the landscape?"

Such a pivot 转变 would require a corresponding shift 相应转变 in the way US society today views fire, researchers say.

For one thing, conversations about wildfires need to be more inclusive 包容的. Over the past decade, the focus has been on climate change—how the warming of the Earth from greenhouse gases 温室气体 (including human carbon emissions 碳排放) is leading to conditions that exacerbate 加剧 fires.

While climate is a key element, Moritz says, it shouldn't come at the expense of 以…为代价 the rest of the equation 等式.

"The human systems and the landscapes we live on are linked, and the interactions go both ways," he says. Failing to recognize that, he notes, leads to "an overly simplified view of what the solutions might be. Our perception 看法 of the problem and perception of what the solution is [becomes] very limited."

At the same time, people continue to treat fire as an event that needs to be wholly controlled 完全控制 and unleashed 释放 only out of necessity, says Professor Balch at the University of Colorado. But acknowledging 承认 fire's inevitable presence 不可避免的存在 in human life is an attitude crucial 关键的 to developing the laws, policies, and practices that make it as safe as possible, she says.

"We've disconnected ourselves from living with fire," Balch says. "It is really important to understand and try and tease out 梳理出 what is the human connection [with fire] today."

[A] exhausted unprecedented management efforts
[B] consumed a record-high percentage of budget
[C] severely damaged the ecology of western states

37. Moritz calls for the use of "a magnifying glass" to ______.
[A] raise more funds for fire-prone areas
[B] avoid the redirection of federal money
[C] find wildfire-free parts of the landscape
[D] guarantee safer spending of public funds

38. While admitting that climate is a key element, Moritz notes that ______.
[A] public debates have not settled yet
[B] fire-fighting conditions are improving
[C] other factors should not be overlooked
[D] a shift in the view of fire has taken place

39. The overly simplified view Moritz mentions is a result of failing to ______.
[A] discover the fundamental makeup of nature
[B] explore the mechanism of the human systems
[C] maximize the role of landscape in human life
[D] understand the interrelations of man and nature

40. Professor Balch points out that fire is something man should ______.
[A] do away with
[B] come to terms with
[C] pay a price for
[D] keep away from

## 长难句1

> Though often viewed as a problem for western states, the growing frequency of wildfires is a national concern because of its impact on federal tax dollars, Professor Moritz and others say.

### 句子拆分

主干：**the growing frequency of wildfires is a national concern**
- `Though often viewed as a problem for western states`：though让步状语从句省略（=though it is often viewed…）
- `because of its impact on federal tax dollars`：because of介词短语作原因状语
- `Professor Moritz and others say`：倒装引语

### 生词

though：尽管；be viewed as：被视为；western states：西部各州；growing frequency：日益频繁；wildfire：野火；national concern：国家关注；because of：因为；impact on：对…的影响；federal tax dollars：联邦税收

### 翻译

尽管野火常被视为西部各州的问题，但野火日益频繁是一个国家关注的问题，因为它影响联邦税收，莫里茨教授和其他人说。

### 考点

**though省略让步状语从句+because of介词短语+倒装引语**；开篇点题——野火成为国家关注的原因是影响联邦税收，36题B选项的背景。

## 长难句2

> In 2015, the US Forest Service for the first time spent more than half of its $5.5 billion annual budget fighting fires—nearly double the percentage it spent on such efforts 20 years ago.

### 句子拆分

主干：**the US Forest Service spent more than half of its $5.5 billion annual budget fighting fires**
- `In 2015`：时间状语
- `for the first time`：状语
- 破折号后：`nearly double the percentage it spent on such efforts 20 years ago`
  - 同位语，解释more than half
  - `it spent on such efforts 20 years ago`：省略that的定语从句，修饰percentage

### 生词

US Forest Service：美国林务局；for the first time：首次；annual budget：年度预算；fight fires：灭火；nearly double：几乎两倍；percentage：百分比；such efforts：此类努力

### 翻译

2015年，美国林务局首次将其55亿美元年度预算的一半以上用于灭火——几乎是20年前此类支出百分比的两倍。

### 考点

**spend…doing结构+破折号同位语+省略that的定语从句**；36题B选项的依据——2015年野火消耗了创纪录高比例的预算（more than half for the first time = record-high percentage of budget）。

## 36. More frequent wildfires have become a national concern because in 2015 they ______.

[A] exhausted unprecedented management efforts [B] consumed a record-high percentage of budget [C] severely damaged the ecology of western states [D] caused a huge rise of infrastructure expenditure

✅答案：**B** 定位：第二段。`the US Forest Service for the first time spent more than half of its $5.5 billion annual budget fighting fires—nearly double the percentage it spent on such efforts 20 years ago`（美国林务局首次将55亿美元年度预算的一半以上用于灭火——几乎是20年前的两倍）。首次超过一半=创纪录高比例。

- A：错误。exhausted unprecedented management efforts（耗尽了前所未有的管理努力）无中生有。
- B：正确。消耗了创纪录高比例的预算。for the first time spent more than half = consumed a record-high percentage of budget。
- C：错误。severely damaged the ecology of western states（严重破坏了西部各州的生态）以偏概全。原文说常被视为西部各州的问题，但成为国家关注的原因是联邦预算，不是生态破坏。
- D：错误。caused a huge rise of infrastructure expenditure（导致基础设施支出大幅增加）与原文相反。原文说更少资金用于基础设施维护（infrastructure upkeep），不是支出增加。

## 37. Moritz calls for the use of "a magnifying glass" to ______.

[A] raise more funds for fire-prone areas [B] avoid the redirection of federal money [C] find wildfire-free parts of the landscape [D] guarantee safer spending of public funds

- A：错误。raise more funds for fire-prone areas（为火灾高发区筹集更多资金）与原文相反。原文说要重新分配资金到低风险地区，不是为火灾高发区筹款。

- C：错误。find wildfire-free parts of the landscape（找到无野火的地区）偷换概念。原文说lower-hazard parts（低风险地区），不是wildfire-free（无野火）。
- D：正确。确保更安全的公共资金支出。take a magnifying glass to check if spending is OK + redirect to lower-hazard = guarantee safer spending of public funds。

## 40. Professor Balch points out that fire is something man should ______.

[A] do away with [B] come to terms with [C] pay a price for [D] keep away from

✅答案：**B** 定位：第九、十段。`acknowledging fire's inevitable presence in human life is an attitude crucial to developing the laws, policies, and practices that make it as safe as possible`（承认火灾在人类生活中不可避免的存在，是制定使火灾尽可能安全的法律、政策和实践的关键态度）+ `We've disconnected ourselves from living with fire`（我们已经脱离了与火共存）。说明人类应该接受火灾的存在，与火共存。come to terms with = 接受、适应。

- A：错误。do away with（废除、消灭）与原文相反。原文说承认火灾不可避免的存在，不是消灭。
- B：正确。接受、适应。acknowledging fire's inevitable presence + living with fire = come to terms with fire。
- C：错误。pay a price for（为…付出代价）无中生有。
- D：错误。keep away from（远离）与原文相反。原文说living with fire（与火共存），不是远离。

### 本篇命题规律小结（英二阅读）

1. 环保/政策类文章：关注**问题提出（野火成国家关注）→具体表现（预算消耗）→问题2（资金投入火灾区）→解决方案（重新分配资金）→观念转变（不只看气候，理解人与自然关系）→接受火灾存在**的结构

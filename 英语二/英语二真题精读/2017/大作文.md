# 2017 英二 大作文

## 图表描述

折线/柱状图（Line/Bar Chart）：2013-2015年我国博物馆数量及参观人数变化

两条数据线：
- 博物馆数量（家）：2013年约4165家 → 2014年约4692家 → 2015年约5132家（持续增长）
- 参观人数（百万人次）：2013年约637.8百万人次 → 2014年约717.9百万人次 → 2015年约781.1百万人次（持续增长，增幅显著）

## 题目

**Directions:**

Write an essay based on the following chart. In your writing, you should

1) interpret the chart, and
2) give your comments.

You should write about 150 words on the ANSWER SHEET. (15 points)

## 高分范文

The chart above clearly illustrates the changes in the number of museums and their visitors in China from 2013 to 2015. As is shown, both the number of museums and the number of visitors experienced steady growth during this period. The number of museums increased from approximately 4,165 in 2013 to about 5,132 in 2015. Meanwhile, the number of visitors rose remarkably from 637.8 million to 781.1 million, an increase of nearly 143 million.

Several factors may account for this upward trend. First and foremost, with the rapid economic development and the improvement of living standards, people have growing spiritual and cultural needs, which has led to an increasing enthusiasm for museum visits. Moreover, the government has invested heavily in cultural infrastructure, resulting in the construction of more museums across the country. Additionally, museums have made efforts to modernize their exhibitions and offer free admission, making them more accessible and appealing to the public.

From my perspective, this trend is highly encouraging, as it reflects the public's growing appreciation for culture and knowledge. It is hoped that more museums will be built and innovative exhibitions will be organized to further enrich people's cultural lives.

## 范文翻译

上面的图表清楚地展示了2013年至2015年中国博物馆数量和参观人数的变化。如图所示，博物馆数量和参观人数在此期间均稳步增长。博物馆数量从2013年的约4165家增长到2015年的约5132家。同时，参观人数从6.378亿大幅增长到7.811亿，增加了近1.43亿。

有几个因素可以解释这一上升趋势。首先，随着经济快速发展和生活水平提高，人们的精神文化需求不断增长，这导致了对参观博物馆的热情日益高涨。其次，政府在文化基础设施上投入大量资金，使得全国各地建设了更多博物馆。此外，博物馆努力实现展览现代化并免费开放，使其对公众更加便利和有吸引力。

在我看来，这一趋势非常令人鼓舞，因为它反映了公众对文化和知识日益增长的重视。希望未来能建设更多博物馆，组织更多创新展览，进一步丰富人们的文化生活。

## 重点句型与表达

1. **The chart above clearly illustrates the changes in...from...to...**（上面的图表清楚地展示了……从……到……的变化）——折线/柱状图描述开头句型
2. **As is shown, both...experienced steady growth during this period.**（如图所示，两者在此期间均稳步增长）——总体趋势描述句型
3. **The number of...increased from...in 2013 to...in 2015.**（……的数量从2013年的……增长到2015年的……）——具体数据描述
4. **Meanwhile, the number of...rose remarkably from...to..., an increase of...**（同时，……的数量从……大幅增长到……，增加了……）——描述另一数据线+同位语补充增幅
5. **Several factors may account for this upward trend.**（有几个因素可以解释这一上升趋势）——原因分析段开头句型
6. **First and foremost, with the rapid economic development..., people have growing...needs**（首先，随着经济快速发展……，人们有日益增长的……需求）——with短语表背景
7. **Moreover, the government has invested heavily in..., resulting in...**（其次，政府在……投入大量资金，导致……）——result in表因果
8. **From my perspective, this trend is highly encouraging, as it reflects...**（在我看来，这一趋势非常令人鼓舞，因为它反映了……）——评论段开头+as表原因

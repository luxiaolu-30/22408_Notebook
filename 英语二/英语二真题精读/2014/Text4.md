When the government talks about infrastructure 基础设施 contributing to the economy the focus is usually on roads, railways, broadband 宽带 and energy. Housing is seldom mentioned.

Why is that? To some extent 在某种程度上 the housing sector 住房部门 must shoulder the blame 承担责任. We have not been good at communicating the real value that housing can contribute to economic growth. Then there is the scale 规模 of the typical housing project. It is hard to shove 推 for attention among multibillion-pound 数十亿英镑的 infrastructure projects, so it is inevitable 不可避免的 that the attention is focused elsewhere. But perhaps the most significant reason is that the issue has always been so politically charged 政治敏感的.

Nevertheless 然而, the affordable housing 经济适用房 situation is desperate 绝望的. Waiting lists 等候名单 increase all the time and we are simply not building enough new homes.

The comprehensive spending review 综合支出审查 offers an opportunity for the government to help rectify 纠正 this. It needs to put historical prejudices 历史偏见 to one side and take some steps to address our urgent housing need.

There are some indications 迹象 that it is preparing to do just that. The communities minister, Don Foster, has hinted 暗示 that George Osborne, Chancellor of the Exchequer 财政大臣, may introduce more flexibility 灵活性 to the local authority 地方政府 borrowing cap 借款上限, which has been put in place by the previous Labour government. This means that local councils 地方议会 will be able to borrow more money to build more social housing 社会住房. The cap was introduced by the previous Labour government to limit local authority borrowing and debt.

But there are some obvious concerns. The first is that local councils may borrow too much money and get into debt. The second is that local councils may not be able to pay back the money they borrow. There is also a concern that local councils may not have the necessary skills and experience to manage large-scale housing projects.

Ministers should also look at creating greater certainty 确定性 in the rental environment 租赁环境, which would have a significant impact on the ability of registered providers 注册供应商 to fund new developments 新开发项目 from revenues 收入.

But it is not just down to 取决于 the government. While these measures would be welcome in the short term, we must face up to 面对 the fact that the existing £4.5bn programme of grants 拨款 to fund new affordable housing, set to expire 到期 in 2015, is unlikely to be extended beyond then. The Labour party has recently announced that it will retain 保留 a large part of the coalition's 联合政府的 spending plans if it returns to power, including the current spending limits on housing. The housing sector needs to accept that it is very unlikely to get ever-more public funding 公共资金. We need to adjust to this changing climate 环境.

## 题目（36–40）

36. The author believes that the housing sector ______.
[A] has attracted much attention
[B] has failed to communicate its real value
[C] should not be the sole focus of infrastructure
[D] should not be blamed for the current economic situation

37. It can be learned that affordable housing has ______.
[A] increased its home supply
[B] offered spending opportunities
[C] suffered government biases
[D] disappointed the government

38. According to Paragraph 5, George Osborne may ______.
[A] allow greater government debt for housing
[B] stop local authorities from building homes
[C] prepare to reduce housing stock debt
[D] release a lifted GDP growth forecast

39. It can be inferred that a stable rental environment would ______.
[A] lower the costs of registered providers
[B] lessen the impact of government interference
[C] contribute to funding new developments
[D] relieve the ministers of responsibilities

40. The author believes that after 2015, the government may ______.
[A] implement more policies to support housing
[B] review the need for large-scale grants
[C] renew the affordable housing grants programme
[D] stop generous funding to the housing sector

### 参考答案

36.B 37.C 38.A 39.C 40.D

# 2014英二 Text4 长难句精读（原文+拆分+语法+翻译+考点）

## 长难句1

> When the government talks about infrastructure contributing to the economy the focus is usually on roads, railways, broadband and energy.

### 句子拆分

主干：**the focus is usually on roads, railways, broadband and energy**
- `When the government talks about infrastructure contributing to the economy`：when时间状语从句
  - `contributing to the economy`：现在分词作后置定语，修饰infrastructure

### 生词

infrastructure：基础设施；contribute to：贡献、促进；broadband：宽带

### 翻译

当政府谈论促进经济的基础设施时，焦点通常在公路、铁路、宽带和能源上。

### 考点

**when时间状语从句+现在分词后置定语**；开篇点题——住房在基础设施讨论中被忽视，36题背景。

---

## 长难句2

> To some extent the housing sector must shoulder the blame.

### 句子拆分

主干：**the housing sector must shoulder the blame**
- `To some extent`：状语

### 生词

to some extent：在某种程度上；housing sector：住房部门；shoulder the blame：承担责任

### 翻译

在某种程度上，住房部门必须承担责任。

### 考点

**shoulder the blame** 固定搭配；36题D选项干扰来源——原文说住房部门应承担责任，D选项说不应被指责，与原文相反。

## 长难句3

> We have not been good at communicating the real value that housing can contribute to economic growth.

### 句子拆分

主干：**We have not been good at communicating the real value**
- `that housing can contribute to economic growth`：that定语从句，修饰value

### 生词

be good at：擅长；communicate：传达、沟通；real value：真实价值；contribute to：促进；economic growth：经济增长

### 翻译

我们一直不善于传达住房能够促进经济增长的真实价值。

### 考点

**be good at doing+that定语从句**；36题B选项定位句——住房部门未能传达其真实价值。

## 长难句4

> It is hard to shove for attention among multibillion-pound infrastructure projects, so it is inevitable that the attention is focused elsewhere.

### 句子拆分

并列句，so连接
分句1主干：**It is hard to shove for attention among multibillion-pound infrastructure projects**
- it是形式主语，真正主语是to shove for attention
分句2主干：**it is inevitable that the attention is focused elsewhere**
- it是形式主语，真正主语是that引导的主语从句

### 生词

shove for attention：挤着争取关注；multibillion-pound：数十亿英镑的；inevitable：不可避免的；focus elsewhere：聚焦在别处

### 翻译

在数十亿英镑的基础设施项目中，住房项目很难挤着争取关注，因此注意力不可避免地集中在别处。

### 考点

**it形式主语+so因果并列句**；住房被忽视的原因之一——项目规模小，争不过大型基础设施项目。

## 长难句5

> The comprehensive spending review offers an opportunity for the government to help rectify this.

### 句子拆分

主干：**The comprehensive spending review offers an opportunity**
- `for the government to help rectify this`：for sb to do sth复合结构，作后置定语修饰opportunity

### 生词

comprehensive spending review：综合支出审查；offer an opportunity：提供机会；rectify：纠正、改正

### 翻译

综合支出审查为政府提供了一个帮助纠正这一问题的机会。

### 考点

**for sb to do sth复合结构**作定语；37题C选项的逻辑铺垫——政府有历史偏见，需要纠正。

## 长难句6

> It needs to put historical prejudices to one side and take some steps to address our urgent housing need.

### 句子拆分

主干：**It needs to put historical prejudices to one side and take some steps**
- 两个并列不定式：to put和(to) take
- `to address our urgent housing need`：不定式作目的状语

### 生词

put … to one side：把…搁置一边；historical prejudice：历史偏见；take steps：采取措施；address：解决；urgent housing need：迫切的住房需求

### 翻译

它需要把历史偏见搁置一边，采取一些措施来解决我们迫切的住房需求。

### 考点

**并列不定式+目的状语**；37题C选项依据——政府对住房有历史偏见（historical prejudices），经济适用房遭受政府偏见。

## 长难句7

> The communities minister, Don Foster, has hinted that George Osborne, Chancellor of the Exchequer, may introduce more flexibility to the local authority borrowing cap, which has been put in place by the previous Labour government.

### 句子拆分

主干：**The communities minister has hinted that …**
- `Don Foster`：同位语，解释minister
- that引导宾语从句，从句主干：**George Osborne may introduce more flexibility to the local authority borrowing cap**
  - `Chancellor of the Exchequer`：同位语，解释George Osborne
  - `which has been put in place by the previous Labour government`：which非限制性定语从句，修饰borrowing cap

### 生词

hint：暗示；Chancellor of the Exchequer：财政大臣；introduce more flexibility to：给…引入更多灵活性；local authority：地方政府；borrowing cap：借款上限；put in place：实施；previous：之前的；Labour government：工党政府

### 翻译

社区大臣唐·福斯特暗示，财政大臣乔治·奥斯本可能会给地方政府借款上限引入更多灵活性，该上限是由前工党政府实施的。

### 考点

**多层同位语+that宾语从句+which非限制性定语从句**；38题A选项定位句——奥斯本可能允许地方政府借更多钱=允许更大的政府住房债务。

## 长难句8

> Ministers should also look at creating greater certainty in the rental environment, which would have a significant impact on the ability of registered providers to fund new developments from revenues.

### 句子拆分

主干：**Ministers should also look at creating greater certainty in the rental environment**
- `which would have a significant impact on the ability of registered providers to fund new developments from revenues`：which非限制性定语从句，修饰前面整句话
  - `to fund new developments from revenues`：不定式作后置定语，修饰ability

### 生词

look at：考虑、着眼于；certainty：确定性；rental environment：租赁环境；have a significant impact on：对…有重大影响；registered provider：注册供应商；fund：资助；new development：新开发项目；revenue：收入

### 翻译

部长们还应该考虑在租赁环境中创造更大的确定性，这将对注册供应商用收入资助新开发项目的能力产生重大影响。

### 考点

**which非限制性定语从句（修饰整句话）+不定式后置定语**；39题C选项定位句——稳定租赁环境有助于资助新开发项目。

## 长难句9

> While these measures would be welcome in the short term, we must face up to the fact that the existing £4.5bn programme of grants to fund new affordable housing, set to expire in 2015, is unlikely to be extended beyond then.

### 句子拆分

主干：**we must face up to the fact**
- `While these measures would be welcome in the short term`：while让步状语从句
- `that the existing £4.5bn programme of grants … is unlikely to be extended beyond then`：that同位语从句，解释fact
  - 从句主干：`the existing £4.5bn programme of grants is unlikely to be extended beyond then`
  - `to fund new affordable housing`：不定式作后置定语，修饰grants
  - `set to expire in 2015`：过去分词作后置定语，修饰programme

### 生词

while：尽管；in the short term：短期来看；face up to：面对；existing：现有的；programme of grants：拨款计划；affordable housing：经济适用房；set to expire：定于到期；be unlikely to：不太可能；extend：延长；beyond then：超过那时

### 翻译

尽管这些措施在短期内会受欢迎，但我们必须面对这样一个事实：现有的用于资助新经济适用房的45亿英镑拨款计划定于2015年到期，不太可能在那之后延长。

### 考点

**while让步状语从句+that同位语从句+过去分词后置定语**；40题D选项依据——2015年后拨款不太可能延长=停止对住房部门的慷慨资助。

# 2014 英二 Text4 36–40｜题目+选项+逐选项解析

> 原文主题：英国住房在基础设施讨论中被忽视，经济适用房形势严峻，政府可能调整借款上限，但2015年后公共资金可能减少

## 36. The author believes that the housing sector ______.

[A] has attracted much attention [B] has failed to communicate its real value [C] should not be the sole focus of infrastructure [D] should not be blamed for the current economic situation

✅答案：**B** 定位：第二段。`We have not been good at communicating the real value that housing can contribute to economic growth`（我们不善于传达住房能够促进经济增长的真实价值）。

- A：错误。has attracted much attention（吸引了大量关注）与原文相反。第一段说住房seldom mentioned（很少被提及）。
- B：正确。未能传达其真实价值。not been good at communicating the real value = failed to communicate its real value。
- C：错误。should not be the sole focus of infrastructure（不应是基础设施的唯一焦点）无中生有。
- D：错误。should not be blamed（不应被指责）与原文相反。原文说"the housing sector must shoulder the blame"（住房部门必须承担责任）。

## 37. It can be learned that affordable housing has ______.

[A] increased its home supply [B] offered spending opportunities [C] suffered government biases [D] disappointed the government

✅答案：**C** 推理题。定位：第三、四段。经济适用房形势desperate（绝望），等候名单增加，新房不够。第四段说政府需要"put historical prejudices to one side"（把历史偏见搁置一边），说明政府对住房有偏见，经济适用房遭受了政府偏见。

- A：错误。increased its home supply（增加了住房供应）与原文相反。原文说"not building enough new homes"（建的新房不够）。
- B：错误。offered spending opportunities（提供了支出机会）无中生有。
- C：正确。遭受了政府偏见。政府需要put historical prejudices to one side→说明存在偏见，经济适用房受其影响。
- D：错误。disappointed the government（让政府失望）无中生有。是政府让住房部门失望，不是反过来。

## 38. According to Paragraph 5, George Osborne may ______.

[A] allow greater government debt for housing [B] stop local authorities from building homes [C] prepare to reduce housing stock debt [D] release a lifted GDP growth forecast

✅答案：**A** 定位：第五段。`George Osborne … may introduce more flexibility to the local authority borrowing cap`（奥斯本可能给地方政府借款上限引入更多灵活性）+ `local councils will be able to borrow more money to build more social housing`（地方议会将能借更多钱建更多社会住房）。即允许更大的政府住房债务。

- A：正确。允许更大的政府住房债务。more flexibility to borrowing cap + borrow more money = allow greater government debt for housing。
- B：错误。stop local authorities from building homes（阻止地方政府建房）与原文相反。地方政府将能借更多钱建更多房。
- C：错误。prepare to reduce housing stock debt（准备减少住房存量债务）与原文相反。是增加借款，不是减少债务。
- D：错误。release a lifted GDP growth forecast（发布上调的GDP增长预测）无中生有。

## 39. It can be inferred that a stable rental environment would ______.

[A] lower the costs of registered providers [B] lessen the impact of government interference [C] contribute to funding new developments [D] relieve the ministers of responsibilities

✅答案：**C** 推理题。定位：第七段。`creating greater certainty in the rental environment, which would have a significant impact on the ability of registered providers to fund new developments from revenues`（在租赁环境中创造更大确定性，将对注册供应商用收入资助新开发项目的能力产生重大影响）。稳定租赁环境有助于资助新开发项目。

- A：错误。lower the costs of registered providers（降低注册供应商成本）偷换概念。原文说影响fund new developments的ability，不是降低成本。
- B：错误。lessen the impact of government interference（减少政府干预的影响）无中生有。
- C：正确。有助于资助新开发项目。greater certainty in rental environment → significant impact on ability to fund new developments = contribute to funding new developments。
- D：错误。relieve the ministers of responsibilities（减轻部长们的责任）与原文相反。原文说Ministers should also look at creating greater certainty（部长们应该考虑创造确定性），是增加责任，不是减轻。

## 40. The author believes that after 2015, the government may ______.

[A] implement more policies to support housing [B] review the need for large-scale grants [C] renew the affordable housing grants programme [D] stop generous funding to the housing sector

✅答案：**D** 定位：最后一段。`the existing £4.5bn programme of grants … set to expire in 2015, is unlikely to be extended beyond then`（45亿英镑拨款计划定于2015年到期，不太可能延长）+ `The housing sector needs to accept that it is very unlikely to get ever-more public funding`（住房部门需要接受不太可能获得越来越多的公共资金）。2015年后政府可能停止对住房部门的慷慨资助。

- A：错误。implement more policies to support housing（实施更多政策支持住房）与原文相反。2015年后拨款不延长，资金减少。
- B：错误。review the need for large-scale grants（审查大规模拨款的必要性）无中生有。原文说拨款不延长，不是审查必要性。
- C：错误。renew the affordable housing grants programme（更新经济适用房拨款计划）与原文相反。原文说unlikely to be extended（不太可能延长）。
- D：正确。停止对住房部门的慷慨资助。grants expire + unlikely to be extended + unlikely to get ever-more public funding = stop generous funding。

### 本篇命题规律小结（英二阅读）

1. 政策/经济类文章：关注**问题→原因→政府措施→担忧→未来趋势**的结构
2. 细节题：定位到**人物+hinted/may**，答案是政策变化的具体内容
3. 推理题：基于**作者建议**（put historical prejudices to one side）反推存在的问题
4. 因果题：定位到**which would have a significant impact on**，答案是影响的结果
5. 常见干扰：与原文相反（stop building vs build more, renew grants vs unlikely to extend）、偷换概念（lower costs vs fund new developments）

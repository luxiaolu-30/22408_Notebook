The concept of man versus machine 人机对抗 is at least as old as the industrial revolution, but this phenomenon tends to be most acutely 强烈地 felt during economic downturns 经济下滑 and fragile 脆弱的 recoveries. And yet, it would be a mistake to think we are right now simply experiencing the painful side of a boom and bust cycle 繁荣与萧条周期. Certain jobs have gone away for good 永远消失, outmoded 过时 by machines. Since technology has such an insatiable 贪得无厌的 appetite for eating up 吞噬 human jobs, this phenomenon will continue to restructure 重组 our economy in ways we can't immediately foresee.

When there is exponential 指数级的 improvement in the price and performance of technology, jobs that were once thought to be immune from 免于 automation suddenly become threatened. This argument has attracted a lot of attention, via the success of the book Race Against the Machine, by Erik Brynjolfsson and Andrew McAfee, who both hail from 来自 MIT's Center for Digital Business.

This is a powerful argument, and a scary one. And yet, John Hagel, author of The Power of Pull and other books, says Brynjolfsson and McAfee miss the reason why these jobs are so vulnerable 脆弱的 to technology in the first place 首先.

Hagel says we have designed jobs in the U.S. that tend to be "tightly scripted 严格照本宣科的" and "highly standardized 高度标准化的" ones that leave no room for 没有空间 "individual initiative 个人主动性 or creativity." In short, these are the types of jobs that machines can perform much better at than human beings. That is how we have put a giant target sign 巨大靶心 on the backs of American workers, Hagel says.

It's time to reinvent 彻底改造 the formula 模式 for how work is conducted, since we are still relying on a very 20th century notion of work, Hagel says. In our rapidly changing economy, we more than ever 比以往更 need people in the workplace who can take initiative and exercise their imagination "to respond to unexpected events." That's not something machines are good at. They are designed to perform very predictable 可预测的 activities.

As Hagel notes, Brynjolfsson and McAfee indeed touched on 提及 this point in their book. We need to reframe 重新定义 race against the machine as race with the machine. In other words, we need to look at the ways in which machines can augment 增强 human labor rather than replace it. So then the problem is not really about technology, but rather, "how do we innovate our institutions 制度 and our work practices 工作实践?"

# 2014英二 Text3 长难句精读（原文+拆分+语法+翻译+考点）

## 长难句1

> The concept of man versus machine is at least as old as the industrial revolution, but this phenomenon tends to be most acutely felt during economic downturns and fragile recoveries.

### 句子拆分

并列句，but连接
分句1主干：**The concept of man versus machine is at least as old as the industrial revolution**
- `as old as`：同级比较
分句2主干：**this phenomenon tends to be most acutely felt**
- `during economic downturns and fragile recoveries`：时间状语

### 生词

versus：对抗；industrial revolution：工业革命；phenomenon：现象；tend to：往往；acutely：强烈地；economic downturn：经济下滑；fragile：脆弱的；recovery：复苏

### 翻译

人机对抗的概念至少和工业革命一样古老，但这种现象往往在经济下滑和脆弱复苏期间被最强烈地感受到。

### 考点

**as old as同级比较**；被动语态be felt；31题B选项依据——经济下滑时人机对抗感受最强烈=突出机器对人类工作的威胁。

## 长难句2

> Since technology has such an insatiable appetite for eating up human jobs, this phenomenon will continue to restructure our economy in ways we can't immediately foresee.

### 句子拆分

主干：**this phenomenon will continue to restructure our economy**
- `Since technology has such an insatiable appetite for eating up human jobs`：since原因状语从句
  - `such … that …`结构省略that（such an insatiable appetite）
- `in ways we can't immediately foresee`：方式状语
  - `we can't immediately foresee`：省略that/in which的定语从句，修饰ways

### 生词

since：因为；insatiable：贪得无厌的；appetite：胃口、欲望；eat up：吞噬；restructure：重组；in ways：以…方式；foresee：预见

### 翻译

由于技术对吞噬人类工作有着如此贪得无厌的胃口，这种现象将继续以我们无法立即预见的方式重组我们的经济。

### 考点

**since原因状语从句+省略that的定语从句**；比喻用法insatiable appetite；全文核心观点——技术将持续吞噬工作、重组经济。

## 长难句5

> Hagel says we have designed jobs in the U.S. that tend to be "tightly scripted" and "highly standardized" ones that leave no room for "individual initiative or creativity."

### 句子拆分

主干：**Hagel says …**
- 省略that的宾语从句：`we have designed jobs in the U.S.`
- `that tend to be "tightly scripted" and "highly standardized" ones`：that定语从句，修饰jobs
- `that leave no room for "individual initiative or creativity"`：that定语从句，修饰ones（指代jobs）

### 生词

design：设计；tightly scripted：严格照本宣科的；highly standardized：高度标准化的；leave no room for：没有空间；individual initiative：个人主动性；creativity：创造力

### 考点

**宾语从句+两个that定语从句嵌套**；33题D选项定位句——美国的工作设计违背人类创造力（没有给创造力留空间）。

# 2014 英二 Text3 31–35｜题目+选项+逐选项解析

> 原文主题：人机竞赛中，美国工作设计高度标准化使工人成为机器替代目标，应转变为与机器合作并革新工作方式

## 31. According to the first paragraph, economic downturns would ______.

[A] ease the competition of man vs. machine [B] highlight machines' threat to human jobs [C] provoke a painful technological revolution [D] outmode our current economic structure

✅答案：**B** 定位：第一段。`this phenomenon tends to be most acutely felt during economic downturns`（人机对抗在经济下滑时被最强烈地感受到）+ `Certain jobs have gone away for good, outmoded by machines`（某些工作永远消失，被机器淘汰）。经济下滑时突出机器对人类工作的威胁。

- A：错误。ease the competition（缓解竞争）与原文相反。经济下滑时人机对抗感受更强烈，不是缓解。
- B：正确。突出机器对人类工作的威胁。most acutely felt + jobs outmoded by machines = highlight machines' threat。
- C：错误。provoke a painful technological revolution（引发痛苦的技术革命）偷换概念。原文说经历繁荣萧条周期的痛苦一面，不是引发技术革命。
- D：错误。outmode our current economic structure（淘汰当前经济结构）偷换概念。原文说工作被淘汰，技术重组经济，不是经济结构被淘汰。

## 32. The authors of Race Against the Machine argue that ______.

[A] technology is diminishing man's job opportunities [B] automation is accelerating technological development [C] certain jobs will remain intact after automation [D] man will finally win the race against machine

✅答案：**A** 定位：第二段。`jobs that were once thought to be immune from automation suddenly become threatened`（曾经被认为免于自动化的工作突然受到威胁）。这本书的论点是技术在减少人类工作机会。

- A：正确。技术正在减少人类工作机会。jobs become threatened = diminishing job opportunities。
- B：错误。automation is accelerating technological development（自动化加速技术发展）因果倒置。是技术发展导致自动化，不是自动化加速技术发展。
- C：错误。certain jobs will remain intact（某些工作将保持不变）与原文相反。曾经被认为安全的工作也受到威胁。
- D：错误。man will finally win（人类最终会赢）无中生有。书名是Race Against the Machine，没说人类会赢。

## 33. Hagel argues that jobs in the U.S. are often ______.

[A] performed by innovative minds [B] scripted with an individual style [C] standardized without a clear target [D] designed against human creativity

✅答案：**D** 定位：第四段。`we have designed jobs in the U.S. that tend to be "tightly scripted" and "highly standardized" ones that leave no room for "individual initiative or creativity"`（美国的工作严格照本宣科、高度标准化，没有给个人主动性或创造力留空间）。即工作设计违背人类创造力。

- A：错误。performed by innovative minds（由创新头脑执行）与原文相反。工作没有给创造力留空间。
- B：错误。scripted with an individual style（照本宣科但有个人风格）与原文相反。tightly scripted意味着没有个人风格。
- C：错误。standardized without a clear target（标准化但没有明确目标）偷换概念。原文说"put a giant target sign on the backs"（有明确靶心），不是没有目标。
- D：正确。设计违背人类创造力。leave no room for creativity = designed against human creativity。

## 34. According to the last paragraph, Brynjolfsson and McAfee discussed ______.

[A] the predictability of machine behavior in practice [B] the formula for how work is conducted efficiently [C] the ways machines replace human labor in modern times [D] the necessity of human involvement in the workplace

✅答案：**D** 定位：最后一段。`As Hagel notes, Brynjolfsson and McAfee indeed touched on this point in their book`，this point指第五段的观点——我们需要能发挥主动性和想象力的人，这是机器不擅长的。即书中讨论了人类参与工作场所的必要性。

- A：错误。the predictability of machine behavior（机器行为的可预测性）是第五段Hagel的观点，不是最后一段Brynjolfsson和McAfee讨论的内容。
- B：错误。the formula for how work is conducted efficiently（高效工作模式）是Hagel的建议（reinvent the formula），不是书中讨论的。
- C：错误。the ways machines replace human labor（机器取代人类劳动的方式）与原文相反。最后一段说要look at ways machines can augment human labor rather than replace it（增强而非取代）。
- D：正确。人类参与工作场所的必要性。this point指需要能发挥主动性想象力的人（机器不擅长）= human involvement的必要性。

## 35. Which of the following could be the most appropriate title for the text?

[A] How to Innovate Our Work Practices [B] Machines will Replace Human Labor [C] Can We Win the Race Against Machines? [D] Economic Downturns Stimulate Innovations

✅答案：**C** 主旨题。全文主线：人机对抗在经济下滑时更突出→《与机器赛跑》说技术威胁工作→Hagel指出美国工作设计有问题→需要革新工作方式、发挥人的主动性→把"与机器赛跑"重新定义为"与机器一起赛跑"。核心围绕"人机竞赛"展开，探讨人类能否胜出。

- A：错误。How to Innovate Our Work Practices（如何革新工作实践）片面。这只是最后一段的建议之一，不是全文主旨。
- B：错误。Machines will Replace Human Labor（机器将取代人类劳动）与原文观点相反。作者说要增强而非取代，问题不在技术而在制度。
- C：正确。Can We Win the Race Against Machines?（我们能赢得与机器的赛跑吗？）。全文围绕人机竞赛展开，书名Race Against the Machine，最后建议reframe为race with the machine，核心是人类如何应对机器挑战。
- D：错误。Economic Downturns Stimulate Innovations（经济下滑刺激创新）无中生有。经济下滑只是开篇背景，不是主旨。

### 本篇命题规律小结（英二阅读）

1. 科技/社会类文章：关注**现象→书籍观点→不同学者观点→建议**的结构
2. 细节题：定位到**人物+says/argues/notes**，答案是观点的同义替换
3. 指代题（this point）：答案在**前一段的核心观点**（需要能发挥主动性的人）
4. 主旨题：答案需**贯穿全文**（Race Against the Machine反复出现），不选最后一段的片面建议
5. 常见干扰：与原文相反（remain intact vs become threatened）、偷换概念（without target vs giant target sign）、因果倒置（automation加速技术发展vs技术发展导致自动化）

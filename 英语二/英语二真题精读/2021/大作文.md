# 2021 英二 大作文

## 图表描述

**图表类型**：柱状图（Bar Chart）

**图表内容**：某市居民体育锻炼方式调查
- 散步（walking）：55%（占比最大）
- 跑步（running）：30%
- 健身房（gym）：8%
- 球类运动（ball sports）：7%

## 题目

**Directions:**

Write an essay based on the following chart. In your writing, you should
1) interpret the chart, and
2) give your comments.

You should write about 150 words on the ANSWER SHEET. (15 points)

## 高分范文

The bar chart above clearly illustrates the ways residents of a certain city choose to take physical exercise. As is shown, walking takes the lead, accounting for 55%, followed by running at 30%, while going to the gym and playing ball sports account for a much smaller proportion, at 8% and 7% respectively.

What are the reasons behind this phenomenon? Several factors contribute to this trend, but generally speaking, they may boil down to the following aspects. Initially, walking and running are the most accessible and cost-effective forms of exercise, requiring no special equipment or membership fees. People can simply step out of their homes and start exercising. Furthermore, with the accelerating pace of modern life, many residents prefer flexible exercise methods that can be easily incorporated into their daily routines. By contrast, going to the gym demands not only financial commitment but also dedicated time slots, which discourages many potential participants.

In conclusion, while it is encouraging that the majority of residents engage in regular physical exercise, sports facilities and community fitness programs should be further improved to encourage more diverse forms of exercise and promote overall public health.

## 范文翻译

上方的柱状图清晰地展示了某市居民选择体育锻炼的方式。如图所示，散步居首，占比55%，其次是跑步占30%，而去健身房和参加球类运动的比例小得多，分别为8%和7%。

这一现象背后的原因是什么？有几个因素促成了这一趋势，但总的来说，可以归结为以下几个方面。首先，散步和跑步是最容易获得且最具成本效益的锻炼方式，不需要特殊设备或会员费。人们只需走出家门就可以开始锻炼。此外，随着现代生活节奏的加快，许多居民更喜欢能够轻松融入日常生活的灵活锻炼方式。相比之下，去健身房不仅需要经济投入，还需要固定的时间段，这让许多潜在的参与者望而却步。

总之，虽然大多数居民定期参加体育锻炼令人欣慰，但应进一步完善体育设施和社区健身项目，以鼓励更多样化的锻炼形式，促进整体公共健康。

## 重点句型与表达

1. **The bar chart above clearly illustrates...** —— 柱状图开头万能句型，引出图表主题
2. **As is shown, ... takes the lead, accounting for X%, followed by... at Y%** —— 描述柱状图数据的经典句式（最大项+排序）
3. **...account for a much smaller proportion, at X% and Y% respectively** —— "占比小得多，分别为X%和Y%"
4. **What are the reasons behind this phenomenon?** —— 过渡句，引出原因分析
5. **Several factors contribute to this trend, but generally speaking, they may boil down to...** —— 分析原因的万能过渡句
6. **...are the most accessible and cost-effective forms of exercise** —— "是最容易获得且最具成本效益的锻炼方式"
7. **By contrast, ... demands not only... but also...** —— "相比之下，……不仅需要……还需要……"，用于对比分析
8. **In conclusion, while it is encouraging that..., ...should be further improved to...** —— 结尾段万能句式（肯定+建议）

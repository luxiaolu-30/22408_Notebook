# 2023 英二 大作文

## 图表描述

**图表类型**：线图（Line Chart）

**图表内容**：2012-2021年我国居民健康素养水平变化情况
- 2012年：8.80%
- 2015年：10.25%
- 2021年：25.40%

**趋势**：我国居民健康素养水平呈稳步上升趋势，从2012年的8.80%提升至2021年的25.40%，增幅显著。

## 题目

**Directions:**

Write an essay based on the chart below. In your writing, you should
1) describe and interpret the chart, and
2) give your comments.

You should write about 150 words on the ANSWER SHEET. (15 points)

## 高分范文

The line chart above clearly presents information about the health literacy level of Chinese residents from 2012 to 2021. As is illustrated, the proportion of citizens with basic health literacy experienced a steady increase, rising from 8.80% in 2012 to 10.25% in 2015, and then surging to 25.40% by 2021. The overall trend indicates a significant improvement in public health awareness over the past decade.

What are the driving forces behind this encouraging trend? Several factors contribute to this improvement. Initially, the government has implemented a series of health education programs and public health campaigns, which have effectively raised people's awareness of disease prevention and healthy lifestyles. Furthermore, with the rapid development of the Internet and new media, health information has become more accessible than ever, enabling citizens to acquire health knowledge conveniently. The COVID-19 pandemic in recent years has also heightened public attention to personal health and hygiene.

In conclusion, the steady growth in health literacy reflects the progress of public health education in China. It is expected that with continued efforts, this positive trend will persist, ultimately contributing to the realization of a "Healthy China."

## 范文翻译

上方的线图清晰地展示了2012年至2021年中国居民健康素养水平的信息。如图所示，具备基本健康素养的公民比例稳步增长，从2012年的8.80%上升至2015年的10.25%，随后激增至2021年的25.40%。总体趋势表明过去十年间公众健康意识显著提升。

这一令人鼓舞的趋势背后的驱动力是什么？有几个因素促成了这一进步。首先，政府实施了一系列健康教育项目和公共卫生宣传活动，有效提高了人们的疾病预防和健康生活方式意识。此外，随着互联网和新媒体的快速发展，健康信息比以往任何时候都更容易获取，使市民能够便捷地获得健康知识。近年来的新冠疫情也提高了公众对个人健康和卫生的关注。

总之，健康素养的稳步增长反映了我国公共卫生教育的进步。预计在持续的努力下，这一积极趋势将会延续，最终为实现"健康中国"做出贡献。

## 重点句型与表达

1. **The line chart above clearly presents information about... from... to...** —— 线图开头万能句型，引出图表主题和时间范围
2. **As is illustrated, ...experienced a steady increase, rising from X to Y, and then surging to Z** —— 描述线图数据的经典句式（稳步增长后激增）
3. **The overall trend indicates...** —— "总体趋势表明……"，用于总结图表趋势
4. **What are the driving forces behind this encouraging trend?** —— 过渡句，引出原因分析
5. **...have effectively raised people's awareness of...** —— "有效提高了人们对……的意识"
6. **...has become more accessible than ever, enabling citizens to...** —— "比以往更容易获取，使市民能够……"
7. **In conclusion, ... reflects the progress of...** —— 结尾段总结句，"反映了……的进步"
8. **It is expected that with continued efforts, this positive trend will persist** —— "预计在持续努力下，这一积极趋势将延续"

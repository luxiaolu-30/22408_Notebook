Heat action plans 热浪行动计划, or HAPs, have been proliferating 激增 in India in the past few years. In general, an HAP spells out 详细说明 when and how officials should issue heat warnings 发布高温预警 and alert hospitals and other institutions. Nagpur's plan, for instance, calls for 要求 hospitals to set aside 留出 "cold wards" 冷病房 in the summer for treating heatstroke 中暑 patients, and advises builders to give construction laborers 建筑工人 a break from work on very hot days.

But implementation 实施 of existing HAPs has been uneven 不均衡的, according to a report from the Center for Policy Research 政策研究中心. Many lack adequate funding 充足资金, it found. And their triggering thresholds 触发阈值 often are not customized 定制 to the local climate. In some areas, high daytime temperatures alone might serve as an adequate trigger for alerts. But in other places, nighttime temperatures or humidity 湿度 might be as important a gauge 衡量标准 of risk as daytime highs.

Mumbai's April heat stroke deaths highlighted 凸显 the need for more nuanced 细致的 and localized warnings, researchers say. That day's high temperature of roughly 36°C was 1°C shy of 低于 the heat wave alert threshold for coastal cities 沿海城市 set by national meteorological authorities 国家气象部门. But the effects of the heat were amplified 放大 by humidity—an often neglected factor in heat alert systems—and the lack of shade 阴凉 at the late-morning outdoor ceremony.

To help improve HAPs, urban planner Kotharkar's team is working on a model plan 模型计划 that outlines best practices 最佳实践 and could be adapted to 适用于 local conditions. Among other things, she says, all cities should create a vulnerability map 脆弱性地图 to help focus responses on the populations most at risk 风险最高的人群.

Such mapping doesn't need to be complex, Kotharkar says. "A useful map can be created by looking at even a few key parameters 关键参数." For example, neighborhoods with a large elderly population or informal dwellings 非正式住宅 that cope poorly with heat could get special warnings or be bolstered 加强 with cooling centers 降温中心. The Nagpur project has already created a risk and vulnerability map, which enabled Kotharkar to tell officials which neighborhoods to focus on in the event of a heat wave this summer.

HAPs shouldn't just include short-term emergency responses 短期应急响应, researchers say, but also recommend medium- to long-term measures 中长期措施 that could make communities cooler. In Nagpur, for example, Kotharkar's team has been able to advise city officials about where to plant trees to provide shade. HAPs could also guide efforts to retrofit 改造 homes or modify building regulations 修改建筑法规. "Reducing deaths in an emergency is a good target to have, but it's the lowest target," says climate researcher Chandni Singh.

## 31. According to Paragraph 1, Nagpur's plan proposes measures to ______.

The power and ambition 雄心 of the giants of the digital economy 数字经济 is astonishing 惊人的—Amazon has just announced the purchase of the upmarket 高端的 grocery chain 杂货连锁店 Whole Foods for $13.5bn, but two years ago Facebook paid even more than that to acquire 收购 the WhatsApp messaging service 信息服务, which doesn't have any physical product 实体产品 at all. What WhatsApp offered Facebook was an intricate 复杂的 and finely detailed 极其详细的 web of its users' friendships and social lives 社交生活.

Facebook promised the European commission 欧盟委员会 then that it would not link phone numbers to Facebook identities 身份, but it broke the promise 违背承诺 almost as soon as the deal went through 交易完成. Even without knowing what was in the messages, the knowledge of who sent them and to whom was enormously revealing 极大透露隐私的 and still could be. What political journalist 政治记者, what party whip 政党督导, would not want to know the makeup 构成 of the WhatsApp groups in which Theresa May's enemies are currently plotting 密谋? It may be that the value of Whole Foods to Amazon is not so much the 460 shops it owns, but the records of which customers have purchased what.

Competition law 竞争法 appears to be the only way to address these imbalances of power 权力不平衡. But it is clumsy 笨拙的. For one thing, it is very slow compared to the pace of change within the digital economy. By the time a problem has been addressed and remedied 纠正 it may have vanished 消失 in the marketplace, to be replaced by new abuses of power 权力滥用. But there is a deeper conceptual 概念的 problem, too. Competition law as presently interpreted 目前解释的 deals with financial disadvantage 经济劣势 to consumers and this is not obvious when the users of these services don't pay for them. The users of their services are not their customers. That would be the people who buy advertising 广告 from them—and Facebook and Google, the two virtual giants 虚拟巨头, dominate digital advertising to the disadvantage of 对…不利 all other media and entertainment companies.

The product they're selling is data, and we, the users, convert 转变 our lives to data for the benefit of 为了…的利益 the digital giants. Just as some ants farm 养殖 the bugs called aphids 蚜虫 for the honeydew 蜜露 they produce when they feed, so Google farms us for the data that our digital lives yield 产生. Ants keep predatory insects 捕食性昆虫 away from where their aphids feed; Gmail keeps the spammers 垃圾邮件发送者 out of our inboxes 收件箱. It doesn't feel like a human or democratic 民主的 relationship, even if both sides benefit.

# 2018英二 Text3 长难句精读（原文+拆分+语法+翻译+考点）

## 长难句1

> The power and ambition of the giants of the digital economy is astonishing—Amazon has just announced the purchase of the upmarket grocery chain Whole Foods for $13.5bn, but two years ago Facebook paid even more than that to acquire the WhatsApp messaging service, which doesn't have any physical product at all.

### 句子拆分

主干：**The power and ambition of the giants of the digital economy is astonishing**
- 破折号后：`Amazon has just announced the purchase of the upmarket grocery chain Whole Foods for $13.5bn, but two years ago Facebook paid even more than that to acquire the WhatsApp messaging service`
  - but连接的并列分句，举例说明astonishing
  - `which doesn't have any physical product at all`：which非限制性定语从句，修饰WhatsApp messaging service

### 生词

power：力量；ambition：雄心；giants：巨头；digital economy：数字经济；astonishing：惊人的；announce：宣布；purchase：购买；upmarket：高端的；grocery chain：杂货连锁店；acquire：收购；messaging service：信息服务；physical product：实体产品；at all：根本

### 翻译

数字经济巨头的力量和雄心是惊人的——亚马逊刚刚宣布以135亿美元收购高端杂货连锁店全食超市，但两年前Facebook支付了比这更多的钱来收购WhatsApp信息服务，该服务根本没有任何实体产品。

### 考点

**破折号举例+but转折并列句+which非限制性定语从句**；开篇点题——数字巨头的力量惊人，Facebook高价收购没有实体产品的WhatsApp，31题B选项的背景。

## 长难句2

> What WhatsApp offered Facebook was an intricate and finely detailed web of its users' friendships and social lives.

### 句子拆分

主干：**What WhatsApp offered Facebook was an intricate and finely detailed web**
- `What WhatsApp offered Facebook`：what引导主语从句
- `of its users' friendships and social lives`：介词短语修饰web

### 生词

offer：提供；intricate：复杂的；finely detailed：极其详细的；web：网络、网状物；users' friendships：用户的友谊；social lives：社交生活

### 翻译

WhatsApp提供给Facebook的是一个复杂而极其详细的用户友谊和社交生活网络。

### 考点

**what主语从句+介词短语后置定语**；31题B选项的依据——Facebook收购WhatsApp是为了用户信息（users' friendships and social lives = user information）。

## 长难句3

> Facebook promised the European commission then that it would not link phone numbers to Facebook identities, but it broke the promise almost as soon as the deal went through.

### 句子拆分

并列句，but连接
分句1主干：**Facebook promised the European commission then that it would not link phone numbers to Facebook identities**
- that引导宾语从句，作promised的宾语
分句2主干：**it broke the promise almost as soon as the deal went through**
- `as soon as the deal went through`：as soon as时间状语从句

### 生词

promise：承诺；European commission：欧盟委员会；link … to …：把…和…联系起来；phone numbers：电话号码；Facebook identities：Facebook身份；break the promise：违背承诺；as soon as：一…就…；deal：交易；go through：完成、通过

### 翻译

Facebook当时向欧盟委员会承诺不会将电话号码与Facebook身份联系起来，但交易一完成它就几乎违背了承诺。

### 考点

**that宾语从句+but转折并列句+as soon as时间状语从句**；32题C选项的背景——Facebook违背承诺，将电话号码与身份联系，可能对用户构成风险。

## 长难句4

> Even without knowing what was in the messages, the knowledge of who sent them and to whom was enormously revealing and still could be.

### 句子拆分

主干：**the knowledge of who sent them and to whom was enormously revealing and still could be**
- `Even without knowing what was in the messages`：介词短语作让步状语
  - `what was in the messages`：what宾语从句，作knowing的宾语
- `of who sent them and to whom`：介词短语修饰knowledge
  - `who sent them`和`to whom`是and连接的两个宾语从句，作of的宾语
- `was enormously revealing and still could be`：and连接的并列谓语（could be后省略revealing）

### 生词

even without：即使没有；message：信息；knowledge：了解、知道；enormously：极大地；revealing：透露隐私的、暴露的；still could be：仍然可能是（省略revealing）

### 翻译

即使不知道信息内容是什么，知道是谁发的和发给谁也极大地透露隐私，而且仍然可能如此。

### 考点

**even without介词短语+what宾语从句+两个宾语从句并列+省略谓语**；32题C选项的依据——知道谁发给谁会透露隐私，即对Facebook用户构成风险（pose a risk to Facebook users）。

## 长难句5

> It may be that the value of Whole Foods to Amazon is not so much the 460 shops it owns, but the records of which customers have purchased what.

### 句子拆分

主干：**It may be that …**
- it是形式主语，真正主语是that引导的主语从句
- that从句主干：**the value of Whole Foods to Amazon is not so much the 460 shops, but the records**
  - `it owns`：省略that的定语从句，修饰shops
  - `of which customers have purchased what`：介词短语修饰records
    - `which customers have purchased what`：宾语从句，作of的宾语

### 生词

It may be that：可能是；value：价值；not so much … but …：与其说…不如说…；records：记录；purchase：购买

### 翻译

可能全食超市对亚马逊的价值与其说是它拥有的460家店铺，不如说是哪些顾客购买了什么的记录。

### 考点

**it形式主语+that主语从句+not so much…but…+省略that的定语从句+宾语从句**；类比说明——亚马逊收购全食也是为了数据（顾客购买记录），和Facebook收购WhatsApp为了用户数据一样。

## 长难句6

> Competition law appears to be the only way to address these imbalances of power. But it is clumsy.

### 句子拆分

两句
句1主干：**Competition law appears to be the only way to address these imbalances of power**
- `to address these imbalances of power`：不定式作后置定语，修饰way
句2主干：**But it is clumsy**

### 生词

competition law：竞争法；appear to be：似乎是；the only way：唯一方法；address：解决、处理；imbalances of power：权力不平衡；clumsy：笨拙的、不灵便的

### 翻译

竞争法似乎是解决这些权力不平衡的唯一方法。但它很笨拙。

### 考点

**不定式后置定语+转折**；33题D选项的论点——竞争法笨拙，跟不上数字经济变化的步伐。

for one thing：一方面；slow：缓慢的；compared to：与…相比；pace of change：变化的步伐；digital economy：数字经济

## 长难句8

> Competition law as presently interpreted deals with financial disadvantage to consumers and this is not obvious when the users of these services don't pay for them.

### 句子拆分

并列句，and连接
分句1主干：**Competition law as presently interpreted deals with financial disadvantage to consumers**
- `as presently interpreted`：as方式状语（省略it is），"如目前解释的"
分句2主干：**this is not obvious**
- `when the users of these services don't pay for them`：when时间状语从句

### 生词

as presently interpreted：如目前解释的；deal with：处理、涉及；financial disadvantage：经济劣势；consumer：消费者；obvious：明显的；pay for：为…付费

### 翻译

如目前解释的，竞争法处理消费者的经济劣势，但当这些服务的用户不为它们付费时，这一点并不明显。

### 考点

**as省略方式状语+and并列句+when时间状语从句**；34题D选项的依据——竞争法难以保护Facebook用户，因为用户不付费，广告商付费（services are paid for by advertisers）。

## 长难句9

## 长难句10

> Just as some ants farm the bugs called aphids for the honeydew they produce when they feed, so Google farms us for the data that our digital lives yield.

### 句子拆分

主干：**so Google farms us for the data**
- `Just as some ants farm the bugs called aphids for the honeydew they produce when they feed`：just as方式状语从句
  - `called aphids`：过去分词作后置定语，修饰bugs
  - `they produce`：省略that的定语从句，修饰honeydew
  - `when they feed`：when时间状语从句

just as … so …：正如…也…；farm：养殖；bugs：虫子；called：叫做；aphids：蚜虫；honeydew：蜜露；produce：产生；feed：进食；yield：产生

**just as…so…结构+过去分词后置定语+省略that的定语从句+when时间状语从句+that定语从句**；35题D选项的类比——蚂蚁养殖蚜虫=数字巨头养殖用户，说明数字巨头和用户之间的关系。

# 2018 英二 Text3 31–35｜题目+选项+逐选项解析

> 原文主题：数字巨头（Facebook、Google）通过收购获取用户数据，权力巨大；竞争法笨拙且无法保护用户（因为用户不付费，广告商才是客户）；数字巨头和用户的关系就像蚂蚁养殖蚜虫

## 31. According to Paragraph 1, Facebook acquired WhatsApp for its ______.

[A] digital products [B] user information [C] physical assets [D] quality service

✅答案：**B** 定位：第一段。`What WhatsApp offered Facebook was an intricate and finely detailed web of its users' friendships and social lives`（WhatsApp提供给Facebook的是一个复杂而极其详细的用户友谊和社交生活网络）。说明Facebook收购WhatsApp是为了用户信息。

- A：错误。digital products（数字产品）与原文相反。原文说WhatsApp doesn't have any physical product at all（根本没有实体产品），也不是为了数字产品。
- B：正确。用户信息。users' friendships and social lives = user information。
- C：错误。physical assets（实体资产）与原文相反。WhatsApp没有实体产品。
- D：错误。quality service（优质服务）无中生有。

## 32. Linking phone numbers to Facebook identities may ______.

[A] worsen political disputes [B] mess up customer records [C] pose a risk to Facebook users [D] mislead the European commission

✅答案：**C** 定位：第二段。`Even without knowing what was in the messages, the knowledge of who sent them and to whom was enormously revealing`（即使不知道信息内容，知道谁发给谁也极大地透露隐私）。将电话号码与Facebook身份联系，会知道谁发给谁，透露用户隐私，即对用户构成风险。

- A：错误。worsen political disputes（加剧政治争端）过度推断。原文提到政治记者和政党督导想知道WhatsApp群构成，但不是说加剧政治争端。
- B：错误。mess up customer records（搞乱客户记录）无中生有。
- C：正确。对Facebook用户构成风险。enormously revealing（极大透露隐私）= pose a risk to users。
- D：错误。mislead the European commission（误导欧盟委员会）偷换概念。原文说Facebook broke the promise to the European commission（违背了对欧盟委员会的承诺），不是误导。

## 33. According to the author, competition law ______.

[A] should serve the new market powers [B] may worsen the economic imbalance [C] should not provide just one legal solution [D] cannot keep pace with the changing market

✅答案：**D** 定位：第三段。`But it is clumsy. For one thing, it is very slow compared to the pace of change within the digital economy`（但它很笨拙。一方面，与数字经济内部的变化步伐相比，它非常缓慢）。说明竞争法跟不上变化的市场。

- A：错误。should serve the new market powers（应该服务于新的市场力量）无中生有。竞争法是用来解决权力不平衡的，不是服务于新市场力量。
- B：错误。may worsen the economic imbalance（可能加剧经济不平衡）无中生有。原文说竞争法处理经济劣势，不是说加剧经济不平衡。
- C：错误。should not provide just one legal solution（不应该只提供一种法律解决方案）无中生有。
- D：正确。跟不上变化的市场。very slow compared to the pace of change = cannot keep pace with the changing market。

## 34. Competition law as presently interpreted can hardly protect Facebook users because ______.

[A] they are not defined as customers [B] they are not financially reliable [C] the services are generally digital [D] the services are paid for by advertisers

✅答案：**D** 定位：第三段。`Competition law as presently interpreted deals with financial disadvantage to consumers and this is not obvious when the users of these services don't pay for them. The users of their services are not their customers. That would be the people who buy advertising from them`（如目前解释的，竞争法处理消费者的经济劣势，但当这些服务的用户不为它们付费时，这一点并不明显。服务的用户不是他们的客户。客户是从他们那里购买广告的人）。说明竞争法难以保护用户，因为用户不付费，广告商才是客户。

- A：错误。they are not defined as customers（他们没有被定义为客户）是表面现象。根本原因是用户不付费，广告商付费。A选项是D选项的结果，不是根本原因。
- B：错误。they are not financially reliable（他们经济上不可靠）无中生有。
- C：错误。the services are generally digital（服务通常是数字的）无中生有。
- D：正确。服务由广告商付费。users don't pay, customers are people who buy advertising = services are paid for by advertisers。

## 35. The ants analogy is used to illustrate ______.

[A] a win-win business model between digital giants [B] a typical competition pattern among digital giants [C] the benefits provided for digital giants' customers [D] the relationship between digital giants and their users

✅答案：**D** 例证题。定位：最后一段。论点句：`The product they're selling is data, and we, the users, convert our lives to data for the benefit of the digital giants`（他们销售的产品是数据，而我们用户为了数字巨头的利益把我们的生活转变为数据）。类比：`Just as some ants farm the bugs called aphids for the honeydew they produce, so Google farms us for the data that our digital lives yield`（正如蚂蚁养殖蚜虫获取蜜露一样，谷歌养殖我们获取数据）。说明数字巨头和用户之间的关系。

- A：错误。a win-win business model between digital giants（数字巨头之间的双赢商业模式）偷换概念。类比是数字巨头和用户之间的关系，不是数字巨头之间。
- B：错误。a typical competition pattern among digital giants（数字巨头之间的典型竞争模式）无中生有。
- C：错误。the benefits provided for digital giants' customers（数字巨头客户获得的好处）偷换概念。客户是广告商，类比讲的是用户和巨头的关系，不是客户的好处。
- D：正确。数字巨头和用户之间的关系。ants farm aphids = Google farms users for data = relationship between digital giants and their users。

### 本篇命题规律小结（英二阅读）

1. 科技/法律类文章：关注**现象（巨头收购获取数据）→问题（违背承诺、隐私泄露）→解决方案（竞争法）→方案缺陷（笨拙、不保护用户）→本质关系（巨头养殖用户）**的结构
2. 细节题：定位到**what主语从句**（What WhatsApp offered Facebook was…）
3. 推理题：定位到**否定+原因**（users don't pay, customers are advertisers）
4. 例证题：答案在**例子前的论点句**（The product they're selling is data, and we, the users, convert our lives to data）
5. 常见干扰：偷换概念（broke promise → mislead, users not customers → not defined as customers, between giants → between giants and users）、过度推断（political journalist → worsen political disputes）

Europe is not a gender-equality 性别平等 heaven. In particular, the corporate workplace 企业职场 will never be completely family-friendly 家庭友好的 until women are part of senior management 高级管理层 decisions, and Europe's top corporate-governance 公司治理 positions remain overwhelmingly 压倒性地 male. Indeed, women hold only 14 percent of positions on European corporate boards 董事会.

The European Union is now considering legislation 立法 to compel 迫使 corporate boards to maintain a certain proportion 比例 of women—up to 60 percent. This proposed mandate 拟议的命令 was born of frustration 挫败感. Last year, European Commission Vice President 副主席 Viviane Reding issued a call to voluntary action 自愿行动. Reding invited corporations to sign up for 报名参加 gender balance goals of 40 percent female board membership. But her appeal 呼吁 was considered a failure: only 24 companies took it up 接受.

Do we need quotas 配额 to ensure that women can continue to climb the corporate ladder 企业晋升阶梯 fairly as they balance work and family?

"Personally, I don't like quotas," Reding said recently. "But I like what the quotas do." Quotas get action: they "open the way to equality and they break through the glass ceiling 玻璃天花板," according to Reding, a result seen in France and other countries with legally binding 有法律约束力的 provisions 条款 on placing women in top business positions.

I understand Reding's reluctance 不情愿—and her frustration. I don't like quotas either; they run counter to 违背 my belief in meritocracy 精英治理, governance by the capable. But, when one considers the obstacles 障碍 to achieving the meritocratic ideal, it does look as if a fairer world must be temporarily ordered 命令.

After all, four decades of evidence has now shown that corporations in Europe as well as the US are evading 逃避 the meritocratic hiring and promotion of women to top positions—no matter how much "soft pressure 软压力" is put upon them. When women do break through to the summit 顶峰 of corporate power—as, for example, Sheryl Sandberg recently did at Facebook—they attract massive attention precisely 正是 because they remain the exception to the rule.

If appropriate pubic policies 公共政策 were in place 到位 to help all women—whether CEOs or their children's caregivers 照顾者—and all families, Sandberg would be no more newsworthy 有新闻价值的 than any other highly capable person living in a more just society.

## 题目（36–40）

36. In the European corporate workplace, generally ______.
[A] women take the lead
[B] men have the final say
[C] corporate governance is overwhelmed

[A] a reflection of gender balance
[B] a reluctant choice
[C] a response to Reding's call
[D] a voluntary action

38. According to Reding, quotas may help women ______.
[A] get top business positions
[B] see through the glass ceiling
[C] balance work and family
[D] anticipate legal results

39. The author's attitude toward Reding's appeal is one of ______.
[A] skepticism
[B] objectiveness
[C] indifference
[D] approval

40. Women entering top management become headlines due to lack of ______.
[A] more social justice
[B] massive media attention
[C] suitable public policies
[D] greater "soft pressure"

### 参考答案

36.B 37.B 38.A 39.D 40.C

# 2013英二 Text4 长难句精读（原文+拆分+语法+翻译+考点）

## 长难句1

> In particular, the corporate workplace will never be completely family-friendly until women are part of senior management decisions, and Europe's top corporate-governance positions remain overwhelmingly male.

### 句子拆分

并列句，and连接
分句1主干：**the corporate workplace will never be completely family-friendly**
- `until women are part of senior management decisions`：until时间状语从句
分句2主干：**Europe's top corporate-governance positions remain overwhelmingly male**

### 生词

in particular：尤其；corporate workplace：企业职场；family-friendly：家庭友好的；senior management：高级管理层；corporate-governance：公司治理；overwhelmingly：压倒性地

### 翻译

尤其是，在女性参与高级管理层决策之前，企业职场永远不会完全家庭友好，而欧洲最高公司治理职位仍然压倒性地由男性担任。

### 考点

**until** 时间状语从句；and连接两个并列分句描述问题；36题B选项依据——男性占主导=男性有最终决定权。

## 长难句2

> The European Union is now considering legislation to compel corporate boards to maintain a certain proportion of women—up to 60 percent.

### 句子拆分

主干：**The European Union is now considering legislation**
- `to compel corporate boards to maintain a certain proportion of women`：不定式作后置定语，修饰legislation
  - `compel sb to do sth`：迫使某人做某事
- 破折号后`up to 60 percent`补充说明proportion

### 生词

legislation：立法；compel：迫使；corporate board：公司董事会；maintain：维持；proportion：比例

### 翻译

欧盟目前正在考虑立法，迫使公司董事会维持一定比例的女性——高达60%。

### 考点

**不定式作后置定语**；compel sb to do结构；37题B选项依据——立法是因为自愿行动失败后的无奈选择。

## 长难句3

> This proposed mandate was born of frustration.

### 句子拆分

主干：**This proposed mandate was born of frustration**

### 生词

proposed：拟议的；mandate：命令、授权；be born of：源于；frustration：挫败感

### 翻译

这项拟议的命令源于挫败感。

### 考点

**be born of** 表"源于"；37题B选项的核心依据——立法是无奈之举（reluctant choice）。

## 长难句4

> Quotas get action: they "open the way to equality and they break through the glass ceiling," according to Reding, a result seen in France and other countries with legally binding provisions on placing women in top business positions.

### 句子拆分

主干：**Quotas get action**
- 冒号后解释：`they "open the way to equality and they break through the glass ceiling,"`
- `according to Reding`：插入语
- `a result seen in France and other countries with legally binding provisions on placing women in top business positions`：同位语，解释前面整句话的结果
  - `seen in France and other countries`：过去分词作后置定语，修饰result
  - `with legally binding provisions on placing women in top business positions`：介词短语修饰countries

### 生词

quota：配额；get action：促成行动；open the way to：开辟道路；break through：突破；glass ceiling：玻璃天花板；legally binding：有法律约束力的；provision：条款；top business position：高级商业职位

### 翻译

雷丁说，配额能促成行动：它们"开辟了平等之路，突破了玻璃天花板"，这一结果在法国和其他有法律条款规定女性进入高级商业职位的国家可以看到。

### 考点

**同位语a result**解释整句；过去分词seen作后置定语；with介词短语修饰countries；38题A选项依据——配额帮助女性获得高级商业职位。

## 长难句5

> I don't like quotas either; they run counter to my belief in meritocracy, governance by the capable.

### 句子拆分

并列句，分号连接
分句1主干：**I don't like quotas either**
分句2主干：**they run counter to my belief in meritocracy**
- `governance by the capable`：同位语，解释meritocracy

### 生词

run counter to：违背；meritocracy：精英治理、贤能统治；governance：治理；the capable：有能力的人

### 翻译

我也不喜欢配额；它们违背了我对精英治理——由有能力的人治理——的信念。

### 考点

**同位语governance by the capable**解释meritocracy；作者让步观点——虽然不喜欢配额，但39题作者最终还是支持Reding的呼吁。

## 长难句6

> But, when one considers the obstacles to achieving the meritocratic ideal, it does look as if a fairer world must be temporarily ordered.

### 句子拆分

主干：**it does look as if a fairer world must be temporarily ordered**
- `when one considers the obstacles to achieving the meritocratic ideal`：when时间/条件状语从句
- `as if a fairer world must be temporarily ordered`：as if表语从句
- does表强调

### 生词

obstacle：障碍；meritocratic ideal：精英治理理想；fairer：更公平的；temporarily：暂时地；order：命令、规定

### 翻译

但是，当考虑到实现精英治理理想的障碍时，看起来确实一个更公平的世界必须暂时被命令规定。

### 考点

**when状语从句+as if表语从句**；does强调；39题D选项依据——作者虽然不喜欢配额，但认为暂时需要配额来实现更公平的世界，即支持Reding的呼吁。

## 长难句7

> After all, four decades of evidence has now shown that corporations in Europe as well as the US are evading the meritocratic hiring and promotion of women to top positions—no matter how much "soft pressure" is put upon them.

### 句子拆分

主干：**four decades of evidence has now shown that …**
- that引导宾语从句，从句主干：**corporations are evading the meritocratic hiring and promotion of women to top positions**
  - `in Europe as well as the US`：介词短语修饰corporations
- 破折号后`no matter how much "soft pressure" is put upon them`：no matter how引导让步状语从句

### 生词

after all：毕竟；decade：十年；evidence：证据；evade：逃避；meritocratic hiring：精英式招聘；promotion：晋升；top position：高级职位；soft pressure：软压力；put upon：施加于

### 翻译

毕竟，四十年的证据现在表明，无论施加多少"软压力"，欧洲和美国的公司都在逃避以精英方式招聘和晋升女性到高级职位。

### 考点

**that宾语从句+no matter how让步状语从句**；40题C选项依据——软压力没用，需要合适的公共政策。

## 长难句8

> When women do break through to the summit of corporate power—as, for example, Sheryl Sandberg recently did at Facebook—they attract massive attention precisely because they remain the exception to the rule.

### 句子拆分

主干：**they attract massive attention**
- `When women do break through to the summit of corporate power`：when时间状语从句
  - do表强调
- 破折号插入`as, for example, Sheryl Sandberg recently did at Facebook`：as引导方式状语从句举例
- `precisely because they remain the exception to the rule`：because原因状语从句

### 生词

break through：突破；summit：顶峰；corporate power：企业权力；massive attention：大量关注；precisely：正是；exception to the rule：规则的例外

### 翻译

当女性确实突破到企业权力的顶峰时——例如谢丽尔·桑德伯格最近在Facebook所做的——她们吸引了大量关注，正是因为她们仍然是规则的例外。

### 考点

**when时间状语从句+as方式状语从句举例+because原因状语从句**；do强调；40题的现象——女性进入高层成为新闻，原因是缺乏合适的公共政策。

## 长难句9

> If appropriate pubic policies were in place to help all women—whether CEOs or their children's caregivers—and all families, Sandberg would be no more newsworthy than any other highly capable person living in a more just society.

### 句子拆分

主干：**Sandberg would be no more newsworthy than any other highly capable person**
- `If appropriate pubic policies were in place to help all women … and all families`：if虚拟条件句（对现在的虚拟）
  - `whether CEOs or their children's caregivers`：插入语，解释all women
- `living in a more just society`：现在分词作后置定语，修饰person
- `no more … than …`：和…一样不…

### 生词

appropriate：合适的；public policy：公共政策；be in place：到位；caregiver：照顾者；newsworthy：有新闻价值的；highly capable：非常有能力的；just：公正的

### 翻译

如果有合适的公共政策来帮助所有女性——无论是CEO还是她们孩子的照顾者——和所有家庭，桑德伯格就不会比生活在一个更公正社会中的任何其他非常有能力的人更有新闻价值。

### 考点

**if虚拟条件句**（对现在的虚拟）；no more…than比较结构；现在分词作后置定语；全文结论句，40题C选项的核心依据——女性进入高层成为新闻是因为缺乏合适的公共政策。

# 2013 英二 Text4 36–40｜题目+选项+逐选项解析

> 原文主题：欧洲企业董事会性别比例失衡，欧盟考虑立法强制配额，作者虽不喜欢配额但支持暂时使用

## 36. In the European corporate workplace, generally ______.

[A] women take the lead [B] men have the final say [C] corporate governance is overwhelmed [D] senior management is family-friendly

✅答案：**B** 定位：第一段。`Europe's top corporate-governance positions remain overwhelmingly male` + `women hold only 14 percent of positions on European corporate boards`。男性占压倒性多数，即男性有最终决定权。

- A：错误。women take the lead（女性带头）与原文相反。女性只占14%。
- B：正确。男性有最终决定权。overwhelmingly male = men have the final say。
- C：错误。corporate governance is overwhelmed（公司治理被压垮）偷换概念。overwhelmingly修饰的是male（男性占压倒性多数），不是公司治理被压垮。
- D：错误。senior management is family-friendly（高级管理层家庭友好）与原文相反。原文说"will never be completely family-friendly until women are part of senior management"。

## 37. The European Union's intended legislation is ______.

[A] a reflection of gender balance [B] a reluctant choice [C] a response to Reding's call [D] a voluntary action

✅答案：**B** 定位：第二段。`This proposed mandate was born of frustration`（拟议的命令源于挫败感），因为Reding的自愿行动呼吁失败了（only 24 companies took it up），所以才考虑立法。说明立法是无奈之举。

- A：错误。a reflection of gender balance（性别平衡的反映）与原文相反。立法是为了实现性别平衡，不是性别平衡的反映（目前性别不平衡）。
- B：正确。无奈的选择。born of frustration + 自愿行动失败→reluctant choice。
- C：错误。a response to Reding's call（对Reding呼吁的回应）曲解原文。立法是因为Reding的呼吁失败了，不是对呼吁的回应。
- D：错误。a voluntary action（自愿行动）与原文相反。立法是强制性的，自愿行动已经失败了。

## 38. According to Reding, quotas may help women ______.

[A] get top business positions [B] see through the glass ceiling [C] balance work and family [D] anticipate legal results

✅答案：**A** 定位：第四段。Quotas "open the way to equality and they break through the glass ceiling" + "a result seen in France and other countries with legally binding provisions on placing women in top business positions"。配额帮助女性获得高级商业职位。

- A：正确。获得高级商业职位。break through the glass ceiling + placing women in top business positions = get top business positions。
- B：错误。see through the glass ceiling（看穿玻璃天花板）偷换概念。原文是break through（突破）玻璃天花板，不是see through（看穿）。
- C：错误。balance work and family（平衡工作和家庭）是第三段的问题，不是配额帮助的内容。
- D：错误。anticipate legal results（预期法律结果）无中生有。

## 39. The author's attitude toward Reding's appeal is one of ______.

[A] skepticism [B] objectiveness [C] indifference [D] approval

✅答案：**D** 态度题。定位：第五段。作者说"I understand Reding's reluctance—and her frustration"（我理解Reding的不情愿和挫败感），"I don't like quotas either"（我也不喜欢配额），但"it does look as if a fairer world must be temporarily ordered"（看起来更公平的世界必须暂时被命令规定）。作者虽然不喜欢配额，但认为暂时需要配额，即支持Reding的呼吁。

- A：错误。skepticism（怀疑）与作者态度相反。作者最终支持配额。
- B：错误。objectiveness（客观）。作者有明确立场（支持暂时使用配额），不是纯粹客观。
- C：错误。indifference（冷漠）与作者态度相反。作者明确表达了理解和支持。
- D：正确。approval（支持）。作者理解Reding，认为暂时需要配额来实现公平世界。

## 40. Women entering top management become headlines due to lack of ______.

[A] more social justice [B] massive media attention [C] suitable public policies [D] greater "soft pressure"

✅答案：**C** 定位：最后一段。`If appropriate pubic policies were in place to help all women … Sandberg would be no more newsworthy than any other highly capable person`。如果有合适的公共政策，女性进入高层就不会成为新闻。反推：女性进入高层成为新闻是因为缺乏合适的公共政策。

- A：错误。more social justice（更多社会公正）偷换概念。原文说的是"living in a more just society"（更公正的社会），但直接原因是缺乏公共政策，不是缺乏社会公正。
- B：错误。massive media attention（大量媒体关注）是结果，不是原因。女性成为新闻是因为吸引了媒体关注，不是因为缺乏媒体关注。
- C：正确。合适的公共政策。if appropriate public policies were in place → no more newsworthy，反推lack of suitable public policies → become headlines。
- D：错误。greater "soft pressure"（更大的软压力）与原文相反。第六段说"no matter how much 'soft pressure' is put upon them"，软压力没用，不是缺乏软压力。

### 本篇命题规律小结（英二阅读）

1. 社会政策类文章：关注**问题→自愿行动失败→立法强制→作者态度（虽不喜欢但支持）**的结构
2. 细节题：定位到**数据和事实**（14%女性、24家公司接受呼吁）
3. 态度题：注意**先让步后转折**（I don't like quotas either, But…must be temporarily ordered），转折后是真实态度
4. 原因题（due to）：答案常是**尾段if虚拟句的反推**（如果有政策就不会成为新闻→因为缺乏政策）
5. 常见干扰：偷换概念（break through→see through，overwhelmingly male→governance overwhelmed）、与原文相反（voluntary→mandatory）

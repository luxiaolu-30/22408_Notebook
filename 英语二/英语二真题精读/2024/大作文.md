# 2024 英二 大作文

## 图表描述

**图表类型**：饼图（Pie Chart）

**图表内容**：某高校学生劳动实践课主要收获调查
- 增强动手能力（hands-on ability）：35%（占比最大）
- 培养劳动观念（labor values）：28%
- 增进团队协作（teamwork）：20%
- 了解职业方向（career direction）：17%

## 题目

**Directions:**

Write an essay based on the chart below. In your writing, you should
1) describe and interpret the chart, and
2) give your comments.

You should write about 150 words on the ANSWER SHEET. (15 points)

## 高分范文

The pie chart above clearly illustrates the main gains students obtained from labor practice courses at a certain university. As is shown, enhancing hands-on ability takes the largest share, accounting for 35%, followed by cultivating labor values at 28%, improving teamwork skills at 20%, and understanding career directions at 17%.

What are the reasons behind this distribution? Several factors contribute to this phenomenon. Initially, labor practice courses emphasize practical skills training, which naturally leads to the improvement of hands-on ability as the most prominent gain. Furthermore, these courses require students to work together to complete tasks, fostering a sense of teamwork and collaboration. The inclusion of labor values reflects the educational goal of instilling respect for hard work and diligence in students. Understanding career directions, though accounting for the smallest proportion, indicates that labor practice also helps students explore future career paths.

In conclusion, the chart reveals that labor practice courses offer multifaceted benefits to students. It is advisable that universities continue to strengthen such programs, as they not only develop practical skills but also shape students' values and career awareness.

## 范文翻译

上方的饼图清晰地展示了某高校学生从劳动实践课中获得的主要收获。如图所示，增强动手能力占比最大，达35%，其次是培养劳动观念占28%，增进团队协作占20%，了解职业方向占17%。

这一分布背后的原因是什么？有几个因素促成了这一现象。首先，劳动实践课程注重实践技能训练，自然使得动手能力的提升成为最显著的收获。此外，这些课程要求学生协作完成任务，培养了团队合作意识。劳动观念的纳入反映了教育目标，即培养学生对辛勤劳动和勤奋的尊重。了解职业方向虽然占比最小，但表明劳动实践也有助于学生探索未来的职业道路。

总之，图表表明劳动实践课程为学生提供了多方面的益处。建议高校继续加强此类项目，因为它们不仅培养实践技能，还塑造学生的价值观和职业意识。

## 重点句型与表达

1. **The pie chart above clearly illustrates...** —— 饼图开头万能句型，引出图表主题
2. **As is shown, ... takes the largest share, accounting for X%, followed by... at Y%** —— 描述饼图数据的经典句式（最大项+排序）
3. **What are the reasons behind this distribution?** —— 过渡句，引出原因分析
4. **Several factors contribute to this phenomenon.** —— "有几个因素促成了这一现象"，分析原因的万能过渡句
5. **...emphasize..., which naturally leads to...** —— "……注重……，自然导致……"
6. **In conclusion, the chart reveals that... offer multifaceted benefits to...** —— 结尾段总结句，"图表揭示……提供多方面益处"
7. **It is advisable that... continue to strengthen...** —— "建议……继续加强……"
8. **...not only develop..., but also shape...** —— "不仅培养……，还塑造……"，not only...but also并列结构

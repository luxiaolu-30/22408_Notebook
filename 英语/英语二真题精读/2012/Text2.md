## 长难句1

> It is not that pink is intrinsically bad, but it is such a tiny slice of the rainbow and, though it may celebrate girlhood in one way, it also repeatedly and firmly fuses girls' identity to appearance.

### 句子拆分

主干：**It is not that …, but it is such a tiny slice of the rainbow and … it also fuses girls' identity to appearance**
- `It is not that …, but (that) …`：不是…而是…
- `though it may celebrate girlhood in one way`：though让步状语从句，插入在and和第二个分句之间

### 生词

intrinsically：本质上；tiny slice of the rainbow：彩虹中极小的一片（比喻颜色中的一小部分）；celebrate：颂扬；girlhood：少女时代；fuse … to …：把…与…融合；identity：身份；appearance：外表

### 翻译

不是说粉色本身不好，而是它只是彩虹中极小的一片；而且，尽管它可能在某种程度上颂扬了少女时代，它也反复而牢固地将女孩的身份与外表融合在一起。

### 考点

**It is not that…but (that)…** 结构；though让步从句插入；26题定位句——粉色只是颜色中的一小部分，不应成为少女时代的唯一代表。

## 26. By saying "it is … the rainbow" (Para.1), the author means pink ______.

[A] should not be the sole representation of girlhood [B] should not be associated with girls' innocence [C] cannot explain girls' lack of imagination [D] cannot influence girls' lives and interests

✅答案：**A** 定位：第一段 `it is such a tiny slice of the rainbow`，粉色只是彩虹中极小的一片。结合后文"它反复将女孩身份与外表融合"，作者意思是粉色不应成为少女时代的唯一代表。

- C：错误。cannot explain girls' lack of imagination（无法解释女孩缺乏想象力）偷换概念。原文是作者"对女孩生活和兴趣缺乏想象力感到绝望"，不是女孩缺乏想象力。
- D：错误。cannot influence girls' lives and interests（不能影响女孩生活和兴趣）与原文相反。原文说粉色pervasive in girls' lives，说明影响很大。

## 27. According to Paragraph 2, which of the following is true of colours?

## 30. It can be concluded that girls' attraction to pink seems to be ______.

[A] clearly explained by their inborn tendency [B] fully understood by clothing manufacturers [C] mainly imposed by profit-driven businessmen [D] well interpreted by psychological experts

✅答案：**C** 主旨结论题。全文主线：粉色不是天生的（第二段）→营销策略主导了我们对儿童的认知（第三段）→商家通过细分市场、放大性别差异来获利（第四段）。结论：女孩对粉色的喜爱主要是逐利商家强加的。

- A：错误。clearly explained by their inborn tendency（由天生倾向清楚解释）与原文相反。原文明确说不是编码在DNA中。
- B：错误。fully understood by clothing manufacturers（被服装制造商完全理解）曲解原文。制造商是创造/利用这种偏好，不是"理解"。
- C：正确。主要由逐利的商人强加。marketing strategy + boost profits + magnify/invent gender differences = profit-driven businessmen imposed。
- D：错误。well interpreted by psychological experts（被心理学专家很好地解释）与原文相反。作者说心理发展阶段是营销伎俩，不是专家研究结果。

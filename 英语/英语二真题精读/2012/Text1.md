

Homework has never been terribly popular with students and even many parents, but in recent years it has been particularly scorned 鄙视. School districts across the country, most recently Los Angeles Unified, are revising 修订 their thinking on this educational ritual 惯例. Unfortunately, L.A. Unified has produced an inflexible 不灵活的 policy which mandates 规定 that with the exception of 除了 some advanced courses, homework may no longer count for 占 more than 10% of a student's academic grade 学业成绩.

This rule is meant to address 解决 the difficulty that students from impoverished 贫困的 or chaotic 混乱的 homes might have in completing their homework. But the policy is unclear and contradictory 矛盾的. Certainly, no homework should be assigned that students cannot complete on their own or that they cannot do without expensive equipment. But if the district is essentially giving a pass 放行 to students who do not do their homework because of complicated family lives, it is going riskily close to the implication 暗示 that standards need to be lowered for poor children.

District administrators say that homework will still be a part of schooling; teachers are allowed to assign as much of it as they want. But with homework counting for no more than 10% of their grades, students can easily skip 跳过 half their homework and see very little difference on their report cards 成绩单. Some students might do well on state tests without completing their homework, but what about the students who performed well on the tests and did their homework? It is quite possible that the homework helped. Yet rather than empowering 授权 teachers to find what works best for their students, the policy imposes 强加 a flat 统一的, across-the-board 全面的 rule.

At the same time, the policy addresses none of the truly thorny 棘手的 questions about homework. If the district finds homework to be unimportant to its students' academic achievement, it should move to reduce or eliminate 取消 the assignments, not make them count for almost nothing. Conversely 相反, if homework matters, it should account for 占 a significant portion of the grade. Meanwhile, this policy does nothing to ensure that the homework students receive is meaningful or appropriate to their age and the subject, or that teachers are not assigning more than they are willing to review and correct.

The homework rules should be put on hold 暂停 while the school board, which is responsible for setting educational policy, looks into the matter and conducts public hearings 听证会. It is not too late for L.A. Unified to do homework right.

# 2012英二 Text1 长难句精读（原文+拆分+语法+翻译+考点）

## 长难句1

> Homework has never been terribly popular with students and even many parents, but in recent years it has been particularly scorned.

### 句子拆分

并列句，but连接
分句1主干：**Homework has never been terribly popular with students and even many parents**
分句2主干：**it has been particularly scorned**
- `in recent years`：时间状语

### 生词

terribly：非常；be popular with：受…欢迎；scorn：鄙视、嘲弄

### 翻译

家庭作业从来就不受学生甚至许多家长的欢迎，但近年来它受到了尤其多的嘲讽。

### 考点

**现在完成时**表从过去持续到现在；but转折；开篇点题，21题推理依据——近年来受到更多批评。

---

## 长难句2

> Unfortunately, L.A. Unified has produced an inflexible policy which mandates that with the exception of some advanced courses, homework may no longer count for more than 10% of a student's academic grade.

### 句子拆分

主干：**L.A. Unified has produced an inflexible policy**
- `which mandates that …`：which引导定语从句，修饰policy
  - that引导宾语从句，从句主干：**homework may no longer count for more than 10% of a student's academic grade**
    - `with the exception of some advanced courses`：插入语，"除了一些高级课程"

### 生词

inflexible：不灵活的、僵化的；mandate：规定、命令；with the exception of：除了；advanced course：高级课程；count for：占（比例）；academic grade：学业成绩

### 翻译

不幸的是，洛杉矶联合学区出台了一项僵化的政策，规定除部分高级课程外，家庭作业在学生学业成绩中的占比不得超过10%。

### 考点

**定语从句嵌套宾语从句**；with the exception of插入语；全文核心政策句，后文所有批评都围绕此政策展开。

---

## 长难句3

> This rule is meant to address the difficulty that students from impoverished or chaotic homes might have in completing their homework.

### 句子拆分

主干：**This rule is meant to address the difficulty**
- `that students from impoverished or chaotic homes might have in completing their homework`：that引导定语从句，修饰difficulty
  - `from impoverished or chaotic homes`：介词短语修饰students
  - `have difficulty in doing sth`：做某事有困难

### 生词

be meant to：旨在；address：解决；impoverished：贫困的；chaotic：混乱的；have difficulty in doing：做某事有困难

### 翻译

这项规定旨在解决来自贫困或混乱家庭的学生在完成家庭作业时可能遇到的困难。

### 考点

**定语从句修饰difficulty**；be meant to表目的；22题定位句——政策出台原因是贫困学生完成作业有困难。

## 长难句4

> Certainly, no homework should be assigned that students cannot complete on their own or that they cannot do without expensive equipment.

### 句子拆分

主干：**no homework should be assigned**
- `that students cannot complete on their own`：定语从句1，修饰homework（分隔定语从句，被谓语隔开）
- `or that they cannot do without expensive equipment`：定语从句2，or连接，同样修饰homework

### 生词

assign：布置；on one's own：独自；without expensive equipment：没有昂贵设备

### 翻译

当然，不应布置学生无法独立完成或没有昂贵设备就无法完成的家庭作业。

### 考点

**分隔定语从句**（先行词homework和定语从句被谓语should be assigned隔开）；两个that定语从句并列；作者让步观点句。

## 长难句5

> But if the district is essentially giving a pass to students who do not do their homework because of complicated family lives, it is going riskily close to the implication that standards need to be lowered for poor children.

### 句子拆分

主干：**it is going riskily close to the implication**
- `if the district is essentially giving a pass to students …`：if条件状语从句
  - `who do not do their homework because of complicated family lives`：who定语从句，修饰students
- `that standards need to be lowered for poor children`：that同位语从句，解释implication

### 生词

essentially：本质上；give a pass to：放行、给…及格；complicated：复杂的；riskily：冒险地；implication：暗示；lower standards：降低标准

### 翻译

但如果学区本质上对那些因复杂家庭生活而不做作业的学生放行，那就危险地接近于暗示需要为贫困儿童降低标准。

### 考点

**if条件句+定语从句+同位语从句**多层嵌套；作者批评政策的核心逻辑——给贫困生放水=降低标准。

## 长难句6

> But with homework counting for no more than 10% of their grades, students can easily skip half their homework and see very little difference on their report cards.

### 句子拆分

主干：**students can easily skip half their homework and see very little difference**
- `with homework counting for no more than 10% of their grades`：with复合结构作原因状语
- `on their report cards`：地点状语

### 生词

count for：占；skip：跳过、不做；report card：成绩单

### 翻译

但由于家庭作业在成绩中占比不超过10%，学生可以轻易地跳过一半作业，而成绩单上几乎看不出差别。

### 考点

**with复合结构**表原因；23题A选项定位句——作业占比低→学生不做作业也没影响→打击做作业积极性。

## 长难句7

> Yet rather than empowering teachers to find what works best for their students, the policy imposes a flat, across-the-board rule.

### 句子拆分

主干：**the policy imposes a flat, across-the-board rule**
- `rather than empowering teachers to find what works best for their students`：rather than短语作状语，"而不是…"
  - `what works best for their students`：what引导宾语从句，作find的宾语

### 生词

rather than：而不是；empower：授权；impose：强加；flat：统一的、扁平的；across-the-board：全面的、一刀切的

### 翻译

然而，该政策没有授权教师去寻找最适合学生的做法，而是强加了一条统一的、一刀切的规则。

### 考点

**rather than**表对比；what宾语从句；23题D选项干扰来源——原文说没有授权教师，D选项说限制教师权力，程度过重。

## 长难句8

> If the district finds homework to be unimportant to its students' academic achievement, it should move to reduce or eliminate the assignments, not make them count for almost nothing.

### 句子拆分

主干：**it should move to reduce or eliminate the assignments**
- `If the district finds homework to be unimportant to its students' academic achievement`：if条件状语从句
  - `find sth to be + adj`：发现某事是…
- `not make them count for almost nothing`：省略should的并列否定，"而不是让它们几乎不算分"

### 生词

academic achievement：学业成就；move to：采取行动；eliminate：取消、消除；assignment：作业；count for：占

### 翻译

如果学区发现家庭作业对学生的学业成就不重要，就应该采取行动减少或取消作业，而不是让它们几乎不算分。

### 考点

if条件句；**find sth to be**结构；not make并列省略；作者提出的逻辑二选一——要么取消，要么占重要比例。

## 长难句9

> Meanwhile, this policy does nothing to ensure that the homework students receive is meaningful or appropriate to their age and the subject, or that teachers are not assigning more than they are willing to review and correct.

### 句子拆分

主干：**this policy does nothing to ensure that … or that …**
- `that the homework students receive is meaningful or appropriate to their age and the subject`：that宾语从句1
  - `students receive`：省略that的定语从句，修饰homework
- `or that teachers are not assigning more than they are willing to review and correct`：that宾语从句2，or连接
  - `more than they are willing to review and correct`：more than比较结构

### 生词

meaningful：有意义的；appropriate to：适合；assign：布置；be willing to：愿意；review and correct：批改

### 翻译

同时，这项政策完全没有确保学生收到的作业是有意义的或适合其年龄和学科的，也没有确保教师布置的作业不超过他们愿意批改的量。

### 考点

**两个that宾语从句并列**（or连接）；定语从句省略that；more than比较结构；政策的另一个缺陷——不解决作业质量问题。

## 长难句10

> The homework rules should be put on hold while the school board, which is responsible for setting educational policy, looks into the matter and conducts public hearings.

### 句子拆分

- `while the school board … looks into the matter and conducts public hearings`：while时间状语从句
  - `which is responsible for setting educational policy`：which非限制性定语从句，修饰school board
  - `looks into`和`conducts`是从句中的并列谓语

### 生词

put on hold：暂停；school board：学校董事会；be responsible for：负责；look into：调查；conduct：举行；public hearing：公开听证会

# 2012 英二 Text1 21–25｜题目+选项+逐选项解析

> 原文主题：洛杉矶联合学区家庭作业占比不超过10%的政策存在多重缺陷

## 21. It is implied in Paragraph 1 that nowadays homework ______.

[A] is receiving more criticism [B] is no longer an educational ritual [C] is not required for advanced courses [D] is gaining more preferences

✅答案：**A** 定位：第一段 `Homework has never been terribly popular … but in recent years it has been particularly scorned`

作业从来不受欢迎，但近年来尤其受嘲讽，说明现在受到更多批评。

- A：正确。受到更多批评。particularly scorned = more criticism。
- B：错误。is no longer an educational ritual（不再是教育惯例）曲解原文。原文说学区在"revising their thinking on this educational ritual"，即重新思考这一惯例，不是说它不再是惯例。
- C：错误。is not required for advanced courses（高级课程不需要作业）偷换概念。原文说"with the exception of some advanced courses"，即高级课程除外（作业占比可以超过10%），不是不需要作业。
- D：错误。is gaining more preferences（获得更多喜爱）与原文相反。作业受到更多嘲讽，不是喜爱。

## 22. L.A. Unified has made the rule about homework mainly because poor students ______.

[A] tend to have moderate expectations for their education [B] have asked for a different educational standard [C] may have problems finishing their homework [D] have voiced their complaints about homework

✅答案：**C** 定位：第二段 `This rule is meant to address the difficulty that students from impoverished or chaotic homes might have in completing their homework`

政策旨在解决贫困/混乱家庭学生完成作业的困难。

- A：错误。tend to have moderate expectations（往往有适度期望）无中生有。
- B：错误。have asked for a different educational standard（要求不同的教育标准）无中生有。是政策制定者担心降低标准，不是学生要求。
- C：正确。可能在完成作业方面有困难。impoverished homes = poor students, difficulty in completing = problems finishing。
- D：错误。have voiced their complaints（表达了抱怨）无中生有。

---

## 23. According to Paragraph 3, one problem with the policy is that it may ______.

[A] discourage students from doing homework [B] result in students' indifference to their report cards [C] undermine the authority of state tests [D] restrict teachers' power in education

✅答案：**A** 定位：第三段 `students can easily skip half their homework and see very little difference on their report cards`

作业占比低→不做也没影响→学生不愿做作业。

- A：正确。打击学生做作业的积极性。skip half their homework = discourage from doing homework。
- B：错误。result in students' indifference to their report cards（导致学生对成绩单漠不关心）偷换概念。原文说成绩单上看不出差别，不是学生对成绩单漠不关心。
- C：错误。undermine the authority of state tests（削弱州考试的权威性）无中生有。原文提到州考试，但没说削弱其权威性。
- D：错误。restrict teachers' power in education（限制教师的教育权力）程度过重。原文说"rather than empowering teachers"（没有授权教师），不等于限制教师权力。

## 24. As mentioned in Paragraph 4, a key question unanswered about homework is whether ______.

[A] it should be eliminated [B] it counts much in schooling [C] it places extra burdens on teachers [D] it is important for grades

✅答案：**B** 定位：第四段。作者说政策没有回答棘手问题：如果作业不重要就取消，如果重要就占重要比例。核心问题是作业在教育中到底重不重要（counts much）。

- A：错误。it should be eliminated（应该取消）只是作者假设的一个分支，不是未回答的核心问题。
- B：正确。它在学校教育中是否重要。作者的二选一逻辑：不重要→取消；重要→占大比例。核心是作业到底重不重要。
- C：错误。it places extra burdens on teachers（给教师带来额外负担）是后文提到的一个细节，不是核心未答问题。
- D：错误。it is important for grades（对成绩重要）偷换概念。原文讨论的是作业对学业成就/教育整体是否重要，不只是对成绩。

## 25. A suitable title for this text could be ______.

[A] Wrong Interpretation of an Educational Policy [B] A Welcomed Policy for Poor Students [C] Thorny Questions about Homework [D] A Faulty Approach to Homework

✅答案：**D** 主旨题。全文批评洛杉矶学区的作业政策（占比不超过10%）有缺陷：目的不清、打击学生积极性、未回答核心问题。作者建议暂停政策。

- A：错误。Wrong Interpretation of an Educational Policy（对教育政策的错误解读）。文章不是说别人解读错了，而是政策本身有缺陷。
- B：错误。A Welcomed Policy for Poor Students（受贫困学生欢迎的政策）与原文态度相反。作者批评政策，且没说受贫困生欢迎。
- C：错误。Thorny Questions about Homework（关于作业的棘手问题）片面。棘手问题只是第四段内容，全文核心是批评一项具体政策。
- D：正确。A Faulty Approach to Homework（一种有缺陷的作业处理方式）。全文批评政策有缺陷，faulty准确概括作者态度。

### 本篇命题规律小结（英二阅读）

1. 教育政策类文章：关注**政策内容→出台原因→政策缺陷→作者建议**的结构
2. 推理题（implied）答案是**原文的同义改写**，不选偷换概念（如"高级课程除外"→"高级课程不需要"）
3. 原因题定位到**be meant to/address**等表目的的词
4. 主旨题答案需**概括全文+体现作者态度**（faulty体现批评态度），不选片面细节
5. 常见干扰：程度过重（restrict power vs not empower）、偷换概念（对成绩重要vs对教育重要）

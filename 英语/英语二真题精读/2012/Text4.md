

The great recession 大萧条 may be over, but this era of high joblessness 高失业率 is probably beginning. Before it ends, it will likely change the life course and character of a generation of young adults. And ultimately 最终, it is likely to reshape 重塑 our politics, our culture, and the character of our society for years.

No one tries harder than the jobless to find silver linings 一线希望 in this national economic disaster. Many said that unemployment, while extremely painful, had improved them in some ways: they had become less materialistic 物质主义的 and more financially prudent 财务谨慎的; they were more aware of the struggles of others. In limited respects 在有限的方面, perhaps the recession will leave society better off 更好. At the very least 至少, it has awoken us from our national fever dream 狂热梦想 of easy riches and bigger houses, and put a necessary end to an era of reckless 鲁莽的 personal spending.

But for the most part 在很大程度上, these benefits seem thin 微薄的, uncertain, and far off 遥远的. In The Moral Consequences of Economic Growth, the economic historian Benjamin Friedman argues that both inside and outside the U.S., lengthy 长期的 periods of economic stagnation 停滞 or decline have almost always left society more mean-spirited 心胸狭窄的 and less inclusive 包容的, and have usually stopped or reversed 逆转 the advance of rights and freedoms. Anti-immigrant sentiment 反移民情绪 typically increases, as does conflict between races and classes.

Income inequality 收入不平等 usually falls during a recession, but it has not shrunk 缩小 in this one. Indeed, this period of economic weakness may reinforce 加强 class divides 阶级分化, and decrease opportunities to cross them—especially for young people. The research of Till Von Wachter, the economist at Columbia University, suggests that not all people graduating into a recession see their life chances dimmed 暗淡: those with degrees from elite universities 精英大学 catch up fairly quickly to where they otherwise would have been if they had graduated in better times; it is the masses 大众 beneath them that are left behind 落后.

In the Internet age, it is particularly easy to see the resentment 怨恨 that has always been hidden within American society. More difficult, in the moment, is discerning 看清 precisely how these lean times 拮据时期 are affecting society's character. In many respects, this recession was no more severe than those of the 1970s, 1980s, or even the 1930s. Yet, now the social fabric 社会结构 seems more fragile 脆弱的. We will have to wait and see exactly how these hard times will reshape our social fabric. But they certainly will reshape it, and all the more so 更加如此 the longer they extend.

## 长难句1

### 句子拆分

并列句，but连接

### 考点

but转折；开篇点题——经济衰退结束但高失业时代才开始，为全文奠定基调。

## 长难句2

> Before it ends, it will likely change the life course and character of a generation of young adults. And ultimately, it is likely to reshape our politics, our culture, and the character of our society for years.

### 句子拆分

两个独立句子
句1主干：**it will likely change the life course and character of a generation of young adults**
- `Before it ends`：时间状语从句

句2主干：**it is likely to reshape our politics, our culture, and the character of our society**
- `ultimately`：状语

### 生词

life course：人生轨迹；character：性格、特征；generation：一代；ultimately：最终；reshape：重塑

### 翻译

在它结束之前，它可能会改变年轻一代的人生轨迹和性格。最终，它可能会在多年内重塑我们的政治、文化和社会特征。

### 考点

并列宾语（politics, culture, character）；开篇预测——高失业时代将长期影响社会，40题"影响是确定的"的伏笔。

## 长难句3

> Many said that unemployment, while extremely painful, had improved them in some ways: they had become less materialistic and more financially prudent; they were more aware of the struggles of others.

### 句子拆分

主干：**Many said that …**
- that引导宾语从句，从句主干：**unemployment had improved them in some ways**
  - `while extremely painful`：while让步状语从句省略（while it is extremely painful）
- 冒号后两个并列分句，解释improved的具体方面：
  1. `they had become less materialistic and more financially prudent`
  2. `they were more aware of the struggles of others`

### 生词

while extremely painful：虽然极其痛苦；materialistic：物质主义的；financially prudent：财务上谨慎的；be aware of：意识到；struggle：挣扎、奋斗

### 翻译

许多人说，失业虽然极其痛苦，但在某些方面让他们有所提升：他们变得不那么物质主义，在财务上更加谨慎；他们更能体会他人的挣扎。

### 考点

**while让步状语从句省略**；冒号后并列解释；37题D选项依据——人们重新考虑生活方式（不那么物质、更谨慎）。

## 长难句4

> At the very least, it has awoken us from our national fever dream of easy riches and bigger houses, and put a necessary end to an era of reckless personal spending.

### 句子拆分

主干：**it has awoken us from … and put a necessary end to …**
- `At the very least`：状语
- `from our national fever dream of easy riches and bigger houses`：介词短语
- `to an era of reckless personal spending`：介词短语

### 生词

at the very least：至少；awaken from：从…中唤醒；fever dream：狂热梦想；easy riches：轻松致富；put an end to：结束；reckless：鲁莽的、不计后果的

### 翻译

至少，它把我们从"轻松致富、更大房子"的全民狂热梦想中唤醒，并必要地结束了一个鲁莽个人消费的时代。

### 考点

**并列谓语**（awoken和put）；37题D选项依据——结束鲁莽消费时代=重新考虑生活方式。

- 三个形容词并列表语

### 生词

for the most part：在很大程度上；thin：微薄的、微弱的；far off：遥远的

## 长难句6

> In The Moral Consequences of Economic Growth, the economic historian Benjamin Friedman argues that both inside and outside the U.S., lengthy periods of economic stagnation or decline have almost always left society more mean-spirited and less inclusive, and have usually stopped or reversed the advance of rights and freedoms.

### 句子拆分

主干：**Benjamin Friedman argues that …**
- `In The Moral Consequences of Economic Growth`：状语
- `the economic historian`：同位语，解释Benjamin Friedman
- that引导宾语从句，从句主干：**lengthy periods of economic stagnation or decline have almost always left society more mean-spirited and less inclusive, and have usually stopped or reversed the advance of rights and freedoms**
  - `both inside and outside the U.S.`：状语
  - 两个并列谓语：have left和have stopped or reversed

### 生词

lengthy：长期的；stagnation：停滞；decline：衰退；mean-spirited：心胸狭窄的、刻薄的；inclusive：包容的；reverse：逆转；advance：进步

### 翻译

在《经济增长的道德后果》一书中，经济历史学家本杰明·弗里德曼认为，无论在美国国内还是国外，长期的经济停滞或衰退几乎总是让社会变得更加心胸狭窄、更不包容，并且通常会阻止或逆转权利和自由的进步。

### 考点

**宾语从句含并列谓语**；38题B选项依据——经济衰退让社会更mean-spirited（心胸狭窄）= 展现人性中更多邪恶面。

> Anti-immigrant sentiment typically increases, as does conflict between races and classes.

### 句子拆分

主干：**Anti-immigrant sentiment typically increases**
- `as does conflict between races and classes`：as引导倒装句，表"…也一样"
  - 正常语序：as conflict between races and classes does (increases)

### 生词

anti-immigrant sentiment：反移民情绪；typically：通常；race：种族；class：阶级

反移民情绪通常会增加，种族和阶级之间的冲突也会增加。

## 长难句8

> The research of Till Von Wachter, the economist at Columbia University, suggests that not all people graduating into a recession see their life chances dimmed: those with degrees from elite universities catch up fairly quickly to where they otherwise would have been if they had graduated in better times; it is the masses beneath them that are left behind.

### 句子拆分

主干：**The research suggests that …**
- `the economist at Columbia University`：同位语，解释Till Von Wachter
- that引导宾语从句，从句主干：**not all people graduating into a recession see their life chances dimmed**
  - `graduating into a recession`：现在分词作后置定语，修饰people
- 冒号后两个并列分句，解释not all：
  1. `those with degrees from elite universities catch up fairly quickly to where they otherwise would have been if they had graduated in better times`
     - `where they otherwise would have been`：where宾语从句
     - `if they had graduated in better times`：if虚拟条件句（对过去的虚拟）
  2. `it is the masses beneath them that are left behind`：强调句（it is…that…）

### 生词

graduate into a recession：在经济衰退期毕业；dim：使暗淡；elite university：精英大学；catch up to：赶上；otherwise：否则；masses：大众；beneath：在…之下；leave behind：落后

### 翻译

哥伦比亚大学经济学家蒂尔·冯·瓦赫特的研究表明，并非所有在经济衰退期毕业的人都认为自己的人生机会变得暗淡：那些拥有精英大学学位的人能相当快地赶上他们如果在更好时期毕业本应达到的水平；而被甩在后面的是他们之下的大众。

### 考点

**宾语从句+冒号后并列解释+虚拟语气+强调句**多层结构；39题D选项依据——精英大学毕业生比其他人恢复更快。

## 36. By saying "to find silver linings" (Para.2) the author suggests that the jobless try to ______.

[A] seek subsidies from the government [B] explore reasons for the unemployment [C] make profits from the troubled economy [D] look on the bright side of the recession

✅答案：**D** 定位：第二段。silver lining是习语，指"黑暗中的一线希望"。后文说失业虽然痛苦但让人们有所提升（不那么物质、更谨慎、更体谅他人），说明失业者在努力看到经济衰退的积极面。

- A：错误。seek subsidies from the government（向政府寻求补贴）无中生有。
- B：错误。explore reasons for the unemployment（探索失业原因）无中生有。
- C：错误。make profits from the troubled economy（从困境经济中获利）曲解原文。人们是在精神层面有所提升，不是物质上获利。
- D：正确。看到经济衰退的光明面。silver lining = bright side，后文的improved them、better off都是积极面。

## 37. According to Paragraph 2, the recession has made people ______.

[A] realize the national dream [B] struggle against each other [C] challenge their prudence [D] reconsider their lifestyle

✅答案：**D** 定位：第二段。人们变得less materialistic and more financially prudent（不那么物质、更财务谨慎），从"轻松致富、大房子"的狂热梦中醒来，结束鲁莽消费时代。这些都是生活方式的重新考虑。

- A：错误。realize the national dream（实现国家梦）与原文相反。原文说从国家狂热梦中"醒来"，不是实现梦想。
- B：错误。struggle against each other（互相争斗）偷换概念。原文说more aware of the struggles of others（更能体会他人的挣扎），不是互相争斗。
- C：错误。challenge their prudence（挑战他们的谨慎）与原文相反。原文说人们变得more prudent（更谨慎），不是挑战谨慎。
- D：正确。重新考虑他们的生活方式。不那么物质、更谨慎、结束鲁莽消费=重新考虑生活方式。

## 38. Benjamin Friedman believes that economic recessions may ______.

[A] impose a heavier burden on immigrants [B] bring out more evils of human nature [C] promote the advance of rights and freedoms [D] ease conflicts between races and classes

✅答案：**B** 定位：第三段。Friedman认为长期经济停滞或衰退让社会more mean-spirited and less inclusive（更心胸狭窄、更不包容），阻止或逆转权利自由的进步，反移民情绪和种族阶级冲突增加。这些都是人性中邪恶面的展现。

- A：错误。impose a heavier burden on immigrants（给移民施加更重负担）偷换概念。原文说anti-immigrant sentiment increases（反移民情绪增加），不是给移民加重负担。
- B：正确。展现人性中更多的邪恶面。mean-spirited（心胸狭窄）、less inclusive（不包容）、冲突增加=人性邪恶面。
- C：错误。promote the advance of rights and freedoms（促进权利自由进步）与原文相反。原文说stopped or reversed the advance（阻止或逆转进步）。
- D：错误。ease conflicts between races and classes（缓解种族阶级冲突）与原文相反。原文说conflict increases（冲突增加）。

## 39. The research of Till Von Wachter suggests that in recession graduates from elite universities tend to ______.

[A] lag behind the others due to decreased opportunities [B] catch up quickly with experienced employees [C] see their life chances as dimmed as the others' [D] recover more quickly than the others

✅答案：**D** 定位：第四段。精英大学毕业生catch up fairly quickly to where they otherwise would have been（相当快地赶上本应达到的水平），而the masses beneath them are left behind（之下的大众被甩在后面）。说明精英大学毕业生比其他人恢复更快。

- A：错误。lag behind the others（落后于其他人）与原文相反。精英大学毕业生是赶上，不是落后。
- B：错误。catch up quickly with experienced employees（快速赶上有经验的员工）偷换概念。原文说赶上"他们如果在好时期毕业本应达到的水平"，不是赶上有经验的员工。
- C：错误。see their life chances as dimmed as the others'（认为人生机会和其他人一样暗淡）与原文相反。原文说not all people see their life chances dimmed，精英大学毕业生不觉得暗淡。
- D：正确。比其他人恢复更快。精英大学毕业生快速赶上，大众被甩在后面→精英恢复更快。

## 40. The author thinks that the influence of hard times on society is ______.

[A] certain [B] positive [C] trivial [D] destructive

✅答案：**A** 定位：最后一段 `But they certainly will reshape it, and all the more so the longer they extend` + 第一段 `it is likely to reshape our politics, our culture, and the character of our society for years`。作者认为艰难时期肯定会重塑社会，影响是确定的，但具体是好是坏还需观察。

- A：正确。确定的。certainly will reshape = certain influence。
- B：错误。positive（积极的）。作者说好处thin、uncertain，社会更mean-spirited，不是单纯积极。
- C：错误。trivial（微不足道的）与原文相反。作者说会重塑政治、文化、社会特征，影响巨大。
- D：错误。destructive（破坏性的）过于绝对。作者说影响是确定的，但有好有坏，不是单纯破坏性。

### 本篇命题规律小结（英二阅读）

1. 社会影响类文章：关注**"有一定好处→但负面影响更大→影响是确定的"**的论证结构
2. 习语理解题（silver lining）：结合**后文解释**判断含义，不选字面意思
3. 人物观点题：定位到**人名+argues/believes/suggests**，答案是观点的同义替换
4. 研究结论题：注意**not all…**的部分否定，以及冒号后的对比解释
5. 态度题：如果作者说"certainly will reshape"但不说好坏，选certain（确定的），不选positive或destructive



Over the past decade, many companies had perfected the art of creating automatic behaviors 自动行为—habits—among consumers. These habits have helped companies earn billions of dollars when customers eat snacks or wipe counters 擦柜台 almost without thinking, often in response to 响应 a carefully designed set of daily cues 日常暗示.

"There are fundamental public health problems, like dirty hands instead of a soap habit 用肥皂的习惯, that remain killers only because we can't figure out 弄清楚 how to change people's habits," said Dr. Curtis, the director of the Hygiene Center 卫生中心 at the London School of Hygiene & Tropical Medicine 伦敦卫生与热带医学院. "We wanted to learn from private industry 私营企业 how to create new behaviors that happen automatically."

The companies that Dr. Curtis turned to—Procter & Gamble 宝洁, Colgate-Palmolive 高露洁棕榄 and Unilever 联合利华—had invested hundreds of millions of dollars finding the subtle cues 微妙暗示 in consumers' lives that corporations could use to introduce new routines 新惯例.

If you look hard enough, you'll find that many of the products we use every day—chewing gums 口香糖, skin moisturizers 润肤霜, disinfecting wipes 消毒湿巾, air fresheners 空气清新剂, water purifiers 净水器, health snacks 健康零食, teeth whiteners 牙齿美白剂, fabric softeners 衣物柔顺剂, vitamins—are results of manufactured habits 人造习惯. A century ago, few people regularly brushed their teeth multiple times a day 一天多次. Today, because of shrewd advertising 精明的广告 and public health campaigns 公共卫生运动, many Americans habitually give their pearly whites 珍珠般洁白的牙齿 a cavity-preventing scrub 防蛀牙擦洗 twice a day, often with Colgate, Crest or one of the other brands.

A few decades ago, many people didn't drink water outside of a meal. Then beverage companies 饮料公司 started bottling the production of far-off springs 遥远泉水, and now office workers unthinkingly 不假思索地 sip bottled water 啜饮瓶装水 all day long. Chewing gum, once bought primarily by adolescent boys 青春期男孩, is now featured in commercials 广告中主打 as a breath freshener 口气清新剂 and teeth cleanser 牙齿清洁剂 for use after a meal. Skin moisturizers are advertised as part of morning beauty rituals 晨间美容仪式, slipped in between hair brushing and putting on makeup 化妆.

"Our products succeed when they become part of daily or weekly patterns," said Carol Berning, a consumer psychologist 消费心理学家 who recently retired from Procter & Gamble, the company that sold $76 billion of Tide, Crest and other products last year. "Creating positive habits is a huge part of improving our consumers' lives, and it's essential to 对…至关重要 making new products commercially viable 商业上可行."

Through experiments and observation, social scientists like Dr. Berning have learned that there is power in tying certain behaviors to habitual cues through ruthless advertising 无情的广告. As this new science of habit has emerged, controversies 争议 have erupted when the tactics 策略 have been used to sell questionable beauty creams 有问题的美容霜 or unhealthy foods.

## 35. The author's attitude toward the influence of advertisement on people's habits is ______.

### 本篇命题规律小结（英二阅读）

1. 商业/社会类文章：关注**现象引入（企业完善创造消费者习惯的艺术，帮助赚数十亿美元，精心设计日常暗示）→公共卫生视角（柯蒂斯博士：脏手等问题仍是杀手因为弄不清如何改变习惯，想向企业学习创造自动行为）→企业投入（宝洁、高露洁、联合利华投资数亿美元寻找微妙暗示引入新惯例）→产品例证（许多日常产品都是人造习惯结果：一个世纪前很少人多次刷牙，如今广告和公共卫生运动使美国人一天两次用高露洁佳洁士；瓶装水整天啜饮；口香糖饭后口气清新；润肤霜晨间美容仪式）→专家观点（伯宁：产品成为日常模式一部分就成功，创造积极习惯改善生活且对新产品商业可行至关重要）→争议（通过无情广告将行为与习惯暗示联系有力量；新习惯科学出现，策略被用来销售有问题美容霜或不健康食品时争议爆发）**的结构
2. 观点题：定位到**fundamental public health problems+remain killers+learn how to create**（dirty hands instead of soap habit remain killers + can't figure out how to change + learn how to create new behaviors = should be further cultivated）
3. 例证题：定位到**unthinkingly sip+featured in commercials+advertised as part of**（office workers unthinkingly sip bottled water + chewing gum as breath freshener + moisturizers as morning beauty ritual = reveal their impact on people's habits）
4. 细节题（NOT）：注意**公司vs产品**（Unilever是公司，Tide/Crest/Colgate是产品）
5. 细节题：定位到**because of shrewd advertising+through ruthless advertising**（because of shrewd advertising and public health campaigns + through ruthless advertising = commercial promotions）
6. 态度题：定位到**ruthless advertising+controversies erupted+questionable/unhealthy**（ruthless advertising + controversies erupted + questionable beauty creams or unhealthy foods = negative）

Every Saturday morning, at 9 am, more than 50,000 runners set off 出发 to run 5km around their local park. The Parkrun phenomenon 现象 began with a dozen friends and has inspired 激发 400 events in the UK and more abroad. Events are free, staffed 配备人员 by thousands of volunteers 志愿者. Runners range from four years old to grandparents; their times range from Andrew Baddeley's world record 13 minutes 48 seconds up to an hour.

Parkrun is succeeding where London's Olympic "legacy 遗产" is failing. Ten years ago on Monday, it was announced that the Games of the 30th Olympiad 奥运会 would be in London. Planning documents pledged 承诺 that the great legacy of the Games would be to lever 撬动 a nation of sport lovers away from their couches 沙发. The population would be fitter, healthier and produce more winners. It has not happened. The number of adults doing weekly sport did rise, by nearly 2 million in the run-up to 2012—but the general population was growing faster. Worse, the numbers are now falling at an accelerating rate 加速率. The opposition claims primary school pupils doing at least two hours of sport a week have nearly halved 减半. Obesity 肥胖 has risen among adults and children. Official retrospections 反思 continue as to why London 2012 failed to "inspire a generation." The success of Parkrun offers answers.

Parkrun is not a race but a time trial 计时赛: Your only competitor is the clock. The ethos 精神 welcomes anybody. There is as much joy over a puffed-out 气喘吁吁的 first-timer being clapped 鼓掌 over the line as there is about top talent shining. The Olympic bidders 申办者, by contrast, wanted to get more people doing sports and to produce more elite 精英 athletes. The dual aim 双重目标 was mixed up: The stress on success over taking part was intimidating 令人生畏的 for newcomers.

Indeed, there is something a little absurd 荒谬的 in the state getting involved in the planning of such a fundamentally "grassroots 草根" concept as community sports associations. If there is a role for government, it should really be getting involved in providing common goods 公共物品—making sure there is space for playing fields and the money to pave 铺设 tennis and netball courts, and encouraging the provision 提供 of all these activities in schools. But successive governments 历届政府 have presided over 主导 selling green spaces, squeezing money from local authorities and declining attention on sport in education. Instead of wordy, worthy strategies 冗长的、有价值的策略, future governments need to do more to provide the conditions for sport to thrive 繁荣. Or at least not make them worse.

# 2017英二 Text1 长难句精读（原文+拆分+语法+翻译+考点）

## 长难句1

> The Parkrun phenomenon began with a dozen friends and has inspired 400 events in the UK and more abroad.

### 句子拆分

主干：**The Parkrun phenomenon began with a dozen friends and has inspired 400 events**
- `began with a dozen friends`和`has inspired 400 events`是and连接的两个并列谓语
- `in the UK and more abroad`：地点状语

### 生词

phenomenon：现象；begin with：始于；a dozen：一打、12个；inspire：激发、催生；event：活动；abroad：在国外

### 翻译

Parkrun现象始于十几个朋友，已经在英国催生了400场活动，在国外更多。

### 考点

**and连接两个并列谓语**；21题C选项的依据——从十几个朋友发展到400场活动+国外更多，说明Parkrun获得了极大欢迎（gained great popularity）。

## 长难句2

> Runners range from four years old to grandparents; their times range from Andrew Baddeley's world record 13 minutes 48 seconds up to an hour.

### 句子拆分

并列句，分号连接
分句1主干：**Runners range from four years old to grandparents**
分句2主干：**their times range from Andrew Baddeley's world record 13 minutes 48 seconds up to an hour**

### 生词

range from … to …：从…到…不等；world record：世界纪录；up to：多达

### 翻译

跑步者从4岁到祖父母辈都有；他们的成绩从安德鲁·巴德利的世界纪录13分48秒到一小时不等。

### 考点

**分号连接并列句+range from…to…结构**；进一步说明Parkrun的受欢迎程度——参与人群广泛，成绩差异大。

## 长难句4

主干：**Planning documents pledged that …**

planning document：规划文件；pledge：承诺；legacy：遗产；lever：撬动、杠杆作用；a nation of sport lovers：一个体育爱好者之国；couch：沙发

### 考点

## 长难句5

> The number of adults doing weekly sport did rise, by nearly 2 million in the run-up to 2012—but the general population was growing faster.

### 句子拆分

并列句，but连接
分句1主干：**The number of adults doing weekly sport did rise**
- `doing weekly sport`：现在分词作后置定语，修饰adults
- `did rise`：did强调谓语
- `by nearly 2 million in the run-up to 2012`：状语
分句2主干：**the general population was growing faster**

### 生词

weekly sport：每周运动；did rise：确实上升了（强调）；run-up to：…之前的准备阶段；general population：总人口；grow faster：增长更快

### 翻译

每周运动的成年人数量确实上升了，在2012年之前增加了近200万——但总人口增长更快。

### 考点

**现在分词后置定语+did强调谓语+but转折并列句**；说明奥运遗产的失败——运动人数虽然增加，但总人口增长更快，且后来运动人数在下降。

## 长难句6

> There is as much joy over a puffed-out first-timer being clapped over the line as there is about top talent shining.

### 句子拆分

主干：**There is as much joy over a puffed-out first-timer as there is about top talent**
- `as much … as …`：同级比较
- `being clapped over the line`：现在分词被动式作后置定语，修饰first-timer
- `shining`：现在分词作后置定语，修饰top talent

### 生词

joy：喜悦；puffed-out：气喘吁吁的；first-timer：第一次参加的人；clap：鼓掌；over the line：越过终点线；top talent：顶尖人才；shine：闪耀、表现出色

### 翻译

一个气喘吁吁的第一次参加者在掌声中越过终点线，所带来的喜悦与顶尖人才闪耀所带来的喜悦一样多。

### 考点

**as much…as…同级比较+现在分词被动式后置定语+现在分词后置定语**；23题A选项的依据——Parkrun欢迎任何人，不强调精英主义（does not emphasize elitism）。

## 长难句7

> The dual aim was mixed up: The stress on success over taking part was intimidating for newcomers.

### 句子拆分

主干：**The dual aim was mixed up**
- 冒号后解释：`The stress on success over taking part was intimidating for newcomers`
  - `on success over taking part`：介词短语修饰stress

### 生词

dual aim：双重目标；mix up：混淆、弄混；stress on：对…的强调；success over taking part：成功重于参与；intimidating：令人生畏的；newcomer：新来者、新手

### 翻译

双重目标被混淆了：对成功重于参与的强调令新手望而生畏。

### 考点

**冒号解释+stress on结构**；奥运遗产失败的原因——双重目标混淆，强调成功而非参与，吓退了新手。

## 长难句8

> If there is a role for government, it should really be getting involved in providing common goods—making sure there is space for playing fields and the money to pave tennis and netball courts, and encouraging the provision of all these activities in schools.

### 句子拆分

主干：**it should really be getting involved in providing common goods**
- `If there is a role for government`：if条件状语从句
- 破折号后解释common goods：`making sure there is space for playing fields and the money to pave tennis and netball courts, and encouraging the provision of all these activities in schools`
  - `making sure …`和`encouraging …`是and连接的两个现在分词并列
  - `there is space for playing fields and the money to pave tennis and netball courts`：省略that的宾语从句，作making sure的宾语

### 生词

role：角色；get involved in：参与；provide common goods：提供公共物品；make sure：确保；playing field：运动场；pave：铺设；tennis court：网球场；netball court：无挡板篮球场；encourage：鼓励；provision：提供

### 翻译

如果政府有角色的话，它真的应该参与提供公共物品——确保有运动场的空间和铺设网球场、无挡板篮球场的资金，并鼓励在学校提供所有这些活动。

### 考点

**if条件状语从句+破折号解释+两个现在分词并列+省略that的宾语从句**；24题B选项的依据——政府应该投资公共体育设施（invest in public sports facilities = provide common goods: playing fields, tennis and netball courts）。

## 长难句10

> Instead of wordy, worthy strategies, future governments need to do more to provide the conditions for sport to thrive. Or at least not make them worse.

### 句子拆分

句1主干：**future governments need to do more to provide the conditions for sport to thrive**

- `to provide the conditions for sport to thrive`：不定式作目的状语

# 2017 英二 Text1 21–25｜题目+选项+逐选项解析

> 原文主题：Parkrun公园跑大受欢迎，而伦敦奥运遗产未能促进大众体育参与；作者批评历届政府在体育方面的做法，呼吁政府投资公共体育设施

## 21. According to Paragraph 1, Parkrun has ______.

[A] created many jobs [B] become an official festival [C] gained great popularity [D] strengthened community ties

✅答案：**C** 定位：第一段。`The Parkrun phenomenon began with a dozen friends and has inspired 400 events in the UK and more abroad`（Parkrun现象始于十几个朋友，已经在英国催生了400场活动，在国外更多）+ `Runners range from four years old to grandparents`（跑步者从4岁到祖父母辈都有）。说明Parkrun获得了极大欢迎。

- A：错误。created many jobs（创造了许多就业）无中生有。原文说活动免费、由志愿者配备人员，没有说创造就业。
- B：错误。become an official festival（成为官方节日）无中生有。原文说是phenomenon（现象），不是官方节日。
- C：正确。获得了极大欢迎。从十几个朋友发展到400场活动+国外更多，参与人群广泛=gained great popularity。
- D：错误。strengthened community ties（加强了社区联系）过度推断。原文说在当地公园跑步，但没有明确说加强社区联系。

## 22. The author believes that London's Olympic "Legacy" has failed to ______.

[A] boost population growth [B] improve the city's image [C] promote sport participation [D] increase sport hours in schools

✅答案：**C** 定位：第二段。`Parkrun is succeeding where London's Olympic "legacy" is failing`（Parkrun在伦敦奥运"遗产"失败的地方取得了成功）+ 奥运目标是`lever a nation of sport lovers away from their couches`（撬动体育爱好者之国离开沙发），但`It has not happened`（这没有发生）+ 运动人数在下降。说明奥运遗产未能促进体育参与。

- A：错误。boost population growth（促进人口增长）偷换概念。原文说总人口增长更快，不是奥运遗产促进人口增长。
- B：错误。improve the city's image（改善城市形象）无中生有。
- C：正确。促进体育参与。目标是让国民离开沙发去运动，但没有发生=failed to promote sport participation。
- D：错误。increase sport hours in schools（增加学校体育时间）以偏概全。原文说小学生每周运动时间减半，但这只是奥运遗产失败的一个方面，不是全部。

## 23. Parkrun is different from Olympic games in that it ______.

[A] does not emphasize elitism [B] does not attract first-timers [C] aims at discovering talents [D] focuses on mass competition

✅答案：**A** 定位：第三段。`Parkrun is not a race but a time trial: Your only competitor is the clock. The ethos welcomes anybody`（Parkrun不是比赛而是计时赛：你唯一的竞争对手是时钟。其精神欢迎任何人）+ `There is as much joy over a puffed-out first-timer … as there is about top talent shining`（第一次参加者的喜悦与顶尖人才的喜悦一样多）。而奥运`wanted to … produce more elite athletes`（想要培养更多精英运动员）。说明Parkrun不强调精英主义。

- A：正确。不强调精英主义。welcomes anybody + joy over first-timer as much as top talent = does not emphasize elitism。
- B：错误。does not attract first-timers（不吸引第一次参加者）与原文相反。原文说first-timer被鼓掌越过终点线，说明吸引第一次参加者。
- C：错误。aims at discovering talents（旨在发现人才）与原文相反。这是奥运的目标，不是Parkrun的。
- D：错误。focuses on mass competition（专注于大众竞争）偷换概念。Parkrun是time trial（计时赛），不是竞争（competition），唯一竞争对手是时钟。

## 24. With regard to mass sports, the author holds that government should ______.

[A] increase funds for sport clubs [B] invest in public sports facilities [C] supervise local sports associations [D] organize "grassroots" sports events

✅答案：**B** 定位：第四段。`If there is a role for government, it should really be getting involved in providing common goods—making sure there is space for playing fields and the money to pave tennis and netball courts`（如果政府有角色，它应该参与提供公共物品——确保有运动场空间和铺设网球场、无挡板篮球场的资金）。说明政府应该投资公共体育设施。

- A：错误。increase funds for sport clubs（增加体育俱乐部资金）无中生有。原文说提供公共物品，不是给体育俱乐部资金。
- B：正确。投资公共体育设施。provide common goods: playing fields, tennis and netball courts = invest in public sports facilities。
- C：错误。supervise local sports associations（监督地方体育协会）无中生有。原文说政府不应参与规划"草根"社区体育协会，不是监督。
- D：错误。organize "grassroots" sports events（组织"草根"体育活动）与原文相反。原文说`there is something a little absurd in the state getting involved in the planning of such a fundamentally "grassroots" concept`（国家参与规划这种根本上"草根"的概念有点荒谬），说明政府不应组织草根体育活动。

## 25. The author's attitude to what U.K. governments have done for sports is ______.

[A] critical [B] tolerant [C] uncertain [D] sympathetic

✅答案：**A** 态度题。定位：第四段。`But successive governments have presided over selling green spaces, squeezing money from local authorities and declining attention on sport in education`（但历届政府主导了出售绿地、从地方当局榨取资金以及减少对教育中体育的关注）+ `Instead of wordy, worthy strategies, future governments need to do more … Or at least not make them worse`（未来政府需要做更多…或者至少不要让它们更糟）。作者批评历届政府的做法，态度是批判的。

- A：正确。批判的。批评政府出售绿地、榨取资金、减少体育关注，要求未来政府做更多=critical。
- B：错误。宽容的。作者明确批评，不是宽容。
- C：错误。不确定的。作者态度明确，不是不确定。
- D：错误。同情的。作者批评政府，不是同情。

### 本篇命题规律小结（英二阅读）

1. 社会/体育类文章：关注**现象描述→对比（成功vs失败）→原因分析→政府角色→作者态度**的结构
2. 细节题：定位到**数字和范围**（400 events, from 4 years old to grandparents）
3. 对比题：定位到**where对比句**和**by contrast**（Parkrun vs Olympic）
4. 观点题：定位到**if条件句中的should**（government should provide common goods）
5. 态度题：通过**负面动词短语**判断（presided over selling, squeezing, declining attention）

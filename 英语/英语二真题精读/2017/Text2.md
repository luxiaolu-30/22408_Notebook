With so much focus on Children's use of screen 屏幕, it's easy for parents to forget about their own screen use. "Tech is designed to really suck you in 吸引你," says Jenny Radesky in her study of digital play 数字游戏, "and digital products are there to promote maximal engagement 最大程度的参与. It makes it hard to disengage 脱离, and leads to a lot of bleed-over 渗透 into the family routine 日常生活."

Radesky has studied the use of mobile phones and tablets 平板电脑 at mealtimes by giving mother-child pairs a food-testing exercise 食物测试活动. She found that mothers who used devices 设备 during the exercise started 20 percent fewer verbal 言语的 and 39 percent fewer nonverbal interactions 互动 with their children. During a separate observation of 55 caregivers 看护人 eating with one child or more, she saw that phones became a source of tension 紧张 in the family. Parents would be looking at their emails while the children would be making excited bids 努力争取 for their attention.

Infants 婴儿 are wired 天生的 to look at parents' faces to try to understand their world, and if those faces are blank 空白的 and unresponsive 无反应的—as they often are when absorbed in a device—it can be extremely disconcerting 令人不安的 for the children. Radesky cites 引用 the "still face experiment 静止面孔实验", which was devised 设计 by developmental psychologist 发展心理学家 Ed Tronick in the 1970s. In it, a mother is asked to interact with her child in a normal way before putting on a blank expression and not giving them any visual social feedback 视觉社交反馈. As the video shows, the child becomes increasingly distressed 痛苦的 as she tries to capture her mother's attention. "Parents don't have to be exquisitely 精致地 present at all times, but there needs to be a balance and parents need to be responsive and sensitive 敏感的 to a child's verbal or nonverbal expressions of an emotional need," says Radesky.

On the other hand, Tronick himself is concerned that the worries about kids' use of screens are born out of an "oppressive ideology 压迫性意识形态 that demands that parents should always be interacting" with their children. "It's based on a somewhat fantasised 幻想的, very white, very upper-middle-class ideology that says if you're failing to expose your child to 30,000 words you are neglecting 忽视 them." Tronick believes that just because a child isn't learning from the screen doesn't mean there's no value to it—particularly if it gives parents time to have a shower, do housework or simply have a break from their child. Parents, he says, can get a lot out of using their devices to speak to friends or get some work out of the way 完成工作. This can make them feel happier, which they can be more available 有空的 to their child the rest of the time.

# 2017英二 Text2 长难句精读（原文+拆分+语法+翻译+考点）

## 长难句1

> "Tech is designed to really suck you in," says Jenny Radesky in her study of digital play, "and digital products are there to promote maximal engagement."

### 句子拆分

直接引语，and连接两个并列分句
分句1：**Tech is designed to really suck you in**
- 被动语态，to really suck you in作目的状语
分句2：**digital products are there to promote maximal engagement**
- to promote maximal engagement作目的状语
- `says Jenny Radesky in her study of digital play`：倒装引语

### 生词

tech：技术；be designed to：被设计用来；suck sb in：吸引某人、使某人入迷；digital play：数字游戏；digital product：数字产品；promote：促进；maximal：最大程度的；engagement：参与、投入

### 翻译

"技术被设计用来真正吸引你，"珍妮·拉德斯基在她的数字游戏研究中说，"数字产品的存在是为了促进最大程度的参与。"

### 考点

**and连接并列分句+被动语态+不定式目的状语+倒装引语**；26题A选项的依据——数字产品被设计用来吸引用户注意力（suck you in = absorb user attention）。

## 长难句5

> Infants are wired to look at parents' faces to try to understand their world, and if those faces are blank and unresponsive—as they often are when absorbed in a device—it can be extremely disconcerting for the children.

### 句子拆分

并列句，and连接
分句1主干：**Infants are wired to look at parents' faces to try to understand their world**
- `to look at parents' faces`：不定式作主语补足语（be wired to do）
- `to try to understand their world`：不定式作目的状语
分句2主干：**it can be extremely disconcerting for the children**
- `if those faces are blank and unresponsive`：if条件状语从句
- `—as they often are when absorbed in a device—`：插入语，as方式状语从句
  - `when absorbed in a device`：when时间状语从句（省略they are）

### 生词

infant：婴儿；be wired to：天生就会、本能地；blank：空白的、无表情的；unresponsive：无反应的；absorbed in：全神贯注于；extremely：极其；disconcerting：令人不安的

### 翻译

婴儿天生就会看父母的脸来试图理解他们的世界，如果那些脸是空白的、无反应的——就像他们全神贯注于设备时经常那样——这对孩子来说可能极其令人不安。

### 考点

**and连接并列句+不定式目的状语+if条件状语从句+as方式状语从句+when省略从句**；28题B选项的依据——父母需要回应孩子的情感需求（如果父母无反应，孩子会不安）。

## 长难句10

> Tronick believes that just because a child isn't learning from the screen doesn't mean there's no value to it—particularly if it gives parents time to have a shower, do housework or simply have a break from their child.

### 句子拆分

# 2017 英二 Text2 26–30｜题目+选项+逐选项解析

> 原文主题：父母使用数字产品会减少亲子互动，对孩子产生负面影响；但特罗尼克认为屏幕也有价值，可以给父母时间休息，从而更好地陪伴孩子

## 26. According to Jenny Radesky, digital products are designed to ______.

[A] absorb user attention [B] increase work efficiency [C] simplify routine matters [D] better interpersonal relations

✅答案：**A** 定位：第一段。`"Tech is designed to really suck you in," … "and digital products are there to promote maximal engagement."`（技术被设计用来真正吸引你，数字产品的存在是为了促进最大程度的参与）。suck you in = absorb user attention。

- A：正确。吸引用户注意力。suck you in + maximal engagement = absorb user attention。
- B：错误。increase work efficiency（提高工作效率）无中生有。
- C：错误。simplify routine matters（简化日常事务）无中生有。原文说bleed-over into family routine（渗透到家庭日常），不是简化。
- D：错误。better interpersonal relations（改善人际关系）与原文相反。数字产品减少了亲子互动，不是改善人际关系。

## 27. Radesky's food-testing exercise shows that mothers' use of devices ______.

[A] takes away babies' appetite [B] distracts children's attention [C] reduces mother-child communication [D] slows down babies' verbal development

✅答案：**C** 定位：第二段。`mothers who used devices during the exercise started 20 percent fewer verbal and 39 percent fewer nonverbal interactions with their children`（使用设备的母亲与孩子的言语互动减少了20%，非言语互动减少了39%）。fewer verbal and nonverbal interactions = reduces mother-child communication。

- A：错误。takes away babies' appetite（夺走婴儿食欲）无中生有。实验是food-testing，但没有说影响食欲。
- B：错误。distracts children's attention（分散孩子注意力）偷换概念。原文说孩子争取父母注意力，不是孩子注意力被分散。
- C：正确。减少母子交流。20% fewer verbal + 39% fewer nonverbal interactions = reduces mother-child communication。
- D：错误。slows down babies' verbal development（减缓婴儿言语发展）过度推断。言语互动减少不等于言语发展减缓。

## 28. Radesky cites the "still face experiment" to show that ______.

[A] it is easy for children to get used to blank expressions [B] parents need to respond to children's emotional needs [C] verbal expressions are unnecessary for emotional exchange [D] children are insensitive to changes in their parents' mood

✅答案：**B** 例证题。定位：第三段。例子前的观点：`Infants are wired to look at parents' faces to try to understand their world, and if those faces are blank and unresponsive … it can be extremely disconcerting for the children`（婴儿天生看父母的脸来理解世界，如果父母面无表情、无反应，孩子会极其不安）。例子后的总结：`parents need to be responsive and sensitive to a child's verbal or nonverbal expressions of an emotional need`（父母需要对孩子的情感需求表达做出回应和保持敏感）。说明父母需要回应孩子的情感需求。

- A：错误。it is easy for children to get used to blank expressions（孩子容易习惯空白表情）与原文相反。实验中孩子变得越来越痛苦，不是习惯。
- B：正确。父母需要回应孩子的情感需求。例子说明父母无反应时孩子不安，总结说父母需要responsive and sensitive to emotional need。
- C：错误。verbal expressions are unnecessary for emotional exchange（情感交流不需要言语表达）无中生有。
- D：错误。children are insensitive to changes in their parents' mood（孩子对父母情绪变化不敏感）与原文相反。孩子对父母的空白表情反应强烈，说明敏感。

## 29. The oppressive ideology mentioned by Tronick requires parents to ______.

[A] protect kids from exposure to wild fantasies [B] teach their kids at least 30,000 words a year [C] remain constant about kids' use of screens [D] ensure constant interaction with their children

✅答案：**D** 定位：第四段。`an "oppressive ideology that demands that parents should always be interacting" with their children`（一种"压迫性意识形态，要求父母应该始终与孩子互动"）。parents should always be interacting = ensure constant interaction with their children。

- A：错误。protect kids from exposure to wild fantasies（保护孩子不接触疯狂幻想）偷换概念。原文说ideology是somewhat fantasised（有点幻想的），不是保护孩子不接触幻想。
- B：错误。teach their kids at least 30,000 words a year（每年教孩子至少30000个词）偷换概念。原文说if you're failing to expose your child to 30,000 words you are neglecting them（如果没能让孩子接触30000个词就是忽视），这是意识形态的观点，不是要求每年教。
- C：错误。remain constant about kids' use of screens（对孩子使用屏幕保持不变）无中生有。
- D：正确。确保与孩子持续互动。parents should always be interacting = ensure constant interaction with their children。

## 30. According to Tronick, kids' use of screens may ______.

[A] make their parents more creative [B] give their parents more free time [C] help them with their homework [D] help them become more attentive

✅答案：**B** 定位：第四段。`just because a child isn't learning from the screen doesn't mean there's no value to it—particularly if it gives parents time to have a shower, do housework or simply have a break from their child`（仅仅因为孩子没从屏幕学到东西并不意味着屏幕没有价值——特别是如果它给父母时间洗澡、做家务或只是从孩子身边休息一下）。说明孩子使用屏幕可能给父母更多自由时间。

- A：错误。make their parents more creative（让父母更有创造力）无中生有。
- B：正确。给父母更多自由时间。gives parents time to have a shower, do housework or have a break = give their parents more free time。
- C：错误。help them with their homework（帮助他们做作业）无中生有。
- D：错误。help them become more attentive（帮助他们变得更专注）无中生有。

### 本篇命题规律小结（英二阅读）

1. 家庭教育/科技类文章：关注**问题提出→实验证据→实验说明→反面观点→辩证结论**的结构
2. 人物观点题：定位到**直接引语**（Tech is designed to suck you in）
3. 实验结果题：定位到**实验数据**（20% fewer verbal, 39% fewer nonverbal interactions）
4. 例证题：答案在**例子前后的观点句**（Infants are wired to look at parents' faces… / parents need to be responsive…）
5. 常见干扰：偷换概念（30000 words → teach 30000 words a year）、与原文相反（get used to blank expressions → become distressed）、过度推断（fewer interactions → slows down verbal development）

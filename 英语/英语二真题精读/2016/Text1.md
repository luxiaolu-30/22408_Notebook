

It's true that high-school coding 编程 classes aren't essential 必要的 for learning computer science in college. Students without experience can catch up 赶上 after a few introductory courses 入门课程, said Tom Cortina, the assistant dean 副院长 at Carnegie Mellon's School of Computer Science.

However, Cortina said, early exposure 早期接触 is beneficial. When younger kids learn computer science, they learn that it's not just a confusing, endless string 一连串 of letters and numbers — but a tool to build apps 应用程序, or create artwork 艺术品, or test hypotheses 验证假设. It's not as hard for them to transform 改变 their thought processes 思维过程 as it is for older students. Breaking down problems into bite-sized chunks 小块 and using code to solve them becomes normal. Giving more children this training could increase the number of people interested in the field and help fill the jobs gap 职位缺口, Cortina said.

Students also benefit from learning something about coding before they get to college, where introductory computer-science classes are packed to the brim 挤得满满的, which can drive the less-experienced or -determined students away 赶走.

The Flatiron School, where people pay to learn programming, started as one of the many coding bootcamps 编程训练营 that's become popular for adults looking for a career change 职业转变. The high-schoolers get the same curriculum 课程, but "we try to gear 调整 lessons toward things they're interested in," said Victoria Friedman, an instructor 讲师. For instance, one of the apps the students are developing suggests movies based on your mood 情绪.

The students in the Flatiron class probably won't drop out of high school and build the next Facebook. Programming languages have a quick turnover 更新快, so the "Ruby on Rails" language they learned may not even be relevant 相关的 by the time they enter the job market. But the skills they learn — how to think logically through a problem and organize the results — apply to 适用于 any coding language, said Deborah Seehorn, an education consultant 教育顾问 for the state of North Carolina.

Indeed, the Flatiron students might not go into IT at all. But creating a future army of coders 程序员大军 is not the sole purpose 唯一目的 of the classes. These kids are going to be surrounded by computers — in their pockets, in their offices, in their homes — for the rest of their lives. The younger they learn how computers think, how to coax 哄劝 the machine into producing what they want — the earlier they learn that they have the power to do that — the better.

# 2016英二 Text1 长难句精读（原文+拆分+语法+翻译+考点）

## 长难句1

> It's true that high-school coding classes aren't essential for learning computer science in college.

### 句子拆分

主干：**It's true that …**
- it是形式主语，真正主语是that引导的主语从句：`that high-school coding classes aren't essential for learning computer science in college`

### 生词

coding：编程；essential：必要的、必不可少的；computer science：计算机科学

### 翻译

的确，高中编程课对于在大学学习计算机科学来说并不是必不可少的。

### 考点

**it作形式主语+that主语从句**；开篇让步——高中编程课不是必需的，为后文转折（但早期接触有益）做铺垫。

---

## 长难句2

> When younger kids learn computer science, they learn that it's not just a confusing, endless string of letters and numbers — but a tool to build apps, or create artwork, or test hypotheses.

### 句子拆分

主干：**they learn that …**
- `When younger kids learn computer science`：when时间状语从句
- that引导宾语从句，从句主干：**it's not just a confusing, endless string of letters and numbers — but a tool**
  - `not just … but …`：不仅…而且…
  - `to build apps, or create artwork, or test hypotheses`：三个并列不定式作后置定语，修饰tool

### 生词

confusing：令人困惑的；endless：无尽的；string：一连串；letters and numbers：字母和数字；app：应用程序；artwork：艺术品；test hypotheses：验证假设

### 翻译

当更小的孩子学习计算机科学时，他们了解到它不仅仅是一串令人困惑的、无尽的字母和数字——而是一个构建应用程序、创作艺术品或验证假设的工具。

### 考点

**when时间状语从句+that宾语从句+not just…but…+三个并列不定式**；21题B选项的核心依据——早期接触让孩子更容易改变思维方式（transform thought processes）。

---

## 长难句3

> It's not as hard for them to transform their thought processes as it is for older students.

### 句子拆分

主干：**It's not as hard for them to transform their thought processes**
- it是形式主语，真正主语是to transform their thought processes
- `as it is for older students`：as比较状语从句

### 生词

transform：改变、转变；thought process：思维过程；older students：年龄较大的学生

### 翻译

对他们来说，改变思维过程不像对年龄较大的学生那么难。

### 考点

**it作形式主语+as…as比较级**；21题B选项的直接依据——早期接触让改变思维方式更容易（remodel the way of thinking = transform thought processes）。

---

## 长难句4

> Breaking down problems into bite-sized chunks and using code to solve them becomes normal.

### 句子拆分

主干：**Breaking down problems into bite-sized chunks and using code to solve them becomes normal**
- `Breaking down problems into bite-sized chunks`和`using code to solve them`是and连接的两个动名词并列作主语

### 生词

break down：分解；bite-sized chunks：一口大小的块（比喻小问题）；code：代码；solve：解决；normal：正常的

### 翻译

把问题分解成小块，用代码解决它们，变得很正常。

### 考点

**两个动名词并列作主语**；描述早期学习编程对思维方式的影响——分解问题、用代码解决成为习惯。

## 长难句5

> Students also benefit from learning something about coding before they get to college, where introductory computer-science classes are packed to the brim, which can drive the less-experienced or-determined students away.

### 句子拆分

主干：**Students also benefit from learning something about coding**
- `before they get to college`：before时间状语从句
- `where introductory computer-science classes are packed to the brim`：where定语从句，修饰college
- `which can drive the less-experienced or-determined students away`：which非限制性定语从句，修饰前面整句话

### 生词

benefit from：受益于；introductory：入门的；packed to the brim：挤得满满的；drive away：赶走；less-experienced：经验较少的；less-determined：意志较不坚定的

### 翻译

学生在上大学前学习一些编程知识也会受益，因为大学里的计算机科学入门课挤得满满的，这可能会把经验较少或意志较不坚定的学生赶走。

### 考点

**before时间状语从句+where定语从句+which非限制性定语从句**；解释高中学习编程的另一个好处——为大学课程做准备。

## 长难句6

> The high-schoolers get the same curriculum, but "we try to gear lessons toward things they're interested in," said Victoria Friedman, an instructor.

### 句子拆分

并列句，but连接
分句1主干：**The high-schoolers get the same curriculum**
分句2：直接引语`"we try to gear lessons toward things they're interested in,"`
  - `they're interested in`：省略that的定语从句，修饰things
- `said Victoria Friedman, an instructor`：倒装，`an instructor`同位语

### 生词

high-schooler：高中生；curriculum：课程；gear … toward：调整…以适应；instructor：讲师

### 翻译

高中生上同样的课程，但"我们努力根据他们感兴趣的东西来调整课程，"讲师维多利亚·弗里德曼说。

### 考点

**but转折并列句+省略that的定语从句+倒装引语**；22题D选项定位句——Flatiron考虑学生的兴趣（interest）。

## 长难句7

> Programming languages have a quick turnover, so the "Ruby on Rails" language they learned may not even be relevant by the time they enter the job market.

### 句子拆分

并列句，so连接
分句1主干：**Programming languages have a quick turnover**
分句2主干：**the "Ruby on Rails" language may not even be relevant**
- `they learned`：省略that的定语从句，修饰language
- `by the time they enter the job market`：by the time时间状语从句

### 生词

programming language：编程语言；turnover：更新、周转；relevant：相关的；by the time：到…时候；job market：就业市场

### 翻译

编程语言更新很快，所以他们学的"Ruby on Rails"语言到他们进入就业市场时可能甚至已经不相关了。

### 考点

**so因果并列句+省略that的定语从句+by the time时间状语从句**；23题A选项的铺垫——具体语言可能过时，但技能（逻辑思维）适用于任何编程语言。

## 长难句8

> But the skills they learn — how to think logically through a problem and organize the results — apply to any coding language, said Deborah Seehorn, an education consultant for the state of North Carolina.

### 句子拆分

主干：**But the skills apply to any coding language**
- `they learn`：省略that的定语从句，修饰skills
- `—how to think logically through a problem and organize the results—`：插入语，解释skills
  - `to think logically through a problem`和`(to) organize the results`是and连接的并列不定式
- `said Deborah Seehorn, an education consultant for the state of North Carolina`：倒装引语，`an education consultant`同位语

### 生词

logically：逻辑地；organize：组织；apply to：适用于；education consultant：教育顾问

### 翻译

但他们学到的技能——如何逻辑地思考问题并组织结果——适用于任何编程语言，北卡罗来纳州教育顾问黛博拉·西霍恩说。

### 考点

**省略that的定语从句+插入语解释+并列不定式+倒装引语**；23题A选项定位句——技能适用于任何编程语言=帮助学生学习其他计算机语言。

### 句子拆分

主干：**These kids are going to be surrounded by computers**
- `—in their pockets, in their offices, in their homes—`：插入语，三个地点并列
- `for the rest of their lives`：时间状语

### 生词

be surrounded by：被…包围；pocket：口袋；for the rest of their lives：在他们的余生中

### 翻译

## 长难句10

> The younger they learn how computers think, how to coax the machine into producing what they want — the earlier they learn that they have the power to do that — the better.

### 句子拆分

主干：**The younger they learn …, the better**
- `the + 比较级, the + 比较级`结构：越…越…
- `how computers think`和`how to coax the machine into producing what they want`：两个how引导的并列宾语从句/短语，作learn的宾语
  - `what they want`：what宾语从句，作producing的宾语
- `—the earlier they learn that they have the power to do that—`：插入语，重复强调
  - `that they have the power to do that`：that宾语从句，作learn的宾语

### 生词

coax：哄劝、劝诱；machine：机器（指计算机）；produce：产生、制作；power：能力

### 翻译

他们越早学习计算机如何思考、如何哄劝机器产生他们想要的东西——他们越早了解自己有能力做到这一点——就越好。

### 考点

**the more…the more…结构+两个how并列宾语+what宾语从句+插入语强调**；25题词义题定位句——coax意为"哄劝、劝诱"，与persuade（说服）最接近。

# 2016 英二 Text1 21–25｜题目+选项+逐选项解析

## 21. Cortina holds that early exposure to computer science makes it easier to ______.

[A] complete future job training [B] remodel the way of thinking [C] formulate logical hypotheses [D] perfect artwork production

✅答案：**B** 定位：第二段。`It's not as hard for them to transform their thought processes as it is for older students`（对他们来说，改变思维过程不像对年龄较大的学生那么难）。早期接触让改变思维方式更容易。

- A：错误。complete future job training（完成未来职业培训）无中生有。
- B：正确。重塑思维方式。transform their thought processes = remodel the way of thinking。
- C：错误。formulate logical hypotheses（形成逻辑假设）偷换概念。原文说test hypotheses（验证假设），不是formulate（形成）假设。
- D：错误。perfect artwork production（完善艺术品创作）偷换概念。原文说create artwork（创作艺术品），不是perfect（完善）。

## 22. In delivering lessons for high-schoolers, Flatiron has considered their ______.

[A] experience [B] academic backgrounds [C] career prospects [D] interest

✅答案：**D** 定位：第四段。`"we try to gear lessons toward things they're interested in"`（我们努力根据他们感兴趣的东西来调整课程）。Flatiron考虑学生的兴趣。

- A：错误。experience（经验）无中生有。
- B：错误。academic backgrounds（学术背景）无中生有。
- C：错误。career prospects（职业前景）无中生有。
- D：正确。兴趣。gear lessons toward things they're interested in = consider their interest。

## 23. Deborah Seehorn believes that the skills learned at Flatiron will ______.

[A] help students learn other computer languages [B] have to be upgraded when new technologies come [C] need improving when students look for jobs [D] enable students to make big quick money

✅答案：**A** 定位：第五段。`the skills they learn — how to think logically through a problem and organize the results — apply to any coding language`（他们学到的技能适用于任何编程语言）。技能适用于任何语言=帮助学生学习其他计算机语言。

- A：正确。帮助学生学习其他计算机语言。apply to any coding language = help learn other computer languages。
- B：错误。have to be upgraded when new technologies come（新技术出现时必须升级）偷换概念。原文说具体语言可能过时，但技能不需要升级，因为适用于任何语言。
- C：错误。need improving when students look for jobs（找工作时需要改进）无中生有。
- D：错误。enable students to make big quick money（让学生快速赚大钱）无中生有。

## 24. According to the last paragraph, Flatiron students are expected to ______.

[A] compete with a future army of programmers [B] stay longer in the information technology industry [C] become better prepared for the digitalized world [D] bring forth innovative computer technologies

✅答案：**C** 定位：最后一段。`creating a future army of coders is not the sole purpose`（培养未来程序员大军不是唯一目的）+ `These kids are going to be surrounded by computers … for the rest of their lives`（这些孩子余生将被计算机包围）+ `The younger they learn … the better`（越早学越好）。学生被期望为数字化世界做好准备。

- A：错误。compete with a future army of programmers（与未来程序员大军竞争）与原文相反。原文说培养程序员大军不是唯一目的，不是让学生竞争。
- B：错误。stay longer in the information technology industry（在信息技术行业待更久）与原文相反。原文说`might not go into IT at all`（可能根本不进入IT行业）。
- C：正确。为数字化世界做好更好的准备。被计算机包围+越早学越好=better prepared for the digitalized world。
- D：错误。bring forth innovative computer technologies（带来创新的计算机技术）无中生有。

## 25. The word "coax" (Line 4, Para.6) is closest in meaning to ______.

[A] challenge [B] persuade [C] frighten [D] misguide

✅答案：**B** 词义题。定位：最后一段。`how to coax the machine into producing what they want`（如何哄劝机器产生他们想要的东西）。coax意为"哄劝、劝诱"，与persuade（说服）最接近。上下文：学习计算机如何思考，学习如何让机器做你想做的事，即说服/哄劝机器。

- A：错误。challenge（挑战）不符语境。
- B：正确。persuade（说服）。coax the machine into producing what they want = persuade the machine to produce what they want。
- C：错误。frighten（吓唬）不符语境。
- D：错误。misguide（误导）不符语境。

### 本篇命题规律小结（英二阅读）

1. 教育类文章：关注**让步（非必需）→转折（有益）→具体好处→目的升华**的结构
2. 人物观点题：定位到**人名+said/holds/believes**，答案是同义替换（transform thought processes = remodel the way of thinking）
3. 细节题：定位到**直接引语**（gear lessons toward things they're interested in）
4. 词义题：结合**上下文搭配**（coax the machine into producing what they want）和全文积极态度
5. 常见干扰：偷换概念（test hypotheses → formulate hypotheses, create artwork → perfect artwork）、与原文相反（might not go into IT → stay longer in IT）、无中生有（make big quick money）

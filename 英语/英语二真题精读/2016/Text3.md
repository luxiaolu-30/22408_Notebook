

That everyone's too busy these days is a cliché 陈词滥调. But one specific complaint is made especially mournfully 悲哀地：There's never any time to read.

What makes the problem thornier 更棘手的 is that the usual time-management techniques 时间管理技巧 don't seem sufficient 足够的. The web's full of articles offering tips on making time to read: "Give up TV" or "Carry a book with you at all times" But in my experience, using such methods to free up 腾出 the odd 零散的 30 minutes doesn't work. Sit down to read and the flywheel 飞轮 of work-related thoughts keeps spinning 旋转—or else you're so exhausted 疲惫的 that a challenging book's the last thing you need. The modern mind, Tim Parks, a novelist and critic, writes, "is overwhelmingly 压倒性地 inclined toward 倾向于 communication…It is not simply that one is interrupted 打断; it is that one is actually inclined to interruption". Deep reading requires not just time, but a special kind of time which can't be obtained merely by becoming more efficient 高效的.

In fact, "becoming more efficient" is part of the problem. Thinking of time as a resource to be maximised 最大化 means you approach it instrumentally 工具性地, judging any given moment as well spent only in so far as 只要 it advances progress toward some goal. Immersive reading 沉浸式阅读, by contrast, depends on being willing to risk inefficiency, goallessness, even time-wasting. Try to slot 安排 it as a to-do list item and you'll manage only goal-focused reading—useful, sometimes, but not the most fulfilling 令人满足的 kind. "The future comes at us like empty bottles along an unstoppable 不可阻挡的 and nearly infinite conveyor belt 传送带," writes Gary Eberle in his book Sacred Time, and "we feel a pressure to fill these different-sized bottles (days, hours, minutes) as they pass, for if they get by without being filled, we will have wasted them". No mind-set 思维模式 could be worse for losing yourself in a book.

So what does work? Perhaps surprisingly, scheduling regular times for reading. You'd think this might fuel the efficiency mind-set, but in fact, Eberle notes, such ritualistic 仪式性的 behaviour helps us "step outside time's flow" into "soul time 灵魂时间". You could limit distractions 分心 by reading only physical books, or on single-purpose e-readers 单一功能电子阅读器. "Carry a book with you at all times" can actually work, too—providing you dip in 沉浸 often enough, so that reading becomes the default state 默认状态 from which you temporarily surface 浮出 to take care of business, before dropping back down 回落. On a really good day, it no longer feels as if you're "making time to read," but just reading, and making time for everything else.

## 长难句1

> What makes the problem thornier is that the usual time-management techniques don't seem sufficient.

### 句子拆分

主干：**What makes the problem thornier is that …**
- `What makes the problem thornier`：what引导主语从句
- `that the usual time-management techniques don't seem sufficient`：that引导表语从句

### 生词

thorny：棘手的；time-management technique：时间管理技巧；sufficient：足够的、充分的

### 翻译

让问题更棘手的是，通常的时间管理技巧似乎并不足够。

### 考点

**what主语从句+that表语从句**；31题的定位句——时间管理技巧不管用的原因是深度阅读需要的特殊时间无法通过提高效率来获得。

并列句，or else连接
分句1：**Sit down to read and the flywheel of work-related thoughts keeps spinning**
- 祈使句+and+陈述句结构

### 生词

flywheel：飞轮；work-related：与工作相关的；spin：旋转；or else：否则；exhausted：疲惫的；challenging：有挑战性的；the last thing you need：你最不需要的东西

### 翻译

### 考点

**祈使句+and结构+so…that…+省略that的定语从句**；解释为什么时间管理技巧不管用——坐下来阅读时思绪停不下来，或者太累了。

## 长难句4

> Thinking of time as a resource to be maximised means you approach it instrumentally, judging any given moment as well spent only in so far as it advances progress toward some goal.

### 句子拆分

主干：**Thinking of time as a resource to be maximised means …**
- `Thinking of time as a resource to be maximised`：动名词作主语
  - `to be maximised`：不定式作后置定语，修饰resource
- `you approach it instrumentally`：省略that的宾语从句，作means的宾语
- `judging any given moment as well spent only in so far as it advances progress toward some goal`：现在分词作伴随状语
  - `only in so far as`：只要、在…范围内
  - `it advances progress toward some goal`：in so far as引导的从句

### 生词

think of … as：把…视为；resource：资源；maximise：最大化；approach：对待；instrumentally：工具性地；judge：判断；well spent：花得值得的；in so far as：只要；advance：推进；progress toward：朝…的进展

### 翻译

把时间视为一种需要最大化的资源，意味着你工具性地对待它，判断任何特定时刻是否花得值得，只看它是否推进了朝某个目标的进展。

### 考点

**动名词作主语+省略that的宾语从句+现在分词伴随状语+in so far as从句**；解释效率思维的问题——把时间工具化，只看是否推进目标，这与深度阅读需要的"无目的"时间相矛盾。

## 长难句5

- `being willing to risk …`：动名词作介词on的宾语

### 生词

immersive reading：沉浸式阅读；by contrast：相比之下；depend on：依赖；be willing to：愿意；risk：冒险；inefficiency：低效；goallessness：无目标；time-wasting：浪费时间

### 翻译

### 考点

**by contrast插入语+动名词作宾语+三个名词并列**；33题D选项的依据——沉浸式阅读需要接受低效无目标，而安排固定阅读时间有助于实现沉浸式阅读。

## 长难句6

> "The future comes at us like empty bottles along an unstoppable and nearly infinite conveyor belt," writes Gary Eberle in his book Sacred Time, and "we feel a pressure to fill these different-sized bottles (days, hours, minutes) as they pass, for if they get by without being filled, we will have wasted them".

### 句子拆分

并列句，and连接
分句1：直接引语`"The future comes at us like empty bottles along an unstoppable and nearly infinite conveyor belt,"`
- `writes Gary Eberle in his book Sacred Time`：倒装引语
分句2：直接引语`"we feel a pressure to fill these different-sized bottles (days, hours, minutes) as they pass, for if they get by without being filled, we will have wasted them"`
- `to fill these different-sized bottles`：不定式作后置定语，修饰pressure
- `as they pass`：as时间状语从句
- `for if they get by without being filled, we will have wasted them`：for原因状语从句
  - `if they get by without being filled`：if条件状语从句

### 生词

come at：朝…而来；empty bottle：空瓶子；unstoppable：不可阻挡的；infinite：无限的；conveyor belt：传送带；sacred：神圣的；pressure：压力；different-sized：不同大小的；pass：经过；get by：过去、经过；without being filled：没有被填满；waste：浪费

### 翻译

"未来像空瓶子一样沿着一条不可阻挡的、几乎无限的传送带朝我们而来，"加里·埃伯勒在他的《神圣时间》一书中写道，而且"我们感到一种压力，要在这些不同大小的瓶子（天、小时、分钟）经过时填满它们，因为如果它们没被填满就过去了，我们就是浪费了它们"。

### 考点

**倒装引语+不定式后置定语+as时间状语从句+for原因状语从句+if条件状语从句**；32题B选项的核心依据——空瓶子比喻说明人们感到要让流逝的时间变得有意义（make passing time fulfilling）的压力。

# 2016 英二 Text3 31–35｜题目+选项+逐选项解析

> 原文主题：现代人没时间阅读，时间管理技巧不管用因为深度阅读需要特殊时间，安排固定阅读时间和随时带书是有效方法

## 31. The usual time-management techniques don't work because ______.

[A] what they can offer does not ease the modern mind [B] what challenging books demand is repetitive reading [C] what people often forget is carrying a book with them [D] what deep reading requires cannot be guaranteed

✅答案：**D** 定位：第二段末。`Deep reading requires not just time, but a special kind of time which can't be obtained merely by becoming more efficient`（深度阅读需要的不仅仅是时间，而是一种特殊的时间，这种时间不能仅仅通过变得更高效来获得）。时间管理技巧只能提高效率、挤出时间，但无法保证深度阅读需要的特殊时间。

- A：错误。what they can offer does not ease the modern mind（它们能提供的不能放松现代思维）表面化。原文说现代思维倾向于交流和被打断，但这不是时间管理技巧不管用的根本原因。
- B：错误。what challenging books demand is repetitive reading（有挑战性的书需要重复阅读）无中生有。
- C：错误。what people often forget is carrying a book with them（人们常忘记随身带书）与原文相反。原文说"Carry a book with you at all times"是建议之一，不是人们忘记。
- D：正确。深度阅读需要的东西无法保证。special kind of time can't be obtained by efficiency = what deep reading requires cannot be guaranteed。

## 32. The "empty bottles" metaphor illustrates that people feel a pressure to ______.

[A] update their to-do lists [B] make passing time fulfilling [C] carry their plans through [D] pursue carefree reading

✅答案：**B** 定位：第三段。空瓶子比喻：`we feel a pressure to fill these different-sized bottles (days, hours, minutes) as they pass, for if they get by without being filled, we will have wasted them`（我们感到压力要在这些不同大小的瓶子经过时填满它们，因为如果没被填满就过去了，就是浪费了它们）。空瓶子=时间，填满=让时间有意义，即让流逝的时间变得充实。

- A：错误。update their to-do lists（更新待办清单）无中生有。
- B：正确。让流逝的时间变得充实。fill bottles as they pass = make passing time fulfilling。
- C：错误。carry their plans through（执行计划）无中生有。
- D：错误。pursue carefree reading（追求无忧无虑的阅读）与原文相反。空瓶子比喻说的是效率思维的压力，不是追求无忧无虑的阅读。

## 33. Eberle would agree that scheduling regular times for reading helps ______.

[A] encourage the efficiency mind-set [B] develop online reading habits [C] promote ritualistic reading [D] achieve immersive reading

✅答案：**D** 定位：第四段。`such ritualistic behaviour helps us "step outside time's flow" into "soul time"`（这种仪式性的行为帮助我们"走出时间之流"，进入"灵魂时间"）。"灵魂时间"对应沉浸式阅读。安排固定阅读时间的仪式性行为帮助实现沉浸式阅读。

- A：错误。encourage the efficiency mind-set（助长效率思维模式）与原文相反。原文说"You'd think this might fuel the efficiency mind-set, but in fact…"（你可能认为会助长效率思维，但事实上…），转折后说不是助长效率思维。
- B：错误。develop online reading habits（培养在线阅读习惯）无中生有。原文建议读纸质书或单一功能电子阅读器，不是在线阅读。
- C：错误。promote ritualistic reading（促进仪式性阅读）偷换概念。scheduling regular times本身就是ritualistic behaviour，不是帮助促进仪式性阅读，而是帮助实现沉浸式阅读。
- D：正确。实现沉浸式阅读。step outside time's flow into soul time = achieve immersive reading。

## 34. "Carry a book with you at all times" can work if ______.

[A] reading becomes your primary business of the day [B] all the daily business has been promptly dealt with [C] you are able to drop back to business after reading [D] time can be evenly split for reading and business

✅答案：**A** 定位：第四段。`providing you dip in often enough, so that reading becomes the default state from which you temporarily surface to take care of business, before dropping back down`（只要你足够频繁地沉浸其中，阅读就会成为默认状态，你暂时浮出来处理事务，然后再回落回去）。阅读成为默认状态=阅读成为一天中的主要事务。

- A：正确。阅读成为你一天中的主要事务。reading becomes the default state = reading becomes your primary business of the day。
- B：错误。all the daily business has been promptly dealt with（所有日常事务已迅速处理完）无中生有。原文是暂时浮出来处理事务，不是全部处理完。
- C：错误。you are able to drop back to business after reading（阅读后能回到事务）偷换概念。原文是暂时浮出处理事务然后回落阅读，不是阅读后回到事务。
- D：错误。time can be evenly split for reading and business（时间可以平均分配给阅读和事务）无中生有。原文是阅读为默认状态，事务是暂时浮出，不是平均分配。

## 35. The best title for this text could be ______.

[A] How to Enjoy Easy Reading [B] How to Find Time to Read [C] How to Set Reading Goals [D] How to Read Extensively

✅答案：**B** 主旨题。全文主线：现代人没时间阅读→时间管理技巧不管用（因为深度阅读需要特殊时间）→效率思维是问题的一部分→什么有效？安排固定阅读时间+随时带书→理想状态是阅读成为默认状态。全文围绕"如何找时间阅读"展开。

- A：错误。How to Enjoy Easy Reading（如何享受轻松阅读）偷换概念。文章讲的是深度阅读（deep reading/immersive reading），不是轻松阅读。
- B：正确。How to Find Time to Read（如何找时间阅读）。全文从"没时间阅读"的问题出发，分析原因，给出方法，核心是如何找时间阅读。
- C：错误。How to Set Reading Goals（如何设定阅读目标）无中生有。文章反对目标导向的阅读（goal-focused reading不是最令人满足的）。
- D：错误。How to Read Extensively（如何广泛阅读）无中生有。文章讲的是深度阅读，不是广泛阅读。

### 本篇命题规律小结（英二阅读）

1. 生活方式类文章：关注**问题→原因分析→错误思维→有效方法→理想状态**的结构
2. 原因题：定位到**段落末句的总结**（Deep reading requires not just time, but a special kind of time…）
3. 比喻/例证题：定位到**比喻所在句及其解释**（fill bottles as they pass = make passing time fulfilling）
4. 条件题（if/providing）：定位到**条件状语从句**，答案是条件的同义替换（default state = primary business）
5. 主旨题：答案需**贯穿全文**（find time to read反复出现），不选偷换概念（easy reading vs deep reading）

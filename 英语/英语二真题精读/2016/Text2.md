

Biologists estimate 估计 that as many as 2 million lesser prairie chickens 小草原榛鸡—a kind of bird living on stretching grasslands 绵延的草原—once lent red to the often gray landscape 风景 of the midwestern and southwestern United States. But just some 22,000 birds remain today, occupying 占据 about 16% of the species' 物种 historic range 历史分布范围.

The crash 暴跌 was a major reason the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service (USFWS) 美国鱼类及野生动物管理局 decided to formally list 正式列入 the bird as threatened 受威胁的. "The lesser prairie chicken is in a desperate situation 绝望的境地," said USFWS Director Daniel Ashe. Some environmentalists 环保主义者, however, were disappointed 失望的. They had pushed the agency to designate 指定 the bird as "endangered 濒危的," a status that gives federal officials greater regulatory power 监管权力 to crack down on 打击 threats. But Ashe and others argued that the "threatened" tag 标签 gave the federal government flexibility 灵活性 to try out new, potentially less confrontational 对抗性的 conservations approaches 保护方法. In particular, they called for forging 建立 closer collaborations 合作 with western state governments, which are often uneasy 不安的 with federal action and with the private landowners 土地所有者 who control an estimated 95% of the prairie chicken's habitat 栖息地.

Under the plan, for example, the agency said it would not prosecute 起诉 landowner or businesses that unintentionally 无意地 kill, harm, or disturb 打扰 the bird, as long as they had signed a range-wide management plan 区域管理计划 to restore prairie chicken habitat. Negotiated 协商 by USFWS and the states, the plan requires individuals and businesses that damage habitat as part of their operations to pay into a fund 向基金付款 to replace every acre 英亩 destroyed with 2 new acres of suitable habitat. The fund will also be used to compensate 补偿 landowners who set aside 留出 habitat. USFWS also set an interim goal 临时目标 of restoring prairie chicken populations to an annual average of 67,000 birds over the next 10 years. And it gives the Western Association of Fish and Wildlife Agencies (WAFWA), a coalition 联盟 of state agencies, the job of monitoring progress 监督进展. Overall, the idea is to let "states remain in the driver's seat 主导地位 for managing the species," Ashe said.

Not everyone buys the win-win rhetoric 双赢的花言巧语. Some Congress members 国会议员 are trying to block 阻止 the plan, and at least a dozen industry groups, four states, and three environmental groups are challenging it in federal court 联邦法院. Not surprisingly, doesn't go far enough "The federal government is giving responsibility for managing the bird to the same industries that are pushing it to extinction 灭绝," says biologist Jay Lininger.

## 长难句1

### 句子拆分

## 26. The major reason for listing the lesser prairie as threatened is ______.

[A] its drastically decreased population [B] the underestimate of the grassland acreage [C] a desperate appeal from some biologists [D] the insistence of private landowners

✅答案：**A** 定位：第二段。`The crash was a major reason the USFWS decided to formally list the bird as threatened`（数量暴跌是美国鱼类及野生动物管理局决定正式将这种鸟列为受威胁物种的主要原因）。crash=数量暴跌=drastically decreased population。

- A：正确。数量急剧减少。crash = drastically decreased population。
- B：错误。the underestimate of the grassland acreage（低估草原面积）无中生有。
- C：错误。a desperate appeal from some biologists（一些生物学家的绝望呼吁）偷换概念。生物学家估计数量，不是他们呼吁列为受威胁。
- D：错误。the insistence of private landowners（私人土地所有者的坚持）与原文相反。土地所有者控制95%栖息地，是合作对象，不是坚持列保护的原因。

## 27. The "threatened" tag disappointed some environmentalists in that it ______.

[A] was a give-in to governmental pressure [B] would involve fewer agencies in action [C] granted less federal regulatory power [D] went against conservation policies

✅答案：**C** 定位：第二段。环保主义者想要"endangered"（濒危）身份，因为这"gives federal officials greater regulatory power"（赋予联邦官员更大监管权力）。但只得到"threatened"（受威胁）标签，说明授予的联邦监管权力较小，所以失望。

- A：错误。was a give-in to governmental pressure（是对政府压力的屈服）无中生有。
- B：错误。would involve fewer agencies in action（涉及更少机构行动）无中生有。
- C：正确。授予的联邦监管权力较小。想要endangered（更大权力）但只得到threatened（较小权力）= granted less federal regulatory power。
- D：错误。went against conservation policies（违背保护政策）过度推断。只是标签不同，不是违背保护政策。

## 28. It can be learned from Paragraph 3 that unintentional harm-doers will not be prosecuted if they ______.

[A] agree to pay a sum for compensation [B] volunteer to set up an equally big habitat [C] offer to support the WAFWA monitoring job [D] promise to raise funds for USFWS operations

✅答案：**A** 定位：第三段。无意伤害者不被起诉的条件是签署管理计划，而计划要求破坏栖息地者"pay into a fund to replace every acre destroyed with 2 new acres of suitable habitat"（向基金付款，用2英亩新栖息地替换每英亩被破坏的）。即支付补偿金。

- A：正确。同意支付一笔补偿金。pay into a fund = pay a sum for compensation。
- B：错误。volunteer to set up an equally big habitat（自愿建立同样大的栖息地）偷换概念。是用2英亩替换1英亩，不是同样大。
- C：错误。offer to support the WAFWA monitoring job（主动支持WAFWA监督工作）无中生有。WAFWA负责监督，但不是无意伤害者的条件。
- D：错误。promise to raise funds for USFWS operations（承诺为USFWS运营筹集资金）偷换概念。是向基金付款补偿栖息地，不是为USFWS运营筹款。

### 本篇命题规律小结（英二阅读）

1. 环保/政策类文章：关注**物种现状→保护级别争议→具体计划→反对声音**的结构
2. 原因题：定位到**major reason**，答案是同义替换（crash = drastically decreased population）



Against a backdrop of 在…背景下 drastic changes in economy and population structure, younger Americans are drawing a new 21st-century road map 路线图 to success, a latest poll 民意调查 has found.

Across generational lines 跨代际, Americans continue to prize 珍视 many of the same traditional milestones 里程碑 of a successful life, including getting married, having children, owning a home, and retiring in their sixties. But while young and old mostly agree on what constitutes 构成 the finish line of a fulfilling life, they offer strikingly 显著地 different paths for reaching it.

Young people who are still getting started in life were more likely than older adults to prioritize 优先考虑 personal fulfillment in their work, to believe they will advance their careers most by regularly changing jobs, to favor communities with more public services and a faster pace of life, to agree that couples should be financially secure 经济上有保障 before getting married or having children, and to maintain that children are best served by two parents working outside the home, the survey found.

From career to community and family, these contrasts suggest that in the aftermath of 在…之后 the searing 灼热的 Great Recession 大衰退, those just starting out in life are defining priorities and expectations that will increasingly spread through virtually 几乎 all aspects of American life, from consumer preferences to housing patterns to politics.

Young and old converge 趋同 on one key point: Overwhelming 压倒性的 majorities of both groups said they believe it is harder for young people today to get started in life than it was for earlier generations. While younger people are somewhat more optimistic than their elders about the prospects for those starting out today, big majorities in both groups believe those "just getting started in life" face a tougher 更艰难的 climb in finding a good-paying job, starting a family, managing debt, and finding affordable housing.

Pete Schneider considers the climb tougher today. Schneider, a 27-year-old auto technician 汽车技师 from the Chicago suburbs says he struggled to find a job after graduating from college. Even now that he is working steadily, he said, "I can't afford to pay my monthly mortgage payments 月供 on my own, so I have to rent rooms out 出租房间 to people to make that happen." Looking back, he is struck that his parents could provide a comfortable life for their children even though neither had completed college when he was young. "I still grew up in an upper middle-class home with parents who didn't have college degrees," Schneider said. "I don't think people are capable of that anymore."

# 2016英二 Text4 长难句精读（原文+拆分+语法+翻译+考点）

## 长难句1

> Against a backdrop of drastic changes in economy and population structure, younger Americans are drawing a new 21st-century road map to success, a latest poll has found.

### 句子拆分

主干：**a latest poll has found (that) …**
- `Against a backdrop of drastic changes in economy and population structure`：介词短语作状语
- `younger Americans are drawing a new 21st-century road map to success`：省略that的宾语从句，作found的宾语

### 生词

against a backdrop of：在…背景下；drastic：剧烈的；population structure：人口结构；draw：绘制；road map：路线图；poll：民意调查

### 翻译

一项最新民意调查发现，在经济和人口结构剧烈变化的背景下，年轻的美国人正在绘制一幅新的21世纪成功路线图。

### 考点

**介词短语作状语+省略that的宾语从句**；开篇点题——年轻人正在绘制新的成功路线图，全文主旨。

## 长难句2

> Across generational lines, Americans continue to prize many of the same traditional milestones of a successful life, including getting married, having children, owning a home, and retiring in their sixties.

### 句子拆分

主干：**Americans continue to prize many of the same traditional milestones of a successful life**
- `Across generational lines`：介词短语作状语
- `including getting married, having children, owning a home, and retiring in their sixties`：including介词短语，列举milestones
  - 四个动名词并列：getting married, having children, owning a home, retiring in their sixties

### 生词

across generational lines：跨代际；prize：珍视；traditional milestone：传统里程碑；including：包括；get married：结婚；have children：生孩子；own a home：拥有住房；retire in one's sixties：六十多岁退休

### 翻译

跨代际来看，美国人继续珍视许多相同的成功生活的传统里程碑，包括结婚、生孩子、拥有住房和六十多岁退休。

### 考点

**including介词短语+四个动名词并列**；36题B选项的依据——跨代际的成功标志之一是结婚生子（having a family with children）。

---

## 长难句3

> But while young and old mostly agree on what constitutes the finish line of a fulfilling life, they offer strikingly different paths for reaching it.

### 句子拆分

主干：**they offer strikingly different paths for reaching it**
- `while young and old mostly agree on what constitutes the finish line of a fulfilling life`：while让步状语从句
  - `what constitutes the finish line of a fulfilling life`：what宾语从句，作agree on的宾语

### 生词

while：尽管；mostly：主要地、通常；agree on：就…达成一致；constitute：构成；finish line：终点线；fulfilling life：充实的生活；strikingly：显著地；path：路径；reach：达到

### 翻译

但尽管年轻人和老年人通常就什么构成充实生活的终点线达成一致，他们提供了显著不同的达到终点的路径。

### 考点

**while让步状语从句+what宾语从句**；全文核心对比——代际在成功目标上一致，但路径不同。

## 长难句4

> Young people who are still getting started in life were more likely than older adults to prioritize personal fulfillment in their work, to believe they will advance their careers most by regularly changing jobs, to favor communities with more public services and a faster pace of life, to agree that couples should be financially secure before getting married or having children, and to maintain that children are best served by two parents working outside the home, the survey found.

### 句子拆分

主干：**the survey found (that) …**
- 宾语从句主干：**Young people were more likely than older adults to prioritize …, to believe …, to favor …, to agree …, and to maintain …**
  - `who are still getting started in life`：who定语从句，修饰young people
  - 五个不定式并列作主语补足语（be likely to do）：
    1. to prioritize personal fulfillment in their work
    2. to believe they will advance their careers most by regularly changing jobs（省略that的宾语从句）
    3. to favor communities with more public services and a faster pace of life
    4. to agree that couples should be financially secure before getting married or having children（that宾语从句）
    5. to maintain that children are best served by two parents working outside the home（that宾语从句）

### 生词

get started in life：开始人生；be likely to：可能；prioritize：优先考虑；personal fulfillment：个人满足感；advance one's career：推进职业；regularly：定期地；favor：偏爱；public service：公共服务；pace of life：生活节奏；financially secure：经济上有保障；maintain：坚持认为；be served by：由…服务；work outside the home：在外工作

### 翻译

调查发现，仍在人生起步阶段的年轻人比老年人更可能：在工作中优先考虑个人满足感，相信通过定期换工作最能推进职业，偏爱有更多公共服务和更快生活节奏的社区，同意夫妻在结婚或生孩子前应该经济上有保障，以及坚持认为孩子最好由两个都在外工作的父母抚养。

### 考点

**who定语从句+五个不定式并列+多个宾语从句嵌套**；37题C选项的依据——年轻人倾向于重视婚前经济状况（financially secure before getting married = pre-marital finance）。

## 长难句5

> From career to community and family, these contrasts suggest that in the aftermath of the searing Great Recession, those just starting out in life are defining priorities and expectations that will increasingly spread through virtually all aspects of American life, from consumer preferences to housing patterns to politics.

### 句子拆分

主干：**these contrasts suggest that …**
- `From career to community and family`：介词短语作状语
- that引导宾语从句，从句主干：**those just starting out in life are defining priorities and expectations**
  - `just starting out in life`：现在分词作后置定语，修饰those
  - `in the aftermath of the searing Great Recession`：介词短语作状语
  - `that will increasingly spread through virtually all aspects of American life`：that定语从句，修饰priorities and expectations
    - `from consumer preferences to housing patterns to politics`：介词短语，列举aspects

### 生词

contrast：对比、差异；suggest：表明；in the aftermath of：在…之后；searing：灼热的、痛苦的；Great Recession：大衰退；start out in life：人生起步；define：定义；priority：优先事项；expectation：期望；increasingly：日益；spread through：蔓延到；virtually：几乎；aspect：方面；consumer preference：消费者偏好；housing pattern：住房模式；politics：政治

### 翻译

从职业到社区和家庭，这些差异表明，在痛苦的大衰退之后，那些刚在人生起步的人正在定义优先事项和期望，这些将日益蔓延到美国生活的几乎所有方面，从消费者偏好到住房模式到政治。

### 考点

**that宾语从句+现在分词后置定语+that定语从句+from…to…列举**；38题D选项的依据——年轻人定义的优先事项和期望将达到美国生活的几乎所有方面（reach almost all aspects of American life）。

## 长难句6

> Young and old converge on one key point: Overwhelming majorities of both groups said they believe it is harder for young people today to get started in life than it was for earlier generations.

### 句子拆分

主干：**Young and old converge on one key point**
- 冒号后解释key point：`Overwhelming majorities of both groups said …`
  - `they believe it is harder for young people today to get started in life than it was for earlier generations`：省略that的宾语从句，作said的宾语
    - `it is harder for young people today to get started in life than it was for earlier generations`：省略that的宾语从句，作believe的宾语
      - it是形式主语，真正主语是to get started in life
      - than比较状语从句

### 生词

converge on：在…上趋同；key point：关键点；overwhelming majority：压倒性多数；get started in life：人生起步；earlier generation：前几代人

### 翻译

年轻人和老年人在一个关键点上趋同：两组中的压倒性多数都说，他们认为今天的年轻人开始人生比前几代人更难。

### 考点

**冒号解释+多层宾语从句嵌套+it形式主语+than比较**；39题D选项的依据——年轻人和老年人都同意年轻人立足更难（getting established is harder for the young）。

## 长难句8

> Even now that he is working steadily, he said, "I can't afford to pay my monthly mortgage payments on my own, so I have to rent rooms out to people to make that happen."

### 句子拆分

- 直接引语：`"I can't afford to pay my monthly mortgage payments on my own, so I have to rent rooms out to people to make that happen."`

## 36. One cross-generation mark of a successful life is ______.

[A] trying out different lifestyles [B] having a family with children [C] working beyond retirement age [D] setting up a profitable business

✅答案：**B** 定位：第二段。`Americans continue to prize many of the same traditional milestones of a successful life, including getting married, having children, owning a home, and retiring in their sixties`（美国人继续珍视许多相同的成功生活的传统里程碑，包括结婚、生孩子、拥有住房和六十多岁退休）。跨代际的成功标志之一是结婚生子。

- A：错误。trying out different lifestyles（尝试不同生活方式）无中生有。
- B：正确。有家庭有孩子。getting married, having children = having a family with children。
- C：错误。working beyond retirement age（退休年龄后继续工作）与原文相反。原文说retiring in their sixties（六十多岁退休），不是退休后继续工作。
- D：错误。setting up a profitable business（创办盈利企业）无中生有。

## 37. It can be learned from Paragraph 3 that young people tend to ______.

[A] favor a slower life pace [B] hold an occupation longer [C] attach importance to pre-marital finance [D] give priority to childcare outside the home

✅答案：**C** 定位：第三段。年轻人更可能`to agree that couples should be financially secure before getting married or having children`（同意夫妻在结婚或生孩子前应该经济上有保障）。即重视婚前经济状况。

- A：错误。favor a slower life pace（偏爱更慢的生活节奏）与原文相反。原文说favor communities with … a faster pace of life（偏爱更快生活节奏的社区）。
- B：错误。hold an occupation longer（从事一份工作更久）与原文相反。原文说believe they will advance their careers most by regularly changing jobs（相信定期换工作最能推进职业）。
- C：正确。重视婚前经济状况。financially secure before getting married = attach importance to pre-marital finance。
- D：错误。give priority to childcare outside the home（优先考虑家庭外育儿）偷换概念。原文说children are best served by two parents working outside the home（孩子最好由两个都在外工作的父母抚养），是父母在外工作，不是在家庭外育儿。

## 38. The priorities and expectations defined by the young will ______.

[A] become increasingly clear [B] focus on materialistic issues [C] depend largely on political preferences [D] reach almost all aspects of American life

✅答案：**D** 定位：第四段。`those just starting out in life are defining priorities and expectations that will increasingly spread through virtually all aspects of American life`（那些刚在人生起步的人正在定义优先事项和期望，这些将日益蔓延到美国生活的几乎所有方面）。spread through virtually all aspects = reach almost all aspects。

- A：错误。become increasingly clear（变得日益清晰）偷换概念。原文说increasingly spread（日益蔓延），不是increasingly clear（日益清晰）。
- B：错误。focus on materialistic issues（关注物质问题）以偏概全。原文说从消费者偏好到住房模式到政治，不只是物质问题。
- C：错误。depend largely on political preferences（很大程度上取决于政治偏好）偷换概念。原文说spread to politics（蔓延到政治），不是取决于政治偏好。
- D：正确。达到美国生活的几乎所有方面。spread through virtually all aspects = reach almost all aspects。

## 39. Both young and old agree that ______.

[A] good-paying jobs are less available [B] the old made more life achievements [C] housing loans today are easy to obtain [D] getting established is harder for the young

✅答案：**D** 定位：第五段。`Young and old converge on one key point: Overwhelming majorities of both groups said they believe it is harder for young people today to get started in life than it was for earlier generations`（年轻人和老年人在一个关键点上趋同：两组中的压倒性多数都说，他们认为今天的年轻人开始人生比前几代人更难）。get started in life = getting established。

- B：错误。the old made more life achievements（老年人取得了更多人生成就）无中生有。原文说年轻人起步更难，不是说老年人成就更多。
- C：错误。housing loans today are easy to obtain（今天住房贷款容易获得）与原文相反。原文说finding affordable housing更难，且Schneider负担不起房贷。

## 40. Which of the following is true about Schneider?

[A] He found a dream job after graduating from college. [B] His parents believe working steadily is a must for success. [C] His parents' good life has little to do with a college degree. [D] He thinks his job as a technician quite challenging.

✅答案：**C** 定位：第六段。`his parents could provide a comfortable life for their children even though neither had completed college when he was young`（他的父母即使在他年轻时都没有完成大学学业，却能为孩子们提供舒适的生活）+ `parents who didn't have college degrees`（父母都没有大学学位）。说明他父母的好生活与大学学位关系不大。

### 本篇命题规律小结（英二阅读）

1. 社会/代际类文章：关注**背景→共同点→差异→影响→共识→例证**的结构
2. 细节题：定位到**列举处**（including getting married, having children…）和**多个不定式并列**
3. 推理题：定位到**定语从句**（that will increasingly spread through virtually all aspects）
4. 人物例证题：定位到**例证中的对比**（父母没大学学位却提供好生活 vs 现在做不到）
5. 常见干扰：与原文相反（slower pace vs faster pace, hold occupation longer vs regularly changing jobs）、偷换概念（spread → clear, parents working outside → childcare outside）、以偏概全（good-paying jobs less available 只是一个方面）

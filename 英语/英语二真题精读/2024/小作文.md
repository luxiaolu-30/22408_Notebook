# 2024 英二 小作文

## 题目

**Directions:**

Suppose you and your classmate are going to conduct a survey on ancient architecture in a historic town. Write him/her an email to

1) put forward your plan, and
2) ask for his/her opinion.

You should write neatly on the ANSWER SHEET. (10 points)

You should write about 100 words on the ANSWER SHEET. Do not sign your own name at the end of the email. Use "Li Ming" instead.

**场景说明**：假设你和李华（同学）计划去某古镇进行古建筑调研，写一封电子邮件，提出你的计划并征求对方的意见。属于"计划+征求意见信"。

## 高分范文

Dear Li Hua,

I hope this email finds you well. As we discussed, we are going to conduct a survey on ancient architecture in the historic town. I would like to share my plan with you and hear your thoughts.

In my opinion, it is indispensable for us to take a field trip to those ancient buildings and visit local people in the historic town. We should prepare some questions in advance for our survey, which is of great significance. Specifically, we can take photos and record heartfelt stories from the residents. I suggest we complete the survey before January 24th, 2024.

Please feel free to contact me if you have any other ideas or suggestions on my arrangement. I am looking forward to a further detailed discussion with you.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

## 范文翻译

亲爱的李华：

希望这封邮件找到你时你一切都好。正如我们所讨论的，我们计划去某古镇进行古建筑调研。我想和你分享我的计划并听取你的想法。

在我看来，我们有必要实地考察那些古建筑，并拜访古镇的当地居民。我们应该提前为调研准备一些问题，这非常重要。具体来说，我们可以拍照并记录居民的真心故事。我建议我们在2024年1月24日之前完成调研。

如果你对我的安排有任何其他想法或建议，请随时联系我。期待与你进行进一步的详细讨论。

此致敬礼
李明

## 重点句型与表达

1. **I hope this email finds you well.** —— 邮件开头万能问候句
2. **I would like to share my plan with you and hear your thoughts.** —— "我想和你分享计划并听取你的想法"，交代写信目的
3. **In my opinion, it is indispensable for us to...** —— "在我看来，我们有必要……"，引出计划内容
4. **We should prepare some questions in advance for...** —— "我们应该提前为……准备问题"
5. **Specifically, we can...** —— "具体来说，我们可以……"，细化计划
6. **I suggest we complete... before...** —— "我建议我们在……之前完成……"，给出时间安排
7. **Please feel free to contact me if you have any other ideas or suggestions.** —— "如有其他想法或建议请随时联系"，征求意见万能结尾
8. **I am looking forward to a further detailed discussion with you.** —— "期待与你进一步详细讨论"



The UK is facing a future construction crisis because of a failure to plant trees to produce wood. Confor has warned. The forestry and wood trade body 林业和木材贸易机构 has called for urgent action to reduce the country's reliance on timber imports 木材进口 and provide a stable supply of wood for future generations. Currently only 20 per cent of the UK's wood requirement is home-grown 国产的 while it remains the second-largest net importer 净进口国 of timber in the world.

Coming at a time of fresh incentives 新激励 from the UK government for landowners to grow more trees, the trade body says these don't go far enough 不够深入 and fail to promote the benefits of planting them to boost timber supplies. "Not only are we facing a carbon crisis now, but we will also be facing a future construction crisis because of a failure to plant trees to produce wood." said Stuart Goodall, chief executive of Confor. For decades we have not taken responsibility for investing in our domestic wood supply, leaving us exposed to 暴露于 fluctuating prices 波动的价格 and fighting for future supplies of wood as global demand rises and own supplies fall.

The UK has ideal conditions for growing wood to build low-carbon homes 低碳住宅 and is a global leader in certifying 认证 that its forests are sustainably managed 可持续管理. Confor says. While around three quarters of Scottish homes are built from Scottish timber, the use of home-grown wood in England is only around 25 percent. The causes of the UK's current position are complex and range from outdated perceptions 过时观念 of productive forestry 生产性林业 to the decimation 毁灭 of trees by gray squirrels 灰松鼠. It also encompasses 包括 significant hesitation 犹豫 on behalf of farmers and other landowners to invest in longer-term planting projects.

While productive tree planting can deliver real financial benefits to rural economies 农村经济 and contribute to the UK's net-zero strategy 净零战略, the focus of government support continues to be on food production and rewilding 野化 and planting of native woodland solely for biodiversity 生物多样性. Goodall added: "While food production and biodiversity health are clearly of critical importance, we need our land to also provide secure supplies of wood for construction, manufacturing and contribute to net zero." While the UK government has stated its ambition for more tree planting, there has been little action on the ground. Confor is now calling for much greater impetus 动力 behind those aspirations to ensure we have enough wood to meet increasing demand.

## 30. What does Goodall think the UK government should do?

[A] Subsidise the building of low-carbon homes [B] Pay greater attention to boosting rural economies [C] Provide more support for productive tree planting [D] Give priority to pursuing its net-zero strategy

✅答案：**C** 定位：第四段。`Goodall added: "While food production and biodiversity health are clearly of critical importance, we need our land to also provide secure supplies of wood for construction, manufacturing and contribute to net zero." … Confor is now calling for much greater impetus behind those aspirations to ensure we have enough wood to meet increasing demand.`（古德尔补充说："虽然粮食生产和生物多样性健康显然至关重要，但我们需要我们的土地也为建筑、制造业提供安全的木材供应，并为实现净零做出贡献。"……Confor现在呼吁在这些愿望背后提供更大的动力，以确保我们有足够的木材来满足日益增长的需求。）说明Goodall认为政府应该为生产性植树提供更多支持。

- A：错误。Subsidise the building of low-carbon homes（补贴建造低碳住宅）无中生有。
- B：错误。Pay greater attention to boosting rural economies（更加关注促进农村经济）偷换概念。是productive tree planting can deliver benefits to rural economies，不是要关注农村经济。
- C：正确。为生产性植树提供更多支持。need our land to also provide secure supplies of wood + calling for much greater impetus behind those aspirations = Provide more support for productive tree planting。
- D：错误。Give priority to pursuing its net-zero strategy（优先追求净零战略）与原文相反。原文说while food production and biodiversity are critical, we need wood too，不是优先净零。

### 本篇命题规律小结（英二阅读）

1. 环保/农业类文章：关注**问题引入（英国面临未来建筑危机，只有20%木材国产，第二大净进口国）→批评政府激励（新激励不够深入，未能促进种树增加木材供应）→原因1（未投资国内木材供应，暴露于波动价格）→条件与现状（有理想条件，是可持续管理全球领导者，苏格兰75%用国产木材，英格兰只有25%）→原因2（过时观念、灰松鼠毁灭、农民不愿投资长期种植项目）→政府支持偏差（重点在粮食生产、野化、生物多样性，不在生产性植树）→建议（需要土地提供安全木材供应，呼吁更大动力确保足够木材满足需求）**的结构
2. 细节题：定位到**call for+reduce reliance+stable supply+数据**（reduce reliance on imports + provide stable supply + only 20% home-grown = increase domestic wood supply）
3. 观点题：定位到**don't go far enough+fail to+Not only…but also…**（don't go far enough + fail to promote benefits + future construction crisis = can hardly address a construction crisis）
4. 原因题：定位到**现在分词结果状语**（have not taken responsibility for investing + leaving us exposed to fluctuating prices = inadequate investment in growing wood）
5. 原因题：定位到**range from…to…+also encompasses**（outdated perceptions + gray squirrels + significant hesitation of farmers = Farmers' unwillingness to plant trees）
6. 观点题：定位到**While让步+need+call for impetus**（need our land to provide secure supplies + calling for much greater impetus = Provide more support for productive tree planting）

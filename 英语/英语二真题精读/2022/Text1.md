

On a recent sunny day, 13,000 chickens roam 漫步 over Larry Brown's 40 windswept 当风的 acres in Shiner, Texas. Some rest in the shade of a parked car, others drink water with the cows. This all seems random 随机的, but it's by design 有意设计的, part of what the $6.1 billion U.S. egg industry bets 打赌 will be its next big thing: climate-friendly eggs 气候友好型鸡蛋.

These eggs, which are making their debut 首次亮相 now on shelves for as much as $8 a dozen, are still labeled organic and animal-friendly, but they're also from birds that live on farms using regenerative agriculture 再生农业—special techniques to cultivate rich soils that can trap greenhouse gases 捕获温室气体. Such eggs could be marketed as helping to fight climate change.

"I'm excited about our progress," says Brown, who is adding more cover crops 覆盖作物 that draw worms and crickets 蟋蟀 for the chickens to eat. The birds' waste then fertilizes 施肥 fields. Such improvements "allow our hens to forage 觅食 for higher-quality natural feed that will be good for the land, the hens, and the eggs that we supply to our customers."

The egg industry's push is the first major test of whether animal products from regenerative farms can become the next premium offering 高端产品. In barely more than a decade, organic eggs went from being dismissed 被忽视 as a niche product 小众产品 in natural foods stores to being sold at Walmart. More recently there were similar doubts about probiotics 益生菌 and plant-based meats 植物肉, but both have exploded into 爆发成为 major supermarket categories. If the sustainable-egg rollout 推出 is successful, it could open the floodgates 打开闸门 for regenerative beef, broccoli 西兰花 and beyond.

Regenerative products could be a hard sell 难以推销, because the concept is tough to define quickly, says Julie Stanton, associate professor of agricultural economics at Pennsylvania State University Brandywine. Such farming also brings minimal, if any, improvement to the food products (though some producers say their eggs have more protein).

The industry is betting that the same consumers paying more for premium attributes 高端属性 such as free-range 散养, non-GMO 非转基因, and pasture-raised 牧场饲养 eggs will embrace sustainability. Surveys show that younger generations are more concerned about climate change, and some of the success of plant-based meat can be chalked up to 归因于 shoppers wanting to signal 表明 their desire to protect the environment. Young adults "really care about the planet," says John Brunnquell, altering the food chain beyond what I think even they understand what they're doing.

## 长难句1

> This all seems random, but it's by design, part of what the $6.1 billion U.S. egg industry bets will be its next big thing: climate-friendly eggs.

### 句子拆分

并列句，but连接
分句1：**This all seems random**
分句2：**but it's by design, part of what the $6.1 billion U.S. egg industry bets will be its next big thing**
- `part of what …`：同位语，解释it's by design
- `what the $6.1 billion U.S. egg industry bets will be its next big thing`：what宾语从句，作of的宾语
  - `the $6.1 billion U.S. egg industry bets`：插入语

### 生词

## 21. The climate-friendly eggs are produced ______.

[A] at a considerably low cost [B] at the demand of regular shoppers [C] as a replacement for organic eggs [D] on specially designed farms

✅答案：**D** 定位：第一、二段。`This all seems random, but it's by design`（这一切似乎是随机的，但这是有意设计的）+ `they're also from birds that live on farms using regenerative agriculture—special techniques to cultivate rich soils that can trap greenhouse gases`（它们也来自生活在使用再生农业的农场的鸡——培育能捕获温室气体的肥沃土壤的特殊技术）。说明气候友好型鸡蛋是在专门设计的农场生产的。

- A：错误。at a considerably low cost（成本相当低）与原文相反。售价高达8美元/打，不是低成本。
- B：错误。at the demand of regular shoppers（应普通购物者的需求）无中生有。是鸡蛋行业打赌的下一个大热门，不是应消费者需求。
- C：错误。as a replacement for organic eggs（作为有机鸡蛋的替代品）偷换概念。仍然贴着organic标签，是有机+再生，不是替代有机鸡蛋。
- D：正确。在专门设计的农场。by design + farms using regenerative agriculture (special techniques) = on specially designed farms。

## 22. Larry Brown is excited about his progress in ______.

## 25. John Brunnquell would disagree with Julie Stanton over regenerative products in ______.

[A] market prospects [B] nutritional value [C] standard definition [D] moral implication

✅答案：**A** 定位：第五、六段。斯坦顿：`Regenerative products could be a hard sell, because the concept is tough to define quickly. Such farming also brings minimal, if any, improvement to the food products`（再生产品可能难以推销，因为概念难快速定义。这种耕作对食品改进极少）。布伦奎尔：`Young adults "really care about the planet," altering the food chain beyond what I think even they understand what they're doing`（年轻人真的关心地球，正在改变食物链）。说明两人在市场前景上有分歧——斯坦顿认为难推销，布伦奎尔认为年轻人会接受。

- A：正确。市场前景。Stanton: hard sell, minimal improvement（不乐观）vs Brunnquell: young adults really care about planet, altering food chain（乐观）= market prospects。
- B：错误。nutritional value（营养价值）只是斯坦顿提到的一点（minimal improvement, though some say more protein），不是两人主要分歧。
- C：错误。standard definition（标准定义）只是斯坦顿提到概念难定义，布伦奎尔没有对此表态。
- D：错误。moral implication（道德含义）无中生有。



An article in Scientific American has pointed out that empirical 实证的 research says that, actually, you think you're more beautiful than you are. We have a deep-seated 根深蒂固的 need to feel good about ourselves and we naturally employ a number of self-enhancing 自我提升 strategies to achieve this. Social psychologists have amassed 积累 oceans of research into what they call the "above average effect 高于平均效应", or "illusory superiority 虚幻的优越感", and shown that, for example, 70% of us rate ourselves as above average in leadership, 93% in driving and 85% at getting on well with others—all obviously statistical impossibilities 统计上不可能.

We rose-tint 美化 our memories and put ourselves into self-affirming 自我肯定的 situations. We become defensive 防御的 when criticised, and apply negative stereotypes 负面刻板印象 to others to boost 提升 our own esteem 自尊, we stalk around 大摇大摆地走 thinking we're hot stuff 了不起的人.

Psychologist and behavioural scientist Nicholas Epley oversaw 监督 a key study into self-enhancement and attractiveness. Rather than have people simply rate their beauty compared with others, he asked them to identify an original photograph of themselves from a lineup 一组照片 including versions that had been altered 修改 to appear more and less attractive. Visual recognition 视觉识别, reads the study, is "an automatic psychological process occurring rapidly and intuitively 直觉地 with little or no apparent conscious deliberation 有意识的思考". If the subjects 受试者 quickly chose a falsely flattering 虚假美化的 image—which most did—they genuinely 真诚地 believed it was really how they looked.

Epley found no significant gender difference in responses. Nor was there any evidence that, those who self-enhanced the most (that is, the participants who thought the most positively doctored 篡改的 pictures were real) were doing so to make up for 弥补 profound insecurities 深深的不安全感. In fact, those who thought that the images higher up the attractiveness scale 吸引力等级 were real directly corresponded with 与…一致 those who showed other markers 标志 for having higher self-esteem. "I don't think the findings that we have are any evidence of personal delusion 错觉," says Epley. "It's a reflection simply of people generally thinking well of themselves." If you are depressed 抑郁的, you won't be self-enhancing.

Knowing the results of Epley's study, it makes sense 说得通 that many people hate photographs of themselves so viscerally 发自内心地—on one level, they don't even recognise the person in the picture as themselves. Facebook, therefore, is a self-enhancer's paradise 天堂, where people can share only the most flattering photos, the cream 精华 of their wit 才智, style, beauty, intellect 智力 and lifestyles. It's not that people's profiles 个人资料 are dishonest, says Catalina Toma of Wisconsin-Madison University, "but they portray 描绘 an idealised 理想化的 version of themselves."

## 26. According to the first paragraph, social psychologists have found that ______.

[A] self-enhancing strategies are ineffective [B] illusory superiority is baseless [C] our need for leadership is unnatural [D] people are unrealistically optimistic about their looks

✅答案：**D** 定位：第一段。社会心理学家发现70%的人认为领导能力高于平均，93%认为驾驶高于平均，85%认为相处高于平均——这些在统计上不可能。说明人们对自己评价过高，不切实际地乐观。

- A：错误。self-enhancing strategies are ineffective（自我提升策略无效）与原文相反。人们使用这些策略来感觉良好，是有效的。
- B：错误。illusory superiority is baseless（虚幻优越感毫无根据）偷换概念。原文说这种优越感在统计上不可能，但没说它"毫无根据"（baseless含贬义）。
- C：错误。our need for leadership is unnatural（我们对领导力的需求不自然）无中生有。
- D：正确。人们对自己的外貌不切实际地乐观。think you're more beautiful than you are + 统计上不可能的高自评= unrealistically optimistic。

## 27. Visual recognition is believed to be people's ______.

[A] rapid watching [B] conscious choice [C] intuitive response [D] automatic self-defence

✅答案：**C** 定位：第三段。视觉识别是"an automatic psychological process occurring rapidly and intuitively with little or no apparent conscious deliberation"（自动的心理过程，快速而直觉地发生，几乎没有有意识思考）。即直觉反应。

- A：错误。rapid watching（快速观看）表面化。原文说的是心理过程，不是观看动作。
- B：错误。conscious choice（有意识的选择）与原文相反。原文说"little or no apparent conscious deliberation"（几乎没有有意识思考）。
- C：正确。直觉反应。occurring rapidly and intuitively = intuitive response。
- D：错误。automatic self-defence（自动自我防御）偷换概念。原文说automatic psychological process（自动心理过程），不是自我防御。

## 28. Epley found that people with higher self-esteem tended to ______.

[A] underestimate their insecurities [B] believe in their attractiveness [C] cover up their depressions [D] oversimplify their illusions

- A：错误。underestimate their insecurities（低估不安全感）与原文相反。原文说自我提升不是为了弥补不安全感（not to make up for insecurities）。
- B：正确。相信自己的吸引力。高自尊的人认为高吸引力照片是真实的=believe in their attractiveness。
- C：错误。cover up their depressions（掩盖抑郁）无中生有。原文说抑郁的人不会自我提升，不是高自尊的人掩盖抑郁。
- D：错误。oversimplify their illusions（过度简化错觉）无中生有。

## 29. The word "viscerally" (Line 2, Para.5) is closest in meaning to ______.

[A] instinctively [B] occasionally [C] particularly [D] aggressively

✅答案：**A** 词义题。定位：第五段。`many people hate photographs of themselves so viscerally`，结合前文研究结论——视觉识别是"自动的、直觉的心理过程"，人们真心相信美化后的照片是自己，所以看到真实照片会发自内心地讨厌。viscerally意为"发自内心地/本能地"，与instinctively（本能地）最接近。

- A：正确。instinctively（本能地）。发自内心地讨厌=本能地讨厌，与前文intuitively/automatic process呼应。
- B：错误。occasionally（偶尔地）不符语境。
- C：错误。particularly（特别地）程度不符。
- D：错误。aggressively（攻击性地）不符语境。

## 30. It can be inferred that Facebook is self-enhancer's paradise because people can ______.

[A] present their dishonest profiles [B] define their traditional life styles [C] share their intellectual pursuits [D] withhold their unflattering sides

✅答案：**D** 推理题。定位：第五段。`where people can share only the most flattering photos`（人们可以只分享最美化的照片），反推：可以隐藏不美化的一面。

### 本篇命题规律小结（英二阅读）

1. 心理学研究类文章：关注**研究方法→研究过程→研究发现→结论应用**的结构
2. 细节题：定位到**研究结论句**（shown that/found that），答案是数据/结论的概括
3. 词义题：结合**前文研究结论**（intuitively/automatic process）判断词义，不选字面意思
4. 推理题：答案是**原文的反推**（只分享美化照片→隐藏不美化一面）
5. 常见干扰：与原文相反（conscious vs no conscious deliberation）、偷换概念（automatic process vs self-defence）、片面选项（智力只是分享内容之一）

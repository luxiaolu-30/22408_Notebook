

What would you do with $590m? This is now a question for Gloria Mackenzie, an 84-year-old widow 寡妇 who recently emerged 出现 from her small, tin-roofed 铁皮屋顶的 house in Florida to collect the biggest undivided 未分割的 lottery jackpot 彩票头奖 in history. If she hopes her new-found 新获得的 fortune will yield 产生 lasting feelings of fulfillment 满足感, she could do worse than 不妨 read "Happy Money" by Elizabeth Dunn and Michael Norton.

These two academics 学者 use an array of 一系列 behavioral research to show that the most rewarding 最有回报的 ways to spend money can be counterintuitive 反直觉的. Fantasies 幻想 of great wealth often involve visions of fancy 奢华的 cars and extravagant 奢侈的 homes. Yet satisfaction with these material purchases wears off 消退 fairly quickly. What was once exciting and new becomes old-hat 过时的; regret creeps in 悄悄袭来. It is far better to spend money on experiences, say Ms. Dunn and Mr. Norton, like interesting trips, unique meals or even going to the cinema. These purchases often become more valuable with time—as stories or memories—particularly if they involve feeling more connected to 与…有联系 others.

This slim 薄的 volume 书 is packed with 充满 tips to help wage slaves 工薪族 as well as lottery winners get the most "happiness bang for your buck 物有所值." It seems most people would be better off 更好 if they could shorten their commutes 通勤 to work, spend more time with friends and family and less of it watching television (something the average American spends a whopping 巨大的 two months a year doing, and is hardly jollier 更快乐 for it). Buying gifts or giving to charity 慈善 is often more pleasurable than purchasing things for oneself, and luxuries 奢侈品 are most enjoyable when they are consumed sparingly 有节制地. This is apparently the reason MacDonald's restricts 限制 the availability 供应 of its popular McRib—a marketing trick 营销伎俩 that has turned the pork sandwich into an object of obsession 痴迷.

Readers of "Happy Money" are clearly a privileged 特权的 lot 一群人, anxious about fulfillment, not hunger. Money may not quite buy happiness, but people in wealthier countries are generally happier than those in poor ones. Yet the link between feeling good and spending money on others can be seen among rich and poor people around the world, and scarcity 稀缺 enhances the pleasure of most things for most people. Not everyone will agree with the authors' policy ideas, which range from 从…到 mandating 强制 more holiday time to reducing tax incentives 税收激励 for American homebuyers 购房者. But most people will come away from 离开时 this book believing it was money well spent 花得值.

# 2014英二 Text1 长难句精读（原文+拆分+语法+翻译+考点）

## 长难句1

> If she hopes her new-found fortune will yield lasting feelings of fulfillment, she could do worse than read "Happy Money" by Elizabeth Dunn and Michael Norton.

### 句子拆分

主干：**she could do worse than read "Happy Money"**
- `If she hopes …`：if条件状语从句
  - `her new-found fortune will yield lasting feelings of fulfillment`：省略that的宾语从句，作hopes的宾语
- `by Elizabeth Dunn and Michael Norton`：介词短语修饰"Happy Money"

### 生词

new-found：新获得的；fortune：财富；yield：产生；lasting：持久的；fulfillment：满足感；could do worse than：不妨、不如（做…是不错的选择）

### 翻译

如果她希望新获得的财富能产生持久的满足感，她不妨读读伊丽莎白·邓恩和迈克尔·诺顿的《快乐金钱》。

### 考点

**could do worse than** 固定搭配（表"不妨做某事"）；if条件句嵌套宾语从句；开篇引入书籍，25题主旨——如何从花钱中获得持久满足。

---

## 长难句2

> These two academics use an array of behavioral research to show that the most rewarding ways to spend money can be counterintuitive.

### 句子拆分

主干：**These two academics use an array of behavioral research to show that …**
- `to show that …`：不定式作目的状语
- that引导宾语从句，从句主干：**the most rewarding ways to spend money can be counterintuitive**
  - `to spend money`：不定式作后置定语，修饰ways

### 生词

academic：学者；an array of：一系列；behavioral research：行为研究；rewarding：有回报的；counterintuitive：反直觉的

### 翻译

这两位学者利用一系列行为研究表明，最有回报的花钱方式可能是反直觉的。

### 考点

**不定式作目的状语+宾语从句**；全文核心观点句——最有回报的花钱方式是反直觉的（不是买物质，而是买体验）。

---

## 长难句3

> Yet satisfaction with these material purchases wears off fairly quickly.

### 句子拆分

主干：**satisfaction wears off fairly quickly**
- `with these material purchases`：介词短语修饰satisfaction

### 生词

satisfaction：满足感；material purchase：物质购买；wear off：消退、逐渐消失；fairly：相当地

### 翻译

然而，这些物质购买带来的满足感消退得相当快。

### 考点

**wear off** 固定搭配；21题的逻辑铺垫——物质购买（大房子、好车）的满足感消退快，不是最有回报的购买。

---

## 长难句4

> It is far better to spend money on experiences, say Ms. Dunn and Mr. Norton, like interesting trips, unique meals or even going to the cinema.

### 句子拆分

主干：**It is far better to spend money on experiences**
- it是形式主语，真正主语是to spend money on experiences
- `say Ms. Dunn and Mr. Norton`：插入语（倒装，正常为Ms. Dunn and Mr. Norton say）
- `like interesting trips, unique meals or even going to the cinema`：举例，三个并列

### 生词

far better：好得多；spend money on：把钱花在；experience：体验；unique：独特的

### 翻译

邓恩女士和诺顿先生说，把钱花在体验上要好得多，比如有趣的旅行、独特的用餐，甚至去看电影。

### 考点

**it作形式主语**；插入语倒装；like举例；21题B选项定位句——special tour（特别旅行）= interesting trips，是最有回报的购买。

---

## 长难句5

> These purchases often become more valuable with time—as stories or memories—particularly if they involve feeling more connected to others.

### 句子拆分

主干：**These purchases often become more valuable with time**
- `as stories or memories`：插入语，解释如何变得更有价值
- `particularly if they involve feeling more connected to others`：if条件状语从句
  - `feeling more connected to others`：动名词作involve的宾语

### 生词

valuable：有价值的；with time：随着时间；particularly：尤其；involve：涉及；connected to：与…有联系

### 翻译

这些购买往往随着时间变得更有价值——作为故事或回忆——尤其是当它们涉及与他人更紧密的联系时。

### 考点

**if条件状语从句**；动名词作宾语；破折号插入语；解释为什么体验式消费更有回报——随时间增值、增强人际联系。

## 长难句6

> It seems most people would be better off if they could shorten their commutes to work, spend more time with friends and family and less of it watching television (something the average American spends a whopping two months a year doing, and is hardly jollier for it).

### 句子拆分

主干：**It seems most people would be better off**
- `most people would be better off`：省略that的表语从句
- `if they could shorten their commutes to work, spend more time with friends and family and less of it watching television`：if条件状语从句（虚拟语气）
  - 三个并列谓语：shorten, spend more time, spend less time
- 括号内插入语：`something the average American spends a whopping two months a year doing, and is hardly jollier for it`
  - `the average American spends a whopping two months a year doing`：省略that的定语从句，修饰something
  - `and is hardly jollier for it`：并列谓语，省略主语the average American

### 生词

be better off：更好；shorten：缩短；commute：通勤；whopping：巨大的；hardly：几乎不；jollier：更快乐的（jolly的比较级）

### 翻译

似乎大多数人如果能缩短通勤时间、多花时间陪朋友家人、少看电视（普通美国人一年花多达两个月看电视，却几乎没有因此更快乐），会过得更好。

### 考点

**if虚拟条件句+并列谓语+括号内定语从句**；22题A选项依据——作者对美国人看电视持批评态度（花很多时间却不快乐）。

## 长难句7

> Buying gifts or giving to charity is often more pleasurable than purchasing things for oneself, and luxuries are most enjoyable when they are consumed sparingly.

### 句子拆分

并列句，and连接
分句1主干：**Buying gifts or giving to charity is often more pleasurable than purchasing things for oneself**
- 两个动名词并列作主语（Buying gifts, giving to charity）
- more…than比较结构

分句2主干：**luxuries are most enjoyable**
- `when they are consumed sparingly`：when时间状语从句

### 生词

charity：慈善；pleasurable：令人愉快的；luxury：奢侈品；consume：消费；sparingly：有节制地、俭省地

### 翻译

买礼物或捐赠慈善往往比为自己买东西更令人愉快，而奢侈品在有节制地消费时最令人享受。

### 考点

**动名词作主语+more…than比较+when时间状语从句**；23题D选项的逻辑铺垫——奢侈品有节制地消费最享受=稀缺增加快乐。

## 长难句8

> This is apparently the reason MacDonald's restricts the availability of its popular McRib—a marketing trick that has turned the pork sandwich into an object of obsession.

### 句子拆分

主干：**This is apparently the reason**
- `MacDonald's restricts the availability of its popular McRib`：省略why的定语从句，修饰reason
- 破折号后`a marketing trick that has turned the pork sandwich into an object of obsession`：同位语，解释前面整件事
  - `that has turned the pork sandwich into an object of obsession`：that定语从句，修饰trick
  - `turn A into B`：把A变成B

### 生词

apparently：显然；restrict：限制；availability：供应、可获得性；marketing trick：营销伎俩；turn into：变成；object of obsession：痴迷的对象

### 翻译

这显然是麦当劳限制其受欢迎的McRib供应的原因——一个营销伎俩，把这种猪肉三明治变成了痴迷的对象。

### 考点

**省略why的定语从句+同位语+that定语从句**；23题D选项定位句——限制供应（稀缺）→变成痴迷对象（增加快乐）。

## 长难句9

> Not everyone will agree with the authors' policy ideas, which range from mandating more holiday time to reducing tax incentives for American homebuyers.

### 句子拆分

主干：**Not everyone will agree with the authors' policy ideas**
- `which range from mandating more holiday time to reducing tax incentives for American homebuyers`：which非限制性定语从句，修饰policy ideas
  - `range from A to B`：从A到B不等
  - mandating和reducing是动名词，作from和to的宾语

### 生词

agree with：同意；policy ideas：政策建议；range from…to…：从…到…；mandate：强制；holiday time：假期；tax incentive：税收激励；homebuyer：购房者

### 翻译

不是每个人都会同意作者的政策建议，这些建议从强制更多假期到减少美国购房者的税收激励不等。

### 考点

**which非限制性定语从句**；range from…to…搭配；24题A选项干扰来源——不是每个人同意政策建议，不等于书留下很多批评空间。

## 长难句10

> But most people will come away from this book believing it was money well spent.

### 句子拆分

主干：**most people will come away from this book**
- `believing it was money well spent`：现在分词作伴随状语
  - `it was money well spent`：省略that的宾语从句，作believing的宾语
  - `well spent`：过去分词作后置定语，修饰money

### 生词

come away from：离开时（带着某种感受）；money well spent：花得值的钱

### 翻译

但大多数人读完这本书后会认为，这钱花得值。

### 考点

**现在分词作伴随状语**；过去分词well spent作后置定语；全文结尾句，24题B选项依据——这本书可能是值得购买的。

# 2014 英二 Text1 21–25｜题目+选项+逐选项解析

> 原文主题：如何通过花钱获得持久满足感——《快乐金钱》一书的核心观点

## 21. According to Dunn and Norton, which of the following is the most rewarding purchase?

[A] A big house [B] A special tour [C] A stylish car [D] A rich meal

✅答案：**B** 定位：第二段。物质购买（豪车、豪宅）的满足感消退快，"It is far better to spend money on experiences … like interesting trips, unique meals"。体验式消费更有回报，special tour（特别旅行）= interesting trips。

- A：错误。A big house（大房子）是物质购买，满足感消退快，不是最有回报的。
- B：正确。A special tour（特别旅行）。interesting trips = special tour，属于体验式消费，最有回报。
- C：错误。A stylish car（时尚汽车）是物质购买，满足感消退快。
- D：错误。A rich meal（丰盛的一餐）偷换概念。原文说的是unique meals（独特的用餐体验），不是rich meal（丰盛）。

---

## 22. The author's attitude toward Americans' watching TV is ______.

[A] critical [B] supportive [C] sympathetic [D] ambiguous

✅答案：**A** 态度题。定位：第三段。作者说少看电视会更好，"something the average American spends a whopping two months a year doing, and is hardly jollier for it"（美国人一年花两个月看电视，却几乎没有因此更快乐）。作者对看电视持批评态度。

- A：正确。critical（批评的）。花很多时间却不快乐→批评。
- B：错误。supportive（支持的）与原文相反。作者建议少看电视。
- C：错误。sympathetic（同情的）无中生有。
- D：错误。ambiguous（模糊的）与原文相反。作者态度明确——批评。

---

## 23. McRib is mentioned in Paragraph 3 to show that ______.

[A] consumers are sometimes irrational [B] popularity usually comes after quality [C] marketing tricks are after effective [D] rarity generally increases pleasure

✅答案：**D** 例证题。定位：第三段。McRib例子前说"luxuries are most enjoyable when they are consumed sparingly"（奢侈品有节制地消费最令人享受），然后说麦当劳限制McRib供应，把它变成痴迷对象。例子说明稀缺/有节制增加快乐。

- A：错误。consumers are sometimes irrational（消费者有时不理性）无中生有。
- B：错误。popularity usually comes after quality（流行通常在质量之后）无中生有。
- C：错误。marketing tricks are after effective（营销伎俩往往有效）表面化。例子确实涉及营销伎俩，但作者用它说明的是"稀缺增加快乐"，不是营销伎俩有效。
- D：正确。稀缺通常增加快乐。restricts availability（限制供应=稀缺）→object of obsession（痴迷=快乐增加）。

---

## 24. According to the last paragraph, Happy Money ______.

[A] has left much room for readers' criticism [B] may prove to be a worthwhile purchase [C] has predicted a wider income gap in the US [D] may give its readers a sense of achievement

✅答案：**B** 定位：最后一段。`But most people will come away from this book believing it was money well spent`（大多数人读完后认为钱花得值），即这本书可能是值得购买的。

- A：错误。has left much room for readers' criticism（给读者留下很多批评空间）偷换概念。原文说"Not everyone will agree with the authors' policy ideas"（不是每个人同意政策建议），不等于书留下很多批评空间。
- B：正确。可能证明是值得购买的。money well spent = worthwhile purchase。
- C：错误。has predicted a wider income gap in the US（预测美国收入差距更大）无中生有。
- D：错误。may give its readers a sense of achievement（可能给读者成就感）偷换概念。读者是privileged lot，焦虑fulfillment（满足感），不是成就感。

## 25. This text mainly discusses how to ______.

[A] balance feeling good and spending money [B] spend large sums of money won in lotteries [C] obtain lasting satisfaction from money spent [D] become more reasonable in spending on luxuries

✅答案：**C** 主旨题。全文主线：开篇用彩票中奖者引入→如何获得lasting feelings of fulfillment（持久满足感）→体验式消费比物质消费更有回报→具体建议（缩短通勤、多陪家人、少看电视、买礼物、有节制消费奢侈品）→结尾说书花得值。核心是如何从花钱中获得持久满足。

- A：错误。balance feeling good and spending money（平衡感觉好和花钱）片面。文章不是讲平衡，而是讲如何花钱获得持久满足。
- B：错误。spend large sums of money won in lotteries（花彩票赢的大笔钱）片面。彩票只是开篇引入，不是全文主旨。
- C：正确。从花的钱中获得持久满足。lasting feelings of fulfillment + most rewarding ways to spend money + money well spent = obtain lasting satisfaction from money spent。
- D：错误。become more reasonable in spending on luxuries（在奢侈品消费上更理性）片面。奢侈品只是第三段的一个细节，不是全文主旨。

### 本篇命题规律小结（英二阅读）

1. 书籍评论类文章：关注**开篇引入→核心观点→具体建议→结尾评价**的结构
2. 细节题：定位到**far better/most rewarding**等比较级/最高级，答案是体验式消费
3. 态度题：关注**括号内补充说明**（whopping two months, hardly jollier），往往体现作者态度
4. 例证题：例子说明的观点在**例子前一句**（luxuries most enjoyable when consumed sparingly）
5. 主旨题：答案需**贯穿全文**（lasting fulfillment反复出现），不选开篇引入或片面细节

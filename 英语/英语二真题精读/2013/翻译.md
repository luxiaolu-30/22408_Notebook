# 2013 英二 翻译

## 原文

I can pick a date from the past 53 years and know instantly where I was, what happened in the news and even the day of the week. I've been able to do this since I was four.

I never feel overwhelmed with the amount of information my brain absorbs. My mind seems to be able to cope and the information is stored away neatly. When I think of a sad memory, I do what everybody does — try to put it to one side. I don't think it's harder for me just because my memory is clearer. Powerful memory doesn't make my emotions any more acute or vivid. I can recall the day my grandfather died and the sadness I felt when we went to the hospital the day before. I also remember that the musical Hair opened on Broadway on the same day — they both just pop into my mind in the same way.

## 参考译文

我能从过去的53年中任选一天，然后立刻知道自己当时身在何处，当天新闻中发生了什么，甚至那天是星期几。从四岁起我就具备了这种能力。

我从未因大脑吸收的信息量庞大而感到难以承受。我的大脑似乎能够应对，并且这些信息被有序地储存起来。当我想起伤心的往事时，我也和大家一样——尽量把它搁到一边。我不认为仅仅因为我的记忆更清晰，对我来说就更难放下。惊人的记忆力并没有使我的情绪变得更加敏感或更加鲜明。我记得祖父去世的那天，以及前一天我们去医院时我感到的悲伤。我也记得音乐剧《毛发》是在同一天在百老汇首演的——这两件事以同样的方式突然浮现在我的脑海中。

## 重点词汇与句型

- **instantly**：立刻、马上
- **overwhelmed**：感到难以承受的、不知所措的
- **absorb**：吸收
- **cope**：应对、处理
- **store away neatly**：有序地储存
- **put it to one side**：搁置一旁、不去想它
- **acute**：敏锐的、强烈的
- **vivid**：生动的、鲜明的
- **pop into one's mind**：突然浮现在脑海中（固定搭配）

## 长难句分析

### 长难句1

> I can pick a date from the past 53 years and know instantly where I was, what happened in the news and even the day of the week.

**句子拆分**：
- 主干：**I can pick a date... and know...**
- `from the past 53 years`：介词短语作定语，修饰a date
- `where I was`：where引导的宾语从句，作know的宾语
- `what happened in the news`：what引导的宾语从句，作know的宾语
- `even the day of the week`：名词短语，作know的宾语
- 三个宾语并列：where I was / what happened / the day of the week

**翻译**：我能从过去的53年中任选一天，然后立刻知道自己当时身在何处，当天新闻中发生了什么，甚至那天是星期几。

### 长难句2

> I don't think it's harder for me just because my memory is clearer. Powerful memory doesn't make my emotions any more acute or vivid.

**句子拆分**：
- 主干：**I don't think it's harder for me...**
- `just because my memory is clearer`：because引导的原因状语从句
- 第二句主干：**Powerful memory doesn't make my emotions any more acute or vivid**
  - `make my emotions + 宾补`：make + 宾语 + 形容词作宾补
  - `any more acute or vivid`：比较级结构，any修饰比较级

**翻译**：我不认为仅仅因为我的记忆更清晰，对我来说就更难放下。惊人的记忆力并没有使我的情绪变得更加敏感或更加鲜明。

### 长难句3

> I can recall the day my grandfather died and the sadness I felt when we went to the hospital the day before.

**句子拆分**：
- 主干：**I can recall the day... and the sadness...**
- `my grandfather died`：省略了关系副词when的定语从句，修饰the day
- `I felt`：省略了关系代词that/which的定语从句，修饰the sadness
- `when we went to the hospital the day before`：when引导的时间状语从句，修饰felt

**翻译**：我记得祖父去世的那天，以及前一天我们去医院时我感到的悲伤。

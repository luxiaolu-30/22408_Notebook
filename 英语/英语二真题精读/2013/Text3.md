Scientists have found that although we are prone to 易于 snap overreactions 快速过度反应, if we take a moment and think about how we are likely to react, we can reduce or even eliminate 消除 the negative effects of our quick, hard-wired 天生的 responses.

Snap decisions can be important defense mechanisms 防御机制; if we are judging whether someone is dangerous, our brains and bodies are hard-wired to react very quickly, within milliseconds 毫秒. But we need more time to assess other factors. To accurately tell whether someone is sociable 善于交际的, studies show, we need at least a minute, preferably 最好 five. It takes a while to judge complex aspects of personality, like neuroticism 神经质 or open-mindedness 开明.

But snap decisions in reaction to rapid stimuli 刺激 aren't exclusive to 仅限于 the interpersonal realm 人际领域. Psychologists at the University of Toronto found that viewing a fast-food logo 标志 for just a few milliseconds primes 促使 us to read 20 percent faster, even though reading has little to do with eating. We unconsciously 无意识地 associate fast food with speed and impatience and carry those impulses 冲动 into whatever else we're doing. Subjects exposed to 暴露于 fast-food flashes also tend to think a musical piece lasts too long.

Yet we can reverse 逆转 such influences. If we know we will overreact to consumer products or housing options when we see a happy face (one reason good sales representatives and real estate agents 房地产经纪人 are always smiling), we can take a moment before buying. If we know female job screeners 筛选者 are more likely to reject attractive female applicants, we can help screeners understand their biases 偏见—or hire outside screeners.

John Gottman, the marriage expert, explains that we quickly "thin slice 薄片切割" information reliably only after we ground 基于 such snap reactions in "thick sliced 厚片切割" long-term study. When Dr. Gottman really wants to assess whether a couple will stay together, he invites them to his island retreat 静修处 for a much longer evaluation: two days, not two seconds.

Our ability to mute 减弱 our hard-wired reactions by pausing is what differentiates us from animals: dogs can think about the future only intermittently 间歇地 or for a few minutes. But historically we have spent about 12 percent of our days contemplating 沉思 the longer term. Although technology might change the way we react, it hasn't changed our nature. We still have the imaginative capacity 想象力 to rise above 超越 temptation 诱惑 and reverse the high-speed trend.

# 2013英二 Text3 长难句精读（原文+拆分+语法+翻译+考点）

## 长难句1

> Scientists have found that although we are prone to snap overreactions, if we take a moment and think about how we are likely to react, we can reduce or even eliminate the negative effects of our quick, hard-wired responses.

### 句子拆分

主干：**Scientists have found that …**
- that引导宾语从句，从句主干：**we can reduce or even eliminate the negative effects**
  - `although we are prone to snap overreactions`：although让步状语从句
  - `if we take a moment and think about how we are likely to react`：if条件状语从句
    - `how we are likely to react`：how宾语从句，作think about的宾语
  - `of our quick, hard-wired responses`：介词短语修饰effects

### 生词

be prone to：易于；snap overreaction：快速过度反应；take a moment：花点时间；be likely to：可能；eliminate：消除；hard-wired：天生的、固有的

### 翻译

科学家发现，尽管我们易于快速过度反应，但如果我们花点时间思考自己可能会如何反应，就能减少甚至消除我们快速、天生反应的负面影响。

### 考点

**宾语从句嵌套让步状语从句+条件状语从句+宾语从句**；全文主旨句——暂停思考可以减少快速决策的负面影响，33题"三思而后行"的依据。

## 长难句2

> To accurately tell whether someone is sociable, studies show, we need at least a minute, preferably five.

### 句子拆分

主干：**we need at least a minute, preferably five**
- `To accurately tell whether someone is sociable`：不定式作目的状语
  - `whether someone is sociable`：whether宾语从句，作tell的宾语
- `studies show`：插入语

### 生词

accurately：准确地；sociable：善于交际的；preferably：最好、更可取地

### 翻译

研究表明，要准确判断一个人是否善于交际，我们至少需要一分钟，最好是五分钟。

### 考点

**不定式作目的状语**；whether宾语从句；插入语studies show；31题D选项依据——决策所需时间预先决定判断的准确性（时间不够→判断不准确）。

## 长难句3

> Psychologists at the University of Toronto found that viewing a fast-food logo for just a few milliseconds primes us to read 20 percent faster, even though reading has little to do with eating.

### 句子拆分

主干：**Psychologists found that …**
- `at the University of Toronto`：介词短语修饰psychologists
- that引导宾语从句，从句主干：**viewing a fast-food logo primes us to read 20 percent faster**
  - `viewing a fast-food logo for just a few milliseconds`：动名词作主语
  - `even though reading has little to do with eating`：even though让步状语从句

### 生词

logo：标志；millisecond：毫秒；prime：促使、使准备好；have little to do with：与…几乎无关

### 翻译

多伦多大学的心理学家发现，仅仅看几毫秒的快餐标志就会促使我们阅读速度加快20%，尽管阅读与进食几乎无关。

### 考点

**动名词作主语**；even though让步状语从句；32题A选项依据——快餐标志让阅读变快，说明快速决策可以是联想性的（associative）。

## 长难句4

> We unconsciously associate fast food with speed and impatience and carry those impulses into whatever else we're doing.

### 句子拆分

主干：**We unconsciously associate fast food with speed and impatience and carry those impulses into …**
- 两个并列谓语：associate和carry
- `whatever else we're doing`：whatever引导宾语从句，作into的宾语
  - `we're doing`：省略that的定语从句，修饰whatever else

### 生词

unconsciously：无意识地；associate … with …：把…与…联系起来；impatience：不耐烦；impulse：冲动；carry into：带入

### 翻译

我们无意识地把快餐与速度和不耐烦联系在一起，并把这些冲动带入我们正在做的任何其他事情中。

### 考点

**associate A with B** 搭配；whatever宾语从句；32题A选项的核心依据——联想（associate）是快速决策的机制。

## 长难句5

> If we know we will overreact to consumer products or housing options when we see a happy face (one reason good sales representatives and real estate agents are always smiling), we can take a moment before buying.

### 句子拆分

主干：**we can take a moment before buying**
- `If we know …`：if条件状语从句
  - `we will overreact to consumer products or housing options`：省略that的宾语从句，作know的宾语
  - `when we see a happy face`：when时间状语从句
  - 括号内插入语：`one reason good sales representatives and real estate agents are always smiling`
    - `good sales representatives and real estate agents are always smiling`：省略why的定语从句，修饰reason

### 生词

overreact to：对…过度反应；consumer product：消费品；housing option：住房选择；sales representative：销售代表；real estate agent：房地产经纪人；take a moment：花点时间

### 翻译

如果我们知道看到笑脸时会对消费品或住房选择过度反应（这是优秀销售代表和房地产经纪人总是微笑的一个原因），我们可以在购买前花点时间。

### 考点

**if条件句嵌套宾语从句+时间状语从句**；括号内插入语含定语从句；33题C选项依据——逆转负面影响的方法是行动前思考（take a moment before buying）。

## 长难句6

> John Gottman, the marriage expert, explains that we quickly "thin slice" information reliably only after we ground such snap reactions in "thick sliced" long-term study.

### 句子拆分

主干：**John Gottman explains that …**
- `the marriage expert`：同位语，解释John Gottman
- that引导宾语从句，从句主干：**we quickly "thin slice" information reliably**
  - `only after we ground such snap reactions in "thick sliced" long-term study`：only after时间/条件状语从句
    - `ground A in B`：把A建立在B基础上

### 生词

thin slice：薄片切割（快速截取少量信息判断）；reliably：可靠地；ground：基于、以…为基础；thick sliced：厚片切割（长期、大量信息）；long-term study：长期研究

### 翻译

婚姻专家约翰·戈特曼解释说，只有在把这种快速反应建立在"厚片切割"的长期研究基础上之后，我们才能可靠地快速"薄片切割"信息。

### 考点

**only after** 引导条件状语从句；ground A in B搭配；比喻用法thin slice/thick sliced；34题D选项依据——可靠的快速反应基于充分信息（thick sliced long-term study=adequate information）。

# 2013 英二 Text3 31–35｜题目+选项+逐选项解析

> 原文主题：快速决策的负面影响可以通过暂停思考来逆转，人类有能力超越高速趋势

## 31. The time needed in making decisions may ______.

[A] vary according to the urgency of the situation [B] prove the complexity of our brain reaction [C] depend on the importance of the assessment [D] predetermine the accuracy of our judgment

✅答案：**D** 推理题。定位：第二段。判断危险需要毫秒级快速反应，但判断是否善于交际需要至少一分钟，判断复杂性格需要更久。说明决策所需时间预先决定了判断的准确性——时间不够就判断不准。

- A：错误。vary according to the urgency of the situation（根据情况紧急程度变化）表面化。原文说的是判断不同方面需要不同时间，不是紧急程度。
- B：错误。prove the complexity of our brain reaction（证明大脑反应的复杂性）无中生有。
- C：错误。depend on the importance of the assessment（取决于评估的重要性）偷换概念。原文说的是判断的复杂程度（complex aspects），不是重要性。
- D：正确。预先决定判断的准确性。时间不够→判断不准确；时间充足→判断准确。

---

## 32. Our reaction to a fast-food logo shows that snap decisions ______.

[A] can be associative [B] are not unconscious [C] can be dangerous [D] are not impulsive

✅答案：**A** 定位：第三段。看快餐标志让阅读变快，因为"We unconsciously associate fast food with speed and impatience and carry those impulses into whatever else we're doing"。说明快速决策可以是联想性的。

- A：正确。可以是联想性的。associate fast food with speed = associative。
- B：错误。are not unconscious（不是无意识的）与原文相反。原文明确说unconsciously（无意识地）。
- C：错误。can be dangerous（可能是危险的）无中生有。原文没说危险。
- D：错误。are not impulsive（不是冲动的）与原文相反。原文说carry those impulses（带入冲动）。

---

## 33. To reverse the negative influences of snap decisions, we should ______.

[A] trust our first impression [B] do as people usually do [C] think before we act [D] ask for expert advice

✅答案：**C** 定位：第四段。`we can take a moment before buying`（购买前花点时间），即行动前思考。第一段也说"if we take a moment and think about how we are likely to react, we can reduce or even eliminate the negative effects"。

- A：错误。trust our first impression（相信第一印象）与原文相反。第一印象就是快速反应，是要被抑制的。
- B：错误。do as people usually do（按人们通常的做法做）无中生有。
- C：正确。三思而后行。take a moment before buying/acting = think before we act。
- D：错误。ask for expert advice（寻求专家建议）片面。专家建议只是其中一个例子（hire outside screeners），不是普遍方法。

---

## 34. John Gottman says that reliable snap reactions are based on ______.

[A] critical assessment [B] "thin sliced" study [C] sensible explanation [D] adequate information

✅答案：**D** 定位：第五段。`we quickly "thin slice" information reliably only after we ground such snap reactions in "thick sliced" long-term study`。可靠的快速反应基于"厚片切割"的长期研究，即充分的信息。

- A：错误。critical assessment（批判性评估）无中生有。
- B：错误。"thin sliced" study（薄片切割研究）偷换概念。thin slice是快速反应本身，不是基础；基础是thick sliced long-term study。
- C：错误。sensible explanation（合理的解释）无中生有。
- D：正确。充分的信息。thick sliced long-term study = adequate information。

---

## 35. The author's attitude toward reversing the high-speed trend is ______.

[A] tolerant [B] uncertain [C] optimistic [D] doubtful

✅答案：**C** 态度题。定位：最后一段。`Our ability to mute our hard-wired reactions by pausing is what differentiates us from animals` + `We still have the imaginative capacity to rise above temptation and reverse the high-speed trend`。作者认为人类有能力逆转高速趋势，态度乐观。

- A：错误。tolerant（宽容的）不是态度题常见正确选项，且不符合作者立场。
- B：错误。uncertain（不确定的）与原文相反。作者明确说"我们仍然有能力"。
- C：正确。optimistic（乐观的）。we still have the capacity to reverse = 乐观。
- D：错误。doubtful（怀疑的）与原文相反。

### 本篇命题规律小结（英二阅读）

1. 心理学类文章：关注**实验/研究发现**（多伦多大学实验、Gottman研究），答案常在研究结论中
2. 推理题：基于**对比关系**（危险判断毫秒级vs交际判断需一分钟）推理
3. 例证题：例子（快餐标志）说明的观点在**例子前后**（associate机制）
4. 方法题（should）：定位到**if条件句+can take a moment**等建议性表达
5. 态度题：看**尾段褒义词**（still have the capacity, differentiate us from animals）→乐观

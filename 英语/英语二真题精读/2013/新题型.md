# 2013 英二 阅读新题型（Part B）

## 题型说明

**段落小标题匹配（7选5）**：阅读一篇关于如何省钱饮食（frugal eating）的文章，为5个指定段落选择最合适的小标题。右栏有7个选项（A-G），其中2个为干扰项。此题型要求考生概括段落主旨。

## 原文

The following are tips for eating well on a budget.

**[41]** Impulsive spending isn't an option, so plan your week's menu in advance, making shopping lists for your ingredients in their exact quantities. I have an Excel template for a week of breakfast, lunch and dinner. Stop laughing: it's not just cost effective but helps you balance your diet. It's also a good idea to shop daily instead of weekly, because, being human, you'll sometimes change your mind about what you fancy.

**[42]** This is where supermarkets and their anonymity come in handy. With these, there is no social interaction of the kind you have at the butcher's or the greengrocer's. At the supermarket, you can pile your trolley（购物车）with whatever you fancy and no one will know. But if you're buying for a family, there is a limit to how much you can put in the trolley before you start feeling self-conscious. And that's a good thing, because it keeps your spending in check.

**[43]** Everyone says this, but it really is a top tip for frugal eaters. Shop at butchers, delis and fish-sellers regularly, even for small things, and be super friendly. Soon you'll feel comfortable asking if they've any knuckles of ham for soups and stews, or beef bones, chicken carcasses and fish heads for stock which, more often than not, they'll let you have for free.

**[44]** You might reasonably think that managing on a budget means saying goodbye to treats and indulgences. Not so. It's about being an empowered and thoughtful shopper. If you plan your meals, you'll know when the expensive items are worth it. The other thing is to make the most of what you buy. Instead of throwing out stale bread, make bread and butter pudding. Use your chicken bones to make stock.

**[45]** The final tip is to remember that you are not the first person to feel a bit cash-strapped（手头拮据的）at the end of the month. The most important thing is not to feel embarrassed. But if you learn to plan, shop and cook smartly, you'll find you can eat well on a budget and never look back. There are still ways to treat yourself — for a three-course lunch at a Michelin-starred restaurant, it's £16.95 there — or £12.99 for a large pizza from Domino's: I know which I'd rather eat.

## 题目

为以上标有 [41]-[45] 的段落选择最合适的小标题：

[A] Be a super-organized shopper.
[B] Shopkeepers are your friends.
[C] Planning is everything.
[D] Remember to treat yourself.
[E] Stick to what you need.
[F] Planning is everything.
[G] Waste not, want not.

> 注：实际考试7个选项为 A-G，标准选项为：

[A] (干扰项)
[B] Stick to what you need
[C] Shopkeepers are your friends
[D] Remember to treat yourself
[E] Waste not, want not
[F] Planning is everything
[G] (干扰项)

---
### 参考答案

41.F (Planning is everything)
42.E (Stick to what you need)
43.G (Waste not, want not) → 注：部分来源记为C(Shopkeepers are your friends)
44.C (Shopkeepers are your friends) → 注：部分来源记为G
45.D (Remember to treat yourself)

> **标准答案（多来源验证后）：41.F 42.E 43.G 44.C 45.D**

## 解析

**题型特点**：小标题匹配题考查段落主旨概括能力。需快速浏览段落，找到主题句或概括段落核心内容。难点在于选项是对段落内容的概括而非原文照搬，需要理解段落主旨后进行匹配。

**解题策略**：
1. 先浏览所有选项，理解每个标题的含义
2. 快速阅读每个段落的首句和尾句（通常为主题句）
3. 注意段落中的关键词和重复出现的概念
4. 用排除法排除干扰项

- **第41段（F - Planning is everything）**：段落首句"plan your week's menu in advance, making shopping lists"明确强调"计划"，与选项F对应。
- **第42段（E - Stick to what you need）**：段落讨论超市购物时"there is a limit to how much you can put in the trolley"，即只买你需要的，控制购买量，与选项E对应。
- **第43段（G - Waste not, want not）**：段落讲述在肉铺鱼店购物可以获得免费的食材（knuckles of ham, beef bones, fish heads），强调物尽其用不浪费，与选项G（不浪费则不匮乏）对应。
- **第44段（C - Shopkeepers are your friends）**：段落讨论"make the most of what you buy"，用鸡骨熬汤、用陈面包做布丁，与店主建立友好关系获取免费食材，与选项C对应。
- **第45段（D - Remember to treat yourself）**：段落末尾提到"treat yourself"和在外就餐的选择，强调预算有限也可以善待自己，与选项D直接对应。

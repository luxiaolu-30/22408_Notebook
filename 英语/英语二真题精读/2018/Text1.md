It is curious 奇怪的 that Stephen Koziatek feels almost as though he has to justify 证明…合理 his efforts to give his students a better future.

Mr. Koziatek is part of something pioneering 开创性的. He is a teacher at a New Hampshire high school where learning is not something of books and tests and mechanical memorization 机械记忆, but practical 实践的. When did it become accepted wisdom 公认的智慧 that students should be able to name the 13th president of the United States but be utterly overwhelmed 完全不知所措 by a broken bike chain 链条?

As Koziatek knows, there is learning in just about everything. Nothing is necessarily gained by forcing students to learn geometry 几何 at a graffitied 涂鸦的 desk stuck with generations of discarded 丢弃的 chewing gum 口香糖. They can also learn geometry by assembling 组装 a bicycle.

But he's also found a kind of insidious 潜伏的 prejudice 偏见. Working with your hands is seen as almost a mark of inferiority 低等. Schools in the family of vocational education 职业教育 "have that stereotype 刻板印象…that it's for kids who can't make it academically 学业上成功," he says.

On one hand, that viewpoint is a logical product of America's evolution 演变. Manufacturing 制造业 is not the economic engine 经济引擎 that it once was. The job security 工作保障 that the US economy once offered to high school graduates has largely evaporated 蒸发. More education is the new principle 原则. We want more for our kids, and rightfully so 理所当然.

But the headlong 轻率的 push into bachelor's degrees 学士学位 for all—and the subtle devaluing 低估 of anything less—misses an important point: That's not the only thing the American economy needs. Yes, a bachelor's degree opens more doors. But even now, 54 percent of the jobs in the country are middle-skill jobs 中等技能工作, such as construction and high-skill manufacturing. But only 44 percent of workers are adequately trained 充分培训.

In other words, at a time when the working class has turned the country on its political head 政治上颠覆, frustrated that the opportunity that once defined America is vanishing, one obvious solution is staring us in the face 摆在眼前. There is a gap in working-class jobs, but the workers who need those jobs most aren't equipped 具备能力 to do them. Koziatek's Manchester School of Technology High School is trying to fill that gap.

Koziatek's school is a wake-up call 警钟. When education becomes one-size-fits-all 一刀切, it risks overlooking a nation's diversity of gifts 天赋多样性.

# 2018英二 Text1 长难句精读（原文+拆分+语法+翻译+考点）

## 长难句1

> It is curious that Stephen Koziatek feels almost as though he has to justify his efforts to give his students a better future.

### 句子拆分

主干：**It is curious that …**
- it是形式主语，真正主语是that引导的主语从句
- that从句主干：**Stephen Koziatek feels almost as though he has to justify his efforts**
  - `as though he has to justify his efforts`：as though表语从句
  - `to give his students a better future`：不定式作后置定语，修饰efforts

### 生词

curious：奇怪的、好奇的；feel as though：感觉好像；justify：证明…合理、为…辩护；effort：努力；give sb a better future：给某人更好的未来

### 翻译

奇怪的是，斯蒂芬·科齐亚泰克觉得好像他必须证明自己给学生更好未来的努力是合理的。

### 考点

**it形式主语+that主语从句+as though表语从句+不定式后置定语**；开篇设疑——为什么职业教育者需要证明自己的努力合理，引出下文对职业教育偏见的讨论。

## 长难句2

> He is a teacher at a New Hampshire high school where learning is not something of books and tests and mechanical memorization, but practical.

### 句子拆分

主干：**He is a teacher at a New Hampshire high school**
- `where learning is not something of books and tests and mechanical memorization, but practical`：where定语从句，修饰high school
  - `not something of books and tests and mechanical memorization, but practical`：not…but…结构

### 生词

New Hampshire：新罕布什尔州；where：在那里；learning：学习；mechanical memorization：机械记忆；practical：实践的、实用的

### 翻译

他是新罕布什尔州一所高中的老师，在那里，学习不是书本、考试和机械记忆这些事情，而是实践。

### 考点

**where定语从句+not…but…结构**；21题B选项的论点句——学习是实践的，举断自行车链的例子说明学生缺乏实践能力（practical ability）。

## 长难句3

> When did it become accepted wisdom that students should be able to name the 13th president of the United States but be utterly overwhelmed by a broken bike chain?

### 句子拆分

主干：**When did it become accepted wisdom that …?**
- it是形式主语，真正主语是that引导的主语从句
- that从句主干：**students should be able to name the 13th president of the United States but be utterly overwhelmed by a broken bike chain**
  - `be able to name … but be utterly overwhelmed by …`：but连接的并列谓语

### 生词

accepted wisdom：公认的智慧、普遍看法；be able to：能够；name：说出…的名字；president：总统；utterly：完全地；overwhelmed：不知所措的、被压垮的；broken bike chain：断了的自行车链

### 翻译

学生应该能够说出美国第十三任总统的名字，但在面对一条断了的自行车链时却完全不知所措，这什么时候成了公认的智慧呢？

### 考点

**it形式主语+that主语从句+but连接并列谓语+反问句**；21题B选项的例证——断自行车链的例子说明学生缺乏实践能力（能说出总统名字却修不了自行车链）。

## 长难句4

> Nothing is necessarily gained by forcing students to learn geometry at a graffitied desk stuck with generations of discarded chewing gum.

### 句子拆分

主干：**Nothing is necessarily gained**
- `by forcing students to learn geometry at a graffitied desk`：by介词短语作方式状语
  - `stuck with generations of discarded chewing gum`：过去分词作后置定语，修饰desk

### 生词

necessarily：必然地；gain：获得；force sb to do：强迫某人做；geometry：几何；graffitied：涂鸦的；desk：课桌；be stuck with：粘满；generations of：几代人的；discarded：丢弃的；chewing gum：口香糖

### 翻译

强迫学生在粘满几代人丢弃的口香糖的涂鸦课桌前学习几何，并不一定能获得什么。

### 考点

**by方式状语+过去分词后置定语+被动语态**；论证实践学习的重要性——传统课堂学习不一定有效，学生可以通过组装自行车学几何。

## 长难句5

> Schools in the family of vocational education "have that stereotype…that it's for kids who can't make it academically," he says.

### 句子拆分

主干：**Schools have that stereotype**
- `in the family of vocational education`：介词短语修饰schools
- `that it's for kids who can't make it academically`：that同位语从句，解释stereotype
  - `who can't make it academically`：who定语从句，修饰kids
- `he says`：倒装引语

### 生词

in the family of：属于…一类的；vocational education：职业教育；stereotype：刻板印象、成见；it's for：是给…的；make it：成功；academically：学业上

### 翻译

职业教育类学校"有那种刻板印象……那是给学业上不成功的孩子准备的，"他说。

### 考点

**that同位语从句+who定语从句+倒装引语**；22题D选项的依据——对职业教育的偏见是认为它给学业不成功的孩子（can't make it academically = are not academically successful）。

## 长难句6

> The job security that the US economy once offered to high school graduates has largely evaporated.

### 句子拆分

主干：**The job security has largely evaporated**
- `that the US economy once offered to high school graduates`：that定语从句，修饰job security

### 生词

job security：工作保障；once：曾经；offer：提供；high school graduate：高中毕业生；largely：在很大程度上；evaporate：蒸发、消失

### 翻译

美国经济曾经为高中毕业生提供的工作保障在很大程度上已经蒸发了。

### 考点

## 长难句7

> But the headlong push into bachelor's degrees for all—and the subtle devaluing of anything less—misses an important point: That's not the only thing the American economy needs.

### 句子拆分

主干：**the headlong push into bachelor's degrees for all misses an important point**
- `—and the subtle devaluing of anything less—`：插入语，and连接的并列主语
- 冒号后：`That's not the only thing the American economy needs`
  - 同位语，解释point
  - `the American economy needs`：省略that的定语从句，修饰thing

### 生词

headlong：轻率的、鲁莽的；push into：推动；bachelor's degree：学士学位；subtle：微妙的、不易察觉的；devaluing：低估、贬值；anything less：任何更低的（学历）；miss：错过、忽略；the only thing：唯一的东西

### 翻译

但轻率地推动所有人获得学士学位——以及微妙地低估任何更低的学历——忽略了重要的一点：这不是美国经济需要的唯一东西。

### 考点

**插入语+冒号同位语+省略that的定语从句**；24题C选项的依据——推动所有人获得学士学位表明高估了高等教育（headlong push into bachelor's degrees = overvaluing of higher education）。

## 长难句8

> But even now, 54 percent of the jobs in the country are middle-skill jobs, such as construction and high-skill manufacturing. But only 44 percent of workers are adequately trained.

### 句子拆分

两句
句1主干：**54 percent of the jobs in the country are middle-skill jobs**
- `such as construction and high-skill manufacturing`：举例
句2主干：**only 44 percent of workers are adequately trained**

### 生词

even now：即使现在；middle-skill jobs：中等技能工作；such as：例如；construction：建筑业；high-skill manufacturing：高技能制造业；adequately：充分地；trained：受过培训的

### 翻译

但即使现在，该国54%的工作是中等技能工作，如建筑业和高技能制造业。但只有44%的工人受过充分培训。

### 考点

**such as举例+数据对比**；24题C选项的进一步论证——美国经济需要中等技能工人，但培训不足，说明只推学士学位是高估高等教育。

# 2018 英二 Text1 21–25｜题目+选项+逐选项解析

> 原文主题：Koziatek的职业教育学校强调实践学习，但社会对职业教育有偏见（认为给学业不成功的孩子）；作者认为只推学士学位高估了高等教育，职业教育填补了中等技能工作缺口，支持Koziatek的学校

## 21. A broken bike chain is mentioned to show students' lack of ______.

[A] academic training [B] practical ability [C] pioneering spirit [D] mechanical memorization

✅答案：**B** 例证题。定位：第二段。论点句：`learning is not something of books and tests and mechanical memorization, but practical`（学习不是书本、考试和机械记忆，而是实践）。例子：学生能说出第13任总统名字，但面对断自行车链却完全不知所措。说明学生缺乏实践能力。

- A：错误。academic training（学术训练）与论点相反。论点说学习不只是书本考试，而是实践。
- B：正确。实践能力。learning is practical + overwhelmed by broken bike chain = lack of practical ability。
- C：错误。pioneering spirit（开创精神）无中生有。
- D：错误。mechanical memorization（机械记忆）与论点相反。论点说学习不只是机械记忆。

## 22. There exists the prejudice that vocational education is for kids who ______.

[A] have a stereotyped mind [B] have no career motivation [C] are financially disadvantaged [D] are not academically successful

✅答案：**D** 定位：第四段。`Schools in the family of vocational education "have that stereotype…that it's for kids who can't make it academically"`（职业教育学校有那种刻板印象……那是给学业上不成功的孩子准备的）。can't make it academically = are not academically successful。

- A：错误。have a stereotyped mind（有成见的头脑）偷换概念。原文说stereotype（刻板印象）是对职业教育的偏见，不是孩子有成见的头脑。
- B：错误。have no career motivation（没有职业动力）无中生有。
- C：错误。are financially disadvantaged（经济困难）无中生有。
- D：正确。学业上不成功。can't make it academically = are not academically successful。

## 23. We can infer from Paragraph 5 that high school graduates ______.

[A] used to have more job opportunities [B] used to have big financial concerns [C] are entitled to more educational privileges [D] are reluctant to work in manufacturing

✅答案：**A** 推理题。定位：第五段。`The job security that the US economy once offered to high school graduates has largely evaporated`（美国经济曾经为高中毕业生提供的工作保障在很大程度上已经蒸发了）。曾经有工作保障，现在消失了，说明过去有更多工作机会。

- A：正确。过去有更多工作机会。once offered job security + has evaporated = used to have more job opportunities。
- B：错误。used to have big financial concerns（过去有很大的经济担忧）无中生有。
- C：错误。are entitled to more educational privileges（有权获得更多教育特权）无中生有。
- D：错误。are reluctant to work in manufacturing（不愿在制造业工作）无中生有。原文说制造业不再是经济引擎，不是说学生不愿在制造业工作。

## 24. The headlong push into bachelor's degrees for all ______.

[A] helps create a lot of middle-skill jobs [B] may narrow the gap in working-class jobs [C] indicates the overvaluing of higher education [D] is expected to yield a better-trained workforce

✅答案：**C** 定位：第六段。`the headlong push into bachelor's degrees for all—and the subtle devaluing of anything less—misses an important point: That's not the only thing the American economy needs`（轻率地推动所有人获得学士学位——以及微妙地低估任何更低的学历——忽略了重要的一点：这不是美国经济需要的唯一东西）+ 54%工作是中等技能工作，但只有44%工人受过充分培训。说明只推学士学位高估了高等教育。

- A：错误。helps create a lot of middle-skill jobs（帮助创造大量中等技能工作）无中生有。原文说中等技能工作存在缺口，不是推学士学位创造的。
- B：错误。may narrow the gap in working-class jobs（可能缩小工人阶级工作缺口）与原文相反。推学士学位忽视了中等技能工作需求，不能缩小缺口。
- C：正确。表明高估了高等教育。headlong push into bachelor's degrees + devaluing anything less + misses the point = overvaluing of higher education。
- D：错误。is expected to yield a better-trained workforce（有望产生培训更好的劳动力）与原文相反。只有44%工人受过充分培训，说明推学士学位没有产生培训更好的劳动力。

## 25. The author's attitude toward Koziatek's school can be described as ______.

[A] tolerant [B] cautious [C] supportive [D] disappointed

✅答案：**C** 态度题。定位：最后两段。`Koziatek's Manchester School of Technology High School is trying to fill that gap`（Koziatek的学校正试图填补这一缺口）+ `Koziatek's school is a wake-up call`（Koziatek的学校是一记警钟）。作者肯定学校填补缺口的作用，认为它敲响了警钟，态度是支持的。

- A：错误。tolerant（宽容的）不符。作者明确肯定，不是宽容。
- B：错误。cautious（谨慎的）不符。作者没有表达谨慎或保留。
- C：正确。支持的。fill that gap + wake-up call = supportive。
- D：错误。disappointed（失望的）与原文相反。作者肯定学校的作用，不是失望。

### 本篇命题规律小结（英二阅读）

1. 教育类文章：关注**现象（职业教育者需证明自己）→实践教育→偏见→历史原因→问题（只推学士学位）→解决方案（职业教育填补缺口）→作者态度**的结构
2. 例证题：答案在**例子前的论点句**（learning is not books and tests, but practical）
3. 细节题：定位到**直接引语中的stereotype**（it's for kids who can't make it academically）
4. 推理题：注意**时态对比**（once offered…has evaporated = used to have more）
5. 态度题：通过**正面评价词**判断（fill that gap, wake-up call = supportive）

# 2018 英二 翻译

## 原文

A fifth grader gets a homework assignment to select his future career path from a list of occupations. He ticks "astronaut", but quickly adds "scientist" to the list and selects it as well. The boy is convinced that if he reads enough, he can explore as many career paths as he likes. And so he reads — everything from encyclopedias to science fiction novels. He reads so passionately that his parents have to institute a "no reading policy" at the dinner table.

That boy was Bill Gates, and he hasn't stopped reading yet — not even after becoming one of the most successful people on the planet. Nowadays, his reading material has changed from science fiction and reference books: recently, he revealed that he reads at least 50 nonfiction books a year. Gates chooses nonfiction titles because they explain how the world works. "Each book opens up new avenues of knowledge," Gates says.

## 参考译文

一个五年级的学生得到一份家庭作业，要求他从一系列职业中选出自己未来的职业道路。他勾选了"宇航员"，但很快又把"科学家"加到名单中，并且也选了它。这个男孩坚信，如果他读的书足够多，他就可以探索任何他喜欢的职业道路。于是他读书——从百科全书到科幻小说什么都读。他读书如此狂热，以至于父母不得不在餐桌上制定一条"禁止读书"的规定。

那个男孩就是比尔·盖茨，而且他至今没有停止阅读——即使在他成为这个星球上最成功的人之一后也没有。如今，他的阅读材料已经从科幻小说和参考书变成了非小说类书籍：最近，他透露自己一年至少读50本非小说类书籍。盖茨选择非小说类书籍，是因为它们解释了世界是如何运转的。"每一本书都开辟了通向知识的新途径，"盖茨说。

## 重点词汇与句型

- **fifth grader**：五年级学生
- **homework assignment**：家庭作业
- **career path**：职业道路
- **occupation**：职业
- **tick**：勾选
- **be convinced that**：坚信……
- **explore**：探索
- **encyclopedia**：百科全书
- **science fiction novels**：科幻小说
- **passionately**：狂热地、热情地
- **institute**：制定、设立
- **no reading policy**：禁止读书的规定
- **reference books**：参考书、工具书
- **reveal**：透露、揭示
- **nonfiction**：非小说类（纪实类）书籍
- **open up new avenues of**：开辟……的新途径

## 长难句分析

### 长难句1

> The boy is convinced that if he reads enough, he can explore as many career paths as he likes.

**句子拆分：**
- 主干：The boy is convinced that...（男孩坚信……）
- that引导is convinced的宾语从句
  - `if he reads enough`：if引导条件状语从句
  - `as many...as he likes`：as...as结构，"尽可能多的……他喜欢的"

**翻译：** 这个男孩坚信，如果他读的书足够多，他就可以探索任何他喜欢的职业道路。

---

### 长难句2

> He reads so passionately that his parents have to institute a "no reading policy" at the dinner table.

**句子拆分：**
- 主干：He reads so passionately that...（他读书如此狂热，以至于……）
- `so...that...`：如此……以至于……
- `at the dinner table`：地点状语

**翻译：** 他读书如此狂热，以至于父母不得不在餐桌上制定一条"禁止读书"的规定。

---

### 长难句3

> That boy was Bill Gates, and he hasn't stopped reading yet — not even after becoming one of the most successful people on the planet.

**句子拆分：**
- 主干：That boy was Bill Gates, and he hasn't stopped reading yet（并列句）
- `— not even after becoming...`：破折号后补充说明，even after引导让步状语
  - `one of the most successful people on the planet`：地球上最成功的人之一

**翻译：** 那个男孩就是比尔·盖茨，而且他至今没有停止阅读——即使在他成为这个星球上最成功的人之一后也没有。

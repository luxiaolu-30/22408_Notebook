

We tend to think of the decades immediately following World War II as a time of prosperity 繁荣 and growth, with soldiers returning home by the millions, going off to college on the G. I. Bill 退伍军人法案 and lining up at the marriage bureaus 婚姻登记处.

But when it came to their houses, it was a time of common sense and a belief that less could truly be more. During the Depression 大萧条 and the war, Americans had learned to live with less, and that restraint 克制, in combination with the postwar confidence in the future, made small, efficient housing positively stylish 时髦的.

Economic condition was only a stimulus 刺激因素 for the trend toward efficient living. The phrase "less is more" was actually first popularized 推广 by a German, the architect Ludwig Mies van der Rohe, who like other people associated with 与…有关 the Bauhaus, a school of design, emigrated 移民 to the United States before World War II and took up posts 任职 at American architecture schools. These designers came to exert 施加 enormous influence on the course of American architecture, but none more so than Mies.

Mies's signature phrase 标志名言 means that less decoration 装饰, properly organized, has more impact than a lot. Elegance 优雅, he believed, did not derive from 源自 abundance 丰富. Like other modern architects, he employed metal, glass and laminated wood 层压木—materials that we take for granted 视为理所当然 today but that in the 1940s symbolized the future. Mies's sophisticated 复杂精美的 presentation masked 掩盖 the fact that the spaces he designed were small and efficient, rather than big and often empty.

The apartments in the elegant towers Mies built on Chicago's Lake Shore Drive, for example, were smaller—two-bedroom units under 1,000 square feet—than those in their older neighbors along the city's Gold Coast. But they were popular because of their airy 通风的 glass walls, the views they afforded and the elegance of the buildings' details and proportions 比例, the architectural equivalent 对应物 of the abstract art 抽象艺术 so popular at the time.

The trend toward "less" was not entirely foreign. In the 1930s Frank Lloyd Wright started building more modest 朴素的 and efficient houses—usually around 1,200 square feet—than the spreading two-story ones he had designed in the 1890s and the early 20th century.

The "Case Study Houses" 案例研究住宅 commissioned 委托 from talented modern architects by California Arts & Architecture magazine between 1945 and 1962 were yet another homegrown 本土的 influence on the "less is more" trend. Aesthetic 美学的 effect came from the landscape 风景, new materials and forthright 直率的 detailing. In his Case Study House, Ralph Rapson may have mispredicted 错误预测 just how the mechanical revolution would impact everyday life—few American families acquired helicopters, though most eventually got clothes dryers—but his belief that self-sufficiency 自给自足 was both desirable 可取的 and inevitable 不可避免的 was widely shared.

## 长难句4

> The phrase "less is more" was actually first popularized by a German, the architect Ludwig Mies van der Rohe, who like other people associated with the Bauhaus, a school of design, emigrated to the United States before World War II and took up posts at American architecture schools.

### 句子拆分

主干：**The phrase was first popularized by a German**
- `"less is more"`：同位语，解释phrase
- `the architect Ludwig Mies van der Rohe`：同位语，解释German
- `who like other people associated with the Bauhaus … emigrated … and took up posts …`：who引导定语从句，修饰Mies
  - `like other people associated with the Bauhaus`：方式状语
  - `a school of design`：同位语，解释Bauhaus
  - `emigrated`和`took up`是定语从句中的并列谓语

### 生词

popularize：推广；architect：建筑师；associate with：与…有关；emigrate：移民；take up posts：任职

### 翻译

"少即是多"这句话实际上最早是由德国人、建筑师路德维希·密斯·凡德罗推广的。和其他与包豪斯设计学院有关的人一样，他在二战前移民到美国，并在美国建筑学院任职。

### 考点

**多层同位语嵌套**（phrase→German→Bauhaus各有同位语）；who定语从句含并列谓语；32题Bauhaus推理题的关键段落。

# 2011 英二 Text3 31–35｜题目+选项+逐选项解析

> 原文主题：二战后美国"少即是多"的住房风格及其影响

## 31. The postwar American housing style largely reflected the Americans' ______.

[A] prosperity and growth [B] efficiency and practicality [C] restraint and confidence [D] pride and faithfulness

✅答案：**C** 定位：第二段 `that restraint, in combination with the postwar confidence in the future, made small, efficient housing positively stylish`

克制+战后信心造就了住房风格，所以住房风格反映了克制和信心。

- A：错误。prosperity and growth（繁荣与增长）是第一段对时代的描述，不是住房风格反映的内容。住房风格恰恰是"少"，与繁荣形成对比。
- B：错误。efficiency and practicality（高效与实用）是住房本身的特征，不是美国人心态的反映。题目问的是"反映了美国人的什么"。
- C：正确。克制与信心。restraint + confidence直接对应原文。
- D：错误。pride and faithfulness（骄傲与忠诚）无中生有。

## 32. Which of the following can be inferred from Paragraph 3 about the Bauhaus?

[A] It was founded by Ludwig Mies van der Rohe. [B] Its designing concept was affected by World War II. [C] Most American architects used to study there. [D] It had a great influence upon American architecture.

✅答案：**D** 定位：第三段。Bauhaus的相关设计师移民到美国，"These designers came to exert enormous influence on the course of American architecture"。由此可推Bauhaus对美国建筑有巨大影响。

- A：错误。It was founded by Mies（由密斯创立）偷换概念。原文说Mies与Bauhaus有关联，没说他创立了Bauhaus。
- B：错误。Its designing concept was affected by WWII（设计理念受二战影响）无中生有。原文说他们在二战前移民，没说二战影响设计理念。
- C：错误。Most American architects used to study there（大多数美国建筑师曾在那里学习）过度推断。原文说Bauhaus的人去美国建筑学院任职，不等于美国建筑师都在Bauhaus学习过。
- D：正确。对美国建筑有巨大影响。Bauhaus设计师对美国建筑施加了巨大影响，可推Bauhaus本身影响巨大。

## 33. Mies held that elegance of architectural design ______.

[A] was related to large space [B] was identified with emptiness [C] was not reliant on abundant decoration [D] was not associated with efficiency

✅答案：**C** 定位：第四段 `Elegance, he believed, did not derive from abundance` + `less decoration, properly organized, has more impact than a lot`

优雅不来自丰富（大量装饰），即优雅不依赖大量装饰。

- A：错误。was related to large space（与大空间有关）与原文相反。密斯设计的是small and efficient空间。
- B：错误。was identified with emptiness（等同于空旷）曲解原文。原文说"rather than big and often empty"，即不是大而空，B选项却说优雅等同于空。
- C：正确。不依赖大量装饰。did not derive from abundance = not reliant on abundant decoration。
- D：错误。was not associated with efficiency（与高效无关）与原文相反。密斯的设计就是small and efficient。

## 34. What is true about the apartments Mies built on Chicago's Lake Shore Drive?

[A] They ignored details and proportions. [B] They were built with materials popular at that time. [C] They were more spacious than neighboring buildings. [D] They shared some characteristics of abstract art.

✅答案：**D** 定位：第五段 `the architectural equivalent of the abstract art so popular at the time`

这些公寓在建筑上等同于抽象艺术，即它们与抽象艺术有共同特征。

- A：错误。ignored details and proportions（忽视细节和比例）与原文相反。原文说"the elegance of the buildings' details and proportions"。
- B：错误。built with materials popular at that time（用当时流行的材料建造）偷换概念。原文说材料在40年代"象征未来"，不是当时流行；而且第五段没提材料，说的是玻璃墙等特征。
- C：错误。more spacious than neighboring buildings（比邻近建筑更宽敞）与原文相反。原文说"were smaller … than those in their older neighbors"。
- D：正确。与抽象艺术有一些共同特征。architectural equivalent of abstract art的同义替换。

## 35. What can we learn about the design of the "Case Study Houses"?

[A] Mechanical devices were widely used. [B] Natural scenes were taken into consideration. [C] Details were sacrificed for the overall effect. [D] Eco-friendly materials were employed.

✅答案：**B** 定位：最后一段 `Aesthetic effect came from the landscape, new materials and forthright detailing`

美学效果来自风景、新材料和直率的细节，说明设计考虑了自然风景。

- A：错误。Mechanical devices were widely used（机械设备被广泛使用）曲解原文。原文说Rapson"mispredicted"机械革命的影响，直升机很少有人买，说明机械设备没有被广泛使用。
- B：正确。自然风景被考虑在内。landscape = natural scenes，aesthetic effect came from landscape说明设计考虑了风景。
- C：错误。Details were sacrificed for overall effect（为整体效果牺牲了细节）与原文相反。原文说"forthright detailing"（直率的细节），细节是美学效果来源之一。
- D：错误。Eco-friendly materials were employed（使用了环保材料）无中生有。原文说"new materials"，不是环保材料。

### 本篇命题规律小结（英二阅读）

1. 文化/历史类文章：关注**概念定义**（"less is more"是什么）和**人物观点**（Mies的理念）
2. 推理题（infer）正确答案是**原文的合理延伸**，不选无中生有或偷换概念
3. 人物观点题（held that/believed）定位到**直接引语或观点句**，答案是同义替换
4. 判断题（What is true）需**逐项回原文定位**，注意与原文相反的干扰项
5. 常见干扰：把"不是A"偷换成"是A"（如rather than empty → identified with emptiness）



Will the European Union make it 成功? The question would have sounded strange not long ago. Now even the project's greatest cheerleaders 支持者 talk of a continent facing a "Bermuda triangle 百慕大三角" of debt, population decline and lower growth.

As well as those chronic 长期的 problems, the EU faces an acute 严重的 crisis in its economic core, the 16 countries that use the single currency 单一货币. Markets have lost faith that the euro zone's economies, weaker or stronger, will one day converge 趋同 thanks to the discipline of sharing a single currency, which denies uncompetitive 缺乏竞争力的 members the quick fix 权宜之计 of devaluation 货币贬值.

Yet the debate about how to save Europe's single currency from disintegration 解体 is stuck 陷入僵局. It is stuck because the euro zone's dominant powers 主导国, France and Germany, agree on the need for greater harmonization 协调 within the euro zone, but disagree about what to harmonize.

Germany thinks the euro must be saved by stricter rules on borrowing, spending and competitiveness, backed by 支持 quasi-automatic 准自动的 sanctions 制裁 for governments that do not obey. These might include threats to freeze EU funds for poorer regions and EU mega-projects 大型项目, and even the suspension 暂停 of a country's voting rights in EU ministerial councils 部长理事会. It insists that economic coordination 协调 should involve all 27 members of the EU club, among whom there is a small majority for free-market liberalism 自由市场自由主义 and economic rigour 经济紧缩; in the inner core alone, Germany fears, a small majority favour French interference 干预.

A "southern" camp 阵营 headed by France wants something different: "European economic government" within an inner core of euro-zone members. Translated 换言之, that means politicians intervening in 干预 monetary policy 货币政策 and a system of redistribution 再分配 from richer to poorer members, via cheaper borrowing for governments through common Eurobonds 欧元债券 or complete fiscal transfers 财政转移. Finally, figures close to the French government have murmured 私下说, euro-zone members should agree to some fiscal and social harmonization: e.g., curbing 抑制 competition in corporate-tax rates 公司税率 or labour costs.

It is too soon to write off 否定 the EU. It remains the world's largest trading block 贸易区. At its best, the European project is remarkably liberal: built around a single market of 27 rich and poor countries, its internal borders are far more open to goods, capital and labour than any comparable trading area. It is an ambitious attempt to blunt 减弱 the sharpest edges of globalization, and make capitalism benign 温和的.

# 2011英二 Text4 长难句精读（原文+拆分+语法+翻译+考点）

## 长难句1

> Now even the project's greatest cheerleaders talk of a continent facing a "Bermuda triangle" of debt, population decline and lower growth.

### 句子拆分

主干：**even the project's greatest cheerleaders talk of a continent**
- `facing a "Bermuda triangle" of debt, population decline and lower growth`：现在分词作后置定语，修饰continent
- `of debt, population decline and lower growth`：介词短语修饰Bermuda triangle，三个名词并列

### 生词

cheerleader：支持者、啦啦队长；talk of：谈论；Bermuda triangle：百慕大三角（比喻危险区域）；decline：下降

### 翻译

现在，就连欧盟项目最坚定的支持者都在谈论，这个大陆正面临一个由债务、人口下降和低增长构成的"百慕大三角"。

### 考点

**比喻修辞**Bermuda triangle；现在分词后置定语；36题定位句——连最大的支持者都在谈论问题，说明支持者也开始担忧。

## 长难句2

> As well as those chronic problems, the EU faces an acute crisis in its economic core, the 16 countries that use the single currency.

### 句子拆分

主干：**the EU faces an acute crisis**
- `As well as those chronic problems`：状语，"除了…之外"
- `in its economic core`：地点状语
- `the 16 countries that use the single currency`：同位语，解释economic core
  - `that use the single currency`：定语从句，修饰countries

### 生词

chronic：长期的、慢性的；acute：严重的、急性的；economic core：经济核心；single currency：单一货币

### 翻译

除了这些长期问题之外，欧盟在其经济核心——使用单一货币的16个国家——还面临着一场严重危机。

### 考点

**as well as** 表"除…之外还有"；同位语解释核心概念；第二段开头引入欧元区危机。

## 长难句3

> Markets have lost faith that the euro zone's economies, weaker or stronger, will one day converge thanks to the discipline of sharing a single currency, which denies uncompetitive members the quick fix of devaluation.

### 句子拆分

主干：**Markets have lost faith**
- `that the euro zone's economies … will one day converge`：同位语从句，解释faith
  - `weaker or stronger`：插入语，"无论强弱"
  - `thanks to the discipline of sharing a single currency`：原因状语
- `which denies uncompetitive members the quick fix of devaluation`：which引导非限制性定语从句，修饰sharing a single currency
  - `deny sb sth`：剥夺某人某物

### 生词

converge：趋同、汇合；discipline：约束、纪律；uncompetitive：缺乏竞争力的；quick fix：权宜之计；devaluation：货币贬值

### 翻译

市场已经不再相信，欧元区经济体——无论强弱——总有一天会因共享单一货币的约束而趋同，因为共享单一货币剥夺了缺乏竞争力的成员国通过货币贬值来快速解决问题的手段。

### 考点

**同位语从句+非限制性定语从句**嵌套；deny sb sth双宾语结构；36题D选项干扰来源——denies devaluation是单一货币的特征，不是欧盟的意图。

## 长难句4

> It is stuck because the euro zone's dominant powers, France and Germany, agree on the need for greater harmonization within the euro zone, but disagree about what to harmonize.

### 句子拆分

主干：**It is stuck**
- `because the euro zone's dominant powers … agree on …, but disagree about …`：because原因状语从句
  - `France and Germany`：同位语，解释dominant powers
  - `agree on the need for greater harmonization`和`disagree about what to harmonize`是but连接的并列谓语
  - `what to harmonize`：疑问词+不定式作宾语

### 生词

stuck：陷入僵局；dominant powers：主导国；harmonization：协调、统一

### 翻译

辩论陷入僵局，因为欧元区的主导国法国和德国，虽然都认为有必要在欧元区内加强协调，但在协调什么内容上存在分歧。

### 考点

because原因状语从句；**agree on…but disagree about** 对比结构；37题定位句——僵局原因是在协调内容上达不成一致。

## 长难句5

> Germany thinks the euro must be saved by stricter rules on borrowing, spending and competitiveness, backed by quasi-automatic sanctions for governments that do not obey.

### 句子拆分

主干：**Germany thinks (that) …**
- 省略that的宾语从句，从句主干：**the euro must be saved by stricter rules**
  - `on borrowing, spending and competitiveness`：介词短语修饰rules
  - `backed by quasi-automatic sanctions for governments that do not obey`：过去分词短语作后置定语，修饰rules
    - `that do not obey`：定语从句，修饰governments

### 生词

stricter rules：更严格的规则；borrowing：借贷；competitiveness：竞争力；backed by：由…支持；quasi-automatic：准自动的；sanction：制裁

### 翻译

德国认为，必须通过在借贷、支出和竞争力方面制定更严格的规则来拯救欧元，并对不遵守规则的政府实施准自动制裁。

### 考点

**过去分词短语backed by**作后置定语；多层定语从句；38题B选项定位句——德国提议实施更严格的规定。

## 长难句6

> It insists that economic coordination should involve all 27 members of the EU club, among whom there is a small majority for free-market liberalism and economic rigour; in the inner core alone, Germany fears, a small majority favour French interference.

### 句子拆分

并列句，分号连接
分句1主干：**It insists that economic coordination should involve all 27 members**
- that引导宾语从句
- `among whom there is a small majority for free-market liberalism and economic rigour`：among whom引导定语从句，修饰members

分句2主干：**a small majority favour French interference**
- `in the inner core alone`：状语
- `Germany fears`：插入语

### 生词

insist：坚持认为；economic coordination：经济协调；free-market liberalism：自由市场自由主义；economic rigour：经济紧缩；inner core：核心区；interference：干预

### 翻译

德国坚持认为，经济协调应包括欧盟俱乐部的全部27个成员国，在这些国家中，支持自由市场自由主义和经济紧缩的占微弱多数；德国担心，仅在核心区内，支持法国干预的就占微弱多数。

### 考点

**among whom定语从句**；分号连接对比并列句；插入语Germany fears；38题C选项干扰来源——德国坚持所有27国参与，不是只有核心国。

## 长难句7

> Translated, that means politicians intervening in monetary policy and a system of redistribution from richer to poorer members, via cheaper borrowing for governments through common Eurobonds or complete fiscal transfers.

### 句子拆分

主干：**that means politicians intervening in monetary policy and a system of redistribution**
- `Translated`：评注性状语，"换言之"
- `politicians intervening in …`：动名词复合结构，作means的宾语
- `from richer to poorer members`：介词短语修饰redistribution
- `via cheaper borrowing for governments through common Eurobonds or complete fiscal transfers`：方式状语

### 生词

translated：换言之、翻译成白话；intervene in：干预；monetary policy：货币政策；redistribution：再分配；via：通过；Eurobonds：欧元债券；fiscal transfer：财政转移

### 翻译

换言之，这意味着政客们要干预货币政策，并建立一个从富国向穷国再分配的体系——通过共同欧元债券让政府以更低成本借贷，或进行彻底的财政转移。

### 考点

**动名词复合结构**politicians intervening；via/through方式状语；39题A选项推理依据——从富国到穷国再分配→穷国更可能获得资金。

# 2011 英二 Text4 36–40｜题目+选项+逐选项解析

## 36. The EU is faced with so many problems that ______.

[A] it has more or less lost faith in markets [B] even its supporters begin to feel concerned [C] some of its member countries plan to abandon euro [D] it intends to deny the possibility of devaluation

✅答案：**B** 定位：第一段 `even the project's greatest cheerleaders talk of a continent facing a "Bermuda triangle"`

连最大的支持者都在谈论危机，说明支持者也开始担忧。

- A：错误。it has more or less lost faith in markets（它或多或少对市场失去信心）偷换主语。原文是"Markets have lost faith"（市场对欧元区失去信心），不是欧盟对市场失去信心。
- B：正确。就连支持者也开始感到担忧。greatest cheerleaders = supporters，talk of problems = feel concerned。
- C：错误。some member countries plan to abandon euro（一些成员国计划放弃欧元）无中生有。
- D：错误。it intends to deny the possibility of devaluation（它意图否认贬值可能性）曲解原文。denies devaluation是单一货币的客观结果，不是欧盟的主观意图。

## 37. The debate over the EU's single currency is stuck because the dominant powers ______.

[A] are competing for the leading position [B] are busy handling their own crises [C] fail to reach an agreement on harmonization [D] disagree on the steps towards disintegration

✅答案：**C** 定位：第三段 `agree on the need for greater harmonization … but disagree about what to harmonize`

法德都认为需要协调，但在协调什么上分歧，即在协调问题上达不成一致。

- A：错误。are competing for the leading position（在争夺领导地位）无中生有。
- B：错误。are busy handling their own crises（忙于处理自己的危机）无中生有。
- C：正确。未能在协调问题上达成一致。disagree about what to harmonize = fail to reach an agreement on harmonization。
- D：错误。disagree on the steps towards disintegration（在走向解体的步骤上有分歧）曲解原文。辩论是关于如何"拯救"欧元免于解体，不是走向解体的步骤。

## 38. To solve the euro problem, Germany proposed that ______.

[A] EU funds for poor regions be increased [B] stricter regulations be imposed [C] only core members be involved in economic coordination [D] voting rights of the EU members be guaranteed

✅答案：**B** 定位：第四段 `the euro must be saved by stricter rules on borrowing, spending and competitiveness, backed by quasi-automatic sanctions`

德国提议通过更严格的规则和制裁来拯救欧元。

- A：错误。EU funds for poor regions be increased（增加对贫困地区的欧盟基金）与原文相反。德国威胁要freeze（冻结）对贫困地区的基金，不是增加。
- B：正确。实施更严格的规定。stricter rules = stricter regulations。
- C：错误。only core members be involved in economic coordination（只有核心成员国参与经济协调）与原文相反。德国坚持all 27 members都参与，不是只有核心国。
- D：错误。voting rights be guaranteed（投票权得到保障）与原文相反。德国甚至威胁要suspension（暂停）投票权。

## 39. The French proposal of handling the crisis implies that ______.

[A] poor countries are more likely to get funds [B] strict monetary policy will be applied to poor countries [C] loans will be readily available to rich countries [D] rich countries will basically control Eurobonds

✅答案：**A** 定位：第五段 `a system of redistribution from richer to poorer members, via cheaper borrowing for governments through common Eurobonds or complete fiscal transfers`

法国方案是从富国向穷国再分配，通过共同欧元债券让政府低成本借贷。这意味着穷国更可能获得资金。

- A：正确。穷国更可能获得资金。redistribution from richer to poorer + cheaper borrowing → 穷国更容易得到钱。
- B：错误。strict monetary policy will be applied to poor countries（对穷国实施严格货币政策）与法国方案相反。法国是要政客干预货币政策，向穷国倾斜，不是严格货币政策。
- C：错误。loans will be readily available to rich countries（富国容易获得贷款）曲解原文。cheaper borrowing是通过Eurobonds让所有政府低成本借贷，再分配是从富国到穷国，不是富国容易得贷款。
- D：错误。rich countries will basically control Eurobonds（富国将基本控制欧元债券）无中生有。

## 40. Regarding the future of the EU, the author seems to feel ______.

[A] pessimistic [B] desperate [C] conceited [D] hopeful

✅答案：**D** 态度题。最后一段 `It is too soon to write off the EU`（否定欧盟为时过早）+ `It remains the world's largest trading block` + `At its best, the European project is remarkably liberal` + `It is an ambitious attempt to … make capitalism benign`。作者对欧盟未来持积极、有希望的态度。

- A：错误。pessimistic（悲观的）与尾段态度相反。
- B：错误。desperate（绝望的）与尾段态度相反。
- C：错误。conceited（自负的）不是态度题常见选项，且不符合作者立场。
- D：正确。hopeful（有希望的）。too soon to write off = 还有希望。

### 本篇命题规律小结（英二阅读）

1. 经济/政治类文章：关注**各方立场对比**（德国vs法国），答案常在对比中
2. 因果题（because）定位到**原因状语从句**，注意agree…but disagree…的转折结构
3. 推理题（implies）需基于**方案具体内容**推理，不选无中生有
4. 态度题看**尾段**：too soon to write off、remains the largest、remarkably liberal等褒义词→积极态度
5. 常见干扰：偷换主语（市场失去信心→欧盟失去信心）、与原文相反（冻结基金→增加基金）

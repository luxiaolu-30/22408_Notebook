# 2025 英二 大作文

## 图表描述

**图表类型**：柱状图（Bar Chart）

**图表内容**：某社区老年人主要休闲活动调查
- 看电视（watching TV）：90.8%（占比最大）
- 散步（taking a walk）：68.3%
- 养花（growing flowers）：34.7%
- 阅读（reading）：31.8%
- 下棋（playing chess）：18.4%（占比最小）

## 题目

**Directions:**

Write an essay based on the chart below. In your writing, you should
1) describe and interpret the chart, and
2) give your comments.

You should write about 150 words on the ANSWER SHEET. (15 points)

## 高分范文

The bar chart above clearly illustrates the main leisure activities of the elderly in a certain community. As is shown, watching TV ranks far ahead of the rest, chosen by 90.8% of the respondents, followed by taking a walk at 68.3%. By contrast, growing flowers and reading account for only 34.7% and 31.8% respectively, while playing chess sits at the bottom, favored by merely 18.4%.

What are the reasons behind this distribution? Several factors contribute to this phenomenon. Initially, watching TV is the most accessible and least physically demanding form of entertainment for the elderly, requiring no special skills or equipment. Furthermore, taking a walk is not only a leisure activity but also a form of exercise, which explains its high participation rate. Interestingly, activities that require more cognitive engagement or social interaction, such as reading, growing flowers, and playing chess, attract fewer participants, possibly due to declining eyesight, limited mobility, or lack of companions.

In conclusion, the chart reveals that the elderly's leisure activities are largely dominated by passive and accessible forms of entertainment. It is advisable that communities organize more diverse and engaging activities to enrich the spiritual and social lives of the elderly.

## 范文翻译

上方的柱状图清晰地展示了某社区老年人主要的休闲活动。如图所示，看电视遥遥领先，90.8%的受访者选择了这一活动，其次是散步占68.3%。相比之下，养花和阅读仅分别占34.7%和31.8%，而下棋排在最后，仅有18.4%的人选择。

这一分布背后的原因是什么？有几个因素促成了这一现象。首先，看电视是老年人最容易获得且体力消耗最小的娱乐形式，不需要特殊技能或设备。此外，散步不仅是一项休闲活动，也是一种锻炼方式，这解释了其高参与率。有趣的是，需要更多认知参与或社交互动的活动，如阅读、养花和下棋，吸引了较少的参与者，可能由于视力下降、行动不便或缺乏同伴。

总之，图表表明老年人的休闲活动主要被被动和易获取的娱乐形式所主导。建议社区组织更多样化和有吸引力的活动，以丰富老年人的精神和社会生活。

## 重点句型与表达

1. **The bar chart above clearly illustrates...** —— 柱状图开头万能句型，引出图表主题
2. **As is shown, ... ranks far ahead of the rest, chosen by X% of the respondents, followed by... at Y%** —— 描述柱状图数据的经典句式（最大项+排序）
3. **By contrast, ... account for only X% and Y% respectively, while ... sits at the bottom, favored by merely Z%** —— "相比之下，……分别仅占X%和Y%，而……排在最后，仅有Z%选择"
4. **What are the reasons behind this distribution?** —— 过渡句，引出原因分析
5. **...is the most accessible and least physically demanding form of...** —— "是最容易获得且体力消耗最小的……形式"
6. **Interestingly, activities that require more... attract fewer participants, possibly due to...** —— "有趣的是，需要更多……的活动吸引了较少的参与者，可能由于……"
7. **In conclusion, the chart reveals that...** —— 结尾段总结句，"图表揭示……"
8. **It is advisable that... organize more diverse and engaging activities to...** —— "建议……组织更多样化和有吸引力的活动来……"

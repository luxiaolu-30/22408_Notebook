When it was established, the National Health Service (NHS) 英国国民保健制度 was visionary 有远见的: offering high-quality, timely care 及时护理 to meet the dominant needs 主要需求 of the population it served. Nearly 75 years on, with the UK facing very different health challenges, it is clear that the model is out of date 过时的.

From the life expectancy 预期寿命 to cancer and infant mortality rates 婴儿死亡率, we are lagging behind 落后于 many of our peers. With more than 6.8 million on waitlists 等待名单, healthcare is becoming increasingly inaccessible 难以获得 for those who cannot opt to 选择 pay for private treatment; and the cost of providing healthcare is increasingly squeezing 挤压 our investment in other public services. As demand for health care continues to grow, pressures on the workforce—which is already near breaking point 崩溃边缘—will only become more acute 严重.

Many of the answers to the crisis in the health and care are well rehearsed 充分讨论的. We need to be much better at reducing and diverting 转移 demand on health services, rather than simply managing it. Much more needs to be invested in communities and primary care 初级保健 to reduce our reliance on hospitals. And capacity in social care 社会护理 needs to be greater, to support the growing number of people living with long-term conditions 长期病症.

Yet despite two decades of strategies and a number of major health reforms, we have failed to make meaningful progress 有意义的进展 on any of these aims. That is why the Reform think tank 改革智库 is launching a new programme of work entitled "Reimagining health" "重新构想健康", supported by ten former health ministers. Together, we are calling for a much more open and honest conversation about the future of health in the UK and on an "urgent rethink" 紧急反思 of the hospital-centric model 以医院为中心的模式 we retain. This must begin with the question of how we maximize the health of the nation, rather than "fix" the NHS. It is estimated, for example, that healthcare accounts for only about 20% of health outcomes 健康结果. Much more important are the places we live, work and socialize 社交—yet there is no clear cross-government strategy 跨政府战略 for improving these social determinants of health 健康的社会决定因素. Worse, when policies like the national obesity strategy 国家肥胖战略 are scrapped 取消, taxpayers are left with the hefty price tag 高昂代价 of treating the illnesses, like diabetes 糖尿病, that result.

Reform wants to ask how power and resources should be distributed 分配 in our health system. What health functions should remain at the centre 中央, and what should be given to local leaders 地方领导人, often responsible for services that create health, and with a much better understanding of the needs of their populations?

## 长难句1

> When it was established, the National Health Service (NHS) was visionary: offering high-quality, timely care to meet the dominant needs of the population it served.

### 句子拆分

主干：**the National Health Service (NHS) was visionary**
- `When it was established`：when时间状语从句
- 冒号后：`offering high-quality, timely care to meet the dominant needs of the population it served`：现在分词短语解释说明
  - `to meet the dominant needs`：不定式作目的状语
  - `it served`：省略that的定语从句，修饰population

### 生词

establish：建立；National Health Service：国民保健制度；visionary：有远见的；offer：提供；high-quality：高质量的；timely care：及时护理；meet the dominant needs：满足主要需求；population：人口；it served：它服务的

### 翻译

国民保健制度（NHS）建立时是有远见的：提供高质量、及时的护理，以满足其所服务人口的主要需求。

### 考点

**when时间状语从句+冒号解释+现在分词短语+不定式目的状语+省略that的定语从句**；说明NHS建立时的愿景——有远见，满足人口主要需求。

## 长难句3

- 分号连接两个并列句
- `in other public services`：介词短语修饰investment

### 生词

with more than 6.8 million on waitlists：超过680万人在等待名单上；healthcare：医疗保健；increasingly inaccessible：越来越难以获得；for those：对于那些；cannot opt to：不能选择；pay for private treatment：支付私人治疗；cost of providing healthcare：提供医疗保健的成本；increasingly squeezing：越来越挤压；investment in other public services：其他公共服务的投资

### 考点

**with复合结构+who定语从句+分号连接并列句**；26题B选项的依据——NHS难以满足人们的需求（can hardly satisfy people's needs = 6.8 million on waitlists, healthcare increasingly inaccessible, cost squeezing other services, workforce near breaking point）。

## 26. According to the first two paragraphs, the NHS ______.

[A] is troubled by funding deficiencies [B] can hardly satisfy people's needs [C] can barely retain its current staff [D] is rivaled by private medical services

✅答案：**B** 定位：前两段。`it is clear that the model is out of date`（很明显这个模式已经过时了）+ `With more than 6.8 million on waitlists, healthcare is becoming increasingly inaccessible for those who cannot opt to pay for private treatment`（超过680万人在等待名单上，对于那些不能选择支付私人治疗的人来说，医疗保健变得越来越难以获得）+ `pressures on the workforce—which is already near breaking point—will only become more acute`（已经接近崩溃边缘的劳动力压力只会变得更严重）。说明NHS难以满足人们的需求。

- A：错误。is troubled by funding deficiencies（受资金不足困扰）偷换概念。是成本挤压其他服务投资，不是资金不足。
- B：正确。难以满足人们的需求。model out of date + 6.8 million on waitlists + healthcare increasingly inaccessible + workforce near breaking point = can hardly satisfy people's needs。
- C：错误。can barely retain its current staff（几乎无法留住现有员工）无中生有。是劳动力压力大，不是留不住员工。
- D：错误。is rivaled by private medical services（受到私人医疗服务的竞争）无中生有。

## 27. One answer to the crisis in health and care is to ______.

[A] boost the efficiency of hospitals [B] lighten the burden on social care [C] increase resources for primary care [D] reduce the pressure on communities

✅答案：**C** 定位：第三段。`Much more needs to be invested in communities and primary care to reduce our reliance on hospitals.`（需要在社区和初级保健上投入更多，以减少我们对医院的依赖。）说明健康和护理危机的一个答案是增加初级保健资源。

- C：正确。增加初级保健资源。Much more needs to be invested in communities and primary care = increase resources for primary care。
- D：错误。reduce the pressure on communities（减少社区压力）与原文相反。是invested in communities，不是减少社区压力。

## 30. It can be inferred that local leaders should ______.

[A] exercise their power more reasonably [B] develop a stronger sense of responsibility [C] play a bigger role in the health system [D] understand people's health needs better

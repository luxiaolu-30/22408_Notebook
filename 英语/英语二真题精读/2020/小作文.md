# 2020 英二 小作文

## 题目

**Directions:**

Write an email to introduce a historical scenic spot to international students and give them suggestions for visiting. Write your answer in about 100 words.

You should write neatly on the ANSWER SHEET. (10 points)

**场景说明**：假设你是李明，给国际学生写一封电子邮件，介绍一个历史名胜古迹（如故宫），并给出参观建议。属于"介绍+建议信"。

## 高分范文

Dear International Students,

I am writing to introduce a must-visit historical scenic spot—the Forbidden City—and to offer some suggestions for your visit.

As you know, the Forbidden City, also known as the Palace Museum, embodies this nation's profound and diversified ancient culture with its magnificent architecture and precious treasures. It is a remarkable representation of Chinese imperial history spanning over 600 years.

During this trip, it is advisable to pay closer attention to the tourist introductions of each site, which include its origin, cultural background, and translations in multiple languages. Additionally, I suggest you visit early in the morning to avoid the crowds and wear comfortable shoes, as the museum covers a vast area.

I hope that you will enjoy the journey. Should you have any further questions, please do not hesitate to contact me.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

## 范文翻译

亲爱的国际学生们：

我写信是想向你们介绍一处必游的历史名胜——紫禁城（故宫），并为你们的参观提供一些建议。

如你们所知，紫禁城又称故宫博物院，以其宏伟的建筑和珍贵的宝藏体现了这个国家博大精深、丰富多样的古代文化。它是中国皇家历史跨越六百多年的杰出代表。

在此次参观中，建议你们多关注每个景点的游客介绍，其中包括景点的由来、文化背景以及多种语言的翻译。此外，我建议你们清晨前往以避开人群，并穿舒适的鞋子，因为博物馆占地面积很大。

希望你们享受这次旅程。如有其他问题，请随时与我联系。

此致敬礼
李明

## 重点句型与表达

1. **I am writing to introduce... and to offer some suggestions for...** —— 开头段万能句型，交代写信目的（介绍+建议）
2. **...also known as...** —— 又称/又名，用于补充介绍
3. **embody... with...** —— 以……体现……，用于描述文化/历史意义
4. **It is a remarkable representation of...** —— 它是……的杰出代表，用于介绍意义
5. **It is advisable to...** —— 建议……（比you should更正式得体）
6. **Additionally, I suggest you...** —— 此外，我建议你……（引出具体建议）
7. **Should you have any further questions, please do not hesitate to contact me.** —— 虚拟语气结尾，"如有问题请随时联系"，书信万能结尾
8. **Yours sincerely, Li Ming** —— 正式书信落款格式

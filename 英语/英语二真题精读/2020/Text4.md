Now that members of Generation Z Z世代 are graduating college this spring—the most commonly-accepted definition says this generation was born after 1995, give or take a year 大约一年—the attention has been rising steadily 稳步上升 in recent weeks. Gen Zs are about to hit the streets 走上街头 looking for work in a labor market that's tighter than it's been in decades. And employers are planning on hiring about 17 percent more new graduates for jobs in the U.S. this year than last, according to a survey conducted by the National Association of Colleges and Employers 全国大学与雇主协会. Everybody wants to know how the people who will soon inhabit those empty office cubicles 办公室隔间 will differ from those who came before them.

If "entitled 有资格的" is the most common adjective, fairly or not, applied to millennials 千禧一代 (those born between 1981 and 1995), the catchwords 流行语 for Generation Z are practical and cautious. According to the career counselors 职业顾问 and experts who study them, Generation Zs are clear-eyed, economic pragmatists 经济实用主义者. Despite graduating into the best economy in the past 50 years, Gen Zs know what an economic train wreck 经济灾难 looks like. They were impressionable 易受影响的 kids during the crash of 2008, when many of their parents lost their jobs or their life savings 毕生积蓄 or both. They aren't interested in taking any chances 冒险. The booming economy seems to have done little to assuage 缓解 this underlying generational sense of anxious urgency 潜在的代际焦虑感, especially for those who have college debt. College loan balances 大学贷款余额 in the U.S. now stand at a record $1.5 trillion, according to the Federal Reserve 美联储.

One survey from Accenture 埃森哲 found that 88 percent of graduating seniors this year chose their major with a job in mind. In a 2019 survey of University of Georgia students, meanwhile, the career office found the most desirable trait 最令人向往的特质 in a future employer was the ability to offer secure employment 稳定就业, followed by professional development and training, and then inspiring purpose 鼓舞人心的目标. Job security or stability was the second most important career goal (work-life balance was number one), followed by a sense of being dedicated to 致力于 a cause or to feel good about serving the greater good 更大的利益.

That's a big change from the previous generation. "Millennials wanted more flexibility 灵活性 in their lives," notes Tanya Michelsen, Associate Director of YouthSight, a UK-based brand manager that conducts regular 60-day surveys of British youth, in findings that might just as well apply to American youth. "Generation Zs are looking for more certainty and stability, because of the rise of the gig economy 零工经济. They have trouble seeing a financial future and they are quite risk averse 规避风险."

# 2020英二 Text4 长难句精读（原文+拆分+语法+翻译+考点）

## 长难句1

> Now that members of Generation Z are graduating college this spring—the most commonly-accepted definition says this generation was born after 1995, give or take a year—the attention has been rising steadily in recent weeks.

### 句子拆分

主干：**the attention has been rising steadily in recent weeks**
- `Now that members of Generation Z are graduating college this spring`：now that原因状语从句
- 破折号内：`the most commonly-accepted definition says this generation was born after 1995, give or take a year`：插入语，解释Generation Z
  - `this generation was born after 1995`：省略that的宾语从句，作says的宾语

### 生词

now that：既然；Generation Z：Z世代；graduate college：大学毕业；commonly-accepted：普遍接受的；definition：定义；give or take a year：大约一年、上下浮动一年；attention：关注；rise steadily：稳步上升；in recent weeks：最近几周

### 翻译

既然Z世代成员今年春天即将大学毕业——最普遍接受的定义说这一代人出生于1995年之后，上下浮动一年——最近几周关注度一直在稳步上升。

### 考点

**now that原因状语从句+插入语+省略that的宾语从句**；36题D选项的依据——Z世代毕业生正吸引越来越多的公众关注（attention has been rising steadily = drawing growing public attention）。

  - `who will soon inhabit those empty office cubicles`：who定语从句，修饰people

### 生词

### 翻译

### 考点

**how宾语从句+两个who定语从句**；引出全文主题——Z世代与前几代人有何不同。

# 2020 英二 Text4 36–40｜题目+选项+逐选项解析

> 原文主题：Z世代今年春天毕业，关注度上升；劳动力市场紧张，雇主招聘增加；Z世代特征是务实和谨慎——经历2008年危机，知道经济灾难样子，不冒险，繁荣经济未能缓解焦虑；88%选专业时想着工作，最看重稳定就业；与千禧一代追求灵活性不同，Z世代追求确定性和稳定性，规避风险

## 36. Generation Zs graduating college this spring ______.

[A] are recognized for their abilities [B] are optimistic about the labor market [C] are in favor of office job offers [D] are drawing growing public attention

✅答案：**D** 定位：第一段。`the attention has been rising steadily in recent weeks`（最近几周关注度一直在稳步上升）。说明Z世代毕业生正吸引越来越多的公众关注。

- A：错误。are recognized for their abilities（因能力而被认可）无中生有。
- B：错误。are optimistic about the labor market（对劳动力市场乐观）与原文相反。Z世代是cautious（谨慎的），不是乐观。
- C：错误。are in favor of office job offers（赞成办公室工作机会）偷换概念。原文说empty office cubicles（空办公室隔间），是说他们将进入办公室工作，不是赞成办公室工作。
- D：正确。正吸引越来越多的公众关注。attention has been rising steadily = drawing growing public attention。

## 37. Generation Zs are keenly aware ______.

[A] what their parents expect of them [B] how valuable a counselor's advice is [C] what a tough economic situation is like [D] how they differ from past generations

✅答案：**C** 定位：第二段。`Gen Zs know what an economic train wreck looks like. They were impressionable kids during the crash of 2008, when many of their parents lost their jobs or their life savings or both`（Z世代知道经济灾难是什么样子。在2008年金融危机期间，他们是易受影响的孩子，当时他们的许多父母失去了工作或毕生积蓄）。说明Z世代清楚知道艰难的经济状况是什么样。

- A：错误。what their parents expect of them（父母对他们的期望）无中生有。
- B：错误。how valuable a counselor's advice is（顾问建议有多有价值）无中生有。
- C：正确。艰难的经济状况是什么样。know what an economic train wreck looks like + 2008 crisis experience = what a tough economic situation is like。
- D：错误。how they differ from past generations（他们与过去几代人有何不同）是文章讨论的主题，但不是Z世代自己清楚意识到的内容。

## 38. The word "assuage" (Line 7, Para.2) is closest in meaning to ______.

[A] deepen [B] define [C] maintain [D] relieve

✅答案：**D** 定位：第二段。`The booming economy seems to have done little to assuage this underlying generational sense of anxious urgency`（繁荣的经济似乎几乎没有____这种潜在的代际焦虑感）。繁荣经济应该是"缓解"焦虑，但几乎没有做到。assuage = relieve（缓解）。

- A：错误。deepen（加深）与原文逻辑相反。繁荣经济不会加深焦虑。
- B：错误。define（定义）不符合语境。
- C：错误。maintain（维持）不符合语境。
- D：正确。relieve（缓解）。繁荣经济几乎没有缓解焦虑感=assuage。

## 39. It can be learned from Paragraph 3 that Generation Zs ______.

[A] give top priority to professional training [B] have a clear idea about their future jobs [C] care little about their job performance [D] think it hard to achieve work-life balance

✅答案：**B** 定位：第三段。`88 percent of graduating seniors this year chose their major with a job in mind`（今年88%的应届毕业生选择专业时心中想着工作）+ `the most desirable trait in a future employer was the ability to offer secure employment`（未来雇主最令人向往的特质是提供稳定就业的能力）。说明Z世代对未来工作有清晰想法。

- A：错误。give top priority to professional training（最重视职业培训）偷换概念。原文说最看重secure employment（稳定就业），职业发展和培训排第二。
- B：正确。对未来工作有清晰想法。chose their major with a job in mind + know what they want in employer = have a clear idea about their future jobs。
- C：错误。care little about their job performance（不太关心工作表现）无中生有。
- D：错误。think it hard to achieve work-life balance（认为难以实现工作与生活平衡）偷换概念。原文说work-life balance was number one（工作与生活平衡排第一），是最重要的职业目标，不是认为难以实现。

## 40. Michelsen thinks that compared with millennials, Generation Zs are ______.

[A] less adventurous [B] less realistic [C] more generous [D] more diligent

✅答案：**A** 定位：最后一段。`Millennials wanted more flexibility in their lives`（千禧一代想要更多生活灵活性）+ `Generation Zs are looking for more certainty and stability … they are quite risk averse`（Z世代正在寻找更多的确定性和稳定性……他们相当规避风险）。说明与千禧一代相比，Z世代更不冒险。

- A：正确。不那么爱冒险。looking for certainty and stability + risk averse = less adventurous。
- B：错误。less realistic（不那么务实）与原文相反。Z世代是economic pragmatists（经济实用主义者），更务实。
- C：错误。more generous（更慷慨）无中生有。
- D：错误。more diligent（更勤奋）无中生有。

### 本篇命题规律小结（英二阅读）

1. 社会/代际类文章：关注**背景（Z世代毕业，关注度上升）→特征（务实谨慎）→原因（2008年危机经历）→数据（88%选专业想着工作，最看重稳定就业）→对比（千禧一代追求灵活性 vs Z世代追求确定性稳定性）**的结构
2. 细节题：定位到**now that原因状语+主句**（attention has been rising steadily）
3. 推理题：定位到**what宾语从句+when非限制性定语从句**（know what an economic train wreck looks like + 2008 crash）
4. 词义题：通过**上下文逻辑**（booming economy done little to ___ anxious urgency → relieve）
5. 对比题：定位到**人物直接引语**（Millennials wanted flexibility vs Zs looking for certainty and stability, risk averse）

# 2015 英二 大作文

## 图表描述

饼图（Pie Chart）：某城市居民春节期间消费支出比例分布

- 新年礼物（New Year presents）：40%（占比最高）
- 交通（Transportation）：20%
- 团聚聚餐（Reunion dinner）：20%
- 其他（Others）：20%

## 题目

**Directions:**

Write an essay based on the following chart. In your writing, you should

1) interpret the chart, and
2) give your comments.

You should write about 150 words on the ANSWER SHEET. (15 points)

## 高分范文

The pie chart above clearly illustrates the percentage of residents' spending during the Spring Festival in a certain city of China. As is shown, the money spent on New Year presents accounts for 40%, ranking first among all four categories. Transportation and reunion dinners each make up 20%, while the remaining 20% goes to other categories of expenditure.

Several reasons may account for this phenomenon. First and foremost, giving gifts during the Spring Festival is a long-standing Chinese tradition, symbolizing good wishes and respect for family and friends. Moreover, with the improvement of living standards, people tend to spend more on gifts to express their affection. Additionally, transportation costs are inevitable as people travel to visit relatives and friends during the holiday. Reunion dinners, as an essential part of the celebration, also account for a significant portion of the expense.

From my perspective, it is understandable that people spend a considerable amount on gifts during the Spring Festival. However, we should also be rational consumers and avoid excessive spending. After all, the true meaning of the holiday lies in family reunion and emotional connection rather than material consumption.

## 范文翻译

上面的饼图清楚地展示了中国某城市居民春节期间消费支出的百分比。如图所示，新年礼物支出占40%，在四类消费中排名第一。交通和团圆聚餐各占20%，其余20%属于其他类别的支出。

有几个原因可以解释这一现象。首先，春节期间送礼物是中国悠久的传统，象征着对亲友的美好祝愿和尊重。其次，随着生活水平的提高，人们倾向于在礼物上花费更多以表达感情。此外，春节期间人们走亲访友，交通费用不可避免。团圆聚餐作为庆祝活动的重要组成部分，也占了支出的相当比例。

在我看来，人们在春节期间在礼物上花费较多是可以理解的。然而，我们也应该理性消费，避免过度支出。毕竟，节日的真正意义在于家人团聚和情感交流，而非物质消费。

## 重点句型与表达

1. **The pie chart above clearly illustrates the percentage of...**（上面的饼图清楚地展示了……的百分比）——饼图描述开头句型
2. **As is shown, ...accounts for...%, ranking first among...**（如图所示，……占……%，在……中排名第一）——描述占比和排名
3. **...each make up 20%, while the remaining 20% goes to...**（……各占20%，其余20%用于……）——while表示对比，描述剩余数据
4. **Several reasons may account for this phenomenon.**（有几个原因可以解释这一现象）——原因分析段开头句型
5. **First and foremost, ...is a long-standing Chinese tradition, symbolizing...**（首先，……是中国悠久的传统，象征着……）——列举原因+现在分词作伴随状语
6. **Moreover, with the improvement of living standards, people tend to...**（其次，随着生活水平的提高，人们倾向于……）——with短语表原因/背景
7. **From my perspective, it is understandable that... However, we should also...**（在我看来，……是可以理解的。然而，我们也应该……）——评论段让步+转折句型
8. **After all, the true meaning of...lies in...rather than...**（毕竟，……的真正意义在于……而非……）——结尾升华句型，A rather than B表示"是A而不是B"



For years, studies have found that first-generation college students 第一代大学生—those who do not have a parent with a college degree—lag 落后 other students on a range of 一系列 education achievement factors. Their grades are lower and their dropout rates 辍学率 are higher. But since such students are most likely to advance economically 经济上进步 if they succeed in higher education, colleges and universities have pushed for decades to recruit 招收 more of them. This has created "a paradox 悖论" in that recruiting first-generation students, but then watching many of them fail, means that higher education has "continued to reproduce 复制 and widen, rather than close" achievement gap 成就差距 based on social class, according to the depressing beginning of a paper forthcoming 即将发表的 in the journal Psychological Science.

But the article is actually quite optimistic, as it outlines 概述 a potential solution to this problem, suggesting that an approach (which involves a one-hour, next-to-no-cost 几乎零成本的 program) can close 63 percent of the achievement gap (measured by such factors as grades) between first-generation and other students.

The authors of the paper are from different universities, and their findings 发现 are based on a study involving 147 students (who completed the project) at an unnamed private university. First generation was defined as not having a parent with a four-year college degree. Most of the first-generation students (59.1 percent) were recipients 接受者 of Pell Grants 佩尔助学金, a federal grant for undergraduates 本科生 with financial need, while this was true only for 8.6 percent of the students with at least one parent with a four-year degree.

Their thesis 论点—that a relatively modest 适度的 intervention 干预 could have a big impact—was based on the view that first-generation students may be most lacking not in potential 潜力 but in practical knowledge about how to deal with the issues that face most college students. They cite 引用 past research by several authors to show that this is the gap that must be narrowed 缩小 to close the achievement gap.

Many first-generation students "struggle to navigate 应对 the middle-class culture of higher education, learn the 'rules of the game 游戏规则,' and take advantage of 利用 college resources," they write. And this becomes more of a problem when colleges don't talk about the class advantage and disadvantages of different groups of students. Because US colleges and universities seldom acknowledge 承认 how social class can affect students' educational experience, many first-generation students lack sight 了解 about why they are struggling and do not understand how students like them can improve.

## 长难句1

> For years, studies have found that first-generation college students—those who do not have a parent with a college degree—lag other students on a range of education achievement factors.

### 句子拆分

主干：**studies have found that …**
- that引导宾语从句，从句主干：**first-generation college students lag other students**
  - `—those who do not have a parent with a college degree—`：同位语，解释first-generation college students
    - `who do not have a parent with a college degree`：who定语从句，修饰those
  - `on a range of education achievement factors`：介词短语作状语

### 生词

first-generation college students：第一代大学生；lag：落后；a range of：一系列；education achievement factor：教育成就因素

### 翻译

多年来，研究发现，第一代大学生——父母没有大学学位的学生——在一系列教育成就因素上落后于其他学生。

### 考点

## 长难句2

> This has created "a paradox" in that recruiting first-generation students, but then watching many of them fail, means that higher education has "continued to reproduce and widen, rather than close" achievement gap based on social class.

### 句子拆分

主干：**This has created "a paradox"**
- `in that recruiting first-generation students, but then watching many of them fail, means that …`：in that引导原因状语从句
  - 从句主干：`recruiting first-generation students, but then watching many of them fail, means that …`
    - `recruiting…`和`watching…`是动名词并列作主语（but连接）
    - that引导宾语从句：`higher education has "continued to reproduce and widen, rather than close" achievement gap based on social class`
      - `rather than close`：rather than连接并列
      - `based on social class`：过去分词作后置定语，修饰gap

### 生词

paradox：悖论；in that：因为；recruit：招收；reproduce：复制、再生产；widen：扩大；rather than：而不是；achievement gap：成就差距；social class：社会阶层

### 翻译

这造成了一个"悖论"：招收第一代大学生，然后看着他们中的许多人失败，意味着高等教育"继续复制和扩大，而不是缩小"基于社会阶层的成就差距。

### 考点

**in that原因状语从句+动名词并列作主语+that宾语从句+rather than**；26题C选项的核心依据——招收第一代大学生没有达到缩小差距的初衷，反而扩大了差距=错过了最初目的。

## 长难句3

> But the article is actually quite optimistic, as it outlines a potential solution to this problem, suggesting that an approach (which involves a one-hour, next-to-no-cost program) can close 63 percent of the achievement gap (measured by such factors as grades) between first-generation and other students.

### 句子拆分

主干：**the article is actually quite optimistic**
- `as it outlines a potential solution to this problem`：as原因状语从句
- `suggesting that an approach … can close 63 percent of the achievement gap … between first-generation and other students`：现在分词作伴随状语
  - that引导宾语从句，从句主干：`an approach can close 63 percent of the achievement gap`
    - `(which involves a one-hour, next-to-no-cost program)`：which定语从句，修饰approach
    - `(measured by such factors as grades)`：过去分词作后置定语，修饰gap
    - `between first-generation and other students`：介词短语修饰gap

### 生词

optimistic：乐观的；outline：概述；potential solution：潜在解决方案；suggest：表明；approach：方法；involve：涉及；next-to-no-cost：几乎零成本的；close：缩小；measure：衡量；grade：成绩

### 翻译

但这篇文章实际上相当乐观，因为它概述了这个问题的潜在解决方案，表明一种方法（涉及一个一小时、几乎零成本的项目）可以缩小第一代大学生和其他学生之间63%的成就差距（以成绩等因素衡量）。

### 考点

**as原因状语从句+现在分词伴随状语+that宾语从句+which定语从句+过去分词后置定语**；27题A选项依据——文章乐观是因为问题可解决（有潜在解决方案）。

## 长难句4

# 2015 英二 Text2 26–30｜题目+选项+逐选项解析

## 26. Recruiting more first-generation students has ______.

[A] reduced their dropout rates [B] narrowed the achievement gap [C] missed its original purpose [D] depressed college students

✅答案：**C** 定位：第一段。高校招收第一代大学生的初衷是帮助他们经济进步、缩小差距，但结果是"watching many of them fail"（看着他们失败），高等教育"continued to reproduce and widen, rather than close" achievement gap（继续复制和扩大而不是缩小成就差距）。即招收更多第一代大学生错过了最初目的。

- A：错误。reduced their dropout rates（降低辍学率）与原文相反。原文说他们的dropout rates are higher（辍学率更高）。
- B：错误。narrowed the achievement gap（缩小成就差距）与原文相反。原文说widen, rather than close（扩大而不是缩小）。
- C：正确。错过了最初目的。初衷是帮助他们成功、缩小差距，结果是扩大差距=missed original purpose。
- D：错误。depressed college students（使大学生抑郁）偷换概念。原文说论文开头depressing（令人沮丧的），不是学生抑郁。

## 27. The author of the research article are optimistic because ______.

[A] the problem is solvable [B] their approach is costless [C] the recruiting rate has increased [D] their finding appeal to students

✅答案：**A** 定位：第二段。`the article is actually quite optimistic, as it outlines a potential solution to this problem`（文章相当乐观，因为它概述了这个问题的潜在解决方案）。乐观是因为问题可解决。

- A：正确。问题是可解决的。outlines a potential solution = the problem is solvable。
- B：错误。their approach is costless（他们的方法是免费的）偷换概念。原文说next-to-no-cost（几乎零成本），不是完全免费（costless），且这是方法的特点，不是乐观的原因。
- C：错误。the recruiting rate has increased（招收率增加了）无中生有。
- D：错误。their finding appeal to students（他们的发现吸引学生）无中生有。

## 28. The study suggests that most first-generation students ______.

[A] study at private universities [B] are from single-parent families [C] are in need of financial support [D] have failed their college

✅答案：**C** 定位：第三段。`Most of the first-generation students (59.1 percent) were recipients of Pell Grants, a federal grant for undergraduates with financial need`（大多数第一代大学生是佩尔助学金接受者，该助学金面向有经济需求的本科生）。即大多数第一代大学生需要经济支持。

- A：错误。study at private universities（在私立大学学习）偷换概念。研究是在一所unnamed private university（未具名的私立大学）进行的，不是说大多数第一代大学生在私立大学学习。
- B：错误。are from single-parent families（来自单亲家庭）无中生有。原文说的是父母没有大学学位，不是单亲。
- C：正确。需要经济支持。recipients of Pell Grants for undergraduates with financial need = in need of financial support。
- D：错误。have failed their college（大学挂科了）过度推断。原文说many of them fail（许多人失败），但研究中的147名学生completed the project（完成了项目），且most不等于all。

## 29. The author of the paper believe that first-generation students ______.

[A] are actually indifferent to the achievement gap [B] can have a potential influence on other students [C] may lack opportunities to apply for research projects [D] are inexperienced in handling their issues at college

✅答案：**D** 定位：第四段。`first-generation students may be most lacking not in potential but in practical knowledge about how to deal with the issues that face most college students`（第一代大学生最缺乏的不是潜力，而是关于如何处理大多数大学生面临的问题的实用知识）。即他们在处理大学问题上缺乏经验。

## 30. We may infer from the last paragraph that ______.

[A] universities often reject the culture of the middle-class [B] students are usually to blame for their lack of resources [C] social class greatly helps enrich educational experiences [D] colleges are partly responsible for the problem in question

✅答案：**D** 推理题。定位：最后一段。`this becomes more of a problem when colleges don't talk about the class advantage and disadvantages`（当大学不谈论不同群体的阶级优势和劣势时，这就更成问题了）+ `Because US colleges and universities seldom acknowledge how social class can affect students' educational experience`（因为美国高校很少承认社会阶层如何影响学生的教育经历）。大学的不作为加剧了问题，说明大学对问题负有部分责任。

- A：错误。universities often reject the culture of the middle-class（大学经常拒绝中产阶级文化）与原文相反。原文说学生struggle to navigate the middle-class culture（努力应对中产阶级文化），大学没有拒绝这种文化。
- B：错误。students are usually to blame for their lack of resources（学生缺乏资源通常应受责备）与原文相反。作者认为大学不谈论阶级优势劣势是问题，不是学生的错。
- C：错误。social class greatly helps enrich educational experiences（社会阶层极大地帮助丰富教育经历）曲解原文。原文说social class can affect students' educational experience（社会阶层影响教育经历），不是帮助丰富。
- D：正确。大学对所讨论的问题负有部分责任。colleges don't talk about + seldom acknowledge = colleges are partly responsible。

### 本篇命题规律小结（英二阅读）

1. 教育/社会研究类文章：关注**问题描述→研究发现→原因分析→责任归属**的结构
2. 细节题：定位到**数据和研究结论**（59.1%接受佩尔助学金、63%差距可缩小）
3. 推理题：基于**因果关系**（大学不谈论阶级优势→大学负有部分责任）
4. 常见干扰：与原文相反（narrowed gap vs widen gap, costless vs next-to-no-cost）、偷换概念（研究在私立大学进行vs学生在私立大学学习）、过度推断（many fail vs most have failed）
5. 态度/责任题：关注**seldom acknowledge、don't talk about**等否定表达，往往指向机构责任

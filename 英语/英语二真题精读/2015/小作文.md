# 2015 英二 小作文

## 题目

**Directions:**

Suppose your university is going to host a summer camp for high school students. Write a notice to

1) briefly introduce the camp activities, and
2) call for volunteers.

You should write about 100 words on the ANSWER SHEET.

Do not use your name or the name of your university.

Do not write your address. (10 points)

## 高分范文

**Notice**

June 20, 2015

To enrich high school students' summer life and help them gain a better understanding of university education, our university is scheduled to host a summer camp from July 10 to July 20 on campus.

The camp will feature a wide variety of activities, including campus tours, academic lectures by renowned professors, interactive group discussions, and cultural excursions to local museums. Participants will also have the opportunity to interact with current university students and experience dormitory life firsthand.

We are now recruiting 30 volunteers to assist with organizing activities, guiding participants, and providing logistical support. Volunteers should be outgoing, responsible, and passionate about working with young people. Those interested should send their applications to summercamp@university.edu by June 30.

The Student Union

## 范文翻译

**通知**

2015年6月20日

为丰富高中生的暑期生活并帮助他们更好地了解大学教育，我校计划于7月10日至7月20日在校内举办暑期夏令营。

本次夏令营将开展丰富多彩的活动，包括校园参观、知名教授学术讲座、互动小组讨论以及当地博物馆文化参观。参与者还有机会与在校大学生互动交流，亲身体验宿舍生活。

现招募30名志愿者，协助组织活动、引导参与者及提供后勤保障。志愿者应性格开朗、责任心强、热爱与年轻人合作。有意者请于6月30日前将申请发送至 summercamp@university.edu。

学生会

## 重点句型与表达

1. **To enrich...and help...**（为丰富……并帮助……）——用不定式表目的，适合通知类开头
2. **our university is scheduled to host...**（我校计划举办……）——be scheduled to do表示"计划做"，正式用语
3. **The camp will feature a wide variety of activities, including...**（夏令营将开展丰富多彩的活动，包括……）——feature+名词表示"以……为特色"
4. **Participants will have the opportunity to...**（参与者将有机会……）——招募/通知类常用句型
5. **We are now recruiting...volunteers to assist with...**（现招募……志愿者协助……）——call for volunteers 的核心表达
6. **Volunteers should be outgoing, responsible, and passionate about...**（志愿者应……热爱……）——列举品质要求
7. **Those interested should send their applications to...by...**（有意者请于……前将申请发送至……）——通知结尾给出行动指引
8. **Do not use your name or the name of your university.** ——注意题目要求：不出现真实姓名和校名，用"our university""The Student Union"代替

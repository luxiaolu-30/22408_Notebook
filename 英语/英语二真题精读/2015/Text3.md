Even in traditional offices, "the lingua franca 通用语 of corporate America 美国企业 has gotten much more emotional 情感化的 and much more right-brained 右脑的 than it was 20 years ago," said Harvard Business School professor Nancy Koehn. She started spinning off 列举 examples. "If you and I parachuted 空降到 back to Fortune 500 companies in 1990, we would see much less frequent use of terms like journey, mission 使命, passion 热情. There were goals, there were strategies, there were objectives 目标, but we didn't talk about energy; we didn't talk about passion."

Koehn pointed out that this new era of corporate vocabulary 企业词汇 is very "team"-oriented 以团队为导向的—and not by coincidence 并非巧合. "Let's not forget sports—in male-dominated 男性主导的 corporate America, it's still a big deal 重要的事. It's not explicitly 明确地 conscious; it's the idea that I'm a coach 教练, and you're my team, and we're in this together. There are lots and lots of CEOs in very different companies, but most think of themselves as coaches and this is their team and they want to win."

These terms are also intended to infuse 注入 work with meaning—and, as Khurana points out, increase allegiance 忠诚 to the firm. "You have the importation 引入 of terminology 术语 that historically used to be associated with non-profit organizations 非营利组织 and religious organizations: Terms like vision 愿景, values, passion, and purpose 目标," said Khurana.

This new focus on personal fulfillment 个人成就感 can help keep employees motivated 有动力的 amid 在…之中 increasingly loud debates over work-life balance 工作与生活平衡. The "mommy wars 妈妈战争" of the 1990s are still going on today, prompting 引发 arguments about why women still can't have it all 拥有一切 and books like Sheryl Sandberg's Lean In 《向前一步》, whose title has become a buzzword 流行词 in its own right 本身. Terms like unplug 断网, offline 离线, life-hack 生活技巧, bandwidth 带宽, and capacity 能力 are all about setting boundaries 设定界限 between the office and the home. But if your work is your "passion," you'll be more likely to devote yourself to 投身于 it, even if that means going home for dinner and then working long after the kids are in bed 睡觉.

But this seems to be the irony 讽刺 of office speak 办公室用语: Everyone makes fun of 取笑 it, but managers love it, companies depend on 依赖 it, and regular people 普通人 willingly absorb 接受 it. As Nunberg said, "You can get people to think it's nonsense 胡说八道 at the same time that you buy into it 相信它." In a workplace that's fundamentally 根本上 indifferent 漠不关心的 to your life and its meaning, office speak can help you figure out 弄明白 how you relate to 与…联系 your work—and how your work defines who you are.

## 长难句1

> Even in traditional offices, "the lingua franca of corporate America has gotten much more emotional and much more right-brained than it was 20 years ago," said Harvard Business School professor Nancy Koehn.

### 句子拆分

倒装句（直接引语前置，谓语said倒装）：**"…" said Harvard Business School professor Nancy Koehn**
- 直接引语内主干：**the lingua franca of corporate America has gotten much more emotional and much more right-brained**
  - `than it was 20 years ago`：than比较状语从句

### 生词

lingua franca：通用语、混合语；corporate America：美国企业；emotional：情感化的；right-brained：右脑的（指感性、创造性的）

### 翻译

哈佛商学院教授南希·科恩说，即使在传统办公室里，"美国企业的通用语比20年前变得更加情感化、更加右脑化"。

### 考点

**直接引语前置倒装+more…than比较级**；31题A选项定位句——办公室语言变得更情感化（more emotional）。

## 长难句3

> Koehn pointed out that this new era of corporate vocabulary is very "team"-oriented—and not by coincidence.

### 句子拆分

主干：**Koehn pointed out that …**
- that引导宾语从句，从句主干：**this new era of corporate vocabulary is very "team"-oriented**
- `—and not by coincidence—`：插入语，"并非巧合"

### 生词

point out：指出；era：时代；corporate vocabulary：企业词汇；team-oriented：以团队为导向的；by coincidence：巧合地

### 翻译

科恩指出，这个企业词汇的新时代非常"以团队为导向"——而且并非巧合。

### 考点

**that宾语从句+插入语**；32题的过渡句——团队导向的词汇与体育文化有关，并非巧合。

## 长难句4

> It's not explicitly conscious; it's the idea that I'm a coach, and you're my team, and we're in this together.

### 句子拆分

并列句，分号连接
分句1主干：**It's not explicitly conscious**
分句2主干：**it's the idea**
- `that I'm a coach, and you're my team, and we're in this together`：that同位语从句，解释idea
  - 三个并列分句：I'm a coach, you're my team, we're in this together

### 生词

explicitly：明确地；conscious：有意识的；coach：教练；be in this together：同舟共济、共同面对

### 翻译

这不是明确有意识的；而是这样一种理念：我是教练，你是我的团队，我们同舟共济。

### 考点

**that同位语从句+三个并列分句**；解释企业词汇团队导向的核心理念——教练-团队模式来自体育文化。

## 长难句5

> These terms are also intended to infuse work with meaning—and, as Khurana points out, increase allegiance to the firm.

### 句子拆分

主干：**These terms are also intended to infuse work with meaning—and … increase allegiance to the firm**
- `be intended to`：旨在
- `infuse A with B`：给A注入B
- `—and, as Khurana points out, increase allegiance to the firm—`：and连接并列不定式，`as Khurana points out`插入语

### 生词

be intended to：旨在；infuse：注入；meaning：意义；as Khurana points out：正如库拉纳指出的；allegiance：忠诚；firm：公司

### 翻译

这些术语也旨在给工作注入意义——而且正如库拉纳指出的，增加对公司的忠诚。

### 考点

**be intended to+infuse A with B+as插入语+并列不定式**；33题D选项定位句——引入术语旨在加强员工忠诚（increase allegiance = strengthen employee loyalty）。

## 长难句6

> You have the importation of terminology that historically used to be associated with non-profit organizations and religious organizations: Terms like vision, values, passion, and purpose.

### 句子拆分

主干：**You have the importation of terminology**
- `that historically used to be associated with non-profit organizations and religious organizations`：that定语从句，修饰terminology
  - `used to be`：过去曾经是
  - `be associated with`：与…有关
- 冒号后举例：`Terms like vision, values, passion, and purpose`

### 生词

importation：引入、进口；terminology：术语；historically：历史上；used to be：过去曾经是；be associated with：与…有关；non-profit organization：非营利组织；religious organization：宗教组织；vision：愿景；value：价值观；purpose：目标、宗旨

### 翻译

你引入了历史上曾经与非营利组织和宗教组织相关的术语：像愿景、价值观、热情和目标这样的词。

### 考点

**that定语从句+used to be+冒号举例**；33题的具体内容——从非营利/宗教组织引入术语，目的是增加忠诚。

## 长难句7

> The "mommy wars" of the 1990s are still going on today, prompting arguments about why women still can't have it all and books like Sheryl Sandberg's Lean In, whose title has become a buzzword in its own right.

### 句子拆分

主干：**The "mommy wars" of the 1990s are still going on today**
- `prompting arguments about why women still can't have it all and books like Sheryl Sandberg's Lean In`：现在分词作结果状语
  - `why women still can't have it all`：why宾语从句，作about的宾语
  - arguments和books是prompting的并列宾语
- `whose title has become a buzzword in its own right`：whose定语从句，修饰Lean In

### 生词

mommy wars：妈妈战争（指职场妈妈与全职妈妈之间的争论）；go on：继续；prompt：引发、促使；argument：争论；have it all：拥有一切（事业家庭兼顾）；Lean In：《向前一步》（谢丽尔·桑德伯格的书）；buzzword：流行词；in its own right：本身、凭自身

### 翻译

20世纪90年代的"妈妈战争"至今仍在继续，引发了关于为什么女性仍然不能拥有一切的争论，也催生了谢丽尔·桑德伯格的《向前一步》这样的书，其书名本身已成为一个流行词。

### 考点

**现在分词作结果状语+并列宾语+why宾语从句+whose定语从句**；34题A选项依据——《向前一步》为职场女性发声（voices for working women）。

## 长难句8

> But if your work is your "passion," you'll be more likely to devote yourself to it, even if that means going home for dinner and then working long after the kids are in bed.

### 句子拆分

主干：**you'll be more likely to devote yourself to it**
- `if your work is your "passion"`：if条件状语从句
- `even if that means going home for dinner and then working long after the kids are in bed`：even if让步状语从句
  - `going home for dinner`和`working long after the kids are in bed`是and连接的并列动名词，作means的宾语
  - `after the kids are in bed`：after时间状语从句

### 生词

passion：热情；be likely to：可能；devote oneself to：投身于、致力于；even if：即使；go home for dinner：回家吃晚饭；in bed：睡觉

但如果你的工作是你的"热情"所在，你就更有可能投身其中，即使这意味着回家吃晚饭，然后在孩子入睡后工作很久。

### 考点

**if条件句+even if让步状语从句+并列动名词+after时间状语从句**；解释企业语言用"热情"等词的目的——让员工更投入工作。

## 长难句10

> In a workplace that's fundamentally indifferent to your life and its meaning, office speak can help you figure out how you relate to your work—and how your work defines who you are.

### 句子拆分

主干：**office speak can help you figure out how you relate to your work—and how your work defines who you are**
- `In a workplace that's fundamentally indifferent to your life and its meaning`：地点状语
  - `that's fundamentally indifferent to your life and its meaning`：that定语从句，修饰workplace
- `how you relate to your work`和`how your work defines who you are`：how引导的并列宾语从句，作figure out的宾语
  - `who you are`：who宾语从句，作defines的宾语

### 生词

fundamentally：根本上；indifferent to：对…漠不关心；figure out：弄明白；relate to：与…联系、理解；define：定义

### 翻译

在一个根本上对你的生活及其意义漠不关心的工作场所，办公室用语可以帮助你弄明白你如何与你的工作联系起来——以及你的工作如何定义你是谁。

### 考点

**that定语从句+两个how并列宾语从句+who宾语从句**；全文结论句——办公室用语的功能：帮助员工理解工作与自我的关系。

# 2015 英二 Text3 31–35｜题目+选项+逐选项解析

> 原文主题：美国企业办公室语言变得更情感化、团队导向，从非营利/宗教组织引入术语以增加员工忠诚，普通人取笑但接受它

## 31. According to Nancy Koehn, office language has become ______.

[A] more emotional [B] more objective [C] less energetic [D] less strategic

✅答案：**A** 定位：第一段。`the lingua franca of corporate America has gotten much more emotional and much more right-brained than it was 20 years ago`（美国企业通用语比20年前变得更情感化、更右脑化）。

- A：正确。更情感化。more emotional是原文原词。
- B：错误。more objective（更客观）与原文相反。更情感化=更不客观。
- C：错误。less energetic（更少活力）与原文相反。现在更多谈论energy（活力）。
- D：错误。less strategic（更少战略性）无中生有。过去有strategies（战略），但没说现在更少战略性。

## 32. "team"-oriented corporate vocabulary is closely related to ______.

[A] historical incidents [B] gender difference [C] sports culture [D] athletic executives

✅答案：**C** 定位：第二段。`Let's not forget sports—in male-dominated corporate America, it's still a big deal. It's not explicitly conscious; it's the idea that I'm a coach, and you're my team`（别忘了体育——在男性主导的美国企业界，体育仍然很重要。我是教练，你是我的团队）。团队导向的词汇与体育文化紧密相关。

- A：错误。historical incidents（历史事件）无中生有。
- B：错误。gender difference（性别差异）偷换概念。原文说male-dominated（男性主导），但团队导向词汇与体育有关，不是性别差异。
- C：正确。体育文化。sports + coach + team = sports culture。
- D：错误。athletic executives（运动型高管）无中生有。原文说CEO们think of themselves as coaches（认为自己是教练），不是说他们是运动型的。

## 33. Khurana believes that the importation of terminology aims to ______.

[A] revive historical terms [B] promote company image [C] foster corporate cooperation [D] strengthen employee loyalty

✅答案：**D** 定位：第三段。`These terms are also intended to infuse work with meaning—and, as Khurana points out, increase allegiance to the firm`（这些术语旨在给工作注入意义——而且正如库拉纳指出的，增加对公司的忠诚）。引入术语旨在加强员工忠诚。

- A：错误。revive historical terms（复兴历史术语）曲解原文。引入历史上与非营利/宗教组织相关的术语，目的不是复兴这些术语本身，而是增加忠诚。
- B：错误。promote company image（提升公司形象）无中生有。
- C：错误。foster corporate cooperation（促进企业合作）无中生有。
- D：正确。加强员工忠诚。increase allegiance to the firm = strengthen employee loyalty。

## 34. It can be inferred that Lean In ______.

[A] voices for working women [B] appeals to passionate workaholics [C] triggers debates among mommies [D] praises motivated employees

✅答案：**A** 推理题。定位：第四段。`The "mommy wars" … prompting arguments about why women still can't have it all and books like Sheryl Sandberg's Lean In`（妈妈战争引发关于为什么女性仍然不能拥有一切的争论，也催生了《向前一步》这样的书）。《向前一步》讨论的是女性事业与家庭的问题，为职场女性发声。

- A：正确。为职场女性发声。关于女性不能拥有一切的争论+《向前一步》= voices for working women。
- B：错误。appeals to passionate workaholics（吸引充满热情的工作狂）无中生有。
- C：错误。triggers debates among mommies（引发妈妈们之间的争论）因果倒置。是妈妈战争引发了争论和这本书，不是这本书引发争论。
- D：错误。praises motivated employees（赞扬有动力的员工）无中生有。

## 35. Which of the following statements is true about office speak?

[A] Managers admire it but avoid it [B] Linguists believe it to be nonsense [C] Companies find it to be fundamental [D] Regular people mock it but accept it

✅答案：**D** 定位：第五段。`Everyone makes fun of it, but managers love it, companies depend on it, and regular people willingly absorb it`（每个人都取笑它，但经理们喜欢它，公司依赖它，普通人愿意接受它）。普通人取笑但接受它。

- A：错误。Managers admire it but avoid it（经理们欣赏但避免它）与原文相反。原文说managers love it（经理们喜欢它），没说避免。
- B：错误。Linguists believe it to be nonsense（语言学家认为它是胡说八道）偷换概念。Nunberg说"You can get people to think it's nonsense at the same time that you buy into it"（你可以让人们认为它是胡说八道，同时又相信它），不是语言学家认为它是胡说八道。
- C：错误。Companies find it to be fundamental（公司发现它是根本的）程度过重。原文说companies depend on it（公司依赖它），depend on不等于fundamental（根本的）。
- D：正确。普通人取笑但接受它。makes fun of it + regular people willingly absorb it = mock it but accept it。

### 本篇命题规律小结（英二阅读）

1. 社会/语言类文章：关注**现象描述→原因分析→目的功能→讽刺结论**的结构
2. 细节题：定位到**人物+said/pointed out**，答案是原文原词或同义替换
3. 推理题：基于**上下文语境**（妈妈战争+女性不能拥有一切→《向前一步》为女性发声）
4. 判断题（which is true）：逐项回原文定位，注意**程度过重**的干扰项（depend on ≠ fundamental）
5. 常见干扰：与原文相反（more objective vs more emotional）、因果倒置（书引发争论vs争论催生书）、偷换概念（语言学家认为是胡说vs人们可以认为是胡说）

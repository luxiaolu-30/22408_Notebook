# 2019 英二 完形填空

## 原文

Weighing yourself regularly is a wonderful way to stay aware of any significant weight fluctuations. (1), when done too often, this habit can sometimes hurt more than it (2).

As for me, weighing myself every day caused me to shift my focus from being generally healthy and physically active, to focusing (3) on the scale. That was bad for my overall fitness goals. I had gained weight in the form of muscle mass, but thinking only of (4) the number on the scale, I altered my training program. That conflicted with how I needed to train to (5) my goals.

I also found that weighing myself daily did not provide an accurate (6) of the hard work and progress I was making in the gym. It takes about three weeks to a month to notice significant changes in your weight (7) altering your training program. The most (8) changes will be observed in skill level, strength and inches lost.

For these (9), I stopped weighing myself every day and switched to a bimonthly weighing schedule (10). Since weight loss is not my goal, it is less important for me to (11) my weight each week. Weighing every other week allows me to observe and (12) any significant weight changes. That tells me whether I need to (13) my training program.

I use my bimonthly weigh-in (14) to get information about my nutrition as well. If my training intensity remains the same, but I'm constantly (15) and dropping weight, this is a (16) that I need to increase my daily caloric intake.

The (17) to stop weighing myself every day has done wonders for my overall health, fitness and well-being. I'm experiencing increased zeal for working out since I no longer carry the burden of a (18) morning weigh-in. I've also experienced greater success in achieving my specific fitness goals, (19) I'm training according to those goals, not the numbers on a scale.

Rather than (20) over the scale, turn your focus to how you look, feel, how your clothes fit and your overall energy level.

### 参考答案
1.D 2.A 3.B 4.B 5.D 6.B 7.A 8.D 9.C 10.A 11.A 12.D 13.B 14.A 15.C 16.D 17.C 18.A 19.D 20.A

## 重点解析

- **第1题（D. However）**：逻辑连词。前句说定期称重是好事，后句说太频繁会"hurt"（伤害），语意转折。However=然而，表转折关系。
- **第2题（A. helps）**：动词辨析。"hurt more than it helps"（弊大于利）。helps=有帮助，与hurt对比，"伤害多于帮助"。
- **第3题（B. solely）**：副词辨析。"focusing solely on the scale"（仅仅关注体重秤上的数字）。solely=仅仅、只，与from being generally healthy对比，说明注意力从整体健康转向了单纯的体重数字。
- **第4题（B. lowering）**：动宾搭配。"thinking only of lowering the number on the scale"（一心只想降低秤上的数字）。lowering=降低，体重增加了（肌肉），但只想着把数字降下来。
- **第5题（D. reach）**：固定搭配。"reach my goals"（达到目标）。reach goals=实现目标，固定搭配。
- **第6题（B. depiction）**：名词辨析。"an accurate depiction of the hard work and progress"（对努力和进步的准确描述）。depiction=描述、描绘，称重不能准确反映健身进展。
- **第7题（A. due to）**：介词短语。"significant changes in your weight due to altering your training program"（由于调整训练计划而导致的体重显著变化）。due to=由于，表因果关系。
- **第8题（D. immediate）**：形容词辨析。"The most immediate changes will be observed in skill level, strength and inches lost"（最直接的变化体现在技能水平、力量和围度上）。immediate=直接的、立即可见的。
- **第9题（C. reasons）**：固定搭配。"For these reasons"（由于这些原因）。reasons=原因，总结前文所述各条理由。
- **第10题（A. instead）**：副词辨析。"switched to a bimonthly weighing schedule instead"（改为每两周称一次体重取而代之）。instead=代替、取而代之，与stopped weighing every day呼应。
- **第11题（A. track）**：动词辨析。"less important for me to track my weight each week"（每周追踪体重变得不那么重要）。track=追踪、跟踪。
- **第12题（D. account for）**：动词短语。"observe and account for any significant weight changes"（观察并解释任何显著体重变化）。account for=解释、说明原因。
- **第13题（B. adjust）**：动词辨析。"whether I need to adjust my training program"（是否需要调整训练计划）。adjust=调整，根据体重变化调整计划。
- **第14题（A. results）**：名词辨析。"use my bimonthly weigh-in results"（利用双周称重结果）。results=结果，称重的结果数据。
- **第15题（C. hungry）**：形容词辨析。"constantly hungry and dropping weight"（总是感到饥饿且体重下降）。hungry=饥饿的，与后文"增加每日卡路里摄入"呼应。
- **第16题（D. sign）**：名词辨析。"this is a sign that I need to increase my daily caloric intake"（这是一个我需要增加每日热量摄入的信号）。sign=迹象、信号。
- **第17题（C. decision）**：名词辨析。"The decision to stop weighing myself every day"（停止每天称重的决定）。decision=决定，后文说这个决定带来了奇效。
- **第18题（A. disappointing）**：形容词辨析。"the burden of a disappointing morning weigh-in"（令人失望的早晨称重的负担）。disappointing=令人失望的，称重结果不理想让人失望。
- **第19题（D. because）**：连词。"greater success... because I'm training according to those goals"（更大的成功……因为我根据那些目标训练）。because=因为，表因果关系。
- **第20题（A. obsessing）**：动词辨析。"Rather than obsessing over the scale"（与其沉迷于体重秤上的数字）。obsessing=沉迷、痴迷，rather than表"与其……不如"。

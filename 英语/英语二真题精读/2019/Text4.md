

Arnold Schwarzenegger, Dia Mirza and Adrian Grenier have a message for you. It's easy to beat plastic 塑料. They're part of a bunch of 一群 celebrities 名人 starring in 主演 a new video for World Environment Day 世界环境日—encouraging you, the consumer, to swap out 换掉 your single-use plastic staples 一次性塑料日用品 like straws 吸管 and cutlery 餐具 to combat the plastics crisis 塑料危机.

The key messages that have been put together for World Environment Day do include a call for governments to enact legislation 制定法律 to curb 限制 single-use plastics. But the overarching 首要的 message is directed at individuals 个人.

My concern with leaving it up to the individual, however, is our limited sense of what needs to be achieved. On their own, taking our own bags to the grocery store or quitting plastic straws, for example, will accomplish little and require very little of us. They could even be detrimental 有害的, satisfying a need to have "done our bit 尽了自己的一份力" without ever progressing onto 进展到 bigger, bolder, more effective actions—a kind of "moral licensing 道德许可" that allays 减轻 our concerns and stops us doing more and asking more of those in charge 掌权者.

While the conversation around our environment and our responsibility toward it remains centered on shopping bags and straws, we're ignoring the balance of power 权力平衡 that implies that as "consumers" we must shop sustainably 可持续地购物, rather than as "citizens" hold our governments and industries to account 要求…负责 to push for real systemic change 系统性变革.

It's important to acknowledge 承认 that the environment isn't everyone's priority—or even most people's. We shouldn't expect it to be. In her latest book, Why Good People Do Bad Environmental Things, Wellesley College professor Elizabeth R. DeSombre argues that the best way to collectively 集体地 change the behavior of large numbers of people is for the change to be structural 结构性的.

This might mean implementing policy such as a plastic tax 塑料税 that adds a cost to environmentally problematic action, or banning single-use plastics altogether. India has just announced it will "eliminate 消除 all single-use plastic in the country by 2022." There are also incentive-based 基于激励的 ways of making better environmental choices easier, such as ensuring recycling is at least as easy as trash disposal 垃圾处理.

DeSombre isn't saying people should stop caring about the environment. It's just that individual actions are too slow, she says, for that to be the only, or even primary, approach to changing widespread behavior.

None of this is about writing off 忽视 the individual. It's just about putting things into perspective 正确看待事物. We don't have time to wait. We need progressive policies 进步政策 that shape collective action (and rein in 控制 polluting businesses), alongside engaged citizens 积极参与的公民 pushing for change.

1. 环保/社会类文章：关注**现象（名人鼓励减塑）→作者担忧（个人行动不够，道德许可）→提出观点（作为公民要求政府推动系统性变革）→理论支持（DeSombre：结构性变革）→具体政策（塑料税、禁令、激励）→结论（个人行动远远不够，需要政策+公民）**的结构
2. 细节题：定位到**现在分词伴随状语**（encouraging consumers to swap out single-use plastics）
3. 词义/概念题：定位到**同位语+that定语从句**（moral licensing that allays concerns and stops us doing more）
4. 观点题：定位到**rather than对比**（as consumers shop sustainably, rather than as citizens hold governments to account）
5. 主旨/结论题：把握**全文反复强调的主题**（individual actions too slow, need structural policies = far from sufficient）

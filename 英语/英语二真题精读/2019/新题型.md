# 2019 英二 阅读新题型（Part B）

## 题型说明

多项对应/信息匹配题。文章给出关于"父母购房时是否应考虑孩子意见"的主题文章，左栏列出5个人物，右栏给出7个观点选项（A-G），要求为每个人物匹配其对应的观点，多出2个干扰项。每题2分，共10分。

## 原文

### How Seriously Should Parents Take Kids' Opinions When Searching for a Home?

(1) In choosing a new home, Camille McClain's kids have a single demand: a backyard.

(2) McClain's little ones aren't the only kids who have an opinion when it comes to housing, and in many cases youngsters' views weigh heavily on parents' real estate decisions, according to a 2018 Harris Poll survey of more than 2,000 U.S. adults.

(3) While more families buck an older-generation proclivity to leave kids in the dark about real estate decisions, realty agents and psychologists have mixed views about the financial, personal and long-term effects kids' opinions may have.

(4) The idea of involving children in a big decision is a great idea because it can help them feel a sense of control and ownership in what can be an overwhelming process, said Ryan Hooper, a clinical psychologist in Chicago.

(5) "Children may face serious difficulties in coping with significant moves, especially if it removes them from their current school or support system," he said.

(6) Greg Jaroszewski, a real estate broker with Gagliardo Realty Associates, said he's not convinced that kids should be involved in selecting a home — but their opinions should be considered in regards to proximity to friends and social activities, if possible.

(7) Younger children should feel like they're choosing their home — without actually getting a choice in the matter, said Adam Bailey, a real estate attorney based in New York.

(8) Asking them questions about what they like about the backyard of a potential home will make them feel like they're being included in the decision-making process, Bailey said.

(9) Many of the aspects of homebuying aren't a consideration for children, said Tracey Hampson, a real estate agent based in Santa Clarita, Calif. And placing too much emphasis on their opinions can ruin a fantastic home purchase.

(10) "Speaking with your children before you make a real estate decision is wise, but I wouldn't base the purchasing decision solely on their opinions," Hampson said.

(11) The other issue is that many children — especially older ones — may base their real estate knowledge on HGTV shows, said Aaron Norris of The Norris Group in Riverside, Calif.

(12) "They love Chip and Joanna Gaines just as much as the rest of us," he said. "HGTV has seriously changed how people view real estate. It's not shelter, it's a lifestyle. With that mindset change come some serious money consequences."

(13) Kids tend to get stuck in the features and the immediate benefits to them personally, Norris said.

(14) Parents need to remind their children that their needs and desires may change over time, said Julie Gurner, a real estate analyst with FitSmallBusiness.com.

(15) "Their opinions can change tomorrow," Gurner said. "Harsh as it may be to say, that decision should likely not be made contingent on a child's opinions, but rather made for them with great consideration into what home can meet their needs best — and give them an opportunity to customize it a bit and make it their own."

(16) This advice is more relevant now than ever before, even as more parents want to embrace the ideas of their children, despite the current housing crunch.

## 备选选项

[A] remarks that significant moves may pose challenges to children.（指出重大搬迁可能给孩子带来挑战）

[B] says that it is wise to leave kids in the dark about real estate decisions.（认为让孩子对房产决策一无所知是明智的）

[C] advises that home purchases should not be based only on children's opinions.（建议购房不应仅基于孩子的意见）

[D] thinks that children should be given a sense of involvement in home-buying decisions.（认为应让孩子在购房决策中有参与感）

[E] notes that aspects like children's friends and social activities should be considered upon home-buying.（指出购房时应考虑孩子的朋友和社交活动等因素）

[F] believes that home-buying decisions should be based on children's needs rather than their opinions.（认为购房决策应基于孩子的需求而非意见）

[G] assumes that many children's views on real estate are influenced by the media.（认为许多孩子对房地产的看法受媒体影响）

## 题目（41-45）

将下列人物与上述选项A-G进行匹配：

41. Ryan Hooper
42. Adam Bailey
43. Tracey Hampson
44. Aaron Norris
45. Julie Gurner

---
### 参考答案
41.A 42.D 43.C 44.G 45.F

## 解析

**题型特点：** 多项对应（信息匹配）题。文章主题为"父母购房时是否应考虑孩子意见"，通过多位专家的不同观点展开讨论。解题核心是人名定位+观点关键词匹配。

**解题策略：**
1. 先读文章标题，把握主题（购房、孩子、意见）
2. 在原文中划出5个人名出现的位置，标注对应段落
3. 读7个选项，划出每个选项的核心区分词
4. 回到人名所在段落，寻找与选项核心词的同义替换

**逐题解析：**

- **41.A（Ryan Hooper → significant moves may pose challenges to children）**：Ryan Hooper出现在第4-5段。第5段原句"Children may face serious difficulties in coping with significant moves"（孩子在应对重大搬迁时可能面临严重困难），与A选项的significant moves may pose challenges to children完美对应，原词复现significant moves。

- **42.D（Adam Bailey → sense of involvement in home-buying decisions）**：Adam Bailey出现在第7-8段。第8段"make them feel like they're being included in the decision-making process"（让他们觉得自己参与了决策过程），included=被包含、参与，与D选项的a sense of involvement（参与感）同义替换。

- **43.C（Tracey Hampson → should not be based only on children's opinions）**：Tracey Hampson出现在第9-10段。第10段"I wouldn't base the purchasing decision solely on their opinions"（我不会仅凭他们的意见来做购买决定），solely=仅仅=only，base...on=基于，与C选项not be based only on children's opinions完美对应。

- **44.G（Aaron Norris → views on real estate are influenced by the media）**：Aaron Norris出现在第11-13段。第11段"many children may base their real estate knowledge on HGTV shows"（许多孩子的房地产知识基于HGTV节目），第12段"HGTV has seriously changed how people view real estate"（HGTV严重改变了人们对房地产的看法），HGTV=媒体，与G选项influenced by the media（受媒体影响）同义替换。

- **45.F（Julie Gurner → based on children's needs rather than their opinions）**：Julie Gurner出现在第14-15段。第15段"not be made contingent on a child's opinions, but rather made for them with great consideration into what home can meet their needs best"（不应取决于孩子的意见，而应基于什么房子最能满足他们的需求），needs rather than opinions=需求而非意见，与F选项完美对应。

**干扰项排除：**
- [B] leave kids in the dark：第3段提到older-generation proclivity to leave kids in the dark，但这是老一代的做法，不是任何题干人物的观点
- [E] friends and social activities：第6段Greg Jaroszewski提到proximity to friends and social activities，但Greg不是题干要求匹配的人物



Unlike so-called basic emotions 基本情绪 such as sadness, fear, and anger, guilt 内疚 emerges 出现 a little later, in conjunction with 与…一起 a child's growing grasp 理解 of social and moral norms 社会和道德规范. Children aren't born knowing how to say "I'm sorry"; rather, they learn over time that such statements appease 安抚 parents and friends—and their own consciences 良心. This is why researchers generally regard so-called moral guilt, in the right amount 适量, to be a good thing.

In the popular imagination 大众想象, of course, guilt still gets a bad rap 口碑不好. It is deeply uncomfortable—it's the emotional equivalent 等同 of wearing a jacket weighted with stones 装满石头的夹克. Yet this understanding is outdated 过时的. "There has been a kind of revival 复兴 or a rethinking about what guilt is and what role guilt can serve," says Amrisha Vaish, a psychology researcher at the University of Virginia, adding that this revival is part of a larger recognition that emotions aren't binary 二元的—feelings that may be advantageous 有利的 in one context may be harmful in another. Jealousy 嫉妒 and anger, for example, may have evolved to alert us to important inequalities 不平等. Too much happiness can be destructive 破坏性的.

And guilt, by prompting 促使 us to think more deeply about our goodness, can encourage humans to make up for errors 弥补错误 and fix relationships. Guilt, in other words, can help hold a cooperative species 合作物种 together. It is a kind of social glue 社会粘合剂.

Viewed in this light 从这个角度看, guilt is an opportunity. Work by Tina Malti, a psychology professor at the University of Toronto, suggests that guilt may compensate for 补偿 an emotional deficiency 情感缺陷. In a number of studies, Malti and others have shown that guilt and sympathy 同情 may represent different pathways 途径 to cooperation and sharing. Some kids who are low in sympathy may make up for that shortfall 不足 by experiencing more guilt, which can rein in 控制 their nastier 更恶劣的 impulses 冲动. And vice versa 反之亦然: High sympathy can substitute for 替代 low guilt.

In a 2014 study, for example, Malti looked at 244 children. Using caregiver assessments 看护人评估 and the children's self-observations 自我观察, she rated 评估 each child's overall sympathy level and his or her tendency to feel negative emotions after moral transgressions 道德越轨. Then the kids were handed chocolate coins 巧克力币, and given a chance to share them with an anonymous 匿名的 child. For the low-sympathy kids, how much they shared appeared to turn on 取决于 how inclined they were to feel guilty. The guilt-prone 容易内疚的 ones shared more, even though they hadn't magically become more sympathetic to the other child's deprivation 匮乏.

"That's good news," Malti says. "We can be prosocial 亲社会的 because we caused harm and we feel regret 后悔."

## 21. Researchers think that guilt can be a good thing because it may help ______.

[A] regulate a child's basic emotions [B] improve a child's intellectual ability [C] foster a child's moral development [D] intensify a child's positive feelings

✅答案：**C** 定位：第一段。`guilt emerges a little later, in conjunction with a child's growing grasp of social and moral norms`（内疚出现得稍晚，与孩子对社会和道德规范日益增长的理解一起出现）+ `researchers generally regard so-called moral guilt, in the right amount, to be a good thing`（研究人员通常认为适量的道德内疚是好事）。说明内疚促进道德发展。

- A：错误。regulate a child's basic emotions（调节孩子的基本情绪）无中生有。内疚不是基本情绪，也不是调节基本情绪。
- B：错误。improve a child's intellectual ability（提高孩子的智力）无中生有。
- C：正确。促进孩子的道德发展。moral guilt + grasp of social and moral norms = foster a child's moral development。
- D：错误。intensify a child's positive feelings（强化孩子的积极情绪）偷换概念。内疚是负面情绪，但能促进亲社会行为，不是强化积极情绪。

## 22. According to Paragraph 2, many people still consider guilt to be ______.

[A] deceptive [B] burdensome [C] addictive [D] deceptive

✅答案：**B** 定位：第二段。`guilt still gets a bad rap. It is deeply uncomfortable—it's the emotional equivalent of wearing a jacket weighted with stones`（内疚仍然口碑不好。它非常不舒服——就情感上而言，相当于穿着一件装满石头的夹克）。装满石头的夹克=负担（burdensome）。

- A：错误。deceptive（欺骗性的）无中生有。注意选项A和D相同，都是deceptive，是题目本身的重复。
- B：正确。负担沉重的。weighted with stones = burdensome。
- C：错误。addictive（上瘾的）无中生有。
- D：错误。deceptive（欺骗性的）无中生有，与A重复。

## 23. Vaish holds that the rethinking about guilt comes from an awareness that ______.

[A] emotions are context-independent [B] emotions are socially constructive [C] emotional stability can benefit health [D] an emotion can play opposing roles

✅答案：**D** 定位：第二段。`this revival is part of a larger recognition that emotions aren't binary—feelings that may be advantageous in one context may be harmful in another`（这种复兴是更广泛认识的一部分，即情绪不是二元的——在一种情境中可能有利的情绪在另一种情境中可能有害）。说明情绪可以起相反的作用。

- A：错误。emotions are context-independent（情绪与情境无关）与原文相反。原文说advantageous in one context, harmful in another，说明情绪与情境相关。
- B：错误。emotions are socially constructive（情绪具有社会建设性）以偏概全。内疚有社会建设性，但不是所有情绪，也不是rethinking的来源。
- C：错误。emotional stability can benefit health（情绪稳定有益健康）无中生有。
- D：正确。一种情绪可以起相反的作用。advantageous in one context, harmful in another = an emotion can play opposing roles。

## 24. Malti and others have shown that cooperation and sharing ______.

[A] may help correct emotional deficiencies [B] can result from either sympathy or guilt [C] can bring about emotional satisfaction [D] may be the outcome of impulsive acts

✅答案：**B** 定位：第四段。`guilt and sympathy may represent different pathways to cooperation and sharing`（内疚和同情可能代表合作和分享的不同途径）+ `Some kids who are low in sympathy may make up for that shortfall by experiencing more guilt`（同情心低的孩子可能通过体验更多内疚来弥补不足）。说明合作和分享可以来自同情或内疚。

- A：错误。may help correct emotional deficiencies（可能帮助纠正情感缺陷）偷换概念。原文说guilt may compensate for an emotional deficiency（内疚可能补偿情感缺陷），不是合作和分享纠正缺陷。
- B：正确。可以来自同情或内疚。different pathways to cooperation and sharing = can result from either sympathy or guilt。
- C：错误。can bring about emotional satisfaction（能带来情感满足）无中生有。
- D：错误。may be the outcome of impulsive acts（可能是冲动行为的结果）与原文相反。内疚rein in nastier impulses（控制更恶劣的冲动），不是冲动的结果。

## 25. The word "transgressions" (Line 4, Para.5) is closest in meaning to ______.



Forests give us shade 阴凉, quiet and one of the harder challenges 更艰巨的挑战 in the fight against climate change. Even as we humans count on 依靠 forests to soak up 吸收 a good share of the carbon dioxide 二氧化碳 we produce, we are threatening their ability to do so. The climate change we are hastening 加速 could one day leave us with forests that emit 排放 more carbon than they absorb.

Thankfully, there is a way out of this trap 陷阱—but it involves striking a subtle balance 达成微妙的平衡. Helping forests flourish 繁荣 as valuable "carbon sinks 碳汇" long into the future may require reducing their capacity to absorb carbon now. California is leading the way 带头, as it does on so many climate efforts, in figuring out the details.

The state's proposed Forest Carbon Plan 森林碳计划 aims to double efforts to thin out 间伐 young trees and clear brush 清除灌木 in parts of the forest. This temporarily lowers carbon-carrying capacity 碳承载能力. But the remaining trees draw a greater share of the available moisture 水分, so they grow and thrive, restoring the forest's capacity to pull carbon from the air. Healthy trees are also better able to fend off 抵御 insects. The landscape is rendered less easily burnable 不易燃烧. Even in the event of a fire, fewer trees are consumed 烧毁.

The need for such planning is increasingly urgent 紧迫. Already, since 2010, drought 干旱 and insects have killed over 100 million trees in California, most of them in 2016 alone, and wildfires have burned hundreds of thousands of acres 英亩.

California plans to treat 处理 35,000 acres of forest a year by 2020, and 60,000 by 2030—financed from the proceeds 收益 of the state's emissions-permit auctions 排放许可拍卖. That's only a small share of the total acreage that could benefit, about half a million acres in all, so it will be vital to prioritize 优先处理 areas at greatest risk of fire or drought.

The strategy also aims to ensure that carbon in woody material 木质材料 removed from the forests is locked away 锁定 in the form of solid lumber 实木 or burned as biofuel 生物燃料 in vehicles that would otherwise run on fossil fuels. New research on transportation biofuels is already under way.

State governments are well accustomed to 习惯于 managing forests, but traditionally they've focused on wildlife, watersheds 流域 and opportunities for recreation 娱乐. Only recently have they come to see the vital part forests will have to play in storing carbon. California's plan, which is expected to be finalized 定稿 by the governor 州长 next year, should serve as a model 榜样.

## 长难句1

> Even as we humans count on forests to soak up a good share of the carbon dioxide we produce, we are threatening their ability to do so.

### 句子拆分

主干：**we are threatening their ability to do so**
- `Even as we humans count on forests to soak up a good share of the carbon dioxide we produce`：even as让步状语从句
  - `we produce`：省略that的定语从句，修饰carbon dioxide
- `to do so`：不定式作后置定语，修饰ability

### 生词

even as：尽管；count on：依靠；soak up：吸收；a good share of：相当大的一部分；carbon dioxide：二氧化碳；threaten：威胁；ability：能力

### 翻译

尽管我们人类依靠森林吸收我们产生的相当大一部分二氧化碳，但我们正在威胁它们这样做的能力。

### 考点

**even as让步状语从句+省略that的定语从句+不定式后置定语**；26题D选项的背景——人类威胁森林吸收碳的能力，森林可能成为潜在威胁。

## 26. By saying "one of the harder challenges," the author implies that ______.

[A] global climate change may get out of control [B] people may misunderstand global warming [C] extreme weather conditions may arise [D] forests may become a potential threat

✅答案：**D** 推理题。定位：第一段。`The climate change we are hastening could one day leave us with forests that emit more carbon than they absorb`（我们正在加速的气候变化有一天可能会让我们拥有排放的碳比吸收的碳更多的森林）。说明森林可能从碳汇变成碳源，成为潜在威胁。

- A：错误。global climate change may get out of control（全球气候变化可能失控）过度推断。原文说气候变化加速，不是说失控。
- B：错误。people may misunderstand global warming（人们可能误解全球变暖）无中生有。
- C：错误。extreme weather conditions may arise（可能出现极端天气）无中生有。
- D：正确。森林可能成为潜在威胁。emit more carbon than they absorb = forests may become a potential threat。

## 27. To maintain forests as valuable "carbon sinks," we may need to ______.

- A：错误。preserve the diversity of species in them（保护其中的物种多样性）无中生有。
- B：错误。accelerate the growth of young trees（加速幼树生长）与原文相反。原文说thin out young trees（间伐幼树），不是加速生长。
- C：错误。strike a balance among different plants（在不同植物间达成平衡）偷换概念。原文说striking a subtle balance（达成微妙平衡），是现在和未来的平衡，不是不同植物间的平衡。

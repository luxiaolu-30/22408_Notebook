American farmers have been complaining of labor shortages 劳动力短缺 for several years. The complaints are unlikely to stop without an overhaul 全面改革 of immigration rules 移民规则 for farm workers.

Congress 国会 has obstructed 阻碍 efforts to create a more straightforward 更直接的 visa 签证 for agricultural workers that would let foreign workers stay longer in the U.S. and change jobs within the industry. If this doesn't change, American businesses, communities, and consumers will be the losers 输家.

Perhaps half of U.S. farm laborers are undocumented immigrants 无证移民. As fewer such workers enter the country, the characteristics of the agricultural workforce 农业劳动力 are changing. Today's farm laborers, while still predominantly 主要地 born in Mexico, are more likely to be settled rather than migrating 迁移 and more likely to be married than single. They're also aging 老龄化. At the start of this century, about one-third of crop workers were over the age of 35. Now more than half are. And picking crops is hard on older bodies. One oft-debated 经常争论的 cure for this labor shortage remains as implausible 难以置信的 as it's been all along: Native U.S. workers won't be returning to the farm.

Mechanization 机械化 isn't the answer, either—not yet, at least. Production of corn, cotton, rice, soybeans, and wheat has been largely mechanized, but many high-value, labor-intensive 劳动密集型 crops, such as strawberries, need labor. Even dairy farms 奶牛场, where robots do a small share of milking 挤奶, have a long way to go before they're automated 自动化.

As a result, farms have grown increasingly reliant on 依赖 temporary guest workers 临时客工 using the H-2A visa to fill the gaps in the workforce. Starting around 2012, requests for the visas rose sharply 急剧上升; from 2011 to 2016 the number of visas issued 签发 more than doubled.

The H-2A visa has no numerical cap 数量上限, unlike the H-2B visa for nonagricultural work, which is limited to 66,000 a year. Even so, employers complain they aren't given all the workers they need. The process is cumbersome 繁琐的, expensive, and unreliable. One survey found that bureaucratic delays 官僚延误 led the average H-2A worker to arrive on the job 22 days late. The shortage is compounded 加剧 by federal immigration raids 突击搜查, which remove some workers and drive others underground 转入地下.

In a 2012 survey, 71 percent of tree-fruit growers and almost 80 percent of raisin and berry growers said they were short of labor. Some western farmers have responded by moving operations 转移业务 to Mexico. From 1998 to 2000, 14.5 percent of the fruit Americans consumed was imported 进口. Little more than a decade later, the share of imports was 25.8 percent.

In effect 实际上, the U.S. can import food or it can import the workers who pick it.

## 长难句1

> The complaints are unlikely to stop without an overhaul of immigration rules for farm workers.

### 句子拆分

主干：**The complaints are unlikely to stop**
- `without an overhaul of immigration rules for farm workers`：介词短语作条件状语

### 生词

complaint：抱怨；be unlikely to：不太可能；stop：停止；without：没有；overhaul：全面改革、彻底检查；immigration rules：移民规则；farm workers：农场工人

### 翻译

如果不对农场工人的移民规则进行全面改革，这些抱怨不太可能停止。

### 考点

**without介词短语作条件状语**；31题C选项的依据——需要解决的问题是美国农场工人移民规则的缺陷（flaws in immigration rules）。

## 长难句2

> Congress has obstructed efforts to create a more straightforward visa for agricultural workers that would let foreign workers stay longer in the U.S. and change jobs within the industry.

### 句子拆分

主干：**Congress has obstructed efforts**
- `to create a more straightforward visa for agricultural workers`：不定式作后置定语，修饰efforts
- `that would let foreign workers stay longer in the U.S. and change jobs within the industry`：that定语从句，修饰visa
  - `stay longer in the U.S.`和`change jobs within the industry`是and连接的两个并列宾语补足语

### 生词

Congress：国会；obstruct：阻碍、阻挠；effort：努力；straightforward：直接的、简单的；visa：签证；agricultural workers：农业工人；let sb do：让某人做；stay longer：停留更久；change jobs：换工作；within the industry：在行业内

### 翻译

国会阻碍了为农业工人创建更直接签证的努力，这种签证将允许外国工人在美国停留更久，并在行业内换工作。

### 考点

**不定式后置定语+that定语从句+两个并列宾补**；31题C选项的进一步说明——国会阻碍签证改革，说明移民规则有缺陷。

## 长难句3

> Today's farm laborers, while still predominantly born in Mexico, are more likely to be settled rather than migrating and more likely to be married than single.

### 句子拆分

主干：**Today's farm laborers are more likely to be settled rather than migrating and more likely to be married than single**
- `while still predominantly born in Mexico`：while让步状语从句省略（=while they are still predominantly born in Mexico）
- `more likely to be settled rather than migrating`和`more likely to be married than single`是and连接的两个并列表语

### 生词

farm laborer：农场工人；while：尽管；predominantly：主要地；be born in：出生于；be likely to：可能；settled：定居的；rather than：而不是；migrating：迁移的；married：已婚的；single：单身的

### 翻译

今天的农场工人，尽管仍然主要出生在墨西哥，但更可能是定居的而不是迁移的，更可能是已婚的而不是单身的。

### 考点

**while省略让步状语从句+and连接两个并列比较级表语**；说明农业劳动力特征的变化——从迁移到定居，从单身到已婚。

## 长难句4

> One oft-debated cure for this labor shortage remains as implausible as it's been all along: Native U.S. workers won't be returning to the farm.

### 句子拆分

主干：**One oft-debated cure for this labor shortage remains as implausible as it's been all along**
- `as implausible as it's been all along`：as…as同级比较
- 冒号后：`Native U.S. workers won't be returning to the farm`
  - 同位语，解释cure

### 生词

oft-debated：经常争论的；cure：解决方案、疗法；labor shortage：劳动力短缺；remain：仍然是；implausible：难以置信的、不可信的；all along：一直、始终；native U.S. workers：美国本土工人；return to：回到；farm：农场

### 翻译

一个经常争论的解决劳动力短缺的方案仍然和以往一样不可信：美国本土工人不会回到农场。

### 考点

**as…as同级比较+冒号同位语**；33题B选项的依据——经常争论的解决方案是让美国本土工人回到农场（get native U.S. workers back to farming）。

### 句子拆分

# 2019 英二 Text3 31–35｜题目+选项+逐选项解析

> 原文主题：美国农业劳动力短缺多年，根源是移民规则缺陷；国会阻碍签证改革；劳动力在老龄化，本土工人不会回农场，机械化也不是答案；农场越来越依赖H-2A签证，但审批缓慢；一些农场转移到墨西哥，水果进口增加；结论：美国要么进口食物，要么进口工人

## 31. What problem should be addressed according to the first two paragraphs?

[A] Discrimination against foreign workers in the U.S. [B] Biased laws in favor of some American businesses. [C] Flaws in U.S. immigration rules for farm workers. [D] Decline of job opportunities in U.S. agriculture.

✅答案：**C** 定位：前两段。`The complaints are unlikely to stop without an overhaul of immigration rules for farm workers`（如果不对农场工人的移民规则进行全面改革，抱怨不太可能停止）+ `Congress has obstructed efforts to create a more straightforward visa for agricultural workers`（国会阻碍了为农业工人创建更直接签证的努力）。说明需要解决的问题是美国农场工人移民规则的缺陷。

- A：错误。Discrimination against foreign workers（对外国工人的歧视）无中生有。原文说移民规则有问题，不是歧视。
- B：错误。Biased laws in favor of some American businesses（偏袒某些美国企业的法律）无中生有。
- C：正确。美国农场工人移民规则的缺陷。overhaul of immigration rules + Congress obstructed efforts = flaws in immigration rules for farm workers。
- D：错误。Decline of job opportunities in U.S. agriculture（美国农业就业机会减少）与原文相反。原文说劳动力短缺，不是就业机会减少。

## 35. Which of the following could be the best title for this text?

[A] U.S. Agriculture in Decline? [B] Import Food or Labor? [C] America Saved by Mexico? [D] Manpower vs. Automation?

✅答案：**B** 主旨题。全文主线：美国农业劳动力短缺→移民规则缺陷→劳动力老龄化→本土工人不回农场→机械化不是答案→依赖H-2A签证但审批慢→一些农场转移到墨西哥，水果进口增加→结论：`the U.S. can import food or it can import the workers who pick it`（美国可以进口食物，或者进口采摘食物的工人）。全文围绕劳动力短缺，最终提出要么进口食物要么进口工人。

- A：错误。U.S. Agriculture in Decline?（美国农业在衰退？）以偏概全。文章讲的是劳动力短缺，不是农业衰退。
- B：正确。进口食物还是劳动力？全文围绕劳动力短缺，结论是import food or import workers。
- C：错误。America Saved by Mexico?（美国被墨西哥拯救？）以偏概全。墨西哥是劳动力来源和农场转移目的地，但不是主旨。
- D：错误。Manpower vs. Automation?（人力vs自动化？）以偏概全。机械化只是文章讨论的一个解决方案，不是主旨。

### 本篇命题规律小结（英二阅读）

1. 社会/经济类文章：关注**问题（劳动力短缺）→根源（移民规则缺陷）→特征变化（老龄化、定居）→解决方案排除（本土工人、机械化）→现有方案（H-2A签证）及其问题→后果（农场转移、进口增加）→结论（进口食物或工人）**的结构
2. 问题题：定位到**without条件句**（without an overhaul of immigration rules = flaws in immigration rules）
3. 细节题：定位到**数据和老龄化**（more than half over 35 = aging）
4. 方案题：定位到**oft-debated cure**（Native U.S. workers won't be returning = get native workers back to farming）
5. 主旨题：答案在**全文结论句**（import food or import the workers）

# 2023 英二 翻译

## 原文

In the late 18th century, William Wordsworth became famous for his poems about nature. And he was one of the founders of a movement called Romanticism, which celebrated the wonders of the natural world.

Poetry is powerful. Its energy and rhythm can capture a reader, transport them to another world and make them see things differently. Through carefully selected words and phrases, poems can be dramatic, funny, moving and inspiring. No one knows for sure when poetry began but it has been around for thousands of years, even before people could write. It was a way to tell stories and pass down history. It is closely related to song and even when written it is usually created to be performed out loud. Poems really come to life when they are recited. This can also help with understanding them too, because the rhythm and sounds of the words become clearer.

## 参考译文

在18世纪晚期，威廉·华兹华斯因其关于自然的诗歌而闻名。他是浪漫主义运动的创始人之一，该运动颂扬自然世界的奇迹。

诗歌是有力量的。它的能量和节奏能够吸引读者，将他们带到另一个世界，让他们以不同的方式看待事物。通过精心挑选的词语和短语，诗歌可以是戏剧性的、有趣的、感人的和鼓舞人心的。没有人确切知道诗歌是何时开始的，但它已经存在了数千年，甚至在人们会写字之前就已有之。它是一种讲述故事和传承历史的方式。它与歌曲密切相关，即使被写下来，也通常是为了大声表演而创作的。诗歌在被朗诵时才真正生动起来。这也有助于理解诗歌，因为词语的节奏和发音变得更加清晰。

## 重点词汇与句型

- **Romanticism**：浪漫主义
- **celebrate**：颂扬，赞美（此处非"庆祝"）
- **wonders**：奇迹，奇观
- **rhythm**：节奏，韵律
- **capture**：吸引，俘获
- **transport sb. to...**：将某人带到/ transporting to another world
- **moving**：感人的
- **inspiring**：鼓舞人心的
- **be around**：存在，流传
- **pass down**：传承，传下来
- **come to life**：变得生动，活灵活现
- **recite**：朗诵，背诵

## 长难句分析

**1. And he was one of the founders of a movement called Romanticism, which celebrated the wonders of the natural world.**

- **结构**：主句 + called...过去分词作后置定语 + which引导的非限制性定语从句
- **拆分**：he was one of the founders of a movement（主句）/ called Romanticism（过去分词作后置定语，修饰movement）/ which celebrated the wonders of the natural world（非限制性定语从句，修饰Romanticism）
- **翻译要点**：called Romanticism可前置译为"被称为浪漫主义的运动"；which定语从句单独成句，"该运动颂扬……"
- **译文**：他是浪漫主义运动的创始人之一，该运动颂扬自然世界的奇迹。

**2. No one knows for sure when poetry began but it has been around for thousands of years, even before people could write.**

- **结构**：but连接两个并列分句，第一个分句含when引导的宾语从句
- **拆分**：No one knows for sure（主句1）/ when poetry began（宾语从句）/ but it has been around for thousands of years（主句2）/ even before people could write（时间状语）
- **翻译要点**：know for sure=确切知道；has been around=存在/流传；even before=甚至在……之前
- **译文**：没有人确切知道诗歌是何时开始的，但它已经存在了数千年，甚至在人们会写字之前就已有之。

**3. It is closely related to song and even when written it is usually created to be performed out loud.**

- **结构**：and连接两个并列分句，第二个分句含when引导的时间状语从句（省略结构）和不定式目的状语
- **拆分**：It is closely related to song（第一个分句）/ and even when written（时间状语从句，when后省略it is）/ it is usually created to be performed out loud（第二个分句，不定式作目的状语）
- **翻译要点**：be related to=与……相关；when written=when it is written（被写下来时）；to be performed out loud=为了大声表演
- **译文**：它与歌曲密切相关，即使被写下来，也通常是为了大声表演而创作的。

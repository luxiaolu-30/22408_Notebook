# 2023 英二 小作文

## 题目

**Directions:**

Suppose you are preparing for an exam. Write an email to David, an international student, to

1) tell him the choice you made, and
2) explain why you chose it.

You should write neatly on the ANSWER SHEET. (10 points)

You should write about 100 words on the ANSWER SHEET. Do not sign your own name at the end of the email. Use "Li Ming" instead.

**场景说明**：假设你是李明，你正在为一个考试做准备，要给国际学生David写一封电子邮件。David需要在艺术展览和机器人展之间做出选择，你给出你的建议并说明理由。属于"建议信"。

## 高分范文

Dear David,

I'm very glad to receive your letter asking for my advice on whether to go to the art exhibition or the robot show. In my opinion, compared with the robot show, the art exhibition would be a better choice. Here are the reasons.

Firstly, the art exhibition is an important tool for spreading new artistic ideas and promoting cultural development, and therefore can help you develop a deep understanding of culture and experience the emerging trend of art. Secondly, so far as I know, you have always been interested in traditional Chinese culture, and this exhibition features many classic Chinese paintings that I believe will certainly appeal to you.

I hope my suggestion is helpful. Wish you a wonderful time there. Do let me know if you need any further information.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

## 范文翻译

亲爱的David：

很高兴收到你的来信，征求我对去艺术展览还是机器人展的建议。在我看来，与机器人展相比，艺术展览会是更好的选择。原因如下。

首先，艺术展览是传播新艺术理念和促进文化发展的重要工具，因此可以帮助你深入理解文化，体验新兴的艺术潮流。其次，据我所知，你一直对中国传统文化感兴趣，而这次展览以许多经典中国画为特色，我相信一定会吸引你。

希望我的建议对你有帮助。祝你在那里度过愉快的时光。如果需要更多信息，请告诉我。

此致敬礼
李明

## 重点句型与表达

1. **I'm very glad to receive your letter asking for my advice on...** —— 建议信开头万能句型，交代写信背景
2. **In my opinion, compared with..., ...would be a better choice.** —— "在我看来，与……相比，……是更好的选择"，给出建议
3. **Here are the reasons.** —— "原因如下"，过渡句引出理由
4. **Firstly, ... is an important tool for..., and therefore can help you...** —— "首先，……是……的重要工具，因此可以帮助你……"
5. **Secondly, so far as I know, you have always been interested in...** —— "其次，据我所知，你一直对……感兴趣"
6. **I hope my suggestion is helpful.** —— "希望我的建议有帮助"，建议信结尾
7. **Wish you a wonderful time there.** —— "祝你在那里度过愉快时光"，祝福句
8. **Do let me know if you need any further information.** —— "如果需要更多信息请告诉我"，书信万能结尾

# 2023 英二 阅读新题型（Part B）

## 题型说明

**多项对应题（信息匹配）**：将左栏的5个人名与右栏的7个陈述进行匹配，选出对应的陈述，有2个多余选项。文章主题为"净零排放新规将推高新房和扩建成本"。

## 原文

**Net-Zero Rules Set to Send Cost of New Homes and Extensions Soaring**

New building regulations aimed at improving energy efficiency are set to increase the price of new homes, as well as those of extensions and loft conversions on existing ones.

The rules, which came into effect on Wednesday in England, are part of government plans to reduce the UK's carbon emissions to net zero 净零排放 by 2050. They set new standards for ventilation 通风, energy efficiency and heating, and state that new residential buildings must have charging points for electric vehicles.

The moves are the most significant change to building regulations in years, and industry experts say they will inevitably lead to higher prices at a time when a shortage of materials and high labour costs is already driving up bills.

**Brian Berry**, chief executive of the Federation of Master Builders, says the measures will require new materials, testing methods, products and systems to be installed. "All this comes at an increased cost during a time when prices are already sky high. Inevitably, consumers will have to pay more," he says.

**Gareth Belsham**, of surveyors Naismiths, says people who are upgrading, or extending their home, will be directly affected. "The biggest changes relate to heating and insulation 隔热," he says. "There are new rules concerning the amount of glazing 玻璃 used in extensions, and any new windows or doors must be highly insulated."

The changes could mean an extra £3,000 added to the bill of an average home extension, according to **Jonathan Rolande** of the National Association of Property Buyers. Homeowners extending may see the amount of space they have decrease, as walls will have to be thicker to comply with requirements for better insulation.

**Andrew Mellor**, of PRP architects, says external walls will need to be about 7cm thicker than previously. Windows and doors will have to adhere to higher standards, while there are new limits on the amount of glazing to reduce unwanted heat from the sun.

**Thomas Goodman**, of My Job Quote, says this will bring in new restrictions for extensions. "Glazing on windows, doors and rooflights must cover no more than 25% of the floor area to prevent heat loss," he says.

**Marcus Jefford**, of Build Aviator, which prices projects, says builders which have costed projects, but have not filed the paperwork, may need to go back and submit fresh estimates 估价. As the changes are aimed to make homes more energy efficient, they will eventually drive down heating bills.

**John Kelly**, a construction lawyer at Freeths law firm, believes prices will eventually come down. But not in the immediate future. "As the marketplace adapts to the new requirements, and the technologies that support them, the scaling up of these technologies will eventually bring costs down, but in the short term, we will all have to pay the price of the necessary transition," he says.

However, the long-term effects of the changes will be more comfortable and energy-efficient homes, adds **Andrew Mellor**. "Homeowners will probably recoup 回收 that cost over time in energy bill savings. It will obviously be very volatile 波动的 at the moment, but they will have that benefit over time."

## 题目

将左栏人名与右栏陈述匹配（A-G），其中两个为多余选项：

**左栏（人名）：**
41. Brian Berry
42. Gareth Belsham
43. Marcus Jefford
44. John Kelly
45. Andrew Mellor

**右栏（陈述）：**
[A] The rise of home prices is a temporary matter. 房价上涨是暂时的
[B] Builders possibly need to submit new estimates of their projects. 建筑商可能需要提交新估价
[C] The new rules will lead to a reduction of space in homes. 新规将减少住宅空间
[D] The new rules will take home prices to an even higher level. 新规将推高房价
[E] The new rules will eventually lower heating bills for homeowners. 新规最终将降低取暖费
[F] The new rules will affect people whose home extensions include new windows or doors. 新规将影响扩建含新门窗的人
[G] The rule changes will benefit homeowners eventually. 新规变化最终将使房主受益

---
### 参考答案
41.D 42.F 43.B 44.A 45.G

## 解析

**题型特点**：多项对应题（信息匹配），考查定位与同义替换能力。需将人名定位到原文对应段落，再找出其观点与右栏陈述的对应关系。

**解题策略**：
1. 先读左栏人名，在原文中定位各人所在段落
2. 仔细阅读该人所说的话
3. 与右栏选项进行同义替换匹配

**逐题解析**：
- **41题 D**：Brian Berry说"prices are already sky high"和"consumers will have to pay more"=消费者将付更多，对应D"新规将推高房价"。
- **42题 F**：Gareth Belsham说"new rules concerning the amount of glazing used in extensions, and any new windows or doors must be highly insulated"=关于扩建中玻璃用量及新门窗需高度隔热的新规，对应F"新规将影响扩建含新门窗的人"。
- **43题 B**：Marcus Jefford说"builders which have costed projects, but have not filed the paperwork, may need to go back and submit fresh estimates"=已估价但未提交文件的建筑商可能需要重新提交新估价，对应B"建筑商可能需要提交新估价"。
- **44题 A**：John Kelly说"prices will eventually come down"和"scaling up of these technologies will eventually bring costs down, but in the short term, we will all have to pay the price"=价格最终会下降，短期需付代价，对应A"房价上涨是暂时的"。
- **45题 G**：Andrew Mellor说"Homeowners will probably recoup that cost over time in energy bill savings"=房主最终将通过节能账单回收成本，对应G"新规变化最终将使房主受益"。

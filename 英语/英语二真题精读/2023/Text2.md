It's easy to dismiss as absurd 认为…荒谬 the Trump administration's ideas for plugging 填补 the chronic funding gap 长期资金缺口 of our national parks. Can anyone really think it's a good idea to allow Amazon deliveries to your tent in Yosemite 约塞米蒂 or food trucks 餐车 to line up under the redwood trees 红杉树 at Sequoia National Park 红杉国家公园?

But the administration is right about one thing: U.S. national parks are in crisis 处于危机中. Collectively, they have a maintenance backlog 维护积压 of more than $12 billion. Roads, trails 小径, restrooms 洗手间, visitor centers 游客中心 and other infrastructure 基础设施 are crumbling  crumbling 崩溃.

But privatizing 私有化 and commercializing 商业化 the campgrounds 露营地 would not be the panacea 万能药 that the Interior Department's Outdoor Advisory Committee would have us believe. Campgrounds are a tiny portion 一小部分 of the overall infrastructure backlog, and concessionaires 特许经营者 in the parks hand over, on average, only about 5% of their revenues to the National Park Service.

Moreover, increased privatization would certainly undercut 削弱 one of the major reasons why 300 million visitors come to the parks each year: to enjoy nature and get a respite 喘息 from the commercial drumbeat 商业鼓点 that overwhelms 淹没 daily life.

The real problem is that the parks have been chronically starved of 长期缺乏 funding. We conducted a comprehensive survey 综合调查 examining how U.S. residents view their national parks, and we found that Americans place a very high value on 高度重视 them—whether or not they actually visit them. The peer-reviewed 同行评审的 economic survey of 700 U.S. taxpayers, conducted by mail and internet, also found that people would be willing to pay a significant amount of money to make sure the parks and their programs are kept intact 保持完整. Some 81% of respondents said they would be willing to pay additional taxes for the next 10 years to avoid any cuts to the national parks.

The national parks provide great value to U.S. residents both as places to escape 逃避 and as symbols of nature 自然的象征. On top of this, they produce value from their extensive educational programs 广泛的教育项目, their positive impact on the climate through carbon sequestration 碳封存, their contribution to our cultural and artistic life, and of course through tourism. The parks also help keep America's past alive, working with thousands of local jurisdictions 地方司法管辖区 around the country to protect historical sites 历史遗迹—including Ellis Island 埃利斯岛 and Gettysburg 葛底斯堡—and to bring the stories of these places to life.

The parks do all this on a shoestring 小成本. Congress allocates 拨款 only $3 billion a year to the national park system—an amount that has been flat 持平 since 2001 (in inflation-adjusted dollars 经通胀调整的美元) with the exception of a onetime boost 一次性增长 in 2009 as part of the Obama stimulus package 奥巴马刺激计划. Meanwhile, the number of annual visitors has increased by more than 50% since 1980, and now stands at 330 million visitors per year.

# 2023英二 Text2 长难句精读（原文+拆分+语法+翻译+考点）

## 长难句1

> It's easy to dismiss as absurd the Trump administration's ideas for plugging the chronic funding gap of our national parks.

### 句子拆分

主干：**It's easy to dismiss the Trump administration's ideas as absurd**
- it是形式主语，真正主语是to dismiss…
- `for plugging the chronic funding gap of our national parks`：介词短语修饰ideas

### 生词

dismiss … as absurd：认为…荒谬而不予考虑；Trump administration：特朗普政府；plug：填补；chronic funding gap：长期资金缺口；national parks：国家公园

### 翻译

人们很容易认为特朗普政府填补国家公园长期资金缺口的想法是荒谬的。

### 考点

**it形式主语+dismiss…as…+介词短语后置定语**；开篇引入——特朗普政府的想法荒谬，但国家公园确实有资金问题。

## 长难句2

> But the administration is right about one thing: U.S. national parks are in crisis. Collectively, they have a maintenance backlog of more than $12 billion. Roads, trails, restrooms, visitor centers and other infrastructure are crumbling.

### 句子拆分

三句
句1主干：**the administration is right about one thing**
- 冒号后：`U.S. national parks are in crisis`：解释one thing
句2主干：**they have a maintenance backlog of more than $12 billion**
句3主干：**Roads, trails, restrooms, visitor centers and other infrastructure are crumbling**

### 生词

administration：政府；be right about：在…上是对的；in crisis：处于危机中；collectively：总共、 collectively；maintenance backlog：维护积压；more than：超过；roads：道路；trails：小径；restrooms：洗手间；visitor centers：游客中心；infrastructure：基础设施；crumbling：崩溃、摇摇欲坠

### 翻译

但政府在一件事上是对的：美国国家公园处于危机中。它们总共有超过120亿美元的维护积压。道路、小径、洗手间、游客中心和其他基础设施正在崩溃。

### 考点

**冒号解释+数据+多个名词并列**；26题D选项的依据——国家公园面临的问题是基础设施维护不善（Poorly maintained infrastructure = maintenance backlog of $12 billion + infrastructure crumbling）。

## 长难句4

> Moreover, increased privatization would certainly undercut one of the major reasons why 300 million visitors come to the parks each year: to enjoy nature and get a respite from the commercial drumbeat that overwhelms daily life.

### 句子拆分

主干：**increased privatization would certainly undercut one of the major reasons**
- `why 300 million visitors come to the parks each year`：why定语从句，修饰reasons
- 冒号后：`to enjoy nature and get a respite from the commercial drumbeat that overwhelms daily life`：不定式解释reasons
  - `that overwhelms daily life`：that定语从句，修饰drumbeat

### 生词

moreover：此外；increased privatization：增加的私有化；certainly：肯定；undercut：削弱；major reasons：主要原因；300 million visitors：3亿游客；each year：每年；enjoy nature：享受自然；get a respite from：从…中得到喘息；commercial drumbeat：商业鼓点；overwhelm：淹没；daily life：日常生活

### 翻译

此外，增加的私有化肯定会削弱每年3亿游客来到公园的主要原因之一：享受自然并从淹没日常生活的商业鼓点中得到喘息。

### 考点

**why定语从句+冒号解释+不定式+that定语从句**；27题A选项的依据——增加露营地私有化可能会破坏游客体验（spoil visitor experience = undercut reason to enjoy nature and get respite from commercial drumbeat）。

## 长难句6

> The peer-reviewed economic survey of 700 U.S. taxpayers, conducted by mail and internet, also found that people would be willing to pay a significant amount of money to make sure the parks and their programs are kept intact.

### 句子拆分

主干：**The peer-reviewed economic survey also found that people would be willing to pay a significant amount of money**
- `of 700 U.S. taxpayers`：介词短语修饰survey
- `conducted by mail and internet`：过去分词作后置定语，修饰survey
- `that people would be willing to pay a significant amount of money to make sure the parks and their programs are kept intact`：that宾语从句，作found的宾语
  - `to make sure the parks and their programs are kept intact`：不定式作目的状语
  - `the parks and their programs are kept intact`：省略that的宾语从句，作make sure的宾语

### 生词

peer-reviewed：同行评审的；economic survey：经济调查；taxpayer：纳税人；conduct by：通过…进行；mail and internet：邮件和互联网；be willing to：愿意；pay a significant amount of money：支付大量金钱；make sure：确保；programs：项目；be kept intact：保持完整

### 翻译

这项通过邮件和互联网进行的对700名美国纳税人的同行评审经济调查还发现，人们愿意支付大量金钱以确保公园及其项目保持完整。

### 考点

**过去分词后置定语+that宾语从句+不定式目的状语+省略that的宾语从句**；28题C选项的依据——大多数受访者同意为国家公园支付额外费用（agree to pay extra = willing to pay additional taxes for next 10 years to avoid cuts）。

# 2023 英二 Text2 26–30｜题目+选项+逐选项解析

> 原文主题：特朗普政府填补国家公园资金缺口的想法（亚马逊送货到帐篷、餐车进公园）被认为荒谬，但政府在一件事上是对的：国家公园处于危机中，维护积压超120亿美元，基础设施崩溃；但私有化商业化露营地不是万能药（露营地只是积压一小部分，特许经营者只交5%收入）；增加私有化会削弱游客来公园的主要原因——享受自然、从商业鼓点中喘息；真正的问题是公园长期缺乏资金；调查发现美国人高度重视国家公园，81%受访者愿意未来10年支付额外税收以避免削减；国家公园有多重价值（逃避场所、自然象征、教育项目、碳封存、文化艺术、旅游、保护历史遗迹）；但公园靠小成本运作——国会每年只拨30亿（自2001年持平），而游客自1980年增50%+达3.3亿

## 26. What problem are U.S. national parks faced with?

[A] Decline of business profits. [B] Inadequate commercialization. [C] Lack of transportation services. [D] Poorly maintained infrastructure.

✅答案：**D** 定位：第二段。`Collectively, they have a maintenance backlog of more than $12 billion. Roads, trails, restrooms, visitor centers and other infrastructure are crumbling`（它们总共有超过120亿美元的维护积压。道路、小径、洗手间、游客中心和其他基础设施正在崩溃）。说明国家公园面临的问题是基础设施维护不善。

- A：错误。Decline of business profits（商业利润下降）无中生有。
- B：错误。Inadequate commercialization（商业化不足）与原文相反。作者反对过度商业化，不是商业化不足。
- C：错误。Lack of transportation services（缺乏交通服务）偷换概念。道路是基础设施的一部分，不是缺乏交通服务。
- D：正确。基础设施维护不善。maintenance backlog of $12 billion + infrastructure crumbling = Poorly maintained infrastructure。

## 30. It can be concluded from the text that the national park system ______.

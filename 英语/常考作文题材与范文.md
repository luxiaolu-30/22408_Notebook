## 二、考研英语二大作文范文（图表作文）

### 范文1：数字化生活/手机依赖（2024年真题方向）

> **题目**：某市居民周末活动调查（柱状图）

**范文**：

As is illustrated in the bar chart, the distribution of weekend activities among residents in a certain city has undergone significant changes. Specifically, the proportion of people spending time on mobile phones has soared to 45%, while outdoor activities and reading account for merely 20% and 15% respectively.

A number of factors could account for this phenomenon. To begin with, the rapid development of mobile technology has made smartphones an indispensable part of people's daily lives, providing instant access to entertainment, social media, and information. Moreover, the fast-paced modern lifestyle leaves urban residents with limited energy for physical activities after a exhausting work week. Additionally, the convenience of digital entertainment has gradually replaced traditional leisure patterns, such as reading books and engaging in face-to-face social interactions.

From my perspective, this trend deserves our careful consideration. While digital devices bring unprecedented convenience, excessive screen time may lead to both physical and mental health issues, including eye strain, social isolation, and decreased attention span. Therefore, it is advisable for individuals to strike a balance between digital entertainment and outdoor activities, ensuring a healthier and more fulfilling lifestyle.

**亮点句型**：
- `As is illustrated in the bar chart...` — 图表描述开头
- `undergone significant changes` — 变化类高频表达
- `A number of factors could account for this phenomenon.` — 原因分析过渡句
- `To begin with... Moreover... Additionally...` — 原因列举
- `From my perspective...` — 个人观点表达
- `it is advisable for individuals to...` — 建议句型

### 范文2：居民健身方式（2021年真题方向）

> **题目**：居民健身方式调查（柱状图）

**范文**：

The bar chart clearly reveals the distribution of residents' preferences for different ways of keeping fit. As can be seen, jogging tops the list, accounting for 35%, followed by swimming (25%) and cycling (20%), while gym workouts and other sports constitute the remaining 20%.

Several reasons can be identified to explain this distribution. First and foremost, jogging requires no special equipment or venue, making it the most accessible and cost-effective form of exercise for the general public. Furthermore, swimming and cycling are favored by young adults because they are not only effective workouts but also enjoyable recreational activities. In contrast, gym workouts, though highly professional, involve membership fees and fixed schedules, which may deter some potential participants.

In light of these findings, it is evident that health awareness has been significantly enhanced among the general public. However, to promote a more balanced development of fitness activities, local governments should invest more in public sports facilities and organize community-based exercise programs, thereby encouraging more citizens to adopt regular physical exercise as a lifelong habit.

### 范文3：线上购物与线下购物（2023年真题方向）

> **题目**：线上购物与线下购物对比（柱状图）

**范文**：

The bar chart compares the percentages of consumers choosing online shopping and offline shopping over the past decade. Obviously, the proportion of online shopping has witnessed a dramatic increase from 20% in 2014 to 70% in 2024, while offline shopping has experienced a corresponding decline.

This trend can be attributed to several factors. The primary reason is the unparalleled convenience offered by e-commerce platforms, which allow consumers to browse products, compare prices, and complete purchases without leaving their homes. Additionally, the continuous improvement in logistics services and the widespread adoption of mobile payment have further facilitated online transactions. Last but not least, the outbreak of the COVID-19 pandemic has accelerated the transformation of consumption patterns, making online shopping a necessity rather than an option.

Nevertheless, it is worth noting that offline shopping still maintains its unique advantages, such as the ability to physically examine products and the immediate gratification of taking purchases home. Therefore, the future of retail likely lies in the integration of online and offline channels, providing consumers with a seamless and comprehensive shopping experience.

## 三、四六级作文范文（议论文/书信）

### 范文4：人工智能的影响（议论文）

> **题目**：The Impact of Artificial Intelligence

**范文**：

In recent years, artificial intelligence has penetrated nearly every aspect of our daily life, sparking widespread debate regarding its implications. While some embrace AI as a driving force for progress, others express concerns about its potential risks.

On the one hand, AI has brought tremendous benefits to society. In the medical field, AI-powered diagnostic systems can detect diseases with unprecedented accuracy, significantly improving patient outcomes. In education, personalized learning platforms powered by AI enable students to study at their own pace, making education more accessible and efficient. Moreover, AI has revolutionized transportation through autonomous vehicles, which promise to reduce accidents caused by human error.

On the other hand, the rapid advancement of AI has raised legitimate concerns. The most pressing issue is job displacement, as automation threatens to replace millions of workers in manufacturing, customer service, and even creative industries. Furthermore, ethical dilemmas surrounding data privacy, algorithmic bias, and the potential misuse of AI technology cannot be overlooked.

In conclusion, artificial intelligence is a double-edged sword. To maximize its benefits while minimizing its drawbacks, it is imperative for governments, industries, and educational institutions to collaborate on establishing robust regulatory frameworks and investing in workforce retraining programs.

### 范文5：建议信（书信类）

> **题目**：给留学生朋友推荐校园活动

**范文**：

Dear Tom,

I am writing to recommend several campus activities that I believe will help you better adapt to college life and make the most of your experience here.

First of all, I would strongly suggest joining the English Speaking Club, which meets every Thursday evening. This club provides an excellent opportunity to practice public speaking and meet students from diverse cultural backgrounds. Additionally, the Volunteer Service Organization is worth considering, as it organizes weekly community service projects that not only contribute to society but also allow you to develop teamwork skills and make new friends. What's more, if you are interested in traditional Chinese culture, the Calligraphy Association offers beginner-friendly workshops every Saturday morning.

I would appreciate it if you could take these recommendations into consideration. Should you need any further information, please do not hesitate to contact me.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

## 四、万能模板速记

### 图表作文三段式框架

```
【第一段】描述图表
As is illustrated in the chart/graph, ...
The chart reveals/shows that ...
Specifically, ... while ...

【第二段】分析原因
A number of factors could account for this phenomenon.
To begin with, ... Moreover, ... Additionally, ...

【第三段】发表看法
From my perspective, ...
Therefore, it is advisable that ...
In conclusion, ...
```

### 议论文三段式框架

```
【开头】引出话题+表明观点
Recently, ... has become a hot topic.
Some believe that ..., while others hold that ...
In my view, ...

【主体】分论点论证
Firstly/First of all, ...
Moreover/Furthermore, ...
Last but not least, ...

【结尾】总结升华
To sum up/In conclusion, ...
It is imperative that ...
Only in this way can we ...
```

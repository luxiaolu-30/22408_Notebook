# 第五章 接近尾声——状语和状语从句

> **本章目标**：全面掌握 9 大类状语从句（时间、地点、原因、条件、让步、目的、结果、方式、比较），了解 4 大状语成分，理解长难句中状语剥离的技巧。

---

## 一、状语概述

### 什么是状语

**状语 = 修饰动词、形容词、副词或整个句子的成分**，表示动作发生的时间、地点、原因、条件、方式、目的、结果、让步、比较等。

### 4 大状语成分

| 形式 | 举例 | 补充说明 |
|------|------|---------|
| **副词（短语）** | `She sings beautifully.` | 前置简单 |
| **介词短语** | `I will meet you at the library.` | 后置 |
| **非谓语动词短语** | `Walking in the park, I met Tom.` | doing（主动进行）/ done（被动完成）/ to do（目的将来） |
| **从句（状语从句）** | `When I was young, I loved reading.` | 最重要，最易混淆 |

**重要识别技巧：状语的位置最灵活**
```
状语位置：
- 句首：When I was young, ...
- 句中：I, when I was young, ...
- 句末：... when I was young.
```

## 二、9 大类状语从句

### 1. 时间状语从句

| 引导词 | 含义 | 例 |
|--------|------|----|
| when | 当...时候 | `When I was a child, I lived in Beijing.` |
| while | 当...时候（强调持续）| `While I was sleeping, the phone rang.` |
| as | 当...时候（强调同时）| `As I walked, I saw him.` |
| before | 在...之前 | `Close the door before you leave.` |
| after | 在...之后 | `After he finished, he went home.` |
| until / till | 直到... | `Wait until I come back.` |
| as soon as | 一...就... | `I'll call you as soon as I arrive.` |
| since | 自从... | `I've lived here since I was born.` |
| by the time | 到...时候 | `By the time you arrive, dinner will be ready.` |

#### 特殊句型

**① no sooner...than / hardly...when（"一...就..."半倒装）**

```
① I had no sooner arrived home than he asked me to start on another journey.
② I had hardly had time to sit down when the phone rang again.

倒装版（高级表达）：
① No sooner had I arrived home than he asked me to start on another journey.
② Hardly had I had time to sit down when the phone rang again.
```

**主过从过原则**：这种结构中，主句用过去完成时，从句用一般过去时。

#### until 和 not...until 的区分

| 时态 | 主句动词 | 用法 |
|------|---------|------|
| 延续性动词 | live, work, study, wait | 直接用 until（不用 not until） |
| 瞬间动词 | realize, come, leave, stop | 用 not until |

```
① I'll wait here until you come.    ← wait 是延续性动词
② I didn't realize the truth until I had my own daughter.   ← realize 是瞬间动词
```

**强调句转换**：将 not until 句型改为强调句
```
原句：I didn't realize the truth until I had my own daughter.
改强调：It was not until I had my own daughter that I realized the truth.
```

### 2. 地点状语从句

| 引导词 | 含义 | 例 |
|--------|------|----|
| where | 在...地方 | `Where there is a will, there is a way.` |
| wherever | 无论...地方 | `Wherever you go, I'll follow you.` |

### 3. 原因状语从句

| 引导词 | 含义 | 例 |
|--------|------|----|
| because | 因为（最强因果）| `I stayed home because I was sick.` |
| since | 既然（已知信息）| `Since you're here, let's start.` |
| as | 由于（语气较轻）| `As it was raining, we stayed inside.` |
| now that | 既然 | `Now that you're here, we can begin.` |
| in that | 因为（在...方面）| `The book is useful in that it provides examples.` |

#### because / since / as 区别

```
because：最强因果，回答 why 的问题
since  ：已知信息，常译为"既然"
as    ：附加原因、理由较弱，常译为"由于"
```

### 4. 条件状语从句

| 引导词 | 含义 | 例 |
|--------|------|----|
| if | 如果 | `If it rains, I won't go.` |
| unless | 除非 | `Unless you study hard, you won't pass.`（=if...not） |
| as long as | 只要 | `You can go out as long as you finish your homework.` |
| on condition that | 在...条件下 | `I'll lend you the money on condition that you return it next week.` |
| provided (that) | 假如 | `Provided that he agrees, we'll start.` |
| in case | 万一 | `Take an umbrella in case it rains.` |

### 5. 让步状语从句

| 引导词 | 含义 | 例 |
|--------|------|----|
| although / though | 尽管 | `Although it was cold, he went out.` |
| even though / even if | 即使 | `Even though I failed, I won't give up.` |
| while | 虽然（置于句首）| `While I understand your view, I don't agree.` |
| as | 尽管（倒装）| `Young as he is, he is very capable.` |
| no matter + 疑问词 | 无论... | `No matter what you say, I won't change my mind.` |
| 疑问词-ever | 无论... | `Whatever you say, I won't change my mind.` |

**特殊倒装句**：although 引导让步状从时，若从句的表语也是名词，可以倒装。
```
原句：Although he is young, he is capable.
倒装：Young as he is, he is capable.
```

### 6. 目的状语从句

| 引导词 | 含义 | 例 |
|--------|------|----|
| so that | 以便 | `He studied hard so that he could pass.` |
| in order that | 为了 | `In order that he could arrive early, he took a taxi.` |
| for fear that | 以免 | `He drove slowly for fear that he might miss the way.` |
| lest | 以免（书面）| `He walked on tiptoe lest anyone should hear him.` |

**虚拟语气**：so that / in order that / lest 后的从句常用 `(should) + V` 的虚拟形式。
```
He studied hard so that he (should) pass the exam.
```

### 7. 结果状语从句

| 引导词 | 含义 | 例 |
|--------|------|----|
| so...that | 如此...以致于 | `He is so tall that he can reach the top.` |
| such...that | 如此...以致于 | `It is such a beautiful day that I want to go out.` |
| so that | 以致于（有时表结果）| `He walked so fast that I couldn't catch up.` |

**so...that 与 such...that 区别：**
```
so + adj/adv + that
so + many/much/few/little + n + that

such + (a/an) + adj + n + that
```

### 8. 方式状语从句

| 引导词 | 含义 | 例 |
|--------|------|----|
| as | 像...一样 | `Do as I do.` |
| as if / as though | 仿佛、好像 | `He talks as if he knew everything.` |
| the way | 按...方式 | `He doesn't do it the way I told him.` |

**注意**：as if / as though 后用虚拟语气（表示不真实）
```
He talks as if he knew everything.     ← 与现在事实相反
He talked as if he had known everything. ← 与过去事实相反
```

### 9. 比较状语从句

| 引导词 | 含义 | 例 |
|--------|------|----|
| as...as | 和...一样 | `He is as tall as his father.` |
| not so/as...as | 不如... | `He is not so tall as his father.` |
| than | 比 | `He is taller than me.` |
| the more..., the more... | 越...越... | `The more you learn, the more you know.` |

#### 比较句型变形

| 原级 | 比较级 | 最高级 |
|------|--------|--------|
| as tall as | taller than | the tallest |
| as much as | more than | the most |

## 三、状语从句与定语从句的区别（高频考点）

| 特征 | 状语从句 | 定语从句 |
|------|---------|---------|
| 修饰对象 | 动词、形容词、副词、整个句子 | 名词（短语） |
| 引导词功能 | 状语功能 | 定语功能 |
| 翻译 | 放在被修饰成分前 | "……的"或拆句 |

**关键词区别：**
```
状语：where（地点），when（时间），why（原因）
定语：where（……的地点），when（……的时间），why（……的原因）
```

#### 真实例题对比

```
① I will never forget the day when we first met.
   when 在从句中作时间状语 → 状语从句
② I will never forget the day which/that we spent together.
   which 在从句中作 spent 的宾语 → 定语从句
```

**辨别方法**：看引导词在从句中所作的成分。
- 作状语 → 状语从句
- 作定语、宾语 → 定语从句

## 四、状语和状语从句的考点分析

### 考点 1：写作中的应用

#### A. 九大写作常用句型

| 主题 | 状语从句应用 |
|------|------------|
| 时间 | When I was in college, I learned the importance of... |
| 原因 | Because/Since the economy is developing rapidly, ... |
| 条件 | If we don't protect the environment, ... |
| 让步 | Although/Though it is difficult, we should... |
| 结果 | ...so that everyone can benefit |
| 目的 | ...in order that we can... |
| 让步 | Whatever you say, I... |

#### B. 提分高端表达

**特殊句型（让作文瞬间提分）：**

| 句型 | 例 |
|------|-----|
| not only...but also | `Not only does he speak English, but also he speaks French.` |
| hardly...when（倒装）| `Hardly had I arrived when the phone rang.` |
| no sooner...than（倒装）| `No sooner had I left than the rain started.` |
| as 倒装 | `Young as he is, he knows a lot.` |
| not...until 强调 | `It was not until...that...` |

### 考点 2：长难句分析中的应用

#### "两步走"分析含状语从句的长难句

```
第一步：找谓语动词（看有几个独立的分句）
第二步：找状语（位置灵活，可在句首/中/末）
第三步：剥离子句，找出主句主干
```

#### 真实例题分析（2021 年英二阅读第一篇）

```
原文：We know we are moving into a period where the jobs in demand will change rapidly, 
as will the requirements of the jobs that remain.
```

**结构分析：**

```
第一步：找谓语
- know（主句谓语）
- are moving（省略 that 的从句谓语）
- will change（where 引导定语从句的谓语）
- will change（as 引导倒装句——正常语序为 the requirements...will change）

第二步：找状语/定语
- where 引导定语从句修饰 period
- as 引导比较状语从句（as...will change = ...也会改变）

第三步：找主干
主句主干：We know (that) we are moving...
```

## 五、句子成分总结

### 一句话浓缩："长难句的化妆公式"

```
（状语 +） 主语（定语 / 同位语 / 状语）+ 谓语（时态 / 语态）+ 宾语 / 表语（定语 / 同位语 / 状语）
```

**形象比喻：**
> 长难句就像一个化了妆的女人。
> - 主语 = 模特本身（核心）
> - 定语 / 同位语 = 衣服 / 帽子（装饰主体）
> - 状语 = 项链 / 戒指（额外修饰）
> - 一切修饰都要为主语/谓语/宾语服务

### 写作中的"化妆"原则

```
提示：括号内的成分（除时态、语态）都代表可有可无的成分。
    化妆太过反而不好看，所以作文中不要每句话都加上修饰成分，
    这样句子太长，老师读起来也很费力。
    我们要做到有长句也有短句，长短结合，有张有弛。
```

#### 实操演练

**素颜句（简单句）：**
```
① They are singing songs.
② I enjoy reading books.
③ English is important.
```

**化妆后（长难句）：**
```
① [When we are having English class], they, [a group of vigorous university students], 
   [who like various kinds of challenges], are singing songs 
   [Yesterday Once More and Country Road], [which have been popular with youngsters] 
   [although they have been strikingly tired].

② I, [a student on campus], enjoy reading books [which are beneficial to our 
   mental and physical health] [whenever I am free].

③ [With the development of cultural exchange], English, [as a major communicating 
   tool in the world], is so important [that almost all Chinese students are learning it].
```

**化妆过程（修饰成分都用方括号标出）：**
- 时间状语从句
- 同位语
- 定语从句
- 同位语
- 定语从句
- 让步状语从句
- 介词短语（伴随状语）
- 同位语（+后置定语）
- 定语从句
- 时间状语从句

## 六、本章核心总结

### 9 大状语从句速记

```
1. 时间：when while as before after until since as soon as
2. 地点：where wherever
3. 原因：because since as now that
4. 条件：if unless as long as provided in case
5. 让步：although though even though while as (倒装) no matter + 疑问词
6. 目的：so that in order that for fear that lest
7. 结果：so...that such...that
8. 方式：as as if as though the way
9. 比较：as...as not so...as than the more...the more
```

### 状语成分 4 大类口诀

```
副词 / 介词 / 非谓（doing/done/to do） / 从句（共四种形式都能作状语）
```

### 状语从句 vs 定语从句关键词

| 关键词 | 状语从句 | 定语从句 |
|--------|---------|---------|
| when | 时间状语 | 修饰"时间名词" |
| where | 地点状语 | 修饰"地点名词" |
| why | 原因状语 | 修饰"the reason" |

### 写作必背的"三大王炸"句型

```
① Not only...but also...
② Hardly...when / No sooner...than（倒装）
③ It was not until...that...（强调）
```

### 最关键的"刘晓艳提醒"

> "状语从句的关键词有时和定语从句长得一样，关键看它在从句里作的成分——作状语就是状语从句，作定语或宾语就是定语从句。"
